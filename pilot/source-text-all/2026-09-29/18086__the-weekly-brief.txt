The Weekly Brief
Thought of the week
Despite continued disruptions to energy supply, the global economy has shown
remarkable resilience. September’s flash Purchasing Managers’ Indices (PMIs)
signal solid momentum across the private sector, with strength evident in both
manufacturing and services. Importantly, the underlying details suggest the
expansion is broadening beyond output alone, with evidence that demand is
beginning to translate into firmer hiring intentions. At the same time, robust
activity appears to be coinciding with renewed price pressures, suggesting that
cost increases are being passed through more readily. Taken together, strong
growth, firmer labour demand and stickier inflation dynamics help explain why
policymakers have recently leaned towards tightening policy. Recent rate hikes
reflect resilience in activity as much as the inflation impulse – though we still
expect this to be a mid‑cycle adjustment in rates rather than the start of a full-
blown hiking cycle.
