Bull do Brazil
Brazil has been one of the biggest momentum trades around, but things are getting stretched. EWZ is
overbought, call positioning is at records and shorts have been squeezed hard, all with the election just
weeks away.
Near term, expect noise. Longer term, the commodity backdrop keeps the Brazil bull case very much
alive.
Too much, too fast
The Brazil bull has been absolutely huge. EWZ reversed right at the key $39 area, and RSI remains
extremely overbought. This is still a largely mean-reverting asset, with a history of producing huge
counter-trend moves.

The vol shock
Spot up, vol up has been a powerful dynamic in EWZ. Investors have been forced to chase upside
optionality in Brazil, which in turn has spilled over into spot, adding further fuel to the rally.
