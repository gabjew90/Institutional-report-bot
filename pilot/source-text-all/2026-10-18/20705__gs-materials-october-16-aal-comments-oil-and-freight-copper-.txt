Oil - A few comments on Oil from Pete in Trading – Freight - .... WTI-Brent spread narrowing and some
heat coming out of the insane tightness in physical/dated brent. What's happening?
- freight rates are starting to come off extremely stretched highs. Why? some tankers are coming out of
ME towards the atlantic. Potentially due to increasing Iranian attacks? Unclear.
- the premium for using a products tanker to transport crude (clean/dirty spread) is eye watering and likely
driving some switching
- the very prompt Brent bbls were being bid by a small number of mkt participants, with spec that it was
related to CFDs/other positions. That trade is over. The atlantic basin does not look particularly tight right
now (maybe also some bbls leaking in from the Sep surge in SoH flows)
- US pushing for more G7 SPR release
- Crude draws in US inventories
- EQNR and AKRBP had been seen as "winners" from higher dated brent. I really, really, really don't think
we should be capitalising freight volatility into our oil companies - at best this is a p/e of 1. More
important for both is Johan Sverdrup being firmly in decline (EQNR has a number of other issues as well)
- Does this cut the same way for intl refiners? Yes, probably, although I would argue that we're also past
peak distillate, particularly for the equities, given windfall tax risk and unclear earnings momentum
