Global fund flows, week ending October 14 
Flows into mutual funds and related investment products were positive across 
n
both equities and fixed income. 
Global equity funds saw net inflows in the week ending October 14 (+$65bn vs 
n
+$12bn in the previous week). DM saw renewed strength in net inflows driven by 
US funds. EM also saw meaningful net inflows driven by continued inflows to 
Brazil funds, alongside Mainland China and Taiwan funds. At the sector level, 
technology funds saw the largest net inflows and a meaningful pickup ahead of 
the AI-related tech sell-off later in the week (see Chart of the Week). Industrial 
funds saw the largest net outflows.  
Flows into global fixed income funds remained largely well-supported. Short- 
n
and long-duration bond funds and inflation-protected bond funds saw inflows in 
the week ending October 14. In EM, hard-currency bond funds saw continued 
net outflows while local-currency bond funds saw net inflows. Money market 
fund assets decreased by $53bn. 
Cross-border FX flows were positive and remained well-supported. USD and 
n
BRL continued to see the strongest net demand while CNY and INR saw 
continued net outflows.
