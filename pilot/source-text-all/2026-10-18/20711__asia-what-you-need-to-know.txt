Asia - What You Need to Know
I do not expect yesterday’s stress to translate into sustained follow-through selling today. The flow tells a
more constructive story than the tape: selling was concentrated where positioning was richest, AI exposure
has already been materially cleaned up, and October fiscal year-end seasonality provides a logical
explanation for the de-risking, while the fundamental backdrop remains intact.
• Notional Perspective: Notional values traded across the franchise were up 42% but the Desk was only 1.1x
better to sell. The interesting aspect of the Desk flow was that no sector was more than 1.4x better to sell.
The country level gets interesting. Korean turnover was up only 9% while Japan was up 83% and Taiwan
was up 51%. The Desk saw much more selling in Japan 1.4x relative to both Taiwan at 1.06x and Korea
which was only net sold by $2 million dollars.
Source: Global Banking & Markets, 16 October 2026, Past performance is not indicative of future results
• Record Taiwan: Yesterday was a record net selling day in Taiwan with Taiwan's net sold stock on the Desk
5x the net sold amount in Korea. Investors traded 1.4x more notional in Japan but the Desk saw 1.1x more
net selling in Taiwan than in Japan. Again, showing the aggressive de-risking by Hedge Funds in Taiwan
(and Japan) relative to Korea. I will caveat that foreigners did net sell Korea by $2.7 billion but this was not
reflected in Goldman flow.

Source: Global Banking & Markets, 15 October 2026, Past performance is not indicative of future results
• To understand this dynamic, one needs to understand the positioning.
Prime Data - Taiwan: Based on the September data, Taiwan saw net buying for the month with strong risk
on flows as Hedge Funds added longs and shorts in the market. Allocations increased, with Net allocation
ending the month at 7.2% (79th percentile 1-year, 96th percentile 5-year), while Gross allocation ended at
4.5% (93rd percentile 1-year, 99th percentile 5-year).
Source: Prime Services, 2 October 2026, "Prime Services Asia Monthly: September 2026"
• Prime Data - Korea: To the contrary, Korea continued to experience large net selling and de-grossing
flows in September with long sales exceeding short sales in a ratio of 5 to 1. Net allocation to Korea declined
72 bps to 4.4% (47th percentile 1-year, 89th percentile 5-year), while Gross allocation marginally declined to
3.0% (79th percentile 1-year, 96th percentile 5-year).

Source: Prime Services, 2 October 2026, "Prime Services Asia Monthly: September 2026"
• TMT Drawdown: On Wednesday, Global AI stocks (GSXGGLAI constituents) collectively saw the
second largest net selling in the past year after July (-2.8 Z score), driven by long-and-short sales 3.5 to 1
(GS PB). The GS sentiment indicator has been negative for 6 straight weeks and last reading was its lowest
at -1.3 (as of Friday), aka, overall length is not max long. The AI trade is still long, but now a 6 out 10 in
terms of length and much cleaner than it was in Jun/July. Unlike in the U.S., the Desk flows were not as busy
until Thursday. Yet, the Desk net sold -$3.3bn in TMT in the past 6 sessions.
Source: Bloomberg, 16 October 2026, Past performance is not indicative of future results
• Tax Loss Selling: Finally, I remain very constructive the region as October is bringing seasonal flow to
bare. October is fiscal year end for 1 out of every 4 US mutual funds. This equates to 2.3t USD of active

AUM and 1.7t USD of passive AUM. So, these flow dynamics make sense in context of mutual fund fiscal
year end seasonality. The pace and the size is selling is relatively moderate compared to previous drawdown
periods which reflects the cleaner positioning in the Equity markets.
Source: Goldman Sachs Global Investment Research, Aditya Sharma, as of 10/12/26. Past performance is not indicative of future returns
