expense matters in France as it has high levels of private sector debt as well as 
public sector debt. According to the Bank for International Settlements (BIS), 
France’s nonfinancial corporate debt-to-GDP is 155%. In the United States, 
nonfinancial corporate debt to GDP is 73%. While much of the focus has been 
on the negative impacts of rising bond yields on French government finances,
