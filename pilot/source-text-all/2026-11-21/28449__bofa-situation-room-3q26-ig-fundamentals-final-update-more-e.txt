3Q26 IG fundamentals final update: more earnings, stable 
debt 
By now, we have 3Q fundamentals for 89% of US IG issuers in our sample (public US IG 
issuers ex. Financials and Utilities). This almost-final estimate shows IG credit 
fundamentals improved in 3Q. Net leverage declined led by BBBs to the lowest level 
since 2021, and gross leverage reached the lowest level since 2018. Earnings growth 
and margins are near the highest since 2022. Cash levels increased modestly in 3Q in 
line with the seasonal trend. Debt growth remained slow, and capex – moderate for a 
median US IG issuer. 
IG leverage: lower 
We estimate median gross leverage declined to 2.45x in 3Q (Exhibit 1), and net leverage 
declined to 1.91x in 3Q from 1.95x in 2Q.  
Further details on IG credit fundamentals 
Both gross and net debt growth was modest at +1.0% and +0.7% YoY in 3Q, 
respectively. (Exhibit 9, Exhibit 10). The median YoY LTM EBITDA growth accelerated to 
+9.9% in 3Q from +9.3% in 2Q, the highest since 2021 (Exhibit 12). Strong earnings 
pulled the coverage ratio higher to 10.74x (Exhibit 15), while borrowing costs were 
roughly stable (Exhibit 16). Net leverage declined for BBBs, to the lowest level since 
2018 (Exhibit 2, Exhibit 3). Finally, cash growth came in about typical at +1.7% QoQ 
(Exhibit 19).

higher leverage, especially for their rating. For these US non-financial non-utility issuers
