Erasca Inc. (ERAS): ERAS-0015 (pan-RAS) data at Triple Meeting is 
encouraging and suggests potential to differentiate
20 November 2026 | 1:32PM EST
ERAS presented updated monotherapy data for ERAS-0015 (oral pan-RAS)at the 
EORTC-NCI-AACR (“Triple”) Meeting this morning and hosted a conference call. Key 
data included a 44% confirmed overall response rate (cORR) in 2L+ pancreatic ductal 
adenocarcinoma (PDAC; n=25) and 50% cORR in 2L+ non-small cell lung cancer 
(NSCLC; n=10), with 60% in post-ICI/platinum NSCLC (n=5), both at 32mg. Dose 
intensity and key adverse events (rash/stomatitis/GI) appeared consistent with prior 
data, along with early but positive durability (all 11 PDAC responders and all 10 
32mg NSCLC patients remain on treatment).  
We view the muted stock reaction (-1.5%, ~in line with XBI intraday) as potentially 
reflecting built-in efficacy/tolerability expectations, and believe the data are 
encouraging relative to expectations as well as our key debate - potential 
differentiation vs. RVMD’s (covered by Andrea Newkirk) daraxonrasib - given: i) 44% 
cORR in 2L+ PDAC compares well (cross-trial caveats) with daraxonrasib’s 32% ORR 
in RASOLUTE-302 given the heavily pre-treated population (84% 3L+ vs. 100% 2L) 
and high representation of G12R mutations (44% vs. 12%), where one would expect 
lower responses; ii) tolerability at 32mg looks encouraging on dose 
interruption/reduction and in the context of no protocol-recommended prophylaxis; 
iii) progress in 1L PDAC, with pivotal timing pulled forward to 1H27 (vs. 2027 prior) 
and potential to explore the likely monotherapy dose in combination with 
chemotherapy (potentially differentiating); and iv) combination progress in NSCLC 
and colorectal cancer (CRC), including no dose-limiting toxicities for 32mg plus 
pembrolizumab thus far, initiation of ERAS-0015 plus 
chemotherapy/immunotherapy in 1L NSCLC, and initial responses with 
panitumumab in CRC. 
On the forward, we look to 1H27 combination data (at least CRC, potentially PDAC 
and NSCLC) and progress around pivotal trial initiations (particularly in 1L PDAC) as 
key catalysts for the stock. Details within.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.
