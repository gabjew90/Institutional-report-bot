GS So - Taiwan Weekly Kickstart: TAIEX rallied 12% on a sharp
Tech rebound alongside global tech recovery and policy support;
QFII outflows moderated but shorts rose, while retail margin
unwound and ETF inflows remained strong 14 Nov 2026
Alvin So, CFA, Timothy Moe, CFA, Kinger Lau, CFA, Terry Chan  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  Portfolio Strategy Research
14 Nov 2026, 3:57am HKT (Fri 13 Nov, 2:57pm ET)
Performance: TAIEX/TPEx gained 12%/10%, outperforming MXAPJ by 7pp, driven by sharp rebounds in
Chemicals (+16%), Tech (TSMC +10%, other semis +16%, hardware +11%), and Capital Goods (+9%), while
Utilities, Telcos, Mining, and Autos (flat) lagged. (Exhibit 23)
Flows: QFIIs net sold US$2.9bn of cash equities, concentrated in Tech (TSMC: -US$1.2bn; other Tech: -
US$3.1bn), while buying Industrials (+US$570mn) and Financials (+US$350mn) and increasing net-short
futures positions by US$3.1bn (Exhibit 32). Domestic funds net bought US$3.1bn broadly across sectors
(Exhibit 27). Retail investors were only modest net buyers of cash equities amid margin unwinds but
continued adding ETF exposure (Exhibit 33). ETFs saw US$5.8bn of inflows, led by broad-market passive
(+US$2.2bn), active (+US$1.7bn), leveraged (+US$1.1bn), and dividend (+US$0.8bn) ETFs (Exhibit 42).
EPS Revisions: Consensus 2027E EPS rose 0.3% (Exhibit 61). 3Q26 results concluded with +74%/+7%
yoy/qoq EPS growth, a 4% aggregate beat but 2% median miss (Exhibit 15). Oct sales rose 54% yoy (vs.
+50% in Sep), tracking ahead of consensus (Exhibit 16). Macro Data: Exports +59% yoy (vs. Cons +51%, Sep
+61%) on strong tech demand.
Charts of the Week
Tightened Short-Selling Rules: Foreign trading activity has moderated since the short-selling limit was cut
from 30% to 3% of 30-day ADV, while retail participation has increased alongside the activation of the
National Stabilization Fund. Short interest has still risen over the past week, possibly reflecting investors
establishing short positions ahead of tighter quota availability.
Foreign De-Risking vs. Retail Leverage: QFIIs have net sold US$54bn since early October, led almost
entirely by Tech, while hedge funds have also de-risked to near March exposure levels. Retail remains risk-
on, with leveraged ETF exposure rising to US$15bn (0.9% of free float) on renewed inflows. Margin-unwind
risks have eased over the past week, with margin loans down US$2.5bn to US$13bn, the maintenance ratio
recovering to ~165%, and the share of stocks below 130% falling to 27% from 39%.
MSCI Index Review: In its November 2026 review, Taiwan will see 2/15 additions and 4/10 deletions from
the MSCI Standard/IMI Index benchmarks, effective after November 30th. We estimate US$3.8bn of gross
two-way passive flows and US$450mn net passive inflows. (Exhibit 12).
