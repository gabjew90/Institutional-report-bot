1) Asia FX Update
As the market goes through significant risk off, Sun Lu (EM Market Strats) highlights a few dynamics…
INR: The only clear long in the region with lower oil and AI de-risking. RM still have decent UW in
Indian equities to cover and RBI providing USD windows to OMC is essentially still selling
US$10bn/month. Flows clearly skew to positive and market is surprised RBI has not started USD buying
in a meaningful fashion yet. Due to medium-term concern on AI, and strong nominal growth in India+
RBI on hiking cycle, any rebound in USDINR is an opportunity for the INR carry trade.
CNY: Fix still provides stability post APEC on goodwill, despite nothing substantial coming out. Spot was
volatile over the past 24hrs but we continue to see fixing-driven CNY appreciation in the next few months.
Continue to like short USDCNY NDF.
TWD & THB: Both have seen suspected strong defence in the past few days. Major Taiwan equity selloff
and large equity outflows continued, but 33 has not broken for over a week now. In THB, 34 has not
broken despite sharp gold weakness. We think if these key levels do not break, some tactical strength in
TWD (if tech equity recovers) and THB (on firmer tourism seasonal) is likely.
KRW: Remaining in a range with continued outflows countered by exporter flows. Our flow math
suggests if daily equity outflow is less than US$500mio from foreigners, FX is still skewed to USD
selling. We think USDKRW may stay range-bound and could see some rebound into the 27th Nov
KRW30tn div payment date of Samsung (est US$8bn outflow to foreigners). Such rebounds could provide
opportunities for USDKRW downside still. We think spot KRW inflow is likely to stay strong despite how
volatile the future expectations of equity earnings has been.

2) BRL Update
With DIs +50bps wider during the intraday highs and USDBRL trading above 5.95 spot, we synthesise
views from Rafael Fonseca (BRL Rates Trading)…
Performance: Brazilian assets under heavy pressure, with the move turning into a sharp bear flattening in
rates. The session had already started on a weak mood amid continued fiscal/political uncertainty,
including news that current Planning Ministry Bruno Moretti was gaining traction as a potential Finance
Minister, while a broader global risk-off move across US equities and EM FX added to the pressure. The
sell-off accelerated after the BCB intervened in FX, with the rates market initially translating the operation
into rate hikes. Offshore stops then added momentum to the move: DI Jan29 sold off as much as 50bp
intraday and implied Selic reached 16%. The curve bear-flattened aggressively, with the front end
currently around 40bp wider and spreads flattening roughly 5-15bp.

Positioning: An important amplifier. The market had been concentrated in front-end receivers and
steepeners, exactly the pain trade in yesterday’s move. DIs had also outperformed the BRL sell-off over
the previous few sessions, leaving more room for rates to catch up once stops started being triggered.
USDBRL traded above 5.95, around 1.3% higher on the day, with the BCB selling US$1bn spot.
Our View: Despite the similarities with 2024, the current macro backdrop is meaningfully different. With a
material economic slowdown expected ahead, we see virtually no room for the BCB to hikes as a tool to
re-anchor markets. We therefore would favour front-end receivers and look to add on further sell-offs,
while acknowledging that the near-term environment remains extremely difficult to navigate given fiscal
uncertainty, a potentially lengthy MoF nomination process and the lack of visibility around upcoming
BCB board appointments.
