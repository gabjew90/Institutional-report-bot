The great silver washout
Silver has suffered a spectacular collapse, with weekly RSI plunging from above 90 to 30 in almost a
straight line. Prices have retraced all the way back to the original long-term trendline, while volatility
has exploded.
The technical damage is extreme, but positioning remains relatively elevated, and the dollar continues
to play a major role in the price action.
