USA: FOMC Minutes Note “Almost All” Participants Judged That “Further Action” to Raise the Fed
Funds Rate “Would Likely Be Appropriate”
• The minutes to the FOMC’s October meeting showed that “most” participants supported holding the fed
funds rate unchanged at 3.75-4.00%, with “many” citing the “benefits in accumulating additional
information” needed to determine the appropriate policy stance.
• “A few” of these participants nonetheless described the decision as “a close call,” while “some”—
including dissenters Governor Cook and President Hammack—favored a 25bp hike, characterizing the
policy stance as “not sufficiently restrictive.”
• Looking ahead, “almost all” participants judged that, under their baseline outlooks, “further action to
raise the target range for the federal funds rate... would likely be appropriate,” and “a couple” flagged that
a hike larger than 25bp could warrant consideration at the next meeting if incoming inflation data were
“unfavorable.” “
CARBONOMICS - Geo-engineering: Thinking the unthinkable - cooling the planet without de-
carbonization
• De-carbonization's progress remains insufficient to limit warming to 1.5-2°C: the current emission
trajectory is more likely to see warming hit 2.5-2.9°C, and coal consumption is still increasing, despite
tremendous progress in clean tech innovation. As the urgency for climate adaptation solutions increases,
we review some of the global cooling technologies available within the 'geo-engineering' category.
• These aim to replicate some of nature's cooling mechanisms, observed over centuries, such as volcanic
eruptions and strong ocean winds – which increase Earth's albedo, reflecting more sunlight back into

space. We view geo-engineering not as a substitute for de-carbonization, but as a future climate-
management tool, requiring a deeper debate on governance, environmental risks and execution.

Airtel Mobile Commerce (AMCA.L) - Tapping into payments digitisation across sub-Saharan Africa;
initiate at Neutral
• Airtel Money (the operating name of Airtel Mobile Commerce) is a mobile money platform in sub-
Saharan Africa, offering cash-in/cash-out, payments and transfers, and an expanding suite of financial
services such as lending, savings and insurance across 13 markets in East and Francophone Africa.
• We expect Airtel Money to deliver a solid revenue ex-FX CAGR of c.26% over FY26-31E, driven by
rising penetration of Airtel Africa's subscriber base and growing ARPU as customers move beyond wallet
services into higher-value payments and financial services.
