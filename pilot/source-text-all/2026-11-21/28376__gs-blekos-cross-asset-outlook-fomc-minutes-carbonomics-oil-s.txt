GOAL: GLOBAL OPPORTUNITY ASSET LOCATOR - Outlook for 2027: Finding Balance Around Peak Growth
 
• We remain modestly pro-risk but favour more balance across and within assets as nominal and earnings growth
approach a peak following the AI capex boom and energy-driven inflation boost in 2026. Near-term risks remain
elevated, notably AI concerns, renewed energy shocks, more aggressive Fed tightening and persistent fiscal
concerns.
• The balance between slowing growth and rates relief should drive risk appetite in 2027. A peak in nominal growth
need not end the bull market, especially if inflation falls and real growth remains resilient. Easing energy prices and
normalising inflation should limit further policy tightening and allow bond yields to decline gradually.
 
 
USA: FOMC Minutes Note “Almost All” Participants Judged That “Further Action” to Raise the Fed Funds Rate “Would
Likely Be Appropriate”
 
• The minutes to the FOMC’s October meeting showed that “most” participants supported holding the fed funds rate
unchanged at 3.75-4.00%, with “many” citing the “benefits in accumulating additional information” needed to
determine the appropriate policy stance.
• “A few” of these participants nonetheless described the decision as “a close call,” while “some”—including
dissenters Governor Cook and President Hammack—favored a 25bp hike, characterizing the policy stance as “not
sufficiently restrictive.”
• Looking ahead, “almost all” participants judged that, under their baseline outlooks, “further action to raise the target
range for the federal funds rate... would likely be appropriate,” and “a couple” flagged that a hike larger than 25bp
could warrant consideration at the next meeting if incoming inflation data were “unfavorable.” “
 
CARBONOMICS - Geo-engineering: Thinking the unthinkable - cooling the planet without de-carbonization
 
• De-carbonization's progress remains insufficient to limit warming to 1.5-2°C: the current emission trajectory is more
likely to see warming hit 2.5-2.9°C, and coal consumption is still increasing, despite tremendous progress in clean
tech innovation. As the urgency for climate adaptation solutions increases, we review some of the global cooling
technologies available within the 'geo-engineering' category. 
• These aim to replicate some of nature's cooling mechanisms, observed over centuries, such as volcanic eruptions
and strong ocean winds – which increase Earth's albedo, reflecting more sunlight back into space. We view geo-
engineering not as a substitute for de-carbonization, but as a future climate-management tool, requiring a deeper
debate on governance, environmental risks and execution.
 
EUROPE ENERGY: OIL SERVICES - The LNG FID wave gets bigger; GTT our preferred exposure, Technip down to
Neutral
 

• Our latest Top Projects analysis points to a further acceleration in the LNG investment cycle, partly catalysed by
disruption through the Strait of Hormuz, which has increased the focus on supply security and diversification
outside major geopolitical chokepoints. 
• Leveraging our Top Projects database, we now identify c.280 mtpa of capacity reaching FID across 2025-30, a
c.52 mtpa or 23% increase from c.228 mtpa in TP2026 earlier this year.
 
ASML Holding (ASML.AS): Key takeaways from visit to ASML's Wilton site
 
• We attended a tour of ASML's facility in Wilton with investors, hosted by Louis Lu, Head of ASML Operations in
Connecticut, and Peter Convertito, Director Investor Relations Americas at ASML. 
• Key takeaways include: 1) Strong momentum on High NA and the associated ecosystem, 2) Deep competitive
moat gives sustainable long-term advantage, 3) We see multiple avenues for output optimisation over time, 4)
Installed based management and upgrades business to see strong momentum in our view, and 5) Match Act
concerns overdone, in our view. Reiterate Buy (on CL).
 
Airtel Mobile Commerce (AMCA.L) - Tapping into payments digitisation across sub-Saharan Africa; initiate at Neutral
 
• Airtel Money (the operating name of Airtel Mobile Commerce) is a mobile money platform in sub-Saharan Africa,
offering cash-in/cash-out, payments and transfers, and an expanding suite of financial services such as lending,
savings and insurance across 13 markets in East and Francophone Africa. 
• We expect Airtel Money to deliver a solid revenue ex-FX CAGR of c.26% over FY26-31E, driven by rising
penetration of Airtel Africa's subscriber base and growing ARPU as customers move beyond wallet services into
higher-value payments and financial services.
 
Europe Technology: Hardware: Read-across from Nvidia's 3QFY27 results for European AI indexed names
 
• In our view, ASML is best positioned in our coverage given its exposure to both leading-edge Logic and Memory
chips, a stronger competitive moat and longer lead times, while ASMI, BESI and TPRO should benefit, in our view,
from increasing process complexity and advanced packaging and testing requirements, respectively.
 
Tencent Holdings (0700.HK) - 3Q26 review: EPS reset; FCF pressure peaking as new AI products scale; Buy
 
• We believe Tencent's 3Q26 results (our first take) centered on three investor concerns: record-high capex/compute
prepayments driving FCF outflow, a slowdown in core business top-line/profit growth, and a continued step-up in
new AI product investments. 3Q revenue/operating profit of +8%/-6% yoy came in 2ppts/6ppts below GSe on
softer int'l games and elevated AI spending, and we cut our 4Q26E/FY27E/FY28E adj. net profit by 5%/11%/11%.
