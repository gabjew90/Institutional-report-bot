US Retail Fund Monthly Commentary - October 2026
Our view: Our analysis and comments on recently released US retail open-end fund data from Strategic
Insight Simfund for the month of October 2026 are contained within this research note. In October, total
actively-managed equity net fund outflows, ex ETFs and index funds, were flat m/m, with an estimated
annualized organic decline of 8.9% in the month. Actively-managed US equity retail net outflows, ex ETFs
and index funds, worsened m/m, with an estimated annualized organic decline of 10.5% vs 9.6% decline
in September. International equity net outflows improved m/m, with an estimated annualized organic
decline of 5.7% in the month vs a decline of 8.1% in September. Actively-managed fixed income net
flows worsened meaningfully m/m, with an estimated annualized organic decline of 12.1% in the month
vs 5.8% organic decline in September; the weakening bond fund net flow trends would be consistent
with our view that bond fund organic growth could be negatively impacted by rising yields, based on
our analysis of historical trends.
Estimated change in revenue trends: Our analysis of retail asset mix composition and estimated
management fee rates suggest annualized management fee revenue trends improved by 0.1% m/m,
on average, in the month for our select coverage group of traditional asset managers, even with lower
equity returns (MSCI World index -0.8% m/m). We note asset manager stock returns declined -0.9%
m/m by comparison. We break down specific, estimated annualized revenue trends by company in
the table below. We note over the course of the last several months, we have tended to see a rough
relationship between revenue trends and stock returns. In our view, variances between changes in
estimated revenue and changes in stock prices could be driven by factors outside of market performance
on AUM and thus could be the basis for further investigation.
Notable retail product category net flows across the industry: For the month, looking at actively-
managed fund flows within US equity, there were significant net outflows in large-cap growth, large-
cap core, mid-cap core, large-cap value, and equity income partially offset by inflows into mid-cap
growth. Within international equity, net outflows were driven by international large-cap growth, global
large-cap growth, and international large-cap core. Within sector equity, net outflows were driven by
real estate, utility, and global health/biotech. Within fixed income, net outflows were driven by core
bond, intermediate municipal debt, general & insurance municipal debt, multi-sector income, and core
plus bond partially offset by net inflows into short investment grade, short municipal debt, and loan
participation.
We include company-specific AUM and fund flow details for APAM and IVZ on page 4.

Explanation of RBC Capital Markets Equity rating system
An analyst's 'sector' is the universe of companies for which the analyst provides research coverage. Accordingly, the rating assigned
to a particular stock represents solely the analyst's view of how that stock will perform over the next 12 months relative to the
analyst's sector average.
Ratings
Outperform (O): Expected to materially outperform sector average over 12 months.
Sector Perform (SP): Returns expected to be in line with sector average over 12 months.
Underperform (U): Returns expected to be materially below sector average over 12 months.
Restricted (R): RBC policy precludes certain types of communications, including an investment recommendation, when RBC is
acting as an advisor in certain merger or other strategic transactions and in certain other circumstances.
Not Rated (NR): The rating, price targets and estimates have been removed due to applicable legal, regulatory or policy constraints
which may include when RBC Capital Markets is acting in an advisory capacity involving the company.
Risk Rating
The Speculative risk rating reflects a security's lower level of financial or operating predictability, illiquid share trading volumes,
high balance sheet leverage, or limited operating history that result in a higher expectation of financial and/or stock price volatility.

risks) with the reinsurance recoverable at $2.7bn as of year-end 2020.
