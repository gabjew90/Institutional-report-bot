Global fund flows, week ending November 18 
Flows into mutual funds and related investment products turned negative for 
n
fixed income but remained positive for equities. 
Global equity funds saw net inflows in the week ending November 18 (+$67bn vs 
n
+$98bn in the previous week). DM saw continued strength in net inflows driven 
primarily by US funds. EM also saw net inflows driven by Taiwan and Global EM 
Benchmark funds. At the sector level, technology, real estate and telecom funds 
saw net inflows; elsewhere sector funds saw net outflows.  
Flows into global fixed income funds turned negative. Long-duration and 
n
short-duration bonds funds saw inflows in the week ending November 18. In EM, 
hard-currency bond funds and local-currency bond funds saw net outflows; local 
currency bond funds have seen outsized outflows in recent weeks (see Chart of 
the Week). Money market fund assets increased by $0.3bn. 
Cross-border FX flows were positive and remained well-supported with USD, 
n
EUR and JPY seeing the strongest net demand.
