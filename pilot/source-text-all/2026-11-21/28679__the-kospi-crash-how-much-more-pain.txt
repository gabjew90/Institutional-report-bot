The KOSPI crash
KOSPI continues to implode, breaking major support despite already deeply oversold conditions. The
parallels with silver's recent unwind are striking, while the relatively muted reaction in volatility
suggests investors may simply be dumping exposure.
Meanwhile, SK Hynix is flashing fresh technical warnings, even as valuations are starting to look
extraordinarily cheap.
The puke continues
Another overnight puke in KOSPI, with the index closing decisively below the big 5,000 level. The death
cross we flagged in early November remains firmly in place.
The problem with bear markets like this is that oversold can stay oversold for much longer than most
expect. KOSPI has been deeply oversold for weeks, yet the selling continues relentlessly.
No meaningful support until the 4,200 area.

The longer-term picture
KOSPI is approaching its 100-week moving average, while weekly RSI has reached its most oversold
levels in recent memory.
The 200-week moving average sits much lower, around 3,600. We are not suggesting KOSPI needs to
trade down there, but other parabolic manias have unwound all the way back to their long-term
averages. Silver is one recent example.

Another bubble imploding
Most parabolic manias end in tears. We were laughed at when we argued silver was making a major
top (here), and the same happened with KOSPI (here).
The problem with bubbles is that investors get trapped. Short-term trades turn into long-term
investments, and the unwind takes time as people struggle to accept their losses.
Believe it or not, KOSPI is still up on the year. If silver's recent collapse is any guide, there could be
considerably more downside.

Just get me out
Despite the latest puke in KOSPI, volatility has remained surprisingly calm. KOSPI VIX is up from its early
October lows, but the reaction looks relatively muted given the scale of the sell-off.
One possible explanation is that investors are simply dumping their longs rather than chasing
downside protection. Stop-loss selling without the usual scramble for puts. At this point, you just want
out.

Dumping
GS prime brokerage data confirms the liquidation. Hedge funds continue dumping Asian AI/tech
exposure, with long sales outpacing short sales by 2.4 to 1. Korea and Taiwan have dominated the
selling, with funds both unwinding longs and adding fresh shorts. Net allocation to Korea is now back to
levels last seen in early January.

The AI giant's death cross
SK Hynix is THE poster child of the Asian AI mania. The stock may look extraordinarily cheap, but it has
just flashed a death cross. Not exactly a technical signal you want to trade against, no matter how
attractive the valuation looks.

LSEG Workspace
Cheap can get cheaper
Privorotsky on SK Hynix. The stock looks extraordinarily cheap on a blended 24-month P/E basis. The
problem is earnings sustainability. If estimates start coming down, cheap can get a lot cheaper.
That said, the valuation cushion is becoming increasingly meaningful, making it difficult to press the
short here. If you want to express a bearish view on AI and growth, there may be better places to do it.
