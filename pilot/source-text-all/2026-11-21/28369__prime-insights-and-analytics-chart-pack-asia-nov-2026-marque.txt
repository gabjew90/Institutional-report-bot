FICC & Equities
Source: Goldman Sachs FICC and Equities and Prime Services data as of Nov 19, 2026. Past performance is not indicative of future results.
Hedge Fund Performance
•
Hedge Funds continue to face performance challenges MTD November, but drawdowns were relatively contained vs. the steep October declines.
•
Asia focused Fundamental L/S managers are down 1.6% MTD November (vs. MXAP -5.6%). Drawdown was led by beta and alpha from Info Tech tilts, Japan and Volatility 
factors. YTD performance is now at 12.4%, with dispersion levels remaining elevated. The simple average YTD estimate is at 6.3%, while the median estimate is notably lower at 
0.2%, vs. the weighted average estimate of 12.4%.
•
China focused Fundamental LS managers are down 1.4% MTD, with YTD now at 15.1%. Similar to the broader Asia managers, dispersion in this segment remains considerable 
and both YTD returns as well as MTD drawdowns are driven by investments in the AI space.
•
Japan focused managers down 2.0% MTD (vs. MXJP down 4%), with YTD now at 7.6%. Beta led the MTD drawdown over alpha. Median performance remains low at 2% YTD.
•
Global Fundamental LS managers down 0.7% MTD, +8.4% YTD. Global Systematics up 1.6% MTD, +17.9% YTD. 
Positioning and Flows
•
The Global Prime book experienced large net selling flows MTD, driven by long sales. North America and Asia dominated the selling activity.
•
Asian equities continue to see heavy net selling in the Info Tech/AI space, with long sales exceeding short sales 2.4 to 1. Activity was risk-off with Emerging markets dominating 
the selling and de-grossing over Developed markets.
•
Selling was seen across markets with Taiwan and Korea continuing to dominate the net selling activity. Both markets saw Hedge Funds unwind longs as well as add new shorts. 
Net allocation to Taiwan has reverted to levels seen in February while that to Korea has reverted to levels seen in early January.
•
Japan saw moderate selling flows MTD led by long selling with overall risk off activity.
•
Allocations to Asia continued to decline sharply in the MTD period but continue to remain overweight vs. the MSCI World AC benchmark.
•
The net allocation to Asia in the Global Prime book declined 260 bps MTD, with the latest level at 24.3%, representing an 8.2% overweight compared to MSCI 
World benchmark. This has declined from 20% overweight around mid-June and is back to levels seen in late January.
•
Gross allocation declined 66 bps to 23.4%, reflecting a 7.2% overweight relative to MSCI World.
•
9/11 sectors were net sold so far this month. Info Tech continued to lead the selling flows, followed by Industrials and Consumer Discretionary. Marginal buying was observed in 
Utilities and Cons Staples.
•
Info Tech continues to see net selling flows MTD. Korea and Taiwan dominated the net selling in this space, followed by smaller contributions from Japan and China. While 
allocations continue to decline, the sector remains 10.3% overweight vs. MXAP. While this is down from a ~22% peak in June, the current level has dipped to YTD lows.

vs. MXAP ticked higher to 4%.
