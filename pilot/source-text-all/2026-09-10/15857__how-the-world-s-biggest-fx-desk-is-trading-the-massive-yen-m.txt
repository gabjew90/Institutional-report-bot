How The World's Biggest FX Desk Is Trading The 
Massive Yen Move 
 
BY TYLER DURDEN 
WEDNESDAY, SEP 09, 2026 - 05:45 PM 
The yen ascent continues, sending the USDJPY plunging from a 160.39 high last week to an 
overnight low of 152.89, with spot volumes running at roughly 2x normal. Goldman's FX vol 
desk attributes this to a hawkish BOJ — almost two hikes now priced into year-end — plus talk 
of GPIF domestic reallocation worth as much as ~$100bn, and CTA/barrier stop-outs through 
the 158.50 200-day average. Crucially, the desk says clients see this as structural (“this time 
feels different”), with little appetite to fade. Meanwhile, Goldman's Delta One head Rich 
Privorotsky connects the dots to equities: strangely heavy mega-caps may reflect yen-funded 
carry quietly diffusing out of the system.And then there was the big highlight of the overnight 
session, when Scott Bessent went Judge Dredd on yen bears, warning that "I am the house 
now." 
Below we summarize the key thoughts from Goldman G10 FX vol trader Praneet Shah 
summarizing the recent movement in USDJPY, as well as client flows and what’s happening in 
USDJPY vol space. ​
​
How much has USDJPY moved and what has been driving it? 
Spot move: 
●​ USDJPY has traded from a high of 160.39 last week to an overnight low 152.89

venues

●​ Talk of increased GPIF domestic allocation (would amount to ~$100bio if max

●​ CTA stop losses through key levels (158.50 200d MA) and option barrier related stops

What client flows have we seen...? 
Thursday- Friday: 
●​ Overwhelming demand from HFs to buy 1w-3m downside optionality (152/148 type 
strikes) 
●​ Directional interest to play for a move into 150 
●​ Systematic / RV stop outs in gamma (managing derive from short skew positions) 
●​ ‘this time feels different’ – very little interest to fade and play for retracement unlike July 
intervention episode 
Post NFP 
●​ As spot stalled into 155, HFs used more leveraged expressions via RKOs and EKOs to 
play for a slower move into 148 
●​ This took skew pricing from the highs of 1m RR at 3v to 2.5v 
This week: 
●​ More interest to own options further out the curve 6m-1y outright downside puts and 
digitals given the vol curve inversion 
●​ Clients taking profit on some shorter-dated downside options 
●​ First signs of buying TOPSIDE on the move into 153.00 (protect cash shorts)

What does Goldman think from here and what’s the bank's favourite trade now? 
Levels: 
●​ Into CPI/PPI expect USDJPY to hold into the 152-153 area as clients take profit and 
some CTA positioning cleaned up  
●​ Expect spot to stall into 155.20 and 158.50 to be major resistence  
●​ On a break of 152.00, target 148.00 (‘Takaichi Gap’) 
Why Goldman likes JPY stronger: 
●​ July intervention helped to stabilise the FX 
●​ BOJ/GPIF flows can help turn the tide on local perceptions on JPY – generating more of 
a structural shift 
●​ Higher vol and narrower rate differentials now make it tough to hold JPY funded carry -- 
so think the persistent JPY sell flow may subside  
●​ In context July-Aug 2024 moves USDJPY traded 162 to 142, then stabilised 148 to what 
was a similar positioning setup (retail/CTA stop outs) 
●​ Think short-term 152.50/153.00 could act as a base and 155.00 acts as eventual 
resistence – targetting end-Oct move to 148.00  
●​ If this really is repatriation flow and encourages sustained retail/ life insurance flow – a 
larger medium-term move into 140.00 could be on the cards (NB 1y USDJPY 140 digi 
put marks 17%) 
 Trades: 
●​ BUY 6w USDJPY 148 digi put 13.5% (spot 154.20) 
●​ BUY 6w EURJPY 173 digi put 14%  
●​ BUY 6m6m USDJPY forward vol (or 1y atm outright at 8.8v) – to play for this being a
