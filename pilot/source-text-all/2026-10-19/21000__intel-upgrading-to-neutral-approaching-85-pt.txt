Style Exposure
We are upgrading INTC to Neutral from Underweight as the stock approaches our 
$85 price target where risk/reward is much more balanced as the stock has pulled 
back ~36% from its peak in June (versus the group at ~20-25%), and since our Nov 
2022 downgrade to Underweight, the SMH, SOX, our top pick AVGO, and 
competitor AMD have outperformed INTC by 65%, 37%, 115%, and 148% 
respectively. Since our downgrade in 2022, Intel’s server x86 CPU share (revenue) 
has dropped from 77% to 53%, and PC CPU share has dropped from 81% to 68%, 
and we see a few more quarters of share declines, especially in server CPU.  On the 
foundry front, although INTC appears to be making some strong inroads on yield/
manufacturability/new customer engagements, the team barely approached $1B in 
annualized external foundry business in 1H26 (versus TSMC at $150B in 1H 
annualized revenues).  More importantly, since Lip Bu Tan’s appointment to CEO, 
we believe the team’s execution and competitiveness in their core client/datacenter 
compute market has improved, the team’s execution on next-gen manufacturing 
has significantly improved (yields, manufacturability, foundry process/design rule 
compatibility, advanced packaging) and the team has several initiatives (ASICs) 
which exploit INTC’s strong IP portfolio, SOC design capabilities, and leading 
edge internal manufacturing capabilities. We continue to monitor the team’s 
execution on its datacenter CPU roadmaps, overall AI compute strategy, foundry 
customer engagements, ASIC services design-win pipeline, and potential 
strengthening of its portfolio with datacenter networking capabilities going 
forward.  Despite near-term concerns on AI infrastructure spending sustainability, 
we believe increasing agentic AI workloads (CPU intensive) and a very tight 
supply chain for leading edge silicon should drive continued positive EPS 
revisions in the upcoming earnings season and through CY27 (our CY28 EPS 
estimates are ~15% above consensus).  Risk/reward is more balanced at current 
levels and we move to a Neutral rating.

18A-P in risk production, and 14A now fully committed to 2028 HVM and risk 
production targeted for 2H27 (0.9 PDK  landing in October). We would also point to 
materially better yields, manufacturability, foundry process/design-rule compatibility, 
and advanced packaging (EMIB-T backlog building toward 2027 ramps) as evidence that

services initiative is approaching a ~$2B run-rate (~3x Y/Y) with a path toward $4B, 
leveraging INTC’s strong IP portfolio, SoC design capabilities, and leading-edge internal 
manufacturing - an increasingly important optionality lever as hyperscaler custom silicon 
spend accelerates.
•
Key proof-points we continue to monitor….market will need to continue to be 
patient...  While we are stepping off UW, we remain Neutral pending (1) continued 
execution on the datacenter CPU roadmap and the competitive response to AMD’s Turin/
Venice/Verano all critical to sustaining the DCAI margin trajectory; (2) more concrete 
progress on the overall AI compute strategy, including the Crescent Island GPU (recently 
rebranded as “D-Series”) ramp and incremental AI-ASIC and inference-accelerator 
design-win disclosures; (3) external foundry customer wins; and (4) potential 
strengthening of the portfolio with datacenter networking capabilities (either organic or 
inorganic) given the increasing importance of networking as the fastest-growing layer of 
the AI stack. We would also continue to monitor the capital structure trajectory, with 
mgmt explicit that 2027 FCF is “more challenged” and that further capital markets 
activity may be required, incremental dilution is a key risk.

Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks
Intel (Neutral; Price Target: $85.00)
Investment Thesis
Our Neutral rating reflects our view that risk/reward is relatively balanced. Since Lip Bu 
Tan’s appointment to CEO, we believe the team’s execution and competitiveness in its core 
client/datacenter compute market has improved (DCAI/server emerging as the largest profit 
pool with record Y/Y growth and strong LTA-backed visibility), execution on next-gen 
manufacturing has significantly improved (yields, manufacturability, foundry process/
design rule compatibility, advanced packaging), and the team has several initiatives 
(notably ASIC services) that exploit INTC’s strong IP portfolio, SoC design capabilities, 
and leading-edge internal manufacturing. We continue to monitor the team’s execution on 
its datacenter CPU roadmap, overall AI compute strategy, external foundry customer 
engagements, ASIC services design-win pipeline, and potential strengthening of its 
portfolio with datacenter networking capabilities going forward. 
Valuation
Our Dec-27 PT of $85 is based on a group multiple of 20-25x (24x) P/E, times $3.60 EPS 
in CY28.
Risks to Rating and Price Target
•
Upside Risks:
•
Faster-than-expected execution on the datacenter CPU roadmap and improved 
competitive positioning vs. AMD, which could drive a stronger DCAI revenue/
margin trajectory than currently modeled.
•
Named external foundry anchor customer wins on leading-edge nodes, which would 
materially improve Intel Foundry economics and validate the ongoing capex 
inflection.
•
Accelerated traction in the ASIC services design-win pipeline with named 
hyperscaler/AI-lab customers, supporting a scaled multi-year revenue ramp.
•
Downside Risks:
•
Delays or execution issues on next-generation node ramp, yield/performance, or 
product mix extension, which would pressure the GM trajectory.
•
Deeper or more prolonged memory-driven BOM inflation pressuring PC/consumer 
demand, with Client segment economics eroding faster than modeled.
•
Continued share loss to AMD in both client and server CPU, with the next-
generation competitive dynamic still unresolved.
