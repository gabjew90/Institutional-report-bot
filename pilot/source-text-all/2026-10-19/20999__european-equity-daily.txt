CEO Dieter Weisskopf returning as interim CEO until a permanent successor is found; expected in 2Q27. 
Weisskopf frames the ambition as "significant brand investment while improving profitability", while a 

margin reset is not management’s message today, the market will worry it will be needed to get back to

to avoid. That leaves 2027 as a potential point for a margin reset, particularly with a new permanent CEO 
expected in 2Q27 who will have the opportunity to reassess the investment and profitability framework. 
We expect the share price to react negatively this AM on the uncertainty and set up for strategic change 
into next year.

Primary Health Properties (PHP LN, BUY, PT: 107.00p) First View: 50/50 Private hospital JV with GIC at 
5% discount to book [Sarim Chaudhry] 
 PHP has agreed a 50:50 JV with GIC focused on private hospital infrastructure, seeded with 27 hospitals 
valued at c.£0.7bn. The assets will transfer at a 5% discount to Dec-25 book value, with a 22-year WAULT 
and long-term, index-linked leases. PHP will retain a 50% stake and act as property and asset manager, 
creating recurring fee income and potential performance fees.Completion is expected in Nov-26, 
generating c.£0.5bn of net proceeds, which will repay the Assura acquisition bridge facility and £266m 
term loan. Pro forma net debt is expected to fall to c.£2.9bn, with net debt/EBITDA below 10x and LTV 
below 55%. Government-backed income should return above 80%. The JV provides PHP with a long-term 
institutional partner to fund further growth in private healthcare infrastructure while accelerating 
deleveraging and supporting its BBB+ or higher credit rating target.
