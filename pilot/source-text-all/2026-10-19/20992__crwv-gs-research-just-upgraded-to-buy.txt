CoreWeave Inc. (CRWV): Upgrade to Buy: NTM datapoints on contract economics are likely to be more
positive than the market expects
We upgrade CoreWeave from Neutral to Buy with 73% upside to our price target of $106 (down from
$139 prior). The stock is down 43% since Aug 11 (2Q EPS) and 24% in the last 5 trading days, per our
understanding due to a) ongoing volatility in the credit market, which raises questions on if and at what
rate CoreWeave will be able to finance their next phase of growth; b) debate on demand for intelligence at
the frontier vs. off frontier, and whether changing mix impacts token monetization at CoreWeave’s
customers, which in turn is key for the ecosystem to realize returns on compute investments. Going back
to Finance 101, we see CoreWeave's enterprise value as being directly correlated to the magnitude and
durability of the spread between its unlevered IRR and WACC -- and we think investors may be
underestimating the magnitude of upward revisions to uIRR (due to tight supply/demand), and
overestimating the magnitude of upward revisions to WACC (given CoreWeave's asset-backed financing
tools tied to specific contracts). We recognize the challenges with forecasting out further out in time; and
in particular, predicting when supply/demand will ultimately normalize and the implications for residual
value. However, we believe that datapoints in the next 12 months are likely to be positive for CoreWeave,
and we would use this recent correction as an opportunity to buy the stock.
