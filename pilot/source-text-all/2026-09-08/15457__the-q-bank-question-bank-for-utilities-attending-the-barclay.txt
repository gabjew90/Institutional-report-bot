US Utilities: Question Bank and Core Themes We're Pondering Into the Barclays Energy-
Power Conference: We provide a preview and Q-Bank for the upcoming September 8-10

for all companies attending the conference.
How We're Feeling About the Macro Set-Up? Bottom of the Barrel. Utilities are the third
worst performing GICS sector in the S&P 500 YTD, up 1% vs. the SPX +12%, now trading at a 12%
discount on FY2E P/E vs. the broad market. The affordability narrative, Gubernatorial elections,
increasing NIMBY-ism on data center customers, higher interest rates and the S&P being at all-
time highs have been a headwind. Failure of California Phase 2 legislation has been the latest hit
to sector confidence. The backdrop has clearly been noisy and we see EEI / 3Q as a potential
major relief point for the group, as we move past the election season and companies provide
updates on long-term growth. We see the conference as an opportunity to engage with
companies into 3Q reporting and the annual EEI update where we continue to expect an uplift in
sector RAB (9% avg), and EPS growth (8% avg) fueled by large load customer demand.

pressure on Texas forward power prices to be topical along with strength in PJM that is an offset
for some (CEG); however, others such as NRG could communicate further weakness into 2027.
