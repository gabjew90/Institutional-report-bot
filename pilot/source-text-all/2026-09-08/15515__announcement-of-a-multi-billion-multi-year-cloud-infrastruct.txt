RUM Group Inc.
Buy
Announcement of a Multi-Billion, Multi-Year Cloud
Infrastructure Deal; Potentially Transformative –
Reiterate Buy, $22 PT
Summary
●Yesterday pre-Market open, RUM Group announced a potentially transformative
AI infrastructure agreement with a cloud customer that represents ~$13.7B and
potentially $48.0B+ in revenue over its lifetime.
●The deal includes the issuance of warrants to the customer for up to 50.8M shares,
representing 9% of the company's outstanding shares.
●RUM Group will need to expand its facility in northeastern Georgia to have
sufficient capacity. According to prior press releases by Northern Data, which it
acquired in June 2026, the facility was expected to be operational in 2026 and able
to be expanded in 2027.
●No update to guidance was given. Recall that guidance was provided for the
first time when the company reported 2Q26 results, representing 3Q26 revenue
growth of 251%–276% y/y.
●RUM Group ended 2Q26 with $203.3M in cash and Bitcoin valued at $20.4M.
The company took on $358.8M in convertible debt related to the acquisition of
Northern Data; previously, it was debt-free. Given the capital intensity of the deal,
management indicated that it expects to finance a substantial portion of the
required expenditures through additional debt and/or equity financing.
●At this time, we are not changing estimates as no timeframe was provided for the
deal's start date or the related completion date for its associated facility.
●We reiterate our Buy rating and 12-month price target of $22, which is based on
our 10-year DCF analysis. Shares currently trade at 11.7x EV/revenue, compared
with the peer average of 7.6x. Our price target implies a 25x multiple on our 2027
projection.
Details
A potentially transformative cloud infrastructure deal. Yesterday (8/24) pre-
Market open, RUM Group announced, via an 8-K filing, a cloud infrastructure
agreement with an unaffiliated U.S.-based third party customer governing the
customer’s purchase of access to GPUs and GPU services for a total order value
(which we interpret as revenue) of ~$13.7B over a term of 6 years, with the potential
to exceed $48.0B. The deal includes RUM issuing the warrants to the customer for
up to 50.8M Class A shares. Based on our estimate for $240M in revenue in 2026,
the deal has the potential to be transformative.
A Mega Deal...similar to other ones. Based on our analysis of the deal, order value
could exceed $48.0B over its lifetime, which we estimate could be as much as 11
years. To put that in context, in February 2026, OpenAI agreed to spend $138B with
Amazon's AWS (AMZN - Buy) over an 8-year period, and in April 2026, Anthropic
committed to spend $100B with AWS over a 10-year period.
A Mega Deal...different from other ones. One important difference between those
deals and this one, in our view, is that Amazon agreed to invest up to $50B in OpenAI
and up to $33B in Anthropic, whereas, in this deal, the customer may be acquiring
up to 50.8M shares in RUM representing a current value of ~$475M and a 9% stake
in RUM, based on RUM having 495.8M shares outstanding (across three classes: A,
C, and D), as of 8/6/26.

Additional details on the customer agreement:
●The name of the customer was not provided in the filing; however, the filing
indicated that the customer was based in the U.S.
●The initial term is six years, separated into three tranches, which are spread
evenly.
●The delivery of the third tranche is subject to customer approval, based on a
proposed delivery date.
●The AI infrastructure effort is expected to be provided at the company's facility in
Maysville, GA, which is in the northeastern part of the state.
●The filing did not indicate when RUM Group expects the Maysville, GA, facility to
be ready to accommodate this deal and for the company to begin recognizing
revenue from the deal. Therefore, at this time, we are not changing our estimates
but acknowledge that when the company starts recognizing revenue from the deal
it should be transformative, given the relative scale of the deal versus its other
efforts.
❍Note, Northern Data, which RUM Group acquired on June 17, 2026 had issued
a press release on December 4, 2024 that suggested it aimed to be fully
operational at a capacity of 120 megawatts by 1Q27, with the potential to
expand to 180 megawatts in the future. No details were provided in the filing
on how many megawatts were required to service this deal.
❍On April 30, 2025, Norther Data indicated that it anticipated the Maysville, GA,
location would be operational in 2026 and could expand in 2027.
Additional Details on the warrants:
●RUM Group is expected to issue the warrants to the customer for up to 50.8M
shares, subject to a vesting schedule.
●The first half of the warrants vests in equal amounts of 16.7%, coinciding with the
purchases of the three tranches of services.
●The second half of the warrants vests over a period of five additional tranches,
presumably after the first six years, subject to RUM Group and the customer
entering into additional agreements, at a rate of 10% per tranche.
●In order for all of the shares to vest, the total value of services purchased by the
customer would have to exceed $48.0B.
●The exercise price is $0.01 per share.

highlight the ones we consider most pertinent
Risks associated with the agreement:
●To service the agreement, the company will need to finish expanding the Maysville,
GA facility, which will require it to purchase a significant number of GPUs.
●RUM Group may need additional capital, from debt, equity financing, or both, to
satisfy this agreement.
No new guidance was provided in the filing. Recall that management had
provided guidance for 3Q26 revenue when it reported 2Q26 results on August 10:
●$87M–$93M, up 251%–276% y/y.

related to the acquisition of Northern Data; previously, it was debt-free. Given the
capital intensity of the deal, management indicated that it expects to finance a

Reiterate Buy rating and 12-month price target of $22. Our price target is based
on our 10-year DCF model, which includes a 10% discount rate and 40% adjusted
EBITDA margin. Shares currently trade at 11.7x EV/revenue on our 2027E versus
the peer average of 7.6x. Our price target represents 25x our 2027 forecast. We

Tether and, most recently, the announcement of a potentially transformative cloud
infrastructure deal.
Company description: RUM Group Inc. is an AI infrastructure and video company with
two operating segments: Quake AI and Rumble. Quake AI delivers AI compute as a service,
including GPU, CPU, storage, and networking at scale. Rumble is a video-sharing and cloud
services provider that helps content creators manage, distribute, and monetize their work.
Its mission is to maximize the power of human imagination.

RISK RATINGS
Risk ratings take into account both fundamental criteria and price volatility.
Speculative – Fundamental Criteria: This is a risk rating assigned to early-stage companies with minimal to no revenues, lack of
earnings, balance sheet concerns, and/or a short operating history. Accordingly, fundamental risk is expected to be significantly
above the industry. Price Volatility: Because of the inherent fundamental criteria of the companies falling within this risk category,
the price volatility is expected to be significant with the possibility that the investment could eventually be worthless. Speculative
stocks may not be suitable for a significant class of individual investors.
High – Fundamental Criteria: This is a risk rating assigned to companies having below-average revenue and earnings visibility,
negative cash flow, and low market cap or public float. Accordingly, fundamental risk is expected to be above the industry. Price
Volatility: The price volatility of companies falling within this category is expected to be above the industry. High-risk stocks may
not be suitable for a significant class of individual investors.
Medium – Fundamental Criteria: This is a risk rating assigned to companies that may have average revenue and earnings visibility,
positive cash flow, and is fairly liquid. Accordingly, both price volatility and fundamental risk are expected to approximate the
industry average.
Low – Fundamental Criteria: This is a risk rating assigned to companies that may have above-average revenue and earnings visibility,
positive cash flow, and is fairly liquid. Accordingly, both price volatility and fundamental risk are expected to be below the industry.
