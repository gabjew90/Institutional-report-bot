Rumble, Inc.
Northern Data Acquisition Closed; Company Focused
on Data Center Execution
SUMMARY
Maintaining Perform following Northern Data acquisition close, securing ~85% of
shares. 2Q revenue +61% y/y vs. 1Q +7%, including $10.1M of data center revenue.
Initiated formal guidance with 3Q revenue $90M or +263% y/y at midpoint vs. 2Q
+61%, not comparable given 3Q represents first full quarter with Northern Data. We
believe investors are now focused on the Data Center business: ~22K H100/H200
GPUs running at >85% utilization, a multiyear Together AI agreement, and 250MW of
unmonetized capacity targeted for 2027, including the 180MW Atlanta site. We want
to see further detail on MW adds, capex/D&A, and the related timeline before getting
more constructive. Currently trading 2x 2028 advertising revenue and 2x 2028 data
center revenue vs. 3x data center peers.
KEY POINTS
■2Q total revenue +61% y/y vs. 1Q +7%, 32% above Opco. MAU +12% y/y or +2%
q/q vs. 1Q +8%, 3% below Opco. ARPU (using Audience Monetization revenue ex
Tether ad commits) +6% q/q vs. 1Q -20%, 3% below Opco. Audience Monetization
revenue +20% q/q vs. 1Q -5%. Other Initiatives ex-DC revenues +9% q/q vs. 1Q
-11%. $10.1M of Northern Data revenue in 2Q.
■2Q GAAP gross profit of $9.8M was $12.1M above. Gross margin 24.2% vs.
our -7.7% estimate vs. 1Q -6.0%. EBITDA loss of ($16.6M) was $10.1M better. Ad
GAAP opex +17% q/q vs. 1Q +11%, 18% above Opco. D&A $12.3M above, and
SBC $2.1M above Opco.
■Initiated 3Q revenue guidance +263% y/y or +123% q/q at midpoint, 1% above
Opco. Includes the first full quarter of Northern Data revenue.
■Business update. Closed Northern Data acquisition on June 17, securing ~85%
of shares and establishing Rumble and Quake AI segments. Quake AI signed a
multiyear Together AI agreement for NVIDIA HGX B300 GPU cloud capacity and
reported ~85% utilization; 250MW of unmonetized 2027 capacity represents a $3B
+ ARR opportunity. Ended 2Q with $203M cash.
■Estimate revisions. Decreasing FY26/FY27 Advertising revenue 13%/15% with
Advertising gross profit $7M/$10M lower and Advertising EBIT $79M/$26M lower.
Increasing FY26/27 data center revenue 26%/118% with data center gross profit
16%/118% higher and EBIT $86M/$11M lower. Total revenue 3%/68% higher, while
EBIT is $166M/$36M lower. FY28 total revenue/EBIT 105%/$69M higher.

INVESTMENT THESIS
We believe Rumble Inc. (NASDAQ:RUM) will continue to take
share of the digital advertising market through its website,
Rumble.com. While believing the company is well-positioned for
the future, we believe the current valuation adequately reflects
the company's potential upside. We encourage investors to wait
for a better entry point for the name, which came public via the
SPAC process in September 2022.
