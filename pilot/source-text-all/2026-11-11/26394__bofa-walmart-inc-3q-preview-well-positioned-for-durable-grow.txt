Reiterate Buy; expect share gains to continue   
We reiterate our Buy rating; we think WMT is poised to deliver continued share gains 
across both grocery and gen merch (see CEO meeting takeaways). We forecast 3Q EPS 
of $0.63 and expect WMT to update its FY sales and EPS guidance to the upper end of 
its prior range. Our 3Q forecast includes +2.8% WMT US comps ex-fuel. Following the 
recent sector rotation, we think the market needs to see an acceleration in WMT US 
comps ex H&W and gain confidence that the marketplace and ad businesses can help 
drive an acceleration in EPS growth next year. We raise our PO to $139 (from $126), 
now based on 44x P/E (from 40x) due to rerating in peer multiples. 
Inflation ticking higher 
The move higher in oil prices is driving upward pressure on commodity costs and 
vendors are starting to increase prices. We think inflation expectations have increased 
50-100bp in grocery and note price increases will likely be recognized over the next 6-12 
months. The potential risk from higher inflation is greater elasticity and a pullback in 
demand in gen merch but we think this risk is low given Walmart's focus on value. 
Remaining on the forefront of agentic 
We argue Walmart is the biggest winner in our coverage in a world with greater agentic 
commerce adoption. This quarter, Walmart expanded Sparky’s capabilities to process 
pictures (can turn handwritten lists into a shopping cart) and developed a "shop to light" 
location tool that helps shoppers locate products in stores. Walmart announced the 
launch of Agent Profiles, which allows consumers to authorize AI agents to shop using 
their Walmart account with spending controls and limits. Bottom line, Walmart's product 
assortment, low prices, and fast delivery capabilities position them well to succeed. 
Advertising strength key pillar of bull case 
We expect WMT to maintain the recent momentum in its advertising business (note this 
quarter will include VIbe.co acquisition). Continued marketplace growth (+52% in 2Q) is a 
key to help maintain success of the ad business; we estimate marketplace sellers spend 
10-12% of GMV on advertising compared to Walmart suppliers that spend roughly 4-6% 
of GMV. While we acknowledge there are risks to how the advertising model changes 
with greater agentic adoption, we think Walmart is well suited given its unique customer 
data set and growing non-endemic advertising businesses (see WMT Connect note).

Price objective basis & risk 
Walmart Inc (WMT) 
Our $139 price objective is based on 44x our F28E adj. EPS, which is above WMT's 
average 1-year forward P/E multiple of 36x over the last 2 years. Our premium multiple 
reflects an outlook for positive US comps (with positive traffic) and WMT's leading edge 
on deploying technology to drive consistent share gains and operating income growth 
nearly double the pace of sales growth. 
 
Downside risks to our PO are slowing food inflation or deflation, Walmart's longer-term 
ability to continue gaining incremental market share given its large size, a weakening 
global retailing environment, competitive pressures at Sam's Club and/or Walmart 
International.
