reflected in estimates, with FH&B >10% margin already in FY27 cons/UBSe and Food’s ~5% margin
below current expectations; key incremental positive is profit growth ahead of sales alongside further

Targets mid-to-high-single-digit medium-term sales growth with profit growing ahead of sales. Food aims to
double sales over time and deliver ~5% operating margin (FY27E: VA cons 5.6%; UBSe 5.4%), while FH&B
targets >10% margins in both channels (FY27E: VA cons 10.2%; UBSe 10.5%), alongside online participation
moving towards 50% and 180 highly productive stores. FY27 sales growth is Food +9.3% (VA cons; UBSe
+9.7%) and FH&B +9.5% (VA cons; UBSe +7.8%).
The Group remains on track for £600m structural cost savings by FY28, expects positive FCF in each of the
next three years and will double the FY26/27 dividend to 8.4p from 4.2p. Overall, the >10% FH&B margin
target broadly supports current estimates (VA cons 10.2%; UBSe 10.5%), while the ~5% Food margin
ambition is below current expectations (VA cons 5.6%; UBSe 5.4%), suggesting the Food opportunity is more
dependent on sustained sales/share growth than further margin expansion.

• ‘BellRing (UBS Research Rec Neutral) - Scouting Report: What To Expect From F4Q26 Report BRBR is
scheduled to report F4Q earnings next Wednesday (11/18) before the open. UBS think the setup for the print
remains difficult, with our conversations suggesting sentiment is mixed despite meaningful YTD
underperformance in the stock. On one hand, (1) valuation remains very attractive relative to the company’s
LT growth profile, (2) BRBR continues to deliver top-line growth ahead of most Staples companies, and (3)
stronger-than-expected tracked trends should support a decent F4Q print. On the other hand, investors remain
focused on FY27 given the number of moving pieces, including +DD pricing, elevated commodity/logistics
costs and unfavorable competitive dynamics, making it difficult to have conviction in the NT top/bottom-line
trajectory. In addition, UBS think management could embed some flexibility into its initial outlook given the
recent CEO transition. As such, while UBS agree that valuation screens as attractive relative to the LT growth
profile, UBS think it remains difficult to step in ahead of F4Q results and would wait for greater clarity on the
FY27 earnings trajectory before becoming more constructive.
• ‘Somnigroup (UBS Research Rec Buy) – Making the Bed for 2027 Report SGI reported 3Q adjusted EPS
of $1.04, versus our $1.06 and consensus of $1.05, and reduced FY26 EPS guidance to $2.85–2.95 from
$2.95–3.25. The more consequential development, in our view, was management’s first framework for 2027:
approximately $3.40 of EPS in a flat industry. The bridge starts with the $2.90 midpoint of FY26 guidance,
adds approximately $0.25 from a full year of LEG, and another approximately $0.25 from synergies, including
$50m of remaining Mattress Firm cost synergies and approximately $25m from LEG. From there, every point
of industry growth contributes approximately $0.10 of EPS, beginning with the first dollar of growth. By
contrast, pre-results consensus FY27 EPS of $3.93 appeared to require mid-single-digit industry growth—an
assumption UBS believe is too aggressive for 2027. UBS move our FY27 EPS estimate to $3.50, modestly
above management’s floor.
