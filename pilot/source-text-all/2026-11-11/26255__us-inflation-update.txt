Preview | Risks tilted to a stronger October core CPI
MUFG US Macro Strategy | Inflation Update

Data Preview & Forecast
▪MUFG forecasts October 2026 headline CPI at 0.29% MoM and core 
CPI at 0.23% MoM, in line with the median estimate from 
Bloomberg contributors.
▪Risks tilted to a stronger core CPI: Since 2022, monthly core inflation 
readings of less than 0.15% have been uncommon. A strong reversal 
in OER after last month’s unusual strength can help produce a softer 
inflation print in October, but more items would need significant 
disinflation for core CPI to come in below 0.2% MoM. Instead, 
elevated shipping costs and reinstituted tariffs may accelerate the 
MoM contribution from core goods, offsetting some of the 
improvement in OER. Meanwhile, airline fares, lodging and 
recreational services prices are expected to apply upward pressure 
on October’s core CPI, tilting the risks toward a stronger print.
Market Thoughts
▪Base-case: To partially stop last week’s “flight to safety” UST rally, a 
very strong October NFP was needed. We think the bar is high to 
trigger a rates selloff as markets are now focused on the benefits of 
lower oil prices and they might be less sensitive to the October CPI 
(viewing it as old news). With fed funds futures still showing a hike in 
December, we doubt markets move much on a 0.2% MoM reading.
▪Stronger risk: Last month’s stronger than expected core CPI wasn’t 
enough to trigger a lasting sell-off, and this month may be the same. 
Fragility in the equity market will continue to dominate the narrative.
▪Weaker risk: A 0.1% MoM core reading would ease December hiking 
odds closer to our 55-60% expectations of a hike.
