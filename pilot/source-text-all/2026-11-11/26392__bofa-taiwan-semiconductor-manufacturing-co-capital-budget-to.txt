Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. 
Capital budget toward advanced nodes; 
investing into frontier labs and semis chain 
Maintain Rating: BUY | PO: 3,260 TWD | Price: 2,285 TWD   
  
 
 
Solid budget for advanced front-end ramp acceleration 
BoD approved capital appropriations of US$41bn, putting the budget +116% YoY in ‘26. 
Notably, 56% of its budget in ’26 (Exhibit 1) is related to advanced front-end process 
expansion and upgrade, the highest level since ’22 and suggesting it is undergoing a 
period of steep capacity ramp on 3nm and 2nm. It is also important to note TSMC’s 
accumulated capital appropriation YoY is +50% now, above the long term trend at +40-
45% YoY and potentially lifting ’27 capex to US$80-90bn in ’27 (vs. BofAe/street 
US$83bn/~US$80bn) following a step up in ’26 (BofAe US$62bn). While it is growing 
capacity to fulfill demand, our model shows its advanced node utilization is still above 
100% in ’27 and could stay healthy in ’28 even if AI demand moderates/declines. 
Raising dividend to NT$9/quarter; inv’t in frontier AI labs 
TSMC’s BoD raised its quarterly dividend from NT$7/share to NT$9/share effective for 
its earnings in 3Q26, putting the payout ratio at ~30%. With its steadily growing payout 
policy, ~US$29bn annual cash outflow is required for the distribution which should be 
backed by its FCF set to grow from US$53bn in ’26 to US$76bn/US$109bn in ‘27/28 
based on our estimates. Separately, its board approved to invest up to US$1bn in 
frontier AI labs while it sets up a wholly owned VC for the strategic investments in the 
semiconductor chain. It is noteworthy that the IPO investment made by TSMC before 
was on ARM in ’23 for US$100mn which was strategic for cooperation with its suppliers 
and partners. 
Stock compelling backed by benign competitive landscape 
We keep Buy rating with PO at NT$3,260 on 20x '27 P/E. The valuation is undemanding 
at 15x/13x '27/28E P/E (vs. its 11-21x range) on 34% EPS CAGR and could be defensive 
as noted in our scenario analysis on AI compute addition with its technology leadership.

Company Description 
TSMC was founded in 1987 and is listed in Taiwan 
(2330TT) and the US (TSM/NYS).TSMC is the largest and 
global leader in integrated circuit (IC) manufacturing. As a 
build-to-order foundry, it provides a wide range of value-
add activities: IC manufacturing, mask-making, IC design 
services, turnkey solutions, and process development. We 
attribute its success to its proven, winning business model, 
unparalleled scale advantage, optimized execution, and 
technology scope and depth. 5 shares = 1 ADR. 
Investment Rationale 
The ongoing semi content growth in mobile, rise of artificial 
intelligence (AI), and proliferation of Internet of Things (IoT) 
should result in sustainable upside in aggregate computing 
power globally. TSMC, as the leading contract manufacturer 
of semiconductor chips, is in a good position to capitalize. 
We are structurally positive on TSMC and expect it to 
sustain over 15% growth with rising FCF from 2026 
onwards.

Price objective basis & risk 
Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMWF / TSM) 
Our PO for TSMC is NT$3,260 per share (US$620 per ADR), based on 20x 2027E P/E. 
TSMC's valuation has traded in the range of 10-26x PE from 2017. 20x is in the mid to 
high end of the historical range, above the average P/E of 16x. We think a PE above the 
average is justified given TSMC's enhanced industry position and mid-to-high 50% 5-
year CAGR in AI should underpin a rerating 
 
Downside risks to our price objective are: (1) greater-than-expected slowdown in global 
smartphone/consumer electronics demand, (2) Intel's potential insourcing strategy and 
ambitions in foundry service, (3) execution risks on advanced nodes, and (4) higher than 
expected tariffs.
