Sector Roundup
Resilient Travel Demand Despite Geopolitical Uncertainty 
Marriott: “Excluding the World Cup, third quarter RevPAR in the US and Canada rose 6.2%. Growth continued to be 
broad based across customer segments and chain scales. Luxury and resort hotels again led the region in the 
quarter, with luxury RevPAR increasing 12%, while select service RevPAR had its third quarter of solid year-over-
year growth, rising over 6% compared to the prior-year quarter … with strong base demand across chain scales 
generally expected to continue, we are again raising our full-year 2026 guidance. We now expect 4.5% to 5% global 
RevPAR growth. This is well above the 1.5% to 2.5% growth range we initially forecast on our year-end 2025 
earnings call, as travel demand has been remarkably robust this year, despite geopolitical uncertainty.”
Host Hotels: “RevPAR growth in the third quarter meaningfully exceeded our expectations, with broad-based 
strength across markets and business mix. In fact, 23 of our 24 domestic markets delivered increases in 
comparable hotel RevPAR in the quarter. Growth was driven by leisure transient, bolstered by special events, 
group demand pickup, and improving business transient demand.”
Agentic AI Continues to Accelerate
AMD: “Enterprises are moving fast as well, putting agents to work across software development, supply chain and 
IT operations. These agents work continuously and in parallel across applications and enterprise systems, adding 
to accelerator demand, while creating a substantial increase in CPU demand. Adoption is just starting, and we 
expect demand to grow sharply as agents are deployed more broadly and take on more complex work.”
Airbnb: “AI changed the speed of everything. We'd spent years laying the groundwork, but now AI is helping us 
build, test, and improve products faster than we've ever done before. In Q3 alone, we shipped more than 50 
products and features, nearly 70% more than a year ago, because of AI. So when you put it all together, that's 
what's driving our growth. We spent years changing how Airbnb operates, and now we're seeing the results.”
