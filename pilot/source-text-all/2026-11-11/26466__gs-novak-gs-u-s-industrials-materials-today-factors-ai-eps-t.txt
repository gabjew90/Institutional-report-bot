weeks to discuss.

AI / data center we’ll get a few smaller cap names tomorrow in the space: 

 
FPS which pre-announced positively in late October after announcing a CFO change
suggesting Q1 revenue and EBITDA at the higher end or exceeding guidance. Will
watch for updates on fiscal Q2 and full year. Company reports pre-market tomorrow with
a call at 11am. Volatile name all around peaked at $65 in June and has potentially
bottomed in early November at $18. Rate of change vs. accelerating earnings is the
balance here with a 3x+ book to bill difficult to sustain but drives near term estimates
higher. We’ll also look for more direct color on the CFO transition 10 months into being a
public company. 
 
LGN similar level of volatility will report tomorrow. Stock hit $100 in May reaching a
bottom of $25 only a few days ago. This went public in September of 2025. Company
has beat topline by 10%+ each of the four quarters it has reported so far with EBITDA
beats 20%+. M&A has been additive here adding to topline growth at reasonable
multiples. Will look for updates there as well as to 2027 and beyond given the backlog. 
 
DPC lastly will report tomorrow pre-market after going public over the summer – second
report for them – moving up 4% first time and a 13% implied move tomorrow. Less direct
AI play but moves with the theme. Stock peaked above $55 in July and hopefully has
bottomed around $25 in late October. Watching comments on Aerospace and IGT the
latter of which will be in focus for the AI trade. Margins also an important KPI after
declines despite 30%+ revenue growth in Q2.
