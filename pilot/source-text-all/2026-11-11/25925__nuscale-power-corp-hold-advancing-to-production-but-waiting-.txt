◆ Mixed 3Q26; cash burn narrows on higher interest income 
◆ Strong institutional support but commercial conversion 
remains key; our base case is for an order in 2027 
◆ Maintain Hold rating; lower TP to USD5.00 (from USD11.00)  
3Q26 results: NuScale reported 3Q26 revenue of USD0.3m, up from USD0.1m  
in 2Q26 but missed our USD0.5m estimate and the consensus estimate of USD4.4m. 
As cost of sales and operating expenses increased by USD1.0m q-o-q, the EBITDA 
loss widened to USD64.5m from USD63.7m and missed our estimate of a USD62.2m 
loss and the consensus estimate of a USD59.1m loss. However, higher interest 
income narrowed the EPS loss to USD0.11 from USD0.13 in 2Q26, beating the 
consensus estimate of a USD0.12 loss. Working capital inflow helped to limit the 
cash burn to USD28.7m, as NuScale ended the quarter with over USD1.9bn in cash 
& other liquid assets. 
Strong institutional support: Management recently met with strategic partners in 
Korea and Japan, as it continues to advance its commercial readiness by 
progressing from development to the production phase. The company signed an 
agreement with Doosan Enerbility (034020 KP, covered by Yushin Park, Buy, TP 
KRW160,000, CMP KRW49,300) to begin fabrication of the most complex 
component (the upper reactor coolant system) of its NuScale Power Module (NPM), 
including production of long-lead materials for 12 modules. In addition, nuclear 
deployment enjoys broad bipartisan support in the US, with the Department of 
Energy’s Office of Energy Dominance Financing prioritizing nuclear in October with 
nearly USD10bn in loan commitments. 
Commercial updates: While NuScale did not book any power plant or NPM-related 
services revenue for the second consecutive quarter, the company signed a contract 
to open a new Energy Exploration Center. NuScale expects its 13th global training 
center to open shortly, signalling continued global demand for nuclear energy. 
Management noted its commercial opportunities extend beyond RoPower and TVA, 
as it “continues to engage utilities, hyperscalers and major industrial customers 
seeking reliable carbon-free baseload power.” 
Lower TP to USD5.00 (from USD11.00) and maintain Hold: Our DCF-derived 
target price reflects our revised estimates, a terminal EV/Sales multiple of 2.4x (down 
from 4.3x based on a peer group average per Bloomberg), and a higher market risk 
premium of 8% (from 6% to reflect the risk of project delays). NuScale’s net cash 
balance drives the majority of our enterprise value estimate. We rate NuScale shares 
Hold as the company will need to draw on its cash balance to advance the ENTRA1 
project. We expect revenue to increase in 2027 as the company advances a 
commercial order for delivery in 2030, with our base case assuming delivery of 20 
modules in 2035 and 40 in 2040.

Recent trends and achievements 
◆ Deploying nuclear-specific AI tools to accelerate advanced reactor development: 
NuScale started deploying nuclear-specific AI tools from NPX and Nuclearn to support its 
advanced reactor program. The initiative combined NuScale’s advanced reactor expertise 
with Nuclearn’s AtomAssist nuclear AI platform and NPX’s nuclear project expertise. An 
initial proof of concept demonstrated a reduction of up to 80% in information-retrieval time, 
helping streamline access to relevant information for reactor development activities. 
◆ Advancing manufacturing technology for first-of-a-kind reactor safety component: 
NuScale and MillenniTEK advanced manufacturing technology by fabricating first-of-a-kind 
boron oxide pellets for the company’s passive emergency core cooling system (ECCS). The 
pellets are designed to dissolve into the reactor coolant following ECCS actuation, which 
help control and maintain core reactivity at safe levels without operator intervention.

Methodology: DCF methodology (FCF) 
Assumptions: Our WACC estimate for NuScale is 
29.9% (up from 25.2% and above Bloomberg’s 
24.1% estimate) and incorporates HSBC’s global 
risk-free rate of 4.25% (unchanged) and US equity 
risk premium of 4% plus 4% for a high beta growth 
company (up from 6% for risk of project delays), as 
well as a beta of 3.2 for NuScale (down from 3.5, per 
Bloomberg). Given the company has yet to generate 
positive EBITDA or EPS, we choose a terminal 
EV/Sales multiple of 2.4x (down from 4.3x) based on 
the average multiple for a select group of companies 
per Bloomberg. A higher terminal EV/Sales multiple 
or a change to an EV/EBITDA or PE multiple may be 
warranted as revenue scales and if the company’s 
modules achieve material cost savings. 
Our DCF estimate implies a fair value target price of 
USD5.00 per share for NuScale, equivalent to 0.5x 
our 2027e revenue estimate (was 14.1x). The shares 
are trading at 6.7x currently, an c83% discount to the 
historical average of 39.6x. Our TP implies upside of 
14.4% and we maintain a Hold rating as the company 
will need to utilize its cash balance to advance the 
ENTRA1 project. NuScale’s USD1.9bn in cash drives 
more than 90% of our fair value estimate. 
Downside risks 
◆ High upfront cost for FOAK technology. FOAK capital cost 
remains the key bottleneck to SMR deployment. The UAMPS 
Carbon Free Power Project was terminated in late 2023 as costs 
soared from USD5.3bn to USD9.3bn due to higher interest rates 
and post-pandemic inflation. SMRs are unlikely to be commercially 
viable before 2035. 
◆ High reliance on ENTRA1 to advance new projects globally. 
ENTRA1 holds exclusive rights for worldwide commercialisation, 
distribution, sales and development of the company’s products, 
services and power plants. As a relatively new and small company, 
ENTRA1 may advance projects more slowly than larger, 
established players. 
◆ High cash need as ENTRA1 advances SMR projects. Under its 
PMA, NuScale must fund milestone contributions of 15% (MOU), 
35% (PPA) and 50% for OEM, with only milestone 3 guaranteeing 
revenue.  
◆ Subject to ongoing litigation. NuScale faces a class action 
lawsuit alleging false and misleading statements relating to 
ENTRA1, seeking unspecified damages and attorney fees. The 
company was also accused of fraud by Iceberg Research in 2023 
and may face additional lawsuits. 
Upside risks 
◆ Strong global demand and policy support. The Clean Air Task 
Force estimates that USD9trn of nuclear investment is required over 
25 years to triple nuclear capacity, equivalent to approximately 
USD250bn per year. The VOYGR-6 RoPower plant is estimated at 
USD4.9bn, requiring support from both the Romanian and US 
governments. 
◆ High operating leverage at existing nuclear sites. NuScale 
plants can utilise existing labour and resources at nuclear sites for 
significant operating leverage. Each NPM runs for c20 months 
before refuelling, taking approximately 10 days vs 21 days for large 
reactors. NuScale operates at c95% capacity vs the industry best-
in-class of c90%. 
◆ Operationally focus on commercial deployment. NuScale’s 
focus has shifted from R&D to large-scale commercial deployment. 
The collaboration between ENTRA1 and Tennessee Valley 
Authority entails up to 6 GW (72 NPMs) of new SMR deployment in 
the US, establishing an operational benchmark for domestic 
execution.
