Chart of the Day: Got Money?
Nonfarm payrolls rose by a stronger than expected 165k in October, more than 2x the consensus forecast, while August and September 
were revised higher by a combined 84k. Job gains were broad-based, led by professional services and healthcare. Unemployment fell to 
4.1% despite higher labor force participation, while the three-month average of job gains rose from 79k to 137k. With the labor market 
on solid footing, the Fed can remain focused on inflation, reinforcing our expectation for a 25 bp hike in December.
