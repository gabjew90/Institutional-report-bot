US Daily: Adding a Third Hike in March
• The October CPI report this morning surprised meaningfully to the upside across several categories and
marked a third month of worse inflation news. We continue to expect a second 25bp rate hike in December
and are adding a third 25bp hike to our forecast in March 2027.
• A third hike could come earlier in January, but we think March is a bit more likely because we expect the
November and December inflation reports to be softer but then expect larger start-of-the-year price
increases amidst ongoing cost pressures to boost inflation in January.
Siemens Energy (ENR1n.DE): Returns of value rising out to FY 2030 as new targets are set
• If we include dividends, we see today's announced buyback implying €27bn of returns of value (buyback
and dividends) out to FY 2030. This compares to Visible Alpha at €24bn and GSe at €30bn. The question
we see naturally following is how conservative is the capital allocation.
• We see tomorrow's Capital Markets Days painting a stronger profit outlook and a material acceleration in
cash flow. At the moment capital allocation out to FY 2028 is for 1/3 of cash flow for capital expenditure,
1/3 for returns of value and 1/3 to be retained. We see this allocation changing as the company adds
guidance to FY 2030. All in all, we see the announcement as a positive, showing confidence in the
business outlook. We remain Buy rated (on CL).
Zalando SE (ZALG.DE): Zalando CFO Meeting — Key Takeaways
• Management discussed: 1) a good start to 4Q with autumn/winter trading on track; 2) the B2C strategy
across its three distinct apps has limited overlap (10%), and remain confident in reaching 3-4% retail
media target by 2028; 3) AI and Agentic Commerce are growth catalysts and an efficiency driver; 4)
continued momentum in B2B, with Scayle pipeline full; 5) management reiterated the 6-8% 2028 adj.
EBIT margin target, with four building blocks: a flat gross margin, overhead efficiencies, logistics network
reshaping and payment/commercial buying synergies (merging AY and Zalando).
ON Holding (ONON) - 3Q26 results highlight continued outperformance in a promotional market; TP to
$41, remain Buy

• On's 3Q26 results underscored continued progress for the company despite an ongoing promotional
sportswear market with cFX sales +17.8% yoy, underlying gross margin +320bps yoy and adj.EBITDA
margins expanding to 21.9% excl. tariff refunds. Importantly, the company noted that they saw a strong
improvement in US wholesale sell-out versus 2Q and that partners' inventory levels are healthy with
exceptional early signals from the launch of the Cloud Surfer 3 with run specialty partners.
• Additionally, On is beginning to gain more traction with the younger consumer as demonstrated by the
fact that the share of online customers under 24 almost doubled yoy. Looking ahead, we continue to see
multiple levers of growth for On as we head into 2027 as the company refreshes its entire running
franchise with new superfoams, begins to enter Football and Golf, continues to gain market share in
Sneaker and scales its apparel offering from a low base. We remain Buy-rated with our 12m TP moving to
$41 (from $44).
ConvaTec Plc (CTEC.L) - Reflecting on growth, margin and valuation; Reiterate Buy
• Over the next 12 months, if Convatec can execute on its strategy to drive accelerating growth and
navigate cost inflation, we see upside potential to its multiple alongside ~mid-teens EPS growth. Our
estimates are broadly unchanged; PT unchanged at 300p.
Watches of Switzerland Group (WOSG.L) - Post 1H27 Trading update: Continued strength in winning
categories, PT 930p (was 920p), remain Buy
• Watches of Switzerland reported a strong 1H27 trading update, with Group revenue of £974mn, up 15%
cFX, ahead of GSe/Visible Alpha Consensus of £946mn/£941mn. Growth was broad-based across both
markets, with US revenue up 22% cFX to £501mn and UK revenue up 9% to £473mn. Management
expects 1H27 Pre IFRS Adjusted EBIT of £87–89mn, representing growth of around 28% and margin
expansion of 80–100bps.
• Following this performance, the company raised the lower end of its FY27 guidance, with cFX revenue
growth now expected at 7–10%, versus 5–10% previously, and pre-IFRS 16 adjusted EBIT margin
expansion of 60–80bps, versus 40–80bps previously.
