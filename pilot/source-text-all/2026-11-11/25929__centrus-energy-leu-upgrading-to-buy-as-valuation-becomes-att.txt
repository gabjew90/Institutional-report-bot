LEU: Upgrading to Buy as Valuation Becomes
Attractive and Near-term Concerns Subside
We are upgrading LEU from Neutral to Buy. We note we previously had concerns
about a significant decline in revenue looming and a high market valuation creating
significant downside risk. However, with the company's shares declining significantly
in value and the acquisition of Westerman offsetting the looming revenue decline,
we believe it is time to become more constructive on the company. We are also
maintaining our $115 price target and point to LEU's December Investor Day as a
potential positive catalyst.
Upgrading to Buy. In H1 2025, Centrus's shares began to see significant upward
momentum as several factors combined to drive strong investor interest. Centrus's
market valuation peaked at over $450 per share in October 2025 as peak momentum
appeared to ignore near-term fundamentals, in our view. Since then, LEU's shares
have fallen over 80% as investor sentiment has shifted dramatically. However, we
note that during this period, LEU was able to arrange significant government funding,
raise significant public funding, and achieve major milestones. This left us with
one main concern, a potential revenue decline in 2028 due to the ban on Russian
uranium imports, which officially takes effect January 1, 2028, barring a change from
the U.S. government. However, we note that through a new non-Russian contract as
well as the acquisition of Westerman Holdings, LEU has mitigated this near-term risk
significantly, in our opinion. Thus, we believe it is an opportune moment to upgrade
from Neutral to Buy, while maintaining our $115 price target.
Q3 results weak as expected. As we noted in our Q3 earnings preview note, we
anticipated a weak Q3 due to the 2-year cyclicality of LEU's business. However,
revenue of $55.5 million and adjusted EPS of a loss of $0.46 ($1.41 profit
unadjusted) were slightly better than our estimates of $50.0 million and an EPS loss
of $0.53. We note adjusted earnings is a cleaner comparison to our estimates. We
also note that we continue to believe quarterly results are less indicative of LEU’s
performance than annual results are and thus, should not drive significant investor
reactions.
Investor Day should provide clarity on Westerman potential. In this update, we
have added the Westerman acquisition to our model. We note that there is limited
historical information available for Westerman as it was a private company and
that management has not provided much additional detail. However, we elected to
assume the company generates $160 million per year in revenue based on LEU's
prior filling about the potential acquisition, and we elected to assign a gross margin
of 25%. In doing so, the acquisition had essentially no impact on our valuation even
though we believe it is likely to be positive in the long run. We also note that LEU's
management plans to provide additional information at its December Investor Day.
We believe this event could serve as a significant positive catalyst depending on the
level of detail and expectations for Westerman.

Valuation: Centrus Energy Corp. (LEU)
Our valuation of LEU is based on a 9x multiple on our 2034 EBITDA estimate ($289.9 million) and results in a total EV of $2.6 billion to
which we add current estimated net cash of $941.3 million. This results in a total value of $3.55 billion or $114.90 per future estimated
fully diluted share (30.9 million). We round this up to reach our $115 price target, and we are upgrading to Buy.
Factors that could cause Centrus's shares to fail to reach our price target include, but are not limited to: regulatory changes, interruptions
to suppliers, customer bankruptcies, capital overruns, signing new contracts that differ from our expectations, and other unforeseeable
market events.
Risks: Centrus Energy Corp. (LEU)
•
Political risk. Nuclear companies have politically based risks as changes in regulatory requirements can impact their ability to sell
their products. Additionally, nuclear companies must adhere to existing regulations. Failure to do so could negatively impact revenue
and earnings. LEU also has risk related to international agreements such as the Russian Suspension Agreement. A significant
portion of the company's brokerage business is reliant on the Russian entity Tenex. If Tenex is unable to obtain an export license
to deliver to Centrus this could materially impact the company's earnings.
•
End market risks. All nuclear energy input suppliers have risks related to the nuclear energy utilities. In the event nuclear utility
customers discontinue operating nuclear power plants, this could negatively impact nuclear fuel suppliers.
•
Operational and technical risk. Currently, LEU does not produce its own SWU on a commercial scale, but has operational and
technical risks related to its uranium and SWU suppliers.
•
Market risk. Nuclear fuel cycle companies have less market risk as compared to other industries due to the stability of the energy
utility market. However, these companies can be impacted by abrupt changes in the broader market.
Company Description: Centrus Energy Corp. (LEU)
Centrus Energy Corp. engages in the supply of nuclear fuel and services for the nuclear power industry. It operates through the Low-
Enriched Uranium (LEU) and Technical Solutions segments. The LEU segment includes various components of nuclear fuel to utilities
from its global network of suppliers. The Technical Solutions segment offers advanced engineering, design, and manufacturing services
to government and private sector customers, and is deploying advanced nuclear fuel production capabilities to power existing, and
next-generation reactors around the world. The company was founded in October 1992 and is headquartered in Bethesda, Maryland.
