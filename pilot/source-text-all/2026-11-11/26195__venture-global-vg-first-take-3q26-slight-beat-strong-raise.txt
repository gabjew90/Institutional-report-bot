Venture Global (VG): First Take: 3Q26 Slight Beat, Strong Raise; CP2 First 
LNG Production Guidance Accelerated
10 November 2026 | 7:31AM EST
Venture Global (VG, Buy) reported 3Q26 adj. EBITDA of $2,583m, above GSe of 
$2,517m and FactSet consensus of $2,532m (+3%/+2%). Lower than expected 
O&M drove the slight beat vs our estimate, while underlying volumes and margins 
were in line following the company’s operating data pre-release in October. Looking 
forward, VG raised 2026 adj. EBITDA guidance to $10.0-10.3b (up from $8.7-9.1b 
prior), above GSe/consensus of $9.03b/$9.23b. Most notable was the acceleration of 
CP2 first LNG guidance to mid-2027 (vs 2H27 prior and GSe of June 2027) and the 
introduction of 3Q27 CP2 production guidance of 10-20 CP2 cargoes (vs GSe of 16), 
now both in-line with GSe, though we think the timing of this announcement 
suggests our estimates could now be conservative. Elsewhere on the operating front, 
Plaquemines Phase 1 reached commercial operations on 10/31, as expected, 
triggering the start of ~13 mtpa of Phase 1 contracts. On the commercial front, VG 
increased a 20-year agreement with an existing customer by 0.5 mtpa, which adds to 
the recent ConocoPhillips and China Gas deals announced intraquarter - we will look 
for details on the expected pace of commercializing VG’s unsold capacity going 
forward, particularly given headlines that QatarEnergy remains in discussions with 
US LNG operators for incremental US LNG supply. There were no new updates on the 
last pending CP1 arbitration case following the adverse Galp decision in October 
(damages capped at $170m). We will look to the call for next steps on VG’s 
behind-the-meter power opportunity set after VG announced a series of major 
equipment orders with GEV and BKR in October; we continue to see the BKR/GEV 
agreement as a low-cost call option for VG to become a large data center developer. 
Overall, we see this as a positive release, and we would expect details on CP2 first 
LNG timing and next steps on the power strategy to drive relative performance 
today.

shell on/around VG’s Louisiana footprint, covering feedgas, combined-cycle generation, 
and buildings, but not chips. From here, we will look for an update on the pace of 
customer discussions. In our view, holding the late 2028/early 2029 in-service timing 
without on-spec balance-of-plant spend likely requires a commercial commitment 
within 1H27.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.
