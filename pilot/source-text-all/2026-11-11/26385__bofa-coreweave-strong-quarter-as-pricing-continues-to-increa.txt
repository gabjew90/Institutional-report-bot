Coreweave 
Strong quarter as pricing continues to 
increase; funding ability is manageable 
Reiterate Rating: BUY | PO: 140.00 USD | Price: 48.73 USD   
  
 
 
Improving capacity economics drive robust quarter 
Strong 3Q with revenue growth of 168% YoY, 700bps above Street expectations, and 
operating margin at 10.4%, 350bps above Street estimates and up 540bps QoQ. The 
outperformance was driven by continued active power conversion and strategic capacity 
allocation to shorter-term contracts. Active power increased 200MW QoQ to 1.7GW, and 
we model full year active power to reach 1.9GW. Remaining performance obligations 
(RPO) increased 138% YoY to ~$133bn, with more than half the associated contracts 
expected to begin generating revenue by year-end. We update our estimates to reflect 
the improved economics and revised outlook, reiterating our Buy rating and $140 PO. 
Strong pricing power to persist, supporting margin upside 
Pricing continues to increase, as long dated contracts (4-5 years) are now being signed 
at $20-25mn/MW vs ~$14mn/MW earlier this year, with shorter dated contracts being 
signed at ~$40mn/MW. We expect CoreWeave’s pricing power to persist as customers’ 
improving ROI on inference deployments support demand, with 2027 demand exceeding 
expected supply by 10x and 2028 demand already several times larger than anticipated 
capacity. These trends should support margin expansion, and we model OM to increase 
from 10.1% in FY26E to 18.1% in FY27E and 20.8% in FY28E. We expect the company 
to maintain a balanced approach between the better economics of shorter-term 
contracts and the visibility and financing benefits of longer-term agreements.   
Diversified funding options provide flexibility 
CoreWeave’s funding needs are manageable, in our view, supported by a diversified set 
of financing options. While the company does not expect to access the unsecured 
market through 2027, CoreWeave can fund incremental capex through customer 
prepayments, which have been included in 70% of deals signed this year and should 
become more prevalent, as well as vendor financing and contract-backed DDTLs. The 
company plans to execute another DDTL in the near term, targeting a 7-7.5% yield, and 
we view this financing mechanism as currently the most attractive. We do not forecast 
financing to be a constraint on CoreWeave’s capacity ramp over the next few years, and 
expect active power to reach 3.3GW by YE27 and 4.6GW by YE28.

Company Description 
CoreWeave (NASDAQ: CRWV) is a fully-managed AI 
hyperscaler that provides GPU compute capacity via 
infrastructure services and software services. CoreWeave 
was first founded in 2017 in Roseland, New Jersey as a 
crypto mining company (discontinued) and launched the 
current CoreWeave Cloud Platform in 2020. Michael 
Intrator, Brian Venturo and Brannin McBee are the 
cofounders. 
Investment Rationale 
We believe CoreWeave's structural advantages position it to 
capture accelerating demand for AI-optimized 
infrastructure. The company's growth profile and strong 
data center pipeline should allow the company to benefit 
from demand for large-scale AI compute. CoreWeave 
delivers materially faster GPU deployment timelines, 
allowing customers to scale workloads more quickly, 
improve efficiency, and reduce total cost of ownership for 
clients.

Price objective basis & risk 
Coreweave (CRWV) 
Our PO of $140 is based on 26x our CY27E EV/EBIT, above the high end of the cloud 
datacenter group at 14-19x given elevated revenue growth expectations. 
 
Upside risks: 1) revenue upside and consistent beat and raise, 2) quality and sizable 
contract with hyperscalers and more AI-native customers, and 3) new customer 
additions among non-AI enterprises and SMBs to reduce customer concentration risk. 
 
Downside risks are: 1) high price volatility , 2) customer concentration risk, 3) supplier 
concentration risk (in case of capacity constraint), and 4) currently competing with 
Microsoft Azure, Google GCP, and Amazon AWS which are at much bigger scale/have 
higher capacity to spend on capex.
