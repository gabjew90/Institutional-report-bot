DeskTalk - Fedspeak has entered the chat
By Rui Ding
Waller has reignited focus on Fedspeak. OIS markets now imply up to 3x25bp (or 1x50 and 1x25) of hikes left in this cycle
after the combination of Waller and strong CPI. In today's quiet, we take a look at upcoming Fedspeak for potential
pushback. 
FOMC members appear to be telegraphing a more hawkish reaction function than expected and markets have responded
accordingly with 28bp priced for Dec vs 21bp pre Waller. Ultimately, we think the bar for a 50bp hike is high, and see hikes
being pushed into 2027. 
Overnight - Logan said it would make sense to pause after two more increases (conservative relative to market pricing).
Schmid described the current rate as fairly accommodative while Goolsbee described the Oct CPI report as "borderline
disturbing". Bowman, who has been extremely dovish in the past, admitted there is still work to do. Paulson implicitly pushed
back gradual tightening seems appropriate
This is marked contrast to Governor Warsh's preference for limited forward guidance. Our trader speculates a shift in the
political environment post-midterms may have prompted this, all while inflation data remains hot. Warsh is scheduled to give
opening remarks on Mon at the Productivity and Jobs Task Force Conference so it's not clear if he will address monetary
policy. 
We'll watch upcoming appearances such as Hammack, Schmid, Williams, Collins, Daly while we wait for PPI Friday. We
wouldn't rule out some surprise media appearances either (Kashkari perhaps?), Williams could be more indicative of the
dovish/centrist contingent.
