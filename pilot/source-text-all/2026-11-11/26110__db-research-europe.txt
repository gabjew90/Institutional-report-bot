EUROPEAN DIAGNOSTICS  - Weekly Flu Update (CW 43): So Far, So Good 
Jan Koch: The latest CDC data (CW43) confirm an earlier start to the flu season this year in the US, with activity 
running above levels typically seen at this point in the season. This is evident across laboratory positivity, ED 
diagnoses, outpatient visits and hospitalizations. Moreover, the CDC forecasts 6,900-30,100 new flu hospital 
admissions for the week ending November 21, 2026, which would represent a 2-8x increase vs. the comparable 
period last year. Absolute numbers nevertheless remain relatively low and an early start does not necessarily 
translate into a stronger season overall, as activity could simply peak earlier. That said, every additional week of 
flu activity above last year's levels should be supportive for respiratory testing volumes.
