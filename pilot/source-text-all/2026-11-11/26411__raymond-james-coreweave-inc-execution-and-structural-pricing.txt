Execution and Structural Pricing Tailwinds Could Ease AI Funding
Concerns
 
RECOMMENDATION
The Call: Robust execution and an accommodating pricing environment (see RJ Token
Observer LINK) led to operating profit upside, potentially easing concerns amidst a cautious AI
funding backdrop. GPU pricing rose ~25% in July and a further ~10% later in 3Q with additional
gains in 4Q, while short-term contracts command >$40M in annual revenue/MW vs. $20–25M for
longer-term deals with payback shrinking to one to two years for long-dated contracts (vs. two
to three years prior). Recontracting offers substantial upside potential with ~130K GPUs rolling
off in 2027 supporting ~$4B in potential ARR, rising to ~$10B including 2028 with no incremental
GPU capex.
●
Importantly, funding visibility is improving (see RJ early funding scenarios LINK) through ~
$9B of near-term customer prepayments, ~$9B of construction-in-progress, ~$5B of undrawn
delayed draw term loan (DDTL), a current DDTL expected to finance at 7-7.5%, and rising OCF
generation (RJ modeling $7.5B in 2026 vs. $3B in 2025). On net, we think $5B of parent-level
financing (characterized as more of an existing backlog requirement) is a reasonable starting
point for 2027 and expect incremental capex (beyond 2026 levels) to be funded via a mix
of prepayment and secured lending with parent-level (35M share ATM authorized) a smaller
contributor.
We remain Outperform rated as we believe investors underappreciate the opportunity for
CoreWeave to develop into a top-tier hyperscaler with 8GW of critical IT power ambitions by
2030. We expect underlying economics will improve as the installed base matures and attractive
growth initiatives expand, specifically managed inference (rapid growth to $250M of booked ARR)
and the first Omni customer (cloud-as-a-service).
EPS Recap
●
3Q revenue of $3.7B (+168% y/y) was ahead of the Street at $3.6B while adjusted EBIT
margins reached 10% vs. Street at 7% ahead of plan.
RJ Updates
●
Our revenue estimates go to $13.2B/$26.6B from $12.8B/$25.5B for 2026/2027.
●
We raise our adj. EBIT estimates to $1.33B/$4.00B from $1.06B/$3.38B for 2026/2027.
●
We lower our OCF estimates to $9.07B/$18.96B from $9.36B/$20.23B for 2026/2027.
●
We raise our capex estimates to $38B/$46B from $37B/$46B for 2026/2027.
●
As a result, OCF less capex moves to ($28.94B)/($27.05B) from ($27.64B)/($25.78B) for
2026/2027.
VALUATION
We maintain our $75 price target based on 10x 2028E EV/EBIT, towards the middle range of the
range of hyperscaler peers (AMZN, ORCL, DOCN).

COREWEAVE, INC.
 
CALL HIGHLIGHTS
Power
●
Active power: CoreWeave added >200MW in 3Q, bringing active
power to >1.7GW across 52 data centers. It maintained its >1.85GW
year-end target. It expects to meet 2027 deliveries based on the
permitting and construction status of its sites. Most Texas capacity
already operates or has permits and is under construction.
●
Contracted power: CoreWeave added >900MW of contracted
power, taking the total to >4.6GW. It has contracted >1.3GW
internationally across Canada, Europe, and Asia, following
customer demand for capacity outside the U.S.
Supply/Demand
●
Contracts/Customers: CoreWeave now serves 10% of Fortune 500
companies, and financial services accounts for 10% of backlog.
Industries outside AI labs and technology contributed more than
half of new customers this year, while the number of customers
committing >$100M grew >2.5x y/y.
●
Demand/Pricing: CoreWeave sees qualified demand at ~10x
available capacity through 2027, giving it room to choose among
customers and contract terms. It increased SKU pricing ~25% in July
and another ~10% later in 3Q, with further 4Q gains exceeding cost
inflation. Pricing on new four- to five-year contracts is moving toward
$20-25M of annual revenue per MW, while selected short-term sales
exceed $40M.
Platform
●
Attach and cross-sell: Forge is helping CoreWeave convert
software relationships into infrastructure customers. Existing Forge
users accounted for 70% of new 3Q cloud customers. Managed
inference surpassed $250M of booked ARR, reaching its prior
year-end target early. CoreWeave’s capacity allocation primarily
constrains further growth.
●
Cloud stack licensing: Omni lets customers run CoreWeave’s
platform on infrastructure it owns, allowing CoreWeave to serve
demand without funding those assets. It has signed one customer
and sees interest from others.
Chips
●
GPUs: Ampere/Hopper pricing rose 30-50% q/q, and CoreWeave
signed multi-year renewals extending beyond the GPUs’ depreciable
lives. More than 130K GPUs roll off contract in 2027 and a
further ~170K in 2028. At current pricing, CoreWeave sizes the
annualized recontracting opportunity at nearly $4B for the 2027
cohort and nearly $10B across both cohorts, without incremental
capex. CoreWeave also brought Vera Rubin into production with
Cognition, achieving 4.8x GB200 token throughput on its workloads.
Capital Structure
●
Debt: CoreWeave reduced its weighted-average debt cost by 250
bp y/y to 7.9%. It plans another contract-backed delayed draw
term loan (DDTL) in the coming weeks and expects a 7-7.5% yield
with >90% financing of associated capex, based on early lender
discussions. Investment-grade and enterprise customers account
for >75% of remaining committed 2027 capex not already attached
to a DDTL. CoreWeave expects existing contracted cash flows to
service and repay both asset-level and parent debt before renewals.
●
Prepayments and liquidity: CoreWeave ended 3Q with $6.5B
in cash, restricted cash and marketable securities, plus nearly
$5B of committed undrawn DDTLs. It expects ~$9B of customer
prepayments and deposits over the next three months, including >
$3B for fully paid-off infrastructure. Its funding plan also assumes
growing operating cash generation and additional asset-backed
borrowing, leaving an expected <$5B of incremental parent-level
capital through 2027.
Financial Recap
Recap: Revenue backlog of $132.5B (+138% y/y) was ~6%/~1% above
RJe/Street of $125.5B/$131.1B. Revenue of $3.7B (+168% y/y) was ~4%/
~3% above RJe/Street, while capex of $14.5B exceeded RJe/Street of
$12.5B as customer deployments accelerated. 2026 revenue guidance
increased to $13.0-13.3B and exit ARR guidance rose to $20-21B, while
capex guidance narrowed to $37-39B from $35-39B.
●
Margins: Adj. EBIT reached $379M, or 10.4% margin, versus RJe/
Street of $230M/$245M and 6.5%/6.9% margins. 2026 adj. EBIT
guidance increased to $1.275-1.375B, up 26% at the midpoint.
●
Power: Active power increased more than 200MW q/q to over 1.7GW
across 52 active data centers, while contracted power grew more
than 900MW to over 4.6GW. CoreWeave deployed infrastructure
against power received late in 2Q, reducing construction in progress
to $9.3B from $11.9B. Year-end active power guidance remained
above 1.85GW.
