Our 3Q Uniform Rental Sentiment Survey, which provides a rolling six-month outlook 
across multiple dimensions of the industry, indicates that respondents expect 
revenue growth of 5.6% y/y on average, up from 4.7% in our 2Q survey. Respondents 
cited new customer wins, cross-sell/upsell and pricing as the leading revenue growth 
drivers, with pricing expectations increasing to 3.6% y/y from 2.2% in 2Q. Labor cost 
growth expectations increased modestly to 3.3% y/y, while fuel cost growth 
expectations rose sharply to 6.3% y/y from 1.8% in 2Q. Respondents also reported 
more cautious US macro and industry outlooks, although the business outlook 
remained net positive and customer and wallet retention expectations were stable to 
modestly improved. We continue to expect Buy-rated CTAS to outgrow the broader 
industry through market share gains and more effective penetration of the unvended 
market, supporting approximately 7-8% y/y organic revenue growth over the next 
six months, while we remain cautious on Sell-rated VSTS due to service quality 
challenges, weak price realization and low sales force productivity. 
Uniform Rental Sentiment Survey Overview 
 
 
Overview of our Uniform Rental Sentiment Survey. Our quarterly Uniform Rental 
Sentiment Survey provides a rolling six-month outlook for the industry across 
multiple dimensions, including revenue trends, pricing, key growth drivers, costs and 
the broader economy. The survey provides a forward-looking perspective, offers 
insights across the P&L and captures the views of industry executives and managers. 
In this quarter’s survey, 57% of respondents were C-level executives or presidents, 
with the remainder comprising VPs or directors. Respondents were evenly split 
between food & beverage and industrial end-market exposure. Among the 
companies surveyed, 43% generated more than $50mn of revenue on an LTM basis 
and the remainder generated less than $50mn.

Cost Outlook 
 
 
Labor cost growth expectations increased but remain slightly below inflation. 
Respondents expect labor costs to rise 3.3% y/y on average over the next six months, up 
from 3.0% in our 2Q survey. The latest outlook remains slightly below current US 
inflation of 3.4%. That said, it is modestly above the 3.1% increase in average hourly 
earnings reported for August by the US Bureau of Labor Statistics. CTAS and VSTS are 
investing in technologies such as smart routing and delivery tracking to improve labor 
productivity. These initiatives should help the companies offset continued labor cost 
growth through greater route and delivery efficiency.

Fuel cost growth expectations increased significantly. Survey participants expect fuel 
costs to rise 6.3% y/y on average over the next six months, up sharply from 1.8% in our 
2Q survey. The increase likely reflects higher energy prices related to the ongoing war in 
Iran and disruption to regional energy supplies. Neither CTAS nor VSTS hedges fuel costs 
or automatically passes them through to customers via fuel surcharges. CTAS offsets 
higher energy costs through unit pricing, which introduces forecasting risk but does not 
reverse when energy prices decline. In contrast, VSTS occassionally uses temporary 
energy surcharges, which provide a more direct offset but create difficult comparisons 
when they lapse. 
 
Macro and Industry Outlook 
 
 
US macro outlook turned more negative. Survey respondents reported a more 
negative view of the US macro environment compared with our 2Q survey. In 3Q, 43% 
indicated that their outlook had worsened over the past 90 days, 43% reported no 
change and 14% indicated that their outlook had improved. This compares with an even 
split in 2Q, when 33% of respondents reported an improved outlook, 33% reported no 
change and 33% reported a worsened outlook. Respondents were most positive about 
GDP growth and supply chain conditions. In contrast, the political landscape and 
inflation represented the leading areas of concern.

Investment Conclusions 
 
 
CTAS remains an attractive Business Services compounder. We believe CTAS is 
positioned to deliver healthy revenue and earnings growth, supported by stable 
customer spending, strong retention and favorable new-business activity. Approximately 
two-thirds of the company’s volume growth typically comes from penetration of the 
no-programmer market, providing a durable source of new customers. We forecast 
organic revenue growth of approximately 8% in F2027, with healthcare and education 
representing key vertical growth opportunities. CTAS should also benefit from continued 
market share gains and cross-selling across uniform rentals, facility services and first aid 
and safety. We forecast operating margin expansion over the next year, supported by 
sourcing, Six Sigma and engineering initiatives. Together, these drivers support our view 
of CTAS as a durable compounder with attractive revenue growth and margin expansion 
potential. 
CTAS’ proposed acquisition of UNF remains on track for a 2H 2026 close. CTAS 
indicated that its acquisition of UNF remains on track to close in the second half of 
calendar 2026. On 6/11, CTAS and UNF received a Second Request for additional 
information from the US Federal Trade Commission in connection with its review of the 
proposed transaction. The Second Request extends the waiting period under the HSR 
Act until 30 days after both parties have substantially complied, unless the period is 
extended voluntarily or terminated earlier by the FTC. CTAS and UNF indicated that they 
continue to cooperate fully with the FTC’s review. On 6/12, UNF shareholders approved 
the proposed acquisition at a special meeting. The transaction remains subject to 
regulatory approval and other customary closing conditions. 
CTAS F1Q 2027 preview: We forecast 8.6% organic revenue growth and 30 bps of 
margin expansion. CTAS will report F1Q 2027 results on 9/23. We forecast organic 
revenue growth of 8.6% y/y, operating margins of 23.0%, up 30 bps y/y, and EPS of 
$1.33, with our estimates unchanged. By segment, we forecast organic revenue growth 
of 7.5% y/y in uniform rental and facility services, 13.0% in first aid and safety and 
11.0% in all other, which includes fire protection services and uniform direct sales. CTAS 
has historically outgrown our survey expectations by 200-300 bps, supported by market 
share gains and penetration of the unvended market. Applying a similar differential to 
the survey’s 5.6% y/y six-month industry growth outlook would imply approximately 
7-8% y/y organic revenue growth for CTAS over the next two quarters. Our F2Q and F3Q 
2027 organic revenue growth forecasts average 7.6% y/y, consistent with that 
framework. 
VSTS’ near-term revenue growth remains constrained by execution challenges. We 
believe VSTS has limited near-term revenue visibility due to service quality issues, weak 
price realization and low sales force productivity. New sales have been insufficient to 
offset customer churn, reflecting elevated sales force turnover, dated systems and 
incentive misalignment. We forecast normalized same-day organic revenue growth of 
2.2% y/y in F4Q and a decline of 1.0% in F2026. VSTS’ F2026 guidance for a 0-2% 
decline in normalized revenue compares with CTAS’ F2027 guidance for revenue growth 
of 7.4-8.7%. Although the guidance periods and revenue definitions differ, the 
substantial growth gap is consistent with continued market share pressure at VSTS. We 
leave our revenue and EBITDA margin estimates unchanged.

Valuation and Risks 
 
 
Cintas (CTAS, Buy): We maintain our 12-month price target of $231 based on 25.0x our 
NTM +1 YR EBITDA estimate of $3,778mn, NTM net debt of $1,442mn and NTM shares 
outstanding of 402.4mn. Our target multiple represents a premium to the Business 
Services peer group median of 8.3x to reflect CTAS’ stronger organic revenue growth 
outlook, track record of execution and market share leadership. Risks to the downside 
include cyclical exposure, a further deceleration in pricing increases, competition, 
acquisition integration risk and energy-related risk. 
Vestis (VSTS, Sell): We maintain our 12-month price target of $9.30 based on 6.3x our 
NTM + 1 YR EBITDA estimate of $361mn, NTM net debt of $1,021mn and NTM shares 
outstanding of 136mn. Our target multiple comes below the Business Services peer 
group median of 8.3x to reflect lower revenue growth, EBITDA margins and EPS growth, 
as well as higher financial leverage. Key risks to the upside include an improved macro 
environment, market share gains, new business growth acceleration, effective 
cross-selling and pricing upside.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.  
M&A Rank 
Across our global coverage, we examine stocks using an M&A framework, considering both qualitative factors and quantitative factors (which may vary 
across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring 
companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 
representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard 
departmental guidelines we incorporate an M&A component into our target price. M&A rank of 3 is considered immaterial and therefore does not 
factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.
