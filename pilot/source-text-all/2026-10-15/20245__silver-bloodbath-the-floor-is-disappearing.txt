Silver is getting crushed
The silver selloff is turning ugly.
A major trend line is breaking, volatility is surging, and the dollar and rising yields are adding serious
pressure. Meanwhile, China's tech carnage may be forcing more selling, while CTAs continue to press
the downside.
With $56 now the line in the sand and little support below, is the silver liquidation about to get even
nastier?
The silver puke
Silver is breaking below the trend line that has been in place since April 2025.
We are now testing the line in the sand at $56. A close below this level would leave little meaningful
support until the $48 area.

Rare in silver
Silver volatility rising as the metal pukes is unusual. Silver typically trades with an "upside skew."
This tells us the crowd is scrambling for downside protection as silver continues to implode, now rather
aggressively.

The dollar connection
You trade silver, you trade the dollar. The relationship has remained remarkably strong since early this
year. With the dollar this well bid, silver is struggling to find support.

The rates connection
Silver can't take rising rates. The latest leg lower in silver started just as yields began their latest move
higher. Looks like silver still has some catching up to do.

Poor Chinese

Pushing momentum
CTAs are happy to press their shorts further. These guys basically trade one thing: momentum. And
