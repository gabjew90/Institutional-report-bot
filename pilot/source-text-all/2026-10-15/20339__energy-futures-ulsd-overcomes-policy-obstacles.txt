Energy Futures: ULSD Overcomes Policy Obstacles
US Diesel rebounded over the past week, overcoming various policy changes aimed to cool prices.
Significant speculative buying, via outrights and calendar spreads, probably ensued. If true, this would
mark a substantial change from the previous month. The option market reflected less incremental
downside posturing. Resultant implied volatility levels appear more reasonable relative to realized,
creating opportunities for asymmetric expression leading into the US Midterm elections.
• US Diesel rebounded over the past week, overcoming various policy changes aimed to cool prices. IEA
members announced a strategic oil release and the US Treasury reportedly suspended sanctions on Russian
diesel exports. However, both initiatives underwhelmed. The exact details of the IEA stock draw remain
undisclosed. Moreover, US politicians plus other nations criticized the "Russian lifeline" (link). Thus
November ULSD jumped ~8% and the rolling front ULSD-WTI crack approached record highs.
• Significant speculative buying, via outrights and calendar spreads, probably ensued. Aggregate open
interest (OI) surged $5.2bn, marking the largest 5 day increase this year. Most new OI surfaced in
December - March with a reasonable uptick in June.
• If true, this would mark a substantial change from the previous month. Per Commitment of Traders,
Managed Money net sold ULSD in 4 of the 5 weeks from September 1st - October 6th for a cumulative
$2bn. Liquidation was the dominant driver.
• The option market reflected less incremental downside posturing. ULSD 1 month normalized 25 delta
put-call skew retreated from an approximate 1 year max and implied volatility cheapened.
• Resultant implied volatility levels appear more reasonable relative to realized, creating opportunities for
asymmetric expression leading into the US Midterm elections. Both the US and Iran are seemingly
preparing for a return to hostilities (link).
