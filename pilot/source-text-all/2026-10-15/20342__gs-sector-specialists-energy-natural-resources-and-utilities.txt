I’ll be hosting a Midstream pre-EPS breakfast with Research and a group of investors tomorrow in NYC -
let me know if would like to connect afterwards on key debates / names / prints.
HAL. The company will report EPS on the 20th before market open – and inbounds ahead of the print
have generally leaned cautious. Investor feedback continues to highlight near-term negative revisions (3Q
headline EBITDA numbers have moved lower with 4Q EBITDA largely still intact over the last 3m).
Consensus view is frac pricing will be pushed higher, though largely debated on the rate of change here
(gradual or not) and over what timeframe. Since the last print – several headlines on the long-term
agreement / MOU fronts (Kuwait, Brazil, Deepwater, Venezuela) – expect there to be more focus on this
on the call. The magnitude of the stock moves in OFS over the last couple of months (and even last
quarter) have been eye opening – and more folks are highlighting positioning as a main focus area into
EPS vs actual fundys. From my POV – investors still lean cautious (though do think the bear inbounds
have been gradually slowing) – likely setting up an interesting r/r for earnings. Recall HAL reported an
essentially in-line EBITDA print last quarter and was down ~7% at one point during the day – with the 3Q
margin guide in focus. Cost inflation for both Services and E&P has been a key theme over the last couple
of weeks – and expect there to be more commentary around labor + rig mobilization here.
XOM. Has been the most topical Major on the desk over the last week – and the delayed 8K has been the
number one inbound. EUROs reported generally in line trading updates with buyside over the last couple
of weeks – and while we typically can get a read to some of the biz segments for the US Majors – most
eyeballs were on ME updates and Downstream for XOM. Outside of more questions vs answers on the 8K
– we still think specialists tilt more positive on the European Majors vs US Majors (solid results from BP
today reinforce this), though sentiment had been improving on XOM since the 2Q print – with
Downstream upside to Street + improving Hormuz flows in focus. Recall the company guided to a full
3Q26 closure of SoH could reduce regional production by 750 kbd relative to 2025 levels. While Hormuz
volumes did improve during the quarter – would think there are more questions on volume risks + path
forward for Qatar.
