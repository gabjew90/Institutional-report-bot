Japan Electronic Components/Semiconductors: NVDA read-across: 
Strong earnings outlook and product roadmap provide reassurance
18 November 2026 | 10:12AM JST
NVIDIA (NVDA, covered by US semiconductor/semiconductor equipment analyst 
James Schneider, Ph.D.) announced its 3Q results on November 18 (early morning 
JST). FY27 3Q (August-October) revenue of US$115.8 bn (+20% qoq) beat GSe of 
US$110.1 bn, consensus of US$109.5 bn, and the midpoint of guidance at US$108.0 
bn. The transition from Blackwell to Rubin is proceeding smoothly, and sales across 
the Rubin platform were c.US$20 bn. Non-GPU product sales are also expanding; 
revenue for networking was US$21.0 bn, and CPU revenue is expected to double in 
FY1/28. Meanwhile, the company explained that they are no longer shipping Hopper 
products to China. NVDA guided for 4Q (November-January) revenue of US$130.0 
bn at the midpoint, beating estimates (GSe US$124.8 bn, consensus US$124.8 bn). In 
addition, the revenue growth outlook for FY1/28 was raised from 70% to 75%. 
Management explained that the launch schedule for new products is also proceeding 
as planned (2027: Vera Rubin Ultra, 2028: Feynman).  
NVDA shares were up c.4.5% in after-hours trading (as of 18:00 local time). The 
strong results and outlook presented by NVDA are consistent with the robust 
outlook seen from the Asian upstream supply chain, and the fact that product 
shifts and new product launch schedules are proceeding as planned should 
provide a sense of reassurance to the entire Asian AI-related supply chain. In the 
Japan electronic components/semiconductor sector, we have Buy 
recommendations on five passive component and ceramic component 
companies (Murata Mfg, Taiyo Yuden, TDK, Nichicon, MARUWA) that left a 
highly positive impression with their 1H FY3/27 results. For Ibiden, while we await 
clarity on the outlook for its yet-to-be-finalized new plant construction (we assume 
funding from customers including NVDA, based on prior capacity expansions), we 
believe the latest NVDA results should provide reassurance for now (we maintain our 
Buy rating). In analog/power, we note differences between Rohm, which has larger 
NVDA-related exposure via Taiwanese power supply makers, and Renesas which 
seeing substantial expansion in PMICs for ASICs in addition to those for NVDA, and 
maintain our Buy ratings on both.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.
