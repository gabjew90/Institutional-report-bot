Valuation implies too low of a multiple on Construction  
In our view, it is important to place CAT’s positive Compact Construction event in the 
context of what is happening in the stock market. CAT’s PE multiple has de-rated from 
30x to 17x in a few months as the AI sentiment continues to unwind. Based on SOTP 
analysis (see inside), we believe the market is applying a discount to CAT’s Machinery 
Construction unit. Recently, CNH and Kobelco announced a Construction Equipment JV 
valued at ~10x EBITDA (i.e., put a floor on valuation). CAT’s construction unit is a best-
in-class leader that is gaining share. While all eyes remain on the AI theme, CAT is 
planting the seeds to sustain outgrowth in construction for years to come. Maintain Buy.

Valuation update: what’s the value for CAT’s construction? 
In the Exhibit 1 below, we conduct a sum of the parts valuation exercise to assess the 
valuation of CAT’s business segments. To be clear, CAT’s multiple could de-rate further 
if we are entering an industrial recession. That said, our sum of the parts valuation 
exercise helps assess how the market is valuing certain markets and competitor 
companies. We believe the market is implying a lower multiple for CAT’s segments than 
its peers. For example, CNH and Kobelco recently announced (November 9th) a 
Construction Equipment JV valued at ~10x EBITDA. In our view, this puts a floor on 
valuation for Construction Industries – CAT’s business is best-in-class, industry leading, 
and gaining share. In our view, CAT’s Power & Energy segment is being valued at the 
very low end of its engine & turbine peer group – which we view as too penalizing given 
CAT’s high quality portfolio, services, and diverse end markets. Overall, we believe CAT’s 
positive Compact event (Construction) highlights that there are growth opportunities to 
the overall CAT portfolio beyond the power theme too.

Price objective basis & risk 
Caterpillar Inc (CAT) 
Our $746 PO on CAT is based on 22x 2027e EPS which is above the 15y long term 
historical range of 15-18x and in the recovery multiple range (20-30x) as we see a multi-
year EPS recovery: cyclical Machinery upcycle (higher commodity prices, gaining share, 
expanding fleet) and structural growth drivers (power generation, pipelines). While we 
see some headwinds that are likely keep a lid on margin expansion (tariffs), there are 
unique macro and business cycle factors that are underpinning stronger cycle over cycle 
earnings: infrastructure, construction spending mix shifts towards heavy vs light, higher 
miner free cash flow generation, aging fleets, gas pipelines, and data centers. 
 
Downside risks to our PO: 1) worse than expected tariff policy that tilts global economy 
into recession, 2) a greater-than-expected reduction or delay in capital spending among 
large mining, and oil and gas customers, 3) intensifying pricing pressure in the 
construction and mining equipment industries, 4) greater than expected dealer 
destocking, 5) ongoing deterioration in dealer sales growth. 
 
Upside risks: 1) a faster recovery in the global economy, 2) firming earthmoving 
construction equipment market, 3) stronger-than-expected fleet replacement, 4) 
continuing recovery in commodity prices, 5) stronger-than-expected demand trends in 
gas compression.
