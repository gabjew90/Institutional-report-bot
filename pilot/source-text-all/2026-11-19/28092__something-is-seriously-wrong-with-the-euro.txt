The euro is losing the plot
The euro continues trading lower despite several developments that should ordinarily provide support.
Oil and gas prices have fallen, credit conditions have improved, and relative economic surprises have
started bouncing. Yet EURUSD continues grinding lower.
Meanwhile, dollar momentum is strengthening, TS Lombard remains bearish EURUSD, and Deutsche
Bank highlights some unusual foreign buying of US equities.
The bearish momentum is difficult to ignore, but positioning is becoming increasingly one-sided just as
EURUSD approaches major technical support.
Soggy euro
EURUSD continues to grind lower, breaking below 1.10 as we write. We are well below the 200-day
moving average, with the pair now testing the major trendline and entering the first serious support
zone.
RSI is already in oversold territory, but still some distance from the extreme oversold readings we saw
earlier this autumn.

The euro's oil problem
Lower energy prices aren't necessarily bullish for the euro. TS Lombard argues that softer oil could
make the ECB less hawkish, while sticky US services inflation keeps the Fed tightening. That would
widen rate differentials further in favour of the dollar.
The bank argues Brent may need to fall towards $70 before FX markets start pricing a broader global
growth recovery.
Dollar bulls taking control
TS Lombard's dollar bull/bear indicator has surged to its strongest level since 2023. The bank expects
further dollar strength as resilient US growth, a recovering labour market and sticky services inflation
keep the Fed more hawkish than markets currently anticipate.
TS Lombard
The dollar's unusual support
The dollar continues pushing higher despite the brutal tech sell-off, overshooting the widening in US
interest rate differentials.

Deutsche Bank notes that foreigners have been aggressively buying US equities into the weakness. Its
tracker of more than 100 foreign-domiciled ETFs shows the largest two-week buying spree of US stocks
this year.
DB questions whether the aggressive equity buying and widening rate differentials can both continue if
market turmoil persists.

Already short
Bearish euro positioning is already substantial. TS Lombard's tactical FX positioning indicator stands
around -1.0, yet the bank maintains its negative EURUSD view.
With EURUSD approaching major support and RSI already oversold, the crowded positioning is worth
keeping in mind.
