Tech is a quarter of global trade but over half its growth
As we noted in an earlier note, the AI supply chain is highly concentrated when viewed 
through a trade lens: two buyers and five suppliers account for 80% of all AI trade. 
Much of this trade is the counterpart of hyperscaler capex, which we estimate is running 
at close to $1 trillion in 2026 (3% of US GDP) and could rise to $1.75 trillion in 2027 (a 
bit over 5% of GDP). For context, 172 of the 193 UN member countries have a GDP 
smaller than $1 trillion.
Not all tech trade is AI-related, however. Our 'Tech' aggregate for global exports came to 
nearly $6 trillion over the last 12 months, or roughly 27% of global exports by value (up 
from 22% in 2022). That category includes telecommunications equipment, TVs, 
computers, optical and medical instruments, photographic equipment and a range of 
other electronics. But a large part of the growth in that aggregate comes from AI-related 
trade, including automatic data processing machines and semiconductors. Although 
'Tech' is only a bit over a quarter of global trade by value, it is currently driving 55% of 
the annual growth in global exports. See our Global Markets Outlook for a fuller 
discussion of how much global activity is currently being driven by technology.
