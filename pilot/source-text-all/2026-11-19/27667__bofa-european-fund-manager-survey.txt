2027: upside with constraints
17 November 2026 | Equity Strategy | Europe
Key takeaways
• European equity optimism builds, with 6% expected upside and Euro Stoxx the third global pick for 2027
• Investors face a crowding-FOMO dilemma, with 33% concerned about crowded trades and 30% worried they
are underinvested
• Industrial Gds & Svs takes the top overweight spot, as positioning signals rotation rather than broad risk-on

OW/UW: overweight/underweight
European optimism builds for 2027: net 67% of survey respondents expect European equities to rise over the next 1-3 months, the highest since
February. Expected 12-month equity upside has recovered to 6%, alongside 6.6% expected upside to forward EPS, while global investors rank Euro Stoxx
third among the equity indices expected to outperform in 2027, behind Nasdaq and MSCI Emerging Markets.
The crowding-FOMO catch: 33% cite concentration in crowded trades as their biggest portfolio risk, while 30% worry about being underinvested and
missing out on an equity rally. Finding the next source of upside could be diﬃcult: 23% of investors expect Healthcare to outperform, with just 7% viewing
Quality defensives as crowded. By contrast, 17% favour Financials despite seeing it as crowded and overweight Bank positioning, while another 17% favour
Discretionary despite current broad underweight positioning.
Cost eﬃciencies to drive earnings: 50% of investors surveyed see earnings upgrades as the most likely driver of further European equity upside, with
40% expecting upgrades to be broad across sectors, but concentrated in larger companies. At the same time, the earnings narrative has shiﬅed from top-
line growth towards eﬃciencies: 43% see cost discipline as the main earnings driver.
Better growth than policy suggests: net 40% of survey participants expect European growth to strengthen despite net 10% judging monetary policy too
restrictive. Fiscal policy is viewed as accommodative by a net 10%, yet Euro area ﬁscal expansion is only the second-most-cited catalyst for stronger
growth at 27%, behind geopolitical de-escalation, at 30%.
Rotation rather than a risk-on trade: Industrial Gds & Svs is now the most overweight sector for the ﬁrst time since Sep '25, while Construction and
Healthcare move from underweight to overweight and Basic Resources and Energy move to neutral. High Beta is expected to outperform Low Beta by a net
17%, the highest since Aug '21, but High Quality remains the strongest style preference, while expected cyclical outperformance has moderated. Among
countries, investors surveyed favour Switzerland and Germany, while France remains the least preferred market at net -60%.
