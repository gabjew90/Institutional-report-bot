High Grade Copper Futures: FCI Tightening vs Potential Tariffs
Commitment of Traders covering November 3th - 10th displayed the 7th straight week of speculative selling in High
Grade Copper futures. Notably, Producer / Processor / Merchant / User remained the dominant bull. Since then,
tighter financial conditions plus ongoing Data Center weakness pressured price, triggering a key momentum flip and
more bearish speculative flows. The option surface even revealed impairment to long-term sentiment. PMPU's
resolve in waiting for any potential tariff signaling seems crucial. 
 
• Commitment of Traders covering November 3th - 10th displayed the 7th straight week of speculative
selling in High Grade Copper futures. Specifically Managed Money, Other, and Non-Reportable sold $1.3bn,
bringing the running total since September 22nd to $10.8bn. Other was the main driver with a sizeable uptick in
shorts.
• Notably, Producer / Processor / Merchant / User remained the dominant bull. PMPU bought $1.1bn net, half
of which represented gross long additions. As a result, PMPU gross longs as a percentage of total open interest
reached a new multiyear max.
• Since then, tighter financial conditions plus ongoing Data Center weakness pressured price, triggering a
key momentum flip and more bearish speculative flows. During November 10th - 17th, High Grade Copper lost
4.1%. GS's US Financial Conditions Index tightened post inflation data and Fed speak. Moreover, Data Centers
slipped 3.2%. According to GS Futures Strategists' framework, medium-term momentum turned negative,
prompting significant CTA sell estimates. 
• The option surface even revealed impairment to long-term sentiment. High Grade Copper 1 year normalized
25 delta put-call skew reached a 99% 1 year rank.
• PMPU's resolve in waiting for any potential tariff signaling seems crucial. Recall this category was unable to
withstand a geopolitical reversal in Brent Oil throughout the past few weeks.
