SSE PLC (SSE LN, BUY, PT: 3060.00p) 1H26/27 First View: Strong Networks-led Growth; Guidance 
unchanged [Ahmed Farman] 
 Adjusted EPS was 66.5p, within the 64-68p range flagged in October, driven by a doubling of Transmission 
operating profit to £584m, Renewables +21% to £332m and a turnaround in Thermal to £78m from a £22m 
loss; Distribution was broadly flat at £132m. Investment up 63% to a record £2.6bn, c.70% in networks, 
while net debt rose to £11.4bn. SSE reiterates FY27 EPS guidance of 168-193p and 2030 EPS of 225-250p, 
unchanged from Q1/pre-close, with BU guidance also unchanged. FY27 capex remains >£5bn, versus the 
c.£5bn indicated at Q1, and the £33bn five-year plan is unchanged. The interim dividend rises 7% YoY to 
22.9p, with SSE explicitly expecting 5-10% FY27 DPS growth, consistent with its policy to FY30. 
Strategically, all 11 Transmission projects are now under construction, while the upcoming ED3 plan 
envisages c.50% higher baseline/uncertainty-mechanism spend vs ED2. Dogger Bank has c.150 turbines 
installed; Berwick B targets 2H27 FID, with c.60% of expected debt locked below 5%. 
 https://javatar.bluematrix.com/pdf/EGikoEL5

Thermal Solutions was lower yoy. There's still a bit of work to do in the remainder of the year, and despite 

the potentially attractive recovery potential, we stay at Hold.
