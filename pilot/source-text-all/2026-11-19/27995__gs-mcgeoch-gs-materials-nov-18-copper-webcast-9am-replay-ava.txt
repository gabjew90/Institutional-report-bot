China
*Mkt wrap - China A-shares ended the day with small gains. Intraday price action continues to be volatile with the
index traded ranged bound. T/O was notably lighter - finished below 1.4 Tn RMB and refreshed the lowest level
since July 2025. Thematically, chip control related headlines remains the major driver of sector performance - Nvidia

is no longer shipping Hopper data center chips (less advanced vs Blackwell/Rubin) to China, despite winning the US
government's blessing to sell a version of it to China last year. GS China Domestic AI +3.78% led the outperformance
again as the strategic importance of domestic compute development gets further reinforced. On the other hand,
following Reuter's report that US is going to move forward with ban on China Optical Transceivers despite US
hyperscalers' pushback, China optic names didn't fall as much today partly due to much of the negativity has been
reflected in prices. Elsewhere, with lighter liquidity, small/micro caps continue to underperform. NT favorite ETFs
(510300, 159915) continue to see activity pickup into close, extending the pattern to 3 days in a row now.
