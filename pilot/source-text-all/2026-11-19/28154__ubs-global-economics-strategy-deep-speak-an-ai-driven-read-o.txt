Deep Speak: An AI-driven read on tone shifts at 
the Fed, ECB and BoJ
A new LLM-based tracker of central bank sentiment and thematic shifts
We developed an AI-based framework to measure policy tone for the Fed, ECB, and 
BoJ. Using LLMs, we analyze policy-relevant remarks in speeches, interviews and 
press conferences and score them on a hawkish/dovish scale, offering insights into 
sentiment shifts as well as thematic drivers and speakers' contributions. Updated 
daily, the dataset spans over two decades and is accessible via an interactive 
dashboard and a Python API (>Access Dataset).
