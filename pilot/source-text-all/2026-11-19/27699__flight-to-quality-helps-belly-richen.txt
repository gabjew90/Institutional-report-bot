Flight-to-quality helps belly richen 
◼ 
After flight-to-quality trades at the start of the week, markets will focus 
on retail sales data and FOMC minutes this week. 
◼ 
Fed Chair Kevin Warsh focused on AI’s potential impact on the economy 
rather than monetary policy yesterday, not a surprise given his style of 
not providing forward guidance. 
◼ 
We are biased towards flatteners. The intermediate sector has seen high 
volatility recently. While cheapening during the massive selloff last 
week, it outperformed on the curve yesterday in the flight-to-quality rally. 
After flight-to-quality trades at the start of the week, markets will focus on retail 
sales data today and FOMC minutes tomorrow. Our economists forecast retail 
sales headline to come in at 0.0% MoM in October vs a 0.8% MoM increase prior 
month, given consumers have been spending despite headwinds from the surge 
in gas prices due to the Iran war. This forecast would leave the ex-auto & gas 
number at 0.3% MoM in October. 
The minutes will offer further insight into the FOMC deliberations at the October 
meeting. Market participants are interested in any rationale for an October skip. 
Ahead of an upside surprise in the October CPI data last week, Fed Governor Chris 
Waller put the option of a 50bp hike on the table for December. Dallas Fed 
president Lorie Logan also speculated on accelerating the pace of hikes, begging 
the question as to why the Fed did not hike in October. The market now prices 
about 27bp for December and 44bp by January. 
Fed Chair Kevin Warsh focused on AI’s potential impact on economy rather than 
monetary policy yesterday, not a surprise given his style of not providing forward 
guidance. While AI might increase productivity with gains shared widely in global 
economy and drive substantial labour-market disruption, Warsh pointed out risks 
of AI disruption through bad actors, as the Fed considers potential vulnerabilities. 
We are biased towards flatteners, as additional rate hikes would weigh on short 
rates. Intermediate sector has seen high volatility recently. While cheapening 
during the massive Treasury selloff last week, the 2-5-10Y butterfly richened about 
3bp in the flight-to-quality rally yesterday (Figure 1).
