Downgrade to Neutral, turnaround pushed 
out on PC/supply headwinds 
Rating Change: NEUTRAL | PO: 100.00 USD | Price: 92.07 USD   
   
 
 
Agentic tide more than offset by PC/supply headwinds 
We downgrade INTC to Neutral from Buy. In our view, the stock has not communicated 
enough traction with external foundry customers, while its data center strength is offset 
by a surprisingly sharp drop in PC shipments (down 25-30% QoQ in Q4, further inventory 
correction expected in 1H27).  Data center CPU is doing well in a rising agentic tide, but 
faces a tight supply (substrate) environment, and products are not yet technically competitive 
against AMD/ARM-based rivals that are growing 2x-4x faster.  On the positive side, we 
acknowledge INTC’s longer-term ability to develop a competitive domestic foundry with 
any signs of traction potentially driving a recovery in a relatively under-owned stock. 
Lower CY30 EPS power to $5-$6 from $6-$7 prior 
Overall, we lower CY27/28E EPS by -16%/-4% to $1.69/$2.79.  Our longer-term EPS 
model forecasts foundry wins becoming more prominent in CY28-30, driving EPS power 
towards $5.89, on which we apply a ~20x PE multiple, discounted back to CY28 to get to 
our $100 PO (down from $145 based on ~29x prior CY30 EPS power of $6.14).  Our new 
$100 PO conceptually implies a $550bn market cap, made of: 1) $450bn in Products 
(roughly half of AMD market cap which comprises an overlapping server CPU, but a 
distinct GPU business), and 2) remaining ~$100bn in the emerging Foundry which could 
inflate as/when INTC announces external customer traction on 18A-P/14A.  Best-in-class 
foundry TSMC trades ~11x P/S, so a $100bn implied valuation suggests an implicit $8-
10bn in external business (of which can eventually expand to 8-10% of overall wafer 
foundry TAM of ~$380bn as a US-based second or third source supplier). 
Read-across: positive AI/semicaps, incr. negative AMD 
PC weakness is likely to weigh on rival AMD (up to $750mn PC headwind, offset by 
$500mn+ strong server CPU revenue), which reports next week, with consensus calling 
for client PC flat into Q4 vs. INTC down 25-30%. However, AMD Helios/MI ramp could 
be a strong positive offset.  INTC data center strength is positive for AI semis NVDA, 
AVGO, MRVL, while spending intentions (CY27 growth, with a rising mix of equipment 
spend) are a positive for semicaps, especially KLAC, lithography, AMAT, LRCX.

 EPS power, valuation framework 
 
Fully established IDM by CY30, EPS power ~$5.89 
Particularly, we now value INTC as a fully integrated IDM company with EPS power of 
~$5.89 by CY30, with our PO based on 20x CY30E PE, discounted back two years. 
- 
Products: We expect INTC DCAI (server CPUs + custom/merchant accelerators + 
offloads) sales to reach ~$60bn by CY30E (vast majority of DCAI segment is server 
CPUs). This implies about ~25% share of $220bn server CPU TAM. 
-

there is further clarity on specific deal details, we present the multiple deals INTC is 
currently engaged with, size the potential opportunities below.  Opportunities span 
across: Apple M-Series wafers, MediaTek TPU packaging, Terafab IP/packaging, and 
potentially other ARM-based server CPUs, edge AI expansion into Client – all made 
more possible via recent CDNS 14A collab announcement.
