  I found this last night, exactly 1 year ago I wrote this note GS Materials - Coppers Spinal Tap and SXPP upside ….

I watched GOOG.US wipe out the entire mkt cap of BHP + RIO + AAL + GLEN and others yday in the post mkt move.
Its not that one coy is bigger than the entire idustry it relies on, the knee jerk reaction to one headline is bigger…. I
mean honestly, if ever there was a time, that the smartest thing to do was to produce less, show the value of what you
have, the value of what you do, the complexity in delivering and how mispriced it is. The largest national producer,
Codelco, said exactly that “value over volume”. The whole AI phenom is like 3yrs old, these mines are like deacdes in
building, surely you have to put the right multiple on that long duration risk…. But if those who produce cant help you
do that, it’s a bit chicken and egg. As one person said, if workers can go on hunger strike, why cant the bosses?   
   
 So many thoughts in my head yesterday….not sure where to start (see all results notes links below) . Ended the
Day on RIO (overnight it traded s short -2% v BHP) – the four words no one wants to hear, “underground, seismic,
evacuated, injured”, the only word not in there fortunately was deaths. RIO clarified link , 3 workers injured, tremors
are part of block caving (if you don’t know what it is take a minute), sadly it happens. Whilst I am not saying this is the
case, it was put to me – in the stack: lithium, Ali, Iron ore all sucking wind, so you push the one that isn’t harder and
things happen. I don’t subscribe, I see miners operating below the red line, mining is a hard business, where the
multiple? Yesterday it was reported Antamina had a death lionk . That’s on top of 4 separate mines in the Chile in the
last few weeks losing people. 5 mines, 5 weeks (and that’s the reported ones). There will be a knock on impact to the
supply line. … I put my takeaways on the prints yesterday in the below. The standout was TECK, perversely it is bound
by AAL and the arb, or put the other way AAL outperfomed as it is bound to TECK. The messiest print was the reset at
EGO.US, basically traded in line with AEM. Thas wll you need to know about what the mkt sees in the
forward. GLEN was just fine, look at what they need to do in Q4 and look at what they did last Q4. …overnight we see
VALE deliver a beat (against a heavily revised cons, %3.5bn v cons 3.3, GS moved from 4.0), prodn was lower in Iron
ore fines and Copper (pellets and Nickel better), guidance reiterated – Copper and nIckel upper half, of note, Iron ore
realised price also missed at $85.10 (cons 86), it was -10% q/q.. C1 costs +12% q/q, all in costs +21% y/y, Capex will
come at lower end of range... …of all the things that flexed me most yday it was GOLD. Given the FOMC, a “run it hot”
comment in parts, we drift lower. Dec hike priced, Oil at $115, PCE inline yday, I just think the mkt is saying call me in
2027.

 China  
 *Midday wrap -  China’s A-share market experienced a sharp risk-off session this morning, led by a significant sell-off
in AI hardware names as investors digested disappointing third-quarter earnings. CPO and PCB stocks gapped down
more than 10% at the open, reflecting widespread bottom-line misses and margin compression concerns relative to
both consensus and prior-quarter performance. However, losses narrowed rapidly within the first hour of trading,
suggesting dip-buying interest. Conversely, flows rotated defensively into CRO and Baijiu sectors, which benefited
directly from AI-related unwinding; notably, China spirits traded on light volume amid persistently lackluster consumer
sentiment, indicating the rotation is liquidity-driven rather than fundamental re-rating. The robotics theme posted
moderate declines as Unitree’s Q3 report highlighted R&D-driven profit pressure, reinforcing sector-wide concerns
about the timeline for scalable unit economics.
