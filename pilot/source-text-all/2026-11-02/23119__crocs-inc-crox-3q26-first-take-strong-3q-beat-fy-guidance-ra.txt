Crocs Inc. (CROX): 3Q26 First Take: Strong 3Q beat; FY guidance raised
29 October 2026 | 5:59AM MDT
CROX reported 3Q adjusted EPS of $3.69, above GS/FactSet consensus at 
$3.30/$3.31. Net revenues increased 4.6% Y/Y (+4.6% ex-FX), compared with 
GS/consensus at +0.8%/+0.7%, driven by growth across the Crocs and HEYDUDE 
brands. Adj. EBIT margin came in at 22.3%, above GS/consensus at 21.6%/21.7%. 
Both gross margin and SG&A control drove the margin outperformance, with gross 
margin up ~180bps Y/Y to 60.3% vs. GS/consensus at 60.2%/59.9% and adj. SG&A 
at 38.1% of sales vs. GS/consensus at 38.7%/38.3%.  
Looking ahead, CROX raised its FY26 guidance. Management now expects FY26 adj. 
EPS of $14.45-$14.65 (vs. $13.70-$14.00 prior), compared with GS/consensus at 
$14.02/$13.96. Revenues are now expected to increase ~3% (vs. +1% to +2% prior), 
with Crocs brand revenue guidance at +4% vs. +2% to +3% prior and HEYDUDE 
revenues expected to be flat vs. -4% to -2% prior. CROX raised its operating margin 
guidance to modest expansion from 22.5%, compared with GS/consensus at 22.5%. 
For 4Q, CROX expects adj. EPS of $3.25-$3.45, compared with GS/FactSet consensus 
at $3.17/$3.11. Revenue is expected to increase 7-9%, compared with 
GS/consensus at 2.7%/3.8%. Crocs brand revenue is expected to increase 6-8% vs. 
GS/consensus at 1.8%/4.2% and HEYDUDE revenue expected to increase 10-12% 
vs. GS/consensus at 6.3%/5.2%. CROX expects 4Q adjusted operating margin of 
20.5%, compared with GS/consensus at 20.4%/20.6%. 
This is a strong print from CROX, with better-than-expected 3Q results across the 
P&L and a better-than-expected outlook. The breadth of the performance is 
encouraging, with strength across both the Crocs and HEYDUDE brands alongside 
better margin delivery. Share repurchases was also a bright spot in the quarter. 
Against a backdrop of muted investor expectations in the footwear sector, we 
believe the combination of stronger underlying brand trends (including the inflection 
to HEYDUDE brand growth) and the improved forward outlook should be 
well-received. That said, we look for a better understanding of the North America 
Crocs brand trend on today’s conference call, which declined -2.1% Y/Y in F3Q and 
remains under pressure overall, though we acknowledge some noise as a result of a 
partner channel shift within the results. We also note that CROX is not embedding 
tariff refunds in their guidance or non-GAAP numbers in FY26, creating a cleaner 
base for FY27. On the conference call, we look for further color on: (1) Drivers of 
Crocs brand performance, including the cadence across North America and

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.  
M&A Rank

across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring 
companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2
