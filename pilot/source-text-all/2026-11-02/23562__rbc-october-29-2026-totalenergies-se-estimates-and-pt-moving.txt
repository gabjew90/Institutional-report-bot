EQUITY RESEARCH MODEL UPDATE
October 29, 2026
TotalEnergies SE
Estimates and PT moving higher
Our view: We update our estimates following TTE's 3Q26 results, as well
as incorporating our latest price deck. Our PT moves from €85 to €90 and
we maintain our Outperform rating.
Key points:
Integrated value chain bears fruit. As highlighted at the CMD, TTE's
integration across the upstream and downstream continued to be
beneficial in a supportive 3Q26 macro environment. Refining strength and
strong gas trading results were a feature of 3Q results, and look set to
repeat into the fourth quarter. Elsewhere, in the upstream, the Middle East
impacts on TTE, while fluid, have notably moved much lower compared to
the start of the conflict (~15% of production at 1Q26 results, currently <5%)
meaning the negative earnings impact is minimal at the group level.
Sticking to the distribution framework. Despite the pre-announcement
of the 4Q26 and 1Q27 buybacks, management reinforced the message,
noting the company was willing to top up distributions out to the end of
the year, in order to reach the 40% payout ratio (on CFFO). We incorporate
a special dividend announcement in 4Q26 (€0.2/sh, $507m), to be paid
in 1Q27, which brings our payout ratio to in line with guidance by 1Q27
(rolling 12m). With gearing moving below 10%, TTE has breathing room to
maintain elevated distributions given the supportive FCF outlook and we
forecast $9bn worth of buybacks in 2027 ($6.3bn in 2026).
Estimate changes: We update our estimates for continued strength in
refining margins, with most of our estimate changes driven by our updated
price deck which calls for $91/bbl Brent in 2026 and $88/bbl in 2027.
Our price target moves from €85 to €90, with TTE still trading at a slight
premium to the sector, at a 2027E EV/DACF of 5.4x vs 4.7x peer average
with an in-line FCF yield (10.6%), both assuming forward curve pricing.

Valuation
We value TotalEnergies based on a 50/50 weighting between
SOTP and normalized EV/DACF using a multiple of 8.0x – in
line with its historical multiple. On this basis, our normalised
EV/DACF is €91.2 per share, and our SOTP is at €87.6, with
a weighted average of €89.4, rounded up to €90 per share,
which supports our Outperform rating.
Upside scenario
We assume $100/bbl Brent in 2027, as well as mid-cycle
refining & chemicals earnings, which assumes a strong
recovery vs. 2020. With this assumption our upside scenario
is €115.
Downside scenario
We assume $50/bbl Brent in 2027, as well as weaker refining,
chemicals and marketing earnings nearer to recession levels in
the downstream, using earnings in ’08-09 as reference points.
With this assumption, our downside scenario is €60.
Investment summary
We rate TotalEnergies shares Outperform.
Amid broader market turmoil, a key positive attribute of
the TTE investment is its defensiveness, on show through
the worst of the pandemic when it was the only European
major to not cut the dividend. The management team has
navigated the company well through various downturns over
the past decade while also showcasing strength in executing
counter-cyclical activity. In bull markets, this could mean
the management team is more cautious than peers, but
this cautious approach has been the right one on several
occasions.
Looking beyond the near-term, amid evolving views on the
energy transition, we think focus is likely to shift to who can
grow underlying cash flow medium term without support
from the macro. In recent years, TTE has portrayed itself as
the ‘growthy’ European major, with less emphasis on winding
down its oil and gas business to build its new transition
engine(s) than peers. The company is currently executing on a
number of upstream projects that look set to deliver growth
into the 2030 time period.
Risks to rating and price target
The principal risks to our price target and rating include:
downside risk on the oil price, high exposure to Africa, which
remains exposed to civil unrest, and risk with respect to
operations in Russia due to the current environment.
