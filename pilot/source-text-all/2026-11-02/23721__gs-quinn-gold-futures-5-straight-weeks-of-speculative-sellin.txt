• Per Commitment of Traders, speculative Gold selling continued at a faster pace over October 20th - 27th.
Managed Money, Other, and Non-Reportable sold $8bn, marking the 5th straight week of bearish flows
for a cumulative $19.5bn since September 22nd. Liquidation ($5.2bn) and new shorts ($2.8bn)
contributed.
• Categorically, Other and Non-Reportable dominated, accounting for ~95% of the total. Other gross long
selling ($4bn) was most significant.
• This helped explain lackluster Gold price action, despite the moderation in real rates. During the
observation window, Gold slipped 1.6%, seemingly ignoring the 11bp drop in US 10 year real yields.
However, the period also included Brent Oil (4.1%) and Dollar (0.6%) strength. Fear of sovereign
liquidity management and Dollar resilience potentially sparked some of the Other unwinds. Consider the
rolling 6 month correlations for Other gross longs versus DXY and Other gross longs versus the 1st Brent
contract were moderately negative.
• Given still elevated Other positioning, the set-up remained challenging. While Managed Money net
positioning stayed relatively low, Other gross longs on October 27th were still above the 2 year median in
notional terms and as a percentage of open interest.
• Interestingly, Gold's weakness extended into month-end alongside higher Brent Oil prices. From October
27th - 30th, Gold lost 4.3%. Another jump in Brent (3.7%) seemingly overshadowed retracements in DXY
and real yields. Changes in the option surface displayed hedging and/or outright downside
implementation. Gold 3 month implied volatility and normalized 25 delta put-call skew richened, with the
latter reaching an 85% 1 year rank.
