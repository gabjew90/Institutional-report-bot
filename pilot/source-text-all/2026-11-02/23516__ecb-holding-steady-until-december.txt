ECB: Holding steady until December 
MARKET LINES 
The ECB left rates unchanged on Thursday, as expected, while keeping the door open to another 
hike in December. Although the energy shock continues to pose upside risks to inflation, the central 
bank acknowledged that tighter financial conditions will also weigh on growth and prices. New ECB 
staff estimates suggest that higher bond yields could cumulatively reduce growth by 1 percentage 
point and inflation by 0.9 percentage points in 2027-28 relative to the September baseline. Tighter 
financing conditions are therefore already acting as a brake on the economy, although this does 
not rule out further rate increases. The debate over French sovereign stress also highlighted the 
limits of the ECB’s support. Christine Lagarde reiterated that the institution has tools to address 
disorderly market moves that threaten monetary policy transmission, but neither named a specific 
instrument nor indicated that intervention was being considered. The lack of a more explicit signal 
disappointed investors, and the OAT-Bund spread widened again following her comments. 
The meeting was also marked by Lagarde’s announcement that she intends to step down in 
February 2027, adding institutional uncertainty to an already challenging outlook. Attention now 
turns to Friday’s data, including preliminary third-quarter GDP figures for the euro area and October 
inflation readings across several member states. These releases should help clarify the balance 
between slowing growth and persistent inflation pressures ahead of the ECB’s next decision in 
December.

► 
Marvell is reportedly in advanced talks to acquire Lumentum in an all-stock deal, although 
neither company has confirmed and exact terms remain undisclosed. From an industrial 
standpoint, the deal makes sense: Lumentum’s lasers and circuit switches would pair with 
Marvell’s DSPs to create a vertically integrated optics platform against Broadcom and Coherent.

the position of Nvidia, a shareholder in both companies and a major Lumentum customer,

continued strength at AWS, where growth accelerated for a sixth consecutive quarter (+39% YoY).
