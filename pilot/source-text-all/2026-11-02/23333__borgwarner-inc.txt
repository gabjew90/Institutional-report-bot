Key takeaways and implications 
We believe the company’s report was relatively solid compared to 
tier 1 peers that have already reported (many of which have lowered 
full year guidance), although more mixed on an absolute basis. 
Specifically, BorgWarner reported 3Q26 adjusted operating income, 
margin and EPS above the Street (FactSet), while revenue was about 
in line. The company increased its 2026 adjusted EPS guidance 
range due to buybacks and a lower tax rate. The company 
maintained its adjusted operating margin guidance despite 
modestly lowering its revenue outlook.  
Importantly, management commented that it is making good 
progress in developing its turbine generator and BESS products, with 
the company highlighting that backlog and demand for its turbine 
generator continues to be strong. Management also highlighted its 
bi-directional microgrid inverter, including that it has now shipped 
samples to seven customers and begun quoting with its first major 
customer. Moreover, the company announced an expansion of its 
datacenter/energy application product set with a PSU (power 
supply unit). 
We maintain our Buy rating on the stock. We believe the company 
remains on track for solid revenue and profit growth. Specifically, we 
believe BorgWarner’s continued progress on new business awards in 
autos sets the company up to return to outgrowth in the medium 
term (with the company expecting modest outgrowth in 2027 and 
more meaningful outgrowth in 2028). This is highlighted by awards 
for both its foundational and eProducts portfolio including wins on 
higher CPV electric and hybrid platforms, and awards across 
geographies with Chinese and Western OEMs. Importantly, we think 
that BorgWarner has good participation on Chinese export volumes. 
Finally, we expect its planned expansion into the datacenter market 
to be a key driver of growth starting in 2027.

Results 
BorgWarner reported 3Q26 revenue of $3,487 mn (down 4% qoq; down 3% yoy 
organically) which was above GS at $3,464 mn and about in line with the Street at 
$3,497 mn. 
Adjusted operating income in the quarter was $381 mn (10.9% of revenue), above both 
GS at $369 mn (10.7% of revenue) and the Street at $372 mn (10.6% of revenue). 
Operating EPS of $1.39 was $0.16 above GS at $1.23 and $0.14 above the Street at 
$1.25. 
FCF was $195 mn. The company used ~$35 mn for dividends and $175 mn for share 
repurchases this quarter. 
The company noted that it executed a debt tender and make-whole redemption to 
reduce its gross debt balance by $864 million during the quarter. 
Products and awards updates 
For its turbine generator product that is applicable for the datacenter market, the 
company highlighted that customer interest remains strong with external 
demonstrations planned in 4Q, with management still expecting >$300 mn in sales next 
year. Recall the company had announced a turbine generator product and award with its 
4Q25 results. 
BorgWarner reiterated that it is actively quoting a battery energy storage system 
product, and expects this to be ready for 2027 production. 
The company noted that it has shipped samples of its bi-directional microgrid inverter 
to seven customers and begun quoting with its first major customer. The company 
reiterated that it expects to have SOP ready for 2027. Management also announced its 
new power supply unit (PSU) product that it believes will help it expand its TAM with 
demand in the whitespace. 
In autos, the company highlighted multiple customer awards including an engine control 
module for a global OEM’s new ICE pickup truck platform, three conquest turbochargers 
awards with a global powertrain leader, its first aluminum-wire eMotor program award 
with a major Chinese OEM for hybrid vehicles, and three eMotor awards with leading 
OEMs in China for EREVs, BEVs, and hybrid applications. 
On growth over market, the company discussed that it continues to expect modest 
growth over market in 2027 for its auto revenue driven by business awarded over the 
last 3 years. Management expects more meaningful outgrowth in 2028.  
Outlook 
The company now expects its weighted vehicle production markets in CY26 will be down 
2% to down 1% (from down 3% to about flat prior) with a slightly different regional mix 
(with Europe better but offset by weaker China and North America). 
BorgWarner adjusted its CY26 revenue guidance to $14.0 bn-$14.2 bn from $14.0 
bn-$14.3 bn prior, which compares to both the Street and GS prior at $14.2 bn. The 
updated yoy organic growth outlook is now -4.0% to -2.5% from -3.5% to -1.5% prior.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.

companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 
representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard

factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.

on an Investment List is determined by a stock’s total return potential relative to its coverage universe. Any stock not assigned as a Buy or a Sell on an 
Investment List with an active rating (i.e., a  stock that is not Rating Suspended, Not Rated, Early-Stage Biotech, Coverage Suspended or Not Covered), is 
deemed Neutral. Each region manages Regional Conviction Lists, which are selected from Buy rated stocks on the respective region’s Investment Lists 
and represent investment recommendations focused on the size of the total return potential and/or the likelihood of the realization of the return across 
their respective areas of coverage.  The addition or removal of stocks from such Conviction Lists are managed by the Investment Review Committee or 
other designated committee in each respective region and do not represent a change in the analysts’ investment rating for such stocks.    
Total return potential represents the upside or downside differential between the current share price and the price target, including all paid or 
anticipated dividends, expected during the time horizon associated with the price target. Price targets are required for all covered stocks. The total 
return potential, price target and associated time horizon are stated in each report adding or reiterating an Investment List membership.
