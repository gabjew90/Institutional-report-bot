UBS US Mid Term Elections Liquidity
Predictions
Harriet Stewart
02-Nov-2026, 13:12 ET (EST)
• With US Midterm Elections approaching on November 3rd, UBS Futures Execution desk has looked at
how liquidity metrics such as volume, volatility, top of book size and average spread evolved during the
equivalent days in the 2022 cycle and included predictions for 2026. Liquidity estimates are derived using a
historical event-adjustment approach. A baseline for each liquidity metric, including daily volume, bid-ask
spread, top-of-book size and daily volatility, is first calculated using the 15 trading days preceding the event.
Historical occurrences of the same event are then analyzed to determine the relative change in each metric on
the event date and subsequent trading days compared with their respective pre-event baselines. The resulting
historical variation profile is applied to the current baseline to estimate liquidity conditions during the event
window.*
• Based on our estimates, post-election liquidity may peak on Thursday November 5th across much of the
Financial Futures complex, including Treasuries, Bunds and FX futures. Based on our estimates, energy
futures activity may instead peak on Election Day itself.
• Similarly, volatility may remain elevated beyond Election Day: Based on 2022 trends, volatility in Treasuries,
Bunds, Energy and Equity Index Futures could peak on November 5th. For FX, Precious Metals and
Short-Term Rates, based on 2022 trends, the peak could be November 10th – this suggests that the execution
window may extend into the days following the election rather than being concentrated solely on November
3rd.
