US Daily: Early Signs of a Tougher Labor Market for Workers Displaced from AI-Exposed Jobs
• We find that workers displaced from AI-exposed jobs face more difficult transitions back into
employment than other displaced workers. They take about a month longer to find new jobs and are
roughly 10pp more likely to remain unemployed a year after displacement—effects comparable in
magnitude to those seen in earlier waves of technological change.
Haleon (HLN.L) - Guidance cut due to unexpected China and LatAm headwinds; EPS cut by 3%
• Haleon reported weak Q3 results with 2.6% organic sales growth, below company-compiled consensus
at +3.2% (first take here).
• The shares dropped heavily on the back of a cut to guidance related to two events not previously
communicated: 1) the ceasing of importation of Centrum OTC products in China (c.£75m FY25 sales)
until further notice, which implies a c.70bps headwind on a full-year basis, all else equal, and 2) a
voluntary change to route to markets in LatAm creating a further £15-20m headwind to sales in Q4.
Beyond the miss in organic sales growth in Asia Pacific (+3.9%) and in Europe (-0.6%), we flag the
ongoing strength in Oral Health at +8.8% (38% of group sales in 9M).

adidas (ADSGn.DE) - 3Q26 results underscore continued apparel momentum, albeit lifestyle footwear
challenges persist; TP unchanged at €180, remain Neutral
• Adidas' 3Q26 results highlighted ongoing momentum the brand is seeing in apparel which grew +19%
cFX yoy driven in part by originals apparel +45% yoy, as well as in performance footwear and apparel
where the brand continued to grow robustly with Running +19% yoy, Football +13% yoy and Training
+17% yoy.
• On the other hand, the lifestyle footwear market remains challenged given excess wholesale inventory
levels in the US and Europe which contributed to flat footwear growth overall, with management noting
that the lifestyle footwear market may begin to strengthen again globally in 2H27.
• Looking ahead, we continue to believe investors will remain cautious pending greater visibility on a
potential inflection in footwear growth and the extent to which Adidas can lap strong apparel comparatives
in 2027. In this context, we retain a €180 12m target price and Neutral rating.

HelleniQ Energy (HEPr.AT) - Risk-reward more balanced after a strong run; down to Neutral
• We downgrade Helleniq Energy to Neutral from Buy, while maintaining our €20.0 12-month price target.
Fundamentally, our view of the company remains positive: Helleniq Energy is one of the best-positioned
European refiners to benefit from structurally tight product markets, with c.50% exposure to middle
distillates, strong operational performance and substantial FCF generation.
• However, following the material share-price re-rating, we see a more balanced risk-reward from here. At
€18.71/share, our €20 price target implies 7% upside vs 30% average for our Buy-rated names in the EU
Energy space.
ASM International (ASMI.AS): Robust growth opportunities underpinned by leading-edge Logic/Foundry
demand
• In this report we update our estimates and 12M PT for ASM International following its 3Q26 results.
• Key takeaways from the results call include:1) Advanced Logic/Foundry expected to be the key growth
driver in 2027, 2) Memory demand remains supported by HBM-related capacity additions, with limited
impact from potential delays in transition to 4F2, 3) Gross-margin trajectory remains constructive despite
component-cost inflation, supported by pricing, supply-chain initiatives and operating efficiencies, and 4)
China demand remains robust, supported by a broader mix of end markets, although the implications of
the MATCH Act are still to be seen.
