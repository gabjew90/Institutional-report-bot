Headlines
 
▪ 
Real GDP increased 
at an annualized 3.4% in the third quarter in the initial print 
– a notable improvement
 from 2.2% in the second quarter
. 
 
▪ 
Investment, consumer spending, and government spending 
were the growth
 
drivers , partially offset by a decrease in exports.
 
 
▪ 
Imports surged 19.7%, primarily reflecting the AI and data center construction 
buildout.  
 
▪ 
The personal consumption 
expenditure
 (PCE ) price index rose 2.1% in the third 
quarter , down sharply 
from 5.0% in the second quarter
. The core PCE 
price 
index, excluding food and energy, slowed to 
2.5% from 3.3% in the previous 
quarter .  
 
▪ 
PNC’s baseline forecast is for real GDP growth to 
remain around 2.0% through 
the second half
 of next year .  
 
▪ 
PNC expects the 
Federal Open Market Committee 
(FOMC) to hike the fed 
funds rate by 25 basis points in December and March amid elevated inflation
.
