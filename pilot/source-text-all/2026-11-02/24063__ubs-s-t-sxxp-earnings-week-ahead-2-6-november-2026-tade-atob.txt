SXXP Earnings — Week Ahead 2–6
November 2026
Tade Atobatele
02-Nov-2026, 05:15 ET (EST)
Hello all,
The earnings calendar eases next week, both in reporter count and market-cap coverage, with 93 SXXP
companies representing c.13% of the index due to report. Activity builds through Tuesday and Wednesday
before peaking on Thursday, when 46 names, almost half the week’s calendar, report.
The largest sector exposure sits in Communication Services, with c.43% of sector market cap reporting,
almost entirely on Thursday. Deutsche Telekom anchors that session. Alongside it, National Grid and Engie
put Utilities in focus, with c.28% of the European sector due to report over the week.
By number of reporters, Industrials take the lead: 23 names covering c.15% of sector market cap, including
Rheinmetall on Thursday. In Healthcare, Novo Nordisk on Wednesday precedes Siemens Healthineers on
Thursday, with c.18% of the sector reporting overall.
US read-across
Counts refer to SXXP reporters; key US names are included separately
Technology provides the main US read-through early in the week. Apple and Palantir report on Monday,
followed by AMD on Tuesday and Qualcomm on Wednesday. AI spending and data-centre infrastructure key
themes to watch.
Thursday's Utilities focus extends across the Atlantic, with substantial US sector exposure reporting alongside
the European names. Elsewhere, Amgen and Pfizer on Tuesday offer additional Healthcare context ahead of
Novo Nordisk.
Daily focus
• Monday — 2 names: A quiet European opening across Healthcare and Industrials, but a busier US slate
with Apple and Palantir .
• Tuesday — 17 names: Ferrari leads the European focus. Across the Atlantic, watch AMD, Arista
Networks, Amgen, and Pfizer.
• Wednesday — 21 names: Novo Nordisk is the headline European event, with Qualcomm among key US
reporters.
• Thursday — 46 names: The week's busiest session brings Deutsche Telekom, National Grid, Engie,
Rheinmetall and Siemens Healthineers.
• Friday — 7 names: A quieter finish, with Financials and Industrials accounting for most of the European
reporters.
