UK Budget: More Tweak Than Treat 
UK The Budget delivered tweaks rather than wholesale change, deferring tougher fiscal decisions. 
While macro impacts look limited, our analysts identify meaningful sector implications including 
for Banks, Utilities, Gaming, Defence and Housebuilders. 
Initiations & Rating Changes 
PPC We initiate at Buy with PPC providing the highest growth opportunity across our Euro Utilities 
coverage at a sector-average valuation. SE Europe has several attractive structural dynamics and 
PPC's strong balance sheet gives it the firepower to capitalise. 
Orion Nubeqa exclusivity extension and pipeline optionality remain underappreciated, leaving 
valuation skewed to the upside despite strong YTD performance. March ’27 litigation could extend 
US exclusivity to 2036. Upgrade to Buy. 
Danone We upgrade to Hold following the de-rating to ~15x P/E with risk-reward now more 
balanced, though we remain cautious on Danone's fundamentals (incl. US Dairy and China 
Nutrition). 

SCA Following a 22% rally, improved containerboard pricing is increasingly reflected in 
expectations, while further upside requires a recovery in pulp and wood. With 2027 expectations 
running ahead we downgrade to Hold. 
GSK We downgrade to Hold and see limited re-rating potential, despite GSK's valuation discount, 
given reduced earnings momentum, launch execution risk and fewer pipeline catalysts. 
Veolia We reinstate at Hold with Veolia's relatively low valuation reflecting its low single digit 
growth outlook as boosters under-deliver. With mix shifting towards lower-margin activities and the 
FY27 EBITDA target requiring acceleration, risk-reward looks balanced. 
Best of the Rest 
Genmab Success in all three key catalysts, with the announcement this week of solid Phase III 
headlines for peto. We retain Buy (Franchise Pick) with the result further de-risking the currently 
overlooked locally advanced setting. 
Schneider Electric We reiterate Buy with strong execution offsetting PTC deal concerns. Another 
guidance upgrade, financing progress and software channel checks de-risk the transaction, while 
improved PTC performance strengthens the timing and valuation case. 
Global Highlights 
Japan - Quant TOPIX restructuring puts 683 stocks at risk of removal by July 2028. We highlight 
companies where free-float improvements, crossholding unwinds or parent buyouts could create 
upside ahead of the August 2027 review. 
US - Power & Utilities The sector sell-off continues despite improving pipeline and power 
visibility. Multiples have compressed, risk appetite remains low, but widening valuation dispersion 
and upcoming catalysts are creating selective opportunities. 
India - NSE Market leadership, diversification and new growth levers support 15% revenue and 
16% EPS CAGR through FY29, with further upside potential. Its premium to BSE looks justified. 
Initiate at Buy.
