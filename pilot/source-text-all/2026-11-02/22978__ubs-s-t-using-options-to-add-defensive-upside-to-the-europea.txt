Using options to add defensive upside to
the European AI Ecosystem
Jamie Briggs
29-Oct-2026, 08:13 ET (EDT)
• UBS European Equity Strategist Gerry Fowler is bullish on what he calls the ‘second phase of industrial
acceleration’. Put simply: the supply-side catch-up trade, the spend to build sufficient capacity to catch-up with
the scale of equipment and materials demand from the AI trade. Gerry outlines stocks exposed to the theme at
various different tiers in fig 3 of the note; he distils this to a key list of Bu-rated names exposed to the theme:
Siemens, ASML, Schnieder Electric, Anglo American, Air Liquide, VAT Group & Comet, Inficon, and
SPIE.
• This week Industrials analyst Andre Kukhnin highlights the value implied in European Industrials after
October’s AI related correction. On average Electricals are pricing c160bps lower mid-cycle growth now vs 2nd
October. Looking at implied mid-term growth across Electrification now vs the peak at the start of October
(using reverse DCF), Nexans, Schneider, Siemens Energy and Prysmian have been impacted the most -
now pricing in ½ of less growth vs peak.
• Q3 reporting so far have supported the fundamental momentum: Schneider reported Q3 organic sales
growth of 16.9% vs consensus 13.9%, UBS upgraded forecasts;  ASML results pointed to a strong backlog,
UBS raised numbers; Air Liquide reported strong pricing, leading to a small topline beat; orders form VAT
Group and Comet were both strong enough to point towards upside pressure to consensus forecasts; Inficon
beat and raised with orders reaching a new record; Nexans beat on its grid and copper sales businesses;
Prysmian’s electrification division drove its beat and raise. US Hyperscalers reporting remains supportive
enough, but a little uncertainty on 2027 might make options on suppliers the preferred route: META tightened
the FY26 capex guidance towards the top end; Alphabet was weak after hours but largely on search weakness
and held capex guidance.
• UBS Systematic Advisory data shows investors are quickly returning to the AI Industrials (UBXEAIIN) trade
as the sector started reporting Q3.
