China
* Mkt wrap - China market had a choppy morning session but managed to surge in the afternoon to close
much higher as investors trading on expectation of more policy support measures. STAR50, Chinext and
CSI300 ETFs saw trading volume surge on the day. Surprisingly, growth outperformed high-divy SOEs,
and STAR50 ETFs saw the most inflows. Looks like investors are betting on a broad based rebound after
policy bottom, and positioning themselves in high-beta sectors. Sector wise, AI short-video theme was

most active and sending media sector +7.58% higher. Tech hardware names traded mixed. Dongshan
Precision got lifted with Q3 earnings beat. (one of the few major Hard Tech names with solid Q3
numbers), while Optic and CCL names still trading below water. Robotics traded weak in the morning but
regained momentum in the afternoon. Leaderdrive +5.83% post earning. Financials traded mixed with
high-beta Brokers surging while money rotation out of Banks. On the flip side, Gold names traded much
lower inline with gold spot price weakness. Coal followed.
