Markets On The Move - APAC Equity Flows
Sell Skewed; Locals Pay USDCNH Points
Aditi Samajpati
30-Oct-2026, 01:58 ET (EDT)
Cash Equity:
Japan – Flows are net flat with buying in defensives, railways, and shippers vs. selling in technology and
consumer staples. (Sarath Kutty)
Hong Kong/China – Flows are 53% to sell, including 5% in shorts. The desk is selling energy, tech, and
industrials vs. buying banks, insurance and healthcare. (Max Fung)
Taiwan – Flows are 72% sellers as TAIEX dove on a knee-jerk reaction after Intel slumped 23% as its results
missed expectations, losing 46K and the 6-month moving average at 45314. Tech/AI was the worst hit with
limit-down in Delta (2308) and Chroma (2360). The desk is better selling semiconductor bluechips and
offloading networking vs. buying mature foundry and server rail kit.  (Jessica Wu)
Korea – KOSPI fell more than 5%, with a notable sell down in Tech: foreign investors offloaded $675 mn and
local institutional investors $297 mn amid investor anxiety around AI cash burn into month-end. Market
breadth is at 7:1 decliners. (Hyuntai Lew)
ASEAN – Flows are 63% better to sell, most active in Thailand. Industrials are under selling pressure in both
the Philippines and Singapore. Utilities are attracting inflows across Thailand, the Philippines and Singapore.
Meanwhile, financials, technology, and telecoms flows are mixed. (Kenneth Sui)
India – UBS pad has an active buy skew, driven by inflows in telecoms, IT, industrials, and financials while
there are two-way cares in banks. (Bhoomi Yagnik)
Australia – Flows are 59% sell skewed, most concentrated in telecoms while the desk is buying financials and
industrials. (Samuel Collins)
FX:
USDCNH eased to 6.6550 with dollar rates moving lower overnight. The USDCNY fix was lowered from
6.7147 to 6.7138, but its beta to the basket remains low at around 20%. Forward points rebounded, while
implied yields edged lower: the desk saw profit-taking interest alongside paying interest from local names.
(Andy Lau)
