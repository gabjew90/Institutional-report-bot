Equity ETPs attracted $5.9B last week, easing from $6.2B previously, with developed market equities accounting for 
$5.5B of inflows, down from $6.0B. While global exposures continued to anchor allocations, regional demand shifted 
noticeably towards Europe. Global DM strategies attracted $3.9B, broadly unchanged from the previous week. 
Europe saw the strongest acceleration, with inflows rising to $1.1B from just $75m, while North American demand 
moderated to $517m from $1.8B. APAC remained in positive territory at $195m, although inflows slowed from 
$547m. Among more specific regional exposures, the Eurozone attracted $509m, up sharply from $39m, while 
Europe returned to inflows of $343m following -$212m of outflows. APAC ex-Japan gathered $56m, down from 
$159m, and North America added $29m.
At the single-country level, the US remained the largest destination, although inflows fell to $562m from $1.8B the 
week before. Japan attracted $169m, followed closely by Switzerland at $168m, while Sweden gathered $44m and 
Australia added $16m. France also returned to marginal inflows of $2m after recording -$79m previously. On the 
outflow side, Canada saw the largest redemptions at -$65m, compared with -$19m the previous week. Italy reversed 
into outflows of -$20m from $6m of inflows, while the UK and Germany recorded modest outflows of -$3m and -$2m 
respectively. Within US size exposures, Large Caps continued to attract the bulk of demand at $496m, although this 
was considerably below the $1.9B recorded previously. Small Caps remained under pressure with outflows of -$118m, 
while Mid Caps saw limited inflows of $3m.

Emerging market equity ETPs attracted $320m, up from $177m the previous week, although flows remained mixed 
across broad, regional and single-country exposures.

positive territory with $101m after recording -$76m of outflows. Single-country exposures remained negative at -
$64m, although selling eased from -$87m previously. Regionally, Latin America led the recovery with $86m of 
inflows, reversing from -$70m, while EM APAC and Europe attracted $8m and $7m respectively.
At the country level, South Korea stood out with $340m of inflows, a sharp increase from $58m the week before. Brazil 
also remained in positive territory, gathering $25m, compared with $37m previously. In contrast, China continued to 
experience substantial selling, with outflows increasing to -$411m from -$339m. India recorded more limited 
outflows of -$4m. The divergence between strong South Korean demand and continued Chinese redemptions was the 
defining feature of single-country EM flows this week.

Active equity ETPs attracted $665m last week, down from $967m previously, as flows into global and North American 
strategies moderated despite stronger demand across Europe and APAC. 
Global exposures remained the largest contributor at $310m, although inflows slowed from $560m the previous 
week. North America followed with $129m, compared with $208m previously. Elsewhere, European active strategies 
saw inflows double to $108m from $54m, while APAC gathered $80m, up sharply from $18m. EM strategies remained 
positive at $38m, although demand eased considerably from $126m the week before.
 By investment approach, Alpha-Seeking strategies continued to account for the majority of flows, attracting $504m, 
down from $713m. Outcome strategies gathered $129m, compared with $162m previously, while Exposure products 
recorded marginal outflows of -$2m after attracting $16m the week before.
 Within Alpha-Seeking, CORE remained the principal source of demand at $496m, although inflows moderated from 
$665m. Precision strategies attracted $14m, down from $32m, while Thematics/Megatrends gathered $6m. In 
contrast, Factors reversed into outflows of -$12m after recording $10m of inflows previously. 
 Dividend strategies continued to dominate Outcome flows, attracting $111m, down from $153m. Other Outcome 
strategies gathered $9m, followed by US Buffer at $5m and Sector strategies at $3m. Within Exposure, Precision 
attracted $1m, while CORE saw marginal outflows of -$3m.

Sustainable equity ETPs returned to positive territory last week, attracting $58m following -$337m of outflows 
previously. The recovery was supported by continued demand across Europe and APAC, although persistent selling in 
North America limited overall inflows.
 Europe led regional allocations at $207m, followed by APAC at $169m. Global strategies attracted $73m, while EM 
returned to inflows of $12m after recording -$207m of outflows the week before. North America remained the main 
source of selling, with outflows of -$402m, although this was an improvement from -$531m previously.
