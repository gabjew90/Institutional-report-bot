What’s the latest data on mega projects and construction starts? How is mega project activity
trending? In this note, we look into these topics, update the latest Dodge construction starts
data, and provide and update on mega projects. Togain recurring access to the excel-
based version of this data feed, please reach out to your Bernstein salesperson.
Construction volumes remain bifurcated: AI is partially offsetting weakness in
parking and warehouses. In Sep, Non-Residential construction starts declined ~5% Y/
Y in square footage, an improvement from Aug’s ~15% decline. Despite total non resi sq
ft remains negative, breadth improved meaningfully as the percentage of end-markets
posting positive square-footage growth increased from ~45% in Aug to ~70% in Sep.
Warehouses and parking declined by ~15% and ~30% Y/Y, respectively, partially offset by
office and bank buildings (which include data centers) and schools which increased by 20%
and 40% Y/Y, respectively.
Data centers have overtaken LNG as the largest source of mega-project activity on
a TTM basis and now represent almost 1/3. The most notable shift in the latest data
is that data centers now represent ~30% of trailing twelve-month mega-project starts,
surpassing LNG at ~23% and becoming the largest source of mega-project activity. Mega
projects accounted for ~20% of Starts in Sep, totaling $15B (+~25% Y/Y). August's largest
projects included the ~$4.6B South Valley Data Center in Nevada and the ~$4.3B Beacon
Point Data Center in Texas, reinforcing the continued concentration of spending toward
AI infrastructure. Over the last twelve months, data centers, LNG and public infrastructure
collectively accounted for roughly two-thirds of all mega-project activity, continuing to
reshape the construction landscape.
The deceleration in the non resi category decline rate and the improved breadth of projects
with a positive growth rate should be supportive for machinery, rental and infrastructure-
exposed companies such as CAT, URI, SUNB, PWR and ETN.

European Chemicals
We rate Arkema Market-Perform. Our initiation is linked here, and our upgrade is linked here. We see a key part of the Arkema
bull case as an improvement in US construction, as performance is correlated with US starts by $ value ex-infrastructure. We’d
read the September starts trends as mixed to negative. Arkema’s larger exposure is to residential starts, which decreased -3%
in value, and still faces challenging housing affordability conditions and possible further interest rises (Exhibit 23). In non-resi,
September starts remained strong (+16%), largely driven by office and bank buildings (+104%) but as discussed previously,
this category includes data center construction and the latter is a very small opportunity for Arkema with ~€100m of targeted
sales by 2030 (1% of 2025 sales). Other non-resi buildings still saw 15% growth which is supportive, but overall that does not
change our low expectations of a recovery in US construction relevant to Arkema; to us the more relevant question is becoming
whether we are seeing structural improvements in other parts of the business (see Arkema: Structural improvement versus
cyclical worries).
