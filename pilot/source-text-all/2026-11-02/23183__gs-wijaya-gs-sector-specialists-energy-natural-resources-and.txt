Three Stocks
PBF. Desk had a positioning score of 7/10 into the print – and the story here (aside from crack beta / crude
slate) has really been around the cash build / net debt inflection that was highlighted at 2Q results. 15%
EPS beat vs Street – but would expect there to be most focus on this: total net debt reduction of 150%
compared to 2Q26 and now sitting on $2.5 bn of cash for a company that’s $8.6 bn market cap (net cash of
~$430 mn). There’s healthy debate on use of cash for the entire Refining group – where feedback remains
more mixed on repurchasing shares vs building cash vs investing in smaller projects. Expecting this to be
question 1/2/3 on the call at 8:30 AM. The bar is high for the Refiners – especially for the remaining prints
– even after some of the relative underperformance vs the broader Energy complex. Specialist inbounds
continue to point to crack softness (seasonal roll in gasoline, fine) – but would be mindful of the crude
environment – WCS diff now $31/b… I would highlight this quote from the PSX call yesterday which I
think speaks to where the puck is going next on Refining conversations: ‘So, internally, what we talk about
is heavy crude is essentially a race to the bottom for both Canadian and Venezuelan, and even high-sulfur
fuel oil. So, as you know, weaker heavy differentials play into our kit. Canadian crude is currently favored,
and we import more Canadian crude into the U.S. than any other company.’
FTI. Specialists have been leaning more negative here for some time and I would think the results largely
reinforce the cautious view. Orders have been the most topical for the stock (and have been for the last two
quarters) – where Subsea orders were light again ($2.05 bn vs expectations closer to $2.5 bn). There’s an
ongoing question around market share losses to peers – and would think this will be topical on the call.
The FY2027 early guidance is generally in line with Street on Subsea revenues and adj EBITDA margin of
23% at the midpoint is on par – so the question from here likely to be: ‘stock trades at 11.5x EBITDA on
FY2 – and if we are seeing an offshore cycle / potential for more O&G capex – are there more attractive
relative names?’
DTM. Quiet quarter expected – and quiet quarter delivered. Conversations on the desk have skewed more
negative on the setup for the stock into EPS – expecs were there for an in line quarter, FY guidance raise
and MIST South Phase 1 update (check, check and check). Question will really be ‘what can they say on
the call that gives confidence on next leg of growth?’ Order backlog will be more in focus as we get closer
to YE / annual update. The stock has underperformed WMB and KMI by ~20% YTD … early inbounds
this morning highlighting no real enthusiasm yet.
