GS INDUSTRIALS debates today…
 
AERO… MTX -2% surprisingly weak, margins being blamed, hearing only limited defence in the incoming.
Some concern the drivers of cash are non-repeat (e.g. military pre-payments). Call (starting now) is important
here with CMD a month away. Latest from Sam here. Civil after-market underperforming all of A&D today
despite oil down on the day…
 
AIRBUS +3% seems fair and should, in theory, now do the year-end rip (the SEAG is a beauty and delivery data
mechanically is going to ramp hard in coming weeks). Only pushback is that consensus expecting +100
deliveries or 11-12% yoy growth in deliveries in 2027 is a high bar (certainly guidance likely to be set more
conservatively). More from Sam here.
 
DEFENCE +2%...KOG Q3 beat helping today and BAE rebounding again on back of UK plane. It does now
stand to reason that Eu Defence will go up on ME de-escalation (eases fiscal concerns). Sam out with note
today pointing out that both valuations and positioning are right at the lows – “the GS European Defence basket
hedge-fund long/short crowding ratio has fallen to c.0.7x, below its historical 10th percentile and down from a
peak of c.3.0x in early 2024” - note.
 
PRYSMIAN +4%… rallying the call after some push and pull earlier today. Stock rallying despite some of the
cautious takes I think is probably a positive sign. On the call, saying margins will improve in Q4 over Q3, very
upbeat on profitability of fibre & Electrification going forwards. Bulls arguing there is every reason to see further
upgrades and DS is early in the cycle. Anecdotally has been more de-risked than other electricals. More from
Daniela here.. 
 
AUTOS... Q4 squeeze upon us? STLAM +15% yesterday, RNO +7% today, VW +2% despite caution on ’27
margins (*VW CFO SAYS HIGHER EV SALES TO WEIGH ON MARGINS Q4, INTO 2027). No one yet
embracing the rally here, but hard to press shorts into year end now that the cuts for ’27 are materialising.
 
TRUCKS…another day of negative news – engcon miss, KBX miss. IMI the exception as they talked to
strength from transport today. Incoming is mostly arguing to defend KBX (temporary order weakness and
prudent project selectivity), and to fade the truck OEMs (Traton & VOLVB in particular).

More results triage and bullets today:
 
EPIROC continuing to fade, -4% or so since the print mid-morning yesterday. Christian sees weakness as
buying oppo (note), with signs of improving execution despite order slippage (and equipment delivery delays).
On the call, mgmt saying efficiencies are coming through in T&A, and in mining demand v strong in underground
with robust pipeline, gold customers not blinking despite lower price.
 
GSXEPULP has been coming under pressure again… UPM miss yesterday and then SW -5% yesterday.
Gabriel on SW: “weak print, but outlook into ’27 still looks promising” - note. On the call, mgmt arguing N Am
volumes recovering in Nov. And on Europe, think market will remain a little more rational than feared, with
existing players driving the capacity additions (would be self-defeating to disrupt the market).
 

PRYSMIAN beat & raise, details more mixed… Ebitda beat by 7%, driven by 20% beat in Electrification (Encore
finally shining, just as the market starts de-rating all things datacentre). Transmission also small beat, but DS
(fibre) missed expectations a little with top line surprisingly light vs expectations. Power Grid also a miss with
margins continuing to drag their feet. FCF is light in the quarter (albeit FY FCF guide raised). Ebitda guide is
raised to 3.1bn mid-point, implying 6% upgrades to consensus. More from Daniela: here.
 
AIRBUS after the call… Confidence in the ramp… “we feel reasonably comfortable to be somewhere in the 870
plus or minus 20 with what we see and what we have today”. Tone of the call last night very much underwriting
confidence in the ramp, with all the known risks included in current guide, and everything in place for the
Nov/Dec scramble. Management saying P&W engines remain the “pacing item” but nothing has changed to put
planned ‘27/’28 output at risk. This is driving some relief in the incoming. Other item of note is that the share
buyback announced at CMD is due to start at some point between now and year end. More from Sam: here.
 
MTU Aero in-line Q3 and FCF raise… after results from peers (SAF, GE) have served to lower expectations,
MTU’s FCF upgrade today should be well received ahead of the CMD. Q3 ebit is a -1% miss, with some
weakness in OEM, whilst Commercial MRO is strong as expected. FCF is very strong at 172m vs consensus
109m. Guidance is unchanged for OE, but raised for Spares (up mid-teens vs up low to mid-teens previously)
and raised for MRO (up mid-teens vs up low to mid-teens previously). Cash flow guide is narrowed to 55-60% of
net income from 50-60% previously (consensus at 54% has room to move up). Latest from Sam here. 
 
KNORR BREMSE pre-release last night driven by RVS order weakness, which the company blame on “project
driven fluctuations” and expect recovery in 4Q to €1.2bn order intake (vs consensus at €1.14bn). Rest of the Q3
is a touch light with ebit -1.5% miss. Let me know if you want to speak to Daniela or Meihan on this, per their
note: “ likely to see minor LSD downgrades given the anticipated order recovery in 4Q does not fully
compensate the order miss in 3Q and consensus is already at the top end of updated full year revenue guidance
(narrowed to upper end of range). … we view the project timing issue and weaker RVS orders and a negative
read to rail OEMs in our coverage as Alstom and Siemens”. link .
 
RENAULT Q3 sales 2% beat, guidance maintained… RNO sticking to margin guide around 5.5% and slight
improvement in 2H despite cost headwinds. Scepticism seems likely to persist given the cost backdrop and
challenging competition in Europe (consensus models 5.1% margins in 2h vs 5.2% in 1h). Inventory is guided “to
trend towards the lower end of the 525 – 550k units range”, perhaps implies improvement vs prior comment to
end the year below June 26 level of 546k. European orderbook now at c.2 months of sales (2.1 mths at end of
2Q). => Feedback on RNO has remained very cautious given all the pressures, not sure what there is here to
change that but the sector has been very squeezy on anything “not a warning”.
 
AIR LIQUIDE Q3 sales … comparable growth +3% vs consensus +2.8%. Mixed divisional performance. The
positives are accelerating Electronics growth and broadening IM (hardgoods back to growth) with pricing coming
through (6.5% in the US). EMEA and LI is soft, however, and Healthcare decelerated (company citing portfolio
management actions earlier in the year). Air Liquide expect Q4 growth to be similar to Q3 (cons a touch above
this).
 
VOLKSWAGEN Q3 miss, driven by VW Brand where margins were 2.1% vs consensus 3.1%. Auto cash flow
was a bright spot and came in a touch ahead of expectations, driven by the decreasing investment burden and
portfolio actions. Guidance is unchanged post the recent cut. Overall : compares a little unflatteringly with RNO
(small sales beat) or STLAM (big operating beat vs low numbers), P911 (very in-line, strong cash) or even MBG
