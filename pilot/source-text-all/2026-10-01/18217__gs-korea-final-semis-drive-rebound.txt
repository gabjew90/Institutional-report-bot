MARKET RECAP
KOSPI steadily climbed throughout the session to finish near its 7,000-marker. Foreigners though
extended their 5-day net-selling streak in KOSPI, while local instos and Other Corporates (+$1.2bn;
buyback inflow) helped support the index. Retail investors started the session as net buyers, but ended net
sellers as the index gained into the afternoon. SK HYNIX(000660) +3.2% and SEC(005930) +2.8%
picked up momentum from the late-AM to eventually close higher amid MU's constructive outlook and
strong Sept semiconductor export data. Semi equipment names continued to rally to outperform
Sammy/Hynix, helping KOSDAQ to finish +4.5%. Elsewhere, NC(036570) +10% rallied after its AION2
early access topped Steam's global sales chart. KOSPI turnover was @ -17.5% vs 20-day average. KRW
depreciated by 22bps s to W1,358.8.
MAIN HEADLINES
•
SEMIS (+): SK HYNIX(000660) +3.2% and SEC(005930) +2.8%. Both started off the session sluggish,
but rebounded into the PM. Micron's overnight beat and strong forecast ( coupled with S.Korea's
semiconductor exports surpassing $60bn for the first time, appeared to provide a supportive backdrop for
the sector. Semi equipment names, such as WONIK IPS(240810) +9.1%, LEENO(058470) +10.9%, and
FADU(440110) +9.8% (leading KOSDAQ to 4.5% outperform vs. KOSPI) also rallied in tandem with
stronger price action. Separately, note GIR's 3Q25E preview for SEC (Buy-rated, CL), where they expect
a solid quarter with W100tn+ OP backed by the strong underlying fundamentals in both DRAM and

NAND. SEC's 3Q prelims is expected to report next week (Oct 7/8 expected). Flow-wise, foreigners and
retail investors jointly net sold in both SEC and SK HYNIX, while local instos and Other Corporates
(buyback inflow) ended as net buyers.
•
NC(036570) +10.0%: NC's MMORPG Aion 2 began global early access on Sept 30 and topped Steam's
global sales chart on its first day. The game had also reportedly surpassed 1.3mn pre-launch wishlist
registrations, with its official launch scheduled for Oct 5. GIR 3Q25E Preview: In 3Q, we expect NC's
earnings to be broadly in line with consensus, with revenue of W691bn (+92% YoY/-10% QoQ) and OP of
W81bn (-53% QoQ). AION2 global's Oct. 5 global launch remains the key near-term catalyst, with Steam
wishlist and top-seller rankings of #35 and #1 indicating encouraging pre-launch demand. We maintain
our positive view on NC, as we continue to see stable retained earnings from existing pipeline, as well as
increasingly visible AAA. pipeline.
•
HD MOBIS(012330) +4.1%: HD Mobis announced that it will carve out its lamp business as a 100%
subsidiary and intends to sell it to OPmobility. The lamp business contributed ~2% of Mobis revenue in
2025, but 7% of core-parts revenue. We (GIR) believe the decision by the company underscores the
management direction to prioritize growth in areas such as future mobility and robotics actuators. The
strength today also appeared driven by Appraisal rights which can be exercised at W408,394 per share for
dissenting shareholders that oppose the sale of Mobis' lamp business.

MACRO | SEPTEMBER EXPORTS: Korea's headline exports rose sharply by 18.1% mom sa, up from
1.7% in the prior month and recording the strongest momentum since 1985. The outcome came in well
above our above-consensus forecast. Exports gained broadly across major sectors, with the sharpest gains
seen in semiconductors (+16.4% mom sa, non-annualized) as well as autos and auto parts (+36.9%).
Exports to the US gained the most, rising by 37.1% mom sa, and accounted for roughly 40% of the
headline sequential increase, while exports to other major destinations broadly rose. (GIR)
