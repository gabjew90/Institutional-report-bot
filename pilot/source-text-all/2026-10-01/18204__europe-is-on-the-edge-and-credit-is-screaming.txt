France: the market gets a vote
Goldman's Rich Privorotsky flags France as one to watch today as the 2027 budget lands. Lecornu is
targeting roughly €54bn of adjustment, taking the deficit to 5.0% from 5.4%, versus ~6.5% without new
measures.
But with OAT/Bund around 120bp, the market gets the problem. With no government majority, the
opposition response probably matters more than the budget itself.
The bigger tail may actually be EUR rather than OATs. A genuine confrontation between Paris and
Brussels would have much broader implications for the union.

French domestics have already seen heavy selling, but with positioning skewed and spreads wide, it
may not take much good news to squeeze.
