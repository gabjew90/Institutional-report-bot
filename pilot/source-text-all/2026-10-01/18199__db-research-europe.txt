ADIDAS AG  (ADSGn.DE) - Industry pressures building in 3Q - FY guidance looking less conservative; Tgt 
EUR210 to EUR205. Last Close EUR145.3. Buy. 
Adam Cochrane: adidas has been a frustrating stock for investors with the stock dragged down by the wider 
industry dynamics and a lack of earnings upgrades rather than any operational errors. The 14% 1H cFX sales 
growth was impressive even if this included a benefit from the World Cup (which saw a greater marketing 
investment than anticipated). The Innovation Day highlighted the depth of management, excitement over the 
new product pipeline and the technical expertise in the Performance category. Whilst there is still some World 
Cup benefit to be realised in 3Q we now take a more cautious view on the overall market outlook for 2H. The 
c.6% cFX guidance given at 1H was viewed as conservative. Now it appears more realistic. 
  

  
BMW  (BMWG.DE) - More self-help, less hope - CMD review; Tgt EUR78 to EUR71. Last Close EUR55.32. 
Buy. 
Tim Rokossa: Over the last two days, we attended the CMD of BMW. BMW largely delivered what we expected 
(see our preview note here): a structurally lower medium-term margin profile, but also a more credible path 
to improvement driven primarily by self-help rather than hopes of an industry recovery. We see the investment 
case as partially de-risked, although China remains the key uncertainty: management is taking more decisive 
action on costs, localisation and the dealer network, while still assuming broadly stable volumes and continued 
positive EBIT. The absence of a formal capital-return framework was likely the biggest source of initial investor 
disappointment. However, management indicated that the topic remains under review and reiterated its 
willingness to distribute excess cash.

GREGGS PLC  (GRG.L) - Strong Q3 update – First look; Tgt 1330p to 1420p. Last Close 2028p. Sell. 
Tim Barrett: 3Q LFL sales growth of +3.4% was slightly ahead our 2.9% forecast. This is an acceleration vs. 1.7% 
reported in 2Q (2.5% for 1H). YTD trading is now +2.6% (Bloomberg consensus shows 2.3%). Having opened 
net 34 stores in 1H, it has opened a further 23 net stores this quarter (57 ytd) and retained guidance for 100-
110 net shop openings for the year (cut at the interims). Cost inflation still expected to be +2% but "signs of 
greater inflationary pressure in 2027". It is closing four manufacturing sites at a cash cost of £60m but aiming 
to generate £20m in annual savings from 2028/29 (c.1% of cost base). Improved trading performance in recent 
months and continued strong cost control lead GRG to expect a modestly improved outcome for 2026, 
quantified on the analyst call by the CFO as a mid-single-digit uplift. 
  

GAMBLING  - The carnival is cancelled, for now; Target Price Changes: Allwyn (ALWr.AT) – Hold, Tgt 
EUR13.20 to EUR10.20, Entain PLC (ENT.L) – Buy, Tgt 914p to 872p, Flutter Entertainment (FLUT.N) – Buy, Tgt 
USD148 to USD141. 
Richard Stuber: Ahead of next month's elections, Lula da Silva (Brazil's president) has signed an executive order 
to ban online betting and gaming. The Brazilian gambling market has only been regulated since January 2025 
and was already the third-largest regulated gaming market globally. Whilst all the main operators are abiding 
by the prohibition, it is far from certain that this will be permanently banned: a) it needs to receive 
Congressional approval within 120 days, ie, after the election dust has settled; b) there are already multiple 
legal objections from the c.85 operators who have already acquired a R$30m 5-yr license; c) the government 
receives >$2bn per year in taxes and supports many sports, including at the grassroot level; and d) customers 
are unlikely to stop spending on gambling but simply play with black market operators (which are still highly 
prevalent) who offer no customer protection and provide no tax receipts.

SCOUT24 SE  (G24n.DE) - Reiterating the positives; remains one of our top picks; Tgt EUR126. Last Close 
EUR67. Buy. 
Nizla Naizer: Scout24 will report Q3 results on 4 Nov. As previously discussed, the AI disruption narrative has 
been weighing on Scout24 since late last year and it continues to impact sentiment when new developments 
hit the market. For instance, since the US launch of Meta's Muse, an Agentic AI solution for consumers, the 
major Classified portals are down ~12% on average with Scout24 down ~10%. We believe this is not justified 
and continue to stress the points that keep us positive on Scout24: 1) Scout24 is more than just a real estate 
listings platform - it has built an ecosystem in German real estate with products ranging from subscriptions and 
software, to valuation services supported by proprietary data, and this is hard to replicate, 2) Scout24 already 
has a well-articulated strategy in place to tackle a world that may evolve into searches done by more AI agents, 
and is prepared to be the party in the middle that provides trusted data and vets agents on either side of the 
equation (seekers and real estate agents/landlords).
