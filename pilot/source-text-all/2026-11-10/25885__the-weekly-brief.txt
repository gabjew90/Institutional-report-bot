The Weekly Brief
Thought of the week
Small-cap stock indices currently trade below their long-run median valuations
across the US and Europe. The valuation premium they historically commanded
over large caps has also largely disappeared, as reflected by their latest forward
price to earnings (P/E) ratios. Small-cap stock indices have nonetheless
underperformed in recent years, partly because they tend to carry more
leverage and are more sensitive to higher interest rates than large caps. Yet
small-cap stocks have slightly outperformed the large-cap benchmark in
developed markets in 2026, particularly after the recent technology sector
volatility. If energy prices fall further and financing conditions become less
restrictive, some cyclical pressures may ease, which could help small caps
retain momentum.
