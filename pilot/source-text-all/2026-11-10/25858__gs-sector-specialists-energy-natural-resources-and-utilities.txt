KGS. Has been one of the more debated names in BTM over the last couple of months – particularly
amongst the Midstream community. We have gone through the thought exercise of ‘what am I pricing in
for power’ to ‘what’s the right multiple for compression / how much more upside / downside do I have to
estimates’ to ‘will this just move with the entire BTM trade’. Company reported 3Q EPS this morning and
updated FY guidance – at first glance – this looks good enough (especially with the stock trading at
$41/sh). On FY2026 updates: increased 2026 adj EBITDA guidance range to $840-$860 mn (from
$820-$860 mn), increased 2026 discretionary cash flow guidance to $580-$600 mn, and lowered 2026
power infra spend to $350-$400 mn. Cautious convos highlighted more concerns on (1) base biz front –
specially on cost inflation (lubes) and (2) OEMs flooding the market on the capacity front for power. Has
been a show me story on the power side and the update is quiet here, though don’t think there were high
expectations for any material update. Company is highlighting confidence in long term trajectory for both
power and compression outlooks in the press release. Think there has been little doubt regardless of oil px
– gas coming out of the Permian will likely continue to surprise to the upside on volumes and given
company’s large presence here – can continue to be a relative winner (recall all the pipe FIDs in the last
couple of months out of the basin). Feedback welcome.
NRG. This one will likely be the most in focus today based on the early inbounds. Quarter itself looks
good enough – though worth noting the most of the IPP prints have reported beats for the quarter on adj
EBITDA (VST +11%, CEG +21%). FY2027 guidance – on adj EBITDA looks more in line with Street
expectations (though does not reflect impacts of announced 4.9 GW sale), though worth noting over the
last 3m we have seen estimates grind lower. More mixed feedback on this front: *NRG TO SELL 4.9 GW
OF NAT GAS ASSETS TO LS POWER FOR $4B CASH … bulls indicating ramping a buyback with the
stock trading at ~7x EBITDA is attractive while bears highlighting concerns on read through from sale of
gas assets (ie why not keep and potentially contract out over time). Company is calling out the 1.2 GW of
BYOP for a data center remains on track, no change to LT targets and continued focus on debt reduction +
share buybacks. We think specialists have been leaning more cautious NRG relative to other IPPs into EPS
season. Estimate revisions, headline TX exposure, ERCOT weakness and less PJM insulation have all
been cited in recent convos.
