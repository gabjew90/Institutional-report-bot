3Q26 Fundamentals  Check-in
3Q26 results so far point to continued improvement in credit 
fundamentals, supported by solid earnings growth. Leverage is 
declining across IG, HY, and Loans, while interest coverage and 
cash generation are improving. Gains are broad based, though 
reporting remains skewed toward higher-quality issuers.

 In this report, we provide an early read on the fundamental trends that we typically 
assess in our quarterly fundamentals work based on companies that have reported 
3Q26 results through November 3. At this stage of earnings season, approximately 
~51% of companies across the IG, HY, and Loan universes have reported.
Given that our analysis currently reflects a smaller subset of the overall universe, 
the results may differ from those presented in our prior report and are likely to 
evolve as the remainder of the earnings season progresses. With that caveat in 
mind, the early read has been constructive, with reported results thus far 
pointing to continued improvement in credit fundamentals across the US credit 
markets.

Key Takeaways
 3Q26 results so far across IG, HY, and Loan issuers (51% reported) show generally 
improving credit fundamentals, with broad-based gains across ratings, unlike the 
quality-based strength in prior years.
Quarterly y/y EBITDA growth is positive, currently standing at 9.2%, 9.6%, and 
8.9% across IG, HY, and Loans, respectively.
IG leverage decreased on both a net and gross basis, and interest coverage rose 
to 11.20x, with the difference between BBB and single A gross leverage narrowing 
to 0.5x.
HY credit metrics improved overall, with gross leverage down to 3.21x and FCF-
to-debt up to 11.8%; only capex to sales saw deterioration q/q and only +<0.1ppt.
Loan fundamentals improved on earnings and cash metrics, but net leverage at 
2.86x remains 0.16x above year-ago levels. On a q/q basis, only capex-to-sales 
deteriorated.

3Q26 Current Reporters Update
Credit metrics improved broadly for the 158  HY issuers that have reported to date 
(52% of the universe). Gross leverage fell to 3.21x (-0.14x q/q, -0.24x y/y) and net 
leverage to 2.56x (-0.17x q/q, -0.28x y/y), while interest coverage rose to 5.28x (+0.19x y/
y). Cash generation also strengthened, with LTM FCF-to-debt up to 11.8% (+2.55ppt q/q).  
Margins and liquidity also improved: LTM EBITDA margin rose to 15.9% (+0.47ppt q/q, 
+0.85ppt y/y) and cash-to-debt rose to 12.8% (+0.17ppt q/q, +0.79ppt y/y).
 We note that a greater share of the BB cohort has reported than single-B (63% vs. 36%), 
which may impact metrics as the mix evens out through additional earnings.

3Q26 Current Reporters Update
Gross leverage improved on the quarter, falling 0.23x to 3.43x, and now sits 0.13x below 
year-ago levels. Net leverage eased 0.20x q/q to 2.86x but remains 0.16x above where it 
stood a year ago. Interest coverage improved 0.11x q/q to 4.86x and 0.33x y/y. Cash-to-

FCF to debt climbed roughly 0.67ppt q/q and 3.45ppt y/y to 12.4%. Capex to sales ticked 
up about 0.14ppt q/q to 3.95% but sits roughly 0.25ppt below last year. Overall, based on 
this subset of the market, fundamentals are broadly improving on the earnings and 
cash side, while net leverage is still higher than a year ago
