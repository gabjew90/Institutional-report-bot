Global Power: Agents, Aircons, Allshoring: All Not Priced In
Our 2025 call on power markets' changing face is now evolving beyond AI: it's also an energy security, El Niño and 
industrial capex supercycle thematic. 
Key Surprises 
•
Surprise #1: Powering AI→ Powering Everything
•
We raise our above street global power consumption estimates by 10% to 4.2% CAGR through 2030, with 
demand inflecting across the US, Europe, Japan, LatAm and Asia.
•
Quality of demand is improving: The Three A's – Agentic AI, Allshoring and Aircons – broaden growth beyond 
data centers, supported by energy security and the industrial capex supercycle
•
Surprise #2: Fossil Fuels Take Share and “Bring Your Own Power”
•
Natural gas and coal are complementing batteries and renewables more than ever 
•
Dependable fossil-based generation gains scarcity value as power markets tighten.
•
Behind-the-meter capacity requirements rise to ~47GW in the US and ~8GW in Asia by 2030
•
Surprise #3: Power Prices: Duck Curves Are Changing Shape
•
Daily power needs are surging before and after daylight- driving inflation in power prices.
•
Merchant and long-term power supply contracts are seeing increases for gas, renewables and nuclear 
generation
•
Merchant pricing and tiered tariffs gain importance as power markets evolve.
•
Surprise #4: Pricing Power Shifting to Power Infrastructure Owners from Equipment Suppliers 
•
Power generators are seeing ASP increases after inflation in equipment producers last 18 months.
