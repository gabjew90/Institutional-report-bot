GS - Biswas - Credit MarketStrats - Important to differentiate
between risk and uncertainty - 8 Nov 2026
Avishek Biswas  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  Vice President, Global Banking & Markets, FICC & Equities
Sun 8 Nov 2026, 7:58:53 AM ET
Past couple of weeks’ price action has been indicative of investors starting to price an economic/growth
slowdown. Equities/bonds correlation has taken a complete U-turn with negative correlation over the past
month (Graphic 2) after having been realizing positive for the bulk of 2026. Over the past month, 5y USD
rates have rallied around 40bps with real rates rallying 30bps contributing to the bulk of that (Graphic 3). I
am sure that NDX/US tech air-pocketing lower hasn’t been in investors’ blind spot either - in fact the past
month’s rates rally has coincided with the sell-off in NDX. Market is clearly pricing the AI story and US
growth as the 2 sides of the same coin – how the AI story plays out going forward, I think the verdict is
still pretty much open.
Couple of key observations within credit :
o Improving credit fund flows helping the demand side dynamics
Looking specifically in credit, the recent macro developments have been net positive though. The rates
rally has improved credit total returns which is helping improve the fund flow picture (Graphic 4) with net
inflows over the past 1 month in each of EUR IG/USD IG/EUR HY/USD HY (Graphic 5). The improving
fund flow picture is helping the demand side dynamics ; based on our Syndicate team’s tracking – last
week saw €40bn of issuance across EUR IG unsecured financials and corporates (= second busiest week
on record for the EUR IG market) with the issuance being reasonably well absorbed as new issues are
performing strongly on the break and concessions starting to come down from mid double digits to high
singles. The improving technicals is translating into credit price action with most of the segments ending
the week tighter (Graphic 1).
o Rates/credit spread correlation has turned negative
In line with equity/bond correlations, rates/credit spread correlation has flipped (Graphic 6) – this is an
important development for credit given rates/duration is now back to being a natural hedge for credit
portfolios. All-in yield investors will love this development and I expect stronger demand from IG all-in
yields investors going forward especially with all-in yields at very compelling levels (Graphic 7). Net : I
remain broadly constructive on IG credit .
Important to differentiate between risk and uncertainty
This differentiation is now more important than ever – we are transitioning into a period of very high
uncertainty rather than into a period of high risk. Both hard and soft data prints don’t point towards any
meaningful deterioration in the economic backdrop (in fact last week NFP was strong, unemployment rate
didn’t move higher, UMich consumer sentiment improved); it’s more that there is way too much
uncertainty around how things evolve from here – AI/hyperscalers remains a tricky place to decipher and

geopolitical uncertainties continue (there is a case to be made that the US mid-term results have actually
increased the uncertainty rather than suppressing it). I am inclined towards an overall portfolio framework
where investors own uncertainty convexity (for instance owning x-asset gamma) and funding those via the
carry earned through being long risk IG credit (especially in all-in yield format) .
Longs I like within credit : iTraxx Main 10y index , iTraxx Main 5y5y forward , buy iTraxx receivers
funded by selling payer spreads , EUR IG SnrFins cash , EUR IG corp hybrids .
But market could continue to remain wary around growth
Price action in rates is demonstrating that investors see US growth as the channel through which they are
pricing AI-related upside and downside. Recent price action has clearly been suggestive of investors
pricing the downside risk to US economy from the past couple of weeks’ AI sell-off. And it feels that this
pricing can continue for the next couple of weeks. In any case economy has a clear headwind to worry
about – real rates continue to screen wide (even after the past weeks’ rally) and the growth vs rates picture
doesn’t look overly constructive (Graphic 8). I would advocate having some asymmetric expressions
against this risk – the cleanest way to trade that (in my view) is keeping some short risk positions in HY
credit and funding those shorts via longs in other place in credit.
A trade expression I am starting to like is short risk CDX HY vs long risk iTraxx SnrFin index . The
iTraxx SnrFin index has been among the credit underperformers over the past 3 months (some of that
underperformance being driven by the OAT/France weakness); while OATs/France remains a key risk, the
iTraxx SnrFin underperformance is screening as a decent valuation dislocation to fade (investors are better
of setting more bepsoke/targeted credit shorts against French risk – more on that below). Spread ratio of
CDX HY index to iTraxx SnrFin index (Graphic 9) shows a resonable entry point to enter this RV trade
and the non-dealer positioning (Graphic 10) on the 2 indices also favors legging into this trade (non-
dealers net short risk in iTraxx SnrFin vs net long risk in CDX HY).
France
Strong performance of French credit last week in line with the compression in OAT-Bunds (Graphic 11).
The theme remains fluid and I continue to remain skeptical here seeing this as a “fade-the-rally” space
rather than a “buy-the-dip” space. Worth noting that while French names have meaningfully
underperformed vs rest of IG credit within the Fins space, a similar underperformance hasn’t played out in
the Non-Fins space (Graphic 12). Shorting baskets of these French non-Fins names screens as a convex
downside expression within credit.
Hyperscalers
META came with it’s first ever EUR denominated issuance last week bringing €6bn to the EUR market
which also saw €9bn of issuance from Alphabet ~ >60% of the EUR IG corp issuance last week was
contributed by these 2 hyperscalers. The EUR market is clearly seeing the hyperscalers diversify away
from the USD markets – our research team expects more of this trend going forward; our EUR hyperscaler
basket ( GSLCEUHS ) is a simple/liquid/scalable expression for investors to get exposure to this theme.
