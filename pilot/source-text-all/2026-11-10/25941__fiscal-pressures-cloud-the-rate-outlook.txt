THE RATE OUTLOOK 
With roughly 75% of federal outlays tied to mandatory programs, 
newly-elected policymakers will have limited flexibility to address 
rising deficits and debt. 
	
—
As investors assess the next Congress’s priorities following this week’s elections, policymakers face a 
challenging fiscal backdrop.
	
—
The federal deficit sits at approximately 6% of GDP, while the national debt has surpassed $40 trillion.¹
	
—
Yet meaningful fiscal reform may prove difficult: Roughly three-quarters of federal outlays are tied to 
mandatory programs and interest costs, leaving a relatively small share subject to annual appropriations.¹
	
—
Treasury yields have retreated from recent highs, but persistent deficits and rising interest costs could 
pressure long-term rates even as monetary policy evolves.
	
—
Higher yields offer core fixed income greater income potential but may also create a less 
certain path for total returns.
