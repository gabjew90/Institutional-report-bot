US Rates Watch 
Flows & positioning: short momentum loss 
     
  
 
 
UST shorts continue to face headwinds 
Positioning and flows point to continued two-way risk in rates. Shorts continue to see 
momentum turn against them and CTA short covering now faces the most risk at the 
front end as those triggers remain a key threshold for a rally extension (see: Systematic 
Flows Monitor). Asset managers moved long in futures last week, while active funds 
decreased duration overweights in favor of IG. Broader fixed income inflows 
strengthened, led by IG and HY corporate funds that offset a pullback in front-end 
duration funds. Our futures positioning proxy now suggests a bias for the curve to 
flatten.

 Positioning 
Futures proxy biased for rally 
After another significant rally, our futures positioning proxy now implies a bias for rates 
to move lower across the curve, ex TU (Exhibit 10, Exhibit 11). Longs retreated as shorts 
moved more OTM. Shorts remain the more concentrated position.  
Asset managers go long in futures 
Asset managers covered shorts and added to longs in futures across the curve last week 
(Exhibit 8). The divergence between front end / belly positioning and the long end shrank 
slightly in the past week, pointing to a reprieve of strong steepener positioning from 
asset managers over the last month (Exhibit 7, Exhibit 9). 
Front end CTA shorts close to covering  
As of Friday, only front end CTA shorts remain outside of covering range after belly and 
long end shorts rallied through their ranges the prior week (Exhibit 2). Front end trigger 
ranges are within reach, which leaves risk of short covering should the rally extend.

Flows 
Rebound in US fixed income fund flows 
US fixed income fund inflows rebounded last week as investors rotated from duration to 
spread risk, specifically corporates. After weeks of strong inflows into front-end 
Treasury funds, flows shifted to belly duration and IG & HY corporate funds instead 
(Exhibit 36). Long-end Treasury funds still face disfavor, and Agg style allocation funds 
continue to receive only a third of the rolling 52w average inflows.  
Active funds reduce duration overweights for IG 
Active funds reduce duration overweights in favor of moving less underweight IG, 
signaling a return to spread products over duration. They stay modestly overweight MBS 
(Exhibit 42-47). Fund outperformance has narrowed notably over recent weeks following 
broad rate selloff.  
Banks assets decline 
In the week ending Oct 21, bank assets declined significantly from a drawdown in 
domestic cash and other foreign assets (Exhibit 50). Domestic bank deposits also 
declined dramatically (Exhibit 51). Domestic bank MBS holdings are now even with July 
‘25 levels while UST and agency holdings continue to increase (Exhibit 55).

Below we compare the signals from various positioning metrics historically. We find that 
the fund regression tends to move largely in a trend-like fashion: when positioning is 
stretched long, rates tend to rally in subsequent months. By contrast, our futures 
positioning proxy is more contrarian for duration: when the net out of the money 
position is long, rates tend to sell off in subsequent months. CTA betas don’t show a 
strong relationship to future curve or duration moves.
