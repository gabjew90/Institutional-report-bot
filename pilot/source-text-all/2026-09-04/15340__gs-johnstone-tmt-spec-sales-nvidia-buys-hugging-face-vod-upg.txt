GS TMT SPEC SALES – Friday 4th September
Before Waller’s comments helped the market and semis bounce, yesterday’s debate was focused on why
EU semicaps and related names were underperforming so sharply despite AVGO’s AI demand
commentary still looking broadly supportive for the supply chain. The concern was less about end-demand
and more about positioning, de-grossing and valuation: memory had sold off first and stabilised, but
investors still appeared crowded in semicap longs and were starting to capitulate, with muted desk activity
and supply showing up in analog and optical names. The key pushback was that if NVDA/AVGO trade on
~10x out-year P/E despite very strong AI revenue growth, investors are asking what the rest of the supply
chain should be worth, especially against a risk-off tape, renewed tariff angst and continued multiple
compression. That said, ASML has already de-rated materially to sub-20x 2027 EV/EBITDA from ~28x in
July, numbers are still moving higher, and the re-rating trigger now likely needs to come from renewed
confidence in the durability of AI spend, with GS Communacopia and Anthropic disclosures potentially
important catalysts; on a re-risking move, quality AI exposure such as ASML and ASM (lower memory
exposure and lower China export-control risk) should be bought first.
To me, the most significant overnight development is Nvidia’s confirmed $12.93bn acquisition of Hugging
Face (Nvidia IR), giving Nvidia control of a leading open-source AI platform used by 18m+ developers
while pledging to keep it open across models, clouds and compute providers. Strategically, the prize is
distribution control over open weights and a deeper role in the AI developer ecosystem, which should
support demand for Nvidia hardware and cloud capacity; the key risk is regulatory slippage, with closing
targeted for 2027 and antitrust review likely. The deal is also Nvidia’s largest acquisition, ahead of
Mellanox at $6.9bn, and comes alongside a much broader funding push into frontier AI labs: Nvidia has
reportedly committed around $30bn to OpenAI and up to $10bn to Anthropic, with additional participation
in xAI, taking total AI-related commitments to more than $50bn across labs, cloud infrastructure and
strategic partnerships.

| • | CPO: Silicon photonics and co-packaged optics (CPO) are gradually entering a critical pre-mass-
production phase. Industry sources indicate that the next major bottleneck for CPO mass production lies in
"shifting testing upstream"—specifically, identifying optical defects and controlling yields at the wafer
stage, while ensuring test data continuity through to the optical engine and module testing phases.
Consequently, equipment suppliers are shifting from competing with standalone units to forming
partnerships; typically, one company assumes the role of a "turnkey" provider, integrating equipment from
various vendors to serve as the primary system-level point of contact for the customer. (Digitimes) | | • |

SHAREHOLDER RETURNS LOOK INCREASINGLY ATTRACTIVE. The shareholder returns yield
rises to c.6%/7% in 2027/28E, versus the next-highest defensive sector at c.4% and with less growth.
Returns could rise further if telcos lean into leverage. We already model shareholder returns above
consensus, while ND/EBITDA falls 0.4pp to 2.0x over three years. With growth now inflecting, investor
attitudes toward gearing may also shift after two decades of de-gearing. If telcos hold sector ND/EBITDA
at today’s 2.4x, shareholder returns yields could reach 8%/9% in 2027/28E.

| • | Scout24 nice little note from Adam doing a data scrap- on ImmoScout24 Tenant Network. Scout24 is
enhancing the Search+ offering is through the ImmoScout24 Tenant Network. This feature enables tenants
in the network to share pre-market rental listings, before they move. This gives Search+ subscribers access
to a grey market of additional listings that would not have historically appeared on classified portals.
These listings are only available to Search+ subscribers who take up the "Pro" or "Unlimited" tiers. Pre-
market listings not only attract new subscribers, but they can also drive higher ARPU. The "Unlimited"
tiers costs €10 more per month than the "Standard" tier (on a six-month commitment). GIR have been
tracking the number of pre-maket listings on a weekly basis since the May CMD. The number of listings
has nearly tripled from 25k in May to ~70k as of today (3 Sept) vs. ~63k in the last week of August. This
is notable because marketplace products typically become more valuable as inventory increases. A larger
pool of pre-market listings improves the utility of the network for prospective renters, which can in turn
drive further engagement and subscription uptake to "Pro" and "Unlimited" tiers. Scout24 is on track to
deliver its CMD targets~700k private subscribers by FY28. They had reached 528k in July 2026 | | • |
