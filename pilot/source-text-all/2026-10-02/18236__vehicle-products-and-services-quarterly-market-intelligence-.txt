Practice overview
About Piper Sandler: Our vehicle products and services team covers the full supply chain of products 
and services supporting passenger, fleet and recreational vehicles. Collectively, this coverage 
universe represents the largest asset class in North America, with more than 310 million vehicles in 
operation. The range of services these vehicles provide—and the ways in which they are 
maintained—is vast, spanning nearly every segment of the modern economy. This landscape 
continues to evolve as new vehicle technologies and mobility innovations emerge.

Q3 2026 commentary
This issue is our inaugural Quarterly Market Intelligence Report:
Given the breadth of our coverage universe and the demands on our attention from deal 
execution, we have historically struggled to design a newsletter that we felt contained 
sufficient information to be valuable to our client base. While it is helpful to periodically 
distribute M&A transaction data, we have found that executives in auto service markets are 
less focused on acquisition trends in the wholecar auction space or the heavy-duty 
aftermarket—and vice versa. This is one of the challenges we aim to address with this report.
We have been able to aggregate this compendium of information from our coverage universe, 
using both publicly available and Piper Sandler proprietary data. We present this data in what 
we hope is a digestible format. For each of the public constituents in our coverage subsectors, 
we detail KPIs and macroeconomic factors that drive these companies’ revenue and EBITDA 
performance. In future editions we will add incremental macroeconomic data, as well as 
introduce company and sector-specific “deep dives” that we hope you will find valuable.
As always, we welcome any feedback you may have. You can email our team at 
pscvehicleprod&services@psc.com
Also, do not hesitate to reach out to anyone on the Piper Sandler team to discuss anything in 
this report or to chat about deals, financings or ideas in our sector.
Treavor, Jim, Ari and Derek

Other notable news
Industry news   Collision claims ·   September 10, 2026
Repairable claims fell over 10% in 2025 as total-loss frequency hit nearly 23%
Collision repair industry data shows repairable claims dropped more than 10% in 2025 while total-loss 
frequency climbed to nearly 23%, driven partly by higher insurance deductibles.
Industry news   Technician shortage ·   July 4, 2026
Auto repair sector faces annual shortfall of nearly 37,000 qualified technicians
The industry needs to replace roughly 76,000 technicians per year to keep pace with retirements and 
growing demand, per NADA data.
Executive moves   Grismer Tire & Auto Service ·   September 24, 2026
Grismer appoints Chris Blanchette CEO, Mark Hedstrom CFO
Grismer Tire & Auto Service named Chris Blanchette CEO and Mark Hedstrom CFO, nearly six months after 
CenterOak Partners completed a majority recapitalization of the 28-location Ohio dealership.  Blanchette 
previously served as CEO of Service Minds and held roles with QAS, Advance Auto Parts, Bridgestone 
Retail Operations and Best Buy.  Hedstrom, with over 30 years of finance experience, most recently was 
CFO of W.S.
Executive moves   Classic Collision ·   June 1, 2026
Classic Collision CEO Toan Nguyen steps down, Brad Anderson named successor
Toan Nguyen stepped down as CEO in June 2026 while remaining on the board. Brad Anderson, former 
CEO of Pilot Travel Centers, was appointed as his successor.
Executive moves   Tire Industry Association ·   August 23, 2026
TIA Elects Josephine Foley To Executive Committee As Secretary
The Tire Industry Association elected Josephine Foley, manager of safety and fleet for Sullivan Tire and 
Automotive Service, as secretary of its executive committee during its Mid-Year Board Meeting.  Her term 
begins November 2 at the TIA Board of Directors meeting in Las Vegas ahead of the 2026 Global Tire 
Expo/SEMA Show, and the role positions her to become TIA president for 2028-29.
Strategic review   Driven Brands ·   September 15, 2026
Driven Brands Approves $100 Million Stock Buyback, Sets New Debt Target
Driven Brands' board approved a $100 million stock buyback with no time limit on Sept. 15, roughly 5% of 
total market value, funded by cash on hand and operating cash flow.  The company also adopted a long-
term net debt target of two to three times adjusted EBITDA, having already reduced leverage from 5.0x at 
end of 2023 to an expected 3.0x by Q3 2026.
Strategic review   Monro ·   May 27, 2026
Monro Board Initiates Strategic Alternatives Review
Board will evaluate options including asset sales, refinancing, strategic acquisitions and operational 
improvements, with financial and legal advisors assisting the review.
Source: trade press.
Milestones   Valvoline ·   August 5, 2026
Franchise Equity Partners' Velocity Auto Care hits 50 Valvoline Instant Oil Change locations
Velocity Auto Care, a Franchise Equity Partners portfolio company, opened its 50th Valvoline Instant Oil 
Change service center at 2930 W Trenton Road in Edinburg, Texas, the 12th location opened since FEP 
acquired 38 VIOC franchise locations from Valvoline in December 2024.  The acquisition included a 
development agreement for 75 additional service centers over seven years.
