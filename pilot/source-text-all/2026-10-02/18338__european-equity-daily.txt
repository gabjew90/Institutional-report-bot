Autos & Auto Parts European auto parts valuations remain depressed despite resilient 3Q trends. We see 
Valeo as best positioned to navigate regional mix headwinds and upgrade to Buy, while we downgrade Dauch 
to Hold on slowing momentum.

product performance, helping assess the sustainability of biosimilar growth as recent launches mature into 
established franchises. 
Beverages We launch our LatAm Cocktail series, highlighting resilient beverage demand, warmer weather 
trends and stronger production data. Coke bottlers continue to show resilient volumes, margin gains and zero-
sugar momentum. 
Capital Goods We revisit management compensation trends across the sector, finding that revenue is 
favoured over ESG while PBT as a component continues to decline. LTIPs remain primarily driven by EPS and 
TSR. 
Best of the Rest

sovereign AI infrastructure and realising productivity and earnings gains from AI adoption.
