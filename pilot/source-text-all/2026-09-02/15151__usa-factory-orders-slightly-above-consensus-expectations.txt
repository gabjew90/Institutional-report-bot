USA: Factory Orders Slightly Above Consensus Expectations
2 September 2026 | 11:03AM EDT
BOTTOM LINE: Factory orders increased 0.9% in July, slightly above consensus 
expectations. Growth in core capital goods orders was revised down 0.2pp to flat 
and growth in core capital goods shipments was revised down by 0.2pp to 1.2%. We 
left our Q3 GDP tracking estimate unchanged at +2.7% (quarter-over-quarter 
annualized). 
KEY NUMBERS: 
Factory orders +0.9% (MoM) for July vs. GS -0.2%, median forecast +0.7%, prior 
revised -0.2% 
MAIN POINTS: 
1. Factory orders increased 0.9% in July, slightly above consensus expectations. 
Growth in factory orders was revised up 0.1pp to -0.2% in June. Factory orders 
excluding transportation increased by 0.6%, slightly above consensus expectations, 
and the growth rate in June was revised up 0.3pp to -0.1%. Growth in core capital 
goods orders was revised down 0.2pp to flat and growth in core capital goods 
shipments was revised down by 0.2pp to +1.2%. Core capital goods orders have risen 
by 17.8% on a 6-month annualized basis, largely driven by increases in the primary 
metals (+32.0%), machinery (+21.5%), and computers and electronic products 
(+19.1%) categories. Growth in durable goods orders was unrevised at +1.1% in July 
and growth in durable goods orders ex-transportation was unrevised at +0.4%. 
Manufacturing inventory growth was revised up 0.1pp to 0.4% in July.
