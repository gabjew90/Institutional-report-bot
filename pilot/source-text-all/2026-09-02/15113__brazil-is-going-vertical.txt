Brazil goes vertical
What started as a bounce from must-hold support has turned into a proper squeeze. EWZ is up some
12% since we flagged the level in August, Ibovespa has rallied for 11 days straight and Brazil is finally
catching up to the commodity bull.
But this has gotten very overbought, very quickly. Spot up, vol up and call OI building is classic upside

of move, chasing it here looks increasingly uncomfortable.

A short‑cover / sentiment squeeze is live and accelerating. Ibovespa printed its 11th consecutive daily
