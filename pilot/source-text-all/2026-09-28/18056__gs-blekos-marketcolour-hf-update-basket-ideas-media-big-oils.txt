Good Morning,
US equities finished Friday in the green, aided by modest relief in oil - SPX +51bps to 7743 (8/11 sectors
+ve, 30Y UST highest since 2004, Tech +0.99%, Industrials +0.94%, Energy -0.87%), NDX +42bps to
30,608, RTY +7bps to 2837. GS High Beta TMT momentum index (GSTMTMMOM, +2.47%), and
MSFT +3.7% after announcing a new copilot capabilities app, including Autopilot personal agent. In
Europe, the EU AI vertical well bid on the week: Tech (SX8P +2.5%), EU Semis (GSSBSEMI +3.5%) and
Data Centres (GSXEDATA +2.3%).
Interesting: “Muse and similar agentic AI assistants could soon sweep household cash automatically into
accounts paying 3.3% to 5.0%, instead of the 0.1% national average on checking accounts.”
Flow: Asset managers were net sellers on the week, with supply concentrated in consumer discretionary,
communication services, tech, and health care vs small demand in macro products. Hedge funds were
better for sale with supply in macro products, consumer discretionary, and communication services vs
demand in tech and health care.
HF Update: Fundamental LS managers up 0.4% (all beta), now up 0.2% MTD and 12.9% YTD. Global
book was net sold driven by North America, with Europe being the only net bought region. Across sectors,
Info Tech was the most net bought while Communication Services was the most net sold (after 10
consecutive weeks of net buying). Utilities saw its largest short selling in almost 6 years. Average turnover
week.
Basket Ideas: The Agentic (GSXUAGNT) vs Consumer Inertia trade (GSXUSWCH) // Short Non
Profitable Tech (GSXUNPTC) // Long German Fiscal Beneficiaries (GSXEGFSC).
Trump/Xi: Low market expectations leading into the event proved warranted, as concrete deliverables
were limited primarily to a two-month extension of the “trade truce” and a potential “AI hotline”. Deeper
underlying tensions remain, though both presidents arguably have incentives to keep the relationship on
the rails in the near term.
HALMA: Christian upping numbers +4-5%, mostly reflecting recent acquisitions and upgrading of
portfolio (buying high margin businesses, selling lower margin ones), whilst also making some minor
organic tweaks. Christian continues to expect ~40% growth in photonics vs the company ~30% guide. Full
1H numbers 19th November.

Europe Media: Publicis (Buy, 12m PT €116). We have increased confidence that Publicis can deliver
organic growth above consensus in 2027, buoyed by strong new business performance. Our latest analysis
of Comvergence data suggests it is having a strong year for new media billings. The stock has fallen back
to 11x 2027E EPS, with upside risk to consensus (including from LiveRamp). Scout24 (Buy, 12m PT
€108). We are positive on the launch of new products that we think can help Scout 24 deliver on private
subscriber targets faster than consensus (e.g., Deposit Guarantee+). Trading on 14x 2027E EPS, valuation
is attractive for a stock we expect to deliver an 18% 2027-29 EPS CAGR.
Big Oils Preview (We are 12% above consensus on FY26 EPS): Record-high profits expected in 3Q26
supported by refining and Oil/LNG trading where Repsol and BP stand out. We see strong upside to
refining margins for 3Q26, with our EU Big barometer +74% qoq/+279% yoy, supported by higher
refining results (+35%qoq). Companies with higher exposure to refining and trading (Repsol
+%67qoq/+308%yoy, BP +23%qoq/+138%yoy) are seeing the largest increases in our barometer.
