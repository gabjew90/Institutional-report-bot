Land Securities (LAND LN, UNDERPERFORM, PT: 462.00p) First View: £211m Office sale at 6%, to lower 
leverage [Sarim Chaudhry] 
 Landsec has agreed the unconditional sale of 123 Victoria Street, SW1 to SevenCitiesLdn (on behalf of 
Seven Capital PLC) for £211m, continuing its strategy of recycling capital out of offices into higher-growth 
sectors. The 245k sq ft office building, originally developed in the 1970s and refurbished in 2012, is being sold at 
an estimated foregone NRI yield of 6.0% over the next 5 years. Completion is expected next month, with £181m 
payable on completion and a further £30m deferred for up to 36 months, attracting 6.0% annual interest, above 
Landsec's marginal borrowing cost.The transaction supports Landsec's target to dispose of £2bn of office assets 
by 2030, having already sold more than £550m of offices and £1bn of assets overall since announcing its 
updated strategy in early 2025. Financially, the disposal reduces EPRA NTA per share by 0.3%, but improves 
leverage, lowering pro-forma LTV by 0.9 ppts from 38.7%, and reducing net debt/EBITDA by 0.3x from 8.4x. 
 https://javatar.bluematrix.com/pdf/RGgaoyO7
