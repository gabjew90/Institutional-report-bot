Good morning – what a day yesterday with market indices meaningfully higher, volatility collapsing but
rotations as violent as we have seen them under the surface. The debate is whether this continues or not.
We lean more in the camp that it does given nothing actually changes in the next few months until
congressional seats turnover. In that vacuum of information, the other catalyst to consider is a swifter
conclusion / resolution to the geopolitical situation – which we also believe would lead to lower rates,
lower inflationary pressures and more of what we saw on the screen yesterday. The catalyst path appears
murky heading into year end just as investors have degrossed waiting on the sidelines to chase
performance into year end.
Coming in this morning to see US futures little unchanged with technology shares continuing their sell off.
In Asia seeing tech heavy KOSPI down 528bps overnight, Japan down 179bps, SHPROP continues to
stick out well bid up 156bps. Europe higher at the index level up 50bps. Eurozone PPI +11.1% vs. +10.6%
consensus in September. German factory orders rebound in September in line with expectations. In
commodities, most everything a little higher including crude, gold, silver but WTI remains sub $90 and
Brent $96. Rates continue their march lower on growth concerns 10-year in the US 4.88% at local lows,
dollar flat and Bitcoin back just above $80k. Watching claims on the data front today.
Chart to flag: Second worst day on records (since 2013) in industrials momentum pair – GSINMOMO.
