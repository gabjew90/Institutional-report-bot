DB CoTD: October: A (small) nightmare on Wall Street... 
 
Peter Sidorov has just published our October and YTD performance review. See it here. Below are some 
highlights with all the returns in USD terms. 
1. Oil led the gains. Brent (+13.6%) and WTI (+15.2%) were October's top performers, taking YTD gains 
to +93.3% and +81.3%. Brent ended the month at $117.64/bbl, close to its 2026 high. Houthi attacks, 
Hormuz disruption, and fears of renewed US strikes on Iran after the midterms kept the risk premium 
rising. European gas jumped +43.6% to €103.94/MWh, its highest since 2022. 
2. Tech was the biggest drag. Doubts about AI capex spread from semiconductors to the wider sector. 
1. The Philly semiconductor index fell -15.3% and was down -19% from 6 October to month-
end. 
2. The Nasdaq lost -5.3%, its worst month since March 2025. Alphabet's -12.64% post-results 
day was its worst on record. 
3. The KOSPI (-14.7%) was the weakest of the major indices we track. The Nikkei fell -5.3%. 
3. Treasuries staged a sharp rebound. The 10yr yield hit a post-2002 high of 5.31% early in the month, 
then fell back to 4.93%. Weaker risk sentiment, plus on-hold decisions from the Fed, ECB and BoJ, 
restored the safe-haven bid. Treasuries returned +2.3%, their best month of 2026, though they are 
still -0.5% YTD. Gilts gained +2.6%, while Bunds managed only +0.4% in USD, with a weaker euro 
offsetting most of the rally. 
4. France was the focus of European stress. The 10yr OAT-Bund spread reached 174bps on 22 
October, its widest since November 2011, and the French 10yr yield hit 5.07%. 
1. OATs lost -1.7%, and BTPs and EU sovereigns lost -0.5%. 
2. The euro fell to 1.1160, its lowest since spring 2025, which hurt USD returns on all euro 
assets. 
3. EU HY lost -2.5%, and European HY spreads widened +65bps to 370bps. 
4. European banks fell -11.7%. 

5. Equities rotated beneath the headlines. The S&P 500 fell -0.9%, but the equal-weighted index 
gained +5.0%. In USD, the DAX rose +0.9% and the STOXX 600 +0.5%, so Europe held up outside 
France and the periphery, though the weaker euro trimmed gains. 
6. Elsewhere, losses were broad. 
1. MSCI EM fell -8.0%, and Brazil's Bovespa fell -14.3% after Lula's election win. 
2. The Shanghai Composite lost -5.3% (-2.7% YTD). 
3. Gold lost -6.8% and silver -18.7%, leaving both among the weakest assets YTD (-10.3% and -
31.5%). 
7. Overall : October was a rotation out of tech and French risk into energy and core government bonds. 
Oil's +93.3% YTD gain remains the dominant theme in the year-to-date table but even with the 
October stresses, the Philly semiconductor index remains +51.6% YTD.
