Verisk Analytics Inc. (VRSK)
 
 

Subscription revenue increased 8.2% y/y OCC, largely consistent with 8.0% growth in 
2Q, driven by expanding client relationships in forms, rules and loss costs as well as 
strong renewals and new logos in anti-fraud. Transactional revenue increased 3.2%, 
inflecting positively from a 4.2% decrease in 2Q, driven by stronger services revenue in 
life solutions mitigated by continued pressure from below-average weather-related 
volumes in property & restoration solutions and softer transactional activity in North 
America across property lines. Subscription revenue represents ~84% of VRSK’s total 
revenue. 
EBITDA margins and EPS outperform. EBITDA margins expanded 90 bps y/y to 56.7% 
and came above our estimate of 56.2% and consensus of 56.3%, driven by operating 
leverage. EPS of $1.96 beat our forecast and consensus of $1.92. 
3Q 2026 Positives / Negatives 
Positives: 
Subscription growth supports a durable return to the medium-term framework. 
n
Subscription revenue grew 8.2% y/y OCC and represented ~84% of total revenue, 
supported by forms, rules and loss costs, catastrophe risk solutions and anti-fraud 
analytics. Growth benefited from pricing, client expansion, new logos and adoption 
of Mozart, Forms AI Builder, Synergy Studio and digital media forensics. Consistent 
subscription growth despite elongated sales cycles and softer insurance markets 
supports organic revenue growth within VRSK’s 6-8% medium-term target range. 
Transactional revenue returned to growth with potential for longer-term 
n
recovery. Transactional revenue increased 3.2% y/y OCC as temporary headwinds 
eased, supported by stronger Life Solutions services revenue and data-pull overages. 
The return to positive growth reduces the drag from the 16% transactional revenue 
mix. A normalization in weather activity and property transactions would provide 
incremental upside. 
AI strengthens pricing, monetization and VRSK’s data moat. VRSK is monetizing 
n
AI through direct product licenses and stronger pricing on core contracts, with 
potential for separate MCP charges as usage expands. Core Lines Reimagined 
remains on track to deliver 25 new modules in 2026, and additional MCPs are 
planned across other applications and datasets. A customer that attempted to use 
AI with internal data for fraud detection ultimately returned under a significant 
multi-year agreement after VRSK’s broader industry data produced better 
outcomes, reinforcing the value of its contributory data and supporting continued 
value-based pricing. 
AI productivity supports reinvestment and margin expansion. FAST Studio has 
n
generated productivity gains of up to 30% across the software development 
lifecycle, with additional benefits emerging in data ingestion and calibration. VRSK 
plans to balance reinvestment in innovation and go-to-market capabilities with 
incremental operating leverage. These efficiencies support annual margin expansion 
within the 25-75 bps medium-term target despite continued product investment.

Negatives:  
Transactional pressure could persist into early 2027. The 3Q transactional 
n
inflection partly reflected stronger Life Solutions services revenue and data-pull 
overages, and VRSK does not expect the overall strength to repeat in 4Q. 
Below-average weather activity continues to reduce property and restoration 
volumes, and softer property markets in the US and Canada are separately 
constraining transactional activity. Contractors and adjusters represent ~10% of 
revenue, with lower weather-related demand contributing to collection pressure and 
a small number of customer bankruptcies that could remain a modest headwind into 
early 2027. 
Longer sales cycles delay broader revenue conversion. Extended customer review 
n
and approval processes continue to delay the conversion of strong engagement into 
revenue, with AI-related enterprise agreements requiring additional testing around 
reliability, regulation, governance and contributory data. Smaller and mid-sized 
customers are adopting AI licenses more quickly, but larger opportunities can 
involve thousands of claims and underwriting professionals and take longer to 
implement. VRSK expects AI adoption to broaden over the next 12-24 months, 
leaving the timing of the related 2027 contribution uncertain. 
AI could pressure less differentiated software and analytics interfaces. 
n
Customers may increasingly consume VRSK data through APIs, MCP connectors and 
their own analytics environments, reducing the value of products that primarily 
provide dashboards, interfaces or workflow functionality. Data, models and analytics 
tied directly to VRSK’s contributory datasets are more defensible, and MCP access is 
currently supporting CoreLine pricing. The longer-term risk is that third-party AI 
platforms control more of the customer interface, requiring VRSK to preserve its 
economics through stronger core pricing, explicit data licenses or usage-based 
charges. 
Full Year Guidance 
2026 guidance reiterated. VRSK maintained its full year 2026 guidance for revenue of 
$3,190-3,240mn and EBITDA of $1,790-1,830mn with margins of 56.0-56.5% and EPS 
of $7.45-7.75. Guidance points to broadly stable consensus estimates for revenue and 
EBITDA margins, and modest negative consensus estimate revisions for EPS. Prior to the 
3Q print, our 2026 forecasts included $3,210mn in revenue, $1,808mn in EBITDA with 
56.3% margins and $7.68 in EPS, compared to FactSet consensus of $3,219mn in 
revenue, $1,814mn in EBITDA with 56.4% margins and $7.71 in EPS. 
GS Estimate Changes 
Estimates largely unchanged. We leave our 2026/2027/2028 revenue estimates 
essentially unchanged at $3,214mn/$3,423mn/$3,646mn. We also leave our 
2026/2027/2028 EBITDA margin estimates unchanged at 56.3%/56.9%/57.4%. Our 
2026/2027/2028 EPS estimates go from $7.68/$8.80/$9.97 to $7.70/$8.79/$10.01.

Valuation and Risks 
 
 
We lower our 12-month price target from $235 to $228 based on 23.5x (25.0x prior) our 
rolled-forward NTM + 1YR EPS estimate of $9.71. We lower our target multiple to reflect 
less near-term visibility into transactional revenue growth. Our target multiple comes at 
a premium to the median Info Services peer group multiple of 17.4x, supported by 
VRSK’s high quality and proprietary data assets, solid historical track record of organic 
revenue performance, and robust EBITDA margins, mitigated by the company’s 
transactional revenue exposure. 
Risks to the downside include transactional revenue volatility, macro headwinds, a 
deceleration in insurance premium growth and carrier spending cuts as they focus on 
profitability. Risks to the upside include strong price realization, positive momentum in 
subscription revenue growth, ramping benefits from AI and margin expansion from 
operating leverage.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.  
M&A Rank 
Across our global coverage, we examine stocks using an M&A framework, considering both qualitative factors and quantitative factors (which may vary 
across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring 
companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 
representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard 
departmental guidelines we incorporate an M&A component into our target price. M&A rank of 3 is considered immaterial and therefore does not 
factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.
