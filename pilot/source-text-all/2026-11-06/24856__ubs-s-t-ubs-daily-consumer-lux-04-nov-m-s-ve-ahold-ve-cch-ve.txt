with the discounters is already high but stable, while Aldi and Lidl appear increasingly focused on profitability
and cash generation rather than expansion at any cost. Tesco’s enlarged Aldi Price Match programme, now
covering roughly 900 items, and Sainsbury’s focus on about 750 matched products should reinforce price
perception. Overall, slower discounter expansion, rational pricing, online grocery growth and stronger self-help
