STRATEGY MATTERS - Why Europe’s Earnings Defy Its Economy
 
• We raise our STOXX 600 EPS growth forecasts to 18% for 2026, 7% for 2027 and introduce our forecast for 2028
of 5%. The upgrade reflects the results of our updated earnings framework, with strong contributions from
Financials and Commodities.
• European earnings remain stronger than domestic growth would suggest. Our economists now expect nominal
Euro-area GDP growth of 3.3% in 2026, (and 2.8% in 2027), well below the pace of earnings growth. This gap
reflects the different sector composition and global exposure of the listed market.
 
Global Markets Daily: Framing Off-Balance-Sheet Liabilities in the AI Ecosystem
 
• As hyperscalers’ free cash flow has turned negative, debt issuance has accelerated. With more than $40 billion of
deals announced since October 29th, the hyperscalers have issued $271 billion of debt so far this year—above our
full-year 2026 forecast for $250 billion. In some instances, this reflects a pull-forward of planned 2027 supply. We
see scope for more of this activity, although we expect capital markets access to be somewhat uneven across
issuers and highly dependent on the market tone.
• The true scale and scope of AI-related debt issuance is also much larger than ‘just’ the five hyperscalers. Through
November 2nd, we estimate $657 billion of year-to-date, global, AI-related debt issuance across syndicated credit
markets—underscoring the importance of managing overall exposure.
 
Bayer AG (BAYGn.DE) - Pharma entering a key new phase of growth; Crop remains volatile near term. Remain Buy.
 
• We remain Buy rated driven by the potential imminent court ruling on the glyphosate settlement, which could
potentially enable Bayer to move past the litigation overhang, coupled with strong performance in Pharma, as the
division enters a new phase of growth, which we believe remains under-appreciated by the market. 
• With this note, we raise our forecasts for Nubeqa, driven by continued strong growth momentum, and roll forward
our DCF and SOTP valuation bases, leading to a 4% increase to our 12m Price Target to €66.0 from €63.5.
 
Saudi Aramco (2222.SE) - Resilience and Flexibility pay off with a net income beat and strong cash flow generation;
Buy
 
• Aramco is currently trading at a 12% discount to its 5Y average P/E. We are Buy rated; our 12m PT of SAR34
implies c.30% upside. 

• Following 3Q26 results, we make adjustments to our EPS estimates of +0.4%/+0.0%/+0.0% for 2026/27/28,
incorporating 3Q26 results into our latest forecasts.
 
Gecina (GFCP.PA) - Pipeline lettings slower than we expected, downgrade to Neutral (from Buy)
 
• Following our recent asset tour with the company, we revise our forecasts lower, with 2026-30E EPS changing by
-8% to 0%, factoring-in longer lead-time to let pipeline project lettings, slightly lower rental reversion, and we also
remove our share buyback assumptions. 
• Our 2027E/28E EPS estimates are now c.3%/4% below Visible Alpha Consensus Data, and we forecast 1% 4Y
EPS CAGR, with 27E/26E EPS -4% reflecting guidance of 2027 as a transition year. Our 12m PT decreases
c.10% to €72.6 (previously €80.4), implying c.11% upside potential vs. 9% average upside for our coverage.
