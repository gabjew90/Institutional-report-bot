We remain constructive on SN and step away from the quarter with increased conviction 
on the company’s broad-based innovation-led growth profile. 3Q results demonstrated 
the increasing gains offered by SN’s investments in social commerce, which is 
increasingly a halo to the overall business. We see multiple avenues for the company to 
compound growth as momentum in the core is coupled with adjacent category 
expansion, scaling of International geographies, and a stronger omnichannel model. 
That said, we acknowledge that valuation at this level will increasingly become a debate 
following the stock’s recent appreciation, raising the importance of continued execution.  
What Happened? 
3Q result: SharkNinja reported adjusted 3Q26 EPS of $2.34, above GS/FactSet 
n
consensus of $1.87/$1.89. The beat was supported by stronger-than-expected 
sales and gross margin, including a benefit from tariff refunds. Net revenues 
increased 21.2% Y/Y (21.2% ex-FX), above GS/consensus at +13.9%/+14.8%. By 
geography, Domestic sales increased 19.4% Y/Y, while International growth was 
25.2%. Adjusted gross margin expanded ~560bps Y/Y to 55.9%, above 
GS/consensus expectations of 54.3%, and adjusted operating expenses as a 
percentage of sales came in at 34.4%, compared with GS/consensus at 
35.0%/35.7%. Net, adjusted EBITDA margin of 23.3% was reported above 
GS/consensus at 21.2%/21.1%. 
Outlook: SN raised its FY26 adjusted EPS guidance to $6.65-$6.75 versus 
n
$6.45-$6.55 prior, which compares with GS/FactSet consensus of $6.53/$6.57. 
Sales growth is now expected at 18.0%-19.0%, versus 16.0%-17.0% prior, and 
adjusted EBITDA at $1.380bn-$1.391bn, versus $1.357bn-$1.369bn prior, 
compared with GS/FactSet consensus at +17.0%/+17.4% and $1.369bn/$1.367bn, 
respectively. The high end of the updated outlook implies ~17% 4Q revenue growth 
and 9% adjusted EBITDA growth, with adjusted gross margin expected to decline 
approximately 100bps Y/Y as previously planned tariff-refund reinvestments shift 
from 3Q into 4Q. Management emphasized that these investments are expected to 
be contained within 4Q and are intended to support growth in 2027 and beyond. 
Estimates, Valuation, and Key Risks 
We update our FY26/FY27/FY28 adjusted EPS estimates to $6.78/$7.75/$8.71 from 
$6.53/$7.46/$8.39 prior to reflect the 3Q beat, stronger near-term growth momentum 
in several geographies, and modest adjustments to our margin forecasts. Our 12-month 
price target increases to $234 from $204 prior as we roll forward our Q5-Q8 EV/EBITDA 
valuation methodology. Our target valuation multiple increases to 18.85x from 17.5x 
prior on recent market multiple rerating and a stronger growth profile. Key downside 
risks include: (1) wholesale and/or social-channel partner concentration; (2) slowing 
innovation; (3) corporate structure; and (4) input costs, commodities, and tariffs.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.  
M&A Rank 
Across our global coverage, we examine stocks using an M&A framework, considering both qualitative factors and quantitative factors (which may vary 
across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring 
companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 
representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard 
departmental guidelines we incorporate an M&A component into our target price. M&A rank of 3 is considered immaterial and therefore does not 
factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.
