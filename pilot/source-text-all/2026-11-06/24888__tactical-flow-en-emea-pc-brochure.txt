Developed market equity ETPs attracted $6.2B last week, up from $5.5B previously. The increase was driven by a 
sharp recovery in North American demand, which more than offset slower allocations to global and European 
exposures.
North America led regional inflows at $3.0B, up from $519m the previous week. Global DM strategies remained 
another major destination, gathering $2.8B, although this was down from $3.9B. European inflows halved to $523m 
from $1B, while APAC reversed into outflows of -$70m after attracting $196m. Looking at more specific regional 
exposures, the Eurozone gathered $243m, down from $509m, while broader European allocations slowed to $86m 
from $343m. APAC ex-Japan recorded outflows of -$219m, reversing from $56m of inflows, while North America 
attracted $25m and APAC added $13m.
At the single-country level, the US accounted for the bulk of demand, attracting $2.9B compared with $562m 
previously. Switzerland and Japan followed with $161m and $117m respectively, although Japanese inflows 
moderated from $169m. The UK returned to positive territory with $81m after marginal outflows, while Canada also 
reversed into inflows of $25m following -$65m of outflows. Australia attracted $26m, followed by Sweden at $14m 
and France with $8m. Germany experienced the largest outflows at -$52m, compared with -$2m the previous week, 
while Italy recorded a more limited -$2m.
Within US size exposures, Large Caps attracted $1.3B, up considerably from $496m previously. Mid Caps reversed 
into outflows of -$92m after marginal inflows of $3m, while Small Cap outflows eased to -$45m from -$118m. The 
stronger Large Cap allocations reinforced the concentration of US equity inflows this week.

Emerging market equity ETPs attracted $556m last week, up from $313m previously, supported by a return to inflows 
across single-country strategies and continued demand for broad EM exposures.
Broad EM strategies remained the largest contributor at $322m, while single-country products reversed into inflows 
of $177m after recording -$69m of outflows. Regional EM strategies gathered $57m, down from $101m. Within 
regional exposures, APAC attracted $46m, up from $8m, while Latin American inflows moderated sharply to $7m from 
$86m. EM Europe gathered a more modest $3m.
At the country level, Brazil stood out with $162m of inflows, up significantly from $25m the previous week. South 
Korea remained positive at $43m, although demand slowed considerably from $340m, while Mexico added $9m. 
China continued to experience selling, but outflows eased to -$136m from -$411m previously. India recorded more 
limited outflows of -$7m. The combination of stronger Brazilian demand and reduced Chinese selling supported the 
improvement in overall EM flows.

Active equity ETPs attracted $714m last week, up from $665m previously. Global strategies remained the main 
source of demand, while North American inflows also strengthened and APAC allocations slowed.
Global exposures gathered $397m, up from $310m the previous week, followed by North America at $157m, 
compared with $129m previously. European strategies attracted $103m, broadly maintaining the prior week's $108m 
of inflows. APAC demand moderated considerably to $24m from $80m, while EM strategies remained positive at 
$31m.
By investment approach, Alpha-Seeking strategies continued to account for the majority of allocations, attracting 
$506m, broadly unchanged from $504m previously. Outcome strategies saw a notable increase in demand, with 
inflows rising to $214m from $129m, while Exposure products recorded modest outflows of -$7m.
Within Alpha-Seeking, CORE strategies remained the principal contributor at $503m, followed by Precision at $20m. 
Factors continued to experience selling, recording -$8m, while Thematics/Megatrends reversed into outflows of -$8m 
after attracting $6m previously.
Outcome flows were overwhelmingly concentrated in Dividend strategies, which attracted $205m, up from $111m the 
previous week. Sector and Other Outcome products gathered $4m and $3m respectively, while US Buffer added a 
marginal $1m. Within Exposure, CORE recorded outflows of -$7m, while Precision flows were broadly flat. The 
stronger Dividend contribution was the main source of growth in active equity allocations this week.
