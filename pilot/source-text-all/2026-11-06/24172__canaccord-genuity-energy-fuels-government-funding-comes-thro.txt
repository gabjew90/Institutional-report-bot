Government funding comes through for Donald
This morning, EFR announced that Export Finance Australia and the National
Reconstruction Fund Corporation have conditionally approved up to A$180 million
(US$126 million) for Phase 1 of the Donald Rare Earth and Mineral Sands project in
Australia. As a reminder, Donald is fully permitted and a JV with Astron Limited (EFR
earning in to 49%), which is still subject to a final investment decision.
Our take - positive. The JV has continued to make progress towards a final investment
decision for Donald, and the debt financing package announced today solves for the
majority of the remaining funding needed to FID the project.
Donald represents a key upstream component of Energy Fuel's emerging mine-to-
magnet supply chain. It will provide critical REE mineral feedstock to EFR's White
Mesa mill in Utah, where the company is looking to expand its rare earth separation
capabilities. The A$180M is part of a proposed A$200M senior project finance debt
facility and is still conditional and subject to due diligence and final approvals.
Financing terms
• A$180M facility with up to A$70M from EFA and A$110M from NRF
• The remaining A$20M will come from commercial lenders.
• The government tranches have a 10-year tenor and will be priced at BBSY plus a
margin.
• Proceeds of the debt will cover construction, commissioning, financing costs and
ramp-up costs at Donald.
Next steps for Donald
• A final investment decision for Donald is now expected before year-end 2026, a delay
from Q3/2026 previously.
• The construction window is expected to be 23-months.
• Phase 1 of the project is expected to produce about 9,000tpa of rare earth
concentrate and 225,000tpa of heavy mineral concentrate (zircon and titanium
minerals).
• Energy Fuels will buy all the rare earth concentrate from Donald as feed for its
separation plant at the White Mesa mill in Utah.
• Energy Fuels is earning a 49% JV interest in Donald by funding the first A$183M of
equity.
• To date, EFR has invested A$60M, for a 15.3% interest.
• To reach 49%, it still needs to invest ~A$86M (US$60M) along with issuing
US$14M of shares.
• The Donald Project has a Phase 1 capital cost of A$450 million
• The capital cost is largely covered by EFR's A$183M of equity (to buy in 49%) and
the A$200M debt package announced today
EFR well funded for remaining equity contribution to Donald: EFR ended Q2 2026
with ~$937 million in cash and marketable securities.
Reiterate BUY rating and C$30.00 target price: Our target price is based on 1.0x
NAV as at October 1, 2027.
