MMM out post close with a resolution of AFFF personal injury litigation. This is a meaningful step in a
multiyear (decade?) process of resolving liabilities associated with the chemical. $7.35 pre-tax nominal
commitment over nine years with a present value of $5.8bn. Addresses 99% of current personal injury
claims inside and outside the AFFF MDL – involving 115-120k plaintiffs. Includes a $50M payment in
2026, and no payments in 2027, with ~20% of payments due in the final two years, to align to 3M's
remaining payment obligations for other legacy settlements. Remaining claims – inside and outside the
MDL will be managed through settlement mechanisms. The company will take a $5.8bn charge in Q4 for
the present value. Company will host a conference call tomorrow at 8:30am to discuss. Separately, the
company authorizes $6bn in common stock repurchase in addition to the $1.5bn meaning under the exiting
authorization. Details on the company website here. We first addressed the point on MMM here – making
the point that the distressed multiple AND the liability could not go hand and hand. We use ITW as the
case study with the multiple rebounding in recent years but still holding potential. MMM at many points
traded at a similar multiple to ITW – currently ITW at 17x EBITDA with MMM at 13x. While the liability
comes out of the EV, the multiple likely expands closer to parity.
