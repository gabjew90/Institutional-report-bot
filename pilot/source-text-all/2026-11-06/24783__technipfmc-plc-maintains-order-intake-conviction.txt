TechnipFMC plc
Maintains order intake conviction
Our view: TechnipFMC reiterated its conviction for a strong order outlook
at its 3Q update, with expected intake of at least $14.5bn across the next
five quarters. We think this remains integral to FTI's investment case, and
is supported by the wider macro backdrop. We remain Outperform, $80
Price Target.
Key points:
Strong 3Q results from operational delivery and Services: FTI beat
consensus expectations for Subsea revenue and EBITDA, driven by higher
iEPCI project activity in Indonesia and Brazil alongside increased services
activity. We have updated our model to reflect 3Q results, including the
raised FY26 Subsea guidance ($9.6-9.8bn revenue, 21-22% EBITDA margin)
and the newly introduced FY27 Subsea guidance ($10-10.4bn revenue,
22.5-24% EBITDA margin).
Order intake remains key: 3Q26 order intake of $2.0bn, 0.9x book-to-bill,
fell below consensus expectations. However, the global Subsea pipeline
of opportunities (Exhibit 1) grew q/q and, underpinned by this and direct
client discussions, FTI has extended its view that it expects its Subsea orders
over the next five quarters to be at least $14.5bn. This reflects some timing
uncertainty around 4Q, but is ultimately unchanged from FTI's previous
communications that a Subsea order step-up is expected in FY27, and
maintains this view. Management reiterated that this momentum should
also lift the quality of inbound orders with substantially more Subsea 2.0,
iEPCI, and direct awards expected in FY27 and beyond.
Wider macro supports delivery: Underpinned by energy security concerns,
demand for more diversified energy supply, and competitive breakevens,
we see a strong demand outlook sustaining offshore oil and gas activity into
the 2030s. This is expected to sustain the high level of offshore project FIDs
into 2027 which is required for FTI to meet its targets and support growing
EBITDA outlook.
Maintaining Outperform recommendation, $80 Price Target: Our rounded
$80 Price Target is underpinned by a 12x FY27E EV/EBITDA multiple, in
line with current trading relative to FY26E EBITDA and slightly ahead
of mid-cycle. The raised revenue and FCF guidance, combined with the
initiation of FY27 Subsea financial guidance, provides increased visibility
on the earnings growth trajectory supporting our valuation. We see the
continued step-up in Subsea inbound orders underpinning the durability of
the offshore upcycle, and expect shares to re-rate accordingly as contracts
are announced.

Valuation
We value the energy services businesses using EV/EBITDA
multiples, as we have done across the sector for some time.
This allows us to factor in cyclicality, which comes with wide-
ranging margins, intermittent write-offs, M&A, and variability
in profits due to commodity prices.
Our $80/share price target is based on 12.0x FY27E EBITDA,
which reflects the forecast growth in revenues and EBITDA
margins, alongside higher FCF generation relative to previous
years from higher utilisation driven by the company’s backlog,
plus dividend and buyback. While this is towards the top end
of the company’s historical range it reflects the rerating of the
stock due to expanded addressable market from its product
offering and focused strategy. This supports our Outperform
rating.
Upside scenario
Our $115/share upside scenario assumes TechnipFMC
revenue and EBITDA margins in the Subsea business continue
to accelerate in FY26-28, on a similar trajectory to what we
have seen in FY23-25, driving group margins above 24%. In
turn, we would expect this to drive FCF higher than forecast
and for further buybacks to be authorised alongside an
enlarged cash dividend.
Downside scenario
Our downside scenario of $60/share is based on a material
shift in macro sentiment driving a lower outlook for order
intake. In this scenario, we think that the market could
downgrade growth expectations for FY27/28, and would be
willing to pay a lower multiple for the delivery of the existing
backlog, closer to 8x where the company was in FY24.
Investment summary
We rate TechnipFMC as Outperform, based on:
• TechnipFMC is an integrated offshore and onshore energy
service provider with patented technologies, a strong
operational track record and reputation in the energy
contractor market.
• The sustained level of offshore activity is underpinned by a
decade of underinvestment in offshore oil and gas, strong
breakeven economics for offshore projects and oil and
gas demand to replace declining production. As companies
continue to announce project FIDs and awards to the
offshore installation companies, we are seeing schedule
tightness from 2027, driving a sustained offshore cycle.
• Growing utilisation and improved pricing power is expected
to drive material margin growth for the business in FY26
and FY27, largely underpinned by the >$16bn order backlog,
largely in Subsea.
• Differentiated shareholder returns underpin a large part
of our investment thesis for our Outperform rating. The
company has top quartile implied TSR relative to market
cap compared to peers and potential for additional upside
in FY26E, and the highest ROACE (35%) across our global
energy services coverage for FY26E. The company benefits
from its lean operating strategy, with joint ownership or
leased vessels fulfilling most of its contracts and strategic
ownership of a smaller number of vessels. It intends to
return 70% of FCF to investors over the medium term.
Risks to rating and price target
We see commodity prices and contract execution as the
highest risks to TechnipFMC’s outlook.
• Commodity prices: TechnipFMC’s backlog and revenue/
cashflow outlook is driven by medium-term commodity
prices, which largely dictate the capital budgets of NOCs
and Major oil companies. We expect this to help drive the
momentum of the stock.
• Execution risk: Operational issues are a key risk for all
companies executing E&C contracts and delivering services
for energy companies. This is compounded by the company
having suffered from some operational issues in offshore
wind and on certain projects and geographies historically.
Execution errors and remediation can impact margins on
projects and can impact EBITDA margins for the group.
• Competitive bidding: While TechnipFMC differentiates itself
with its integrated offering, and early engagement to
limit competitive bidding, some contracts are still obtained
through a competitive bidding process. While service
quality, technological capability, reputation, and experience
are considered in client decisions, price remains one of the
main determining factors in most contract awards.

Key fundamental questions
Our view
What services does TechnipFMC
provide?
TechnipFMC is an integrated offshore and onshore energy service provider with
patented technologies, a strong operational track record and reputation in the
energy contractor market. It is an onshore and offshore contractor operating across
energy hubs, delivering projects from concept, to engineering, construction and
installation, as well as decommissioning.
What is TechnipFMC’s balance sheet
position?
TechnipFMC has a strong balance sheet with net cash of ~$602m at end FY25 (ex-
leases), comprising $1bn cash and $400m debt; after having returned ~$1bn to
shareholders in FY25. The strong FCF outlook under our forecasts and investment
grade rating provides confidence in the company’s ability to refinance its debt as
required.
What is TechnipFMC's dividend
policy?
TechnipFMC has committed to returning more than 60% of annual free cash flow
to shareholders until at least 2027, which we include in our forecasts out to 2028.
TechnipFMC extended its share repurchase authorisation increased by $1bn; current
authorisation allows for repurchases up to $1.2bn — more than 10% of outstanding
shares.
How does TechnipFMC’s bidding
pipeline look in Subsea?
In the Subsea business, TechnipFMC has given guidance that it believes it can secure
$10bn of orders in FY26 and expects the order intake to increase in FY27. In the
three-year period FY23-25, the company secured about $30bn of awards, in line with
guidance. The company estimates its tender pipeline to be ~$32bn for award over
the next two years (as at 3Q26).
Despite recent concerns around the commodity price, the company continues to see
a very strong pipeline of subsea bidding opportunities that serve as a strong baseline
for projects likely to be sanctioned in 2026, which notably includes New Frontiers.
Additionally, the FEED pipeline for subsea developments remains at a record level,
many of which are for projects advancing towards FID in the latter half of the
decade. These outlooks are underpinned by our commodity outlook and underpin
our forecasts out to 2027 and supplementary DCF valuation.

Key sustainability questions
This section is intended to highlight key sustainability discussion points relevant to this company, as well as our views on the outlook. Both the
questions we highlight and our responses will evolve over time as the dialogue between management, analysts and investors continues to
advance. We welcome any feedback on the topics.
Our view
What are the most material
sustainability issues facing this
company?
As an onshore and offshore contractor in the oil and gas industry, the most important
sustainability issues for TechnipFMC are emissions, safety, environmental impact
of activities (including leaks and employee safety) and contracts (governance).
The company publishes a sustainability scorecard and analysis within its annual
report each year, highlighting the efforts it takes to address these issues. These
demonstrate the company's progress, and it has a good track record for taking safety
and sustainability seriously.
Does the company integrate
sustainability considerations into its
strategy?
TechnipFMC uses a three-year Environmental, Social, and Governance (‘‘ESG’’)
Scorecard to set ambitious targets and hold itself accountable to these on an annual
and three-year basis. The company has a sustainability report within its annual
report and sets challenging targets, including actions tied to its "50 by 30" initiative
to reduce GHG emissions by 50% by 2030. Broadly its targets are in line with its peers
in both the Subsea and US-listed OFS.
What is diversity like at board /
management level?
Four out of nine of TechnipFMC's board of directors are female and there is broad
diversity in both experience and nationality across board members. This is in line
with industry norms.
TechnipFMC's senior management appears to have less gender diversity; however
this is also similar to peers.
Does TechnipFMC integrate
sustainability considerations into its
strategy and remuneration?
Performance against the company's sustainability scorecard has a 25% weighting in
the company's annual bonus determination.
What are the company's strategies
for preventing corruption and
bribery?
As is customary across the OFS industry, TechnipFMC has standards and processes
to provide a clear and comprehensive framework for its business in the countries in
which it operates to limit bribery and corruption risks.

Risks to rating and price target
We see commodity prices and contract execution as the highest risks to TechnipFMC’s outlook.
• Commodity prices: TechnipFMC’s backlog and revenue/cashflow outlook is driven by medium-term commodity prices, which
largely dictate the capital budgets of NOCs and Major oil companies. We expect this to help drive the momentum of the stock.
• Execution risk: Operational issues are a key risk for all companies executing E&C contracts and delivering services for energy
companies. This is compounded by the company having suffered from some operational issues in offshore wind and on certain
projects and geographies historically. Execution errors and remediation can impact margins on projects and can impact EBITDA
margins for the group.
• Competitive bidding: While TechnipFMC differentiates itself with its integrated offering, and early engagement to limit
competitive bidding, some contracts are still obtained through a competitive bidding process. While service quality, technological
capability, reputation, and experience are considered in client decisions, price remains one of the main determining factors in
most contract awards.
