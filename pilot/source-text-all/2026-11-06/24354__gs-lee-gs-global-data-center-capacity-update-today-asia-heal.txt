Data Centers. Michael Ng out with Global Data Center Capacity Update. We update our global data center capacity
forecasts using the latest data from 451 Research's database, and we now forecast 273GW of global data center
supply by 2030 (v. 217GW prior from our last update in July 2026), representing 170GW of additional capacity after
2025 and 2026/27/28 net additions of 25GW/32GW/35GW. For the US alone, we forecast 152GW of capacity by
2030 (v. 125GW prior), representing 103 GW of additional capacity post 2025. We recognize rising execution risk of
building and delivering data centers from power supply issues, regulatory uncertainty, and high leverage. That said,
we still see potential for sustained, outsized growth in capacity through projects with high visibility into power and
permitting.
 
AI Adoption Tracker. October 2026: Adoption Rises to 24.6%. AI-related investment growth remains strong,
particularly for semiconductors, where equity analysts expect global revenue to reach $872bn (ann.) by the end of
2026. AI adoption by firms rose by 0.8pp to 24.6% among US establishments (with adoption expected to rise to
28.6% in the next 6 months). Meanwhile, AI’s labor market impact has become increasingly visible in recent months.
We continue to observe employment drags in specific industries where AI-use cases have been established—such
as marketing, graphic design, customer service, and some tech occupations—and gains in construction jobs
exposed to data centers (up 315k since 2022 relative to broader construction employment trends). We continue to
observe large impacts on labor productivity in the limited areas where generative AI has been deployed but only
modest signs of economy-wide impacts.

Korea Financials. Sinyoung Park shares US marketing feedback: Shaken near term, but fundamentals point to
recovery. 
During our US marketing trip last week, we saw investors revisit KR Financials with market confidence weak amid
the Tech correction and continued foreign net selling. In our view, the bank share correction has overshot the
temporary fundamental softness we had anticipated, with the market pricing recent NIM weakness as permanent
when the drivers were transitory rather than a break in trend. Discussions centered on the marginal buyer and the
trigger for re-engagement, while within insurance Samsung Life drew the most questions following its recent hybrid
issuance.
 
CXMT (688825.SS). 3Q26 GM up to 90%; 4Q26 / 2027 revenues continue to grow on DDR5/ LPDDR6; 3Q26 beat;
Buy
The company has started mass production of DDR5 products with continuous shipment ramp up in 3Q26, and its
LPDDR6 has been adopted by the smartphone brand clients. Meanwhile, CXMT is also working on the next
generation DDR6 platform on verification of the engineering samples. Mgmt also highlighted the company has
developed 4F^2 architecture based on its in-house VSG (Vertical Surrounding Gate) technology, which could lower
the memory cell area with higher density.
 
MediaTek (2454.TW). AI ASIC ramp up with stronger momentum; 4Q26 guidance in line; TP up to NT$7,100, Buy

project also progresses on track, and the company is also expanding to new clients on AI ASIC products, supported
by its strong capability demonstrated by the first project. For the second project, mgmt. notes a couple of dies have
already been taped out, which is on track for high volume production in early 2026. We see the company's
technology advantages in SerDes IP and advanced packaging, with its 448G SerDes IP in measurement and
verification with key clients.
