Authors: Eric Potoker, CIO Equity Strategist, US Health care, UBS Financial Services Inc. (UBS FS); Diana Na, Healthcare Sector Equity Analyst, UBS AG

• Eli Lilly reported a strong 3Q26 earnings beat and raised 
guidance, driven by strength across its GLP-1 franchise. 
We expect meaningful upgrades to consensus estimates 
over the medium term, while the breadth of its incretin 
pipeline supports franchise durability, long-term growth, 
and valuations.
• Swiss health care also delivered a strong 3Q26 reporting 
season overall. Roche showed resilient underlying 
momentum with solid growth drivers and pipeline 
progress, Novartis beat expectations and raised its

into 2027, and Galderma was the standout with a broad-
based beat and sizable guidance upgrade.
• Overall, the 3Q26 results continue to reinforce a 
constructive view across the sector, with healthy 
underlying growth, improving earnings visibility, and 
supportive product/pipeline momentum.

Thoughts of the week
Eli Lilly: A strong beat and guidance raise
Eli Lilly delivered a strong third quarter, with adjusted 
revenue growth of approximately 34%, 4% above 
consensus estimates, and earnings per share (EPS) growth 
of 46%, 23% above expectations, when adjusting for one-
time items. The company raised 2026 revenue guidance by 
about 6%, or approximately 5% excluding a one-time third-
quarter benefit. GLP-1 products drove the upside across 
US and international markets and in both diabetes and 
obesity. Oral GLP-1 Foundayo missed consensus by about 
USD 40mn, but the shortfall was small relative to the USD 
1.1bn injectable GLP-1 outperformance. We view Foundayo 
as an important part of LLY’s obesity/diabetes portfolio over 
the long term, especially in international markets. Near term, 
we think Foundayo’s performance in the US, in particular, is 
somewhat limited by oral Wegovy’s first-mover advantage 
and brand awareness. Over time, we think LLY will build 
Foundayo’s brand awareness and gradually gain market 
share as the overall GLP-1 market continues to grow.
We think these strong third-quarter results will drive forward 
consensus revenue and earnings estimates meaningfully 
higher for 2027-30. Longer term, Lilly’s deep incretin 
pipeline could extend franchise durability into the 2040s 
and mitigate the impact from Mounjaro/Zepbound patent 
expiration in 2036. We think the durability of LLY’s obesity/
diabetes portfolio will help moderate potential longer-term 
pressure on LLY’s valuation; we also believe LLY’s strong and 
durable revenue and earnings growth support its current 
valuation and continue to see attractive upside to consensus 
estimates.

The key debate remains whether Novartis’ newer growth 
drivers can offset patent expirations and sustain the

Rhapsido is also becoming a more meaningful part of the

broaden through 4Q and into 2027, which should support 
further sales acceleration.
Leqvio is another important asset to watch. The cholesterol-
lowering medicine has two major cardiovascular outcomes 
trials approaching: ORION-4 is expected to read out in 4Q26, 
followed by VICTORION-2-PREVENT in 2027. Management 
flagged confidence that both studies will be positive and 
support continued growth.
However, on profitability, core margins are expected to 
decline sequentially in 4Q, and Novartis sees some pressure 
over the next couple of years as US Xolair biosimilars reduce 
the high-margin royalty and other revenue contribution. 
Management remains committed to reaching a 40% core 
operating margin by 2029 at the latest.
Overall, the 3Q update reinforces our positive stance on 
Novartis. Near-term earnings expectations are improving, 
growth should accelerate into year-end, and we continue to 
take the view that newer products can sustain growth into 
2027 and beyond.
Roche: Good underlying momentum with giredestrant 
in focus for 2027
Roche delivered a good 3Q26 (7% CER growth), with 
sales coming in 2% ahead of consensus, supported by 
strength across several Pharma products. Diagnostics was 
broadly in line despite continued pricing pressure in China. 
Roche continues to expect mid-single-digit sales growth 
and high-single-digit core EPS growth at constant exchange 
rates for FY26. Management expects sales momentum to 
carry into 2027 alongside further margin expansion, despite 
continued investment in R&D.
The more important positive is the increasing visibility on 
Roche's next wave of growth drivers. Management remains 
confident in a strong launch trajectory for giredestrant 
and views consensus expectations of CHF 500-600mn of 
sales next year as reasonable. We continue to take the 
view that giredestrant has the potential to be a significant 
drug in terms of peak sales (CHF 10bn). Fenebrutinib 
could also provide a further meaningful opportunity in 
multiple sclerosis. At the same time, momentum in the 
existing portfolio remains healthy: Vabysmo remains on 
track for low-double-digit growth with a CHF 6bn peak-sales 
ambition.
On balance, we believe the quarter reinforces a constructive 
setup into 2027 with underlying Pharma demand remaining 
healthy, foreign exchange pressure easing, margins 
expected to expand, and upside potential from multiple new 
products that we expect to strengthen the medium-term 
growth profile.
Galderma: Firing on all cylinders
Galderma's 3Q26 update was another excellent quarter, 
with sales coming in 5% ahead of consensus and upside 
across all three divisions. Group sales grew 26% on a 
CER basis to USD 1.63bn, with Injectables, Skincare, and 
Therapeutic Dermatology all beating expectations.
Most importantly, Nemluvio sales came in better, despite 
recent concerns around prescription trends and Ebglyss 
competition. Management pushed back on the read-
across from IQVIA data and remained highly confident in 
the underlying trajectory. Nemluvio has already achieved 
approximately 45% share in prurigo nodularis and 
approximately 9% in atopic dermatitis, has turned 
profitable, and management continues to see a path to 
more than USD 1bn in sales globally this year, potentially 
reaching USD 1bn in the US alone.
Unsurprisingly given the momentum, Galderma raised 
2026 organic sales growth guidance from 19-21% to 
22.5-23.5%, while retaining the approximately 26% core 
EBITDA margin target. The new sales range implies 
around 2% upside to consensus at the midpoint. Just 
as importantly, management maintained confidence in 
medium-term margin expansion.
Regarding the recent ZO Skin Health acquisition, the 
company flagged that this should be complementary to 
Alastin, but also offers meaningful channel and geographic 
opportunities. We expect the deal to be earnings-accretive 
from 2028 onward. On Relfydess, outstanding FDA 
manufacturing issues remain, but management highlighted 
tangible progress at Uppsala and sounded cautiously 
optimistic following a productive Type A meeting.
We view this quarter as being as good as could reasonably 
have been hoped for, with a broad-based beat, a sizable 
guidance upgrade, and reassuring commentary on most of 
the key areas of investor concern. Galderma continues to 
stand out for both the scale and consistency of its growth 
across aesthetics, skincare, and therapeutics.

Equity selection system
CIO Selected stocks are intended to support the UBS House View (see global asset class preferences definitions) and are 
chosen based on their overall risk-return profiles and other relevant considerations. The starting universe for the CIO Selected 
designation generally consists of securities covered by IB Global Research and rated Buy or Neutral. From that universe, CIO 
Equities applies its own investment judgment, considering a variety of factors such as the fundamental outlook, expected 
performance relative to an identified benchmark, market, sector and industry trends, risk and return characteristics, downside 
considerations, diversification benefits, and other relevant factors. No single factor is necessarily determinative. Rather, CIO 
considers these factors collectively and holistically in determining which securities warrant inclusion in a CIO Equity Selection 
List.
In certain circumstances, CIO may designate a security that is not covered by IB Global Research as CIO Selected where 
CIO determines that the security presents a relevant investment opportunity and applicable eligibility, reasonable-basis and 
approval requirements for such selections have been satisfied. A security will no longer be eligible for inclusion in any CIO 
Selection List if IB Global Research changes its rating to Sell. In such cases, CIO Equities will remove it from applicable 
Selection Lists within a reasonable timeframe.
• The CIO Selected designation is not a rating.
• Inclusion reflects CIO Equities' current preference within the defined universe and objective of the applicable Selected List.
• It does not create a separate Buy, Neutral, or Sell rating, does not establish a CIO Equities price target, and does not 
supersede or modify the underlying IB Global Research rating or price target.
• Exclusion or removal of a security for any reason other than a change in IB Global Research rating to “Sell” does not 
indicate a negative investment view or constitute a recommendation to sell.
• Inclusion of a security on a selection list does not constitute initiation of coverage or imply continuous coverage. Likewise, 
removal from a selection list does not constitute termination of coverage.
• Recipients should not assume that a security remains CIO Selected after the date of this publication. The current status 
of a security should be verified using the most recent CIO publication.
A CIO Selected designation reflects CIO Equities’ current, dynamic view within the applicable list’s opportunity set. It is not a 
view on the security’s expected standalone absolute performance. The Selection Lists reflect CIO Equities’ views at a specific 
point in time and are subject to change without notice. CIO strategists will review and update the selections as market 
conditions, investment opportunities, and their assessment of relevant factors evolve.
CIO Equity Portfolios
CIO Equity Portfolios are published research model portfolio comprising stocks with specified weights that reflect each 
security’s allocation within the portfolio and, in aggregate, total 100%. These weights represent portfolio allocations and 
are not active weights or measures of overweight or underweight relative to the benchmark. Portfolios may focus on 
sectors, countries, regions, investment themes, or another stated objective. Each portfolio is constructed relative to an 
identified benchmark, which is used to define the relevant opportunity set, but the portfolio is not designed to replicate the 
benchmark exactly. The CIO view applies to the portfolio in aggregate, including how the holdings work together, rather than 
each individual holding. It is a research model, not a client-specific portfolio, managed investment strategy, or guaranteed 
outcome. CIO Portfolios do not represent actual accounts, trading or investments and performance will not be published.
Portfolio weights are not security-level ratings or rankings and do not express the relative or absolute investment merit, 
expected performance, or degree of conviction associated with any security or issuer. A higher weight should not be 
interpreted as reflecting a more favorable view, greater conviction, or higher expected return.

investment view of the security; establish or imply continuous research coverage; or constitute the initiation of research
