Ferrari (UBS research rec Buy) Q3 Review: A Clear Road Ahead
Report by Zuzanna Pusz, Patrick Hummel, Robert Krankowski, Emily Teo, Ana Gogichaishvili
A more durable growth story; Reiterate Buy
• Strong demand across the range, with order book reaching into 2028 and Luce fully covered for 2027,
continues to highlight Ferrari's ability to sustain growth (UBSe cars and spare parts +9% OSG) while
preserving exclusivity.
• In our view, this reinforces the durability of Ferrari's growth story at a time when many luxury peers are
facing softer and more volatile demand.
• Personalisation at >21% of sales and very healthy resale values provide further support, with our latest
UBS Evidence Lab survey (>Access dataset) highlighting the latter as an important driver of demand for
Ferrari cars.
• Against this backdrop, UBS expect sales growth at more than 2x the pace of the broader Luxury sector
and ahead of Ferrari's historical avg. in 2026E and 2027E.
• UBS expect this to be accompanied by further margin expansion (UBSe EBITDA margin 39.5% in FY27),
with a clear path towards at least 40% EBITDA margin by 2030.
• While Ferrari’s premium to luxury peers remains elevated versus historical levels, UBS believe investors are
increasingly focused on absolute valuation, with RACE’s P/E still below its long-term average, rather than
relative multiples, particularly as Luxury-sector valuations remain near cyclical lows.
• Reiterate Buy.

Ferrari NV (UBS research rec Buy) Conf call feedback: Personalisation keeps delivering
Report by Zuzanna Pusz, CFA, Patrick Hummel, CFA, Robert Krankowski, Emily Teo, Ana Gogichaishvili
Management confirms ASP in H2 could be better than H1
• One of the key areas of focus during today’s call with Ferrari CEO Benedetto Vigna and CFO Antonio Picca
Piccon was the development of ASP into H2 and the contribution from the F80. Management reiterated that it
expects H2 ASP to be slightly above H1, supported by continued strength in personalisation.
• Personalisation once again performed ahead of expectations, and now accounts for more than 21% of
Cars and Spare Parts revenues (versus medium-term target of ~19%), with management expecting the strong
contribution to continue into Q4. Part of the ASP strength also reflects elevated personalisation on special
models, which are particularly concentrated this year and are likely to normalise going forward. Visibility on

3
personalisation beyond Q4 is naturally more limited than for the order book, as customers typically finalise
personalisation choices only three-to-four months ahead of delivery.
Volumes growth set to reaccelerate in Q4
• Management expects shipments to return to yoy growth in Q4, helped by easier comparatives and
broader global distribution of the new range.
• F80 deliveries, however, remain in the relative gradual stages of the ramp, with ~50 units delivered during
the quarter, pointing to a deliberately steady progression.
• Ferrari Luce execution also remains firmly on track, with production under way, first deliveries expected in
Q4 and the entire 2027 allocation already sold.
• Management highlighted broad-based demand across customer types, age groups and geographies, while
Q1 2027 media and client test drives could provide a further catalyst as customers gain physical exposure to
the car.
• Finally, when asked about latest development of residual values, management highlighted very healthy
levels across both models and geographies, stressing that in some areas consumer prefer the hybrids over
ICE.
FX turning more favourable
• When asked about recent move in the currency and its potential implications for Ferrari, management said
FX is becoming less of a headwind, with Ferrari now expecting a €150–200m negative impact versus its
previous indication of ~€200m, helped by a more favourable USD spot rate.
• When it comes to 2027, management refrained from providing much colour aside from stressing its 12
months rolling FX hedging policy with it currently hedged at ~70%.
Valuation: Buy, PT US$450
• RACE trades on 19.3x and 18.0x 2027E and 2028E EV/EBITDA, respectively. Our PT is based on a relative
EV/EBITDA methodology.

Report by Zuzanna Pusz, Patrick Hummel, Robert Krankowski, Emily Teo & Ana Gogichaishvili
How did the results compare vs expectations?
• Q3 revenue of €1,896m was -1% below cons (+2% vs. UBSe), with EBITDA of €753m, +1% above cons.
(+3% vs. UBSe), and an EBITDA margin of 39.7% vs. cons. 39.0% (UBSe 39.3%).
What were the most noteworthy areas in the results?
• Q3 organic sales growth was +10% (cons +9%, UBSe +8%).
• The core Cars and Spare Parts division posted organic sales growth of +10% (cons +9%, UBSe +8%),
with volumes -1% (cons, UBSe -1%).
• Elsewhere, sponsorships up +7% (cons +6%, UBSe +4%), other +17% (cons +21%, UBSe +18%).
• Q3 EBIT margin came in at 29.7% (cons and UBSe 29.5%).
Has the company's outlook/guidance changed?
• Yes, Ferrari raised FY26 guidance with sales ~€7.65bn (vs. ~€7.6bn previously, cons €7.64bn, UBSe
€7.60bn), adj. EBITDA ≥€3.0bn (vs.≥€2.97bn previously, cons €3.00bn, UBSe €2.98bn) with an EBITDA
margin ≥39.2% (vs. ≥39.0% previously, cons 39.2% and UBSe 39.1%) and Industrial FCF up to €1,650m (vs.
≥€1,550m previously, cons €1,615m, UBSe €1,583m).
• The press release also mentions "Our order book now reaches into 2028, and we still have two more
launches to come this year" as well as noting that "Luce fully allocated for 2027".
How would we expect investors to react?
• In our view, the initial negative reaction is largely a function of expectations and positioning rather
than a fundamental disappointment.
• Some investors were looking for more from the guidance range (EBITDA guidance is in line with current
consensus), despite the upgrade coming in ahead of our initial expectations.
• For us, any weakness today presents a further buying opportunity, as the order-book commentary is
particularly reassuring, while the EBITDA margin progression is also encouraging.
• Attention now turns to the conference call, with three key areas of focus:
• more colour on the order book by specific models;
• contribution from personalisation; and
• F80 deliveries in the quarter
