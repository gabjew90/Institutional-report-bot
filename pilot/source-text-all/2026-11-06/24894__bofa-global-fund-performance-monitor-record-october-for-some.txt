Global Fund Performance Monitor 
Record October for some funds 
     
   
 
 
High ASR funds and Yield funds had a record October 
In October, 46% of Active funds globally outperformed benchmark (median relative 
return -0.18%), but a few groups of funds achieved record results.  Funds with a high 
Active Share Ratio (>80%) had the best month on record (data since 2015) with 65% of 
funds outperforming benchmark by a median +1.16%.  It was also a record month for 
Yield funds with 72% outperforming by a median +1.77%.  Among other fund groups, 
66% of Value outperformed (+0.87%) but only 36% of Growth outperformed (-0.42%).  
Recent drivers of relative performance seem to be interest rates, market breadth, and 
sentiment towards AI-related global themes. 
Yield funds’ largest active stock exposures 
Globally, Yield funds’ largest overweight positions are GSK, Sanofi, Targa Resources, 
TotalEnergies, DT Midstream, Johnson & Johnson, and Taylor Wimpey.  Yield funds’ 
largest underweights are NVIDIA, Apple, Amazon, Microsoft, HSBC plc, and ASML. 
High Active Share Ratio funds’ largest active exposures 
High ASR (>80%) funds’ largest overweight positions are TSMC, Edenred, SPIE, Games 
Workshop, Weir, SpaceX, and CATL.  These funds’ largest underweight positions are 
Apple, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Broadcom, Meta, and Tesla.

In this report we analyse relative returns of large long-only equity funds which declare 
portfolio holdings on a regular basis (frequency differs by fund, but is typically monthly 
or quarterly), manage more than US$500m in any one region, for which we have 
identified and have access to their benchmark, and which have a ready-made “fund 
price” available from various data sources (FactSet, Bloomberg). The table below 
summarizes the total number of funds, their funds under management and number of 
unique benchmarks used in this analysis.

We divide funds by type based on various characteristics, including Region, Investment 
Style, Active / Passive, Active Share Ratio (ASR), Funds Under Management (FUM), 
Market Cap of Stocks, Portfolio Size (# of stocks in the fund), and Turnover. 
 
For each period of return we currently include in the report (1m, 3m, 6m, 12m, Year-to-
date, 2yr, 3yr) we only include funds which were in our analysis and have price return for 
the entire period in question.  We calculate relative returns of each fund for each period.  
Relative return is defined as the difference between a fund's return and the benchmark 
return.  We calculate Median, Top Quartile, and Bottom Quartile relative returns across 
all funds for each group of fund in the analysis. 
 
The data in this report is based on the most recent month-end data.  The number of 
funds may change each month based on data availability and an annual refresh of the 
funds included in this analysis.

 Global Quant Strategy Publications 
Global Wave 
Our regime Indicator.  The Global Wave quantifies multi-year trends in global economic 
activity and major turning points in the cycle.  The direction of the global cycle 
influences equity market performance, regional allocation, country allocation, style 
allocation, and stock selection. The Bunkers are stocks with style characteristics for a 
downturn and the Boosters are stocks for an upturn. 
Triple Momentum Allocator 
Monthly report highlighting the benefits of combining three types of momentum, namely 
Earnings Momentum, Price Momentum, and News Momentum.  Popular uses include 1) 
identifying stocks with the strongest/weakest Triple Momentum, 2) using Triple 
Momentum for Allocation (regions/sectors/countries), 3) monitoring changes in Triple 
Momentum to highlight when signals are fading and when new opportunities are 
emerging, and 4) monitoring time series in Triple Momentum to show which sectors and 
region-sectors have been in vogue at any point in time in the past. 
Global Positioning in Stocks 
Our Positioning analysis monitors the overweights and underweights of active long-only 
funds globally (AUM $38 trillion) by aggregating active positions by stock, sector, 
country, and region.  Additionally, “Equity Flow” indicates which stocks, sectors, 
countries, and regions long-only funds are buying and selling (dollar value of stocks 
bought and sold each month).  Also, the challenge is to determine which crowded trades 
may outperform and which may face performance hurdles.  We combine our positioning 
analysis with Triple Momentum to highlight which crowded positions have positive 
catalysts “Crowded Positives” and which have negative catalysts “Crowded Negatives”.  
Similarly, we screen for under-owned stocks with negative momentum “Under-owned 
Negatives” and under-owned stocks with positive momentum “Under-owned Positives”.  
There is a Global version (Global Positioning in stocks) and an Asia version (Pacific 
Positioning).  
Quant View of Global Themes 
In this regular report, we quantitatively determine which stocks have exposure to various 
Investment Themes (AI, Robotics, Space, Luxury Lifestyle, etc.).  For each theme, we 
analyze long-only positioning, valuation, and trends in Triple Momentum for 1) the 
Theme in aggregate, and 2) each stock within each Theme.  We refresh the analysis 
regularly to include emerging Investment Themes. 
High Frequency Monitor 
A weekly report monitoring high frequency data to help equity investors track short-term 
changes in market-moving data in order to swiftly identify hot topics and turning points. 
Topics include performance, earnings revisions, news, tactical indicators, commodities, 
rates, valuation, geographic revenue exposure, Triple Momentum, and Global Investment 
Themes. 
Global Contenders & Defenders 
A stock selection model based on inexpensive momentum.  This model has been running 
live since 2002.  For each stock, this report graphically shows how consensus earnings 
expectations have changed over decades versus the market, and whether recent 
earnings revisions appear to have been reflected in relative share price performance. 
Popular ways of using this analysis include 1) reviewing the Contenders (buys) and 
Defenders (Sells) lists, 2) perusing individual stock charts, 3) monitoring region, country
