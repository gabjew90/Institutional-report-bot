Copers Labour risk - Chile Copper: Labour Negotiations Add Another Supply Risk Into 2027 (
negotiations are most relevant for BHP, RIO, LUN and ANTO: Based on the scale of their operations and
the unions involved, BHP has the greatest exposure through Escondida and Spence (GSe ~75% of copper
production), alongside RIO through Escondida (~35%), LUN at Caserones (~40%), and ANTO through
Los Pelambres and Centinela (~55% and ~30%). Escondida and Caserones have a history of industrial
action, while the recent strike at Centinela provides a relevant precedent for ANTO, although the
upcoming Los Pelambres negotiations are with a smaller union. GLEN and AAL have more limited
exposure through Lomas Bayas and El Soldado, respectively, with neither operation having a history of
industrial action.
