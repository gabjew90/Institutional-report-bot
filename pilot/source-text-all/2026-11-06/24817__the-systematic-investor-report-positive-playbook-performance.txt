Positive playbook performance: picking pockets of persistence

◼ Uncertainty has been a feature of markets this year, but has helped provide pockets of 
profitable persistence for systematic investors as insurance premia rise, prices drift, and stocks 
are perturbed. Here we provide an update of Playbook performance ahead of the US midterms.  
◼ Persistent premia: selling equity tail risk offered the best risk-adjusted performance YTD (Fig. 1 
and overleaf for all strategies). As we wrote last week, equity volatility selling has been resilient, with 
uncertainty progressively priced into the downside tails in particular. This resilience continued 
again last Friday as S&P tail variance delivered a positive return and Nasdaq gap risk a very modest 
decline. So far this year a cautious market testing the upside has kept associated risk premium flat. 
◼  There are several sources of uncertainty in USDJPY, but pricing has again provided an adequate 
buffer for selling gap risk. Doing so has printed a new performance high every month, while FX carry, 
after a solid H1, has turned down after the Brazilian election and rate differentials weighing on MXN. 
◼ Persistent prices: market timing is notoriously difficult, and trend-following has been an 
effective way to manage beta this year. Commodity trend delivered through energy- and climate-
related disruption. Rates trend was helpful in September, contributing to positive cross-asset trend 
performance YTD, and commodity basis-momentum, which looks at the slope of commodity 
curves, helped in March/April and printed a new high in October, performing strongly YTD (Fig. 2).  
◼ Persistent perturbations: single-stock volatility and dispersion have helped long-short 
strategies deliver positive performance YTD. Such strategies aid diversification through lower 
market exposure. Equity multi-factor returns have been steady (Fig. 1), while mean-reversions’ have 
been more volatile as evolving correlation dynamics present an opportunity and challenge (Fig. 2).  
◼ In hedging, being long rates volatility at longer maturities helped portfolios in September across 
USD, EUR, JPY, though giving back some returns in October. Combining this with short-dated vol 
carry provided additional returns through the year (Fig. 2 for USD). US equity hedges have in general 
lost in a steady market, while we continue to prefer Synthetic Down Variance for tail protection.
