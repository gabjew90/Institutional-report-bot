Jack Henry & Associates Inc. (JKHY): FY1Q27 Key Takeaways: Decent 
quarter in a good tape
4 November 2026 | 9:04PM EST
Bottom line: JKHY put up solid results, but the stock outperformed significantly, 
which we think likely reflected the positive tape and the weaker results at peers. 
Overall feedback on the print focused on the margin and revenue outperformance in 
the quarter, and while we agree that these items were strong, the company left the 
margin guide unchanged, and pointed to some timing related items that resulted in a 
stronger 1H relative to 2H. On a more fundamental level, JKHY showed strong 
momentum on Bank Core wins, and continued its recent trend of “trifecta” deals, 
while also leaning into the cyber and payments products it has recently released to 
banks. Putting it together, 1Q saw strong momentum, but also benefited from some 
pull-forward benefits, and we think the reaction in the stock appears a bit extreme 
for relatively modest estimates revisions. We believe JKHY continues to execute at a 
very strong level, and believe the stock will continue to be anchored by the 
multi-year acceleration trajectory the company laid out at investor day. However, at 
~23x our C2027 EPS numbers, we see shares as relatively fully valued, and we look 
for a better entry point on valuation or a more sustained improvement in top line 
momentum in order to get more constructive.  
Consolidated results: JKHY reported F1Q27 adjusted revenue of ~$682mn, +1% vs 
consensus. Adjusted segment income came in at ~$309mn vs. $301mn consensus, 
with a ~45.3% margin (~44.8% consensus). JKHY posted non-GAAP operating 
income of ~$193mn (vs. consensus of ~$180mn) and GAAP EPS of $2.28, ahead of 
the Street’s $2.09 estimate. 
Formal Guidance: JKHY provided their updated FY2027 guidance. The company 
expects non-GAAP revenue between $2,667mn-$2,687mn, guiding for $30mn in 
deconversion fees. The company also expects non-GAAP operating expenses to fall 
between $2,024mn-$2,033mn (vs. consensus of $2,020mn) resulting in non-GAAP 
operating income between $643mn-$653mn with margins between 24.1% and 
24.3%. From a GAAP perspective, the company expects revenues between 
$2,698mn-$2,718mn, with expenses between $2,033mn-$2,043mn resulting in 
operating income between $665mn-$676mn with margins between 24.7%-24.9%. 
Putting it altogether, the company expects 2027 GAAP EPS between $7.47-$7.51. 
Additionally, management called out on-prem implementations and consulting 
engagements in Core revenue (ex. those items the core segment grew 8.6%). During

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.
