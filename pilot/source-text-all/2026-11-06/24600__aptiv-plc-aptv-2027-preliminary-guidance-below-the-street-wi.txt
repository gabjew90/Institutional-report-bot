Results 
Aptiv reported total 3Q26 revenue of $3,199 mn (down 2% qoq, up 2% yoy, and up 2% 
yoy when adjusted for currency exchange, commodity movements and acquisitions), 
which was within the guidance range of $3,120-$3,220 mn, and 1% above both GS at 
$3,170 mn and the Street (FactSet) at $3,167 mn. 
Non-auto revenue was solid in 3Q (+11% yoy organically), but auto organic revenue was 
down 1% yoy with growth in line with its customer weighted vehicle production. 
3Q26 bookings were ~$4 bn for Aptiv, with the company noting YTD bookings are >$13 
bn. 
3Q26 adjusted EBITDA was $567 mn (17.7% of revenue) compared to the guidance 
range of $545 mn-$575 mn (17.5-17.9% of revenue) and both GS and the Street at $560 
mn (17.7% of revenue). 
Operating EPS of $1.42 was above the high end of guidance of $1.25-$1.35, $0.10 
above GS at $1.32 and $0.09 above the Street at $1.33. 
3Q26 FCF (operating cash flow less capex) was about $194 mn. The company used 
~$233 mn to repurchase shares in the quarter. 
Key commentary from the call 
For the full year, management reiterated that it is on track to reach ~$20 bn in bookings, 
but noted there could be a push-out in 4Q bookings due to negotiations on commercial 
recoveries. Moreover, the company highlighted that it grew YTD China bookings by 30% 
of which >20% are tied to export programs. 
On China export revenue, the company discussed that ~10% of its revenue is on export 
platforms and given it bookings momentum it expects this to grow to the mid-teens 
range in 2027. 
Management noted that it continues to make progress in non-automotive markets. 
Specifically, the company commented that it is on track to generate >$3 bn of revenue in 
2026 (including $2 bn in A&D, telecom & datacom, and diversified industrial), which is up 
from ~$2.9 bn in 2025.  
On the headwinds from two German luxury OEMs, the company discussed a headwind 
of ~$250-$300 mn of which about a 1/3 comes from softer schedules on export 
volumes from Europe to China and the balance is related to program changeovers. 
On capital allocation, Aptiv highlighted that for 2026 it plans to buyback at least $650 
mn in shares (with YTD repurchases at ~$560 mn) and the company expects to use 
100% of its FCF on buybacks in 2027.

Outlook 
Recall Aptiv completed the spinoff of its EDS segment on April 1. 
Aptiv formally guided 4Q26 revenue to $3,110 mn-$3,210 mn which compares to GS 
prior at $3,188 mn, the Street $3,192 mn, and the previously implied 4Q guide of $3,188

The company expects 4Q26 adjusted EBITDA to be in the range of $582 mn-$642 mn. 
This compares to GS prior at $618 mn, the Street at $627 mn, and the previously implied 
4Q guide of $618 mn at the midpoint. The adjusted EBITDA margin of 19.4% at the 
midpoint compares to GS prior at 19.4%, the Street at 19.6%, and the previously implied 
4Q guide of 19.4%. 
Aptiv guided 4Q26 operating EPS to $1.35-$1.55, which compares to GS prior at $1.59, 
the Street at $1.62, and the previously implied 4Q guidance of $1.84 at the midpoint. 
The company adjusted its 2026 pro forma revenue guidance to $12,650 mn-$12,750 mn 
from $12,600 mn-$12,800 mn prior, which compares to GS prior at $12,700 mn and the 
Street at $12,703 mn. Aptiv’s guidance still implies 2% yoy organic growth at the 
midpoint. 
Aptiv maintained its adjusted EBITDA guidance for 2026 of $2,310 mn-$2,370 mn 
(18.4% of sales at the midpoint), which compares to GS prior at $2,340 mn (18.4% of 
sales) and the Street at $2,345 mn (18.5% of sales). 
Aptiv maintained its 2026 operating EPS guidance of $5.60-$5.80, which compares to 
both GS prior and the Street at $5.70. 
Aptiv adjusted its 2026 free cash flow guidance to $625 mn-$675 mn from $625 
mn-$725 mn prior. 
The company also gave a preliminary 2027 revenue and adjusted EBITDA margin outlook 
of $13.2-13.5 bn/17.2%-17.5% (which implies an adjusted EBITDA range of $2,270 
mn-$2,365 mn). This compares to GS prior at $12,968 mn/18.7%/$2,429 mn and the 
Street at $13,206 mn/19.2%/$2,644 mn. 
The company noted this assumes a weighted global production outlook of down 2% at 
the low end and about flat at the high end. In addition, Aptiv is expecting $700 mn of 
cost inflation recoveries tied to semis (and mostly from memory) and a headwind of 
$300 mn related to net program roll-offs and lower exports from the EU to China. 
Additionally, the company expects 1 point of adjusted EBITDA margin dilution from 
material inflation pass-throughs. Notably, management discussed that beyond these 
items, its guidance includes an element of incremental conservatism.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.  
M&A Rank 
Across our global coverage, we examine stocks using an M&A framework, considering both qualitative factors and quantitative factors (which may vary 
across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring 
companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 
representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard 
departmental guidelines we incorporate an M&A component into our target price. M&A rank of 3 is considered immaterial and therefore does not 
factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.
