Mirion Technologies (MIR)
Q3 Quick Take: Margin Miss May Overshadow
Growth Outlook
Summary
We think MIR's Q3 results may be taken negatively as Adj EBITDA slightly
missed ($65 million vs. consensus of $69 million) and while '26 revenue
guidance was increased (22%-24% to 24%-25%), Adj EBITDA guidance
was pushed to low-end of prior range. We think a fair amount of pessimism
is already priced in given valuation (13.7x EV/EBITDA '27 vs. peers almost
16x), but this update will maintain the questions on MIR's margins and
expectations looking into 2027.
Key Points
• Q3 2026 Solid Revenue, Lower Margins: MIR had mixed Q3 results.
Revenue of $278 million was slightly above consensus/our estimate
of $276 million but Adj EBITDA of $65 million was below consensus
of $69 million and our estimate of $67 million. Adj EBITDA margin of
23.3% missed management guidance of higher margins y/y (Q3 2025
was 23.5%).
• Guidance Update Mixed Bag: MIR updated 2026 guidance to reflect
stronger growth but lower margins.
○Total revenue growth of 24%-25%, up from 22%-24%
○Organic revenue growth increased from 5%-7% to 7%-8%. Q4 is
expected to have mid-teens+ organic growth for Nuclear & Safety and
mid-single-digits+ for Medical.
○Adjusted EBITDA narrowed to low-end from $285-$300 million to
$285-$292 million, comparing to consensus of $290 million and our
estimate is $287 million. Adj EBITDA margin decreases from 25%-26%
to 24.7%-25.2%
○Implied Q4 Adj EBITDA of ~$101-$108 million is a bit better than
consensus of $102 million and our estimate of $100 million.
○Adjusted FCF increased from $155-$175 million to $170-$185 million.
○Non-GAAP EPS changed from $0.48-$0.55 to $0.50-$0.54.
• Continued Success in Large Opportunity Pipeline: Another
impressive quarter with its large opportunity pipeline. MIR booked $140
million in large orders in Q3, an $85 million increase from the $55 million
it had secured in Q3 at the time of Q2 earnings. The Company is also
targeting $125 million for Q4, which would bring full-year to $369 million,
an increase from last quarter's full-year opportunity set of $318 million.
• Another Record for Backlog: Large orders and solid across-the-board
performance drove backlog to a new record at ~$1.2 billion (up from 1H
2026 prior record of ~$1.1 billion). Organic orders grew 62% y/y to $340
million and total orders grew 85% y/y to $386 million. Nuclear power end
market had organic order growth of 105%, largely driven by the installed
base, showing that MIR is more than just a bet on newbuilds/SMRs.
• Buyback: MIR repurchased 3.6 million shares in Q3 for $56 million (~
$15.56/Share). $235 million is remaining on its $250 million buyback that
was recently refreshed.

Company Description
MIR engages in the provision of radiation safety, science and medicine. It operates through the Nuclear
and Safety, and Technologies segments. The Company was founded by Thomas Logan in 2005 and is
headquartered in Atlanta, GA.
Valuation
Our PT is based on 18x EV/EBITDA ('27) with an additional $1.64/Share ascribed via DCF of the nuclear
power opportunity from 2030-2050.
Risks to the achievement of price target
Highly Regulated Industries: MIR operates in highly regulated industries, including nuclear power,
governments and medical. Noncompliance or changes in regulations could negatively impact the
Company’s performance.
Debt/Leverage: The Company uses debt to fund a portion of its operations. This creates a risk for common
shareholders in that the debt must be serviced and MIR must adhere to various covenants, and the debt
has claims on assets that are senior to common stockholders.
Exchange Rates: MIR conducts business around the world, which exposes it to exchange rate risks relative
to its U.S. Dollar reporting currency. Changes in exchange rates may negatively impact the Company’s
performance.
Acquisition Risks: MIR has been acquisitive in recent history and may continue to do so in the future. There
are no assurances that acquisitions will generate the benefits expected by the Company.
