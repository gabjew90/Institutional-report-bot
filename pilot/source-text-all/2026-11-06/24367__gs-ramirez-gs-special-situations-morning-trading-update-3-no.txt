GS Special Situations | Morning Trading Update
Topical Situations: LITE/MRVL/VAL/RIG/ROKU/FOXA
 
M&A remains active heading into November, with LITE/MRVL in focus after Bloomberg reported Nvidia CEO
Jensen Huang reportedly gave his blessing to CEOs Matthew Murphey & Michael Hurlston ahead of their
~$103bn combination. The spread finished yesterday a little over 12.5% gross // 9% Ann IRR to a 4/1/28 close.
The VAL/RIG merger is approaching the finish line, with Valaris confirming that required regulatory approvals
have been received and guided to a close on or about December 1. ROKU/FOXA closed yesterday at with only
84bps of gross spread left after the companies announced they had received all regulatory approvals Ex-US.
Elsewhere, ON delivered a largely in-line Q3 as Industrial strength offset softer Automotive results, while the
company disclosed $180mn of AI Data Center revenue for the first time. Looking back at October Merger arb
spreads tightened last month by +30bps (average) & +70bps (mkt cap weighted average). Both metrics are +4%
YTD. The spread pool finished the month at ~$24bn, a +16% increase compared to September driven by two
large deal announcements - CART ($2.1bn spread opportunity) & PTC ($1bn spread).  
 
Merger Arb/Event
 
• LITE/MRVL - Nvidia’s Huang Gave Blessing to Marvell-Lumentum Megadeal (link). The heads of Marvell
Technology Inc. and Lumentum Holdings Inc. got the blessing of Nvidia Corp.’s Jensen Huang before
striking their nearly $103 billion tie-up. Marvell Chief Executive Officer Matthew Murphy and Lumentum CEO
Michael Hurlston sought Huang’s views during a phone call before the deal was announced, according to
people familiar with the matter.
 
• VAL/RIG – Reports Q3 Results & Anticipates Merger To Close On or About December 1st (link). “Our
pending business combination with Transocean has received the required regulatory approvals and is
anticipated to close on or about December 1, 2026. We remain confident that the pending transaction will
create meaningful value through expected synergies and the enhanced capabilities of the combined
company.”
 
• ON – Reports Q3 Earnings (link). Quarterly results in line with the Street on revenue, just above on EPS:
onsemi reported 3Q revenue of $1.72 bn, in line with GS at $1.72 bn and the Street (Visible Alpha
Consensus Data) at $1.71 bn. Automotive revenue of $777 mn was below GS at $820 mn and the Street at
$809 mn, Industrial revenue of $467 mn was well above GS at $423 mn and the Street at $426 mn, and
Datacenter/Other revenue of $479 mn was in line with GS at $477 mn and the Street at $473 mn. In

addition, the company disclosed AI Data Center revenue for the first time at $180mn in 3Q. Gross margin of
41.2% was in line with GS and the Street at 41.1%. EPS of $0.90 was in line with GS at $0.90 and just
above the Street at $0.88.
 
• GS Special Situations | U.S. Special Situations Monitor (November 2026) (link). Merger arb spreads
tightened in October by +30bps (average) & +70bps (mkt cap weighted average). Both metrics are +4%
YTD. The spread pool finished the month at ~$24bn, a +16% increase compared to September driven by
two large deal announcements - CART ($2.1bn spread opportunity) & PTC ($1bn spread).
