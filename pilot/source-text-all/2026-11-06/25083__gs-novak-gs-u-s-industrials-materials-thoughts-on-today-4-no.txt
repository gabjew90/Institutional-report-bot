Industrials getting hit today down 200bps while materials are higher by 150bps matching broader market
strength. Zooming out consumer and healthcare leading the market while energy is sharply lower.
Housing, airlines and autos are the subsectors higher on the screen with data center, defense and aerospace
lower. Factors are completely upside down with short interest ripping and momentum getting hit hard.
ADP higher 86k vs. 67k survey and ISM services essentially in line 54.5 vs. 54.8. More single name focus
post market with all eyes on CF CGNX ITT NTR and APD CMI IBP MAIR PH ROK WMS tomorrow
AM as well as FAST October sales (~15% bogey).

Big movers:
AI / data center down the most … FIX PWR ETN VRT notable in large cap world down 10-20% … we
believe concerns around project delays, pushouts, moratoriums will continue in the near term. Some
inbounds on when to buy the dip but zero conviction that were are there today.
Defense down 5% LMT LHX NOC RTX GD … we think there are enough mitigating factors that these
moves could get bought … clean quarter essentially across the board … geopolitical situation working
down stockpiles … DoW push for volumes … spending likely higher not lower near term.
Housing is the other side here up 5-7% depending on the name with rates lower on growth concerns … oil
lower … all in theory bodes well for rate sensitive and the consumer – seeing this reaction though more
subdued in autos and airlines … we think this continues near term.
Single name / subsector inbounds:

SWK +15% - bull feedback is gross margins, bear feedback is pricing negative and implied Q4 double
digits below consensus.
MLM essentially flat: why not reacting with the rest of rate sensitive names. Revenue growth above
EBITDA growth some of the pushback. M-HSD pricing on a mix adjusted basis in line – debate on the
mix impact. Welcome feedback on the quarter / guide.
EMR +1%: Feedback muted here all around but outperforming with oil lower. Debate on what the
underlying order growth rate is – whether 14% in fiscal Q4 or something less given some of the very long
cycle orders.
WCC -12%: This is a well owned name for multiple reasons – data center (trading lower on this), ISM
leverage (cyclicals getting hit – we are risk off), distributor inflation winner (inflation expectations coming
in) and crowding.
MOD -22%: how much is micro vs. macro with other SMID AI names down 15-20%. We think most
macro with MOD having lower expectations than others into the print. The quarter missed and the
guidance was lowered.
Transports weakness broadly: not clear where this group falls other than crowded driving UPS high, XPO
lower to start … think CP GXO other areas of short crowing with KNX more owned.
Agriculture: what is the impact the ag machinery DE AGCO CNH and other ag related names CTVA
ADM BG – welcome your feedback here.
