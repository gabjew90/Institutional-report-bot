  UNITED STATES
Tech now accounts for two-thirds of
the US trade deficit
There has been a lot of commentary about the contribution of tech
investment to GDP growth, but with the US running a trade deficit of
$64bn in September in tech products alone, out of an overall trade deficit
of $105bn, we need to remember that this aspect actually subtracts
from growth. Instead, it is a boon to the likes of Korea and Taiwan
High tech imports are contributing substantially to the US trade deficit
Tech imports fuelling the surge in the trade deficit
The US trade balance figures don't normally get hearts racing, but I think this chart below is
astonishing. The US trade deficit was $105bn in September, of which $64bn was due to a
deficit in computers, peripherals and semiconductors. The chart shows how imports of tech
products that are destined for data centres around the country have not merely gone
exponential, but almost vertical. Less than two years ago, the US was importing around $20bn
per month of these products. In September, it was $87bn.

Tech imports offsetting much of the capex boost within GDP
So while there is lots of discussion about how much the tech/AI/data centre investment blitz is
boosting growth, much of the value added is coming from overseas. This is a net negative for
US growth as money is flowing out of the country to the likes of Korea and Taiwan, from which
US imports are up 35% and 60% respectively.
The result is the chart below showing the contribution to year-on-year US GDP growth. The
black line is the YoY% growth rate with the blue bars showing the contribution from tech capex
and AI token spending (including the chips going into data centres). The purple section is the
data centre building (the concrete, steel, etc), but then there is the net exports section in
orange, which, because of the scale of the trade deficit in tech, is a significant drag on overall
growth right now – much of it actually ended up in inventory building of tech products that will
be used in coming quarters.
Consequently, the US tech roll-out has been a huge boost to tech-orientated exporting
nations. Only three years ago, the US was a net exporter of tech products!
