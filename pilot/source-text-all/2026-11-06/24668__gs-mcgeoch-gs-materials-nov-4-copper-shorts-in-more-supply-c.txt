RAMACO - cost driven miss, sales guide cut and costs guide hiked....3Q26 results, with revenue above
expectations on higher sales volumes, but higher costs driving an earnings miss. Looking ahead, management
lowered its FY2026 production and sales guidance and raised its cash cost outlook, although improving met
coal prices and a stronger 2027 domestic sales book provide a more constructive setup into next year. Separately,
Brook Mine development continues to progress, with strong initial recovery rates, but construction of the pilot plant
has been pushed to 2H27 from 1H27 previously. We remain Neutral rated, with our full takeaways from the quarter
contained here within.....Management was also constructive on 2027 pricing, pointing to the Aus PLV forward curve at
~$255/ton, while ~600k tons of excess inventory could provide a ~$75mn working capital tailwind through mid-2027.
Nearer term, management expects 4Q26 adj. EBITDA to exceed the ~$7mn generated YTD, supported by improving
pricing and margin expansion. Lastly, the company increased capex guidance to $97mn-$102mn from $92mn-$97mn
as Maben's first underground section starts in 4Q26 vs. 1Q27 previously. Link to GIR
