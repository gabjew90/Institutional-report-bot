Tuesday Thoughts – 2 Prints
MPC. Marathon Petroleum Corp to Divest Minnesota and North Dakota Refineries & MPLX LP to Divest
Related Midstream Assets … will be the focus on the call today at 11 AM ET. Quarter itself – looking at
4% beat vs Street numbers on EPS … recall we have seen double digit beats in Refining throughout EPS
season thus far (where most prints have been faded) and would think buyside was closer to 3 handle on
EPS. Capture for the quarter at 93% was well telegraphed (ie more in line with historic average for 3Q
results) and highlighting $3 bn of share repo during the quarter (early questions around shareholder returns
strategy – could we see a slight shift here?). Company is now sitting on +$12 of cash – recall target range
has been closer to ~$1 bn over time. The bar was high for the print itself – especially considering how well
the stock held up vs Large Cap peers over the last month - but would think with the developments on asset
sales / since we are later innings of Refining reporting - the print will matter less. Asset sales – basically
180 kbd capacity for $3.45 bn net to MPC (MPLX receiving $750 mn) – company is talking about
investing in the GC (debottlenecking?) and supporting MPLX growth. Expect there to be a ton of eyeballs
on the MPLX portion from both Refining and Midstream investors – specifically on where / if inorganic
growth fits into the narrative.
SU. One of the more well owned stocks here in the US and the bar is high (again) for the EPS print.
Conversations on the desk have highlighted strong expectations for a beat specifically on Downstream –
recall this rhymes with 2Q where Downstream cash flow / results were more in line with buyside
expectations. Most have just been watching SCO and the disty chart over the last couple of months –
highlighting relative preference to still be here. Will be expecting more questions on core vs non-core / use
of cash on the call. CAD peers have signaled willingness to grow and there have been several headlines /
updates on consolidation north of the border. Expect messaging to be tight here – ie no real change to
assets competing for capital, running kits at high utilization rates, taking cost out of the business.
Positioning is something to be mindful of for the print itself – but with WCS still wide – think most
investors would rather camp out in non-heavy exposed names.
