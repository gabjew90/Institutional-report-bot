The Weekly Brief
Thought of the week
Global earnings per share (EPS) growth expectations for 2027 have improved
over the course of this year by a few percentage points. The latest estimate is
tracking at 17% year-over-year, well above trend growth. The technology sector
again accounts for most of the 2027 global EPS growth upgrade, offsetting
large growth downgrades from the energy, communication services and
consumer discretionary sectors (the latter two growth downgrades were
seemingly due to earnings arithmetic – adverse base effects – following
blockbuster second-quarter earnings releases of some of their mega-cap AI-
related names). Meanwhile, most other sectors’ 2027 EPS growth estimates
have been relatively stable this year. If economic growth therefore continues to
pick up, particularly if energy prices fall, then those segments – and the
headline EPS figure – could experience further growth upgrades ahead.
