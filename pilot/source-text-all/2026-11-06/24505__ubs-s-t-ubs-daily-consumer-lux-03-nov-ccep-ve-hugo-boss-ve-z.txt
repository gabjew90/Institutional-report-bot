• ‘Estée Lauder (UBS Research Rec Neutral) – A Prettier Print, But Priced In; Stay Neutral Report EL
delivered a 1Q EPS beat above both UBSe and Street expectations, driven by stronger OSG and better
GM/OPM, while also raising its FY27 outlook across the board. Top-line strength was broad-based across
geographies, particularly in emerging markets and travel retail, while makeup and fragrance remained resilient
despite the broader luxury slowdown. On the bottom line, while EPS guidance moved higher, the midpoint of
the updated range implies estimates for the balance of the year need to move lower - though UBS think this
will likely prove conservative. Taken together, UBS think it is clear that the business is on better footing today
vs. what we have seen over the past several years, but as has been the case for some time we exit the day
with limited visibility on (1) the company's LT organic sales trajectory and (2) normalized EPS power. At this
stage UBS think FY27 EPS guidance will prove more than achievable but if organic sales growth slows in the
2H (which is the assumption embedded in guidance), UBS struggle to see how shares continue to re-rate
higher. As such, UBS would wait for a more attractive entry point and or greater visibility on the top/bottom line
outlook longer term before stepping in.
