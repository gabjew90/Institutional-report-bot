The verdict is in. Amid Election Night results still pouring in, it has been a bloodbath for Trump 
and the Republicans. As expected, Democrats have flipped control of Congress, winning ≥35 
projected House seats (vs the net three needed) and flipping at least seven Senate seats (ME, NC 
and ruby-red TX, OH, SC, KS, IA) vs the four they hoped for. Blue inroads in deep red states were 
powered by a total collapse of Trump’s 2024 coalition and a record midterm turnout of young, 
Black and Latino voters. After a decade of dividing and dominating American politics, the 
twilight of the Trump era is here, with the kick off to the 2028 elections starting today and the 
future of the post-Trump Republican party up in the air. But don’t underestimate a President 
famed for doubling down, happy to govern by executive action and keen to cement his legacy 
from foreign policy (keep an eye on N. Korea/Cuba, not just Iran/Russia/China) alongside 
trade/tariffs, immigration and AI.
