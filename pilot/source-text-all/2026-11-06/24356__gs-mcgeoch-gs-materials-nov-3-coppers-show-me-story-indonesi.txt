China
* Midday wrap - A volatile session for China equities with shrinking volumes. The policy support from
yday failed to lift indices as most measures came in inline with previous market expectation. Indices
opened lower, popped intra-day along with surge of ETF inflows, but dipped after lunch break weighed by
regional tech sell off. However, market managed to bounce into close on chatters of guidance from
regulators to slow down IPOs. {NSN TNWY1WT96OSG <GO>} Sector wise, Hard tech got hit the most
along with global tech selloff. Selling pressure in CPO persists on concerns over earnings and outlooks,
followed by CCL and chips, and weighed on STAR50 and Chinext. On the bright side, defensive sectors
show resilience. Previous underweight sectors becomes market darling - Agriculture, Consumer and
Renewable moved much higher. Non-banks financials had a second day rally on expectation of policy
support to benefit.
