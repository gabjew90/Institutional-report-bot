The Gold Standard: Market Review – October 30
In this publication, we review detailed changes within global equity, fixed income, commodity, and
currency markets.
• The S&P500 decreased by 0.6% and the VIX index remained flat at 19.5. 10Y yields decreased by 7bps
to 4.93%, explained by real yields decreasing by 6bps to 2.59% and inflation expectations decreasing
by 1bps to 2.34%. The 2–10y yield curve decreased by 2bps to 0.34%. The DXY dollar index increased
by 0.2% to 102. Larger foreign exchange moves include a weakening of the Brazilian real (1.3%) and
Indian rupee (1.2%).
• Gold decreased by 3.0% to $3,875/oz and physical gold ETFs recorded outflows of 203koz. Silver
decreased by 8.9% to $49.10/oz and physical silver ETF flows recorded inflows of 3moz. CFTC net long
gold positions decreased by 12k to 180k contracts and net long silver positions decreased by 0.5k to
17k contracts.
• Gold equities decreased by 3.9%, while gold equity ETFs realized inflows of $25m. Silver equity ETFs
realized inflows of $228m. Relative to the index, notable weekly movers included Greatland Resources
(+13.5%), FireFly Metals (+10.6%), Westgold Resources (+10.4%), DPM Metals (-13.3%), Kinross Gold
(-9.9%), and G Mining (-9.6%). Short position data was reported in the U.S. this week – notable outliers
include an increase in Perpetua Resources and a decrease in Fortuna Mining and SSR Mining.
• Copper prices decreased by 0.4% to $6.48/lb, WTI increased by 0.8% to $104.12/bbl, and Bitcoin
prices decreased by 4.5% to $76.7k.
