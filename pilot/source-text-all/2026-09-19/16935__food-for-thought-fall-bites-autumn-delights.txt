Fall Bites & Autumn Delights 
McDonald’s Investor Day 
On 9/23, MCD is hosting an Investor Day at its HQ in Chicago (see preview), where 
we expect the company to lay out select financial targets for 2027 and the longer 
term (2028-2030). We think MCD could provide an update on recent US trends to 
level set expectations and help bridge the gap between near-term dynamics and 
long-term messaging. This is consistent with a report from Bloomberg this week 
indicating MCD is going to work with franchisees in the coming weeks to develop 
a longer-term approach to value as well as build a near-term bridge plan of 
temporary items and digital offers, while MCD is also making some temporary 
changes to its training and support for franchisees. We believe one of the most 
anticipated areas of focus is around MCD’s partnering contribution for the 
upcoming US remodel cycle and what that means for incremental capex, though 
we think a moderation in unit growth is anticipated (we assume unit growth 
moderates to 3.8-3.9% vs 4-4.5% from 2024-2026), which could help offset some 
of the reinvestment capex. We also expect a focus on technology, which should 
support efficiencies at the corporate level (MCD has already signaled G&A 
leverage in 2027) and unlock productivity at the restaurant level to help fund top-
line efforts (e.g., hand-breaded chicken, beverages). 
Exclusive Drops to Drive Engagement, Loyalty and Traffic 
Restaurants appear to be leaning into exclusive drops to drive engagement, 
loyalty and traffic. Starbucks offers one of the best examples, noting its limited-
quantity Bearista cups have been selling out nearly immediately upon launch, 
while the broader merchandise collection also drives incremental traffic to the 
store. Taco Bell introduced Tuesday Drops two years ago, offering rewards 
members access to exclusive deals and merchandise (often in limited quantities), 
supporting consistent engagement and encouraging loyalty. In July, Olive Garden 
brought back the Never Ending Pasta Pass for the first time in six years, limited to 
10,000 passes and intended to drive excitement ahead of the return of the Never 
Ending Pasta promotion. Earlier this month, Wingstop debuted its first-ever Wing 
Pass, limited to 100 passes for $27.99, granting the holder up to 18 weekly bundle 
meals for the football season. Both passes sold out immediately.
