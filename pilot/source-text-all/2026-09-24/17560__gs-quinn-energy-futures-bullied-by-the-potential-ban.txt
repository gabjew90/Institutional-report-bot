Energy Futures: Bullied By the (Potential) Ban
_Rhetoric surrounding measures to cool US diesel prices, including a potential export ban_ _, rattled the
ULSD market on September 23rd._Aggregate open interest (OI) nosedived, signaling a flush of bullish
exposure._Both physical and speculative participants engaged._The coming days should bring more
fireworks as industries defend interests_ _ and details eventually emerge.
• | _Rhetoric surrounding measures to cool US diesel prices, including a potential export ban_ _, rattled the
ULSD market on September 23rd. _November ULSD slipped -2.7%, significantly underperforming other
energy contracts which received a boost from more elevated risk premium post Iranian President
Pezeshkian's defiant speech at the United Nations General Assembly. Consider, ULSD-Gasoil plummeted;
RBOB-ULSD spiked; ULSD-WTI cracks retraced.
• | _Aggregate open interest (OI) nosedived, signaling a flush of bullish exposure. _ULSD OI tumbled
-$3.8bn, the largest drop for at least the past year and approximately 9x the daily average. Importantly,
deferred months accounted for a reasonable portion of the drop. Specifically, December 2026 and January
2027 OI shed -$1.6bn combined. Thus term structure posturing also suffered. • | _Both physical and
speculative participants engaged. _According to the latest Commitment of Traders report, combined
Managed Money, Other, and Non-Reportable net length resided at a 2 year high on September 15th.
Similarly, the Swap Dealer cohort - which conceivably represents facilitation for producers, consumers,
and institutional investors - also exhibited max net positioning. • | _The coming days should bring more
fireworks as industries defend interests_ _ and details eventually emerge._ Energy Secretary Wright
already dismissed the idea of an outright export ban and instead discussed voluntary restrictions.
