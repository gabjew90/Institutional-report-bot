GS Quinn - MSCI EM Futures: Widespread Selling in Mid
September 22 Sep 2026

Through mid-September, MSCI Emerging Market (EM) futures net length decreased as the index
underperformed. According to Commitment of Traders, Non-Dealers sold -$2.1bn from September 8th -
15th, marking the largest 1 week amount since March. New shorts (+$2.3bn) were the sole driver. Recall
the index shed -4.1% over the observation window, a large drag relative to S &P 500 (-1.1%). Categorical
changes revealed widespread participation and renewed sensitivity to higher energy prices. Asset Manager
(-$1.2bn), Hedge Fund (-$120mm), Other (-$320mm), and Non-Reportable (-$420mm) all sold. For Asset
Manager, the breakdown was 30% / 70% for liquidation / short augmentation. The latter appeared driven
by large increases in Brent (+11.1%) and TTF (+5.6%). Specifically, rolling 3 month correlations between
Asset Manager shorts and energy prices became very positive. Since then, energy markets retraced, AI
tailwinds surfaced, and MSCI EM recuperated. Over September 15th - 21st, Brent and TTF fell -7.7% and
-8.5% respectively. GS's AI Semiconductors basket surged +12% due to Meta's new AI agent , providing
some, albeit limited, positive read across. EM bounced +3.2%. However interestingly, positioning
indicators revealed mixed shifts. While MSCI EM 3 month normalized 25 delta put-call skew declined, 3
month swap funding failed to recover. * It will be interesting to see if there is a delayed reaction or
redeployment of equity capital to other indices.
