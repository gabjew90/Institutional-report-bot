AutoZone Inc. (AZO): First Take: 4Q SSS were much softer than 
expected; EPS beat driven by tariff refunds
22 September 2026 | 8:18AM EDT
AZO reported 4Q26 EPS of $56.05, above the GS estimate of $54.11 and consensus 
of $54.08 (FactSet), driven by better-than-expected margins. However, we note 
AZO’s gross margin included a 145 bps benefit from tariff refunds, and excluding this 
benefit, we estimate EPS would have been closer to $51.46. Total company 
same-store sales increased 1.5% (ex. FX), well below the GS/consensus estimates of 
4.3%/3.8%, as domestic same-store sales increased 1.6% (vs. GS/consensus of 
4.6%/3.3%) and international (ex. FX) increased 1.3%. Domestic commercial sales 
increased 8.6% y/y, below the GS estimate of 10.8% and below the year-to-date 
growth of 11.6% through 3Q. Management attributed the softer sales result to a 
difficult selling environment during the first eight weeks of the quarter, though noted 
that sales trends strengthened during the final eight weeks. EBIT margin of 20.0% 
(+81 bps y/y) came in above the GS estimate of 19.5% and consensus of 19.4%, as 
gross margin increased 182 bps y/y to 53.3% (vs. GS/consensus of 51.3%/52.0%) 
while SG&A as a % of sales increased 101 bps y/y to 33.4% (vs. GS/consensus of 
31.8%/32.5%). Excluding tariff refunds, we estimate EBIT and gross margin to be 
approximately 18.5% and 51.9%, respectively. 
Analysis 
Domestic same-store sales increased 1.6% y/y during the quarter. AZO also

Domestic commercial sales increased 8.6% y/y, below the GS estimate of 10.8% 
n

from a higher DIFM mix. Excluding the tariff refunds, we estimate gross margin to 
be approximately 51.9%. 
SG&A expense increased 8.9% y/y to $2,201mn; the SG&A expense ratio of 
n

deleverage primarily driven by growth investments.

Implications 
We expect a positive reaction to the 4Q print as results may have been better than 
feared given broader macro pressure and the company’s overexposure to DIY. On the 
conference call, we will be looking for commentary regarding quarter-to-date trends; 
LIFO impacts; same-SKU inflation; the current pricing environment; recent demand 
trends by category; DIFM share gains; the health of their core DIY consumer; inventory 
availability; SG&A spend; and the state of the supply chain.
