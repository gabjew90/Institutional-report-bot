Burberry Group plc (LSE: BRBY; 1,025.50)
Published September 22 2026 00:45:00 EDT
Downgrade to Sector Perform
Rating:
Sector Perform (prev: Outperform)
Price Target:
1,200 ↓ 1,400
Our view: We downgrade Burberry to Sector Perform with a revised 1,200 GBp
PT. Burberry has sufficient self-help to support healthy retail LFL growth trend,
despite lower than peer price contribution. However, the macro and luxury demand
environment appears to have sequentially softened in cal-3Q26E, which we expect
to continue from here (link). Consensus estimates anticipate continuation of +5-6%
revenue growth and +200bps EBIT margin expansion in FY29E which we believe
is too optimistic in the absence of additional cost reduction actions (e.g. store
closures). We reduce FY28E-29E EPS by 6-8% and sit 7-11% below consensus.

LVMH Moet Hennessy Louis Vuitton (NXT PA: MC; 405.50)
Published September 22 2026 00:45:00 EDT
Growth outlook muted; downgrade to SP
Rating:
Sector Perform (prev: Outperform)
Price Target:
475.00 ↓ 575.00
Our view: We reluctantly downgrade LVMH to Sector Perform with revised €475
PT. Our previous thesis anticipating growth acceleration in 2H26E supported by
creative renewal at Dior is offset by an incrementally softening macro + luxury
demand environment in 3Q26E (Middle East conflict, impact on Asian travel flows,
higher oil prices pressuring disposable incomes, equity market volatility, and central
banks rate tightening cycle). We expect these conditions to persist into FY27E. As a
result, we are cutting FY27E EPS estimate by 10% to €23.03 (9% below consensus at
€25.20). LVMH trades at 17.5x RBC FY27E P/E, not as cheap looking as consensus-
based valuations suggest (16.0x). We await mid-term consensus expectations to
rebase + tangible signs of luxury improvement.

Published September 22 2026 00:45:00 EDT
3Q26 preview - Moderating the SmartGlasses contribution
Rating:
Outperform
Price Target:
190.00 ↓ 230.00
Our view: We expect 3Q26 print to reflect a more normalized revenue growth
profile with the Core (ex Smartglasses) business remaining healthy at +4-5% cFX
growth and contribution from Smartglasses similar to 2Q26 at +~2%. Given recent
stock underperformance and 19x CY27E P/E valuation, the setup is approaching
a floor in terms of growth expectations re-base towards more realistic levels but
with some adjustments to come (e.g. FY27E organic +9% cons vs +6% RBCe)
which we believe will be the last leg down. Underlying business quality remains
unchanged whilst valuation is dislocated on the back of the Smartglass revenue
and valuation merry-go-round which should normalize once competitor products
are purchasable and EL growth assumptions rebase. We reduce EPS assumptions
by 2-7% for FY26-27E, predominantly reflecting a recalibration of AI Smartglasses
growth trajectory. PT to €190 (from €230), retain Outperform rating.

Hermes International (NXT PA: RMS; 1,344.00)
Published September 22 2026 00:45:00 EDT
3Q26E preview - stable + steady growth expected
Rating:
Sector Perform
Price Target:
1,600.00 ↓ 1,700.00
Our view: For 3Q26, we expect Hermes to deliver +6% organic sales growth,
with Leather Goods division +11%. Regionally, we expect Americas to remain
the strongest growth driver. In absolute terms, Hermes' growth remains healthy,
however given a more challenging luxury demand environment in 3Q based on
our observations, the prospects of an improvement vs 1H26 run-rate is less likely.
Hermes' shares have been under pressure lately (-20% last 3 months) which reflects
valuation de-rating to 28x FY27E P/E (vs sector 20x). We recently downgraded
the stock to Sector Perform, as the relative growth differential is narrowing and
premium valuation is a risk in the absence of earnings upgrades and/or revenue
growth acceleration. Our DCF derived price target moves from €1,700 to €1,600.

3Q26E preview - all eyes on exit rate for 4Q Gucci Core collection
Rating:
Sector Perform
Price Target:
280.00 ↓ 350.00
Our view: 3Q26 is unlikely to be a pivotal print for Kering, as the commercial appeal
of the Primavera collection is narrower than what is expected in 4Q26 with Gucci
Core which has broader appeal, the new store concept will start to appear and
brand/product marketing is expected to ramp up. Nonetheless, we still expect some
modest sequential improvement in 3Q for Gucci (-1.4% organic), and investors will
be keen to hear the September exit rate to better understand the trajectory shape.
For Kering and other luxury names we believe FY27E consensus estimates are too
high, and RBC EPS estimates are 6% below consensus.

Moncler S.p.A. (MILAN: MONC; 43.48)
Published September 22 2026 00:45:00 EDT
3Q26E preview - Balanced set-up
Rating:
Sector Perform
Price Target:
55.00 ↓ 59.00
Our view: Moncler enters 2H26E with a balanced set-up, underpinned by its historic
track record of delivering in peak trading, partially offset by tough comparatives
and a likely soft 3Q26E based on management commentary on June/July exit rate
and our observations. Macro and luxury demand conditions have incrementally
softened in our view (link) and we find ourselves reducing estimates across the
board, to which Moncler is not immune. 3Q26E matters to the extent that the
September exit rate shows some sequential improvement. We lower estimates
by 1-2% on revenues and 2-5% on EBIT for FY26-28E. PT to €55 (from €59).
Balanced risk/reward, Sector Perform despite attractive valuation which reflects
typical seasonality for the stock.

market
Rating:
Outperform
Price Target:
45.00
Our view: The next-gen Varian linear accelerator is expected to be launched at the
ASTRO conference on September 26-30th. Based on our bottom-up installed base
analysis, proprietary KOL work and company commentary, we estimate scenarios
in which a differentiated product could push Precision Therapy sales 6-14% above
consensus, and could add €3-5/share to our valuation. Management describes
the launch as something that "will change the role radiation therapy can play in
treatment of important cancer types" with "extremely positive" early customer
feedback.

RBC Luxury Datawatch
Published September 22 2026 00:45:00 EDT
Assessing the FY27E luxury outlook through a more cautious lens
Our View: We refresh our luxury company views estimates which results in rating
downgrades for LVMH and Burberry, and estimate reductions for Kering, Moncler,
Hermes and Swatch Group. Three key reasons underpin our more prudent luxury
sector view into 2H26E and FY27E: 1) Macro and luxury demand conditions have
incrementally softened in 3Q26E - with mixed datapoints in China and potential US
moderation, and oil price led inflation and rising interest rates as well as volatile
equity markets. 2) Consensus mid-term estimates are too optimistic assuming
accelerating revenue growth expectations and margin expansion which in our
view is not justified based on current momentum, 3) Creative renewal for soft
