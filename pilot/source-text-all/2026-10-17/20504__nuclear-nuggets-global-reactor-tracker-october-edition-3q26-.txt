Uranium pricing and volume trackers 
 
 
Spot pricing remained broadly range-bound. Spot U₃O₈ prices moved modestly higher 
through the first half of September, reaching ~$90/lb and briefly exceeding that level 
mid month, before softening through late September and finishing the month around 
~$89.50/lb. Despite broadly bullish sentiment following the WNA Symposium and 
expectations for stronger utility demand, spot market activity remained subdued, with 
September volumes falling to their lowest monthly level since December 2009. Prices 
have remained stable into early October, holding around ~$89.50-$90.00/lb. 
Term pricing continues to hold. Term uranium activity remained active through 
September, with multiple utilities evaluating offers, advancing RFIs, and pursuing mid‑ 
and long-term coverage, while additional utility requests were also submitted during the 
month. Despite continued engagement across the term market, limited changes in offer 
levels were observed, and the Long‑Term U3O8 price remained unchanged at $96/lb.

Nuclear preview 3Q26 
 
 
Nuclear equities materially underperformed the broader market over the past three 
months, with our nuclear coverage averaging a -21% return over the period compared 
to the S&P 500, which returned +3%. Similar to prior quarters, performance varied 
across the group, although weakness was broadly spread across both SMR and 
uranium-exposed names. In particular, OKLO declined 29%, the weakest performance in 
our coverage, which we attribute to continued risk-off sentiment toward advanced 
nuclear developers and limited near-term commercial catalysts. SMR declined 21%, as 
investors remained focused on the timing of definitive commercial agreements and 
project awards. UEC declined 13%, outperforming the SMR-focused names on a relative 
basis, though still materially trailing the broader market.
