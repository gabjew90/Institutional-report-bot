Since our last update about a month ago, we have come out
with a latest update on our AI-bucketed stocks' trend. AI-
bucketed share prices rebounded by 7% from our last report
but are down ~19% on average from peaks since we first
started tracking, with implied AI multiples moving up for
most names. 
Roughly a month ago (Implied AI Multiples - Most Multiples Move Lower, priced as of 09.08.26), we
updated our AI exposure math that we debuted in early February (AI Update, 02.11.26). We
continue to track how implied AI multiples have trended across our AI-bucketed stocks since we
started this series. Recall, we started this analysis to assess what was embedded in our AI-
related stocks across different sub-segments and exposure percentages.
Since the last report, on average, our AI-bucketed stocks' prices have run 7% (vs. SPX up 2% and
NDX up 4% from 9.8.26-10.12.26). HPE has run the most since the prior report, as the stock is up
26% since our prior report (9.8.26-10.12.26) given the raised FY27 networking guidance from its
Networking Day that took place around two weeks ago. GLW has sold off the most (down 11%
since September 8th). EMS players were up LSD% on average (FN +10%, CLS +5%, JBL flat, FLEX
+3%). Additionally, if we look at current share prices of AI-related stocks vs their peaks over the
time we've been tracking (2.11.26-10.12.26), they are down ~19% on average, with ANET/DELL/
KEYS/HPE currently near peak stock prices during that period. JBL and GLW have reached
trough AI-implied multiples since our initial reading in early February. AI multiples across the
group have increased on average since the prior reading (+12%, ~ 4 turns). These AI-bucketed
stocks' prices are up ~90% YTD on average vs. SPX +14% YTD.
As a reminder, the select companies under our coverage that are commonly bucketed as 'AI
plays' represent ~20% of total Cloud/AI capex, per our estimates, and these companies are
experiencing meaningful revenue growth in their respective Cloud/AI businesses. In our original
AI Update report, most of these stocks had implied AI multiples of 20-40x, with some much
higher (GLW, KEYS, CIEN) and a few much lower (SMCI, HPE). In this current update, GLW's AI-
implied multiple remains much higher at 68x. SMCI continues to remain much lower (at 6x) due
to company-specific issues, in our view.
