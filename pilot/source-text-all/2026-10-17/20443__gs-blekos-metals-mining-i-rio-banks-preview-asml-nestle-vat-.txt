EUROPE METALS & MINING - More Than Just Gold: Initiating on African Gold Miners: Buy AGA,
EDV; GFI Neutral
• We initiate coverage of Endeavour Mining and AngloGold Ashanti at Buy, and Gold Fields at Neutral.
EDV offers the strongest combination of organic growth and FCF yield, supported by sector leading
production growth. AGA offers operational consistency, a capital efficient brownfield pipeline and the
highest prospective shareholder returns across our gold coverage, with Nevada providing significant
longer-term optionality.
• GFI retains a high quality and geographically diversified asset base, but its medium term growth outlook
increasingly depends on Windfall, while Tarkwa risk and cost pressure in Australia introduce greater
uncertainty.
Rio Tinto Plc (RIO.L): 3Q26 result: In-line result supported by strong performance at Oyu Tolgoi; Buy
• RIO reported an in-line SepQ, with a resilient Pilbara performance and group CuEq production (+1%
YoY) supported by the stronger than expected recoveries and ramp-up at Oyu Tolgoi. Group copper
production of 205kt came in-line with our estimates, with the Oyu Tolgoi beat (+14%/+17% vs. GSe/VA
consensus) offsetting a weak quarter at both Kennecott (70 day furnace rebuild) and Escondida (weather).
EUROPE BANKS - Looking ahead to Q3 and medium-term expectations
• Ahead of the European banks' Q3 earnings season, we analyse consensus expectations for the quarter and
for full-year 2026/27/28E for 38 European banks.
• For the quarter, Visible Alpha Consensus Data expects aggregate ROTE for the sector of 15.4%, up
1.4ppt y/y, supported by higher revenues, more than offsetting a y/y increase in costs and impairments
which drives a 16% improvement in PBT for the quarter. For the medium term, consensus expects ROTE
to improve in every year, reaching c.17% in 2028E.
• We remain the most constructive on BNP, BCP, BBVA, SocGen, Intesa, ING, STAN and Erste for the
quarter, noting upside to VA estimates.
ASML Holding (ASML.AS): Pricing step-up and favourable mix drive margin outlook, while demand
visibility underpins capacity roadmap
• We raise our FY26-30 revenue estimates by +2% and +12% to reflect our positive view on continued
strong demand for ASML's Lithography solutions (especially Litho services), in tandem with tailwinds
from its planned value-based increases in ASPs.

• As such, we see scope for upside to our current revenue estimates from further increase in EUV
production from ASML's new manufacturing facility, as well as potential for higher Immersion ASPs
underpinned by value based pricing dynamics. Further, our FY26-30 gross margin estimates change
between 0-1pp reflecting our latest product mix assumptions. As such, our FY26-30 EBIT estimates are
c.4-12% higher, reflecting our latest topline, gross margin and opex estimates. Finally, our EPS forecasts
increase by 4-12% across FY26-30 (factoring in our updated EBIT and share count assumptions).
Nestle (NESN.S) - COGS inflation remains manageable into 2027 while recent FX is supportive
• Lapping tougher comps, we expect +3.4% organic sales growth with +1.6% RIG in Q3 (company-
compiled consensus at +1.5% RIG). We expect the group will see a fading headwind from retailer
delistings in Europe, the IMF recall, and retailer inventory adjustments in US Pet.
• Nestlé's turnaround is materialising, with its 2% RIG target achievable in FY27 in our view, driven by 1)
ongoing strong demand in pet care driven by cat food, where it overindexes and 2) strong momentum in
coffee. At 16x CY27 P/E (14.3x excluding L'Oréal) and 10.7x EV/EBITDA, valuation remains
compelling, in our view.
GXO Logistics (GXO) - Attractive risk-reward on the turnaround, up to Buy
• To frame an upside case, assuming 5%pa organic revenue growth and 5% EBIT margin in 2030, we
forecast (GAAP, unadjusted) EPS of $3.8 and FCF/share of $4.3 (GAAP EPS includes M&A related
amortization charges). Assuming a fairly standard asset-light logistics multiple of ~20x earnings/CF
Exhibit 16, this would imply a share price of ~$80, and an annual return in the high teens, before
considering additional cash returns via div/buybacks.
• GXO's closest peer, DHL, has higher financial targets than what we forecast for GXO by 2030. DHL SC
aims to outperform 4-6% annual industry growth, at a 6-7% EBIT margin (albeit on IFRS reporting)
VAT Group (VACN.S): 3Q26 strong orders beat; 2027 EPS cons likely to move up low DD
• VAT consensus FY27 EPS is likely to move up low DD on the back of strong 3Q26 orders beat vs
company-compiled consensus. Orders beat consensus by 40% with OOG further accelerating to 188%
(highest ever growth we have seen, +ve read for Atlas, Exhibit 3) with book-to-bill now at 1.9x (highest
ever).
• VAT mentioned that the strong investment conditions are expected to last into 2027 and beyond with
record order backlog provides visibility.
