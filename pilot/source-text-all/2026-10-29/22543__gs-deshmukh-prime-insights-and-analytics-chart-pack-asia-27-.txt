GS Prime Insights & Analytics Chart Pack - Asia: A curated compilation of the most topical
charts on hedge fund performance and positioning focused on Asia organized into the following
categories: (1) L/S Performance Estimates, (2) Leverage Ratios and Trading Flows in Asia, (3) Asia
Positioning and (4) Asia Sector and Thematic Highlights. 
 
Themes that we find particularly topical in Asia at the moment:
 
Hedge Fund Performance
• October has been challenging from a performance perspective across strategies and regions. Asia
focused managers experienced sharp drawdowns amid the accelerating AI sell off. 
 
• Asia focused Fundamental L/S  managers are down 8.9% MTD October (vs. MXAP -6.4%).
Drawdown was led by beta and alpha from asset selection, Momentum and Volatility factors. YTD
performance is now at 14.5%, with dispersion levels remaining elevated. The simple average YTD
estimate is at 9.3%, while the median estimate is notably lower at 2.7%, vs. the weighted average
estimate of 14.5%.
 
• China focused Fundamental LS  managers are down 10.9% MTD, with YTD now at 16.8%.
Similar to the broader Asia managers, dispersion in this segment remains considerable and both
YTD returns as well as MTD drawdowns are driven by investments in the AI space beyond China.
 
• Japan  focused managers down 1.9% MTD, with YTD now at 10.7%.
 
• Global Fundamental  LS managers down 3.8% MTD, +8.8% YTD. Global Systematics down
1.1% MTD, +18.7% YTD.  
 

Positioning and Flows
• The Global Prime book experienced large net selling flows MTD, with short sales exceeding long
sales significantly. North America and Asia dominated the selling activity.
 
• Asian equities saw heavy net selling as the AI sell off accelerated, with short sales exceeding long
sales 1.7 to 1. Activity was risk-on. Emerging Markets dominated the selling vs. Developed
Markets.
 
• Taiwan, Korea and Japan led the selling flows MTD with smaller contribution from China, with Hedge Funds
continuing to unwind AI exposures. Risk on flows in Japan and China were offset by de-grossing in Korea.
 
• Japan was net sold MTD with short sales in single stocks dominating. Net allocation declined
sharply while Gross saw a relatively smaller decrease.
 
• China saw moderate net selling activity with short sales dominating over long buys. A-shares led
the net selling as well as risk-on flows. US ADRs also contributed to the net selling flows while H-
shares saw risk on activity.
 
• Allocations to Asia declined sharply in the MTD period, but continue to remain significantly overweight vs. the
MSCI World AC benchmark.
• The net allocation to Asia in the Global Prime book declined 289 bps MTD, with the latest level at 27.3%,
representing a 10.9% overweight compared to MSCI World benchmark. This has declined from 20%
overweight around mid-June and has recovered from a 10.9% low on 30th July.
• Gross allocation declined 135 bps to 24.5%, reflecting an 8.0% overweight relative to MSCI
World.
 
• 6/11 sectors were net sold so far this month. Info Tech continued to lead the selling flows, followed
by Financials, Communication Services and Industrials. Health Care was the most net bought
sector, followed by Materials and Energy.
• Info Tech continues to see net selling flows MTD. Taiwan and Korea dominated the net selling in
this space, followed by smaller contributions from China and Japan. While allocations continue
to decline, the sector remains 14.2% overweight vs. MXAP. While this is down from a ~22%
peak in June, the level at the start of the year was 11% overweight.
 
Korea Allocations
Korea continues to dominate net selling and de-grossing flows in the region. Long sales dominated the
MTD selling. Net allocation declined to 3.4% (29th percentile 1-year, 86th percentile 5-year). The
recent peak in June was ~8%, while the level at the start of the year was at 2.5%. Gross allocation
remained declined to 2.4% (30th percentile 1-year, 86th percentile 5-year).
 

 
 
Source: Marquee MarketView
 
 
Taiwan Allocations
Taiwan saw large selling MTD with long and short sales contributing to the flows in equal proportions.
Risk activity was net flat. Net allocation to the market declined to 6.4% (56th percentile 1-year, 91st
percentile 5-year), while gross allocation ended at 4.3% (74th percentile 1-year, 95th percentile 5-
year).
