Global Views: Downside Growth Risks
 
• Global growth has been remarkably unremarkable in 2026, with fiscal easing and the AI boom offsetting drags from
energy and tariffs. Our baseline forecast is a similar pace in 2027, but downside risks are building because of
higher energy prices, higher bond yields, and the prospect of slower AI investment growth next year. 
• With AI labor market effects becoming more visible and inflation likely to decline, we expect fewer rate hikes than
priced for most major DM central banks.
 
GOAL KICKSTART - Below zero – Risk Appetite Indicator negative as Tech dominates cross-asset moves
 
• We remain modestly pro-risk in our asset allocation: OW Equities for 3m/12m, balanced by UW Credit (for
3m/12m), OW Cash for 3m and OW Bonds for 12m. 
• AI concerns, Middle East tensions and mid-term elections could sustain near-term volatility. That said, we think
earnings support, lighter positioning and lower valuations should help equities into year-end.
 
Apple Inc. (AAPL) - F4Q26 preview: iPhone 18 family + Duo to drive growth in F2027
 
• We expect AAPL to deliver in-line F4Q26 results, which including (1) iPhone revenue growth of +15% yoy as price
increases on the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max and older generation phones, as well as the launch of the Duo offset
modest volume declines as a result of price-volume elasticity and the delayed launch of the iPhone 18; (2)
Services revenue growth of +10% yoy despite slowing App Store growth as iCloud+, AppleCare+, subscription
services, and advertising grow double-digits; and (3) gross margins of 47.8% which includes a 1pp benefit from
tariff refunds and reflects headwinds from component cost inflation.
 
Allianz SE (ALVG.DE): 3Q'26 results preview
 
• Allianz reports its 3Q'26 results on November 12th. Overall, our 3Q'26 operating profit estimate is c.1% below
company-collected consensus (published 26/10/26) primarily driven by P&C (-2%) and Asset Management (-2%). 
• Our lower P&C operating profit estimate is underpinned by a c.15bps less favourable headline COR and c.2%
lower Insurance Revenue, where we expect the focus to be on the rate / volume trends in both Commercial and
Retail lines.
 
DSV A/S (DSV.CO) - Back on track, reiterate Buy (on CL)
 

• Overall we've made only limited changes to our 2026-30 estimates, down slightly on EPS, up slightly on FCF
(+/-2% on avg.). Our 12m, DCF-based price target is unchanged at DKK 2,300. Stepping back, the company's
earnings power has increased since the DBS deal. 
• When it was announced in 2024, DSV made ~DKK 4bn/quarter in EBIT. In Q326, DSV made DKK 6.3bn with Road
running c. DKK 1bn/quarter behind schedule, and we expect DSV to improve to a DKK 7-8bn quarterly EBIT range
during 2027.
 
Gjensidige (GJFG.OL) - Downgrading to Neutral as pricing normalises, leaving limited room to beat high expectations
 
• Looking ahead, we expect the pricing environment to normalise as claims inflation moderates. While the earn-
through of prior pricing actions should continue to support profitability, we expect the pace of further margin
expansion to become more measured from here. 
• Reflecting lower growth and margin expansion, we reduce our FY27e-30e EPS by c.2.4% on average.
 
Refreshing our European M&A basket (GSTRACQN) and LBO candidates
 
• We have rebalanced our European M&A basket (GSTRACQN) since our last semi-annual update, with additions
including Abivax, Haleon, Moncler, Burberry, Prada, Swatch Group, Rightmove Plc, Scout24 and Zegona. c.65% of
the basket comprises Industrials, Consumer Discretionary and Healthcare stocks, with c.35% UK-domiciled.
 
Unibail-Rodamco-Westfield (URW.PA) - Updating estimates post 9M26 results; maintain Buy
 
• We update our estimates post 9M26 results. Our 2026E-2030E EPS forecasts are down 0-2% on higher
refinancing costs, with management reiterating its 2028 guidance but closer to the lower end. Our 12m PT is 2%
lower at €121.0 (previously €124.0), implying 34% upside potential
