VZ 3Q'26: First Take
Conference call: Today at 8:30am ET (HERE for webcast link)
3Q headline results: Verizon's 3Q mobility & broadband rev met expectations,
growing +2.9% y/y to $23.49B (est / cons: $23.5B), which coupled with lower
equipment of $5.11B (est / cons: $5.5B) informed total revenue of $34.31B (est / cons:
$34.8B). Adj. EBITDA beat at $13.80B (est / cons: $13.7B) for margins of 40.2%.
Informed better adj. diluted EPS of $1.31/sh. (est: $1.25 / cons: $1.27), which excludes
$0.27B in after-tax charges for amort of acq-rel intangibles and acq. & integration
charges. Adj. FCF beat expectations at $7.80B (est: $6.6B / cons: $6.2B) on greater
CFFO of $11.52B (est: $10.7B / cons: $10.2B) and lower capex of $3.72B (est / cons:
$4.0B), with $4.0B repurchased YTD vs. $4.5B full-year expectation.
Source: Visible Alpha, Company filings, Wolfe Research, LLC. estimates
2026 guidance: Management raised guidance across the board. For the topline,
2026 postpaid phone net adds to +1.1-1.2M (prev. upper half of the +0.75-1.0M)
and Mobility & Broadband service rev growth to the upper half of +2.5-3.0% y/y
(prev. +2.5-3.0%). Adj diluted EPS raised to $5.04-5.06/sh. (prev. $4.99-5.04) and
FCF approaching $22.5B (prev. $21.9-22.1B), while 2026 repurchases maintained at
$4.5B.
Source: Visible Alpha, Company filings, Wolfe Research, LLC. estimates
