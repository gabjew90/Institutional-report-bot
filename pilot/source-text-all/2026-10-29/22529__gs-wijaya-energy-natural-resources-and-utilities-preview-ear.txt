GS - Wijaya - Energy Natural Resources and Utilities Preview
Earnings Ramping What's Topical - 27 Oct 2026
Adam Wijaya  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  Associate, Global Banking & Markets, FICC & Equities (Energy, Natural Resources &
Utilities Sector Specialist)
27 Oct 2026
On the back of the EQT/RRC deal – will be hosting a call with Research and our Special Sits desk to
discuss the merger and feedback from yesterday. 11 AM ET via Zoom.
BKR. Baker will report 3Q EPS tonight after the close. Desk has positioning score of 6/10 into the print.
We think the stock was well owned by the specialist community for the better part of 3Q – especially
given the uncertainty in the ME, strong 2Q IET order momentum and Chart guidance in the rear view
mirror. Over the last month – the stock has underperformed vs HAL and SLB – which we think is more
indicative of a ‘relief rally’ in the broader OFS complex – likely given how light / underweight positioning
has been in the Services group levered to activity. For the quarter – we think the bar is relatively low for
OFSE – 3Q OFSE consensus revs have actually stepped up over the last couple of months and guidance
historically more in-line with Street numbers (seeing broadly unchanged 4Q OFSE revs expectations). IET
likely to be the focus for folks (more industrial investor inbounds on this lately) – and the one thing we are
watching more closely is the commentary around Qatar on the LNG front. Recall over the last week –
QatarEnergy headlines have all broadly highlighted the same thing – securing contracts / offtakes with
non-ME players (LNG, WDS, VG). Aside from the more obvious answer of Gulf countries / companies
looking for more exposure to Western assets over time – incremental questions on whether this means we
see a slowdown / pause / change in rhetoric around the LNG buildout in the ME – and the readacross to
IET specifically for LNG. Worth noting we have seen a number of headlines from QatarEnergy around
force majeure extensions (one this AM to Italy’s Edison until end of December). Aside from the IET LNG
specific concerns – expect more eyeballs on the turbine outlook / rate of change on order front…
especially with overcapacity narratives looming for the better part of two quarters.
DINO. The inland vs coastal refining trade has been extremely topical on the desk over the last month –
and would think this has been one of the more crowded trades in Refining (take a look at DINO/MPC vs
VLO or DK vs PARR). Desk has positioning score for DINO at 7/10 into the print. Street numbers have
consistently moved higher throughout EPS season and similar to the rest of the group – would expect
Refining earnings to be eyeopening good (Street at $1.67 bn of Refining EBITDA for 3Q). Focus has
largely been on a handful of items – and most of these have not changed since the 2Q print. (1) CEO
announcement expected near-term (reach out for feedback) – the main question here being ‘any change to
strategic priorities?’ (2) Midstream capex expected to be moving higher on go forward – seeing Street at
$200 mn for 2027 and $245 mn for 2028 – looking for any update here. (3) Refining commentary likely to
center around crude slate / mix – especially on the heavy side given how diffs have moved recently.
EXE. This will be the new CEO’s first EPS call – and after the EQT/RRC deal – expect there to be more
eyeballs on this print and the call. Would think expectations are low for the print itself (desk has

positioning score of 4/10) – and have frankly been getting more questions on what gets folks interested in
the story again relative to other parts of the gas complex. 2027 Henry Hub views aside (which we have
discussed more in a cautious light) – market really looking for a couple of things here. Marketing strategy
post Twin Eagle – what’s the path forward for more growth here. LT offtakes – has been more topical for
gas E&P broadly and market feedback has generally been more constructive. Haynesville – seeing
guidance for 4Q production flat QoQ – with recent gas price pressure – could we see flex in guidance
here. Feedback welcome.
