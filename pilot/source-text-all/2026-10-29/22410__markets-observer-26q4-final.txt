Bond markets across the board fell in the third quarter as inflation, deficit concerns, and the September Federal Reserve rate hike stretched yields and depressed prices. Equity markets sputtered in the 
third quarter but remain broadly positive for the year. Small-cap US stocks fell 5.6% in the third quarter as investors favored larger companies in the rising rate environment. Commodities recovered in
