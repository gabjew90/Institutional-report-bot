Another beat/raise for high quality 
compounder on track for Rule of 65 
Reiterate Rating: BUY | PO: 420.00 USD | Price: 322.17 USD   
  
 
 
Maestro adds an AI model layer; Reiterate Buy, $420 PO 
CDNS delivered its third FY26 raise, most of it coming from 4Q sales/EPS +4%/+10% 
vs. cons. FY26E now implies ~21% sales growth on 44.7% pf-OpM, above “Rule of 65” 
framework. CDNS launched Maestro, a family of chip-design-specialized post-trained 
open-weight models, adding a model layer on top of CDNS's agents and core design 
tools. CDNS continues to target incremental pf-OpMs of at least 50%, though CDNS 
delivered an average 54% across FY21-25, suggesting conservatism. Near-term, backlog 
rose to a record $8.2bn while cRPO was flat QoQ at $4.2bn ahead of FY27E guide in 4Q. 
We reit. Buy, raise FY27/28E pf-EPS +4%/+5%, and see out-year ests. as conservative 
relative to peer SNPS given lack of l-t commentary from mgmt. and strong track record 
of beat/raising off prudent guidance. We maintain our $420 PO now on 35x CY28E (44x 
CY27E prior) while the stock continues to trade at ~35x NTM PE vs. 43x 3Y median. 
Likes: Record HW, broad share gains, faster buybacks  
Hardware posted another record quarter with supply no longer a constraint. Hardware 
demand remains very strong across several large customers, including Intel. Share gains 
are broadening beyond digital implementation as CDNS logged 16 full-flow digital wins 
in the quarter, plus displacements in analog (Spectre FX at 2nm) and PCB (Allegro at an 
EV OEM). The 3Q buyback doubled to $400m, and the new $2.5bn authorization is 
mgmt.'s largest buyback increase. Authorizations are typically used over ~1-1.5yrs, while 
CDNS is already on track to repurchase $1.2bn of shares in FY26E. 
Risks: China/Hexagon FY26E contribution, 50% OpM path 
We flag China upside (initially guided 12-13% of $5.95bn initial revenue guide, now on 
track for ~15% of $6.4bn current guide, or ~$200m upside) and Hexagon contribution 
(~$160m) account for ~80% of the total FY26E raise from 4Q25, with normalized (ex-
Hexagon, China upside) growth rate of only 14%. Further, to reach 50% pf-OpM by 
FY30E, CDNS will need to deliver ~57% incremental op. margins, slightly ahead of recent 
54% average. With premium to SNPS historically due in-part to higher profitability and 
SNPS targeting 50% pf-OpM by FY30E, CDNS may have less relative upside if SNPS is 
able to close the profitability gap, partially through Ansys synergies and IP royalties.

Company Sector

Company Description 
Cadence is the second largest provider of electronic design 
automation (EDA) software and tools used to design, verify, 
and lay out semiconductor chips and electronics system. Its 
core competency lies in analog/custom chip design. 
Cadence is also the 3rd largest supplier of semiconductor 
intellectual property, and has aspirations to expand its TAM 
into the adjacent system analysis market. 
Investment Rationale 
We rate CDNS Buy. We like CDNS leading position in an EDA 
industry that is levered to the same secular trends as semis 
but with much more muted cyclicality. We think CDNS has 
defensiveness/scarcity value, and is a unique beneficiary of 
rising chip complexity.

Price objective basis & risk 
Cadence (CDNS) 
Our $420 PO is based on 35x CY28E P/E, in the lower half of its historical range of 33x-
66x and justified in our view given the strategic importance of EDA in an increasingly 
fragmented global electronics supply chain offset by recent multiple compression in 
software. 
 
Downside risks are: (1) Share loss in existing markets to primary competitors, (2) a 
broader economic downturn dampens semis R&D spending and corresponding spend on 
EDA tools and services, (3) escalation of US-China trade war limits CDNS' ability to sell 
to key customers, (4) semiconductor industry consolidation accelerates which could 
diminish customer spending power, (5) venture into adjacent system analysis market 
fails to meaningfully accelerate revenue growth and incremental investments suppress 
margin expansion.
