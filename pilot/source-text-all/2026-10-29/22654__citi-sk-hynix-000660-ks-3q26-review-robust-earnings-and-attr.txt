SK Hynix (000660.KS)
3Q26 Review: Robust Earnings and Attractive Shareholder Returns
CITI'S TAKE 
SK Hynix reported robust 3Q26 OP of W83.3tr (+38% QoQ/+632% 
YoY/OPM: 80%) driven by rising memory ASPs and increased sales of high-
value products despite FX headwinds. Hynix announced a ~W100tr FY26 
shareholder return, including ~W14tr in 3Q dividends and an additional 
W20tr buyback. Reiterate Buy.
3Q26 Review — Hynix’s 3Q26 DRAM B/G came in at +10% QoQ, with server DDR5 
shipments growing >40% QoQ, while DRAM ASP at +high-20% QoQ, supported by 
conventional DRAM price increases and a higher HBM4 mix. Hynix 3Q26 NAND B/G 
declined slightly QoQ amid a shift toward higher-value 321L TLC and eSSD products, 
while NAND ASP rose +mid-20% QoQ on stronger pricing and a higher SSD mix.
4Q26E & 2027E Outlook — Hynix expects 4Q26E DRAM B/G to increase +mid-20% 
QoQ on 1cnm expansion and M15X HBM4 ramp-up, and NAND B/G to grow +MSD% 
QoQ, supported by increased 321L eSSD shipments. For 2027E, the company 
projects achievable DRAM/NAND B/G at +mid-20%/+high-teen% YoY. With LTAs 
covering >50% of 27-28E capacity, Hynix views DRAM/HBM as effectively sold out 
through 2029Em including customer volumes under discussion. Hynix is seeing 
robust customer requests exceeding 2x available supply despite HBM spec issues, 
whose impact could be largely offset by higher capacity per die.
FY26 Shareholder Return Plan Unveiled — Hynix announced a ~W100tr 
shareholder return plan for FY26, comprising ~W30tr in cash dividends and ~W60tr 
in share buybacks/cancellations, including the previous W40tr buyback. For 3Q26, 
Hynix declared ~W14tr in dividends (DPS: W20K), including a W10k special dividend 
from Kioxia investment gains. Additional W20tr buyback was announced, with 
W10tr earmarked for cancellation and the remainder for employee compensation 
excluded from shareholder returns. Management plans to continue quarterly 
buybacks, viewing the current share price as undervalued relative to its robust 
earnings and cash generations.
Implication — Reflecting robust 3Q results and a constructive memory outlook, we 
lift 4Q26E OP to W107tr from W89.8tr and raise our 26E/27E OP estimates by 
7%/12%. Under the ~W100tr FY26 shareholder return, we forecast ~W35tr in 
shareholder returns for 4Q26E, comprising ~W15tr in buyback and ~W20tr in 
dividend. Thus, we reiterate our Buy rating with an unchanged TP of W3mn.

Company description
SK Hynix manufactures semiconductors such as DRAM, NAND flash memory 
and SRAM chips.
Investment strategy
We rate SK Hynix shares as Buy. We believe SK Hynix is well positioned to 
benefit from the DRAM market recovery driven by limited supply growth. In 
particular, we see SK Hynix's technology leadership in DRAM, coupled with 
the high exposure to fast-growing server applications, supporting the 
company's long-term earnings growth. Additionally, we think SK Hynix's 
acquisition of Intel's NAND business should produce synergies with the 
company's existing NAND business and strengthen its product portfolio.
Valuation
Our 12-month target price for SK Hynix of W3,000,000 is derived using a 
sum-of-the-parts (SOTP) methodology, based on 2026E EBITDA. In 
calculating total operating value, we break down SK Hynix's operating 
businesses into HBM and commodity/others business to reflect a structural 
change in next-gen memory market. We reference a global peer (TSMC) in 
assigning a fair-value EV/EBITDA multiple for HBM business, as we project the 
memory market is evolving from a traditional commodity market into a highly 
customized & customer-specific market much closer to the foundry business. 
For commodity memory business, we apply a historical average of 12m fwd 
EV/EBITDA during the beginning or early stage of memory upcycles in the 
past. We apply 7.9x EV/EBITDA to HBM segment and 6.3x to commodity 
segment to derive the target price.
Risks
Downside risks that could prevent the shares from reaching our target price 
include: 1) a downturn in DRAM demand; 2) weaker NAND demand than our 
forecasts; and 3) weakness in global consumption.
