FED RATE HIKES CHALLENGE 
CORE FIXED INCOME 
Recent Fed cycles highlight core bonds’ sensitivity to tightening: 
The Agg returned -4.1% annualized during hikes versus gains during 
holds and cuts. 
	
—
U.S. core fixed income came under pressure in Q3, with the Bloomberg U.S. Aggregate Bond Index (the Agg) 
falling 3.5% as resilient economic data, persistent inflation and high energy prices drove Treasury yields 
sharply higher.¹
	
—
Recent history underscores the challenge rising rates pose for traditional fixed income. Since late 2017, 
the Agg has returned −4.1% annualized during Fed hiking cycles, versus 3.3% when the Fed held rates 
steady and approximately 4.3% during easing cycles.²  
	
—
While the Fed is expected to hold next week, markets are currently pricing in two to three hikes over the 
next 12 months.¹
	
—
The prospect of further tightening complicates the outlook for core fixed income. Higher yields provide 
greater income, but continued rate uncertainty leaves the path for total returns less certain.
