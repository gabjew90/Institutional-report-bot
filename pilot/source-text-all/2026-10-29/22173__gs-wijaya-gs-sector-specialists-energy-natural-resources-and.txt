Feedback Across the Board
I'm on the road today meeting with investors to discuss early EPS thoughts, setups for the remainder of the
year, and key themes we are focused on… let me know if want to connect next week on some of the areas
of focus / feedback.
Gas pipes – there is continued interest here. This has been one of the more topical groups on the desk / in
conversations over the last 72 hours. Leaving the macro tape aside – there’s real interest in talking about
gas infra (again). It’s important to note - over the last two years, there’s been more generalist interest in the
space and the focus has shifted from E&Ps into Midstream. While the rate backdrop is still top of mind –
the number of requests for ‘WMB bogeys’ or ‘what’s next for KMI or ‘what gets DTM working again’ has
been eye-opening. KMI provided an extremely strong set of results on Wednesday after the close – the
backlog step-up was well above buyside and sellside expectations (more buyside inbounds had been $1-$2
bn increase)… but we are already getting the question of ‘okay this was great – but now what do I do with
it?’ Positioning was tilted more positive into EPS than we have seen in several quarters (desk had 7/10)…
which is telling. Ie – strong backlog update, constructive commentary on the call … and yet there are more
questions around whether defending here is the next step. WMB also screens as well owned to us into EPS
– and to be clear – this has been one of the more well owned stocks over the last year +. Messaging around
BTM project execution path and contract announcement is still top of mind. We are running through
various scenarios around what could come at EPS (ie small project vs no project vs no project and Transco
updates etc)… feedback generally has been ‘even with a quiet quarter – this is one of the most exciting
stocks in Midstream – it should be fine’.
BTM – macro vs micro. I wrote a similar note last week highlighting ‘what’s priced into the stocks at
current levels from a power standpoint’ (– and last week we would have said there’s little / no power
embedded in several of the topical names on the desk (LBRT, KGS, PUMP)… and after yesterday– would
say we are nearly fully back to looking at base biz valuations in what’s being reflected in current share
price. LBRT EPS was the most topical yesterday – when the print first hit – the inbounds were
directionally positive. 500 MW headline eased concerns that it could be two contracts / industrial player /
non AI customer – but the contract remains TBD and there are still questions on financing. When I look
across the entirety of the power spectrum (BTM, IPPs, solar, battery storage, regulated utilities, nuclear) –
BTM still screens as a pocket of the market more folks are excited about.

Refining – the bar is high. It’s very clear to me that specialists are still searching for answers on ‘what
makes me want to buy the 3Q prints’ (taker of all feedback). Take a look at the VLO print from yesterday -
$23.90 of EPS in a quarter is a huge number – yet buyside was looking for something closer to $25. Use
of cash has been debated for the entirety of the group and would think the market looks to VLO as a solid
indicator for how these companies will position balance sheets for a return to mid cycle cracks over time
… sitting on $10 bn of cash – buybacks will continue, but generally feedback has been constructive on
building cash at these levels. Cracks have rolled here as of late and would think with stocks still near
highs, 4Q revisions more in focus, high bars into EPS and cash balances scrutinized – more specialists are
leaning cautious on the setup for the group near term.
