ANTOFAGASTA PLC  (ANTO.L) - Guidance reset: upgrade to Hold; Sell to Hold. Tgt 3600p to 3300p. Last 
Close 3174p. 
Liam Fitzpatrick: Following ANTO's Q3 production report, we have refreshed our estimates and upgrade from Sell 
to Hold, with a revised TP of £33/sh (from £36/sh). The shares have pulled back sharply following the latest 
update, underperforming copper and listed peers by ~5-15% over the past month, and the revised guidance now 
reflects a more realistic and achievable level, in our view. Production guidance has been missed each year since 
2021, weighing on investor confidence. On average, production has come in ~4% below the mid-point and ~1% 
below the lower end of the respective guidance ranges. We believe 2027 guidance has now been appropriately 
reset, with the range of 630-690 kt (DB 657 kt) implying only modest YoY production growth, despite expected 
higher grades and throughput at Los Pelambres. 
  
BASF  (BASFn.DE) - Deal overhang, downgrade to Hold; Buy to Hold. Tgt EUR60 to EUR53. Last Close EUR52.24. 
Virginie Boucher-Ferte: BBG reported on Friday that BASF made a new offer to acquire Evonik at around 
€24/share, 8% above its previous €22.15 proposal. As discussed in detail in our recent note (BASF/Evonik: Even if 
no deal, the genie is out of the bottle), where we analysed a potential transaction at €25/share, we believe a 
combination would make both strategic and financial sense over the long term. However, a transaction of this 
magnitude, with Evonik "core" sales representing 25% of BASF core business sales, would materially increase 
complexity and execution risk, further increase BASF’s exposure to structurally low-growth, higher-cost Europe, 
and appear somewhat at odds with its recent narrative of portfolio simplification, non-Verbund divestments and 
shareholder returns, at least through the remainder of the current strategic period to 2028. We believe the 
potential transaction is therefore likely to create a material overhang on the shares until there is greater clarity 
on the final price, funding structure and implications for shareholder returns, which could take close to 12 months. 
We consequently downgrade the stock to Hold.
