3Q26 Quick Look – OFSE Leads
EBITDA Beat, IET Orders Crush
Again
BKR's 3Q EBITDA was 5%/5% above Barclays/Cons., led by
OFSE with upside from Chart's first quarter. IET orders of
$5.9bn were well above consensus on a 1.1 GW order from a
major U.S. tech company. Management called the upstream
market inflecting and raised the Horizon 2 target to $50bn+.
 
BKR reported Adjusted EBITDA of $1,415mm, 5%/5% above Barclays/consensus, led by
OFSE. Segment EBITDA of $659mm was 6%/5% above Barclays/consensus, and margins rose
47bps q/q to 18.0% on higher volume and pricing. Total revenue of $8,025mm was 3%/3% above
Barclays/consensus, helped by Chart's first partial quarter. Chart margins were 150bps above
our estimate. FCF of $734mm was well ahead of our $276mm.
What stood out to us
• Data centers were the headline in IET's $5.9bn of orders, led by a 1.1 GW order from a
major U.S. tech company. The award pairs 43 NovaLT and Frame 5 turbine packages with
BRUSH generators. A second ~500 MW data center order also uses BRUSH, bringing 3Q AI
power awards to about 1.6 GW. We think Brush is underappreciated in the BKR story. Owning
the generator lets BKR sell a complete power package and grows the installed base for higher-
margin aftermarket work over time. LNG awards (Venture Global's Plaquemines expansion
and Cloud Connector pipeline) and a record quarter for compressor upgrades rounded out
the quarter. IET RPO hit a record $39.4bn, and the Horizon 2 target was raised to $50bn+.
• BKR is calling the upstream market inflecting, similar to what we heard last week.
Management pointed to "a record RPO, robust order pipeline, growing installed base and
inflecting energy upstream market." SLB set the bar with a first look at 2027: mid single-digit
global activity growth, with no Middle East recovery assumed (held flat at 2H26 levels). HAL
and WFRD framed 2027 the same way, and all three still came out roughly in line with
consensus. We expect BKR sees the market the same way, with any Middle East normalization
contributing to OFSE upside. While there are still major unknowns, OFSE indications for 2027
are what we're listening for most on the call.
