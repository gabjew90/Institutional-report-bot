We remain constructive on XYZ shares into its earnings next Tuesday (11/3 AMC); 
spending data indicates largely stable growth while we think changes to Cash App 
Borrow terms (extending from 4-weeks to 6-weeks at a 7.5% rate from ~5%) gives 
the company tools to support ongoing gross profit upside into 2027. The latter 
could come with higher losses (as a % of originations) considering the extension 
of duration, though we expect this to be non-linear with the pricing/duration 
extension and drive accretion to adj. operating profit (and likely gross profit). We 
update our model to take an early view on how this change might impact the P&L 
(lower Borrow originations, slightly higher GP, higher losses as % of originations, 
higher variable profit), which lifts our 2H gross profit modestly while keeping 
overall Transaction and Loan Losses (~$580M, Street $560M) largely unchanged; 
we acknowledge a wide range of outcomes here depending on origination trends, 
but remain confident XYZ can manage losses with flexibility to drive gross profit 
upside by raising limits if they choose to do so. Overall, we remain excited about 
improved product velocity (e.g. Square Kiosks, Dessa, Buzz), and have greater 
conviction in Block’s ability to hit its mid-teens GP growth exit rate in 4Q 
following the Borrow pricing changes, which should support momentum into 
1H27: we reiterate our OW rating and $110 Dec-27 PT. 
•
3Q26 Expectations. JPMe/Street estimates are broadly in-line with guidance 
showing GP growth of 18% and adj. operating margins of 28%, implying Rule 
of 46. Consensus forecasts ~12% Square GPV growth, implying a modest 
deceleration from +13.4% reported in 2Q26 (JPMe at 13%; US +10%, non-US 
+27%); intra-qtr, XYZ described growth trends in July and August as stable.
•
Prelim 2027 Views. Historically, XYZ has occasionally provided preliminary 
year-ahead expectations at its 3Q results. JPMe/Street estimates assume 15% 
gross profit growth and 31% adj. operating profit margins for FY27. Our adj. 
EPS estimates are ahead of Street assuming 34% growth to $5.46, while Street 
is at $5.25 implying 30% growth.
•
Model Update primarily to estimate the impact of Cash App Borrow shifting 
to a six-week product (at 7.5% pricing) from a four-week product (at ~5%), as 
we previously discussed as part of our Small Dollar Loan Survey Results.  We 
directionally expect the change to: (1) reduce Borrow originations, relative to 
the prior structure, (2) be neutral (base case) to notably accretive to gross profit 
(bull case), depending origination outcomes, (3) lift Borrow losses as a % of 
originations, though by less than the pricing change, driving (4) higher Borrow 
AOI, net of losses. As such our 2H26 gross profit and adj. operating profit 
estimates move up modestly, while we keep FY27 largely unchanged for now.

Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks
Block Inc (Overweight; Price Target: $110.00)
Investment Thesis
Block (Square at the time) pioneered micro-merchant card acceptance in 2009, much like 
PayPal did for online payments years ago, and has evolved to be the scale leader in the 
popular software-based POS revolution. Prior to Block, micro and casual merchants rarely 
accepted in-person card-based payments as mobile terminals offered by traditional POS 
technology providers were too costly and banks were generally unwilling to underwrite 
merchant bank accounts for casual sellers. In just 10 years, Block has grown to ~$200bn+ 
in annualized merchant volume, serving ~4 million active sellers. It has also developed Cash 
App, one of the largest consumer Fintechs in the US, with over 50M active users and strong 
engagement, especially in the younger demographic. We rate shares Overweight given 
Block’s large and untapped addressable market, unique growth characteristics, and an 
equally unique mission and corporate culture.
Valuation
Our $110 Dec 2027 price target (unchanged) applies a ~16x multiple against our CY28E adj. 
EPS (implied GAAP multiple also 16x). This multiple is a discount to XYZ’s three-year 
average NTM multiple (~19x). We find it reasonable to strike a balance between today’s 
multiple (15x) and history as we’re cautiously optimistic that management’s efforts to 
improve product velocity and GTM can result in multiple expansion through the forecast 
period. Our A$156 price target for Block - AU is simply an AUD currency translation of our 
USD price target.
Risks to Rating and Price Target
•
Macro sensitivity: a slowdown could pressure Square GPV growth, increase 
bankruptcies, and weigh on Cash App card spending volume; 
•
Slower adoption/lower attachment rates of Square’s value-added services including 
Banking products, which represent a key part of the growth story;
•
Downturn in the small business credit cycle or the inability to fund merchant cash 
advances, which would harm growth and profitability; similarly, higher losses on 
consumer lending origination volumes could impact profits;
•
Net margin compression for Square due to increased competition or pricing pressure at 
the hands of larger SMBs;
•
Significant devaluation of major cryptocurrencies, notably bitcoin, could negatively 
impact user engagement in the product popular on the Cash App platform. Regulation 
of cryptocurrency could also negatively impact the potential for Block to monetize 
crypto investments. 
•
Key person risk – CEO and co-founder Jack Dorsey is the visionary who drives the 
unique culture at Block and has helped attract key talent to the firm.
