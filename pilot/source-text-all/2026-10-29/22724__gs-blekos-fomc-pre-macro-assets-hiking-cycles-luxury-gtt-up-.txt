US ECONOMICS ANALYST - October FOMC Preview: Hiking at a Slower Pace
• Comments from FOMC participants since their last meeting point to a compromise to raise the funds rate
every other meeting. This means that the FOMC is set to skip raising rates at its October meeting but is
likely to deliver a second hike in December.
• Market pricing of the Fed’s path has taken out one hike and now peaks at just over 4.5%. Our baseline
and probability-weighted Fed forecasts are considerably less hawkish, both because we are skeptical that
the FOMC will hike four times and because we expect cuts down the road.
Global Markets Daily: Macro Assets in Fed Hiking Cycles
• Our baseline of a modest number of hikes, growth around potential, and gradual inflation relief points to
a modestly positive path for equities.
• History highlights three potential risk scenarios to that view: an earlier end to tightening on better
inflation news, which would be the most positive for risk assets; an interruption driven by growth or
financial stress concerns, which would at least initially be less friendly; or a more extended tightening
cycle if growth remains resilient, which could raise the risk of a larger
growth downgrade down the road.

GTT (GTT.PA) - An attractive entry point into a highly visible LNG growth story; upgrade to Buy
• We upgrade GTT to Buy from Neutral with a €210 12-month price target, implying c.16% upside.
Following the c.12% post-results share price correction, we see an attractive entry point into a high-
quality, asset-light LNG name with strong medium-term revenue visibility.
• GTT is the global leader in LNG containment systems, with a highly profitable, asset-light licensing
model and near-100% market share at major Asian shipyards.
Reckitt (RKT.L) - Lysol headwind does not derail an attractive investment case; Buy

• Core Reckitt Q3 sales were softer than expected with +3.3% organic sales growth (OSG) vs. consensus
at +4.1% (First Take here). While reported sales were in line, the OSG miss came from the USA where
Lysol (c.35% of US sales) suffered from unexpected manufacturing constraints.
• Buying opportunity post Lysol one-off, with Reckitt trading on 14.5x CY27E P/E at a -19% discount to
GS Staples sector vs. parity historically.
Saint-Gobain (SGOB.PA): 3Q'26 results: Margin guidance reiterated, but volume deceleration more
visible; Neutral
• Saint-Gobain reported 3Q’26 organic growth of +2.5%, broadly in line with VA consensus (+2.7%) /
GSe (+3.0%), but slowing from +3.5% in 2Q as volumes weakened, particularly in France. Americas was
broadly in line with already low expectations, while Scandinavia/APAC were better. FY26 EBITDA
margin guidance was maintained at >15% (Prior GSe 15.1%, VA Consensus 15.2%).
BNP Paribas (BNPP.PA): Q3'26 First Look
• Strong Q3 results (despite accommodating a weaker Arval) support FY26 forecasts with earlier capital
return (new SBB announcement) and a path to additional excess capital return at year-end.
• Revenues c.3%above (led by the CIB, notably Equities c.30% above, FICC -2% below), while Retail
Banking posted a modest 2% miss driven by Arval (c.24% miss on UCS pressures). Opex effectively in
line, leading to PPP 4% above.
Novartis (NOVN.S) - Strong 3Q26 profit beat driven by cost control, however valuation remains full
relative to sector growth; remain Sell
• Following our model changes, our 12m target prices remain unchanged at CHF 113/ADR $136.
However, with Novartis currently trading close to all-time highs (c.7% premium to the sector on 2027E
P/E), despite significant patent expiration risk, we believe the downside risks are not adequately reflected,
and remain Sell rated.
