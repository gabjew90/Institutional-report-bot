US Growth Intact, Antipodeans Busy
Calendar, And BRL Positioning Update
Dimitrios Laloudakis
27-Oct-2026, 07:27 ET (EDT)
*Sales desk commentary. Not a product of UBS Research. For institutional investors only.*
• United States: Swath of macro data including durable goods, federal reserve regional survey data, and
conference board data releases. The trendline in durable goods is still quite strong (figure #1) with recent
months showing signs of acceleration. Conference Board data all screens anemically low relative to history.
Whether one looks at consumer confidence, present situations, or expectations (figure #2), all the time series
fall below the 5th percentile on a 5yr lookback. However current financial conditions still point to being
accommodative to nominal growth (figure #3)
• Antipodeans: calendar is quite busy later tonight with Australia September CPI due with average monthly
headline and median trimmed of +63bps and +27bps, respectively. A hotter-than-expected CPI print doesn’t
seem like a pre-requisite for AUD strength as stabilization in equity markets / risk sentiment can serve as an
offsetting valuation driver. The AI winners, semi trade, and speculative growth baskets have recovered from
recent lows in max drawdowns (figure #4) supporting risk sentiment and thereby risk-on / higher beta
currencies. FX Divergence outlined the case for AUD longs from a prior publication for a technical bounce and
to be held into this week as well given the macro catalysts. In New Zealand, expectations for a 25bps hike by
the RBNZ is broadly expected.
• Emerging Markets (Brazil + South Africa): Positioning in BRL longs according to the FX Flow Monitor
highlights a sharp reduction by HFs yesterday at a -5σ pace. Yesterday’s selloff was nothing short of historic
with the largest daily loss for USDBRL over the past ten years. So far, the spillover to broad EMFX has been
limited (figure #5 of EMFX breadth and depth of returns). However, I am reflagging the view that upside risk
remains prevalent for USDZAR, originally shared from an earlier morning note given the reduced carry appeal,
downside risk to inflation in FY27, and pressure from lower gold prices.
