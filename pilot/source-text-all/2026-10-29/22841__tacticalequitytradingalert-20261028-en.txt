Consider Buying Freeport-McMoRan Inc. (FCX US)
Investment Thesis:
We are initiating a 120 day TETA trade idea on Freeport
McMoran (FCX US), an international natural resources
company, with a take profit level of US$80 and a stop loss of
US$52. FCX stock has pulled back from its YTD high of US$80
in July amidst the rise in US Treasury yields which led to a broad
sell off in the commodity miners. Despite the broad sell off in
the commodity space, copper prices have stayed resilient with
LME pricing holding above $14,000/MT. The disconnect
between FCX’s stock price and copper may be largely
attributable to liquidity pressure on FCX stock, as we note that
FCX is a well-owned stock within the hedge fund community and
thus may have been vulnerable to hedge funds degrossing
equity exposure in 3Q.
The setup for Freeport McMoran is cleaner today with (1)
COMEX-LME premium vanishing due to reduced expectations
of copper tariffs in the US and (2) Grasberg mine disruption in
the rearview mirror and production is resuming. Copper prices
are likely to stay on an uptrend as well due to strong  demand
tailwinds from AI/datecenter related consumption and as such
we see the recent FCX stock price decline as a pullback amidst
a longer term uptrend. Any easing USD or interest rate
environment may provide the catalyst for the stock to converge
with that of copper prices as we believe the selloff in FCX should
be a short term phenomenon.

What is a Tactical Equity Trading Alert (TETA)?
A TETA note indicates that the equity competence center believes
a security is likely to ‘rise’ or ‘fall’; either on an absolute basis or
relative to the country/industry index; over either 15, 30, 45, 60 or
90 days (can close idea before expiry).
‘Positive return’ TETA = ‘Absolute TETAs’ with positive returns and
‘Relative TETAs’ that outperformed the country/industry index
‘Negative return’ TETA = ‘Absolute TETAs’ with negative returns
and ‘Relative TETAs’ that underperformed the country/industry
index
TETAs are not and should not be considered a portfolio. Each
investment idea is chosen based on its own merit and without any
consideration of the other investment ideas chosen. Specifically,
there has been no effort to mitigate the risks of investing in any
collective group of TETAs. Concepts important to a balanced
portfolio, such as negative correlation and diversification, have not
been considered. Treating TETAs as a portfolio will subject you to
the risk of losing all or a substantial portion of your investments.
