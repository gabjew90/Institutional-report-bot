LME Mining conference feedback note - Scarce copper supply, harder growth and changing metal
markets… Discussions centred on the increasing challenge of delivering the next wave of copper supply in
a world characterised by declining grades, rising capital intensity, permitting complexity and growing
geopolitical fragmentation. Resource security emerged as a dominant theme, with governments playing a
more active role in commodity markets through tariffs, strategic stockpiling, financing support and critical
mineral initiatives. We also growing focus on capital discipline, with companies pursuing portfolio

rationalisation, infrastructure monetisation and non-core asset sales to recycle capital into future growth
opportunities.
