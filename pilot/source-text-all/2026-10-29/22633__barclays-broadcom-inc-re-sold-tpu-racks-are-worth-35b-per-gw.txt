in the effort to square AVGO's $/GW. 1) the newly announced SPV (see here) is worth $63B in

us.

What’s Much More Likely – The two variables are a) the total chip + networking $/GW

What this Means for AVGO Forecast – In aggregate we estimate total GWs deployed of 11.3

revenue of ~$138B. In CY28, we estimate ~22.6 potential GWs which implies total potential

What this Means for the AI Ecosystem – External TPU total $/GW is $35B vs. NVDA Vera Rubin
$/GW of ~$40B for AI systems. This represents a ~13% discount for TPU, but we are surprised
how close that runs to NVDA. On the one hand, it is impressive how little discount there is for

historically a big difference vs. GPUs. On the other hand, $5B per GW adds up quickly at scale
and for a multi GW deployment is substantial savings vs. NVDA.

Credit Research View
Andrew Keches, CFA(i) BCI, US  |  Remy Neuhaus(i) BCI, US
Negligible impact to AVGO credit quality, but the supply picture remains a serious
overhang
In the past few months, two chip financings have been placed through Broadcom’s XPV
platform totaling ~$100bn. These deals include ~$75bn of backstops from AVGO in the form of
residual value support mechanisms. 85% of the first transaction was backstopped, stepping
down to 72.5% in the second. The debt sits at a remote SPV, but the rating agencies (and credit
investors) are planning to add the backstops back onto Broadcom’s balance sheet on a rateable
basis tied to the balance of debt outstanding. 
While magnitude of the adjustment is large, and likely growing with future deals, the reality is
that AVGO's credit quality has a significant moat. Earnings and cash flow are accelerating which
absorbs most of the backstop adjustment from a leverage standpoint and rating agencies
remain on Positive outlook. Of course these structures create concentrated exposure to certain
AI labs on a contingent basis which could amplify the risk in an “AI winter”, but that is pretty far
from our view at this point. 
The more tangible risk we see has to do with supply technicals associated with chip financing.

with the newer vintage of TPU pods, the total potential issuance from the platform could be in
