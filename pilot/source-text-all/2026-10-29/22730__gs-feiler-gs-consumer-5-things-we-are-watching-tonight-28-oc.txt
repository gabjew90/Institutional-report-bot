GS Consumer: 5 Things We Are Watching Tonight
Scott Feiler
28 Oct 2026
Some chaotic price action (per usual) today, along with a lot of EPS prints overnight and
ahead in the morning. Here are 5 things we are watching tonight and into tomorrow
morning...
 
1. Restaurants: A ton of questions on today’s restaurant price action. At +3% today, the sub-sector was by far the
best in consumer on the day. Some of the smaller cap names were more like +9-11%. The big question was whether
the strength was due to WEN strategic headlines or if it was due to EAT (+12%) & WING (+12%) EPS results. Given
the small caps outperformed, it appeared more due to positioning/WEN, but most feedback is that this story should
ultimately have limited read-across. We think the bigger story is EAT out-quarter comments being better than feared
(high +MSD type trends) and WING quite soft at -5.4% in 3Q, but better than lower fears. CMG also beat tonight and
what they say on 4Q and the price action will determine if the group rally is durable. We think the biggest focus
remains 4Q SSS in the space. We were better to buy on the desk today in the space. 
 
2.    CMG – 4Q Trends in Focus: The stock is +3% in the post. This has been the biggest focus of restaurants
inbounds and active on our trading desk into the print (much better to buy today). The 3Q comp checks the box
(+2.1% vs Consensus +1.5%) and we think investors will be looking for any hints around QTD trends (which has been
the recent concern) for ultimate stock direction. We think this is one of the more important 4Q updates (if we get it) and
reactions in the group. 
 
3.    SFM – Huge Move Lower. Grocery Issue, or SFM Issue?: Shares are -17% on a well below
consensus 4Q guide. Expectations were low on 3Q and 4Q top-line but you rarely do you see an EPS guide below this
much in grocery. EPS was guided 60% below and comp sales 400 bps below. Leah Jordan downgraded ACI last week
and we got more pushback than expected for a grocery downgrade. Investors had been slightly less negative the
space, given a view that sticky inflation could provide a bit of comp relief. However, SFM has continued to have
weaker sentiment, given the data. The question will be if this is specific to SFM (most will assume yes), but it will be
worth watching, given less negative sentiment in some of the peers recently.
 
4.    BOOT – Price Action: The stock is-3% in the post on very little volume. There have clearly been some
recent data concerns to start the out-quarter. It was one of our most asked about names last week as it
underperformed. The stock made fresh 1.5 year lows yesterday. The out-quarter guide was tough, as expected, but
not really that shocking (-250 bps below on revs), so the main question really will be what is already anticipated/priced
in and will other stocks take any cue from whatever the reaction is to the “expected guide below” from BOOT
tomorrow.
 
5.     MGM – October Comment: A very challenging quarter y/y in both Vegas and Macau, as expected. They
did drop one line in the release though that “October has rebounded favorably (referencing Vegas).” Not entirely
related but many airlines also spoke to better 4Q revenue trends, so it seems clear that travel demand is holding up.
