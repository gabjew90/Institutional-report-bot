PNC Weekly Market Watch
Market Review: Rocky rotation redux
Global equities mostly fell last week, and, on October 22, the S&P 500® posted its largest 
one-day decline in more than two months. Following Meta Platforms, Inc.’s recapitalization 
plan announcement, concerns about the sustainability of artificial intelligence (AI) 
financing weighed on markets. Information Technology and growth stocks were hit the 
hardest, both domestically and abroad. Notably, the Russell 3000® Value ended the week in 
positive territory and delivered its best weekly outperformance over the Russell 3000® 
Growth in more than two years as investors rotated away from mega-capitalization stocks.
Bonds also exhibited risk-off sentiment. The 10-year U.S. Treasury (UST) yield fell 17 basis 
points (bps) on October 22, its biggest one-day drop since August 2024, as investors 
questioned whether AI-supported economic growth could be sustained. The 10-year UST 
yield ended the week near 5.0%, below the early October peak of 5.3%. Meanwhile, the 
price of Brent crude oil rose to a five-month high of nearly $116 per barrel after attacks on 
infrastructure in Saudi Arabia.
Market Outlook
The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meets this week; markets expect no change in 
policy at this meeting, with a high probability of a rate hike in December. The Committee’s 
post-meeting messaging could add to volatility, along with higher bond yields, geopolitical 
uncertainty and the upcoming U.S. midterm elections. We maintain our lean toward 
diversified equities.
