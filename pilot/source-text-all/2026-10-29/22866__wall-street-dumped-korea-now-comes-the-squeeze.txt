Korea: From panic to FOMO
What a difference a few sessions make. Korean equities are surging as AI giants rebound from major
technical support, led by blowout SK Hynix numbers. But beneath the rally, the positioning story is even
more interesting. Hedge funds have been aggressively dumping Korean exposure, downside
protection was chased during the washout, and valuations reached extreme lows. Now investors who
spent the washout cutting exposure and chasing protection are being forced to chase the rebound.
The question is whether this is the start of something bigger or just another violent move inside a wide
trading range.
KOSPI
KOSPI bounced on major supports, surging overnight as AI giants are once again being chased. We are
still below the 200 day MA, that comes in 7-8% higher. This market probably needs to spend some time
trading inside the wide range.

SK Hynix (+11%): Revenue of 104tr won (+328% YoY) beat consensus of 98tr won, while operating

to exceed supply through 2030.
Another massive bounce
Samsung also surged overnight after bouncing off its long-term trendline. Unlike SK Hynix, the stock
has already reclaimed its 200-day moving average.

Selling
Hedge funds have been busy selling Korea and Taiwan. These two are the standouts when it comes to
net selling in Asia. That leaves the rebound running straight into a market where investors have
recently been cutting risk rather than adding it.

New in KOSPI vol
KOSPI's explosive spot-up, vol-up regime has faded. Volatility was aggressively bid during the early
October selloff, but hasn't followed the latest rally. Traders spent heavily chasing downside protection
during the washout and are now being forced to chase upside instead.

Korean equities are trading at record-low forward P/E multiples. Even stripping out the memory giants,
