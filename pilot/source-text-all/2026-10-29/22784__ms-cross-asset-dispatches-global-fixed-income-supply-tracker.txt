Appendix 
We use changes in Bloomberg fixed income indices' amount outstanding as a high-
frequency proxy for net new debt supply. 
We acknowledge this is not  a perfect gauge. Our"net supply" estimates capture only bonds 
meeting the eligibility criteria of  its corresponding indices (see Exhibit 107 and Exhibit 
108 ). Bloomberg fixed income indices include fixed-rate bonds above a minimum issue 
size with more than 12 months to maturity, or callable fixed-to-floating-rate bonds with 
more than 12 months to their call date. As a result, while changes in index amount 
outstanding on projected rolls capture day-to-day gross issuance of eligible bonds, they 
reflect debt approaching maturity one year ahead rather than actual maturities today. 
Changes can also arise from bonds entering or leaving an index, such as fallen angels 
moving from IG to HY. Despite these limitations, we believe the data remains useful for 
assessing relative supply and relative value, particularly for investors using fixed income 
indices as allocation benchmarks.
