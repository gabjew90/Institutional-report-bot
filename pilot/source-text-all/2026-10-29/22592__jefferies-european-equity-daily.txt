Rating Changes 
Iberian Banks Spanish banks have de-rated amid rising market volatility, but we remain positive given 
strong loan growth, resilient asset quality and higher-for-longer rates. We upgrade Bankinter to Buy and 
downgrade Santander to Hold. 
Imperial Brands A sharp, multi-year series of German excise hikes threatens one of Imperial Brands' core 
growth engines. Rising illicit trade and accelerating volume declines drive estimate cuts, increasing the risk of 
FY27 expectations reset. Downgrade to Hold. 
Best of the Rest 
Payments & FinTech Investor sentiment remains cautious, with agentic commerce raising terminal-value 
concerns and macro headwinds weighing on estimates. We continue to favour Adyen, Wise, Nayax and Boku 
where valuation already reflects much of this risk. 
Kingspan Investors still underestimate Advnsys's role in the equity story. We examine its converged data-
centre solution, arguing comparison with data centre peers reflects substantial exposure to a fast-growing end-
market rather than direct product overlap. 
IG Group We remain Buy-rated as IGG's valuation appears to over-discount the earnings impact of an adverse 
regulatory outcome for Underdog, as well as the execution risk around revenue retention optimisation. 
Lodging Investor meetings and site visits point to a cautious near-term outlook for Middle East Lodging. While 
long-term fundamentals remain attractive, geopolitical and macro uncertainty continue to cloud timing of 
RevPAR recovery and trajectory of NUG growth. 
Global Highlights 
US - Specialty Pharmaceuticals Animal health may be entering a new regime as competition erodes 
pricing power. We downgrade Zoetis and Elanco, but see diagnostics as structurally advantaged, with 
innovation supporting double-digit EPS growth at IDEXX. 

Japan - Quant Japan's reform agenda shifts from balance sheet optimisation towards profitability and 
capital allocation. We view incremental ROIC as the best guide to identifying tomorrow's winners, not those 
with the highest profitability today. 
China - Technology China data centre stocks sold off alongside US neoclouds, but the business models 
differ materially. Long-term hyperscaler contracts, customer-funded GPUs and low cost financing reduce risk. 
VNET remains our preferred pick.
