SoFi Technologies Inc. (SOFI): 3Q26 Key Takeaways: Shares outperform 
on stronger growth outlook and limited headwinds from higher rates
27 October 2026 | 8:19PM EDT
Bottom line: Shares outperformed, which we believe was a function of the 2027 
guidance which came in roughly 10% ahead of consensus, as well as the negative 
positioning heading into the print. We believe investors were focused on the 
downside risk to capital and earnings from higher interest rates, and against that, the 
company emphasized their interest rate hedging program and their ability to drive 
growth in a higher rate environment. Overall, we see the momentum in lending 
volumes as the most significant positive in the quarter, with originations coming in 
17% above consensus in the quarter. Continued momentum on personal loan 
originations could put an upward bias on earnings in our view, although we still see 
capital as the biggest question mark, with capital ratios falling ~200bps / quarter for 
the last 3 quarters. Despite the company saying they have no plans to raise capital, 
we note that the current quarter’s annualized RWA growth exceeds the balance 
sheet growth capacity cited by management on the call, which seems to signal either 
a slowdown in absolute levels of balance sheet growth or incremental capital needs 
over the next year.  Putting it altogether we believe the company removed some of 
the downside risk with much better hedging results and stronger growth in 
originations. That said, the company has accelerated growth through more capital 
intensive lending activities over the last year, and thus, we remain focused on the 
potential for growth to remix towards lower yielding loan sales and for origination 
growth to slow, which could be a revenue headwind over the next year. We see a 
more balanced risk / reward given the strong momentum in the business, offset by a 
more challenging macro environment, with respect to rates in particular.   
Consolidated results: SOFI reported 3Q26 adj revenue of $1,371mn, roughly 8% 
ahead of consensus, with Net Interest income coming in 9% ahead of consensus at 
$929.5mn with interest income of $1,343mn and interest expense coming in at 
$414mn. Non-interest income was $456mn in the quarter, 8% ahead of consensus. 
Adjusted EBITDA came in at $470mn, 6% ahead of street expectations. Net income 
came in at $241mn, resulting in EPS of $0.18, slightly ahead of consensus.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.
