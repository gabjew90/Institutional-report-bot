Vertiv Holdings (VRT): 3Q26 First Take
28 October 2026 | 7:08AM EDT
We view this as a strong report, and we’d expect the stock to trade higher. 3Q26 
results and 4Q26/2026 guidance are above both GS and the Street on revenue, 
adjusted EBIT, and adjusted EPS. Importantly, Vertiv spoke to strong demand (with 
the pipeline expanding and organic orders up >100% yoy) and momentum heading 
into 2027, and it continues to take steps to improve supply. Finally, organic growth is 
broad-based regionally.  
We expect commentary on demand, Vertiv’s competitive positioning in the market, 
the supply chain and measures that Vertiv has taken to alleviate the issues seen in 
2Q, and margins (including any cost pressures) to be key topics on the call. 
Results 
Vertiv (VRT; Buy) reported 3Q26 revenue of $4,313 mn (up 32% qoq as reported and 
up 55% yoy organically), compared to GS at $3,765 mn and the Street at $3,749 mn, 
and above the guidance range of $3,650 mn-$3,850 mn. 
On a yoy organic basis, revenue was up about 66% in the Americas, up about 48% in 
APAC, and up 19% in EMEA. This compares to last quarter with revenue up 21% in 
the Americas, up 26% in APAC, and down 2% in EMEA on a yoy organic basis. Total 
organic growth was 55% this quarter vs 18% last quarter. 
 Vertiv reported adjusted operating profit of $1,087 mn (25.2% of sales), compared 
to the Street at $922 mn (24.6% of sales), GS at $924 mn (24.6% of sales), and above 
the guidance range of $898 mn to $938 mn. 
Adjusted EPS (including SBC) of $2.21 compared to the Street at $1.82 and GS at 
$1.81, and above the guidance range of $1.77-$1.83. 
3Q adjusted FCF was $652 mn. 
Vertiv also recently closed the acquisitions of King Environmental Services and 
UtilityInnovation Group (UIG). The UIG transaction was for ~$1.45 bn in cash, which 
represents ~13x expected UIG 2027 EBITDA (per Vertiv), with additional 
considerations of up to $1.15 bn in cash based on certain financial targets over 12 
and 24-month periods. Vertiv expects the UIG acquisition to be accretive to adjusted 
EPS in the first year post-close.

Outlook  
Vertiv increased its 4Q26 revenue guidance to $4,313 mn-$4,513 mn (up 49% to up 
51% yoy organically) from $4,225 mn-$4,425 mn prior. This compares to GS at $4,330 
mn and the Street at $4,307 mn. 
In 4Q, Vertiv expects yoy organic sales growth in the mid-50% range in the Americas and 
APAC, and low-20% range in EMEA. 
Vertiv increased its 4Q26 adjusted operating profit guidance to $1,118 mn-$1,158 mn, 
which compares to GS at $1,132 mn and the Street at $1,119 mn. The adjusted 
operating margin guidance of 25.3-26.3% is unchanged compared to the prior outlook, 
and compares to GS at 26.2% and the Street at 26.0% at the midpoint. 
Vertiv increased its 4Q26 adjusted EPS guidance to $2.22 to $2.28 from $2.17-$2.23 
prior, which compares to GS at $2.25 and the Street at $2.21. 
Vertiv increased its 2026 revenue guidance, and now expects revenue between $14,450 
mn and $14,850 mn, up 37-39% yoy organically ($13,800 mn and $14,200 mn, up 
30-32% yoy organically prior). This compares to GS at $14,019 mn and the Street at 
$14,008 mn. On the supply chain and timing issues that impacted 2Q revenue, recall 
Vertiv commented on its 2Q call that it was taking steps to alleviate these challenges, 
including adding capacity and allowing more time for procurement/production and it 
expected the issue to be resolved in 2H26. The 3Q presentation says Vertiv is continuing 
to invest in footprint and capacity, and it is extending supply chain agreements.  
Vertiv increased its 2026 EBIT guidance, and now expects $3,475 mn-$3,555 mn (from 
$3,285 mn-$3,365 mn prior), with the new midpoint at $3,515 mn or 24.0% of revenue 
(from $3,325 mn or 23.8% of revenue prior). This compares to GS at $3,346 mn (23.9% 
of revenue) and the Street at $3,330 mn (23.8% of revenue). The updated 2026 
operating margin guidance is 23.5-24.5% (from 23.3-24.3% prior). 
The company increased its 2026 adjusted EPS guidance and now expects $7.10-$7.20 
(from $6.65-$6.75 prior). This compares to the Street at $6.73 and GS at $6.75. 
Vertiv increased its 2026 FCF guidance to $2,600 mn-$2,800 mn (from $2,400 
mn-$2,600 mn prior).

Price target and key risks

Key downside risks to our view include: 1) Revenue growth – Vertiv has a portion of its 
datacenter revenue still tied to traditional on-premise datacenters, which is a part of the 
market that could be impacted by either enterprise spending weakness or by the shift to 
the cloud. In addition, Vertiv’s ability to continue to improve its cloud/hyperscale 
revenue and grow in the channel will be important factors in how fast its revenue will 
increase. Also, market share will be a factor in revenue trends. 2) Margins – There are 
risks to our expectation for margin expansion. This could come from increased 
competitive pressure, mix, or execution, tariffs.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.
