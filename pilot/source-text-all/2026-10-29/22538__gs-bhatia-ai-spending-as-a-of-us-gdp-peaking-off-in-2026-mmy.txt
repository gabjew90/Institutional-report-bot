Risk to 2027 global growth estimates 
 
Our US economist is highlighting downside risk to global growth in 2027 as the peak of AI capex boom is
behind us and energy prices continue to remain high. 
 
Specifically on AI growth, Jan thinks that the growth impulse from the AI boom was set to peak in late 2026
or early 2027 even before concerns around the sustainability of the AI investment boom intensified in recent
weeks. Although AI investment is still rising rapidly, he expects the increase as a share of GDP to
slow over the next year. Alongside the fading boost from last year’s increase in US depreciation
allowances, this will weigh on capital spending growth, especially in the US. AI has also boosted US
consumer spending growth via a positive wealth effect worth an estimated 0.4pp (or 0.25% of GDP) over the
past year. This impact is likely to decline over the next year. 
 
 
 
 

INDIA results 
MAKEMYTRIP : While premium hotel companies continue to report strong numbers - India Hotels, Leela and now
ITC hotels - the worry was that maybe mass market travel/leisure spends could be slowing down (weak air travel
data). Makemytrip's results overnight again came out very strong. 20% YoY GBV growth despite weak air travel
growth shows that a) leisure spend budget continues to grow. The type of spend maybe changing / mode of travel
maybe changing - but the spending budget is still growing. and b) MMYT remains at the forefront of capturing the
spend. It has gained market share in Bus segment  and hotels segment (looking at the competitor result so far) and
it has maintained share in air travel. The management clarified on the plans for India listing and fungibility of India
share and the US listed share. 
 
Specifically on numbers  - constant-FX revenue +20% YoY (vs 16% est on the basis of which the stock tanked in the
first place) despite air volumes falling 6%, supported by continued strength in hotels, bus and market-share gains.
EBITDA missed our estimate by 3% as elevated marketing spend pressured margins, but we see meaningful margin
upside if air volumes recover and continue to expect strong medium-term travel demand. We raise revenue
estimates by up to 2% but lower FY27 EBITDA by 5%; TP trims to US$80 from US$81.
 
The stock price continues to languish . Report Link
