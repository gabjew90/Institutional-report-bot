Telecom: Starlink's 80K Towers - Real Deal, or A
Tightening Bear Hug?
October 25, 2026
Starlink's telecom bear hug appeared to tighten Friday on rumors of a contract for
80K macro towers. The rumor is easy to believe after Starlink's recent agreement
to purchase low-band spectrum. While Starlink lacks the spectral depth to serve
a large share of mobile customers, Starlink's new coverage layer could justify
a buildout focused on coverage, leaving capacity solutions for later. Starlink's
potential financial impact on the US retail mobile market is, at worst, years away. Is
it time to buy telecom stocks for a trade? Maybe, but upside seems limited. Within
Telecom & Cable, we rate CHTR Underperform, TMUS, VZ, CMCSA Peer Perform,
and T Outperform.
What's been reported: Thursday after-market, Bloomberg reported that SpaceX
aims to secure 80K+ US cell sites, singling out Crown Castle as the tower operator.
What are the consumer wireless implications? 80K towers is a lot for a low band
(coverage focused) spectrum strategy, suggesting more capacity per cell then our
prior estimate. We increase our estimate of Starlink's theoretical upper limit on
800MHz to ~24M US phones served. Spectrum auctions in '27/28 provide SpaceX
the opportunity to expand its mid-band (capacity focused) portfolio.
How does this pan out for an MVNO? Today's speculation about an MVNO
agreement between Starlink and Verizon following comments by David Faber on
CNBC. We found those comments theoretical and think it's too early for a Big 3 to fold
- Big 3 enterprise valuation multiples aren't low enough yet. Notably, in September
at conferences Verizon management brushed off the notion of "a prisoner's dilema"
with VZ shares not far from today's level.
Then what is this all about? SpaceX mobile business ambitions likely extend to, and
may originate from, an interest in serving a fleet of Teslas, Optimus robots, and
drones alongside efforts in retail wireless. If that's true, Musk need not be convinced
of his stand alone ROIC in retail wireless to see strategic benefits and a long-term
ROI in extending Starlink to terrestrial mobile services.
What does this mean for telco stocks? Telecom stock fundamentals are sturdy at the
moment, yet with industry structure in question, we expect technical bounces in the
stocks will be sold before they get very far. As we detailed when first sketching out the
Starlink risk in June followed by our analysis of SpaceX's 800MHz acquisition, exact
scale timing is semantics while the eventual structural threat from a 4th carrier is
the inherent risk that is / should be priced into the telecom sector.
We rate the Telecom sector Market Weight. At the stock level, we rate CHTR
Underperform, T Outperform, and TMUS, VZ and CMCSA Peer Perform.
