The Weekly Brief
Thought of the week
Elevated stock market concentration has raised the importance of
diversification. Major benchmarks are increasingly dominated by a small
number of stocks: the largest 10 companies in the S&P 500 are all technology-
like names and now represent about 40% of the index’s market capitalisation.
In Emerging Markets (EM), just three semiconductor-related names account for
roughly 30% of the MSCI benchmark. Overall, passive US and EM investors are
carrying at least close to 50% exposure to Artificial Intelligence-related
industries. Equity markets have of course remained resilient to uneasy
geopolitics, helped in part by sustained corporate and government spending.
That resilience, however, doesn’t remove the need to manage risk, particularly
around stock market concentration. Diversification across equities is therefore
important, while core fixed income provides valuable portfolio insurance.
Stock market concentration risk underscores the need for
