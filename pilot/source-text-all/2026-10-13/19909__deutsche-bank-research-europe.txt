FLATEXDEGIRO SE  (FTKn.DE) - Downgrade on slowing growth and governance uncertainty; Buy to Hold, 
Tgt EUR46 to EUR31. Last Close EUR26.06. 
Mengxian Sun: We downgrade FTK to Hold with a new TP of E31. Customer growth is slowing, while trading 
activity and cash balances are normalising. At the same time, increasing competition could drive customer 
acquisition costs higher. Recent changes to the Supervisory and Management Boards have added uncertainty 
around governance and strategic stability, weighing on investor sentiment. German pension reform could provide 
meaningful long-term upside through greater retail participation in capital markets, customer acquisition and 
cross[1]selling, although we expect limited near-term earnings contribution and higher customer acquisition 
costs. Higher interest rates remain supportive for NII, but declining customer cash balances partly offset this 
benefit. We expect net profit growth to slow to 9% YoY in 2027 from ~40% in the past two years.

FLIGHT DECK  - Robotic combat now a reality 
Christophe Menard: Evidence of a faster-than-expected transition from human-centric warfare towards robotic 
combat is emerging in Ukraine. Operation Vivaldi, conducted by Ukraine’s Third Army Corps around Lyman in 
northern Donetsk, provides perhaps the clearest battlefield example to date. Publicly disclosed on 15 September, 
the operation reportedly helped Ukraine regain 176km² by late September. Its most innovative element was the 
use of heavy-lift drones to carry explosive, camera-equipped UGVs more than 10km behind Russian lines. Once 
deployed, these systems could attack command posts, logistics hubs, drone operators and defensive positions 
without exposing Ukrainian personnel.
