MARKET RECAP
KOSPI broke below its 200DMA support to fall as much as -7.4% before finding support near its 6,000-
handle, as geopolitical concerns, SOX weakness, de-risking ahead of US CPI all weighed on the
sentiment. Foreigners were the main net sellers, with bulk of selling again concentrated in Tech (-$3.2bn
while KOSPI selling -$3.4bn), while local instos, retail investors, and Other Corporates (running thin with
Sammy buyback ending and Hynix buyback coming to an end) were net buyers, but their buying was
insufficient to offset heavy FII outflows. Most sectors extended losses, with KRXSEMI -3.8%,
KOSPELEC -7.6%, GSXAKDEF -1.2%, GSXAKVUP -6.6%, KRXHLTH -2.2%, GSXAKHOC -6.8%,
GSXAKPOW -2.8%, GSAKCOO -4.7%, KRXBANK -0.2% all closing in the red. Volumes were elevated
@ 56.4% vs. 20D average, but still around half of the YTD peak levels seen mid-year, suggesting lack of
downside support. KRW depreciated by 56bps to W1,343.2.
MAIN HEADLINES
| • | SEMIS (-): SK HYNIX(000660) -10.1%, SEC(005930) -7.4%, SEC PREFS(005935) -6.7%. Both
Hynix and Sammy found support around their 200DMAs, while foreigners continued to net sell in both
(-$1.8bn in Hynix and -$1.0bn in Sammy). Today's sell-off appeared largely flow-driven amid geopolitical
concerns and continued weakness in SOX, with lack of buying support amid corporate buyback flows
running thin (Sammy already wrapped Oct 6th, Hynix ongoing). Our pad remained sell skewed with
elevated activity levels. Selling continued to be concentrated in Hynix and Sammy, with supplies coming
from both sticky money and fast money communities. In the market, local instos were and retail investors
were dip buyers in both Hynix and Sammy, buying +$462mn/+$839mn in Hynix and +$185mn/+$877mn
in Sammy. |
| • | COSMETICS (-): Amid broad-based weakness across sectors, Cosmetics also tumbled led by a steep
fall in APR(278470) -19.6%. Nothing in the headlines today, but multiple local brokers lowered their TPs
overnight, with one mentioning that 3Q OP is expected to come below consensus and that persistent
logistics cost pressures should weigh on profitability more than expected. Meanwhile, GIR flagged that
they expect APR to deliver better-than-previously-guided revenue growth in 3Q26, but the earnings
outlook is less encouraging, with reported OPM guided at 21-22% (23-24% before the IFRS 18-related FX
impact), reflecting unfavorable currency movements and higher European logistics costs. |
