The Problem That NEAR Solves
Crypto has a usability problem.
There are now hundreds of blockchains, each with its own 
assets, applications, and rules. Moving money between 
them has historically been costly, cumbersome, and risky. 
For crypto to serve billions of people, this fragmented 
system has to feel like one seamless network.
Artificial intelligence has a different problem: centralization. 
A handful of companies control the world’s most powerful 
AI models, along with the data users and companies 
share with them. As AI systems begin to act on our 
behalf—managing money, booking travel, negotiating 
purchases—they will need infrastructure that lets them 
transact securely without broadcasting all of our data and 
our decisions to a few large corporations.
NEAR sits at the intersection of these two problems. 
NEAR was co-founded in 2018 by Alex Skidanov and Illia 
Polosukhin, one of the co-authors of the landmark research 
paper that introduced the architecture behind ChatGPT, 
Claude, and Gemini. With NEAR, Polosukhin’s team set 
out to build a blockchain fast enough, cheap enough, and 
simple enough to serve not just millions of people, but 
billions of AI agents transacting on their behalf. 
That work is now paying off. NEAR’s flagship product, 
NEAR Intents, simplifies trading assets across different 
blockchains into a single step. A user who wants to swap, 
say, $1,000 of stablecoins for Ethereum simply picks the 
assets and the amount, and a network of specialist bots 
(known as “solvers”) competes to execute it for the best 
available price. The user experience is seamless, and the 
product supports more than 35 blockchains. Executing 
that same transaction without NEAR Intents would typically 
involve multiple steps, bridges, and layers of fees. 
Introduced in late 2024 and scaled through 2025, NEAR 
Intents has processed more than $30 billion in cumulative 
volume, with $4 billion of that occurring in the past 30 
days. NEAR recently added perpetual futures (or “perps”) 
on Hyperliquid, giving Intents users access to the world’s 
largest onchain derivatives exchange. They have also 
partnered with Ondo Finance, a leader in tokenized 
equities, to enable users to buy, sell, and hold tokenized 
stocks and ETFs in a crypto wallet. 
NEAR has also built a confidential AI cloud. This lets 
people and businesses add privacy protections while 
running leading AI models, such as Claude, ChatGPT, 
Gemini, or DeepSeek, by removing sensitive information 
from requests before they reach the model provider. The 
highest privacy levels, built on leading open-weight models 
like DeepSeek, ensure no one—not the cloud operator, 
not NEAR, not the model provider—can see the user’s 
prompts, conversations, or information. Simply put, NEAR’s 
AI Cloud lets users get access to AI without handing over 
their sensitive or proprietary data. 
In the rest of this paper, we explain how these pieces fit 
together, why the NEAR token is designed to capture the 
value they create, and what it means for investors.

NEAR’s platform spans three layers that build on each 
other: a high-performance blockchain, a cross-chain 
execution layer, and confidential AI infrastructure.
NEAR’s High-Performance Blockchain:
NEAR Protocol
Every blockchain faces the same bottleneck: how to 
process more transactions without sacrificing security or 
decentralization. Ethereum answered with rollups (separate 
networks that batch transactions back to Ethereum), and 
Solana with one giant, ultrafast chain. NEAR took a third 
path, known as “sharding,” which splits the network into 
parallel lanes that process transactions simultaneously. 
With “dynamic resharding,” live since 2026, the network 
automatically adds lanes when traffic builds—like a 
highway that can automatically add lanes at rush hour.
The results are among some of the best in crypto. In 
2025, NEAR demonstrated it could handle one million 
transactions per second in a public test, a level far beyond 
what leading blockchains handle today. The network 
settles transactions in roughly a second, charges fees 
often less than a penny, and has run essentially without 
interruption since launching in 2020. The protocol is also 
futureproofing itself against future risks: It was one of the 
first major L1s to adopt quantum-safe signatures in July 
2026, raising confidence in its long-term positioning.
NEAR’s Cross-Chain Execution Layer:
NEAR Intents
The bulk of the economic activity across the NEAR 
network is tied to its flagship product, NEAR Intents, a 
smart-contract-based platform built on the NEAR protocol 
that lets users swap assets and trade perpetual futures 
across 35+ blockchains by enlisting “solvers” to find the 
best deal.
In February 2026, NEAR launched NEAR.com, a consumer 
app that allows users to access Intents through a 
single user account. NEAR also introduced Confidential 
Intents the same month, which shields transaction 
details—like the asset being traded, the amount, and 
the counterparty—from public view before settlement 
while keeping execution verifiable and auditable. In 
September 2026, NEAR Intents added perpetual futures 
on Hyperliquid, giving users access to the world’s largest 
onchain derivatives exchange with built-in confidentiality. 
Built for traders and institutions that need to trade without 
publicizing their strategies, NEAR Confidential Intents 
already accounts for 50.6% of the total value held in Intents.
Importantly, NEAR Intents demonstrated significant growth 
through the late stages of the recent crypto winter, and 
demand surged when the market rebounded in Q3. That 
suggests demand could grow further if crypto markets 
strengthen in 2027 and beyond.
NEAR’s Private AI Infrastructure:
NEAR AI Cloud
The third layer is where NEAR’s founding vision comes into 
focus. NEAR AI runs AI models and agents inside trusted 
execution environments (“TEEs”). TEEs are like encrypted 
vaults inside a computer chip that even the machine’s owner 
cannot see into. On that foundation, NEAR has launched 
NEAR AI Cloud, which gives companies private access to 
AI models, from leading open-weight models like DeepSeek 
to frontier models like GPT, Claude, and Gemini.1 NEAR also 
built an embedded agent experience on NEAR.com that 
lets AI agents operate 24/7 without exposing credentials 
or confidential user data to them (like names, passwords, 
and API keys), and a confidential GPU marketplace where 
enterprises and governments can rent computing power 
for sensitive AI workloads. Since July 2026, customers 
have been able to pay for NEAR AI Cloud and for hosting 
embedded agents by staking NEAR tokens, with credits 
scaling with the amount of NEAR staked.
Put together, the three layers form a valuable technology 
stack. NEAR Protocol is the foundation, providing a 
settlement engine fast and cheap enough for AI-scale 
commerce. NEAR Intents builds on that foundation, 
enabling people and institutions to transact across 
dozens of blockchains. Confidential Intents adds privacy 
to those features, leveraging the same trusted hardware 
NEAR uses to run AI models confidentially. NEAR AI gives 
people and AI agents confidential access to leading 
AI models, and gives agents a way to transact through 
Intents, allowing agents to pay for things across dozens of 
blockchains.
Each layer is built to feed the one beneath it. More AI 
agents mean more Intents transactions, and more Intents 
transactions mean more settlement on NEAR Protocol. It is 
infrastructure for a world where humans, institutions, and AI 
agents do business onchain.

NEAR’s native token, NEAR, is the economic engine of the 
ecosystem. The NEAR token is designed to benefit from 
the network’s growth in three ways.
Transaction Fees and Token Burns
Every transaction processed on the NEAR blockchain 
requires a fee that is paid in NEAR. For simple transactions, 
such as sending NEAR from one wallet to another, the 
entire fee is subsequently burned—that is, an equivalent 
number of NEAR tokens are permanently removed from 
circulation. Transactions that involve applications, like 
lending, borrowing, or trading on a DeFi app or using NEAR 
Intents, pay out 30% of the fee to the application and burn 
the remaining 70%. NEAR recently approved an upgrade 
that eliminates the 30% payment; once it goes live, NEAR 
will burn 100% of transaction fees, strengthening the 
token’s value accrual and simplifying protocol economics.
Staking
Roughly 45% of all NEAR is staked. This refers to NEAR 
that is pledged by holders to the validators that process 
NEAR transactions and help secure the network. In 
exchange for staking NEAR, holders receive rewards that 
currently yield 4.5% per year. Staking keeps nearly half of 
the supply committed to the network, reducing the amount 
in circulation. In late 2025, the NEAR community improved 
the supply-side dynamics of the NEAR token by cutting 
the protocol’s inflation rate in half, from 5% to 2.5% per year, 
and proposals could push it lower still. While this change 
reduces the yield earned by stakers, the net impact is 
lower NEAR token inflation and more sound tokenomics.
Revenue-Driven Buybacks
In addition to transaction fees on the NEAR blockchain, 
NEAR also generates revenue from cross-chain swaps 
facilitated by NEAR Intents. Most importantly for NEAR’s 
long-term investment case, a “fee switch” was activated in 
February 2026 that began using NEAR’s share of those 
fees (after payouts to its partners and the applications 
leveraging Intents) to buy back NEAR tokens on the open 
market. Since the fee switch was turned on, NEAR Intents 
users have paid over $29 million in fees, leading to $5.3 
million in NEAR token buybacks.2
Collectively, the value-accrual story is simple. As more 
activity routes through NEAR’s infrastructure, its revenue 
grows, and buybacks grow with it. Additionally, increasing 
activity means more fees are burned, reducing supply 
further. And staking keeps additional supply locked up 
and committed to the network. If NEAR’s infrastructure 
becomes the default home of the AI agent economy, 
NEAR’s fees and demand for NEAR tokens should scale 
with that economy.

As crypto goes mainstream, more and more activity 
involves moving assets across blockchains, and, 
increasingly, AI agents acting on people’s behalf. NEAR’s 
services sit underneath several of the market's most 
important growth stories.
—	
Stablecoins, bitcoin, altcoins, and tokenized 
real-world assets (like stocks and ETFs) live on 
different networks, and the demand for moving and 
reconciling them is likely to increase. Intents already 
routes billions of dollars between chains every month.
—	
Institutions need confidentiality. Confidential Intents 
brings private execution to public blockchains.
—	
For an agent to manage money or data on your 
behalf, it needs verifiable, confidential, and tamper-
resistant access to AI infrastructure. That is precisely 
what NEAR’s hardware-secured AI stack provides.
It’s worth being direct about where NEAR stands today. 
While Intents has achieved measurable traction in cross-
chain trading, the AI business is still in its early days. NEAR 
AI has real products and credible partners, but reported 
revenues have been modest so far.
That makes NEAR a two-part story: a working, fast-
growing execution layer today, and a moonshot built on 
top of it. If AI agents become the dominant users of digital 
infrastructure—as many technologists expect—and 
NEAR becomes the settlement layer where they transact, 
the value unlock would be enormous. The moonshot is 
not required for NEAR to matter. But if it hits, it changes 
everything.

As with any early technology, the future holds risks for 
NEAR. The biggest is execution. NEAR’s AI ambition is a 
swing-for-the-fences bet on a market that is still taking 
shape. Centralized AI platforms could keep users inside 
their own walled gardens, agent commerce could develop 
more slowly than expected, or it could settle on rails other 
than NEAR’s. Investors should size positions with that 
binary quality in mind: The AI upside is enormous, but it is 
not guaranteed.
Competition is real on every front. In one sense, NEAR 
competes with Ethereum, Solana, and other Layer 1 
blockchains for developers and applications (although it 
also supports those ecosystems by improving cross-chain 
functionality). And it certainly competes with a crowded 
field of interoperability providers for cross-chain activity, as 
well as with well-funded centralized AI companies for the 
future of the agent economy.
NEAR’s usage metrics can also be volatile. In early 2026, 
activity on the NEAR base chain fell sharply when activity 
on a single large consumer app dropped off. Today’s 
growth engine, NEAR Intents, is dominated by stablecoin 
and trading flows, and its fee and buyback mechanisms 
have been live for less than a year.
Lastly, NEAR’s tokenomics remain a work in progress. The 
inflation reduction, fee switch, and buyback programs are 
recent, and future governance decisions could refine or 
reshape how value accrues to the NEAR token. Regulation 
also remains a variable. While the U.S. regulatory 
environment has turned constructive, rules governing core 
elements that NEAR’s success depends on, from DeFi to 
privacy tools to AI, are still being written.

that allows investors to gain cost-efficient exposure to

agents grows, NEAR is positioned to benefit.
