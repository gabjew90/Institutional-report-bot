Sector Roundup
Cost Pressures in Food and Beverages
PepsiCo: “Input costs are trending higher and mix has been a headwind in particular … So, it's the margin piece 
that's driving the guide down in EPS. But sales, as you mentioned, we expect to be robust.”
Travel Remains a Top Spending Priority for Consumers
Delta: “Looking at the current environment, demand remains strong, supported by a secular shift in consumer 
behavior. The top 40% of US households, which make up the majority of Delta's customer base are nearly $40 
trillion wealthier than they were just a few years ago and increasingly prioritizing experiences, including travel.”
Memory Market Expected to Remain Supply Constrained Through 2028
Micron: “As strong as fiscal 2026 was, we expect fiscal 2027 to be even better. Industry demand has strengthened 
since our last earnings call, and we expect memory and storage supply-demand conditions to be much tighter in 
fiscal 2027 and 2028 than they were in 2026.”
