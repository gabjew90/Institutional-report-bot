US Auto sales in August were above expectations, running at a SAAR of ~16.9m, 
more than our coming in 16.4m estimate and the prior year, also at ~16.4m units. 
Absolute sales were ~1.388m units, above July's ~1.380m units but lower than 
August 2025's ~1.482m. While the D3 and J3 both came in incrementally higher 
than estimates, Hyundai group was a significant driver of the beat (+14k vs. our 
estimates) with Other brands making up the remainder. Within the important large 
pickup truck segment, most major truck models saw daily sales decline while Ram 
sales were particularly strong, increasing 105 sales per day (to ~1,550) as a result 
of strong incentive spend. Overall inventories fell incrementally in the month to 49 
days, down from 50 in June but up from 47 in 2025. By segment, truck inventory fell 
1 day MoM to 52 while cars fell 2 days to 34. As part of our ongoing build-out, our 
production file now includes days of inventory by vehicle segment. We maintain our 
2026 SAAR at 16.0m units, which is relatively in line with most OEM estimates. We 
also maintain our full year 2027 outlook at 16.1m SAAR.
As part of our ongoing build-out of databases, we have revamped our sales file 
which includes expectations on SAAR, production, sales and inventory. We also 
maintain a pricing file. To be added to either distribution list, please let us know.
