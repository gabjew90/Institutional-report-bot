portfolio would add 13 producing fields (5 operated) with c.115 kboe/d of net production in 9M26, a
c.50% gas share, 414 mmboe of 2P reserves and 101 mmboe of 2C, operatorship of the Greater Ekofisk
Area and ownership of the Norpipe and Teesside terminals in the UK. Pro-forma 2026 production would
reach c.550 kboe/d including BlueNord, and the long-term target would rise to >500 kboe/d into the mid-
2030s. The proposed deal is to be funded from US$7.5bn of available liquidity, with a potential equity
raise of up to 10% to be assessed. we estimate that the acquired assets would contribute US$1.0-1.3bn of
CFFO and US$0.2-0.6bn of FCF in 2028E, the first full year of ownership. Against Vår standalone, our
base case, the pro-forma group (PF: Vår plus COP/BlueNord assets) would be +16% to +25% accretive to
2028E CFFO/share (+7% to +16% with a 10% equity raise) and +15% to +66% accretive to FCF/share
(+7% to +61%). PF net debt is c.US$4.1-7.2bn at end-2027E (c.US$2.7-5.8bn with a 10% equity raise).

RWE - here - GIR preview the March ’27 CMD, where we expect RWE to extend guidance to 2032.
We anticipate
• Near doubling of earnings over 2027-32 (+13% CAGR), supported by c.€45 bn consolidated capex (c.
€80 bn including equity accounted activities)
• Material portfolio shift, with c.35% of profits from power grids by then, c.50% from RES and c.15%
from Flexible Generation
• Rising target leverage, thanks to a larger share of regulated profits
• P/E of 9x by 2032 (RWE is already at a discount in 2028, and outgrows the sector)
