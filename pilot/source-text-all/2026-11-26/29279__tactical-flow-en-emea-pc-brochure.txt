Developed market equity ETPs attracted $10.8B last week, down from $17.6B previously. Inflows moderated across 
North America, Global and Europe, while APAC reversed into outflows.
Global DM strategies led allocations at $4.8B, compared with $6.7B the previous week, closely followed by North 
America at $4.6B, down from $9.0B. European inflows eased to $1.7B from $1.9B, while APAC recorded -$217m after 
broadly flat flows. Within regional exposures, Eurozone inflows increased to $534m from $425m, while broader 
European allocations moderated to $904m from $1.4B. APAC ex-Japan outflows increased to -$183m from -$34m.
At the single-country level, the US remained the main source of buying at $4.6B, compared with $9.0B previously. 
Switzerland gathered $162m, while Sweden returned to inflows of $60m and Canada attracted $7m after -$34m of 
outflows. UK buying slowed to $16m from $59m. Japan recorded -$58m, while German outflows eased to -$53m from 
-$63m. Within US size exposures, Large Cap inflows moderated to $3.8B from $6.5B, Mid Caps returned to inflows of 
$60m, and Small Caps reversed into outflows of -$116m.

Emerging market equity ETPs attracted $908m last week, down from $1.5B previously, as demand for broad EM and 
single-country strategies moderated despite a recovery in regional exposures.
Broad EM inflows declined to $327m from $890m, while single-country strategies attracted $497m, compared with 
$669m the previous week. Regional EM exposures returned to inflows of $84m following -$18m of outflows. The 
improvement was led by EM Asia, which attracted $99m after recording -$15m previously. EM Europe gathered 
$12m, down from $19m, while Latin American outflows increased marginally to -$27m from -$25m.
India remained the largest single-country contributor at $228m, although inflows moderated from $481m. South 
Korea attracted $86m, down from $155m, while China gathered $76m, compared with $88m previously. Mexico 
returned to positive territory with $19m following -$1m of outflows, while Brazil attracted $8m, down from $40m. The 
supplementary country-level data also shows Taiwan attracting $50m, up from $4m the previous week.

Active equity ETPs attracted $672m last week, down from $990m previously, as Global, European and EM inflows 
moderated. North America, however, returned to positive territory.
Global strategies remained the largest contributor at $358m, down from $844m the previous week. North America 
reversed into inflows of $175m following -$103m of outflows, while Europe gathered $50m, down from $128m. EM 
inflows moderated to $60m from $165m, and APAC attracted $35m after broadly flat flows previously.
By investment approach, Alpha-Seeking strategies attracted $519m, down from $886m, while Outcome inflows 
increased to $151m from $135m. Exposure flows were broadly flat following -$34m of outflows. Within Alpha-
Seeking, CORE remained the largest contributor at $552m, although demand eased from $766m. Precision reversed 
into outflows of -$73m after attracting $22m, while Factors gathered $36m and Thematics/Megatrends added $4m. 
Within Outcome, Dividend strategies attracted $93m, down from $139m, while US Buffer returned to inflows of $40m 
following -$11m of outflows.

Sustainable attracted $125m. In contrast, Other Uplift experienced the largest reversal, recording -$315m after 
attracting $750m previously. Other Thematic outflows increased to -$67m from -$20m, while Environment inflows 
slowed to $14m from $120m.
