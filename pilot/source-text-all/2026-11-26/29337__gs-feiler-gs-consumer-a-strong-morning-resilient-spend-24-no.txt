GS Consumer: A Strong Morning. Resilient Spend.
Scott Feiler  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  Managing Director, Global Banking & Markets, FICC & Equities
Tue 24 Nov 2026, 8:12am ET
The key theme this morning is that Retail results were solid. On the back of this, we are seeing some big
moves higher in the pre-mkt, with DKS +18%, ANF +12%, KSS +9%, DLTR+8%, KSS +7%. BBY +6%
and BURL +6%. With all the 4Q guides fine this morning and with Black Friday ahead, expect the view to
be that spend will be solid around the event.
There was a heavy dose of relevant reporters and all stocks this morning are trading up nicely in the pre-
market. Stocks that had a very high bar delivered (BBY best comp in 5 years), those where there were
expected to be generally solid were exactly that (KSS, DLTR, ANF) and those where there were some
concerns came in no worse (BURL) or nicely above expectations (DKS).
As we know, market narratives can shift quickly, even day of. The 1st take from this morning’s heavy
consumer EPS though is solid results. Top-line guides for 4Q are all generally in-line, despite some recent
fears from investors of a slow start to 4Q.
Quick Single-Stock Takes:
BURL (+): Stock is +6% in the pre-mkt, despite the comp miss, as expectations were very low. The +1%
comp missed but was no worse than the 0 to +1% bar and the 4Q comp guide is in-line, which is also
somewhat encouraging. Given the stock is at the recent lows on 3Q concerns (big weather impact hurt),
this feels like it could provide some relief, with the event passed.
BBY (+): Shares +6%. Expectations were very high and they delivered. The +6.1% comp was the best in 5
years. Noted broad-based strength across nearly all categories and service offerings. 4Q EPS guided above
at the mid-point. The question will mainly just be if this was better than a very high bar and if investors
will fully embrace the story (have not yet, despite the clearly solid 2026 they are having).
DKS (+): Shares are +18% on a solid beat vs Consensus and due to somewhat negative positioning. There
is a long way to go for investors to fully embrace the story again but the beat and raise (even though the
raise is not as big as the 3Q beat) is clearly a step in the right direction. Importantly, Foot Locker comps
got back to positive and Foot Locker US comps were +4%.
DLTR (+): Shares are +8%. They were supposed to have a “fine” quarter and we would call this generally
solid. They beat comps by slightly more than expected and it was largely traffic driven. They guided 4Q
EPS and top-line to both be in-line and qualitative comments from mgmt. in the release generally read
constructive. There will remain a freight/margin debate for 2027 but this seems good enough.
KSS (+): Shares are +7%. They beat comps by ~200 bps, which we think was somewhat expected. The
midpoint of the 4Q top-line guide is above consensus and implies a slightly positive comp, same as 3Q.
Will investors be excited about the story? That’s tougher to say, but trends have clearly stabilized.

ANF (+): Shares are +12%. A small beat was expected and we got a big beat. Nearly equal comps at both
Abercrombie banner (+6%( and Hollister (+5%). The issue previously is only one banner has been
delivering at any given time the last few years, but currently, both are.
Source: Goldman Sachs, Scott Feiler, 24 November 2026.
