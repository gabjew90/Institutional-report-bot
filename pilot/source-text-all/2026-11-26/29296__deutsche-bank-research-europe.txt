Gael de-Bray: ABB has announced the acquisition of Ziehl-Abegg for an EV of $2.65bn. The transaction 
multiple looks high at 18.6x 2026e EV/EBITDA, above ABB's own multiple of 16.8x, but management has 
set an ambitious margin improvement target. Ziehl-Abegg is expected to generate $1.4bn of revenues in 
2026, with a mid-single-digit operational EBITA margin that ABB aims to increase to the high-teens within

Closing of the transaction is expected in the second half of 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and 
other customary closing conditions. 
  
CREST NICHOLSON  (CRST.L) - Larger losses and continued refinancing uncertainty; Tgt 65p to 
45p. Last Close 44p. Hold. 

Chris Millington: Crest's update points to a further deterioration in trading through Q4. This is expected 
to drive a larger underlying EBIT loss than previously guided - and also trigger £10-15m of NRV provisions. 
Consequently, we move FY26 PBT from -£24m to -£53m (inclusive of NRV provisions). We expect losses 
of this scale to persist into FY27/28, given continued price/cost headwinds and Crest's prioritisation of

our target price down to 45p (0.2x FY27 P/NTA), a reduction from our previous 0.25x multiple to reflect the 
larger losses/refinancing risks.

BROKERS, ASSET MANAGERS & EXCHANGES  - State of the Private Markets & The Evolving 
Tokenization of US Equity Capital Markets 
Brian Bedell: After a roughly two-decade period of modest market structure evolution post the onset of 
Reg NMS in 2005, US equity & related capital markets are undergoing their first major structural change 
since the 1995-2005 electronification era. This includes not only the oncoming evolution of tokenization 
and blockchain-based trading & processing, but also the advent of decentralized trading platforms as 
well as new trading vehicles (e.g. perpetual futures & event contracts), along with a migration toward 
24x7x365 always-on markets and potential changes to Reg NMS. In this report, we focus mostly on 
developments around equity tokenization and potential impacts to US equity market structure and the 
interplay of innovative private & newly public companies with the traditional exchanges.
