Burlington Stores Inc. (BURL): 3Q26 First Take: Solid margins offset 
weather-driven 3Q comps; freight weighs on F4Q outlook
24 November 2026 | 6:04AM MST
BURL reported adjusted 3Q26 EPS of $1.70 ($1.73 after adjusting for expenses 
associated with bankruptcy acquired leases) compared to GS/FactSet at 
$1.68/$1.73. Comp growth was reported at +1.0%, below GS/FactSet consensus at 
+3.0%/+2.3%. Management indicated the business delivered good sales momentum 
through late September, but noted unseasonably warm weather in October weighed 
on traffic and comp delivery. Adjusted EBIT margin decreased 30bps Y/Y to 5.5% of 
sales (-60bps Y/Y to 5.6% after accounting for expenses associated with bankruptcy 
acquired leases), above GS/FactSet consensus at 5.4%/5.3% 
On the outlook, BURL lowered its FY26 guidance. The company now expects 
adjusted EPS between $11.70-$11.80 vs. $11.77-$11.97 prior, which compares with 
GS/FactSet consensus at $11.76/$11.87. Within this, BURL expects comp growth in 
the range of 2-3% vs. 3-4% prior, compared with GS/FactSet consensus at 
3.4%/3.0%. FY26 adjusted EBIT margin is now expected to increase 20-30bps Y/Y. 
The company also issued guidance for 4Q26, expecting adjusted EPS of $4.85-$4.95, 
compared with GS/FactSet consensus at $5.11/$5.18, on comp growth of 1-3% vs. 
GS/FactSet consensus at 3.0%/2.1%. The 4Q EPS outlook includes $0.20/share of 
incremental fuel and freight expense, while adjusted EBIT margin is expected to 
decline 80-100bps Y/Y. The 4Q outlook also includes the remaining $35mn of tariff 
refunds that BURL expects to reinvest during the quarter. 
At first glance and against a backdrop of what we perceive to be low investor 
expectations, we view this update as mixed. We are encouraged by management’s 
strong margin delivery, which enabled F3Q EPS above the high end of the company’s 
guided $1.60-$1.70 margin range despite comps coming in at the low end of the 
range. Additionally, we view management’s F4Q comp guidance of 1-3% as 
encouraging given the tougher Y/Y comparison in F4Q, though we believe 
commentary on today’s conference call will be critical as investors look to better 
understand QTD momentum vs. embedded assumptions for the remainder of the 
quarter. However, we acknowledge that BURL’s quarterly comp delivery at 1% is the 
lowest of comp rate of the off-price peer group and comes despite several initiatives 
(home, localization, etc). Incremental freight and fuel pressure are also driving a 
reduction to management’s FY guide, representing an incremental $0.20 of 
headwind to F4Q and driving the entirety of the guidance reduction.

strategic initiatives to drive stronger traffic and comp momentum, including the

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.
