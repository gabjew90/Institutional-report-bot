Research in focus this morning
 
China Consumer. Pulse Check: 3Q26 wrap-up: Muted demand with deleverage/cost weigh on margins; HH CF
pressured by still elevated saving rate
We review 3Q26 consumption trends, noting that muted demand, cost inflation, and operating deleverage drove
broad-based earnings misses. Company-level performance diverged, with execution, product cycles, and share
gains differentiating winners amid weak macro conditions. Cost inflation became a visible P&L headwind, making
2027 cost resets and selective pass-through abilities key debates. Growth deceleration is an increasing concern for
structural winners, while investors shift focus toward bottoming-out opportunities. Accordingly, we update our sector
preferences, continuing to prefer restaurants, F&B value retailers, dairy, condiments, snacks, and leading white
goods. We turn neutral on projectors and apparel and footwear OEMs, given rising overseas contributions and an
easier 2H26 base. Furniture, discretionary small kitchen appliances, non-super-premium spirits, sports retailers, and
pet food remain our least preferred sectors. Preferred Buy-rated names include Yum China, Guming, Moutai, Midea,
Anta, and Chow Tai Fook.
 
China Banks. Assessing the potential impact of reported taxation of government-bond coupon income
Recent Bloomberg news suggests that Chinese authorities are assessing the potential impact of imposing a 25%
income tax on interest income from central and local government bonds. Importantly, the report does not make
comment on whether or when the current tax treatment will change, or whether any potential tax would apply to
existing holdings or only to newly issued or purchased government bonds. Against this backdrop, we assess the
potential impact on China banks under different tax-scope assumptions. The direct earnings impact would depend
primarily on whether existing government-bond holdings are included, while any associated repricing in government-
bond yields could also affect OCI valuations. We therefore focus on: 1) the potential earnings impact under different
tax-scope assumptions; and 2) the potential valuation impact on banks' bond portfolios if government-bond yields
reprice higher.
 
China CRO/ CDMO. 
China CRO & CDMO: 3Q26 Wrap-up: Robust orders reinforce 2027 visibility despite recent market volatility
Sector fundamentals remain on an upward trajectory despite recent market volatility and broader de-risking. Robust
3Q order momentum and accelerated capacity expansion provide higher visibility into 2027. 3Q26 earnings were
broadly solid despite continued FX headwinds, while new-order momentum remained strong, with WuXi AppTec
accelerating to roughly 60% YoY in 9M26. WuXi AppTec notably raised its FY26 continuing-operations revenue
growth guidance to 40%-42% from 35%-39%. The recovery is broadening beyond late-stage manufacturing into
early-stage activity, with AI-based drug discovery emerging as a tangible incremental demand driver. Near-term
geopolitical implementation risks have moderated following WuXi AppTec’s removal from the Pentagon's Section
1260H list. As investor attention shifts toward 2027 execution and commercial-stage conversion, we favor companies
with stronger order visibility and exposure to faster-growing modalities. We prefer WuXi AppTec, Pharmaron and
GenScript as differentiated ways to position for the next phase of sector growth.
 
China Healthcare Corp Day wrap. Ziyi Chen shares November Corp Day Takeaway: CDMO still preferred into
2027; Opportunities Broaden
Following our semi-annual Corporate Day with over 70 management teams, we see the investor debate shifting from
re-entry to positioning for 2027. CDMO remains our preferred subsector as robust 3Q order momentum and capacity

expansion provide higher manufacturing visibility. Demonstrating this strength, WuXi AppTec raised its FY26
continuing-operations revenue growth guidance to 40-42% from 35-39%. For biotech, the go-global theme
increasingly depends on the clinical and regulatory validation of China-originated assets into 2027. In pharma,
product cycles are driving a divergent recovery, with new launches offsetting legacy weakness. We are incrementally
more positive on WuXi AppTec, Pharmaron and Mindray, driven by gaining commercial order validation and
concluding channel destocking. Conversely, we are incrementally more cautious on Topchoice Medical, CMS and CR
Pharm amid volume and pricing pressures
