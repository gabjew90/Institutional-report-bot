 
Gold's Safe-Haven Status Tested By Volatility Shifts 
 
BY TYLER DURDEN 
THURSDAY, NOV 12, 2026 - 01:40 PM 
Authored by Frank Monkam, Bloomberg macro strategist, 
Cross-asset volatility dynamics suggest gold may be trading less as a haven asset and more as 
an expression of the cyclical rates narrative. 
Gold is struggling to find renewed upside momentum, sandwiched between an equity market 
still licking its wounds from last week’s flushout, and a rates market where yields reversed 
sharply higher after a hot CPI print on Tuesday. 
Volatility has now come off the recent lows, seen when markets were wrestling for direction 
around the $4k psychological level on spot gold. 
 
The correlation between gold volatility and the VIX Index has fallen to virtually zero, from 
historically positive levels.  
 

 
 
This suggests gold vol is becoming increasingly detached from broader risk-off dynamics, 
challenging the conventional framework through which investors assess its defensive 
characteristics. 
On the flip side, the 60-day rolling correlation between gold vol and the MOVE Index has 
collapsed to approximately -0.65, near its most negative reading in two years.  
This also comes despite a similar convergence in volatility levels, highlighting a growing 
disconnect in how both markets are pricing macro uncertainty. 
 

 
 
This dichotomy reflects the precious metal’s growing sensitivity to monetary policy expectations, 
real yields and inflation uncertainty, rather than traditional flight-to-quality demand.  
If gold is increasingly trading the cyclical rates story, its ability to provide reliable diversification 
during episodes of broader market stress could become less predictable.
