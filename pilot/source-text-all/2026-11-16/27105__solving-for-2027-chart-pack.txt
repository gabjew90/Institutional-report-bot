THEME 1: 
The Golden Age of 
Productivity
What it shows: 
Industries with high AI exposure, including 
information, finance, professional and business 
services, are seeing the highest productivity 
gains from AI.
Why it matters: 
Productivity drives company profitability and overall 
economic growth. When companies or economies 
become more productive, they can generate higher 
earnings and dividends. 
How you can apply it: 
As AI proliferates across industries, we expect to see 
broader productivity gains, but not all companies 
will benefit uniformly. The wide gaps among the 
industries and firms within them argue for stock 
selection over broad exposure.

THEME 2: 
Higher Capital 
Costs
What it shows: 
Rates are rising across several major economies as 
central banks tighten, with market pricing implying the 
U.S. Federal Reserve, Bank of Japan and European 
Central Bank will hike several more times through 
July 2027. 
Why it matters: 
Rising rates imply increased borrowing costs for 
businesses and consumers heading into 2027, but 
they also present opportunities for higher yields for 
fixed income investors. 
How you can apply it:
We think the current cycle of rate hikes will be shorter 
than market pricing implies, especially if core inflation 
remains relatively contained. Investors may find 
opportunities in short to intermediate maturity bonds, 
with opportunistic additions to duration during what 
we expect to be a synchronized but relatively short 
tightening cycle.

THEME 3: 
Earnings 
Durability
What it shows: 
Analysts are broadly optimistic on the trajectory of 
corporate earnings. As of September 30, forward 
earnings estimates were significantly above the 10-
year trend line, and analysts expect earnings per share 
for the MSCI ACWI Equal Weighted Index to rise more 
than 18% year-over-year, according to FactSet.
Why it matters: 
Earnings are the cornerstone of global equity markets. 
While the AI buildout and rising commodity prices 
have been the biggest earnings drivers, the long-
awaited global manufacturing rebound may broaden 
that growth; however, rising discount rates could curb 
the present value of those earnings.
How you can apply it: 
History suggests that equity markets can absorb 
higher nominal rates to a certain extent, especially 
when growth remains strong. In the meantime, we 
favor companies with defensible moats and sturdy 
balance sheets spanning technology, cyclicals and 
health care.

THEME 4: 
Resilient 
Infrastructure 
What it shows: 
Global data center electricity use is expected to 
roughly double by 2030, with the U.S. and China 
accounting for nearly 80% of that growth.
Why it matters: 
Infrastructure resilience has moved from a defensive 
concern to a sustained capital-formation priority. The 
need to expand and modernize physical and digital 
capacity—from power generation and grids to chip 
fabrication, data centers and networks—creates a 
multiyear investment opportunity. 
How you can apply it: 
We think infrastructure resilience across cyber, the 
power grid and water is an opportunity for investors. 
Stay selective and favor providers of scarce capacity 
over broad regional exposure. Stay cognizant of the 
political risk associated with data center exposure.

THEME 5: 
Faster Disruption, 
Faster Markets 
What it shows: 
Since 2022, the stock-bond correlation has been 
mostly positive, albeit unstable and regime-
dependent. 
Why it matters: 
Understanding the correlation between assets is the 
foundation of diversification in investment portfolios.
How you can apply it: 
Allocations to inflation-sensitive assets (such as 
commodities and private real estate) and uncorrelated 
diversifiers (such as hedge funds, private equity and 
private credit) can add value in portfolios if the 
stock-bond correlation remains positive.
