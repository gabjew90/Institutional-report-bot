management and highlighted a particular focus on capital allocation 
alongside the company’s commercial strategy. EXE also provided 
more detail on the Twin Eagle integration and potential commercial

The Challenges. EXE did not disclose any incremental commercial 
or marketing agreements alongside 3Q26 earnings and talked down 
data center opportunities in Appalachia on the call, while 
management did note the Twin Eagle acquisition enabled 
opportunities nationwide on top of the company’s already strong

management’s emphasis on share repurchases in periods of stock

Key Takeaways 
 
 
Strategic Updates. Ahead of 3Q26 earnings, EXE announced key management changes 
including the hiring of a new CEO, and management commentary around earnings 
suggests the focus for new management is on executing the current commercial and 
marketing strategy of targeting $750 mn in incremental EBITDA over time. We highlight 
the Twin Eagle acquisition as additive to this strategy, as the business is expected to 
contribute ~$200 mn in annual EBITDA with upside of an incremental $150 mn by 
YE2028 through synergy capture related to the transaction. One key facet of the plan is 
EXE’s ability to facilitate and capture new demand, and EXE noted its new demand 
growth - which EXE targets to contribute an incremental $250 mn in annual EBITDA 
longer-term - is expected to be primarily driven by LNG opportunities. Management 
noted on the call that Twin Eagle has strong existing industrial relationships that the 
business can leverage in this push for EBITDA enhancement. We continue to seek clarity 
on EXE’s pro forma competitive positioning around incremental commercial and 
marketing supply agreements in the Appalachian and US Gulf Coast regions, as well as 
other broader opportunities that emerge given the national Twin Eagle platform. 
Capital Allocation. Amid a soft natural gas pricing environment, EXE has benefited from 
its “hedge-the-wedge” risk management strategy which has provided a higher floor as 
the company realized $311 mn in hedge gains for 3Q26. Downside protection translates 
to greater capacity for shareholder returns, as EXE repurchased $305 mn in shares 
during 3Q26 alongside closing the Twin Eagle acquisition. Year-to-date through 3Q26, 
the company has reduced gross debt by ~$1.3 bn, repurchased $900 mn in shares, and

NEPA and 1 rig in Southwest Appalachia. EXE’s production estimates for 4Q26 in 
Appalachia were also 3% lower relative to prior 4Q26 guidance, while Haynesville

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.
