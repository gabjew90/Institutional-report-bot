Rates and Credit Strategy
More curve impulses
Bond futures are rather supported this morning, partly on the back of lower oil prices and negative
Tech headlines. Yesterday's session was rather subdued with the US out on holiday, and US CPIs
and Fed reverberating.
Curve flattening thus extended further, led by the front end, with Schatz yields back above 3.10%
and the 2-10y IRS curve inverting. At the long-end, Bunds were rather rangebound, both outright
and vs. swaps. EGB spreads were not able to profit, leaving 10y BTPs vs. Bunds hovering around
100bp, while OATs continue to lag behind (see chart below).

US CPI print and rumours about Williams, Fed speak remains in focus. A long line-up of speakers is
on the agenda this afternoon and evening, with Hammack, Daly, and Williams scheduled to deliver
remarks, among others. If they were to add to their recent hawkish comments, it will be intriguing
to see the spillovers into ECB expectations. Central bank expectations have been the dominant
driver for UST-Bund spreads since summer. While spreads have widened following the US CPI, they
remain shy of the highs from early October (see chart below).
