Ivonescimab (Akeso / Summit Therapeutics): Imminent High-Stakes FDA Decision

• Ivonescimab is an immunotherapy designed for advanced non-small cell lung cancer. It simultaneously unmasks
cancer cells so the patient’s own immune system can attack them while choking off the tumor's blood supply to
prevent further growth. An FDA approval decision for patients with genetically mutated lung cancer whose disease
progressed after initial targeted therapy. Results from the HARMONi-3 global clinical trial in squamous lung cancer
will determine whether the drug can displace current global standards of care.
 
Zoci (Zai Lab) – Targeted Precision in Aggressive Lung Cancer
• Zoci is a precision-targeted therapy engineered for extensive-stage small cell lung cancer—a fast-growing, highly
lethal cancer with few effective treatment alternatives—as well as rare neuroendocrine tumors. It seeks out a
specific marker expressed on these tumor cells to deliver concentrated cell-killing therapy. Zai Lab is initiating a
global Phase 3 registrational trial by year-end 2026 to evaluate Zoci as a first-line treatment in small cell lung
cancer. Topline results from an expansion study in neuroendocrine tumors are expected in the first half of 2027,
unlocking a clear pathway toward accelerated global approval.
 
BeOne Medicines and Innovent Biologics
• BeOne Medicines (formerly BeiGene): Provides the commercial foundation for the basket. Anchored by global
market leadership in blood cancers (leukemia and lymphoma), BeOne generated over $5.3 billion in 2025 revenue.
The company is converting global clinical milestones into recurring commercial royalties as late-stage trials in
gastrointestinal and breast cancers advance toward international regulatory submissions.
• Innovent Biologics: Accelerating late-stage clinical collaborations with global partners such as Takeda,
transitioning from upfront licensing payments into a multi-year stream of development milestones and eventual
global royalties.
 
Sac-TMT (Kelun Biotech / Merck MSD) – Broadening Across Solid Tumors
• Sac-TMT functions like a guided missile for cancer cells, delivering a lethal chemotherapy payload directly inside
tumors while sparing healthy surrounding tissue. It is designed to treat hard-to-treat solid tumors, notably
metastatic breast cancer (including aggressive triple-negative breast cancer) and advanced lung cancer. Following
strong clinical outcomes in China, global Phase 3 clinical readouts across 2027 will evaluate Sac-TMT in stomach
cancer, lung cancer, and hormone-driven breast cancer. Positive readouts will pave the way for international filings
through Kelun's multi-billion dollar partnership with Merck.
