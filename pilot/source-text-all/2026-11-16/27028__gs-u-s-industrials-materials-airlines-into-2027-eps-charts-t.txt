Pre-Market Movers:

Privo on risk: Time will tell whether this was anything more than a bear market rally. There are plenty of
reasons to be constructive, but a durable breakout probably requires lower rates, lower energy and broader
leadership. The logical candidates outside the immediate AI infrastructure complex are hyperscalers,
transportation, cyclicals and aerospace. Defense also looks washed out. But it’s difficult for many of these
groups to work sustainably against an aggressive hiking cycle and stubbornly high oil prices. Ultimately,
the market needs oil fixed and rates lower… and those two things may well be connected. Absent that, I
suspect we chop rather than break out. That said, positioning has been reset materially, volatility remains
elevated and the market has already absorbed an extraordinary amount of bad news. Low positioning and
high vol are powerful short term tailwinds.
In Europe a few prints to highlight, SIE GR DI the bright spot and Q1 guide ahead. Highlighting China
momentum, strong start to October. RR trading statement in line with EFH run rating towards the lower
end but financial guide unchanged. Buyback accelerated. Mid term targets to be revisited in February.
BAE/ LN trading statement – raising cash guide due to strong operational performance. HLAG GR Q3

Airlines – desk thoughts: our operating assumption is that by middle of 2027 inflation fears will be lower,
rates TBD and oil here or lower. In the near term that leads us positive on airlines with a Q4 tailwinds
building. Think smaller, less well capitalized and perhaps out of favor names continue to outperform with
near term JBLU balance sheet concerns likely overdone, ALGT proving a good mouse trap along with
higher revisions and ALK almost pure optionality at $40. Over the medium term we lean cautious with full
year 2027 RASM vs. CASMx spread likely to narrow substantially. While RASM can/could grow in 2027,
we think low to mid single digits is a very generous outcome. CASMx has surprised us to the upside
(worse) in 2026 with costs likely stickier leading to less relief on CASMx vs. expectations. Fuel a
wildcard but embedded EPS growth is already very robust. We worry the spread despite what fuel does
will not reach those current expectations, the industry will lap the first liquidation of a carrier in multiple
decades creating a capacity headwind (more not less) and we’re starting to see signs of demand elasticity
with TSA data down at some points year over year. Welcome your view.
