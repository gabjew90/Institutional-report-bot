China Outlook - Link to Report - we expect real GDP growth to moderate slightly from 4.6% in 2026 to 4.5% in 2027,
assuming that the government’s growth target will be nudged down from “4.5-5.0%” this year to “around 4.5%” next
year…. This benign growth forecast is not predicated on significant policy easing or a property market turnaround.
Rather, it is built on the arithmetic of less drag from existing growth headwinds. By our estimates, the drag from the
property market should lessen from 1.8pp of GDP in 2026 to 1.3pp in 2027, a net lift of 0.5pp. Similarly, we expect
China’s augmented fiscal deficit to decline more slowly in 2027 compared with 2026, so the negative impulse from
continued fiscal tightening will also become 0.5pp smaller….. Compared with market expectations, our growth
projection is slightly above consensus, our inflation forecast is significantly below consensus, and our current account
forecast is notably stronger than consensus. These projections underpin our views of falling Chinese interest rates and
a strengthening CNY.
