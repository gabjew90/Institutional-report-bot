Europe Outlook 2027: Finding Growth
We have published our 2027 Outlook for European 
equities; below we highlight our main takeaways: 
1. We expect earnings growth to slow in 2027 to 7%, from 
18% in 2026, concurrently with energy EPS declines and 
weaker economic growth and margins next year. Historically 
this pace of EPS growth is consistent with positive but low 
returns (Exhibit 1). We forecast STOXX Europe 600 at 670, 
680, 700 over 3, 6 and 12 months, for a 6% price return and 
9% total return over 12 months. 
2. Investors are allocating to Europe (Exhibit 2), as it offers 
several attractive characteristics and AI-diversification 
benefits, as the recent rotation reaffirmed, and a 5.5% FCF 
yield. On the other side, in the US, companies’ free cash flow 
has eroded as a consequence of increasing cash spending. 
3. European equities have also the most to gain from 
relief on energy supply and prices.  European valuations 
have closely tracked the moves in energy prices since 
February (Exhibit 3). Our Commodities strategists expect 
energy supply relief through 2027 and look for Brent oil to 
moderate to $78/bbl and TTF to EUR 31/MWh by end-2027. 
This normalization would majorly help ease input cost 
pressure on European companies.
