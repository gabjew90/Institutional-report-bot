Valuation & Risks 
 
F3Q - Solid quarter, but the picture is still 
rendering 
Adobe reported a solid F3Q, but we don't expect results or encouraging leading 
indicators will be sufficient to resolve persistent debates overhanging the stock. 
Top-line and segment-level revenues were ahead, though Total ARR of $27.5bn 
was only in line with the Street and FY ARR guidance was maintained. CEO-
designate Anil Chakravarthy's thoughts on the company's future outlook 
highlighted vast opportunity across the entire Adobe portfolio and customer 
base, possibly a bit different from what some might have expected to be a more 
narrow focus on enterprise. We look forward to learning more in the coming 
months about Mr. Chakravarthy's vision for Adobe. 
AI KPIs and other leading indicators were once again encouraging. AI-first ARR 
surpassed $650mn up ~$150mn sequentially and accelerating from ~$100mn 
added last quarter. Firefly enterprise usage accelerated and Firefly app and credit 
pack ending ARR grew 40% q/q, all of which we believe reflects accelerating 
adoption and usage of Adobe's AI solutions. Creative freemium MAUs exceeded 
100 million, while Acrobat AI Assistant MAU doubled quarter over quarter, and 
total Acrobat + Express MAU surpassed 900mn. All of these point towards 
continued freemium momentum, though the timeline to monetization remains a 
key question.   
F4Q guidance was roughly in line with Street estimates, though the 1-2pt 
sequential deceleration underscores questions around go-forward growth. We 
note Adobe will have an extra week in F1Q and FY27 which we believe should  
benefit revenue by ~$500mn with likely a more modest tailwind to F1Q NNARR 
of ~$30mn, and may not be well reflected in Street models. While results were 
solid and commentary on growth initiatives encouraging, we expect the positives 
are insufficient to outweigh broader go-forward uncertainty. Maintain Hold and 
$260 Target Price. 
 
What we liked/What we're watching on results 
(+) F3Q total revenue of $6.76bn was ahead of Street expectations of 
approximately $6.70bn, growing 13% y/y (12% cc).  
(=) Total ARR exited the quarter at $27.50bn, in line with Street expectations and 
grew 11.2% y/y reported, and we estimate ~9% organically. Net new ARR of 
~$400mn likely reflects postponed Creative Cloud line optimizations and greater 
emphasis on freemium user acquisition.

(=) We note Adobe will have an extra week in 1Q, which we estimate will add

Estimates, Risks, and Valuation 
Following F3Q results, we adjust our FY26/27 revenue and EPS estimates to 
$26.6bn/$29.6bn and $24.46/$28.68 from $26.6bn/$29.1bn and $24.41/$28.42 
respectively to reflect our updated views on the business and to reflect the extra 
week in 1Q and FY27. We maintain our Hold rating and our DCF-derived $260 
target price. Our DCF is based on a WACC of 12% and a terminal RFR of 4%. We 
use a terminal growth rate of 2%, reflecting sub-sector growth assumptions. 
Downside risks include: 1) competition from disruptors across different forms of 
creative media, 2) underperformance from Digital Experience, and 3) execution. 
Upside risks include: 1) accelerated contribution to financials from monetization 
of Firefly, 2) genAI unlocking greater than expected adoption of creative tools, 
and 3) Digital Media driving sooner than expected adoption of DX solutions.
