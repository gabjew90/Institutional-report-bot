DB CoTD: Only 53 days left... 
My new chartbook is on the Deutsche Bank Research Institute site, where it is open to all. Titled " The Home 
Straight ", it examines the key market themes as we enter the final stretch of the year. 
 
One section of the pack looked at the upcoming midterms, and today’s CoTD updates a chart from it. 
Since the August spike in oil, prediction-market probabilities of the Democrats regaining the Senate have 
moved back above 50%, having dipped to 41% when oil prices fell back towards $70/bbl in July. With Brent 
trading close to $110/bbl at their peak last night, markets have to consider whether Republicans would want 
that to continue over the next seven and a half weeks leading into the midterms. 
Trump said in a speech on Tuesday night that oil prices would remain high until after the midterms before 
falling sharply afterwards. That may have contributed to some of the recent rise in oil prices, as markets 
priced in the possibility of a more prolonged conflict. That said, one cannot help but wonder whether the 
administration will feel compelled to act to ease pressure on oil prices in these coming few weeks. A 10-year 
US Treasury yield within a few basis points of 5% will also focus minds. 
Given the rhetoric on both sides of the US–Iran conflict, any de-escalation looks difficult. However, the final 
two years of the administration will also be challenging if Democrats regain control of both the House 
(currently an 87% probability on Polymarket) and importantly the Senate (53%). 
As such, it is not a foregone conclusion that oil will remain comfortably above $100/bbl through to the 
midterms.
