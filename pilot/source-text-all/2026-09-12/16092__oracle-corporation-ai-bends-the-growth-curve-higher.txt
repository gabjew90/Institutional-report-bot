Oracle Corporation
AI Bends the Growth Curve Higher 
Summary
The +4% after-market reaction in Oracle shares strikes us as reasonable given what we 
conclude was a quarter marked by the positives (1Q/Aug revs and EPS beat, guide for 
further cloud infra acceleration, completion of ATM/secondary) offsetting the negatives 
(no FY27 revs guide raise). Net, the material AI-driven growth acceleration (30% revs 
growth from 11% a year-ago) is playing out. We remain Buy-rated
Key Positives
We’d highlight: 1. Oracle appears to executing well, beating revs/EPS guidance, tightly 
managing opex (-9% y/y) to offset GM pressure, growing the backlog (citing more 
customer diversity) and standing up 850 MW of capacity. 2. The guide for a continued 
infra acceleration (from 120% c/c in 1Q/Aug) throughout FY27 was positive, as we were 
modeling stable IaaS growth. 3. The FY27 capex guidance reaffirm of $90-95b may 
come as a relief to some and Oracle reaffirmed its outlook for dollar capex to peak in 
FY27 or FY28. 4. The completion of the $20b ATM/secondary and the reaffirmed view 
that no additional financing will be required this CY relieves some credit-related risk. 5. 
The 20%+ GPU pricing uplift on renewals/resales is in-line with bullish anecdotes from 
peers and Oracle signaled that the prior guide for $10b in revs per GW may now be 
higher. 6. While the 850 MW of capacity added fell short of the ~1 GW target, we don’t 
want to be overly critical, as this is just short of what Microsoft added last quarter, is such 
a large ramp from the last several quarters, and the big Abilene site (consuming 44% of 
all GPUs delivered) is scaling well. 7. While the SaaS growth rate of 10% remained 
mixed, it did accelerate by a point sequentially (solid back-office growth). 
Key Negatives
We’d note: 1. Despite strong capacity/backlog growth and higher GPU pricing, Oracle 
merely reaffirmed the $90b FY27 revs guide from the 2Q/Nov 2025 quarter call. Oracle 
cited conservatism, the fact that this is 1Q, and uncertainty regarding capacity timing/
conversion. Might there be some growth offset, perhaps the on-prem license segment, 
which was down hard in the quarter? 2. The 2Q/Nov revs guide came with a very wide 
growth range, signaling some source of uncertainty (perhaps capacity timing). 3. GMs of 
61% (GM dollars +15%) were down almost 800bps y/y and slightly below our 62% 
estimate. 
Valuation:
On CY27 estimates, Oracle shares trade at 23x GAAP and 18x non-GAAP EPS, in our 
view attractive given the unprecedented AI-driven growth acceleration, good execution, 
and pending backlog conversion. We remain Buy-rated and are raising our PT from $245 
to $250, based on an unchanged CY28e non-GAAP multiple of 19x, reasonable given 
revs growth of 30%+.
