Global ETP flows: Q3 recap
In Q3,  investors allocated over $843.3B to ETPs globally, up from $658.1B during Q2 2026. Equities led 
allocations, with $542.6B of net inflows relative to $445.7B during the quarter prior, followed by inflows into 
fixed income ($239.8B). Further inflows were also seen into Commodity ETPs ($36.1B), reversing -$8.3B of 
outflows seen during the quarter prior, largely reflecting renewed interest in gold exposures ($17.4B) as 
equity market volatility and geopolitical tensions persist. From a regional perspective, inflows into Equity 
ETPs were led by US exposures which saw $250.3B of inflows, albeit representing a material decline from the 
$352.4B of inflows seen during the quarter prior. Global exposures saw $93.6B of inflows, representing a 
moderate drop relative to the $122.4B observed over the quarter prior, followed by Chinese exposures which 
experienced a sharp rebound with $55.4B of inflows following the -$105.8B of outflows experienced 
following a domestic cool-off during the quarter prior. Within US equities, investor sentiment towards broad 
exposures moderated, with $96.2B of inflows relative to $205.4B of inflows during the quarter prior, while 
allocations to active strategies saw a moderate increase from $19.8B to $20.6B. Dividend exposures also saw 
a moderate increase, with $21.1B of inflows relative to $16.9B during the quarter prior, while Technology 
Hardware & Equipment saw an even larger increase from $7.3B of inflows to $14.4B - reflecting continued 
interest in the AI buildout.
Global fixed income flows totalled $239.8B in Q3, up moderately from $209.3B during the quarter prior. 
Inflows continued to be led by Broad Market exposures, which saw $73.1B of inflows, followed by further 
interest in Treasury exposures ($66.4B) and Investment Grade ($31.6B).

1. Equities: Europe leads DM buying, EM single country flows driven by China
Equity UCITS ETPs attracted $110.7B of net buying in Q3, up from $92.6B in Q2. Developed market (DM) 
equities accounted for the majority of flows, gathering $102B, compared to $85.8B in the previous quarter. 
Emerging market equities also saw stronger demand, attracting $8.8B, up from $6.8B in Q2.
On a regional basis, DM Global exposures remained the largest allocation, attracting $62.1B, broadly in line 
with the $60.9B gathered in Q2. DM North America added $28.7B, also broadly unchanged quarter-on-
quarter. The largest change came from Europe, which attracted $11.1B, reversing the -$5.3B of outflows 
recorded in Q2. DM Asia Pacific (APAC) added a further $2.9B.
Within DM single-country exposures, the US remained dominant with $27.4B of inflows, followed by Japan at 
$2.7B. Switzerland saw a notable acceleration in demand, attracting $2.0B, up from $680m in Q2. The UK 
gathered $563m and Italy added $159m. On the outflow side, Germany was the largest contributor, 
recording outflows of -$714m, although selling moderated from -$1.2B in Q2. France saw outflows of              
-$125m, while Canada and Australia recorded more modest outflows of -$21m and -$26m, respectively.
Sustainable equity UCITS ETPs attracted $9.9B in Q3, more than doubling from $4.1B in Q2.
Regionally, Global sustainable equity strategies led with $6.4B, although this was lower than the $8.6B 
gathered in the previous quarter. North America attracted $3.7B, reversing -$2.7B of outflows in Q2, while 
Europe gathered $1.4B, also coming back from outflows (-$784m). On the outflow side, APAC lost -$856m, 
while EM exposures recorded -$782m of outflows.
By investment type, screened strategies led with $3.3B, although flows slowed from $7.7B in Q2. Climate-
focused strategies attracted $2.0B, reversing -$3.2B of outflows, while Optimised strategies added $1.9B 
after a total drop of -$1.3B in Q2. Factors and Sustainable gathered $1.8B, up from $535m, and Other Uplift 
strategies attracted $1.4B. Sustainable Tilt added $373m and Other Thematic exposures gathered $612m. 
On the outflow side, Best-in-class strategies experienced -$1.2B of outflows, although this represented an 
improvement from -$2.7B in Q2. Environment-focused strategies recorded outflows of -$185m after 
attracting $582m in the previous quarter.
