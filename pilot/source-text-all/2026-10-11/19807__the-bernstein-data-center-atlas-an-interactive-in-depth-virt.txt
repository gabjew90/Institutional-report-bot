Data centers and AI infrastructure are 2026's hottest topic, and they’re not going away
anytime soon. Investors spend every waking hour hunting the next shortage, bottleneck or
sleeper component. The megabuild has dragged generalists into corners of the value chain
that used to belong to specialists: switchgear, CDUs, HBM, optics, the list goes on… so who
is actually doing what? And what does half this stuff even do?
INTRODUCING: THE BERNSTEIN DATA CENTER ATLAS
The Atlas is an interactive walk through a representative U.S. AI data center. Start outside
the fence, where the power lines come in. Walk the corridor, step into the data hall and
crack open a rack. Keep going, past the server and into the GPU package, down to the
memory sitting next to the chip. Then head back out through the network, the meet-me
room and the fiber leaving the building. Last stop is the businesses that fill the place and
how they get paid. Click any component to see what it does, who builds it and which
Bernstein analyst covers them.
As we’ve said many times, no single seat can understand this whole picture. (See our
joint data center forecast here). The Atlas brings together 26 Bernstein analysts across
infrastructure, semis, hardware, networking, electrical equipment, capital goods, energy,
software, internet, telecom, digital assets and building materials, in the U.S., Europe and
Asia. About 400 companies (covered, not covered and private) show up on its cards.
WHAT'S INSIDE
It starts before the first shovel: land, steel and concrete, then the grid and the
interconnection queue that decides when a site actually switches on. Inside the fence, the
power room tames the electricity and the cooling plant figures out where all that heat goes.
The data hall is where the hardware lives, and from there you can zoom from a rack to a
server to the chip itself. The networking and meet-me rooms show how data moves around
the cluster and out to the world. And the finale answers the question that pays for all of it:
who fills the building, whether that's cloud, colocation or the software riding on top.
HOW TO USE IT
Are you a newbie? Take the full tour: 18 stops, one short explainer at each. More seasoned?
Dive right in, click any door or search for a company, ticker or term. Every card shows what
a part does and who makes it, linked to the Bernstein analyst who covers it. Our analysts
are standing in the rooms they cover, so click one to land on their coverage (and let us
know if they don’t look like their avatars). Each room also contains our sector views; and the
glossary is on hand when things start to get confusing.
And yes, this virtual data center was built in a very real AI data center. Like the rest of the
market, our value per token is still TBD.
