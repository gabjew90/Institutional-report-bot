Two Things: CleanTech + Midstream
CleanTech. Will be hosting a breakfast this morning with Brian Lee and a group of investors in Midtown –
if want to catch up on the back of it, please reach out. It’s been tougher to find folks that want to talk on
the CleanTech space over the last couple of months – which we think reflects more of a macro vs micro
view (rates, oil/diesel, concerns on growth, potential project pushout, lack of catalyst path)… but the bar is
low for the group into EPS (see Brian’s preview note). Some of the more topical names / inbounds on the
desk… ENPH vs SEDG. Would think more investors are leaning more constructive on ENPH into the
print – and recall positioning here has essentially flipped QoQ (ie last quarter we were talking about
what’s positive for ENPH should be even better for SEDG etc). Both stocks have essentially erased all
gains from SST potential / hype we saw back in the spring timeframe – and while this represents potential
upside – the more in focus question is ‘how much lower do resi estimates need to move from here?’ NXT.
#1 source of concern for the story has been potential for project pushout / cancellations (more folks
pointing to sensitivity historically of interest rates moving higher and solar project delays). There’s been
an increasing focus on entrance into newer end markets / products for the story – and diversification has
historically been rewarded. Company expected to host CMD next month at RE+ … while historically
these events have been ‘sell the news’ (not just for solar, but across the board) – with the bar low and
positioning relatively light – this time could be different.
Midstream. Wanted to send around a quick pulse check on the Midstream group. The broader punchline is
– similar to the rest of Energy – this is more of a macro call more than anything and rates are cited as the
#1 headwind for the group. Into 2Q EPS – would have said the group was broadly owned by both
specialists and generalists – the hype around Permian volume growth had legs, there was upside to FY26
and even FY27 estimates, and rates were not at the forefront of every conversation. Positioning feels
cleaner now across the board. On Liquids – what are the more owned names at this point relative? TRGP,
PAA, KNTK. Targa has come off the highs more recently – and we would have said the stock was 9/10
owned a few months ago – it’s something closer to 7/10 now. On Gas – what are the more owned names at
this point relative? WMB, KMI*. KMI sticks out to me as we head into EPS season and wrap up previews
– the stock is essentially flat over the last month vs the peer group down ~7% on average. Expecting to get
clarity on the $400 mn of contingent projects and continue to have convos on ‘backlog bogeys’ (reach out
for feedback).
Source: Goldman Sachs FICC & Equities, Marquee, as of 8 Oct 2026.
