Dassault remains mixed, with management flagging a soft Europe amid a challenged 
automotive sector, while the US is expected to stay competitive. In APAC, Asia ex-China 
is performing well even as China stays muted for the year, with a pick-up expected in the 
second half. Defence was also flagged as an area of potential upside. We continue to 
forecast c.4% yoy ex-fx Industrial Innovation growth for 3Q26, growing to c.6% over the 
mid term. 
2) AI product roadmap emerging  
Following our DSY CFO roadshow, we note management expanded on the AI product 
roadmap, with the July release delivering 25 virtual companions across 10 offerings, 
covering 40% of 3DX functionality, expected to rise to 50% at year-end and c.two-thirds 
by mid-2027. Management also expects meaningful scope for pricing uplift on virtual 
companions, underpinned by significant productivity enhancements, and views its core 
PLM dataset and scientific simulation capabilities as a key competitive moat. Internally, 
AI is also being deployed to drive efficiency across R&D, G&A, collections, and 
procurement. We expect investor focus at the 3Q26 results to be on any incremental 
detail on the AI product rollout cadence and the pathway to monetisation. 
3) Cadence of growth in FY26 and range of outcomes factored into guidance 
We believe investor focus will remain on the range of outcomes for FY26 growth and the 
company’s ability to manage and execute against several dynamics within its business 
(end-market backdrop, large deal conversions, Centric subscription transition 
headwinds and continued challenges in Life Sciences). We remain prudent, forecasting 
c.4% yoy ex-fx total revenue growth for FY26, at the mid-point of guidance. 
4) Dynamics within the Life Sciences business  
We expect investors to continue to debate the competitive positioning of Medidata in 
the Life Sciences industry given the continued deceleration in growth over prior 
quarters. While management reported solid H1 bookings that should support growth 
further into the year, with Medidata also expected to improve in H2, we await further 
proof points of growth improvement in the segment. We therefore remain conservative, 
forecasting Life Sciences growth of c.-2% in 3Q26, and also continue to take a prudent 
view on the mid-term growth of the business at a c.1% CAGR across FY27-30 (with FY26 
growth of -3% yoy ex-fx). 
5) Growth trajectory for Solidworks and details around subscription transition 
headwind for Centric PLM under Mainstream Innovation  
Management noted that underlying demand in Mainstream Innovation remains healthy, 
though growth continues to be mechanically dragged by the ongoing migration of the 
SolidWorks base to subscription, with no evidence of share loss flagged. We expect 
SolidWorks to grow at c.6% yoy ex-fx in 3Q26 and c.6% yoy ex-fx in FY26, with 
consistent high single-digit growth over the mid term. For Centric, although H2 is guided 
to be more normalised vs H1 (which benefited from large deals), we expect growth of 
c.22% yoy ex-fx in FY26, normalizing to c.8% over the medium term. Overall, for 
Mainstream Innovation, we forecast c.7% yoy ex-fx growth in 3Q26 and c.9% yoy ex-fx 
growth in FY26.

6) Colour around opex dynamics and the phasing of costs in 3Q26 and beyond 
We expect Dassault to retain flexibility on its cost base to protect margins and so remain

7) Details on FCF dynamics 
We expect cash conversion of +80% for FY26, gradually improving to the mid-80’s

8) Capital Markets Day in focus  
At the Capital Markets Day on November 17 in Paris, we expect the focus to be on AI

monetisation pathway, as well as medium-term revenue growth targets and details on 
capital allocation priorities, including the strategic rational of the Arisglobal acquisition.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.  
M&A Rank 
Across our global coverage, we examine stocks using an M&A framework, considering both qualitative factors and quantitative factors (which may vary 
across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring 
companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 
representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard 
departmental guidelines we incorporate an M&A component into our target price. M&A rank of 3 is considered immaterial and therefore does not 
factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.
