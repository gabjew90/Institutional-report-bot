Investment Thesis
SK hynix Inc.
SKHY US
Hold
SK hynix remains key AI memory upcycle beneficiary with HBM leadership. As primary NVIDIA supplier, secured strategic
position in expanding AI infrastructure. LTAs with ~10 customers enhance earnings stability. In the most recent quarter, SK
hynix reported record 1H 2026 cumulative revenue of KRW131,895B, surpassing the KRW100T mark for the first time, with an
operating margin of 74.4% and EBITDA margin of 80.3%, driven by DRAM ASP rising ~30% and NAND ASP rising by mid-50%
in Q2; however, Q2 revenue of KRW79,319B missed consensus of KRW84,121B. Management guided full-year 2026 DRAM
demand bit growth in the mid-20% range and raised 2026 capex to the “high KRW40T range” to accelerate fab construction.
Taiwan
Semiconductor
Manufacturing
Company Limited
TSM US
Buy
We like TSMC's leadership in advanced manufacturing, backed by sustained AI investment and growing advanced packaging
demand. The company is uniquely positioned to capture AI acceleration as agentic AI expands silicon demand beyond
accelerators to CPUs and networking chips. Management's capex increase to USD60B-USD64B reinforces confidence in
structural demand strength. The successful 2nm ramp and increasing advanced node contribution should improve revenue
quality despite temporary margin dilution.
Samsung Electronics
Co., Ltd.
SMSN LI
Buy
We see improving earnings momentum from a memory rebound. Samsung’s integrated platform combining memory, logic,
foundry, and packaging enables complete AI solutions, supporting deeper customer relationships. Foundry serves as catalyst
given geopolitical diversification needs from Taiwan-concentrated TSMC, with Samsung best-positioned as alternative. Recent
wins like Tesla evidence this materializing. Net cash provides investment flexibility
Tokyo Electron
Limited
8035 JP
Buy
We TEL as a long-term winner in semiconductor production equipment. TEL maintains strong positions in coater/developers,
etch systems, and deposition systems, benefiting from AI-related demand offsetting general-purpose chip softness.
Management’s confidence is supported by GPU/ASIC transition from 4nm to 3nm nodes. TEL's technological leadership and
strong balance sheet position it well for the AI cycle.
