All Eyes on Refining
Is the bear case still alive for the group? One of the more asked questions on the desk over the last 24-48
hours … and price action for the group has been strong relative to the rest of Energy (taking a look at our
baskets over last 5 days: GS Refining +7% vs US Integrated flattish and US E&P -2%). Unless there are
material concerns on growth easing / demand destruction materializing – bulls are highlighting number of
ways to win across the group (ie if Brent rips another $10/b higher from here – likely to see continued risk
premium in products and if Brent falls $10/b from here with flows continuing to progress – shouldn’t this
help cracks with products markets still tight?)...
EU SPR release has been topical (see this AM: DIESEL EXPORT BAN WOULD UNDERMINE US AS
RELIABLE PARTNER: EU) – and most specialist feedback has been highlighting more caution on seeing
a material SPR release from Europe near term (that said – France proposing EU +IEA member countries
release ~100 mb of diesel and crude... diesel moving lower this AM on it). Worth noting in the US - we
have seen several states announce fuel tax relief for the ag industry (AL, LA, NE, NC, ND, OK, etc) with
the midterms rapidly approaching.
What are some of the end markets saying / highlighting?

but for how long? General rule of thumb is diesel can be anywhere from ~10-20% of AISC at mining ops.
