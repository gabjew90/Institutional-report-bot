PA Gubernatorial debate: supply, affordability in focus 
Pennsylvania’s October 7 gubernatorial debate should sharpen the contrast between 
Governor Josh Shapiro and Treasurer Stacy Garrity on power supply, affordability and 
data-center growth. Both candidates support adding generation as tightening PJM supply 
and rising load increase customer costs. The key differences are resource mix and the 
policy response to higher bills. Shapiro supports a broader generation portfolio and has 
taken a more interventionist position on utility rates, while Garrity has emphasized 
natural gas development and tax relief. The debate could provide a clearer read on the 
state policy backdrop for Pennsylvania utilities, generators and PJM market reform. 
ETR/Meta buildout faces a ratepayer-protection test 
Entergy Louisiana’s plan to serve Meta’s expanded Richland Parish campus enters a 
three-day evidentiary hearing on October 7. The proposed buildout includes more than 
5,200 MW of new gas generation, battery storage, nuclear uprates and substantial 
transmission investment. The central question is whether Meta’s commitments fully 
protect existing customers if demand falls short of Entergy’s forecast. The hearing will 
shape the record for the Commission’s expected December 16 consideration and could 
set an important precedent for funding utility investment tied to hyperscaler growth. 
FERC agenda frames the next market catalysts 
FERC’s Sunshine Notice is expected to identify the matters scheduled for its October 15 
open meeting.  Thursday’s agenda will show which major power-market proceedings are 
moving toward near-term Commission action. Large-load interconnection, cost 
allocation, resource adequacy and PJM market design remain the key areas to watch. 
Items not included could still be resolved by notational order, although the timing would 
be less clear.
