Nike 
Another reset 
Reiterate Rating: UNDERPERFORM | PO: 24.00 USD | Price: 35.15 USD   
  
 
 
Weakness to persist into F28 
We reiterate our Underperform as visibility on a sales turnaround remains limited. Despite 
another significant reduction to our estimates, we continue to see downside risk to the 
premium multiple until the timing of a revenue inflection becomes clearer. Management 
now expects F27 sales to decline HSD and EPS of $1.15-$1.35 (vs VA cons of $1.67), with 
pressure from Sportswear, Jordan Brand and Greater China continuing at least into F28. 
Nike introduced a new $2.5bn gross cost savings program (by F31) and reaffirmed its 
commitment to the dividend. We are cutting our F27/F28 EPS by 21/22% and our PO to 
$24 (from $30), still 16x F28 P/E. 
Weak sell-through puts wholesale orders at risk  
Several higher-volume footwear styles sold through below expectations, leaving excess 
inventory and resulting in lower wholesale order books for the coming seasons. Sportswear 
represented ~50% of 1Q sales and declined LDD while Jordan Brand (13% of sales) fell 
mid-teens, as too many retro products weighed on sales. Nike plans to further reduce the 
volume and frequency of Jordan retro launches, with the greatest pressure in North America. 
China to get worse from here 
Greater China sales declined 26% cc in 1Q, and management expects trends to worsen 
through the remainder of F27, with pressure extending into F28. Nike is cutting brand-
dilutive online distribution, narrowing digital sales to fewer sites, and working with partners 
to reduce inventory. We expect the reset to take multiple seasons and weigh on both sales 
and profitability. A more focused online distribution, better brick & mortar presentation 
and localized product are the strategies to turn the business. 
Performance remains a bright spot 
Performance grew HSD in 1Q (or +LDD ex. Greater China reset), with running, football, 
tennis and golf each growing DD. Running delivered another quarter of share gains while 
World Cup demand supported football. We expect the innovation pipeline to be a focus 
of Nike’s November investor day. The eventual sales turn will depend on growing levels 
of newness to drive stronger customer interest.

Company Description 
Nike is the premier global athletic footwear and apparel 
company with roughly 40% global athletic footwear market 
share. It also sells brand Jordan, Converse, and athletic 
apparel. It outsources production to factories in Asia and 
sells product worldwide. 
Investment Rationale 
We are Underperform-rated on NKE. Regional pressure in 
China and EMEA, weak wholesale sell-through trends and 
innovation that has yet to offset weakness in larger lifestyle 
categories limit visibility into a durable sales and earnings 
recovery. Earnings increasingly depend on gross margin 
expansion and cost control, leaving downside risk to 
estimates and the multiple if execution falls short.

Price objective basis & risk 
Nike (NKE) 
Our PO of $24 is based on 16x F28E EPS, in line with the sportswear peer average. This 
reflects Nike's turnaround potential offset by limited visibility into a durable sales and 
earnings recovery. 
 
Upside risks to our PO are faster-than-expected innovation that drives full price sales, 
stronger wholesale sell-through, a recovery in China, and greater-than-expected margin 
improvement or cost savings. 
 
Downside risks to our PO are weaker-than-expected sales in China and wholesale, 
innovation being too slow or not resonating, continued pressure in larger lifestyle 
categories, a promotional retail environment, and margin or cost progress falling short 
of expectations.
