Global fund flows, week ending September 30 
Flows into mutual funds and related investment products were positive across 
n
both equities and fixed income. 
Global equity funds saw net inflows in the week ending September 30 (+$16bn 
n
vs -$10bn in the previous week). DM saw net inflows despite continued outflows 
from US funds. EM saw net inflows driven by China funds. At the sector level, 
technology funds saw the largest net inflows followed by utilities funds. The pace 
of equity inflows to Korea and Taiwan funds has seen a pullback in recent weeks 
(see Chart of the Week).  
Flows into global fixed income funds remained largely well-supported 
n
particularly from inflows to government and municipal bond funds. Short- and 
long-duration bond funds and inflation-protected bond funds saw inflows in the 
week ending September 30. In EM, hard-currency bond funds saw continued net 
outflows while local-currency bond funds saw net inflows. Money market fund 
assets decreased by $118bn. 
Cross-border FX flows were largely positive, and remained well-supported 
n
across DM. USD and EUR saw the strongest net demand while CNY and INR drove 
the net EM outflows.
