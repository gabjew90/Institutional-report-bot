AstraZeneca (AZN.L): Etcamah (camizestrant) FDA approval in 1L breast 
cancer switch setting a positive surprise
7 September 2026 | 5:30AM BST
On Friday (Sep 4) post market close, AZN announced the FDA granted 
accelerated approval of oral SERD Etcamah (camizestrant) in combination with a 
CDK4/6 inhibitor (palbociclib, ribociclib, or abemaciclib) for HR+/HER2-ve breast 
cancer patients who develop ESR1 mutations before disease progression after 1L 
treatment with an aromatase inhibitor and a CDK4/6 inhibitor (the switch setting) 
based on the SERENA-6 trial. The decision follows an approval in Europe in July 2026 
and, notably, a negative FDA Oncologic Drugs Advisory Committee (ODAC) vote in 
April 2026. Following the accelerated approval, the agency requires AZN to conduct 
a confirmatory efficacy trial (per Project Confirm) and we await further details on 
post marketing requirements. The label, (published here) cites the PFS data from 
SERENA-6 (HR:0.44) and from a safety perspective, we note boxed warnings for 
arrhythmia risks with concomitant use of QTc interval prolonging drugs (including 
CDK4/6 inhibitor Kisqali which was noted in the ODAC briefing documents) and 
general warnings including ECG prior to treatment initiation, heart rate monitoring 
for bradycardia, as well as embryo-fetal toxicity. Photopsia is listed as an adverse 
event but not included in any warnings section which we believe is important in the 
context of positioning within the larger adjuvant setting. 
We see the approval as a positive surprise following the negative ODAC vote (see 
our note here) and our recent conversations suggest investor expectations for 
an approval were low. Whilst the commercial opportunity for Etcamah in the 
SERENA-6 setting is modest, we see the approval as positive both from a DCF and 
sentiment perspective. Notably the approval means Etcamah is the first oral SERD 
approved in the 1L setting and, therefore, should theoretically limit the binary nature 
of the upcoming higher risk SERENA-4 trial. With respect to the confirmatory trials, 
we expect focus on OS from an efficacy perspective (given that SERENA-6 was not 
powered for OS), and potentially additional analyses comparing switching to 
camizestrant before versus after disease progression, which was a key question 
raised during the ODAC meeting. Overall, we believe the confirmatory trials will likely 
read out after CAMBRIA-1 (adjuvant switch setting) reads out in 2H27. 
On safety, we acknowledge the black box warning for arrhythmia risks and the 
requirement for ECG before treatment initiation and heart rate monitoring may limit 
Etcamah utility in certain patients with background cardiovascular risk whereas LLY’s 
(covered by Asad Haider) Inluriyo and Menarini’s Orserdu do not have such side 
effects/requirements on their labels (all 3 drugs have embryo-fetal toxicity) which 
could be an important consideration in the adjuvant setting. That said, we note the

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.  
M&A Rank 
Across our global coverage, we examine stocks using an M&A framework, considering both qualitative factors and quantitative factors (which may vary 
across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring 
companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 
representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard 
departmental guidelines we incorporate an M&A component into our target price. M&A rank of 3 is considered immaterial and therefore does not 
factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.

Investment List with an active rating (i.e., a  stock that is not Rating Suspended, Not Rated, Early-Stage Biotech, Coverage Suspended or Not Covered), is

and represent investment recommendations focused on the size of the total return potential and/or the likelihood of the realization of the return across 
their respective areas of coverage.  The addition or removal of stocks from such Conviction Lists are managed by the Investment Review Committee or 
other designated committee in each respective region and do not represent a change in the analysts’ investment rating for such stocks.    
Total return potential represents the upside or downside differential between the current share price and the price target, including all paid or 
anticipated dividends, expected during the time horizon associated with the price target. Price targets are required for all covered stocks. The total 
return potential, price target and associated time horizon are stated in each report adding or reiterating an Investment List membership.
