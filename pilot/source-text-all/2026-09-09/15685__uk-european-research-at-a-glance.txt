Outperform

Our view: We initiate at Outperform with a 14,500p PT. AZN offers the most
compelling risk/reward in our EU Pharma coverage. We believe the stock has been
overly punished by recent concerns, Wainua's ATTR-failure, the BMY pursuit raising
growth questions, and looming SERENA-4/AVANZAR (2H26) uncertainty, driving the

risk, and unnecessary for AZN's $80bn target. We expect a swift re-rating once
resolved, with the market refocusing on fundamentals with leading growth among
EU peers and significant pipeline optionality.

Published September 8 2026 16:05:00 EDT
Initiating at Sector Perform and 1,975p Price Target
Rating:
Sector Perform
Price Target:
1,975
Our view: We initiate coverage of GSK at Sector Perform with 1,975p PT. The stock
has appreciated ~30% over the past year, reflecting improved fundamentals and
market reception to new CEO Luke Miels' focus on accelerating pipeline execution.
However, we think current levels factor in these improvements, with further upside
contingent on demonstrating what comes next. Projected 2025-30 sales growth
trails peers, with visibility unclear given HIV's rising competition and looming patent
cliff. Flawless execution through its 2031 ambition is required to bridge the pipeline,
while promising, remains a delivery story. Current valuation captures these risks;
we favor other opportunities.

Published September 8 2026 16:05:00 EDT
Initiating at Outperform and CHF400 Price Target
Rating:
Outperform
Price Target:
400.00
Our view: We initiate coverage of Roche at Outperform with a CHF400 Price Target.
Despite a near all-time high valuation, we see room for further re-rating. In the
near-to-mid-term the base business is gaining momentum, while the pipeline offers
deeper potential than Street models, in our view. The crown jewel is giredestrant,
a potential c.$14bn opportunity (vs. consensus $9.3bn) targeting the large HR+/
HER2- breast cancer population with extended adjuvant treatment duration. We
believe Roche is a quality compounder with sustained growth visibility through
2030+, warranting a premium for its pipeline optionality and a lack of LOE pressure.

Published September 8 2026 16:05:00 EDT
Initiating at Sector Perform and €80 Price Target
Rating:
Sector Perform
Price Target:
80.00
Our view: We initiate at Sector Perform with an €80 PT. Sanofi is navigating a
pivotal transition amid near-term challenges: limited catalysts, Dupixent's patent
cliff, and skepticism about delivering >€10bn pipeline sales. However, the thesis is
evolving under CEO Belen Garijo. Portfolio-reshaping M&A is likely, while aggressive
Dupixent lifecycle management and potential REGN collaboration expansion could
offer upside. At 9x 2027E EPS, Sanofi trades at a steep discount, reflecting doubts
around 2030 growth visibility and pipeline execution. Despite Dupixent's strength,
the market demands proof of diversified growth. We view Sanofi as a 'show-me'
story requiring tangible progress before re-rating.

RBC hydro tracker - Austria & Germany
Published September 9 2026 00:45:00 EDT
Persistent weakness continues into August
Our view: Austrian and German (Bavaria) hydro output remained materially weak
in August, continuing to track well below historical norms. Austria’s output was
44% lower Y/Y versus a broadly average August 2025 and sits 45% below the 8-year
average (2018-2025) for the month, comfortably the weakest August in our dataset.
Germany (Bavaria) saw a further deterioration from July, with output 20% lower Y/
Y and 15% below the 8-year average. On a YTD basis, Austria now sits 32% below
the 8-year historical average and Germany (Bavaria) 11% below, underscoring the
persistent weakness across both markets YTD in 2026.

RBC hydro tracker - Finland & Sweden
Published September 9 2026 00:45:00 EDT
Sweden weakness persists; better August for Finland
Our view: August output was weaker in Sweden but improved in Finland. For
Sweden, output was down 21% Y/Y, against a slightly above-average August 2025
(+4% vs. avg) and sits 18% below the historical 8-year average. Finland saw a sharp
reversal, with output up 75% Y/Y, though this is flattered by the weakest August
2025 in our dataset; more meaningfully, output sits 28% above the 8-year average,
the first above-average month in 2026. YTD Sweden output is now 4% below 2025
and 5% below the historical average, ranking 6th out of 9 years; Finland’s YTD is -3%
Y/Y and sitting 15% below the 8-year average, still ranking last in our dataset.

Commodity Surveyor: Summer Shift
Published September 9 2026 00:15:00 EDT
Commodity Investor AUM and Flows Analysis
While price effects were a unifying positive last month, the commodity space saw a
midsummer shift. Commodity index AUM stabilized above $300bln ($308bln, +5%
m/m) as price effects offset narrower outflows. ETPs performed stronger, surging
10.5% to $580bln across regions, and were led by precious metals. The stabilization
in gold certainly helped, as did the growing realization that there may not be a near-
term offramp to Iran conflict.
