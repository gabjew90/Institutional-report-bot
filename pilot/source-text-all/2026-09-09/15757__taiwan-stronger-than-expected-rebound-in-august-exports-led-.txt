Taiwan: Stronger-than-expected Rebound in August Exports, Led by 
Tech
9 September 2026 | 6:07PM KST
Bottom line: 
Taiwan’s exports increased by 6.2% mom sa (seasonally adjusted by GS) in August, 
rebounding from a -0.9% decline in the previous month. The reading was above 
Bloomberg consensus and our forecast, reversing the negative surprises seen in the 
prior two months. Tech exports accounted for all of the headline sequential gains. By 
destination, exports to ASEAN and the US increased by 10.4% and 4.3% mom sa, 
respectively, and together accounted for roughly half of the headline sequential 
increase. Imports rebounded by 7.2% mom sa, and the trade surplus (not seasonally 
adjusted) widened to a new historic high of US$22.3bn, up from US$17.2bn in the 
prior month.
