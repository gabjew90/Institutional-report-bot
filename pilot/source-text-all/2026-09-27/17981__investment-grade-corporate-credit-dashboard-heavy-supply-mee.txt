Investment Grade Corporate Credit Dashboard: Heavy Supply 
Meets Macro Test
•
Macro Matters More Than Micro in September; Expect More Volatility Into The Fall: September issuance is likely to 
be heavy, in keeping with seasonal trends. But we see a bigger skew to M&A activity and less AI-driven supply in the near 
term. If inflation stays benign and hikes are off the table (our base case), we think heavy supply will get absorbed by strong 
demand and attractive all-in yields. In this scenario, credit might be more benign in September. But we expect volatility to 
pick up into fall earnings as focus shifts to 2027, where we think consensus expectations remain too low vs. MS forecasts.
•
Back to School: IG spreads widened in August to 80bps, as heavy supply continued in the first weeks of the month. 
Hyperscalers continue to lead the widening and are now 30bps wider on the year. Total returns are now well in negative 
territory with the rate sell-off, but excess returns are modest despite HQ underperformance.
•
AI Debt Financing Tracker: HQ hyperscalers are ~30bps wider YTD, yet supply has not slowed, with $54bn done in July 
and $41bn in August. Even with ~$445bn of issuance across credit channels YTD, we expect a busy fall. In addition to 
public market supply, HQ issuers will likely continue to flex their ratings muscles to provide credit support to others as new 
financing structures emerge. We see better risk-reward in collateral-backed & stabilized assets than unsecured corporates.
•
Hot Supply Summer: Both July ($152bn) and August ($171bn) set monthly records, with one hyperscaler deal pricing in 
each month. YTD issuance is $1,631bn, +30% y/y.
•
Ratings Migrations Stagnated: Activity slowed over the summer, as July saw the lowest volume since 2022. August had 
more upgrades ($34bn) than downgrades ($13.4bn), driven by Healthcare/Tech. The YTD upgrade/downgrade ratio 
remains 1.9x. 
•
Fund Flows Cooled Off: YTD inflows have reached $111bn, slowing their record pace seen over the summer. IG ETFs 
(+$89.1bn YTD) continue to lead the charge, +73% above last year’s record-setting pace. MFs (+$21.5bn YTD) saw 
modest outflows over the summer.

AI Debt Tracker | We See Ample Market Capacity From Current Levels
YTD issuance for the hyperscalers is at $223bn, with 
~$66bn of non-USD debt
NTM capex expectations have nearly tripled from a 
year ago
Source: FactSet, Bloomberg, Morgan Stanley Research; Note: Data as of August 17, 2026
•
Capex has been in focus as the largest driver of AI-related issuance, 12M forward estimates are almost 3x as high as a year ago. We 
expect more clarity over 2027 estimates to come with 3Q26 earnings at the end of October, current MSe for the five hyperscalers and 
SPCX is ~$1.4tr.
•
In terms of market capacity US IG is one of the deepest markets globally at $10tr, owned predominantly by long-term institutions with a 
structural need for high-quality, income-generating assets. AI-related debt is ~7% of the index against the ~15% we think investors would 
tolerate as maximum sector exposure, leaving significant capacity at the right price. Public US IG has absorbed ~61% of AI issuance 
YTD and we expect it to keep taking the bulk.
•
