TACO Time? Trump Reversal Index Hits Highest 
Level Since March 
 
BY TYLER DURDEN 
FRIDAY, SEP 25, 2026 - 02:50 PM 
The Trump Reversal Index (created by Bloomberg macro strategist, Simon White) has risen to 
its highest level since a significant de-escalation in the US-Iran war in late March. 
The index combines yields, stocks and President Donald Trump’s approval ratings. 
 
Typically, elevated readings have coincided with a policy reversal. 
There were some conciliatory signals on US-Iran relations Wednesday, but the news has soured 
somewhat Thursday, with both sides seemingly not quite ready to resume talks. 
The rise in the index is being partly driven by 'good' reasons, including higher growth, as seen in 
this week's punchy PMIs. 
But that stocks are down is probably what is most consequential to the president.

Perhaps the Chinese state visit will dissuade Trump from overseeing a market meltdown while 
China President Xi Jinping is in town. 
Or perhaps it will embolden him to make a show of strength against the Iranians. 
It’s hard to know for sure of course, but the historical record of the reversal index suggests a 
presidential climb down is on the cards.
