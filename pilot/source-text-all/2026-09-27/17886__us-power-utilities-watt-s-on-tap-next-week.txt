PJM FERC order on RBP (anticipated)  
FERC is expected to rule on PJM's Section 205 filing establishing a Reliability Backstop 
Procurement (RBP) mechanism (Docket ER26-3380). The mechanism would let PJM 
procure backstop capacity from resources that are otherwise slated for retirement or 
unable to clear through existing constructs. An order will trigger the submission window 
(opens 9/30 and runs through 10/21) for generators to either document their qualifying 
capacity or opt out of the mechanism. The order's treatment of eligibility criteria, 
compensation, and opt-out mechanics will shape which generators — including coal, gas, 
and nuclear assets across the PJM footprint — will be able to access this backstop 
revenue stream. 
MI PSC: DTE/Google contract expected on agenda 
Michigan Public Service Commission's 10/2 meeting is expected to take up DTE 
Electric's special contract with Google for a data-center load (Docket U-22058). 
Commission action will clarify rate design, cost-allocation, and minimum-take terms 
governing the arrangement, which will stand as one of DTE's first large-load special 
contracts and a template for how the utility structures future hyperscaler agreements 
ahead of its broader IRP resource additions. 
Oklo analyst meeting and facility tour 
Oklo's analyst meeting and facility tour on Tuesday and Wednesday, 9/29-9/30, will give 
investors direct visibility into the Groves Isotope Test Reactor in Lockhart, Texas, which 
reached first criticality in August under the DOE's Reactor Pilot Program. Management 
commentary on manufacturing scale-up, site milestones, and remaining steps on the 
path to licensing will provide a read on both timeline for Oklo's first revenue-generating 
units and advanced nuclear deployment economics for the space more broadly.
