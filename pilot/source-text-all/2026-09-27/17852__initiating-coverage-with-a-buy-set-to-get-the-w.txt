ALM
 Summary
We are initiating coverage of Almonty Industries Inc. (ALM) with a Buy rating and a $25 target
price. Almonty is a global tungsten mine operator with assets across Western countries and
the US. The company has recently started Phase I commercial production at its Sangdong,
South Korea mine with plans to execute Phase II (~2x production) in 2027. The company is
also expanding production at its Panasqueira mine which (along with Sangdong) would result in
the company leading Western Tungsten production by the end of 2028. Concurrently, tungsten
prices are up ~775% since the start of 2025 as China's export controls have structurally changed
the market. We also see long-term upside from the development of additional owned assets
and the build out of a downstream tungsten oxide facility. The company is our first name in our
new Critical Materials coverage which is outlined in our Primer (here).
 Key Points
Almonty Industries Inc. (ALM) is a US-headquartered global producer of tungsten with
international assets set to help fill the global supply gap driven by recent China export
restrictions. The company’s main production assets are in Panasqueira, Portugal and
Sangdong, South Korea with both mines currently undergoing production expansion projects.
Further development projects in Sangdong (molybdenum and tungsten oxide), additional
tungsten assets in Spain (Los Santos and Valtreixal), and the US tungsten asset (Gentung)
could contribute to additional production and tungsten refining capabilities longer-term. Key
investment highlights below:
• China's supply choke hold: Overshadowing the global tungsten market is China's control
of ~80% of global tungsten mine production in 2025. On top of this starting in 2025, China
introduced export controls, internal mining restrictions, and shifted to be a net importer so
far in 2026. As a result, APT tungsten prices are up ~775% since the start of 2025 and the
US has gained awareness of its reliance on China for tungsten in the military industrial base,
making it a poster child for critical materials.
• Persistently high tungsten pricing: We believe China’s restrictions on tungsten exports
have resulted in a structural shift in the global market and created a significant gap between
supply and demand that will keep prices elevated over the near-to-medium term. As a result
of the restrictions and slow-to-ramp friendly production alternatives, we believe APT prices
should normalize ~$2,000/mtu longer-term.
• Production ramp: Almonty has been investing in building out additional tungsten assets
since 2011 and now has multiple expansion projects coming online in the near-term. These
include the build out of Phase I and Phase II at Sangdong as well as the expected expansion
to L4 at Panasqueira. These projects will help the company ramp supply as the market needs
it the most.
• Offtake agreements: Current offtake agreements for both current tungsten production and
expected molybdenum production set a strong revenue base while uncontracted supply
allows the company to take advantage of the record high market APT prices. The company
also just announced a new contract for planned Los Santos tailings production as well.
• Long-term call options: On top of projects expected to ramp in the next two years, Almonty
is also developing additional tungsten assets and downstream refinement projects that we
do not include in our base model but can contribute to significant growth longer term. This
includes projects such as Gentung and the Korean molybdenum and oxide projects.

Key Investor Debates
Bear
Bull
I. Are current near-record high Tungsten APT prices ($2,925/MTU) sustainable?
No – The market cannot sustain prices at this level and demand will rebalance 
causing a collapse in price from the current level even if China remains a net 
importer.
Yes – China restricting exports and transitioning to a net importer has 
structurally changed the market and prices will remain elevated given no short-
term solutions to the supply/demand gap.
Stifel View: Bullish – structurally different market
China’s exit has structurally changed the market and flagged tungsten as a critical materials chokepoint globally. Even if China were to begin exporting material 
again, the DoW has already restricted use of Chinese tungsten starting in 2027 and other international customers are unlikely to turn back to China as a primary 
demand source.
II. Can Almonty deliver on its longer-term projects over the next 1-2 years?
No – The company has taken on too ambitious of an expansion plan and while 
some projects might ramp to production on schedule, more than one will be 
significantly delayed.
Yes – Almonty can ramp Sangdong Phase I + Phase II and Panasqueira L4 
fully by the end of 2027. Expect Korea Moly and Oxide refinement by 2028.
Stifel View: Confident (within reason)
Through the successful operation of Panasqueira and ramp of Sangdong ALM has proven its ability to execute on large scale mining projects. While we are 
aware that mining and materials projects often fall victim to uncontrollable delays, we believe that the company will be largely successful in ramping these projects 
with +/- 2Q of our current expectations.
III. Will elevated prices flood the market with new entrants and increase competition?
Yes – the need for western sourced tungsten (especially backed by defense 
demand) will result in significant capital flowing into the space and expedited 
permitting leading to a rapid supply ramp.
No – the capital intensity and permitting barrier of starting or restarting a 
tungsten mine will prevent any near-term influx of competition.
Stifel View: Neutral
We believe the global focus on tungsten as a critical material specifically for defense and tech will result in a significant uptick in project starts (and maybe 
expedited permitting), but the physical barriers of entry will prevent any rapid near-term ramp up in supply.

Tungsten is a refractory metal known for having the highest melting point (~3,500°C), extreme hardness, and exceptional 
density giving it a rare combination of toughness and heat resistance. These attributes along with the supply 
concentration within China have made it a critical material across the following end markets contributing to a CAGR of 
6.3% over the past five years (2020-2025).

Defense (11% of demand): Tungsten’s density and high melting point make it essentially irreplaceable across the 
industry with defense being the fastest growing end market for the material growing at a 5yr CAGR of 13.8%. 
Defense demand is supported by rising military budgets and allied stockpiling, tightened Chinese export controls, and 
Western sourcing rules (DoW ban on Chinese tungsten). Tungsten is used in kinetic-energy penetrators, 
fragmentation devices, and radiation shielding, as well as counterweights and rotating inertia members.

Mining (30% of demand): Cemented-carbide rock tools utilize tungsten’s hardness to improve functionality and 
increase tool life across items such as rock-drilling bits, button inserts, and wear parts. Demand tracks global drilling 
and mine extraction activity. The mining industry has been growing ahead of the overall market over the past five 
years at a CAGR of 9.6%.

Transport (24% of demand): Within transport, tungsten is primarily used in the automotive industry where it is used 
in tooling for engine and transmission parts, in wear components, and in batteries for EVs. For aerospace, Tungsten is 
used directly in turbine blades, engine components, and radiation shielding, and indirectly as the carbide tooling that 
machines aircraft superalloy and titanium parts. Transportation demand has grown slower than the overall market at a 
5yr CAGR of 3.3%.

Industrial (11% of demand): Tungsten carbide tools’ hardness and temperature resistance allow for greater abrasion 
resistance in manufacturing and tooling. Demand is driven by metalworking, construction, and general manufacturing 
activity. Tungsten Hexafluoride (WF₆) gas is also a key input in the production of semiconductors with demand driven 
by AI applications. General industrial use has been slower than the overall market over the past five years at a 3.2% 
CAGR.

Tungsten Pricing: China exit creating new market dynamic

At the start of 2025, China imposed export controls on Tungsten from intermediates to full heavy alloys, issued export 
licenses to only 15 companies, and set internal mining restrictions making the country a net importer so far in 2026.

As a result, ammonium paratungstate (APT) prices have increased ~775% since the start of 2025 to $2,925/mtu 
(Europe APT 88.5%)

Even if China were to alter their export policy, the DoW will no longer accept tungsten supplied from China starting in 
2027 making it a key investment area for the department. Further, China has proven to be an unreliable long-term 
source for tungsten meaning even if they cut back export restrictions most international customers would not willingly 
become dependent on the country’s supply again.

We would expect global tungsten prices to remain elevated over the near-term until supply ex-China begins to catch 
demand. We expect long-term APT prices to normalize around $2,000/mtu.

Offtake performance: Missing delivery volumes or concentrate specifications can trigger penalties, lower revenue, or 
termination of the supply agreement, and a buyer failing to take material hits revenue.

Currency mismatch: Concentrate sells in US dollars while Iberian costs are euro-denominated and Sangdong 
spending is in won, and results are reported in Canadian dollars, which moved between C$1.36 and C$1.46 per US 
dollar in 2025.

Reserve and resource estimates: Estimates lean partly on small-scale laboratory recoveries, may never convert to 
reserves, and a sustained price decline could shrink them and force asset write-downs.

Permitting and pipeline: Regulators keep broad discretion over permits, the Los Santos restart still depends on 
readiness and regulatory steps, and the oxide facility is pre-construction and not fully financed.

Trade and geopolitics: China supplies most of the market, so export controls, tariffs, or licensing shifts can swing 
pricing and counterparty behavior; Korean peninsula tension is an added exposure.

Dilution and controls: Warrants, options, RSUs, and convertibles can materially dilute holders, and management 
concluded internal control over financial reporting was not effective at year-end 2025.

Hold - We expect a total return between -5% and 10% over the next 12 months with total return equal to the percentage price change

Sell - We expect a total return below -5% over the next 12 months with total return equal to the percentage price change plus dividend yield.
