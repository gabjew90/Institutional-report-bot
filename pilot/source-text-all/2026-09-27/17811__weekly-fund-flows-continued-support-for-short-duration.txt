Global fund flows, week ending September 23 
Flows into mutual funds and related investment products turned negative for 
n
equities but remained positive for fixed income. 
Global equity funds saw net outflows in the week ending September 23 (-$10bn 
n
vs +$80bn in the previous week). Both DM and EM saw net outflows driven by US 
funds and China funds respectively. At the sector level, technology funds saw the 
largest net outflows followed by financial and industrial funds.  
Flows into global fixed income funds remained largely well-supported from 
n
inflows across fund type. Short-duration bond funds and inflation-protected 
bond funds saw continued inflows in the week ending September 23 while 
long-duration funds saw net outflows. More broadly, short duration funds have 
seen outsized inflows this year, relative to long-duration funds (see Chart of the 
Week). In EM, hard-currency bond funds saw net outflows while local-currency 
bond funds saw net inflows. Money market fund assets increased by $12bn. 
Cross-border FX flows were largely positive, and particularly well-supported 
n
across DM. USD and EUR saw the strongest net demand while INR drove the net 
EM outflows.
