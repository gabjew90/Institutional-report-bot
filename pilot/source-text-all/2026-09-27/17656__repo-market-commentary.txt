Market Sell-Offs
During periods of major market sell-offs, customers generally sell off-the-run securities to the Street. Since those
securities are illiquid in fast markets, dealers hedge their positions with on-the-runs. Shorts accumulate in the 
on-the-runs and therefore create more Specials in the Repo market. With the sell-off yesterday and today, we
should see more securities demand over the next few days. Though 2 Year and 5 Year Notes shorts are probably 
hiding in the WIs, we should see the short-base on Wednesday.
Scott E.D. Skyrm
