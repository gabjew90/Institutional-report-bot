Longevity weekly pulse #73: AZN’s pipeline 
setback and medical device weakness
Transformational Innovation Opportunities (TRIO): Longevity
 
Authors: Eric Potoker, CIO Equity Strategist, US Health care, UBS Financial Services Inc. (UBS FS); Diana Na, Healthcare Sector Equity Analyst, UBS AG 
London Branch; Thomas Parmentier, CIO Equity Sector Strategist - Consumers, UBS Switzerland AG
• AstraZeneca’s SERENA-4 failed to show a statistically

Thoughts of the week
AstraZeneca SERENA-4 disappointment shifts focus to 
AVANZAR
Last week, AstraZeneca announced that the Phase 3 
SERENA-4 trial failed to achieve a statistically significant 
improvement in progression-free survival (PFS) versus 
anastrozole when combined with palbociclib in front line
ER+/HER2- metastatic breast cancer. A numerical benefit 
was observed, and no new safety concerns emerged. 
This outcome mirrors Roche's earlier persevERA failure 
and suggests that replacing aromatase inhibitors with oral 
SERDs upfront in an endocrine-sensitive population may not 
generate a meaningful efficacy advantage.
That said, we view the financial and strategic implications as 
manageable. Investor expectations had already been reset 
following the Roche read-through, and consensus value 
for camizestrant is increasingly centered on the approved 
SERENA-6 ESR1-mutation switch indication and the larger 
adjuvant opportunities in the ongoing CAMBRIA program.
The failure also removes an overhang and leaves AVANZAR 
as AstraZeneca's next major pipeline catalyst. AVANZAR is 
evaluating Datroway (Dato-DXd) in front line non-small cell 
lung cancer, a considerably larger commercial opportunity. 
Management has recently sounded increasingly constructive 
about the trial. We think the delayed readout is potentially 
more consistent with ongoing efficacy than with a clearly 
negative outcome, while emerging data from related 
TROP2 ADC studies continue to support the class. A 
positive AVANZAR readout would help rebuild confidence 
in AstraZeneca's late-stage R&D execution following recent 
pipeline disappointments.
Bottom line: We see SERENA-4 as a disappointment, but not 
a thesis-changing event. Camizestrant's value increasingly 
rests on SERENA-6 and the CAMBRIA adjuvant studies, 
while AVANZAR is now the key near-term catalyst for 
AstraZeneca shares and a critical test of the company's 
broader pipeline narrative.
Stryker comments trigger sector weakness despite 
limited read-through
Medical device stocks were notably weak last week 
following comments from Stryker (SYK) at an investor 
conference. We think SYK's comments are largely company-
specific, with weakness continuing after the first-quarter 
cyberattack, which impaired SYK's ability to deliver certain 
products to customers.

At the conference, SYK noted continued weakness in its 
peripheral vascular business, citing ongoing production 
delays following the first-quarter cyberattack. It also cited 
increased seasonality in its orthopedic business, with 
procedure growth slowing in July and August after what it 
described as strong growth in June.
We see some rationale for heightened seasonality in 
orthopedic procedures this year. However, seasonality—
and the potential for a related year-end acceleration in 
surgical volumes—remains speculative until procedure data 
for September and subsequent months are available.
Overall, we think SYK continues to recover from the first-
quarter cyberattack, which is likely weighing on growth 
this quarter. We view any related drag as temporary and 
largely limited to SYK. We think SYK's growth should 
recover later this year or in 2027. And while we think the 
broader medical device sector is experiencing some end-
market deceleration, we think medical device shares and 
valuations have broadly overreacted in recent months. That 
said, a broader reacceleration will likely take well into 2027, 
leaving the sector without a near-term catalyst.

Risk Table
Company name
Risks
AIA Group
Risks include adverse government regulations across its key markets in Asia, a prolonged low interest rate environment, and disruption from non-traditional 
businesses.
Abbott Laboratories
Risks to our thesis include a sustained slowing in ABT’s end markets and competitive pressures on ABT’s market share. We note the litigation related to ABT's infant 
nutritional products and the initial related negative impact on ABT's market value. While this litigation is ongoing, we believe the liability will ultimately be 
manageable for ABT. ABT faces similar ESG issues to other medical device companies, including product liability risk. We believe ABT maintains the systems and 
protocols to adequately manage ESG issues.
AbbVie
Risks to our thesis include market share losses in ABBV’s core franchises and drug pipeline failures. ABBV faces similar ESG issues to its pharmaceutical peers, most 
notably product liability risk and a societal focus on drug pricing and access. We believe ABBV has the systems and protocols in place to manage these and other ESG 
issues.
Alcon
Key risks include failure of pipeline products; new or tougher competition; legal threats including product liability cases; access, reimbursement and pricing 
challenges; a shifting political and regulatory environment; currency and interest rates. Alcon-specific risks include inability to trade patients up to multifocal intra-
ocular lenses and further intangible asset impairments.
AstraZeneca
Key risks facing biopharma companies include access, reimbursement and pricing challenges, new competition including generic drugs, legal threats including 
product liability cases, a shifting political and regulatory environment, failure of pipeline products, currency, and interest rates. AstraZeneca-specific risks include: 1) a 
slowdown in the Chinese business (13% of revenues) driven by government investigations; 2) greater impact of US Part D redesign and IRA vs. peers given its US oral 
oncology portfolio (Calquence, Lynparza, Tagrisso); 3) more onerous volume-based procurement and related sales pressure; 4) new product launches particularly in 
oncology could also miss market expectations, driven by increased competition; 5) and greater-than-expected sales erosion following patent expirations.
Boston Scientific Corp.
 We think recent growth deceleration in certain key cardiology products has negatively affected sentiment, but that BSX's growth should stabilize over the next few 
quarters and its valuation should recover. From an ESG perspective, BSX faces similar issues to other medical device companies, including product liability and 
litigation risk. BSX has experienced material litigation liabilities in its recent past, which remains a key risk.
DBS Group Holdings
Key risks are a prolonged economic recession, higher-than-expected credit costs, a sharper-than-expected decline in interest rates, and asset quality deterioration.
Danaher Corporation
Risks to our thesis include potential budget pressure on governments and academic medical research spending, global trade tensions, and a potential decline in 
global pharma/biotech R&D spending. DHR faces similar ESG-related issues as other life sciences/tools companies, with modest product liability risks. We believe that 
DHR's emphasis on quality through its DBS culture enables the company to manage these issues.
DexCom Inc.
Risks to our thesis include reimbursement pressure and the eventual emergence of additional competition and/or alternative technologies. DXCM faces similar ESG 
issues to other medical device companies, notably product quality and access.
Eli Lilly & Co.
We think the main risks for LLY are pipeline failures. While we view gradual price erosion as in line with forecasts, more severe pricing pressure is a potential risk. LLY 
also faces various industry risks, including potential onerous regulatory/ legislative changes. LLY faces similar ESG risks to its biopharma peers, in particular potential 
product safety and access concerns.
Galderma Group AG
Key risks facing biopharma companies include access, reimbursement and pricing challenges, new competition including generic drugs, legal threats including 
product liability cases, a shifting political and regulatory environment, failure of pipeline products, currency, and interest rates. Galderma-specific risks include: 1) 
slower-than-expected launch of Nemluvio given US payer coverage hurdles from leading players in atopic dermatitis, and 2) an economic slowdown or out-of-pocket 
consumer pressures could also see a slowdown in the self-pay markets.
IQVIA Holdings Inc
The main risks to IQV are slowing R&D spending and onerous regulations. Key risks facing all healthcare companies include: failure of pipeline products; new 
competition; legal threats including product liability cases; access, reimbursement and pricing challenges; a shifting political and regulatory environment; and FX and 
interest rates. Slower pharma/biotech R&D spending and emerging competition in Asia are additional risks for IQVIA.
Innovent Biologics
Key risks include potentially worse-than-expected commercialization of key drugs.
Intuitive Surgical
Volatility in surgical procedure volumes could affect growth. In addition, hospital capital budgetary pressure could affect the short-term demand for ISRG's robots. 
Additionally, we note that surgical robots are a critical competitive differentiator for hospital systems, and we view any pressure on hospital capital budgets as 
temporary, likely resulting in negligible impact on ISRG's long-term growth.  ISRG is subject to the typical ESG issues facing medical technology companies, including 
potential product liability and litigation risk.
Johnson & Johnson
Risks to our thesis include pipeline failures, increasing policy risks, and larger-than-expected litigation liabilities, including from JNJ's ongoing talc litigation. JNJ's 
main ESG issue has been product litigation, specifically around its opioid and talc products. We acknowledge that JNJ has been more exposed to high-profile product 
liability cases than many of its peers in recent years. We believe these cases have informed JNJ's overall ESG approach in recent years and that JNJ is implementing 
systems to manage these and other ESG issues.
L'Oréal
Key risks: Unfavorable economic developments in key markets, weaker consumer confidence, rising competition, increased promotional intensity, unsuccessful 
innovation and M&A, changing beauty trends, and unfavorable FX effects.
Life Time Group
Risks to the thesis are fitness industry trends changing, fitness club competition, access to good quality real estate for growth, and a macro downturn.
Merck & Co.
Risks to our thesis include drug pipeline failures and potential onerous drug pricing legislation in the US. From an ESG perspective, MRK faces similar product liability 
and access risks faced by the entire pharmaceutical industry. We believe MRK maintains adequate systems and procedures to manage these and other ESG issues.
NN Group NV
Key risks include lower interest rates, large falls in equity markets, bonds or private markets, adverse claim experience in both the life and P&C divisions, adverse 
regulatory changes, and unit-linked litigations, adverse development in real estate, and increases in mortgage spreads, lower shareholder return due to lower capital 
ratios.
Novartis AG
Key risks facing biopharma companies include access, reimbursement, and pricing challenges; new competition including generic drugs; legal threats including 
product liability cases; a shifting political and regulatory environment; failure of pipeline products; and currency and interest rates. Novartis-specific risks include 1) 
worse commercial success for key product assets; 2) more aggressive generic erosion for Entresto and other drugs that are facing loss of exclusivity in the coming 
years; 3) price/volume competition for Cosentyx given competitor new launches; and 4) reduced expectations for cardiovascular launches (e.g., Leqvio and 
pelacarsen); 5) the inability for the company to reach its medium-term margin targets.
Novo Nordisk
Key risks facing biopharma companies include failure of pipeline products; new competition including generic drugs; legal threats including product liability cases; 
access, reimbursement and pricing challenges; a shifting political and regulatory environment; and currency and interest rates. Given Novo's reliance on GLP-1 drugs 
for growth, any slowdown in their prescription volumes, significant price cuts, or further supply challenges would be a risk to earnings forecasts. An additional risk is 
adverse clinical data for follow-on obesity drugs such as CagriSema.
Roche Holding AG
Key risks facing biopharma companies include access, reimbursement and pricing challenges, new competition including generic drugs, legal threats including 
product liability cases, a shifting political and regulatory environment, failure of pipeline products, currency, and interest rates. Roche-specific risks include: 1) lower 
market penetration for Vabysmo with Eylea 8mg competitor launch, 2) increased competition for Ocrevus from Kesimpta and BTK inhibitors in multiple sclerosis; 3) 
increased competition in HER2 (Herceptin, Perjeta, Kadcyla) from new entrants.
Sonova
Risks include possible negative consumer spending trends (about 70% of revenues are from out-of-pocket spend), supply chain issues, competition from over-the-
counter hearing aids or standard headphones, global trade restrictions, and currency losses due to its cost-overhang in CHF.
Stryker Corporation
Key risks include reimbursement challenges and competitive threats that could hurt its growth. In addition and similar to its peers, SYK faces potential litigation risk. 
Similar to its medical device peers, SYK faces potential product liability litigation risk. Beyond that, SYK faces ESG risks similar to other health care product 
companies. We believe SYK has the resources and processes to manage ESG dynamics.
Thermo Fisher Scientific
From an ESG perspective, we believe TMO faces similar issues to other LST companies, and lower product liability risk than medical device and biopharmaceutical 
companies, since most of TMO's products are used in research and not implanted into or ingested by humans. We believe TMO has the systems to effectively 
manage ESG issues.
UnitedHealth Group Inc.
As a leading health insurer, UNH faces significant regulatory oversight. In addition, UNH must manage the ongoing societal and media focus on affordable access to 
health care. Other risks to our view include higher medical costs; continued volatility in UNH's OptumHealth (OH) business, particularly its value-based care (VBC) 
assets; increased government regulation; and government reimbursement pressure.
Ventas Inc.
Risks include fluctuating rates, operating costs, rising new capacity, and a COVID resurgence.

Equity selection: An assessment relative to a benchmark
Equity selections in Equity Preferences lists (EPLs) are assessments made relative to a sector/industry, country/regional or 
thematic benchmark. The chosen benchmark is disclosed on the front page of each EPL. It is also used to measure the 
performance of the EPL owner(s). Including a stock in the EPL constitutes neither a view on its expected, standalone absolute 
performance nor a price target. Rather, EPLs are meant to support the UBS House View, with the stocks included in them 
selected for their superior risk/return profiles.
Our selection is based on an assessment of the company's fundamental outlook and valuation, the risks owning the stock 
entails, including material sustainability risks and the diversification benefits it provides in an investment portfolio, among 
many other factors. UBS GWM CIO‘s selection methodology enables wealth management clients to invest in a specific 
investment theme or focus on a sector/industry or country/region.
The lead strategist is responsible for the CIO view on a stock, while the EPL owner(s) is/are responsible for the stock selection 
within an EPL. Stocks can be selected for multiple EPLs. For consistency's sake, a stock can only be selected as either Most 
Preferred or Least Preferred, not both simultaneously. As EPL benchmarks differ, stocks do not need to be included on every 
list to which they could theoretically be added.
