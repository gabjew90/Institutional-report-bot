1) Tariffs - at one stage tis week, over dinner, I canvassed a room of 24 investors, raise your hand if you
think no tariff...zero hands, who thinks the stocks come out of CMX....again zero hands. I spoke to more
than one miner/trader, the types of people who have semi permanent access passes to the Whitehouse,
shock still ZERO HANDS. So thats what makes people bearish, how can Cu be at $14k, it cant be.....the
debunk is the c.1.5mt of "over-imports" to the US are in part why the China premium is so high. Its a
reallocation to this wharehouse v other "downstream" ones. A trader told me last week they thought end
Sept China Cu conc stocks at ports are lowest they had seen (this guy has traded longer than I have been
breathing), quoted c.550kwmt. Equally if scrap is right, then you getting another big chunk of the balance
accounted for.

2) Scrap - I have no edge, I can only lean on the feedback from my colleague, Trina Chen, that we have
lost 500k this year and capacity for additional 500k in 2027 (in absence policy change, but note its not a
copper policy, so take bigger consideration). One of the traders yday on panel, suggested 300kt lost, wont
ease untill 2H27 earliest, they model -150kt in 1H and add back 200kt in 2H (ie like Trina they see the
same run rate in 2027, numbers just differ). That 1H is also when mine supply typically finds it hards,
Super El nino equally a concern and hard to proof for.
3) Mine supply - this one we can litigate all day. The traders/bears. those scared of heights all see $14k and
quote a view that mine supply will recover in 2027 by 2.5-3.0% (GIR +2.3%), Interestingly GIR also have
refined metal prodn +2.7% y/y in 2027 (model Exh.17.. GIR Px upgrade you were at the conference, if
you spoke to those presenting (see also below), mining is not easy, mines will not be run in the red line,
Chile events these last few weeks elevate safety risks and safety is top priority. There have been
downgrades and there will be more. As one of the traders on panel, for 2025/6 the consensus estimates was
3mt of new supply adds, we got 750kt and it was all SXEW (acid aint cheap right now either). Concentrate
supply is down 2024-26 (-300kt), which means smelter output is also lower, (go back to CECN0002
Index) its why we have TC's at nosebleed. China refined will keep falling, 4Q26 by 3%, 1Q27 by 2%,
2Q27 by 2%....so look at Chile below, single biggest supplier globally on mine side, its been down c.9%
1H26 v 1H25, but it will recover, wont it? Unless you were in meetings i was not in, Codelco I beleive has
been in London, however the public stance is also value over volume and safety first. If you want thinking
about doing the math, i would keep in mind that prodn expectations are higher, yet cons capex
expectations are lower (capex/opex). The rtn on projects are lower, the IRR's dont compete with history,
we know the input line is going up, the value of existing prodn to producers goes up everyday, to those
"finacnical players" buying the equity it goes down. That circle does not square in a land that is being built
around grids and AI. As per the below, infrastructure deal will be done and i think M&A will ramp up.
I am unequivocally bullish. I am deeply realistic about the constraint around holding risk in this mkt, i just
think you gotta call a spade a spade and mine supply going up is utter fantasy form those wo cannot hold
their nose at $14k and given the opaque nature of tariff outcome and china scrap policy, they are praying
on the weakest thread of the argument to support prices lower.
