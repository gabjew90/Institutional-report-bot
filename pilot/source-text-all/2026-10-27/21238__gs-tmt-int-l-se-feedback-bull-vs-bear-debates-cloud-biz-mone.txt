GS TMT INT'L | SE Feedback (Bull vs Bear Debates): Cloud Biz,
Monee & MSCI Indonesia
Alexander Joseph
20 Oct 2026

Sharing feedback we have had on SE in recent days (Stock closed -9% Tuesday), with all focus
today around company entering the Cloud Business with Sea Cloud alongside the potential IPO
of their FinTech business Monee (per 6-K release)… Note: Flows on the desk today have been
two-ways (seller-skewed)…
Bulls argue that recent price action creates great opportunity to buy stock (Now <9x FY27
EV/EBITDA on street growing 25%+ EBITDA next two years) and that management have
tendency to be “visionary”…
Bears argue that company should remain focused on executing within e-comm business
(achieving 2-3% EBITDA/GMV target), allocate surplus cash towards the buyback and that
pursuing a scaled Cloud business would be a strategic pivot…  
As to questions on whether the magnitude of today’s move is justified or not? (Today
was the worst 1D move since the Q4 print in Mar)... Preface that a lot of investors were
constructive the name on a thesis of a clean, margin recovery/expansion story delivered via
achieving operative leverage into 2027, and that these headlines have now created some
pause…
Sea Cloud Debates
“Sea Cloud will be an AI-first enterprise platform, built by a team that has spent a decade
running infrastructure at enormous scale, under real commercial pressure, for some of
the most demanding consumer businesses in this region”… (per the 6K)
Feedback here has been understandably skewed negatively with focus of questions around the
required/necessary capital intensity for this business… I.e. There is a difference between the
company having excess compute capacity which they can lease externally via Sea cloud to
boost EPS… vs wanting to stand-up lit DC capacity & becoming a public cloud player like BABA
(with various puts/takes around margin profiles + competitive intensity of cloud in Asia)…  
There are also broader follow-on implications around what could/should be extrapolated from
this (Bull take: Is this a sign that management are confident with the current ROI of their SPX
Express (logistics) investments… vs Is this a strategic pivot as a result of changing view on
longer term profitability in e-comm?
Lastly, frustration that we are unlikely to receive any further clarity until the 3Q EPS earnings call
(expected Nov) as we head into news vacuum…

Indonesia MSCI Downgrade
While debate remains around whether the 3p data or the impact of proposed re-classification of
Indonesia from Emerging Market to Standalone was the bigger driver of yesterday’s price
action….
Thinking through the potential macro implications (outside of FX), Bulls argue that Shopee is
more staples>discretionary in nature and any potential growth slowdown will be insulated.. Vs
Take that there could be tighter credit environment in key market for Monee that affects
returns/credit quality. Sharing thoughts from our GIR strategy team on broader implication to the
local economy (Note):
Beyond direct passive outflows,  the reclassification could sustain a higher governance and
investability risk premium and generate second-round pressure through foreign selling,
currency weakness, and tighter domestic financial conditions…
Household credit growth has slowed to 5% yoy, while retail-sales growth remains around
1%, pointing to continued pressure on domestic earnings. Our economists expect Bank
Indonesia to deliver two additional 25bp rate hikes, in 4Q 2026 and 1Q 2027. While further
tightening could support the rupiah and external stability, it would likely weigh on growth..
Monee IPO
“At our IPO, our financial services business was still tiny compared to where it is today.
Since then, Monee has been running the same race we ran between 2009 and 2017–
building something out of nothing, and working to make it big enough and good enough
to go public…We believe Monee is approaching the point where it can stand on its own
two feet and begin its own race. Going public could give it the resources and the room to
go further and faster than it can today.” (per the 6K)
Lastly, this has been more the nuanced debate today and views have been both-sided (albeit
this has come arguably sooner than expected). Bulls argue that this would help unlock the
(Deep) SOTP discount for the business and there is an appetite for Fintech businesses in
South-East Asia (See Mynt IPO in Philippines o/n.. Recent GRAB/Atome deal in Sept etc..)
The negative feedback would be that harnessing Monee alongside Shopee data makes the
whole greater than the sum of the parts (Data/consumer insights etc) and that monetizing the
asset now feels near-term focused..

Share Price approaching $80/share resistance…
