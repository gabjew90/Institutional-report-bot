Prime Hedge Fund updates: Asia Fundamental L/S
MTD Drawdown reaches -9.3% as Tech Route
Deepens
The Global Tech sell off deepened yesterday, translating into widespread performance drag across Hedge Fund
Strategies globally.
 
Hedge Fund Performance (As of COB Oct 22)
• Asia Fundamental L/S managers experienced a -2.2% drawdown yesterday with MTD drawdown reaching
-9.3%. YTD returns are now at 14.2%.
• Dispersion remains wide with median YTD performance now at low single digits.
• Drawdown from the June peak of 40% is now more than 25%.
• Beta and asset selection alpha remain the main drivers of the MTD drawdown, followed by impacts from
Volatility and Medium-Term Momentum.
• Global Fundamental L/S managers were down 1.4% yesterday, with MTD down 3.8% and YTD now at 8.8%.
Global Systematic L/S managers sustained a 1.3% drawdown yesterday, but are still up 19% YTD. More
details here (h/t Kartik Singhal).
 
Positioning and Flows (As of COB Oct 21)
• After heavy selling last week, the region saw an uptick in WTD flows until Wednesday with short covers
exceeding long sales in a ratio of 1.9 to 1. 
• WTD buying was led by an uptick in flows in Korea (Short Covers), Hong Kong (Long buys) and Taiwan
(Short covers); partially offset by selling in China (Long sales > short covers). 
• China led the de-grossing WTD (A-shares), followed by Korea and Taiwan; partially offset by risk-on
flows in Hong Kong and Japan.  
 
• The region remains net sold MTD, led by Taiwan, Korea, Japan and China; partially offset by net buying in
Australia and Hong Kong. Flows marginally risk-on with short sales exceeding long sales.
• Info Tech sector dominated the net selling, followed by relatively smaller contributions from Industrials
and Financials.
 

• Allocations to the region are down MTD but still remain significantly overweight vs. the benchmarks.
• Net allocation to Asia is down 295 bps MTD, now at 27.25%, still representing a 10.8% overweight vs.
MSCI world AC. 
• Gross allocation is down 141 bps MTD, with the latest level at 24.4% / 7.9% overweight vs. the
benchmark index.
