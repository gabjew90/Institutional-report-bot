DEUTSCHE BOERSE: EBITDA 3% ahead of company-compiled consensus. The beat was revenue driven, with net
revenue 2% ahead of expectations and operating expenses broadly in line. The revenue beat was driven by Fund
Services (+4%) and Trading & Clearing (+4%/+3% vs CC/VA cons), while Investment Management Solutions (IMS)
came in slightly ahead (+1%) and Securities Services was slightly weaker (-1%). Link

 
Essilor: Q3 26 results with +7.2% revenue growth vs cons +6.2% (North America, EMEA and APAC all ahead). We lift
our adjusted EPS estimates by +2-3% in FY26-28e; that is primarily driven by (1) modest increases to our topline
growth assumption for FY26e (to +6.9% from +6.5% previously), and (2) lifting our assumption for adjusted EBIT
margin in FY26 by +30bps due to smaller negative impact from mix and small headwind from FX on margin. Link
 
IAG: We're reducing our 2026EBIT estimate by 6% to €4.3bn, in line with VA consensus, reflecting higher jetfuel costs.

costs. For 2027, our EBIT estimate falls by 13% to €4.29bn, 12% below consensus on significantly higher fuel costs.

Adidas: Key - the upgraded €2.5bn FY26 EBIT guidance is +4.5% above the €2.39bn consensus. Adidas pre-
announced its 3Q26 results with revenues growing +9% yoy to €7,252mn representing cFX growth of +7% yoy
(company compiled consensus €7,150mn, cFX +7% yoy). During 3Q Adidas received the majority of its US tariff
claims representing a refund of c.€200mn, not included in consensus. Including this refund, 3Q gross margin
increased +250bps yoy to 54.3% (51.5% ex refund; consensus 51.3%) driven positively by tariff refund and healthy
full-price sales, albeit unfavourable FX developments had a negative impact. EBIT increased +37% yoy to €1,008mn
(€808mn ex refund; consensus €742mn) representing an EBIT margin of 13.9% (11.1% ex refund; consensus 10.5%).
