October 25
According to GS, Brazil's presidential runoff has tightened considerably. Flávio Bolsonaro's initial 5–6
point lead has narrowed to roughly 51% versus Lula's 49%, within most polls' margins of error. The
October 23 Globo debate is the final major catalyst ahead of the October 25 vote.

Selling the election panic
Brazil's election uncertainty has pushed VXEWZ towards 65. With the November EWZ $43 straddle
trading around $6, election volatility is commanding a substantial premium. Selling volatility, including
covered calls against existing longs, is one way to express a view that the market is overpricing the
eventual move. The obvious risk: a major post-election gap.

Brazil and commodities
Election headlines dominate, but Brazil remains a major commodity trade. The divergence between
EWZ and the Bloomberg Commodity Index remains unusually wide. Once the political dust settles, that
relationship could become increasingly relevant.
