Valuation
Our AGI price target of US$42.00 is based on 1.1x our risk-
adjusted NAV at a long-term gold price of US$4,000/oz and
12x our 2026–28 SFCF estimate, which would be equivalent to
6x EBITDA. These multiples are in line with the intermediate
producer group, reflecting the company’s favourable financial
positioning, North American operating focus, organic growth
outlook, partially offset by more recent operating risks and
execution. Our Outperform rating is supported by AGI’s
implied return to our price target relative to its peers, while
accounting for its company-specific risks.
Investment summary
Alamos Gold is a Canadian-based intermediate gold producer
with diversified production from 3 operating assets in North
America; the Island Gold District and Young-Davidson in
Ontario, and the Mulatos District in Mexico, complemented
by a fully funded development project in Manitoba—Lynn
Lake. 2026 production guidance is 510-560koz (prev. 570–
650koz) at TCC of $1,175-1,275/oz (prev. $1,020–1,120/oz)
and AISC of $1,775-1,875/oz (prev. $1,500–1,600/oz). Longer-
term guidance is 650–730koz in 2027 and 755–835koz in 2028,
with visibility to ~1moz by 2030, representing +65% growth
from 2026 levels.
Investment outlook
• Alamos has been more active on buybacks alongside its
$0.04/sh quarterly dividend, with further dividend growth
and opportunistic repurchases expected as FCF inflects
higher post-2027.
• The Island Gold District remains a core growth piece — shaft
commissioning in early 2027, ramping to 3ktpd by 2029,
with the broader IGD expansion to 20ktpd in early 2028
targeting ~534koz/yr over 10 years.
• At Mulatos, the new sulphide mill is on schedule for
mid-2027 first production, extending mine life >9 years. Lynn
Lake construction began 1Q26, targeting first production
1H29 and 186koz/yr over a 25-year mine life.
• Our Outperform rating reflects AGI's organic growth profile
targeting ~1moz by 2030, a primarily Canadian operating
base, partially offset by more recent operating and
execution risks.
Upcoming potential catalysts and milestones
• Island Gold District Phase 3+ expansion completion (YE26),
new shaft commissioning (early-2027), ramp up to 2.4ktpd
(early-2027), and ramp up to 3.0ktpd (2029).
• Young-Davidson updated mine plan + 3Y guidance (YE26)
• Mulatos District PDA first production (mid-2027)
• Island West Zone resource growth / potential mine plan
inclusion (2029)
• Lynn Lake build milestones (2H26); first production (1H29)
Risks to rating and price target
In addition to general operating and financial risks for AGI
related to commodities (gold) and FX (USD, CAD, MXP), we
highlight:
• Achievement of ramp-up and production targets at Island
Gold, PDA and YD
• Achievement of permitting and construction projections at
Lynn Lake
• M&A risk given growth-oriented nature of business model

Valuation

at a long-term gold price of US$4,000/oz and 12x our 2026–

Investment summary
AngloGold Ashanti is a senior gold producer with a globally
diversified portfolio of precious metals assets with 2026
production guidance of 2.80-3.17moz, TCC of $1,315-1,430/
oz, AISC of $1,780-1,990/oz and capex of $1.83-1.98b.
Investment outlook
• Development of AU's Nevada portfolio could provide
meaningful growth, with North Bullfrog targeting initial
production in 2028/29 and the larger Arthur Gold project
(formerly Silicon-Merlin) potentially contributing in the
early 2030s. Quebradona in Colombia offers additional
longer-term optionality.
• AU introduced a new capital returns framework in 1Q25.
Under this framework, 50% of FCF is paid as a quarterly
dividend, and the company has instituted a minimum $0.50/
sh dividend annually, paid quarterly.
• Our Outperform rating reflects AU's implied return to our
price target relative to peers, with valuation multiples
slightly below the large producer average given above-
average geopolitical risk exposure.

Valuation
We derive our C$62.00 price target through a 50/50 weighted
valuation of 1.25x P/NAV and 12x 3-year average sFCF, above
mid-cap peers on a NAV-basis, reflecting Blackwater’s Tier 1
asset quality, growth through an expanded Phase 2 expansion,
and to capture the scarcity value of a large-scale and high-
margin Canadian asset. Our NAV uses an 8% discount rate and
a $4,000/oz long-term gold price. Our Outperform rating is
driven by the implied return to our price target and supported
by growth upside potential, further exploration potential, and
M&A appeal in a safe jurisdiction.
Investment summary
Our Outperform rating is based on Artemis Gold’s potential
for scalable production at peer-leading margins within
Canada, which warrants a premium valuation reflective of
the scarcity value, in our view.
A Canadian project with scale. In our view, the Blackwater
project stands out amongst peers as one of Canada’s largest
and lowest-cost gold producers with total gold resource of
12.0 Moz (M&I). We estimate production inflecting from the
current Phase 1 300koz run-rate to >500koz by YE 2028 at
mine-site AISC of ~$1,100/oz over a 20-year life, positioning
Artemis for significant scale as the next Canadian-focused
Intermediate producer.
A potential M&A target given focus on Canadian assets. In
our view, Artemis could be an attractive M&A target given
scarcity of large-scale, low-cost assets with growth optionality
in mining-friendly jurisdictions. Given industry consolidation
and depleting reserve lives, we believe Blackwater stands out
as an asset that could be attractive for a larger producer.
Potential catalysts
• Blackwater ramp-up to nameplate production and Phase 1
optimization progress (ongoing)
• Phase 2 expansion progress (full-scale construction
beginning in 2H26, first production by YE 2028)
• Exploration results from initial regional program and at
depth exploration (2026)
Risks to rating and price target
• Commodity risk: Gold price fluctuations could significantly
impact Blackwater project revenue and profitability.
• Capex/opex inflation risk: Rising project development and
operating costs, combined with execution risks on future
Blackwater phases, could lead to cost overruns or delays.
• Operating and single-asset risks: Equipment failures,
labour shortages, or other production-related challenges at
Blackwater could disrupt production and increase costs.
• Litigation risk: Sedgman (former EPC contractor for
Blackwater) claims a lien against the project, plus
damages, costs, and interest for breach of contract alleging
the company’s actions, including site access, personnel
removal, and mismanagement, caused project delays and
lost compensation. Artemis filed a counterclaim seeking
recovery of losses and damages incurred by the company.
• Weather and climate risk: Blackwater is in an area of British
Columbia prone to frequent wildfires, which can disrupt
operations, while winter conditions could present minor
operational challenges.

Valuation
Our price target of US$53.00 is based on 1.1x our NAV8%
at long-term gold prices of US$4,000/oz and 13x our 2026–
28 Sustaining FCF estimate, equivalent to 6x EBITDA. These
multiples are below the average multiples we use to value
the large gold producer peers, reflecting Barrick's high-quality
asset base, but also above-average geopolitical risk exposure.
Our Outperform rating is supported by the implied return to
our price target relative to its peers, while accounting for its
company-specific risks.
Investment summary
Barrick Mining is a senior gold producer with a globally
diversified portfolio of gold and copper assets. Guidance for
2026 includes gold production of 2.90–3.25moz at AISC of
$1,760–$1,950/oz, and copper guidance is 190–220kt at AISC
of $3.45–$3.75/lb.
Investment outlook
• Barrick has shifted from a FCF/share focus to a more growth
oriented outlook, advancing the Reko Diq and Lumwana
copper projects, plus Fourmile where the company has
guided to material resource upside over time.
• Barrick is targeting a 4Q26 IPO of its North American
portfolio with a primary NYSE and secondary TSX listing.
RBC's SOTP analysis suggests minimal value is currently
ascribed beyond the NA portfolio, with 20–40% relative
upside at spot based on comparable multiples.
• At Reko Diq, Barrick disclosed a 12-month project review
through July 2027 following security risks, potentially
pushing first production from late 2028 to beyond 2029. The
current valuation largely reflects this risk; a delay or strategic
decision could improve interim FCF and investor perceptions
of geopolitical exposure.
• Our Outperform rating is driven by supportive near-term
catalysts and a long-term growth profile, at a discounted
valuation relative to the large producer group.
Upcoming potential catalysts and milestones
• Fourmile initial portal development (late 2026), and BFS
(2029)
• PV plant ramp-up (2026), tailings permits (1H26), expanded
tailings commissioning (4Q29)
• Reko Diq project review (2026/27), potential production
(2029+)
• Lumwana superpit expansion first production (2028)
• Goldrush commercial production (2026), and steady-state
production of +400koz (2028)
• Cortez/Robertson initial production (2027)
Risks to rating and price target
In addition to general operating and financial risks for Barrick
related to commodities (gold and copper) and FX (CAD, CLP,
EUR), we highlight:
• Achievement of guidance and 5Y targets, given track record
and Loulo's suspension
• Political risk exposure to Tanzania, DRC, Mali, Pakistan, the
Dominican Republic, Argentina, Chile, and PNG
• Increasing capital requirements to deliver growth and fund
new development projects

Valuation
Our price target of US$14.00 is based on 1.1x our NAV8% at
long-term gold prices of US$4,000/oz and 12x our 2026-28
SFCF estimate, equivalent to 5x EBITDA.
Our Outperform rating is based on accelerating production/
FCF growth, rapid deleveraging, and organic growth
optionality. The implied return to our price target supports our
Outperform rating.
Investment summary
EQX-OLA is a high leverage name with a peer-leading
organic growth pipeline. The combined company offers
visible production growth underpinned by ~70% Canada/
US exposure. The transaction centres on a complementary
FCF profile: OLA delivers front-loaded cash flow while EQX's
growth capex is elevated, effectively self-funding the long-
term development pipeline (Valentine II, South Railroad,
Castle Mountain, Los Filos, Camino Rojo sulphides). 2026
production guidance of 870-920koz, cash costs $1,600-
$1,700/oz, and AISC of $1,900-$2,000/oz. Growth capex:
$600-650m.
Investment Outlook
• Greenstone and Valentine represent near-term Canadian
production growth as each ramps up toward steady-
state, with Greenstone targeting >27ktpd throughput and
Valentine advancing a Phase 2 expansion to ~5mtpa that
could double the output.
• The combined pro forma pipeline provides a pathway to
~1.9moz from internally funded North American projects,
and includes Valentine Phase 2, South Railroad, Castle
Mountain, Los Filos, and Camino Rojo sulphides.
• EQX offers high relative leverage to gold among peers, which
creates outsized upside in a rising price environment but
also disproportionate downside in a correction. The capital
structure remains complex, with convertible debt, warrants,
gold payables, and streams that are materially dilutive.
• Our Outperform rating is based on accelerating production,
further deleveraging, and a valuation that remains
discounted to peers despite the improved growth profile.

• Greenstone throughput above nameplate; trommel install
(YE26), targeting ~30ktpd in 2027
• Valentine Phase 2 full funds approved, early site works
(2H26), ramp-up (2H28)
• Castle Mountain updated FS (2H26), Final EIS/ROD (4Q26),
FID (1H27)
• Los Filos restart commencing (late-3Q/4Q26)

Risks to rating and price target
• Execution risk: The company is simultaneously ramping
two core assets (Greenstone, Valentine) while advancing
multiple development projects.
• Gold leverage: EQX-OLA carries high leverage to gold in the
peer group; in a meaningful correction, valuation would be
disproportionately impacted, with business risks emerging
at ~$3,000/oz.

Investment summary
Our Outperform rating on K92 shares is based on attractive
growth potential, asset quality, and exploration upside vs.
comparable peers. K92 is a Growth producer operating the
Kainantu mine in Papua New Guinea.
Stage 3 & 4 expansions to bring K92 to the next level. K92 has
outlined a path to phased production growth from 174koz in
2025 to >400koz post-Stage 4 via ramp up of the new 1.2 Mtpa
mill (Stage 3) and incremental expansions to the mill (Stage 4).
The expansion is to be funded by current operations.
Significant exploration optionality. Kainantu represents one
of the highest-grade mines within the top 5% globally. The
significant land package held by K92 (~830 km²) has high
prospectivity and a history of resource additions. Discovery
of high-grade dilatant zones has shown potential for greater
ore tonnage per vertical metre. The Blue Lake target also
demonstrates potential for larger-scale copper-gold porphyry
opportunities across the property.
Potential catalysts
• Ramp-up of the new Stage 3 mill (ongoing)
• Increasing underground development rates to the Stage 3
and 4 run-rate (ongoing)
• Arakompa maiden resource (2H26)
• Exploration results across the property (ongoing)
Risks to rating and price target
• Geopolitical risk: Operating in PNG is more volatile than
other jurisdictions. Current operations sit on Mining Lease
150 (ML150), which was renewed in 2022 through 2034.
Mining legislation changes in PNG could introduce headline
risk, but Kainantu appears relatively insulated.
• K92 has operated well in PNG to date, but risks remain:
While the regulatory stability clause protects Kainantu’s
operations on ML150 through 2034, K92’s exploration
licenses outside the current producing footprint pose
potential risks. Converting these licenses into mining leases
could face challenges, including permitting hurdles or state
intervention to capture a greater share of economics
through equity stakes or the more punitive royalty structure.
• Commodity risk: Related to gold/copper price. Near-term
downside risk is mitigated through 120koz covered under
put options at a floor of $3,000/oz.
• Development and capex risk: Related to construction and
ramp-up of new Stage 3 mill and eventual Stage 4 expansion.
• Single-asset risk: Related to operations at Kainantu, with
cash flow to internally fund the Stage 3 & 4 expansions.
• Resource risk: We model 75% M&I+I resources in our
Kainantu model based on successful resource conversion.

Valuation
Our price target of US$39.00 is based on 1.1x our NAV8%
at a long-term gold price of US$4,000/oz and 12x our 2026–
28 SFCF estimates, or an implied 7x EBITDA. These multiples
are average for the large producer group, supported by the
company’s lower average reserve life. Our Outperform rating
is supported by KGC’s implied return to our price target
relative to its peers, while accounting for its company-specific
risks.
Investment summary
Kinross is a senior gold producer with a portfolio of operating
mines and projects in the US (Nevada and Alaska), Canada,
Mauritania, Brazil, and Chile. 2026 guidance (±5%) outlines
production of 2.0mGEO, AuEq cash costs of $1,360/oz, AuEq
AISC of $1,730/oz, and capex of $1.5b. Long-term production
guidance (2027–28) is 2.0mGEO.
Investment outlook
• Near-term production visibility has improved with mine life
extensions at Round Mountain Phase X, Bald Mountain
Redbird II, and Curlew, while Great Bear (potential
production YE29) and Lobo Marte/Maricunga (early-to-mid
2030s) could offset declines after 2031.
• KGC has committed to returning 40% of FCF to shareholders
in 2026 via buybacks and dividends, with authorization to
reduce shares outstanding by 10%.
• Post the Great Bear acquisition, Kinross maintains below-
average geopolitical risk exposure, with above-average risk
concentrated in Mauritania and Brazil.
• Our Outperform rating reflects KGC's attractive near-term
operating valuation and high FCF, slightly offset by longer-
term production risk.
Upcoming potential catalysts and milestones
• Round Mountain Phase S ramp-up (2026), and Phase X initial
production (2028)
• Great Bear construction (2026), and initial production
(2029)
• Bald Mountain Redbird II development (2026), initial
production (2028)
• Curlew state-level tailings permit (2026), initial production
(2028)
• Lobo Marte potential construction (2026+), and first
production (2028+)
Risks to rating and price target
In addition to general operating and financial risks for KGC
related to commodities (gold and silver) and FX (USD, CAD,
BRL, EUR, CLP), we highlight:
• Political risk, including exposure to Mauritania, Brazil, and
Chile
• Cost inflation related to rising input costs (steel, power, fuel,
and labour)
• Expansion project ramp-up risks
• Reserve replacement risk
