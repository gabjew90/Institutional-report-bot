## ECB - FCI Tightening / UK Budget Preview / Sap / Maersk (Dg) /

Ioannis Blekos
22 Oct 2026
European Daily: ECB—Gauging the Recent Tightening in Financial Conditions
 
• We show that tighter financial conditions slow growth consistently across the three FCIs. 
• Our estimates suggest that the recent tightening of conditions could subtract 0.1-0.5% from real GDP across the
indicators, with a 0.2% drag implied by our FCI. The analysis thus supports recent ECB commentary suggesting
that tighter financial conditions could substitute for some ECB policy tightening.
 
European Daily: UK—Autumn Budget Preview
 
• Economic forecast changes will likely lower the Chancellor’s fiscal headroom from £24bn to around £15bn before
new policies at next week’s Autumn Budget, as a stronger wage projection only partly offsets higher rates and
weaker growth estimates.
• Tax rises are likely, but we expect the magnitude of the changes to be notably smaller than in 2024 and 2025 at
around £5bn. On the other side of the ledger, the Chancellor will likely unveil further cost-of-living measures, make
modest changes to departmental spending to fund the Defence Investment Plan, and increase financial
investments.
• We expect the OBR’s updated public sector net borrowing projections to be revised up by around £12bn per year
on average over the forecast period, with the higher projected deficit leaving post-measures headroom at around
£16bn.
 
SAP (SAPG.DE): 3Q26 First take: All KPIs ahead with strong CCB growth; FY26 topline guide towards top-end while
EBIT unchanged
 
• CCB growth came in robust at 26% yoy ex-fx (vs GSe/Visible Alpha Consensus Data at 23.8%/24.6% yoy ex-fx),
beating elevated investor expectations heading into the print. 
• We estimate that, on an organic basis, CCB landed at c.25.2% yoy ex-fx (c.80bps inorganic contribution from
Reltio, Dremio and Prior Labs), also ahead of GSe at 23.0% yoy organic ex-fx. We note that the organic CCB
growth at c.25% has seen no deceleration for the past 3 quarters, underscoring strength in the SAP S/4HANA
migration cycle
A.P. Moeller-Maersk (MAERSKb.CO) - As good as it gets for the container market; down to Sell
 
• Current trading for shipping companies including Maersk remains strong. 
• As we noted in July and September, we expect consensus estimate revisions to remain positive for the next couple
of quarters, given the current levels of freight rates (our 3Q26/4Q26/1Q27 EBITDA estimates are 8%/35%/34%
ahead of Visible Alpha Consensus Data). 

• That said, as we argued in our September note, much of the current tightness in the container market has been
driven by temporary factors: subdued supply growth in 2026, geopolitical and climate-related disruption and US re-
stocking. We believe the impact of all three will likely now start to fade.
 
EUROPE RETAIL - Retail Matters: On continues to outperform in a promotional sportswear market; Chinese
Aggregator European MAUs fall further while online data remains broadly supportive for Zalando and Next
 
• We highlight Buy ratings on Inditex, Next, On, Zalando; Sell rated on H&M.
• Sportswear NPI improves, with On's momentum accelerating
• Chinese Aggregator European MAUs decline further
• H&M's US observed sales inflect positively, albeit remain below Inditex's
 
Iberdrola SA (IBE.MC) - Double-digit earnings growth to 2031, with ongoing upside risk to consensus forecasts
 
• Iberdrola introduced new guidance targeting more than 10% Net Profit growth p.a. until 2030. Achieving this could
imply +c.15% consensus upgrades by then. 
• Growth would be supported by organic investments: management disclosed +60% cumulative expected growth in
RAB and renewable capacity additions of, potentially, +c.3 GW p.a. 
• We forecast double-digit growth to continue until 2031, when we see EPS at €1.69 (implied P/E c.12.5x). Double-
digit growth, limited value at risk compared to many other industries and a robust outlook for the power industry are
likely to support the stock. We remain Buy rated (PT unchanged at €25/sh).
 
Puig Brands S.A. (PUIGb.MC) - Q3 beat supports FY26 expectations; CMD the next re-rating catalyst; Buy (on CL)
 
• Puig delivered Q3 organic sales growth of +5.6%, comfortably ahead of +4.8% Visible Alpha Consensus. The
composition of the beat differed materially from expectations though. 
• Makeup outperformed, growing +16.1% versus +2.8% consensus, implying a sharp acceleration on a two-year
stack as sell-out continued to outpace the market
