Model update post 3Q26
Our view: EL has delivered a reassuring 3Q26 print in our view with healthy
+7.2% cFX revenue growth which is broad based across Wearables (+18%
cFX) and Core (+6.5% cFX), as well as across channels and regions. 4Q
comps remain a near term hurdle after which we believe the setup for EL
is compelling in the context of defensive and predictable mid-single digit
revenue growth and potential for upside to mid-term implied stable margin
outlook supported by improving wearables margins, leverage and more
favourable FX. We increase FY27E estimates by 1% on FX, retain €190 PT
and re-iterate Outperform rating.
Key points:
Conference call highlights - 1) October current trading broadly in-line
for Core business, 2) France/Milan protests - no material impact to the
business, despite some closures and some limited damage, 3) Stellest
US ramp is going to plan with 12,000 wholesale door distribution and
long term potential (50m myopic children wearing single vision lenses). 4)
Wearables lens add-on - Transitions has reached 60% of DTC frame sales
and Rx penetration is 1/3.
Change in estimates - We maintain our FY26E forecasts, and updating for
spot FX results in a 1% increase to FY27E revenues/EPS. DCF derived price
target unchanged at €190 which offers sufficient upside to support our
Outperform rating. The implied FY27E P/E valuation at our price target is
25x which is slightly below its 10yr historic average.
3Q26 results recap (note) - Revenues of €7.49bn are 2% ahead of
consensus with +7.2% cFX revenue growth (vs +6.2% consensus), and all
regions and channels positive and slightly better than expected. Wearables
revenues broken out for first time at €486m (+18% cFX yoy) slightly above
our expectations, whilst Core business revenues of €7.0bn delivered solid
+6.5% cFX growth.
Attractive valuation at 20x FTM P/E, 15x EV/EBIT and 2.3x EV/sales all
of which are more than 1-sd below 10yr historic average. With the
core business maintaining a revenue growth pace at the upper end of
'mid single digit' and FX turning more favourable, we view EL shares as
attractively valued and offering defensive core attributes in a challenging
consumer environment. We believe forward-looking earnings growth and
FCF potential remain compelling, with almost free optionality on AI
Smartglasses potential.

3Q26 results recap 
EssilorLuxottica has delivered 3Q26 revenues of €7.49bn, 2% above consensus on a 
reported basis and slightly stronger on a constant currency growth basis (+7.2% cFX vs 
+6.2% consensus). All regions and channels delivered results slightly ahead of consensus. 
Wearables (AI Smartglass + Nuance) revenues were broken down for the first time with 
€486m in 3Q26 (+18% cFX RBCe) slightly above our expectations. Eyecare + Medtech 
revenues of €7.00bn (+6.5% cFX) confirm healthy underlying core business momentum in our 
view. 
By channel: 
DTC revenues of €4.09bn (10.6% organic) were 2% ahead of consensus (+9.6% cFX). The core 
business maintained growth in high-single-digits whilst comparable store sales grew +6.8%. 
High-single-digit growth in N America, EMEA and Latin America. 
Professional Solutions revenues of €3.39bn (+3.4% organic) were also 2% ahead of 
consensus. APAC was the primary growth engine with double-digit growth. Latin America 
grew mid-single digit, North America low-single digit and EMEA slightly positive. 
By region: 
North America (+4.7% cFX) was 1ppt ahead of consensus. Professional Solutions recorded 
low-single-digit revenue growth and DTC delivered solid high-single-digit growth. 
EMEA (+7.5% cFX) was 1ppt ahead of consensus. Professional Solutions was slightly positive 
and DTC maintained double-digit growth. 
APAC (+15.7% cFX) was 2ppt ahead of consensus. Professional solutions delivered double-
digit growth in Gr China whilst DTC comparable-store sales grew low-single digit. 
LatAm (+7.1% cFX) was broadly in-line with consensus in organic terms and 7ppt ahead in 
reported terms due to more positive FX.

Investment summary
We rate EssilorLuxottica (EL) shares Outperform with a €190
price target.
EssilorLuxottica is a high-quality, industry-leading eyewear
conglomerate which is a fairly unique asset given its scale
advantage vs peers. It offers a balanced cyclical and defensive
category positioning in our view, with healthy 45% N America
revenue exposure.
We note multiple sources of growth across its operations
including its core Optical (75% revenue), recently acquired
Ophthalmology businesses which are well-placed to benefit
from ageing populations and increasing incidence of eye
disease longer term.
Product innovation is driving new category and TAM
expansion, particularly in Wearables - with broader awareness
and adoption of Smartglasses (Ray-Ban Meta) and recently
launched Nuance (combined hearing aid and eyeglass

ASP uplift and improved clinical outcomes for children with
myopia.

and ability to scale margins remains less clear. Valuation de-
rating to (CY27E) 19x P/E, 16x EV/EBIT and 2.6x EV/sales,
where multiples are in line or below long term historic
averages, offers an attractive entry opportunity in our view.

Downside risks: (1) lower-than-guided/expected synergy
delivery; (2) ASP declines driven by consumer trade-down,
and lengthening replacement cycles in prescription; (3)
normalising Stellest momentum in China; (4) 19-20% EBIT mid-
term target not achieved; (5) technological disruption in the
eyewear category.

Company description
EssilorLuxottica (EL) is a vertically integrated eyewear conglomerate, offering Optical (75% mix) and Sun (25% mix) eyewear
products at its core. More recently, it has developed new Wearables products (e.g. Ray-Ban Meta, Nuance) and entered into certain
Ophthalmology segments including Diagnostics and Imaging.
In FY25, EL generated €28bn revenues, €4.5bn EBIT (16% margin) and €2.8bn FCF.
From a category perspective, EL generates around 50% of revenues in optical lenses, 35% in Frames, 7% in Contact Lenses and 8%
in Other. From a channel perspective, EL has a broadly balanced DTC vs wholesale split. From a regional perspective, N America
constitutes 45% of revenues, EMEA 38%, APAC 12% and Lat-Am 5%.
EL operates 17,500 retail stores globally, split across Optical and Sun under various banners including Lenscrafters, Vision Express
and Sunglass Hut. It has a global presence in terms of manufacturing, prescription laboratories and distribution centres. EL has an
in-house brand portfolio including Ray-Ban, Oakley amongst others and a licensed brand portfolio of 50+ which includes Giorgio
Armani, Prada, Chanel and Moncler amongst others.
