COMMODITY VIEWS - Conflict, Climate and the Case for Commodity Diversification
 
• We remain concerned that geopolitical escalation—such as intensified shipping attacks or damage to energy
infrastructure—and, for European natural gas, a cold winter could push energy prices significantly higher.
• Since the Iran war began, equity—and especially bond—prices have become unusually sensitive to energy prices.
This increased sensitivity likely reflects resilient global GDP growth and five years of above-target inflation, which
have prompted central banks to respond to the inflationary impact of higher energy prices.
• The Iran and Russia-Ukraine wars, a high probability of a weather-disrupting Super El Niño, and potential food
export restrictions create upside risks to agricultural prices.
 
EUROPE BRANDED CONSUMER GOODS - Is size a barrier to growth? Revisiting scale in Luxury
 
• Since the publication of our initiation, a recurring theme in conversations with investors has been the perceived
growth limitations facing the largest players in the luxury sector, particularly LVMH (Louis Vuitton brand) and
Hermès. 
• While size is often viewed as a constraint, in our initiation we argued that scale can instead represent a powerful
competitive advantage, and highlighted this as a key component of our 7Cs framework. We examined this debate
in greater depth, including a closer look at why Chanel was able to deliver strong growth in 1H26 despite its scale
(republished below). 
• Why is this relevant? We see it mostly as a counter-argument to some concerns we hear from the market on luxury
brands, but also draw broader lessons from this analysis that helps inform our assessment of the long-term growth
prospects for the companies under our coverage
 
EssilorLuxottica (ESLX.PA) - Q3 26 results modestly ahead of expectations; performance driven by the Core
 
• Overall we lift our adjusted EPS estimates by +2-3% in FY26-28e; that is primarily driven by (1) modest increases
to our topline growth assumption for FY26e (to +6.9% from +6.5% previously), and (2) lifting our assumption for
adjusted EBIT margin in FY26 by +30bps due to smaller negative impact from mix and small headwind from FX on
margin. 
• We increase our target price target to €170; remain Neutral
 
Deutsche Boerse AG (DB1Gn.DE): 3Q26 First Take: Modest EBITDA beat; treasury-driven FY26 guidance upgrade
 
• Deutsche Boerse reported 3Q26 results after-market today, 20 October, with EBITDA of €978mn, 3% ahead of
company-compiled consensus and 2% ahead of both GSe and Visible Alpha consensus estimates. The beat was
revenue driven, with net revenue 2% ahead of expectations and operating expenses broadly in line. 

• The revenue beat was driven by Fund Services (+4%) and Trading & Clearing (+4%/+3% vs CC/VA cons), while
Investment Management Solutions (IMS) came in slightly ahead (+1%) and Securities Services was slightly
weaker (-1%).
 
Legrand (LEGD.PA) - Upgrade to Buy - structural growth with long term margin resilience protection
 
• We upgrade Legrand to Buy (from Neutral) with a 12M target price of €172 (31% upside vs coverage average at
17%). Legrand has underperformed our Electricals coverage in the L12M (down 9% vs Electricals coverage up
10%) as the market became worried about Legrand’s medium term positioning regarding technology shifts in data
centers, in particular 800VDC, as well as about rate hikes hindering growth in ex-data center businesses,
especially given its exposure to France (c. 12% of group sales). 
• However, we now see these data center risks as priced in (Legrand trades at a 16% discount to Global Electricals
on EV/EBIT vs historically trading in line) and we now see Legrand as better positioned for the 800VDC transition
than we had originally anticipated prior to its CMD in September 2026 where its product portfolio announcements
exceeded our expectations.
 
MULTI-INDUSTRY - Primer on 800VDC and implications for Electricals companies
 
• Investor debate this year has been increasingly focused on NVIDIA's 800VDC architecture and the race among
Electrical OEMs to develop the necessary product portfolios. These technological shifts are likely to reshape the
competitive landscape. 
• In this report, we outline the importance of these technologies, map the current competitive environment for
800VDC electrical equipment, and review the positioning, development efforts, and recent progress of companies
across our Electricals coverage. Within our European coverage, we see the best valuation opportunity on the data
center demand topic in Legrand (upgraded to Buy today) and to a lesser extent Siemens (Buy).
 
IAG (ICAG.L) - Reduce estimates on higher fuel costs, maintain our relative preference for IAG vs. peers
 
• We're reducing our 2026EBIT estimate by 6% to €4.3bn, in line with VA consensus, reflecting higher jetfuel costs.
Within this, our estimates are slightly ahead on Q3 driven by unit revenue, and slightly below on Q4 on higher fuel
costs. For 2027, our EBIT estimate falls by 13% to €4.29bn, 12% below consensus on significantly higher fuel
costs.
