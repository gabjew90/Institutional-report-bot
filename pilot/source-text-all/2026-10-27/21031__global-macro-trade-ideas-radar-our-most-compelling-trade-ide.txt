Our analysts sift through their trade idea portfolios to bring you their most 
compelling tactical trade ideas – that are also liquid and leverageable – and 
highlight them in this monthly report.
AI Exposure with Credit Caution — One way to cautiously position for the AI trade 
is through equities vs credit RVs. In short, equities offer long convexity exposure to 
the AI race, as a small number of eventual winners can generate sufficiently large 
gains to offset weakness elsewhere in the market or broader index. On the other 
hand, we think credit has the opposite asymmetry where bondholders only receive 
limited upside if AI investment delivers but remain exposed to the downside if 
returns disappoint. We buy ES1 at 7800 and buy CDX IG S47 5yr at 60.277. In FX, 
leading indicators for AUD suggest a weaker currency. We short AUDUSD.
