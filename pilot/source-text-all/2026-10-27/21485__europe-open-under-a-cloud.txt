Europe Open - Under a cloud
By Varshi Karamsetty
The Europe Open will now be published from London and is abbreviated.
Earnings may have beat expectations but concerns about financing is weighing on global stocks, with Asian indices
plunging once again and Nasdaq futures down -1.5% at print. This is also weighing on USD while treasuries advance.
US/Iran headlines are also impacting risk off sentiment, with new attacks on Saudi Arabia's King Fahd airport, as well as
drone attacks on Saudi Aramco’s Shaybah oil field. We saw reports that US have ordered diplomats to leave Saudi
Arabia and other Gulf countries due to the growing threat of Yemen's Houthis, with four tankers also hit by Iran's IRGC
overnight. 
Ahead, US data returns in the form of initial jobless claims at 13:30 BST, and second tier data such as leading index
(15:00). Riksbank Jansson speaks at 14:00 BST. Philippine rates were raised by 50bp vs 25bp expected. Turkey rate
decision to come at 12:00 BST (-100bp). BoK held rates overnight. Mexico bi-weekly CPI is also on the cards.

In Focus‌
Equities: Mixed tech earnings have dragged Asia stocks weaker once again, with Kospi once again losing -3.8% and Nasdaq
futures lower by -1.5%. Meta revenues beat and fundamentals remain strong, with strong AI tailwinds expected as Muse
usage ramps. However this was not in focus overnight, with stocks underperforming on financing. The company announced
plans to issue a new class of shares from January, though didn't have anything more specific until Q4, bringing question to
the several billions of USD that needs to be raised, as early as late 2026. HSTECH heading for its worst session since July,
nearing pre-stimulus 2024 levels. As for Europe, today marks the heaviest day of European earnings, with 12% of the market
cap set to report. Option market tells us to prepare for the largest moves in, unsurprisingly in Semis, Software, Defensive and
anything Expensive. Head of EU CETS Ross Montgomery discusses further here, and stresses caution given short squeeze
risks persist. 
Rates: Treasuries catching a bid amid risk off sentiment, with duration continuing to grind higher despite US/Iran headlines
hitting wires. Flow wise, our desk saw some buying further out of the curve alongside interest in steepening 2s10s. View

change from Citi Economics, now expecting the RBNZ to hike 25bp next week. Despite softer-than-expected Q3 CPI, their
new call reflects forward looking concerns of higher global neutral rates and elevated oil prices. 3% is likely the peak, given
slowing GDP. French spreads widened to more concerning levels on Wed, but we warned that there is no near-term
catalyst/anchor to contain moves. The upcoming sovereign rating review by Moody's (Friday) is widely expected to be a
downgrade from Aa3 to A1. Our strategists doubt that this would trigger broad-based selling from long-term investors, but it
should continue to add to negative sentiment. New: European Economics - Five Lessons for France from the Eurozone
Periphery
FX: USDJPY sell off stands out overnight, with the pair attempting to break the 157 handle at print, our platforms seeing
leveraged and investor buying JPY in the Asia session. Vols are basing after the significant selloff, our options desk like
owning USDJPY downside through the cluster of events next week (FOMC, BoJ, the latter has a 55bp break-even). Flow
wise, we are starting to see adding to USDJPY and cross JPY put spreads after a period of unwinds. Overall, interbank
volumes were 18% higher than 30d averages. 
Commodities: Oil prices up 2.5% after escalating headlines. Iran Fars reported that the IRGC hit four "violating" oil tankers
simultaneously while transferring oil cargo in the northern Persian Gulf, though there is no date of the attack. Fresh attacks on
Saudi Arabia's energy infrastructure as well as the country's King Fahd airport in Damman. The US have ordered non-
essential diplomatic personall and families from Saudi Arabia due to the growing threat from Yemen's Houthis. The
precautionary measures have also reportedly led to the departure of embassy families from neighboring Gulf countries - UAE,
Qatar, Jordan and Bahrain. NYT also reports that the US and Israeli military have developed specific plans for renewed
strikes against Iran post US midterms, according to US officials. Iranian energy infrastructure, nuclear facilities, and missile
and drone arsenal could be among the targets, with US Defense Secretary Hegseth expected to meet PM Netanyahu this
week. Meanwhile, late Wed, Russian President Putin said Russia's ready to resume trilateral negotiations with the US and
Ukraine on a potential peace deal, during the period of the first ten days of Nov. We flag a client call at 16:00 BST: Pulse
Check on El Niño: What's Next for Agricultural Commodity Markets?
Elsewhere‌
USD: Watch trends in Initial Jobless Claims at 13:30 BST. Second tier data such as Leading Index at 15:00 BST for
September; Kansas City Fed Manufacturing Activity at 16:00 BST for October should be overlooked.
SEK: Riksbank's Jansson speaks in Stockholm at 14:00 BST.
EUR: Eurozone Consumer Confidence at 15:00 BST for October P. Bloomberg cons. expects confidence to turn more
negative towards -17 vs -16.5 prior. ECB's Panetta speaks in Turin, also at 15:00 BST.
PHP: BSP Overnight Borrowing Rate Decision at 07:30 BST. Our economists expect the BSP to raise the policy rate by 25bp
to 5.25%.
TRY: One-Week Repo Rate Decision at 12:00 BST. Our economists expect the CBT to cut the policy rate by 100bp and
maintain their year-end policy rate forecast at 35.0%. The path is supported by the September inflation outturn and softer
economic activity.
ZAR: CEEMEA Strategy are buying SAGB duration and going short MXNZAR. A positive budget, featuring bond buybacks,
reduced SAGB borrowing, and a committed primary surplus reinforces our view that the backend remains cheap vs fiscal
fundamentals, keeping the medium-term ratings upgrade story live. We see downside risks in MXN amid the erosion of a
benign vol bakdop, plus an increasingly dovish Banxico. Pairing it with ZAR makes sense here. Full trade details.
MXN: Bi-Weekly CPI YoY at 13:00 BST. Headline inflation data for 1H of Oct should print +0.34% bw/bw, reflecting an
increase in non-core inflation (0.91%), driven by energy prices and regulated tariffs inflation. Core inflation should rise +0.18%
BW/BW in line with last years ago print. If realized, annual headline inflation would increase to 3.56% YoY in the 1H of
October from 3.49% in the 2H of September, while core inflation would edge up slightly to 3.72%.
KRW: BoK held rates unchanged at 3% in line with expectations in an unanimous decision. Official projections are only going
to be released in Nov MPB but the bank were upbeat on growth, seeing both 2027 and 2028 growth significantly above
previous projections. Franchise flows wise see HFs taking the chance to pay frontend right after decision, but curve retraced a

little bit as presser revealed nothing new, with BoK Shin keeping the door open for further hike without much stronger
language. Citi Economics review here.
