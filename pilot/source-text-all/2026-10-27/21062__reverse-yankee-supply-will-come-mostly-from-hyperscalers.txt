Reverse Yankee supply will come
mostly from hyperscalers
2027 is set to be another heavy year for issuance across currencies and
products, even if supply moderates somewhat from 2026 levels in many
segments. Hyperscalers and the wider technology sector will remain key
drivers of funding activity and are among the largest contributors to our
overall supply forecasts
We expect hyperscalers alone to account for around €60bn of Reverse Yankee issuance in 2027
The key question for 2027 is whether Reverse Yankee supply can remain as convincing as it
has been in recent years. The economics for US issuers looking to fund in euros and swap
proceeds back into dollars have deteriorated markedly, with much of the historical funding
advantage having evaporated. In many cases, the maths is now far less compelling on an
issuer-by-issuer basis.
However, the hyperscaler story remains difficult to ignore. We expect hyperscalers alone to
account for around €60bn of Reverse Yankee issuance in 2027, up sharply from the roughly
€31bn issued in euros during 2026.
As a result, we continue to expect elevated Reverse Yankee supply, albeit increasingly
concentrated within the technology sector. Overall, we forecast another €100bn of Reverse
Yankee issuance in 2027, matching that of 2026.

Importantly, this leaves only around €40bn of issuance from non-hyperscaler borrowers, a
notable decline on what we saw in 2026. The decline reflects the reduced cost-saving incentive
for US corporates to issue in euros and swap back to dollars.

Reverse Yankee issuance has historically been closely linked to both USD and EUR corporate
supply. On average, it has represented around 10-11% of US corporate issuance and 20-21%
of EUR corporate issuance. For 2027, this increases marginally up to 12% and 23% respectively
on the back of the larger hyperscaler and tech issuance. This also suggests that continued
strength in overall corporate funding volumes should still provide a supportive backdrop, even
if the currency economics become less attractive.
