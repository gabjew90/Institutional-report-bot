3Q26 earnings beat  
Lockheed Martin (LMT) 3Q26 results: Sales increased 11% YoY to $20,711mn 
(Bloomberg (BBG) est. $20,474mn), driven by higher volume across every segment, 
including F-35 production and sustainment at Aeronautics (Aero), Missiles & Fire Control 
(MFC) munitions production ramps, new radar awards at Rotary & Mission Systems 
(RMS), and growth on Fleet Ballistic Missile and Next Generation Interceptor at Space; 
EPS was $7.97 (BBG est. $7.51); business segment operating profit increased $200mn 
YoY to $2,229mn due to munition ramps at MFC and higher volume at Aero; business 
segment operating margin was 10.8%; FCF was $2,999mn (BBG est. $1,755mn). LMT 
ended 3Q with a record backlog of $292.0bn, including $52.5bn associated with the 
multi-year award to produce PAC-3 Missile Segment Enhancement (MSE) interceptors. 
3Q26 segment results 
Aero: Sales +13% YoY to $8,214mn; operating profit +9% YoY to $743mn; operating 
margin 9.0% vs. 9.4% YoY. MFC: Sales +24% YoY to $4,501mn; operating profit +24% 
YoY to $631mn; operating margin 14.0% vs. 14.1% YoY. RMS: Sales +3% YoY to 
$4,511mn; operating profit +4% YoY to $524mn; operating margin flat YoY at 11.6%. 
Space: Sales +4% YoY to $3,485mn; operating profit flat YoY at $331mn; operating 
margin 9.5% vs. 9.9% YoY. 
Relative value 
LMT 4.500% ‘36s are offered at a spread of 50bps, rich to Northrop Grumman (NOC) 
5.250% ‘35s which are offered at a spread of 60bps, and rich to the lower rated RTX 
(RTX) 5.400% ‘35s which are offered at a spread of 66bps. While LMT’s business 
remains high quality and defense demand remains strong, we believe this is reflected in 
LMT’s bond pricing; therefore, we view the bonds as trading at fair value.  
We remain MW on bonds and Neutral on CDS 
We maintain a MW recommendation on LMT bonds and a neutral recommendation on 
CDS. While we don’t anticipate spread compression given current trading levels, we 
expect LMT to remain an excellent credit with strong fundamentals, supported by 
continued robust defense demand from both the U.S. and abroad. LMT also serves as an 
excellent flight to quality.

Valuation & risk 
Lockheed Martin Corp (LMT) 
Lockheed Martin is one of the world's largest defense contractors, has a large backlog,
