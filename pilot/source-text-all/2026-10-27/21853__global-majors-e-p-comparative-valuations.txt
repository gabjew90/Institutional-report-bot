Broker Outlook
 
• 
At current levels, North American Senior E&Ps are trading at an average debt-adjusted cash flow multiple of 6.8x in 2026E and 5.3x in
2027E under futures pricing, above Intermediate E&Ps in both years. 
• 
Canadian Intermediate E&Ps are trading above their US counterparts in 2026E and 2027E under futures pricing. 
• 
European Majors continue to trade at a discount to the North American Majors in 2026E and 2027E under futures pricing. 
• 
For reference, our estimates at futures pricing, including Free Cash Flow and Free Cash Flow Yield, are detailed on pages 3-6.
