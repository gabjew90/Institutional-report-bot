Silver Futures: Still Stuck
Commitment of Traders covering October 6th - 13th revealed ongoing bearish Managed Money Silver
flows. Macro and micro headwinds emerged. Despite a Dollar retracement over the ensuing 5 sessions,
softness in structural demand indicators continued to weigh on Silver. Shorts seemingly initiated. While
this presents an opportunity for a tactical bounce, the Iran War and its Dollar impact loom.
• Commitment of Traders covering October 6th - 13th revealed ongoing bearish Managed Money Silver
flows. Specifically Managed Money sold $565mm, marking the 3rd straight week of selling for a
cumulative $2.2bn. Liquidation ($160mm) and new shorts ($405mm) contributed. December Silver lost
3.4% throughout the period.
• Macro and micro headwinds emerged. The US Dollar reached 1 year highs and gained 0.3% overall. US
Solar companies surrendered their recent rebound and fell 4.7%. China Solar also traded poorly, dropping
1.6%. And Data Centers plunged 7.9% as concerns over Memory, Neoclouds, and general credit
worthiness afflicted AI stocks.
• Despite a Dollar retracement over the ensuing 5 sessions, softness in structural demand indicators
continued to weigh on Silver. Over October 13th - 20th, the Dollar backtracked 0.5%. However Data
Centers and Solar remained soft. Thus Silver forfeited another 2.6%.
• Shorts seemingly initiated. Silver futures aggregate open interest rose $1.6bn with most surfacing on the
heaviest day of price action. Moreover, normalized 25 delta put-call skew richened to a 98% 1 year rank at
one point.
• While this presents an opportunity for a tactical bounce, the Iran War and its Dollar impact loom. Absent
an end to the conflict, Silver may struggle to convincingly rally unless FX energy betas dampen much
more. In addition, some of the aforementioned Dollar stall resulted from AI unwinds, which also read
negatively for Silver.
