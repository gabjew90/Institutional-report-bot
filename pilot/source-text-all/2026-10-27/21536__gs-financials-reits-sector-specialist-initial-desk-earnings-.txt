GS Financials & REITs Sector Specialist: Initial Desk Earnings Takes ...
October 22nd 2026; All references to "we/us/our" refer to the views and observations of the desk. 
 
Initial Desk earnings takes below (alphabetical order) on BFH BX CBRE CCI FR HBAN LAZ NDAQ SLG ..
Welcome thoughts & Pushback – MOST earnings feedback so far coming in on BX (leaning bearish),
CBRE (pushback to our negative take), and NDAQ (leaning positive) … reach out for more ..
 
BFH (+/= .. LINK to Nash/GIR first take): EPS beat on 1% better revs (driven by NII), well managed costs
and a smaller reserve build than expected. NCOs also came in lower in quarter. FY26 guide implies slight
PPNR upside to numbers with average loans ~+4% YoY, Revs ~+5% YoY, positive operating leverage, and a
net loss rate below 7%... Call @ 830am
 
BX (-/=.. LINK to Blostein/GIR first take): 1% miss on management fees and a 1% miss on FRE – and looks
like they got the beat on headline earnings from beating the pre announced realization number & lower comp
rates in PRE .. inflows look pretty good w/ $63bn total (street $58bn) and fee paying inflows of $57bn. 
From a high level, PE & infrastructure remain humming along – but real estate is coming back in focus as a
negative (not the datacenter exposure to be clear) and they’re taking some strong portfolio markdowns in RE
(pointing to slower growing RE assets) driving net accrued carry -3% QoQ.. T-1 vintages for some of their
large/global real estate funds that aren’t actively deployment are now putting up IRRs in the 0-3% range,
which, put in the context of some recent changes in RE management, may prompt some questions around
how RE fundraising looks going forward. Why does this matter? The road to double digit management fee
growth next year is fairly locked in by funds poised to ‘turn on’ – however a decent portion of the growth
beyond that is supposed to be driven by the real estate recovery .. May get some pushback on why we
should be surprised real estate is poor in light of what’s been happening in rates (which is fair), and market
may like more conservative treatment here - welcome thoughts
 
CBRE (-/= .. LINK to Julien/GIR first take).. Core EPS beat driven by REI while top line was light across
Project Management / BOE .. Leasing was weaker in advisory but sales was stronger (roughly netting
eachother out) .. They are maintaining the 2026 EPS guide and the 2027 guide, but the moving pieces in
2026 aren’t great: 1) Guide down in Advisory (activity to moderate reflecting higher interest rates) which the
buyside expected . 2) Lower SOP expectations for BOE, specifically on lower expectations in Enterprise
Facilities Management – which we think will get plenty of questions given the durability/quality of this
earnings stream, 3) Project Management reiterated, and 4) a guide up on Real Estate Investments driven by
development (profits from Datacenter Land called out .. total pipeline development portfolio was unchanged
QoQ) …

So all in, ‘lower multiple’ REI guided up & enterprise facilities management a surprise guide down while
advisory outlook in-line with buyside thoughts into the quarter. Overall, maintaining the 2027 guide on its
face was viewed as ‘good enough’ (stock -13% into print); however, Expect visibility into this to get
questioned given BOE guide down .. Looking for more color on 2027 visibility and what’s happening in
enterprise facilities management to get the market more comfortable on the stock post print …
 
CCI (=/- .. LINK to Ng/GIR first take): Mixed quarter as 3Q26 EBITDA of $673mn came in line with cons on
in line site rental revenue and lower network services revenue of $29mn vs $44mn cons which raises
concerns around slower core leasing activity and on top of that CCI is guiding core leasing activity to be at
the low-end of its prior $60-$70mn guidance. That said, organic revenue growth (excluding DISH and Sprint)
was 3.5% YoY (vs 3.2% GSe) and they reiterated their 2026 organic growth outlook and expectations that
organic growth should accelerate in 2027 driven by MLA visibility to future. Additionally, CCI is seeing
momentum in its edge compute infrastructure trials, which is particularly notable as large data center
deployments are facing more construction and power delivery delays. CCI has 100+ sites deployed in
several concurrent trials with most sites utilizing 200-500 kw of capacity, though 25 sites could do 100MW in
aggregate.
 
FR (= w/ some puts & takes .. LINK to Caitlin/GIR first take): FFO of 81c came in 1c below cons though FR
increased FY26 SS NOI growth 75bps at the midpoint to 6.5% and increased Core FFO range +c at the
midpoint to $3.17 in line with the street. Leasing spreads were strong (cash leasing spreads for commenced
space was +52% in 3Q26 up from 39% in 2Q26, but 16% on leases commencing in 2027) and in service
occupancy guidance was increased along side FFO guide. On the other hand, there were no new
development starts, active development pipeline was flat QoQ, and Caitlin/GIR notes investors were
anticipating more development starts/leasing on perceived improved demand dynamics .. Welcome thoughts
 
HBAN (-/= .. LINK to Nash/GIR first take) .. Slight (sub-1%) PPNR beat in the qtr driven by core expenses
.. but guide and key KPIs look pretty soft .. NIM beat by ~4bps, in part driven by slightly better deposit costs
which people will appreciate (2.31% vs 2.33% cons) but also cash-draw down / deployment – importantly,
the 4Q guide looks light and implies ~4% miss on PPNR .. They’re holding the 2027 EPS guide flat, but with
street already at the low end of the range and 4Q PPNR implied 4% miss, expect scrutiny/questions around
the path forward for numbers..
 
LAZ (- .. LINK to Yaro/GIR first take): 14% earnings miss driven by the top line (-6% all in rev miss) w/
advisory missing est by ~9% & asset management by 1%. Margin missed estimates by ~20bps driven by
noncomp as comp expenses were well managed in the context of weaker rev
 
NDAQ (+/= .. LINK to Blostein/GIR first take) Operating income 2% beat with small beats on rev side
across segments (~1% each roughly) … Fintech ARR again looks very strong at +18% YoY, that’s
accelerating from +16% YoY last qtr, and now has the metric running in the high teens YTD (think guide at
12-14%?) .. Took up the expense by $20mn on low end and $10mn at the high end and likely driven by rev,
bought back $323mn vs $280mn street .. broadly solid results w/ the Fintech business continuing to shine.

SLG (- .. LINK to Caitlin/GIR first take): NAREIT FFO of $1.59 came in 3.4% below consensus (Caitlin/GIR
calls out net loss in SUMMIT while 'basis difference & other adjustments' re OVA came in higher, which
mgmt had indicated would see some benefit to the bottom line in 2H26 at 2Q earnings ... Signed 591k sqft of
office leases in 3Q26, bringing the Manhattan same-store office leased rate to 95.0% with cash leasing
spreads on comparable spaces +13.6% in 3Q (net effective spreads on signed leases were flat).
FY26 FFO guidance was raised at the midpoint by 85c to $6.60 - but simply but was driven by a transaction
while core results were indicated lower on higher rates .. Details of the guidance change = driven by $1.10
per share of incremental income from the sale of a 20% JV interest in an entity related to SLG's F&B
hospitality operations (closed in October), offset by $0.25 per share due to the higher interest rate
environment...
Expect focus on the call to be on the extent to which higher interest rates are impacting the transaction
market broadly as well as expectation for SS NOI acceleration in 2027… Call @ 2pm
