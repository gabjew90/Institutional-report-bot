Gold's moment of truth
Gold is flirting with a massive breakdown. Momentum is ugly, longs are retreating, and downside fear is
rising. Even falling yields, a weaker dollar and exploding tech fear can't save the metal. With $4,000 on
the line, is gold about to crack?
Make or break
Gold is trading right at the critical $4,000 level. Price remains well below the now downward-sloping
200-day moving average, and as we all know, nothing good tends to happen below the 200-day. On the
other hand, the major long-term trendline converges around current levels, while $4,000 has acted as
key support over the past year. A massive technical crossroads. Make or break.

Gold loses its hedge appeal
Gold and inverted US 10-year yields have tracked each other relatively closely, but the latest move
lower in yields has failed to support gold. Quite the opposite: gold sold off sharply as rates reversed
lower. If the move in yields reflects broader risk aversion, the message is clear: investors aren't buying
gold as a hedge.
