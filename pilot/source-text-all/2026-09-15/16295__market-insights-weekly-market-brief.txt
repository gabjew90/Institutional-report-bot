The Weekly Brief
Thought of the week
With data centre buildout continuing at pace, US large language model (LLM)
capabilities have noticeably improved. One example is the Artificial Analysis
Intelligence Index, which scores LLMs on various metrics varying from solving
complex problems using agentic Artificial Intelligence (AI), to writing code. While
the index is not a ‘perfect’ measure of AI quality, it has nonetheless continued to
trend higher for the major US providers. These big US LLM providers are of
course still facing challenges and uncertainties. The cost of semiconductors
has surged; cheaper China-origin models are providing competition; concerns
around AI safety and regulation remain. However, American businesses are
continuing to spend more on US AI models, with token volumes soaring. Overall,
these alternative AI metrics are increasingly important to track, especially as
there may be some big LLM-related initial public offerings soon, including
Anthropic and OpenAI.
