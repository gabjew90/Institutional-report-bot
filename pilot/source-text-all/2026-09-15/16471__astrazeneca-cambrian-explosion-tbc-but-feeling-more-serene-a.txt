CAMBRIAN explosion tbc, but feeling 
more SERENE after reset: u/g to Hold*
Summary takes
It is not a frequent occurrence, but almost a year after our d/g to Sell (AZN : Turning 
sceptical on SERENA(4): cut to Sell (16/10/25)) alongside our breast cancer deep 
dive (Breast cancer deep dive: focus on SERD potential to extend lives for the 
many (16/10/25)), the trial at the centre of that report (SERENA4) duly reported 
negatively on Friday and with the stock down c3% in absolute terms over the 
period and having underperformed the EU large cap peer group by c10% (FX-
adj.), we upgrade to Hold. This has clearly been partly for reasons we anticipated 
(e.g. the H2 event overhang, patent cliff concerns) and partly those we did not (e.g. 
Wainua, BMY), but whilst don’t view yesterday’s sector-bounce as either reflecting 
recent fundamentals or particularly durable heading into the mid-terms, we do see 
risk-reward more neutrally here for AZN. We nudge our PT +2% to £117 based on 
a 14x FY27 P/E: a c15% premium to the group on 12x which is a compression vs. 
recent history averaging double that but reflecting mixed recent pipeline newsflow 
(both within and beyond oncology…) and legitimately reasserted long-term post-
LoE growth concerns, offset by a reasonable growth and R&D catalyst outlook 
nearer-term.
Brief further details on our view and outlook
Our recent AZN coverage has been somewhat voluminous: after contemplating 
whether the appropriately severe reaction to entertaining discussions with BMY 
(BMY footnote: Déjà vu feedback, updated scenarios and some loose ends 
(04/08/26)) presented an opportunity to neutralise our stance, we had maintained 
our view on the basis S4 would still be imminently negative and thus remain a 
modest overhang, even in context of diminished expectations. That was despite 
anticipation of duly positive tozorakimab data (AZN: OBERON/TITANIA reconciled, 
but whereof art thou MIRANDA? ERS takes (09/09/26)) and mixed recent newsflow 
(AZN: Landfall R2, but not necessarily the Cape of Good Hope: S6/newsflow takes 
(07/09/26)). Nearer-term key events will be AVANZAR (see below) and Q3 30/10: 
whilst it is early for a preview, our present view remains unchanged from post-Q2 
that it is unlikely to serve as the forum for a guidance u/g. We will revisit our thoughts 
into 2027 in due course but refer to AZN: Feedback and thoughts into H2/’27 post 
the misttransformation (10/07/26) and AZN: Pipeline deep-dive: reappraising the 
potential path to 80+ (06/02/26) in the interim.

Brief recap into dato’s AVANZAR
We’ve covered dato’s fitful progress in more notes than we can easily summarise 
but refer to our most recent comment (AstraZeneca - TROP2 update: dato takes and 
x-reads in TNBC/NSCLC (25/06/26)) plus original TL01 takes (AZN: TROP2 reality 
check: WCLC call takes - feeling a little aTROPhic (10/09/24)) for a brief recap. The 
summary of our view remains that despite excluding AGA, the trial is likely to hit 
on PFS (at least NMR+), but is unlikely to do so on OS, which we believe would be 
required for FDA approval in 1L (and certainly unlikely in any clinically meaningful 
manner, which would be required for broad adoption).
Model changes
Volrustomig we cut in half to $0.5bn after the NSCLC miss; tozo we double to 
$4bn; cami we leave $3.3bn risked peak unchanged but trim slightly near-term in 
light of the EU/US S6 approvals. Finally, we build in AZD5462 after our review of the 
data at ESC (landfall link above) launching 2031, peak sales $1bn, risked to 50%. The 
net effect is sales +LSD mid-term, in line with the PT change with this update.

PT change and recommendation
We upgrade from SELL to HOLD and nudge our PT +2% to £117 based on a 14x 
FY27 P/E: a c15% premium to the group on 12x which is a compression vs. recent 
history averaging double that but reflecting mixed recent pipeline newsflow (both 
within and beyond oncology…) and legitimately reasserted long-term post-LoE 
growth concerns, offset by a reasonable growth and R&D catalyst outlook nearer-
term.
Upside risks - Stronger commercial execution, better than expected pipeline 
delivery, particularly AVANZAR and CAMBRIA-1, and upside to camizestrant or 
tozorakimab forecasts.
Downside risks - Further pipeline setbacks, particularly AVANZAR failing to 
support US approval, weaker commercial delivery, and greater than expected 
patent-cliff or post-LoE growth pressures.
