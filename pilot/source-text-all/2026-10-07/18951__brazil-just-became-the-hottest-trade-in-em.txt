Brazil: the bull case just got louder
EWZ has broken out of a multi-year range on huge volume, BRL is strengthening through a major long-
term trend, and the 2018 playbook suggests the election move doesn't necessarily end after the initial
repricing.
But this isn't just an election trade.
Brazil still offers enormous real carry, valuations remain cheap, and a stronger currency could unlock
a powerful feedback loop: lower inflation, faster Selic cuts and a further rerating of domestic equities.
The catalyst has arrived. The question now is how far the move can run.
The EWZ explosion
EWZ exploded higher yesterday, breaking above a range that has been in place for years, on
absolutely huge volume.
Technically, there is very little in the way here. The first meaningful resistance sits around $48,
followed by the $52 area.

Brazil: the move can run
The 2018 election offers an interesting roadmap for Brazil FX.
Back then, the real continued to rally into the second round even after an initial 10% drop in USD/BRL,
with the move only losing momentum on the Friday before the vote, according to GS.
The focus then shifted from politics to implementation. This time, the fiscal backdrop makes policy
announcements increasingly important as October 25 approaches.
Two near-term political catalysts are worth watching: the Senate debate over ending the six-day
workweek, and the 15% Bolsa Família increase, which could reach roughly 19 million recipients on
October 19, just six days before the second round.

The carry cushion
Brazil has something most election trades don't: investors are being paid handsomely to wait.
With Selic at 13.75% and inflation around 4.2–4.5%, Brazil continues to offer some of the highest real
carry in EM. That means BRL doesn't need another political surprise every day to remain supported.
Think of it as a cushion underneath the election trade. If political risk continues to compress, BRL gets
another leg. But even if the political story goes quiet for a while, investors are still being paid
exceptionally well to stay long.
The election provided the catalyst. The carry can keep the trade alive.

The rerating has barely started
The interesting part is where Brazil is starting from.
JPM has Brazil trading at just 8.9x 12-month forward earnings. A move into the next valuation regime
takes that to 10.9x, while a genuine structural reform scenario could push the multiple toward 13x,
similar to 2016.
In other words, the election rally may look violent on the screen, but Brazil is hardly priced for
perfection yet.

The virtuous circle
The carry isn't the only support.
JPM sees a potential feedback loop developing: a stronger BRL reduces imported inflation pressure,
giving the central bank more room to cut. Lower rates support domestic duration and rate-sensitive
equities, while declining fiscal risk supports both the currency and the equity multiple.
JPM explicitly highlights BRL appreciation as a potential catalyst for more and faster Selic cuts, with
November already live.
Carry supports the currency. The currency creates room for cuts. The cuts support the rerating.
The vol reset
EWZ vol has reset sharply post-event. At current levels it isn't particularly cheap given how depressed
global equity vol remains, but if the Brazil move has further to run, long gamma starts to look
increasingly interesting.
