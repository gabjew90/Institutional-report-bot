Brazil surprises
Flávio Bolsonaro delivered a major upside surprise in Brazil's first round, taking 47.1% versus Lula's
45.1% and beating the poll-of-polls by more than 4 points.
The strength was broad. Bolsonaro outperformed in all three major battlegrounds, winning São Paulo
51.9% to 38.2%, Minas Gerais 48.3% to 43.2% and Rio 53.0% to 39.4%. He also exceeded Jair
Bolsonaro's 2022 first-round baseline, while Lula underperformed his.
The right also had a strong night further down the ballot, with conservative candidates outperforming
in several key Senate and gubernatorial races.
The election now heads to an October 25 runoff. The key question is where the 7.8% of votes cast for
the smaller candidates migrate, with their endorsements potentially important.
Bottom line: the first round was materially stronger for Bolsonaro and the broader right than polls had
suggested.

The Brazil trade...in Europe
Worth watching the European read-across from Brazil today.
Both Santander and AB InBev have traded remarkably closely with EWZ since 2025, but have recently
started to lag. That divergence suddenly looks more interesting with EWZ indicated around 11% higher
premarket.
The fundamental links are real. Brazil accounts for roughly 9% of Santander's group loan book, while
AB InBev controls Ambev, giving it significant exposure to Brazilian consumer earnings and the BRL.
All of this comes as European assets are dealing with French fiscal stress, euro weakness and fresh
Spanish political noise.
If the Brazil re-rating holds, SAN and ABI are two European catch-up trades worth watching.
