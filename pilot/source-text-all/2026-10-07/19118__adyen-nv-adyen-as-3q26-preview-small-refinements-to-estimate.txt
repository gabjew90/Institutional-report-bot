Adyen NV (ADYEN.AS): 3Q26 preview: Small refinements to estimates, 
recent weakness creates attractive opportunity; Buy (CL)
6 October 2026 | 4:44PM BST
We update our estimates on Adyen ahead of 3QFY26 results. While we make no 
changes to our FY26 growth estimates of 22.2% yoy ex-fx (21.2% yoy ex-fx organic), 
which is modestly above Visible Alpha Consensus, we make slight refinements (on 
account of comp effects) to our quarterly cadence of growth in 2H26, forecasting 
21.5% yoy ex-fx for 3Q (19.5% yoy ex-fx organic) and 23.7% yoy ex-fx for 4Q (21.9% 
yoy ex-fx organic). We continue to expect 2H26 organic ex-fx growth of 20.8% yoy. 
In addition, we modestly trim our EBITDA margin estimates to 52.0% for FY27 (vs 
52.4% previously) as we incorporate M&A dilution. We continue to see the 
company’s top-line guide as veering to the conservative with de-minimis headwinds 
factored in and no acceleration in growth implied for 2H26. While margins are 
expected to modestly contract in FY26 given the dilutive impact from acquisitions, 
we expect greater focus to be on the integration of the acquisitions and incremental 
revenue opportunities over the mid-term. Based on feedback from hosting the 
interim CFO at the Communacopia + Technology Conference 2026, we remain 
confident in Adyen’s ability to drive wallet share gains with existing customers 
(recent expansion with Toast in the US plus Shopify in Europe), ramp of prior cohorts 
(the largest cohort signed in 2025 scaling in 2026), new customers, new product 
launches and embedded finance, all underpinned by Adyen’s superior technology 
offering. We also note additional levers of upside optionality including agentic 
commerce, where we see Adyen as well positioned, given its superior tech, to 
support merchants on complex interactions in an increasingly fragmented market. 
Given the recent weakness in the shares, we continue to see meaningful dislocation 
in valuation versus fundamentals, with the shares trading on c.11x/18x/14x on 
CY27E EV/EBITDA, P/E, EV/FCF and at a PEG of 0.9x and an EV/FCF growth of 0.5x, 
with 21%/24%/22%/23% 2027-30E CAGRs for revenue/EBITDA/PF EPS/FCF. 
Further, our reverse DCF implies the market is currently discounting c.10% revenue 
growth over the next decade, which we view as undemanding, creating an attractive 
opportunity. We remain Buy rated (on CL) with a new 12m PT of €1,750 (down from 
€1,850), still offering substantial upside.

We expect growth to decelerate modestly into 3Q, largely driven by tougher comps. We

prior-year cohorts), and pricing dynamics. We continue to see the company’s top-line 
guide as veering to the conservative with de-minimis headwinds factored in and no

3) Market share dynamics and colour on regional performance 
We await further colour on regional performance across core markets and competitive 
intensity. Specifically, we note that EMEA represents one of the most mature markets for 
Adyen, where it still sees significant penetration potential given the level of 
fragmentation of the market. In North America, we expect momentum to remain robust. 
At the segment level, we expect Digital growth of c.17% yoy ex-fx in 3Q26 while in 
Unified Commerce, we expect it to grow at c.26%, with Platforms growth at c.33% for 
3Q26. We continue to expect Adyen to gain share and expand with complex merchants, 
supported by its superior technology offering. 
4) Operating leverage and margin evolution including pace of hiring and opex trends 
We forecast +550 net adds in FY26, stabilising to +650-1250 p.a. over 2027-30 
(unchanged). We trim our EBITDA margins modestly to 52.0% for FY27, broadly in line 
with Visible Alpha consensus. Looking ahead, we believe the inherent operating leverage 
in the business should drive a higher drop-through from any revenue upside in the 
coming years.

Adyen estimates $2 trillion in global GMV to flow through AI agentic channels by FY29. 
While still in nascent stages, we expect investors to focus on agentic commerce with

enterprises to sell through conversational AI platforms without rebuilding their

We continue to see upside on valuation given strong growth as well as a unique and attractive profile

We continue to see valuation upside at current levels given growth optionality over the 
medium term, with robust operating leverage resulting in higher drop-through from any 
revenue upside. Adyen’s current valuation multiples are still well below the long-term 
average which we view as undemanding. Furthermore, we note our reverse DCF implying 
the current share price is discounting c.10% revenue growth over the next decade (we 
assume EBITDA margins stabilise at 60%, holding all else equal), which we view as 
undemanding. Overall, given the recent share price weakness, we see an attractive 
opportunity given a compelling risk reward and strong fundamentals with multiple 
upside optionality levers.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.  
M&A Rank 
Across our global coverage, we examine stocks using an M&A framework, considering both qualitative factors and quantitative factors (which may vary 
across sectors and regions) to incorporate the potential that certain companies could be acquired. We then assign a M&A rank as a means of scoring 
companies under our rated coverage from 1 to 3, with 1 representing high (30%-50%) probability of the company becoming an acquisition target, 2 
representing medium (15%-30%) probability and 3 representing low (0%-15%) probability. For companies ranked 1 or 2, in line with our standard 
departmental guidelines we incorporate an M&A component into our target price. M&A rank of 3 is considered immaterial and therefore does not 
factor into our price target, and may or may not be discussed in research.
