GS Eco on Q3 – full note here: The Q3 2026 earnings season will kick off during the week of October 12.
By the end of October, roughly 70% of S&P 500 market cap will have reported. Most of the hyperscalers
are scheduled to report during the last week of October.
Consensus expects S&P 500 year/year EPS growth of 27% in Q3. This compares with realized growth of
33% in Q2, excluding some accounting distortions. Analyst estimates show the beneficiaries of AI
infrastructure spending accounting for over 50% of S&P 500 EPS growth this quarter alongside
hyperscaler capex growth of 116%.
We expect most companies will once again surpass consensus earnings estimates this quarter. For the
median S&P 500 stock, consensus estimates show year/year EPS growth slowing from 14% in Q2 to 9%
in Q3. Input cost pressures will likely limit the magnitude of sequential margin expansion in Q3, and the
dollar should provide a modestly smaller tailwind to S&P 500 revenue growth this quarter than last
quarter. However, recent economic growth data have been strong and S&P 500 earnings revision breadth
has remained positive.

China data worth flagging: September PMIs increased: All four PMIs (NBS manufacturing, NBS non-
manufacturing, RatingDog manufacturing and RatingDog services) rose and registered above-50 readings
in September. The improvement partly reflected quarter-end residual seasonality, as well as better weather
and increased government spending. The NBS construction PMI rose from 46.9 in August to 50.3, its
highest level this year.
DAL reports Friday October 9th pre-market at 6:30am with the call at 10am. 6% implied move. Stock
more green than red in recent quarters but relatively muted moves in the last four quarters. Expect we will
get an update to full year guide with an implied Q4. Revenue trends have come in strong to better across
airlines anecdotally. Cost trends have been worse including fuel and excluding fuel. Airlines have been a
push pull between pushing price to capture / more than recapture rising jet fuel. DAL is more protected on
balance with better margins and owning Trainer. That has led the stock to relative highs but also sets an
elevated bar in this one. Full year guidance right now for $6.50-7.50 in EPS, $3-4bn in free cash flow.
Street is below the low end of EPS $5.89 with topline growth expected to accelerate to +17% year over
year growth in Q4.
