WEEKLY MARKET REVIEW

Chart #1
Will France’s sovereign debt rating drop 
into “junk” territory?
Based on the relationship between sovereign ratings and 
spreads over Germany, France’s current spread of 135 
basis points is consistent with a BBB- rating, the lowest 
investment-grade notch on the S&P scale.
In other words, France now stands just one notch above 
“junk” territory.
Source: Bloomberg, Eleva capital
Chart #2
Anthropic at $2tn: outrageous or a bet 
on extraordinary growth?
The reportedly leaked IPO prospectus paints two 
contrasting pictures.
The first concerns spending: an operating loss of $8bn in 
2025 and over $500bn of future commitments, around 
80% of which are reportedly non-cancellable.
The second concerns growth:
	
 Revenue in 2025: $4.6bn 
	
 Annualized run rate in July 2026: $65bn 
	
 Revenue forecast for 2028: $200bn
A $2tn valuation therefore implies:
	
 435x 2025 revenue
	
 31x the July annualised run rate
	
 10x forecast 2028 revenue
However, a run rate is not the same as annual revenue, and 
a forecast is not a result.
Can July’s performance be repeated, or was it inflated by 
exceptionally large contracts? Two customers reportedly 
accounted for a quarter of 2025 revenue, with no long-
term commitment in place.
Each new projection makes the valuation easier to justify. 
The real test lies in converting those projections into actual 
revenue.
Source: Dan Runkevicius

Chart #4
The longest duration Treasury bond 
ETF is now down 67% from its peak in 
March 2020
How did this happen? The 30-year Treasury yield has 
risen from a record low of 0.8% in March 2020 to 5.6% 
today.
With rates climbing from record lows, the long duration of 
these bonds amplified the losses.
Source: Charlie Bilello

Chart #7
Four watches, $49.4mn, all for charity
Rolex’s “Four Seasons” auction was held in London, with 
an exceptional outcome.
Sold individually by Phillips, the four one-of-a-kind 
Cosmograph Daytona watches fetched a combined 
$49,467,260, a new record for a charity watch sale.
The individual results:
☀Summer: $13.6mn	
🍁Autumn: $13.5mn  
❄Winter: $13.2mn	
🌸Spring: $9mn
Each piece represents one season, combining precious 
metals, natural-stone dials and striking gemstones.
The full proceeds will go to the Federer Foundation and 
Re, supporting children’s access to education and the 
protection of ecosystems around the world.
It was the combination of rarity, craftsmanship and the 
Rolex name that drew collectors in.
Yet the true value of these bids will be measured by the 
lives and landscapes they help to transform.
Source: Watch Analytics
