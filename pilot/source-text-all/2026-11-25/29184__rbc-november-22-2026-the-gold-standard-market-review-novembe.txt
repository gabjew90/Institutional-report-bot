November 22, 2026
The Gold Standard: Market Review – November 20
In this publication, we review detailed changes within global equity, fixed income, commodity, and
currency markets.
• The S&P500 decreased by 8.6% and the VIX index increased to 35.9 (16.6). 10Y yields decreased by
19bps to 4.95%, explained by real yields decreasing by 11bps to 2.78% and inflation expectations
decreasing by 8bps to 2.17%. The 2–10y yield curve decreased by 2bps to 0.29%. The DXY dollar index
increased by 0.4% to 103.9. Larger foreign exchange moves include a weakening of the South African
rand (3.8%) and Mexican peso (3.4%).
• Gold decreased by 8.9% to $3,538/oz and physical gold ETFs recorded outflows of 284koz. Silver
decreased by 15.6% to $41.16/oz and physical silver ETFs recorded outflows of 4moz. CFTC net long
gold positions decreased by 24k to 157k contracts and net long silver positions decreased by 1k to
15k contracts.
• Gold equities decreased by 16.2%, while gold equity ETFs realized inflows of $378m. Silver equity
ETFs realized outflows of $98m. Relative to the index, notable weekly movers include Seabridge Gold
(-30.2%), Coeur Mining Inc (-23.6%), Sinda (-23.2%), Eldorado Gold (-21.6%), I-80 Gold (-21.2%). Short
position data was reported in the U.S. this week, notable outliers include an increase in Hycroft
Mining, Novagold, Fortuna Mining, I-80 Gold, Eldorado Gold, and a decrease in Perpetua Resources.
• Copper prices decreased by 9.9% to $5.57/lb, WTI decreased by 5.2% to $83.98/bbl, and Bitcoin prices
decreased by 8.5% to $59k.
