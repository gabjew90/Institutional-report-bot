The Weekly Brief
Thought of the week
High-quality government bonds are currently at attractive yields, consistent
with healthy forward returns. The unexpectedly big magnitude of government
and corporate issuance in 2026 was a key driver of higher government bond
yields, as it intensified the competition for capital. Issuers were not helped
either by the surge in energy prices, which prompted a major reassessment of
central bank rate expectations. Looking ahead, the pace of upward revisions to
AI-related spending and borrowing is unlikely to be repeated next year, and we
expect the major Western central banks to raise rates only modestly further.
History also suggests that the current starting yield is consistent with strong
forward returns on a medium-term view. High-quality government bonds
therefore offer compelling income today, and a source of diversification if
economic growth were to disappoint (as we write in our 2027 Investment
Outlook).
