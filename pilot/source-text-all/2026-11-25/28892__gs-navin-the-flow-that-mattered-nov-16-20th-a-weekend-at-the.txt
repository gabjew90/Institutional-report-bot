For the Week that was, EM Asia ex-China markets saw foreign net selling of US$7.4bn w/w, driven by
Taiwan (-US$6.0bn) and Korea (-US$3.9bn) but partly offset by India (+US$2.7bn). Non-Asia EMs saw
net buying of US$0.2bn w/w, led by Brazil (+US$0.1bn) and the UAE (+US$0.1bn). Southbound saw
inflows of US$2.1bn (ytd +US$55bn). Taiwan (+US$2.5bn) and Korea (+US$1.7bn) saw retail buying this
week. Ytd, Asian markets have seen US$90bn retail inflows.
Top SB Bought Stock: Tencent, Semi Manufacturing, Meituan
Top SB Sold Stocks: Baba, The Tracker Fund of HK and BOC HK
Other Points:
1) November HF Positioning: According to GS Prime Services, Asia focused Fundamental L/S managers
are down 1.6% mtd (ytd 12.4%). Net Leverage for Asia Fundamental L/S managers fell sharply by 47.4
points m/m to 10%, reaching the lowest levels in our records (over a decade), amid the recent equity
selloff. Global Prime book continued to see large net selling flows. Selling was observed across Asian
markets: Taiwan and Korea continue to dominate the selling flows.

2) Net allocation in Korea and Taiwan declined to 2.3% (11th percentile 1-year) and 4.4% (24th percentile
1-year) respectively. Allocations to Asia continued to decline sharply, but remain significantly overweight
vs. benchmarks (vs. 20% OW around mid-June). InfoTech remains the most overweight sector in the
region (10.3% OW, down from a ~22% peak in June), while Financials is the most underweight sector.
3) Prelim MF Allocation: Based on prelim October data from EPFR, EM funds are most OW
Brazil/Mexico, and most UW Taiwan. Over the past month, EM funds have raised exposure the most in
Thailand, and trimmed most in Taiwan. Sectorally, funds have cut exposure to China Industrials and
Taiwan Semis the most over the past 1 & 3 months
Source: Goldman Sachs FICC and Equities, Prime Services, EPFR, as of 19–20 Nov 2026.
