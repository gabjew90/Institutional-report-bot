Industry View
Attractive
Transformation Takes Time. On-quarter SaaS results fell short of expectations 
following a strong rally into Q3 prints, with roughly two-thirds of SMID-cap SaaS 
stocks underperforming the index since reporting. The results underscored the scale 
of the AI transition: products must move from assisting work to doing it, pricing 
must evolve from seats toward consumption, and sales motions increasingly require 
forward-deployed engineers, longer trials and heavier discounting. Together, these 
changes are pushing growth stabilization—and eventual reacceleration—further 
out. Heading into off-quarter prints, we expect SaaS shares to take a breather, with 
outperformance concentrated in a more selective set of names. We favor DOCU & 
TTAN into Q3 results.
Company Specific Views Into the Quarter:
WDAY (UW): When Does AI Move the Growth Needle? 
• Heading into Workday’s Q3, sentiment is reserved, with investors expecting 
little to resolve the debate around growth. Investors want evidence that 
Agentic AI ARR can lift overall growth, but Q3 is likely too early given the 
ongoing agent ramp. With 25% of customers using Agentic AI solutions and ~
$600 million in Agentic AI ARR, these products represent just 6% of total 
ARR. Our analysis suggests Agentic AI ARR must grow 65%+ YoY to drive 
upside to consensus FY28 subscription revenue growth of ~11%. That implies 
~$400 million in net new ARR, similar to the past year’s additions, making it 
achievable. However, revenue recognition lags suggest upside would emerge

ZM (EW): Better Checks, but the Growth Bar Remains High. 
• We see a manageable Q3 setup, supported by improving partner checks and 
undemanding valuation (~11x CY27 EV/FCF). All three partners are tracking in 
line to slightly ahead of plan, improving from mixed Q2 trends. One reseller 
increasingly views Zoom as a beneficiary of UC/CC consolidation, though AI 
monetization remains limited and Microsoft Teams/E5 bundling poses 
renewal risk. 
• We expect Q3 revenue to beat guidance by ~$15M (~1.2%), implying 5.1% YoY 
growth, consistent with historical beats. We model Enterprise growth 
accelerating ~50bps to 8.3%, aided by easier comps, while Online moderates 
to ~0.3%. We expect Q4 guidance in line to modestly ahead of consensus, 
lifting FY27 revenue guidance ~$19M to ~$5.11B (+4.9% vs. ~4.5% prior). We 
model ~41% non-GAAP operating margins, ~70bps above consensus. 
• The bigger debate is whether Zoom's expanding portfolio can drive sustained 
acceleration rather than merely offset maturity in Meetings. Management's 6-
8% FY30 growth target requires Enterprise to approach double-digit growth, 
Online to stabilize, and NDR to exceed 100%. While Contact Center traction is 
encouraging, ZCX, Phone and paid AI have yet to demonstrate sufficient scale 
to meaningfully accelerate consolidated growth. Valuation is supportive, 
but greater confidence in the guided growth acceleration is needed to 
become more constructive. Remain EW. Please see our standalone preview 
for more detail: 3Q27 Preview: Better Checks, but the Growth Bar Remains 
High. 
DOCU (EW): Better-Than-Expected Checks on IAM Monetization Make Us 
Incrementally Positive
• We are tactically positive Docusign into Q3 as the stock is only trading at 
10X CY28 FCF, Q3 guidance looks conservative on an easier 40bps YoY 
comp, and IAM monetization could surprise investors to the upside given 
our constructive customer checks. The Q3 guide implies a significant 
downtick in sequential dollars added (~$12 million vs. average of $16 million 
over past three years). A similar beat as the first half of FY27 would imply 
subscription revenue accelerates to 9%+ YoY, a bullish outcome as investors 
could see a more realistic path toward double digit growth, which is not 
priced in at 10X FCF and could drive similar multiple expansion as peer Box 
has had to ~16X FCF as that company has shown growth acceleration. 
• Customer checks pointed to meaningful IAM monetization. Our recent

given its product maturity, established workflows, and integration 
advantages, although emerging AI-native legal vendors represent a potential 
longer-term threat as customers increasingly seek end-to-end automation. 
BOX (EW): Can Recent Bullish Sentiment Continue? Probably. 
• While Box stock has outperformed the median software stock by >15% 
over the past month and has seen its multiple expand to 16X CY28 FCF on 
the back of bullish investor sentiment around its AI positioning, Q3 likely 
keeps the momentum going given accelerating growth. Assuming another 
~$2 million beat to Q3's guidance, this would imply total revenue accelerates 
from 9% in Q2 to 10% in Q3 on a reported basis.
• Customer checks suggest Box's AI capabilities are resonating with 
customers and supporting Enterprise Advanced adoption, but the pace of 
incremental monetization is uncertain. We spoke with two Box customers 
who highlighted growing value from AI-powered document generation, 
extraction, and workflow automation. One customer cited tangible ROI from 
automating contract analysis and document management, including reduced 
headcount requirements and replacement of third-party software, while 
expecting Box penetration to increase from ~45% to 60-65% of corporate 
employees over the next two years. The customer also expects ~11% annual 
growth in document-related consumption, supporting incremental usage-
based revenue. Separately, a large healthcare customer upgraded to 
Enterprise Advanced to access enhanced AI capabilities and automation, 
highlighting willingness to pay for higher-tier functionality. However, this 
customer expects spending to remain largely flat, with no plans to expand 
seats. Both customers continue to use Box alongside Microsoft, but are 
debating which vendor to standardize on over the long-term. 
TTAN (OW): Constructive Weather & End-Market Data
• While ServiceTitan stock is up >40% off the recent lows post the Q2 print 
(see 2Q27 Results - AI Adoption Is Ramping, But Change Management Takes 
Times), it has still underperformed the median software stock by >35% YTD. 
We are constructive into Q3 given: 1) HARDI data showing a meaningful 
acceleration off the lows in June; 2) Weather data showing Q3 was
