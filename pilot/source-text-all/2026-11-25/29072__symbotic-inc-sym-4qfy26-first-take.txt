Symbotic Inc. (SYM): 4QFY26 First Take
23 November 2026 | 4:53PM EST
Synopsis 
Our first take is that results are relatively solid with EBITDA results and guidance 
moderately above the Street (FactSet), albeit with revenue guidance for 1QFY27 
below consensus. Comments in the 10-K on backlog imply ~$3.6 bn to be 
recognized in FY27, in line with consensus for FY27 revenue. Overall, we think 
this explains the muted initial stock reaction in the aftermarket. 
We expect the expanded commercial agreement with Walmart, progress on 
customer engagements (both at Exol and Symbotic), system starts, margins, and 
FCF/capital allocation to be focus items on the call.  
Results 
Symbotic reported 4QFY26 (September) revenue of $778 mn (up 26% yoy, up 8% 
qoq), which was near the upper end of the guidance range of $760 mn to $780 mn, 
and compared to GS at $778 mn and the Street (FactSet) at $774 mn.  
Symbotic reported a 4QFY26 adjusted gross margin of 24.8% compared to 25.0% in 
3QFY26 and 22.1% in 4QFY25. 
Symbotic reported non-GAAP EBITDA (ex. SBC) of $114 mn vs. $95 mn last quarter, 
and compared to GS at $105 mn, the Street at $104 mn, and guidance of $100 mn to 
$105 mn. 
Non-GAAP EPS (ex. SBC) was $0.17. We note that this is the first time the company 
has provided a non-GAAP EPS bridge in the earnings release. 
FCF was roughly $353 mn in the quarter, and the company used ~$38 mn for 
acquisitions of strategic investments. 
Backlog declined slightly to $22.3 bn per the 10-K, down from $22.5 bn last quarter. 
The company expects to recognize 16% over the next 12 months, implying ~$3.6 bn 
(in line with consensus for FY27 revenue).   
Symbotic and Walmart recently announced an expanded commercial agreement to 
bring further automation capabilities to 25 US regional distribution centers. The 
agreement would also support Walmart’s global supply chain. The agreement 
includes an initial commitment of approximately $6 bn in spend, covering work over 
several years across multiple locations, per the original press release.

Outlook 
Symbotic guided 1QFY27 (December) revenue between $805 mn and $825 mn, which 
compares to GS at $837 mn and the Street at $822 mn. 
1QFY27 adjusted EBITDA guidance of $110 mn to $120 mn compares to GS at $117 mn

Rating and price target 
We are Sell rated on the stock. Our 12-month price target is $42. Specifically, our price 
target is based on: 1) 90% weight of our base case valuation of $41 using 50X applied to 
our FY28 EBITDA forecast discounted back; 2) 5% weight of our bull case valuation of 
$74 using 75X applied to a bull case view for FY28 EBITDA (that is 25% above our base 
case discounted back) and 3) 5% weight of our bear case valuation of $20 using 30X 
applied to a bear case view for FY28 EBITDA (that is 25% below our base case estimate 
discounted back). 
Key upside risks to our view relate to 1) Customer traction — Faster or more customer 
wins, particularly with new customers, could lead to upside; 2) Acceleration in Symbotic’s 
ability to install systems at customers — If Symbotic is able to manufacture/install these 
systems faster than we anticipate, this could positively impact the company’s results; 3) 
A potential acceleration in investment into warehouse automation technologies — An 
increase in warehouse operators investment in warehouse automation technology could 
positively impact Symbotic (these systems require upfront investment but also have a 
strong ROI); 4) Better margins - faster improvements in cost structure or more fixed cost 
leverage than we expect could drive more positive results.

GS Factor Profile 
The Goldman Sachs Factor Profile provides investment context for a stock by comparing key attributes to the market (i.e. our universe of rated stocks) 
and its sector peers. The four key attributes depicted are: Growth, Financial Returns, Multiple (e.g. valuation) and Integrated (a composite of Growth, 
Financial Returns and Multiple). Growth, Financial Returns and Multiple are calculated by using normalized ranks for specific metrics for each stock. The 
normalized ranks for the metrics are then averaged and converted into percentiles for the relevant attribute. The precise calculation of each metric may 
vary depending on the fiscal year, industry and region, but the standard approach is as follows: 
Growth is based on a stock’s forward-looking sales growth, EBITDA growth and EPS growth (for financial stocks, only EPS and sales growth), with a 
higher percentile indicating a higher growth company. Financial Returns is based on a stock’s forward-looking ROE, ROCE and CROCI (for financial 
stocks, only ROE), with a higher percentile indicating a company with higher financial returns. Multiple is based on a stock’s forward-looking P/E, P/B, 
price/dividend (P/D), EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF and EV/Debt Adjusted Cash Flow (DACF) (for financial stocks, only P/E, P/B and P/D), with a higher percentile 
indicating a stock trading at a higher multiple. The Integrated percentile is calculated as the average of the Growth percentile, Financial Returns 
percentile and (100% - Multiple percentile). 
Financial Returns and Multiple use the Goldman Sachs analyst forecasts at the fiscal year-end at least three quarters in the future. Growth uses inputs 
for the fiscal year at least seven quarters in the future compared with the year at least three quarters in the future (on a per-share basis for all metrics). 
For a more detailed description of how we calculate the GS Factor Profile, please contact your GS representative.
