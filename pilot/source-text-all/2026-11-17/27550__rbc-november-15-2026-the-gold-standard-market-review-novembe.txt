The Gold Standard: Market Review – November 13
In this publication, we review detailed changes within global equity, fixed income, commodity, and
currency markets.
• The S&P500 increased by 2.5% and the VIX index decreased to 19.3 (-6.3). 10Y yields increased by
28bps to 5.14%, explained by real yields increasing by 27bps to 2.89% and inflation expectations
increasing by 1bps to 2.25%. The 2–10y yield curve decreased by 2bps to 0.29%. The DXY dollar index
increased by 1.1% to 103.4. Larger foreign exchange moves include a weakening of the South African
rand (2.4%), and the Swiss franc (2.2%).
• Gold decreased by 4.6% to $3,884/oz and physical gold ETFs recorded outflows of 217koz. Silver
decreased by 6.1% to $48.74/oz and physical silver ETFs recorded inflows of 0moz. CFTC net long
gold positions decreased by 9k to 171k contracts and net long silver positions increased by 1k to 18k
contracts.
• Gold equities decreased by 6.8%, while gold equity ETFs realized outflows of $511m. Silver equity
ETFs realized outflows of $18m. Relative to the index, notable weekly movers included Novagold
(-12.0%), DPM Metals (-11.4%), Eldorado Gold (-11.2%), Hecla Mining Co (-11.1%), Osisko Gold Group
(-10.8%). Short position data was reported in the U.S. this week – notable outliers include an increase
in Hycroft Mining, Novagold Resources, Fortuna Mining, i-80 Gold, Eldorado Gold, and Triple Flag
Precious Metals, and a decrease in Perpetua Resources.
• Copper prices decreased by 2.4% to $6.18/lb, WTI decreased by 2.5% to $88.56/bbl, and Bitcoin prices
decreased by 9.7% to $68.3k.
