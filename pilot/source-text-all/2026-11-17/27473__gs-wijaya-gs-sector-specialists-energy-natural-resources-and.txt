Three Things
CNQ. Well – we talked about more consolidation north of the border for some time now – and another
supportive headline on that front: *CANADIAN NATURAL TO BUY ADVANTAGE FOR ~C$2.53B
TOTAL VALUE… The desk has seen elevated inbounds on SMID CAD E&Ps for the last couple of
months – and we have seen more of a shift from focus on the US small cap E&Ps to Canadians. It’s
important to note the recent premiums that have been announced on these CAD deals – expecting more
questions on readacross for the US names should we see more consolidation in the space. Don’t think this
deal will be thesis shifting for most of the folks in the US. We are talking about a C$2.53 bn transaction on
a company with a mkt cap of C$144 bn. No changes to target of 100% of FCF return to shareholders on
share repo and would think little concern here with the cash generation we are seeing across the board in
E&P. Looking at the Advantage slide deck from October (link – 86% of production tied to Nat gas with
14% oil / liquids (2026E), three year capital spend plan on major infrastructure is largely complete and
4Q2026 production levels were expected to be maintained through YE2027. We think US HFs have been
more cautious on CNQ for the better part of the year – WCS wider, AECO (small but more narrative
driven) exposure, willingness to do M&A … that said - WCS bottom ticked $30 under and has been
tightening (albeit off a very low base recently) and with the crude flush - some debate on a flight to
quality. Names to watch today on the back of the proposed deal: TOU, OVV, WCP, SDE.
FANG. We talked about more M&A north of the border above, but that doesn’t mean the headlines have
been slower in the US: Diamondback Energy, Inc. Announces Core Midland Basin Acquisition,
Diamondback to Buy Double Eagle Units for $2.15B & 15.56M Shrs. Think it has been well telegraphed
(link that the company has been looking at potential inorganic growth in the Permian and shareholder
letter should have previewed this too from 3Q EPS: ‘While we expect to maintain consistent returns, we
anticipate the next decade will look different. Upstream consolidation in the Permian will continue, albeit
at a more measured pace given how much has already occurred to date’ (link Deal is really about
highlighting undeveloped inventory – 176k net acres in the core of Midland with 68% of the asset
undeveloped. Transaction details pointing to leverage neutral, immediately accretive on CFPS + NAV/sh
and valued at 4x 2027 EBITDA. Outside of the deal – company is also updating early results on the
Barnett development … exceeding internal expectations for full field development and provides
momentum for full field development in 2027+… recall Barnett has been in focus for some time now. On
positioning: we think specialists have been more cautious here relative to Large Cap peers – where
generally there have been more positive conversations on COP / DVN recently. Valuation jumps out on the
comps sheet – with the stock trading at 15% 2027 FCF yield vs EOG at 9% and OXY at 13% on GS
numbers …

Brent. From Rob Quinn on our Futures desk (link ‘According to Commitment of Traders covering through
November 10th, Managed Money selling of Brent oil futures remained sizeable as the US mid-term
elections caused another leg lower in price. Resultant net positioning fell below the 2 year median.
Liquidation from physical participants also contributed. Subsequently, discretionary strategies likely
reinitiated longs. Going forward, a small hiccup in the diplomatic process could invigorate sidelined bulls.
And for the moment, larger CTA pressure has been avoided.’
