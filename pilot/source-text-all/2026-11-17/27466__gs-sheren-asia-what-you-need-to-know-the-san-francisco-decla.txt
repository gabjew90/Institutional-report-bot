Declaration Matters More Than the Headline Suggests 16 Nov 2026

16 Nov 2026
The San Francisco Declaration matters because it potentially takes restrictions on China’s access to
advanced AI compute from a unilateral US policy toward a multilateral one. That is what makes the San
Francisco Declaration interesting for equities: greater technology bifurcation may ultimately strengthen
the investment case for the Chinese hardware supply chain.
• Evidence the existing regime leaks: Estimates from SemiAnalysis and Epoch AI point to substantial
volumes of restricted compute continuing to reach China, while DOJ cases provide a documented lower
bound on diversion. The existing chip-control regime is clearly not airtight.

Source: Marquee MarketView, 16 November 2026, Past performance is not indicative of future results
• What San Francisco potentially changes: San Francisco does not close the offshore cloud loophole, but it
potentially creates the multilateral framework necessary to address it. That is important because effective
compute restrictions become considerably harder to circumvent if allies coordinate both physical chip
controls and remote access.

Source: GIR, 16 November 2026, "Contextualizing implications of evolving US policies around rented
compute access for China's AI models/CSPs/data centers"
• Why it matters for China AI: The implication for China AI is greater bifurcation. Scarcer frontier
compute increases the value of compute efficiency and should favor vertically integrated Chinese
platforms with existing domestic compute, distribution and monetization.

Source: GIR, 16 November 2026, "Contextualizing implications of evolving US policies around rented
compute access for China's AI models/CSPs/data centers"
• Positioning remains differentiated. Mutual funds show relatively little interest in Chinese AI labs, short
interest has increased in MiniMax and Z.AI, while Southbound has generally been buying the group aside
from Alibaba and Kuaishou.
The commercial question is therefore not simply which Chinese internet platform benefits from a more
bifurcated AI ecosystem, but who supplies the hardware required to build it. If San Francisco is another
step toward structural technology bifurcation, I would focus on the China AI hardware and ask where
localization creates the next earnings opportunity.
Source: Goldman Sachs Global Banking & Markets (FICC & Equities), Marquee, GS GIR. Data as of 16 Nov 2026.
