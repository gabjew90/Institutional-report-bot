We remind investors that oil prices are somewhat elevated but refined product
prices (and crack spreads) are near record levels (Exhibit 1 and Americas Oil: Ignore the
facts in front of you...the crack-implied oil price is >$90/bbl).
While clearly the blockage of the Strait of Hormuz is partially responsible for elevated
prices, we remind investors that the ongoing Russia-Ukraine War (in its fifth year of its
current incarnation) has evolved into a massive refinery-attacking drone strategy.
We combine real-time satellite imaging with AI web-scraping (with a focus on Ukrainian
and Russian language sources). Satellite imaging week combined with reports
corroborate 2+ mln bopd of outages just in the last week (Exhibit 2). That is roughly
2% of global refining - a significant amount (especially when coupled with the blockage of
the Strait of Hormuz).
The lost refining capacity has forced Russia to ban refined product exports for the
remainder of the year (Russia extends diesel, gasoline export bans until end of January
2027 | Reuters), tightening the global market and driving up refined product prices and
crack spreads.
We are stunned by the realization that Ukrainian drones have struck ~90% of Russia’s
refining capacity in total and ~80% of that capacity just in 2026 (Exhibit 3). It is a
technological and military breakthrough that we expect to continue.
Even putting the Strait of Hormuz to the side, without some form of peace or truce between
Russia and Ukraine, we’d expect global disruptions to continue at high levels, perpetuating
the price difference between refined products and crude (and thus crack spreads) and thus
benefitting refiners and integrated oil companies at least into 2027 (ExxonMobil,
Outperform, and Chevron, Market-Perform).
Our approach to real-time determination of outages is easily updated upon client
request. We provide real-time fire detection images of all Russian refineries (Exhibit 4
to Exhibit 31).
