RBC Monthly Valuation Recap – Telecom &
Communications Infrastructure, August 2026
Monthly review of valuation trends in the Telecom & Communications Infrastructure sectors
Stock performance and trading multiples
• August stock returns by sub-sector were as follows: Datacenter REITs = 0.6%; Int'l Datacenters = -4.9%;
US Towers = 2.8%; Int'l Towers = 0.2%; Telco & Wireless = 7.6%; Hybrid/Other Infrastructure = -2.7%;
Cable = 14.6%; and Fiber Infrastructure = -9.8%. This compares to the total return of S&P 500 of 3%
and RMZ of -3%.
• Top performing stocks during the month were carriers OPTU, T and CMCSA, with returns of 27.6%,
11.4% and 11.1%, respectively. Underperforming stocks were CCOI, VNET and IRM, with returns of
-29.3%, -15.5% and -5.7%, respectively.
• By sub-sector on a forward EV/EBITDA basis: Telco & Wireless (0.3 turns), Datacenter REITs (0.1 turns),
and Cable (0.1 turns) all saw multiple expansion while Int'l Towers and Net Lease Datacenter REITs
were flat in August; all other subsectors saw multiple contraction: Fiber Infrastructure (-0.2 turns), US
Towers (-0.2 turns), International DC (-1.7 turns), Hybrid/Other Infra (-2.1 turns).
• On P/NTM AFFO, Datacenter REIT multiples contracted an average of 0.3 turns (DLR: -0.6 turns, EQIX:
+0.3 turns) and US Tower multiples expanded an average of 0.2 turns (AMT: +0.1 turns, CCI: -0.2 turns,
SBAC: +0.6 turns).
• On EV/NTM EBITDA, stocks with the greatest multiple expansion were EQIX (+0.4 turns), T (+0.4 turns),
and SBAC (+0.3 turns). The stocks with the greatest multiple contraction were AD (-1.2 turns), IRM
(-1.1 turns), and VNET (-0.6 turns).
Sector commentary and investor sentiment
Carriers: Telecom continued its rebound from June lows, overcoming reactions to potential SpaceX
impacts with some unwind activity likely contributing to the move higher. Cableco outperformance
during the month was likely aided by unwind activity as broadband competitive positioning remains
challenged. AT&T remains our favored carrier pick. Towers: SpaceX’s terrestrial network comments
pressured towercos initially and to a greater extent than telcos, but investors have since dismissed the
threat given the infrastructure and execution risk involved. Our preferred picks on a relative basis include
AMT (on the basis of earnings growth) and CCI (most attractive dividend yield). Datacenters and AI
Infra: Demand fundamentals affecting listed operators such as DLR, EQIX, IRM, and NXT remain robust,
with favorable earnings at each company and DLR and EQIX uncharacteristically raising their multi-year
forecasts at mid-year. Currently we see greatest upside at IRM based on prospects for higher guidance.
Short interest
• Names with the largest increases in short interest during August were OPTU (4.3%), CHTR (3.5%), and
UNIT (2.2%), increasing to 10.8%, 30.0% and 11.8%, respectively. Stocks with the largest declines in
short interest were DBRG (-2.7%), ECHO (-1.2%), and TMUS (-1.1%), decreasing to 8.0%, 21.4% and
3.5%, respectively.
• Names with the largest increases in days to cover during August were UNIT (2.0), OPTU (1.3), and
CHTR (1.3), increasing to 8.7, 2.9 and 7.0, respectively. Stocks with the largest declines in days to cover
were DBRG (-2.2), TMUS (-0.9), and IRM (-0.6), decreasing to 4.2, 3.3 and 4.0, respectively.
