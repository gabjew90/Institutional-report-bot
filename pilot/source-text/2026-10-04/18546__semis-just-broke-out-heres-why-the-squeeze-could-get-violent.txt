The SOX squeeze
SOX is breaking through key resistance, but despite the move, we're still nowhere near the extreme
overbought conditions usually associated with a proper squeeze. Taiwan, the mother ship, continues to
lead, while the fundamental backdrop remains remarkably strong.
Memory remains tight, AI demand keeps delivering and expectations have already gone through a
meaningful reset.
The technical breakout is arriving just as the fundamental story refuses to roll over.

The fundamental squeeze
JPM's latest memory work argues that supply/demand tightness could persist through 2028, with Micron
seeing CY27/28 becoming even tighter than CY26.
Demand continues to outrun supply, cleanroom capacity remains constrained and HBM demand is still
growing considerably faster than conventional server DRAM.
The technical squeeze has a fundamental squeeze underneath it.
JPM
The data refuses to roll over
Underneath the charts, the semi data remains extremely strong.
Taiwan server ODM revenues are running +130% YoY, Aspeed +118%, while Korean DRAM exports
have surged +412% YoY to a fresh record.
Meanwhile, DDR5 and DDR4 spot prices continue to rise and trade well above contract prices.
This still doesn't look like a cycle that is running out of fuel.
GS
The reset
The interesting part isn't just the fundamental strength. Expectations had already been reset.

GS says expectations for conventional memory and HBM pricing were largely washed out and are now
starting to recover, with more investors looking for upside to current pricing assumptions.
That's exactly the type of backdrop where improving price action can force people back into the trade.
Reset expectations + improving fundamentals + a technical breakout is a decent recipe for a squeeze.
