Key Takeaways 
◼ 
The GS Equity Fundamental L/S Performance Estimate2 fell -0.45% between 9/25 and 10/1 (vs MSCI World TR -0.67%), driven almost 
entirely by beta as alpha finished ~flat. The GS Equity Systematic L/S Performance Estimate2 rose +1.14% during the same period, driven by 
alpha of +1.27% on the back of short side gains, partially offset by beta of -0.13%. 
◼ 
Overall book Gross leverage unchanged at 308.0% (55th percentile 1-year) and Net leverage unchanged at 77.4% (29th percentile 1-year).  
Overall book L/S ratio unchanged at 1.671 (28th percentile 1-year).  Fundamental L/S Gross leverage -1.7 pts to 206.2% (21st percentile 1-
year) and Net leverage -2.1 pts to 53.0% (7th percentile 1-year).  
◼ 
Global equities were net bought (+0.7 SDs 1-year), driven by long buys outpacing short sales (2.7 to 1).  
o 
All major regions (sans EM Asia) were net bought, led in $ terms by Europe and North America.  
o 
Macro Products were net bought at the fastest pace in 2 months, driven by short covers and to a lesser extent long buys, while Single 
Stocks were net sold for a 3rd straight week, driven by short sales. 
o 
Health Care, Utilities, and Energy were the most $ net bought global sectors this week, while Comm Svcs, Cons Disc, and Info Tech 
were the most net sold. 
◼ 
Health Care was by far the most $ net bought global sector this week and saw the largest % net buying in 6 months, driven by short 
covers in North America and long buys in Europe. 
◼ 
Info Tech was among the most $ net sold global sectors this week and saw the largest % net selling in 6 weeks, driven by long sales in 
EM Asia and Europe.
