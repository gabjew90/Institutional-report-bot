Holding
The massive Russell trend line is holding for now, with the index back above the 200-day moving
average, although fragility remains.
Washed-out trades like Russell are supposed to feel uncomfortable at potential turning points. The
obvious fear is that the huge trend line finally breaks.
That's what makes the setup interesting. If support holds and rates give even a little relief, the squeeze
potential is considerable.

Russell positioning remains washed out
GS notes that Russell 2K positioning remains deeply depressed. Systematic exposure sits in just the 7th
percentile, while leveraged funds are carrying record shorts according to CFTC data through
September 22.
Softer PCE and payrolls have helped trigger some covering, with around $900mn of open interest
coming out since September 29 and RTY funding spreads richening both outright and versus SPX.
The squeeze fuel is there. All that's missing is some help from rates.

It won't take much
The interesting part is how aggressively Russell squeezed during Friday's initial move lower in rates,
before yields reversed higher.
Despite yields reversing higher, Russell held onto much of the move, opening up a short-term
divergence versus rates.
The message is pretty clear: it probably won't take much relief in rates to get Russell squeezing
aggressively again.
The trigger remains rates.
