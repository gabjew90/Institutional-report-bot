Tech refuses to break
Tech has absorbed a brutal rates shock without breaking, while the underlying AI capex cycle
continues to accelerate.
Now NDX is pressing against a major breakout level just as the rates setup is starting to get interesting.
If yields and bond vol finally take a breather, one of tech's biggest headwinds could quickly turn into a
tailwind.
The fundamentals held up. The tape held up. Now comes the breakout test.
The big NDX level
NDX is closing in on the massive 31,200 level in futures after months of consolidation. The 50-day has
turned higher again, adding to the improving technical setup.
A clean close above 31,200 and things could get very squeezy, very quickly.

Can’t keep this market down
As Privorotsky points out, another two-ton freight truck hit the rates market yesterday. USTs sold off
hard, OAT/Bund blew through 130bp and the stress finally started bleeding into BTPs.
Normally, you would expect equities to care. Instead, the S&P barely moved, while both Nasdaq and
Russell managed to finish green.
Europe and rates blew up. US equities simply compartmentalized the entire thing.
Pretty impressive.
The AI machine isn't rate sensitive
One of the more interesting features of this cycle is just how insensitive the underlying AI buildout has
become to rates.
GS expects hyperscaler debt issuance to surge to $420bn in 2027, yet notes that interest expense
remains a very small part of hyperscaler earnings. In other words, higher yields haven't been enough
to derail the capex machine.
That's interesting if yields finally reverse lower. The spending cycle hasn't needed lower rates. Tech
multiples certainly wouldn't mind them. (Captures debt issued by the following companies: Alphabet,
Amazon, Microsoft, Meta, and Oracle.)

The upgrades aren't over

The AI capex upgrade cycle continues to defy gravity.
MS now sees combined hyperscaler capex at around $1.4tn in 2027, well above consensus at roughly
$1–1.1tn, and expects another round of upward revisions into Q3 earnings.
The market keeps waiting for capex to roll over. Estimates keep going the other way.

Plenty of firepower
The spending numbers look enormous, but so do the balance sheets. MS has hyperscalers at just 0.4x
net leverage with cash equal to 132% of debt.
The AI buildout isn't running into a balance-sheet wall yet.

What if bond vol takes a breather?
This time around, tech simply refused to care about the explosion in bond volatility. Which raises the
obvious question: what happens if bond vol finally takes a breather?
Previous reversals in bond volatility have coincided with sharp moves higher in the NDX.
Tech held up while bond vol exploded. Imagine the pressure if that headwind reverses.
