LSEG Workspace
Protection is interesting
The obvious problem with hedging something that has already become a market favorite is usually
price.
Not necessarily here.
SX7E downside screens better than several other cross-asset tail hedges when current hedge costs are
compared with historical drawdowns. Bank put skew is elevated versus the rest of Europe, but that
actually makes put spreads relatively attractive.
Their Dec-26 30D–20D put spread offers a >4x maximum payout.
If you think the rates problem eventually becomes a growth problem, banks offer an interesting place
to express it.
BofA
Buying the Russell reversal?
The Russell has been crushed by the rates move, but professional investors have started buying the
weakness.
Goldman's Robert Quinn notes that non-dealers bought around $3bn of Russell 2k futures between
September 1 and 22. Asset managers dominated the buying with $2.2bn, with smaller participation
from hedge funds and non-reportables.
The starting point matters: non-dealer positioning entered September at a multi-year low. The
question now is whether this contrarian buying is the beginning of a reversal, or gets overwhelmed by
deteriorating credit. Latest on small caps here.
