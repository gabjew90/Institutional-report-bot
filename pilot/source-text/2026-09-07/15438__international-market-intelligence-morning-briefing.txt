EMERGING MARKETS MONETIZATION – Ellen Wang / Victoria Campos 
The global momentum/tech rebound drove EM 7% higher since the July-end low, 
despite choppy price action. Broadening and AI continue to dominate the narrative in 
EM, with the recent global macro backdrop net supportive. We are bullish on EM 
given (i) improving macro; (ii) weaker USD; (iii) AI CapEx tailwinds, but continue to 
expect divergence in EM performance at the country/sector level and view country-
relative trades as more relevant for the monetization strategy in this environment.  
•         EM MONETIZATION MENU – Key themes and trades include: (i) TACTICALLY 
LONG China H-shares over A-shares: the pair has been trading well in July given 
the momentum rotation. Despite this outperformance, H-shares still lag A-shares YTD 
and we see further room into China Tech earnings next week. (ii) We also express 
this view via a TACTICALLY LONG in China Internet (KWEB). (iii) TACTICALLY 
LONG Taiwan following the Global Tech selloff in July. Taiwan offers a less volatile 
exposure to AI and Asia Tech relative to Korea. 
• HEDGES – (i) We like long LatAm (ILF) as a diversifying play 
against weakness in the EM AI theme and for relative resilience 
against geopolitical risks. (ii) India (INDA) equity strength has been 
driven in part by a rotation during the AI de-risking phase, but foreign 
investor flows have also turned more supportive again, supported by 
improving macro. You may consider a L/S pair with LONG India and 
SHORT Southeast Asia (ASEA). 
  
  
POSITIONING INTELLIGENCE 
POSITIONING INTELLIGENCE IS NOT A PRODUCT OF RESEARCH 
Summary: Not a lot has changed at the aggregate level over the past week, despite 
market gyrations, but there are some potential themes emerging such as further re-


[Page 9]
grossing by HFs, a pause in US Health Care, and a bottoming of Cyclicals vs. 
Defensives. 
Key Points: 
•         HF Re-grossing picked up this week:  Globally HFs added gross 
exposure on 4 of the past 5 days for a +1.3z increase in the past week, the 
highest since late June. 
•         Net changes were muted this week…: HF flows were mixed day-to-day 
but ended with a small sell skew.  We saw a similar thing for our US TPM and 
the level of positioning for our US TPM remains at 40th %-tile, while global HF 
nets are 40-50th %-tile on a 5yr basis. 
• …But sentiment turning less constructive:  Both retail and institutional 
sentiment appear to be reversing lower / pausing, after prior increases. 
• US Health Care could be topping out for now:  Retail sentiment is starting 
to drop from ATHs, HFs are selling, and ETF flows are pausing at highs. 
•         Cyclicals vs. Defensives…will strong growth put cyclicals back in 
favor?  Energy is being bought by HFs and relative net positioning between 
the groups is fairly neutral when excluding TMT and Financials.  In addition, 
Retail sentiment between the two is quite low.  The potential for cyclicals to 
outperform seems present, as long as yields don’t rise so quickly that 
equities turn very risk off.
