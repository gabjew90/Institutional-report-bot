## Marketcolour + HF Performance / Flows / Kering / Safran / Elux
/ Volvo / Sika / Signify ####
Ioannis Blekos
23 Oct 2026
Good Morning,
US equities closed sharply lower yesterday amid focus on rates (10Y UST -15bps to 4.95% = largest move of the
year) & Oil back to ytd highs: SPX -121bps to 7663 (SPW +73bps, VIX +13,7% to 18.49, Staples -331bps, Utils
+207bps, Tech -329bps, Comm svs -142bps), NDX -290bps to 28958 (now lower on 9 of 12 sessions), DJIA
+2bps to 52867. In Europe, Defensives (GSXEDEFS +1.8%) the standout with Pharma (GSXEPHAR 2.5%) and
Staples (GSXESTAP +2.0%) earnings beats. Bond Proxies (GSXEBOND +1.3%) bid as the reflexivity trade played
out.
 
Interesting: The SOX has underperformed the S&P500 by 13% over the last 9 sessions (e.g. since Mon 10/12),
biggest stretch of u/p in 20+ years over 9 days. The SOX now testing its 200-dam for the first time since June of 2025
 
Dispersion: 155 names in the S&P having >/= 2.0σ moves (highest since May 2025 US-China tariff truce), while 335
names in the S&P finished higher on the day (one of the widest spreads between S&P breadth and index performance
going back to the 90’s). 
 
Price Action: AI basket (GSTMTAIP): Now down 23% from its June highs, a similar drawdown we saw before
bouncing in July. High Beta Momentum (GSPRHIMO) has erased all of its YTD gains (was +70% in June), driven
by the AI correlated long leg. SPX ex. AI (SPXXAI) has outperformed the S&P by 6% MTD
 
Flows: Zero signs of offense being played around the AI theme on our pad yesterday. While we saw rotational flows
out of AI and into defensive pockets of the market during last week’s episode, we saw very little of that yesterday
despite the moves higher in Consumer, Utilities, and pockets of Financials.
 
HF Performance Update: GS PB had Fundamental L/S down 1.1% (-1.7sd), Systematics down 1.2% (-3.2sd), and
Multi-Strats down 1.4% (-2.5sd). This marked the first time since Covid that all three strategies were down more
than 1% on the same day. -> Our HF Positioning Pair (GSTHVIPP) finished down ~4.2%, its worst move since 2011,
and is now down 10.4% MTD. 
 
Kering - Q3 2026 Gucci retail (-9% on cfx) miss leads to 8% TP cut, remain Neutral. Link
 
SAFRAN first take – Q3 revenues in-line with consensus, Propulsion and Interiors -1% light whilst Equipment &
Defence 3% ahead. As we saw with GE, pace of Spares & Services growth slowed, to +17% and +19% respectively.
Guidance is unchanged for mid-twenties growth for both at the fy (9m run rate is still 24% and 32% respectively). GE
set the tone here : stock -3.5% despite raising guide, so we likely see some weakness today given in-line result and no
raise (indication -2%).
 
ELECTROLUX solid Q3, LFL growth +3.5% with some recovery in N AM (+2.4% vs cons -2.1%). Adjusted ebit is a
5% beat with lower losses in N Am than expected and LatAM ahead of expectations. The company moderate guide on
price/cost to neutral from positive previously.
 

VOLVO Q3 – Profits in-line thanks to strong CE and Penta, whilst Trucks missed (-6% below consensus) due to softer
revenues and slightly weaker than expected margins. FX tailwind was larger help than GSe (970 tailwind vs GSe 350).
There was also a small net benefit from tariffs in the quarter (+0.2bn). Orders are in line with cons but missed GS est
by -12%. Market guidance given for 2027 is for Europe to be flattish and N Am to be up ~13% next year – this is a little
more bullish on N Am than expected.
 
Sika Q3 – resilient, looks fine - org +4.6% was a decel vs +6% in Q2 but ahead of css +4%... FY26 guide reiterated.
Stronger than expected gm (flat yoy) showing Sika’s pricing power given inflationary / oil based input costs. EMEA
strong +6%, Germany, Middle East Strength (+DD) with a stronger September following heat waves in July/August.
 
SIGNIFY - messy print due to tariff refunds, underlying business still seeing downgrades, stock has been long
forgotten about / little discussed of late and trades right at the lows, but no fundamental reason for it to bounce today
(indication -2%)
 
Momentum has broken away from support levels and has re-tested September lows
