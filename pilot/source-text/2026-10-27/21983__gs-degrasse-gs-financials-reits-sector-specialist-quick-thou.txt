October 25th 2026; All references to "we/us/our" refer to the views and observations of the desk.
Another incredibly choppy week for Financials, where the mood didn’t align with the outperformance vs
the market. 3 things were the primary drivers of stocks last week, 1) HF specialist positioning, 2)
macro/rates, and 3) general sentiment towards AI-driven growth & activity – not on this list is earnings,
further frustrating fundamental investors.. A barbell combination of high quality defensives (P&C
Insurance, Card Networks, Senior housing REITs) with cheap/value risk-on (Regional banks, Capital
Light payments, Lodging REITs, life insurance) continued to outperform – however life insurance began
to leak towards the end of the week with credit spreads & investment portfolios back in focus. The large
rate move lower in the week served as a catalyst for a broad based rally in REITs, while earnings generally
included ‘higher rate related’ pauses/slowdowns on activity (with some exceptions), though this was
generally in-line with buyside expectations (per feedback).
Most debated earnings stocks last week: CBRE (magnitude of underperformance sharply debated in
context of the BOE surprise guide-down) .. ZION (Near term NII trends vs upside to guide .. ‘can you get
there’) …

debated) .. KRC ..
