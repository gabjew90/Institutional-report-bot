The unintended AI winner?
AI is getting hammered, but cybersecurity is telling a very different story. A wave of critical
vulnerabilities, AI-powered cyberattacks and growing institutional demand for defensive exposure are
creating a powerful setup. Cyber stocks are bucking the broader tech selloff, with momentum
accelerating. Could cybersecurity become the ultimate winner of the AI unwind?
Threats on the rise
Goldman's Privorotsky highlights the alarming pace of emerging cybersecurity threats, with critical PHP
exploits, a major Java vulnerability and a Python supply chain compromise hitting simultaneously.
Some of this may have contributed to yesterday's AI concerns. The irony? AI is dramatically lowering
the cost of discovering and exploiting vulnerabilities, while the attack surface keeps expanding. This
could drive a major acceleration in cybersecurity spending, with potentially significant knock-on
effects across software, cloud infrastructure and the broader digital economy.

Catalysts ahead
Cybersecurity has several potential catalysts ahead.
Ongoing exploits could trigger renewed demand for protection, while Q4 earnings may highlight
accelerating AI-driven security spending. The November 3 midterms could also bring tougher tech
regulation. The risks? Stretched valuations after CIBR's powerful rally, broader tech liquidation and a
potential sell-the-news reaction if the latest threats are contained.
