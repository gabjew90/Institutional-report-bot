U.S. Spot Bitcoin ETP Flows 
We estimate U.S. spot Bitcoin ETP preliminary net sales of $529mn on 
Wednesday, October 21, 2026, a reacceleration from the prior session’s net 
inflows. There were a handful of contributors to yesterday’s flows including 
BlackRock’s IBIT and Fidelity’s FBTC which led the group and contributed 
similar net inflows of $235mn and $213mn, respectively. Other sizable vehicles 
include Grayscale’s legacy GBTC and Bitwise’s BITB which gathered $33mn and 
$19mn, respectively. Other contributors are detailed in Table 1 below. The group’s 
aggregate notional trading volumes jumped sequentially to $3,153mn, still now 
in-line with the group’s running daily average of ~$3,196mn/day since the January 
2024 launch.

U.S. Spot Solana ETP Flows
We estimate that the U.S. spot Solana ETPs preliminarily reported no net new flows on 
Wednesday, October 21, 2026 after 12 consecutive sessions of daily net redemptions. None 
of the spot U.S. SOL ETPs we track posted net new flows yesterday, and the group’s AUM 
totaled $1,613mn at close. The group’s aggregate notional trading volumes moderated 
sequentially to $54mn yesterday, more in-line with the group’s daily average volumes of ~
$53mn/day since its launch in October 2025.
