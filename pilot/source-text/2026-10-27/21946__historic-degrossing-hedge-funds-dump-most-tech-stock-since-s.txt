 
Historic Degrossing: Hedge Funds Dump Most Tech 
Stock Since Sept 2024 
 
BY TYLER DURDEN 
FRIDAY, OCT 23, 2026 - 08:10 PM 
Thursday was a brutal day in markets, with Goldman's Prime Brokerage noting that through 
early afternoon, the bank's popular HF VIP longs vs. most-shorted names (GSTHVIPP) basket, 
"finished down ~4.2%, its worst move since 2011, and is now down 10.4% MTD." For context, 
the only bigger one-day drop on Bloomberg's chart, -8.26%, came on August 8, 2011, the first 
trading day after S&P downgraded the US.  
 
Adding to the liquidation frenzy, Goldman FICC trader Matt Kaplan wrote that as of Thursday 
midday Goldman's Prime Brokerage had Fundamental Long/Short funds down 1.1%, 
Systematic funds down 1.2% and Multi-Strats down 1.4%: "This marked the first time since 
Covid that all three strategies were down more than 1% on the same day." 
 

 
And now that the full-day data has hit the blotter, Kartik SInghal of Goldman's Prime Brokerage 
group writes today that across global equities from a flow perspective, hedge funds have 
de-grossed in 7 of the past 8 sessions, and the pace has been accelerating. The last such 
streak was in April 2026, and it did not occur at all in 2025. 
Some details on the dramatic degrossing events observed over the past 2 weeks: 
Yesterday, 22nd October, was a large de-grossing event (5th largest this year) at -1.6 sigma... 
 
... driven entirely by long selling and it was largest long selling since September 2025. 
 

 
 
Almost all major regions saw risk unwinds yesterday, led by North America (long sales), Europe 
(long sales > short covers), and EM Asia (long sales > short sales). China and Hong Kong were 
the most net sold markets within Asia, while the pace of selling in Taiwan, Korea, and Japan 
was relatively more moderate.​
​
De-grossing was broad based as 9 out of 11 global sectors saw risk unwinds led by Info Tech 
and Industrials.  
●​ Info Tech saw long selling alongside short additions. Overall largest net selling since 
September 2024. Every industry was net sold led by Semi & Semi Eqps. which was also 
the most de-grossed. Primarily de-grossed by Fundamental L/S community but also by 
multis and quants.   
 

 
 
●​ Semi & Semi Equipment: Allocations have now dropped to levels seen earlier in the 
year. 
 
●​ Industrials was primarily de-grossed by Multis/Quants with every industry being 
de-grossed. Yesterday was the third largest de-grossing day of the year. 
Going down the list, global AI (GSXGGLAI) names saw the largest net selling since end of July.  
 

 
 
Net/Gross allocations have fallen back to the lows seen after July moves.
