DeskTalk - US equity check: Meta's moment
By Preston Pagli
Risk appetite has improved throughout the session, with the SPX +0.7% and NDX +1.3%, as investors leaned back into the
AI trade following the blockbuster SpaceX (+7%) earnings report that reignited confidence in the durability of the AI capex
cycle. 
Despite the strong index performance, the rally remains relatively narrow beneath the surface, with market breadth weak and
volumes mostly subdued. SPX now sits just 1% below the ATH reached Oct 6. Into the close, a few key CTA levels to watch
include SPX (ESZ6), currently trading above the trigger to flip back 'max long' at $7,746 (ref $7,804) and in NDX (NQZ6)
which has surpassed the 'flip long' and 'max long' level at $29,937 (ref $30,064), a close above these levels suggests
systematics will increase long exposure into Thursday. 
Sector performance is more selective with info tech and industrial leading in the gains department, while defensives,
healthcare, and consumer disc. lag. Vol has eased following an orderly VIX expiry, though elevated skew continues to point
to persistent demand for downside protection particularly in late-October expiries, with notable put spread buying across
both SPX and ES. Jan. ATM implied vol is broadly unchanged in SPX and modestly firmer in QQQ. 
In credit, HY outperformance vs. IG is waning as investors increasingly price in the prospect of continued debt-funded AI
investment. Quant related flows have been in favor of HY risk selling. Treasury vol has also eased modestly, providing a
tailwind for risk assets, while flows show HF short covering.
Heading into the close, attention turns squarely to Meta earnings at 16:30 ET which is viewed as the next major test of the AI
narrative. Our analyst Ron Josey is expecting better-than consensus revenue and profitability with the main focus on Meta's
products and services. Josey recently upgraded his PT to $895 from $800.
