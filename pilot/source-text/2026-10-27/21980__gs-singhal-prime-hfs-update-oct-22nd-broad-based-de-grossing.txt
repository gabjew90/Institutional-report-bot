Prime HFs Update - Oct 22nd - Broad based de-grossing driven by
long selling
Kartik Singhal
23 Oct 2026
Across global equities from a flow perspective, hedge funds have de-grossed in 7 of the past 8 sessions, and the
pace has been accelerating. The last such streak was in April 2026, and it did not occur at all in 2025.
 
Yesterday 22nd October was a large de-grossing event (5th largest this year) at -1.6 SD, driven entirely by long
selling and it was largest long selling since September 2025.
 
 
 
 

 
 
Almost all major regions saw risk unwinds yesterday, led by North America (long sales), Europe (long sales >
short covers), and EM Asia (long sales > short sales). China and Hong Kong were the most net sold markets within
Asia, while the pace of selling in Taiwan, Korea, and Japan was relatively more moderate.
 
De-grossing was broad based as 9 out of 11 global sectors saw risk unwinds led by Info Tech and Industrials. 
• Info Tech saw long selling alongside short additions. Overall largest net selling since September 2024. Every
industry was net sold led by Semi & Semi Eqps. which was also the most de-grossed. Primarily de-grossed by
Fundamental L/S community but also by multis and quants. 
• Semi & Semi Eqps. Allocations have now dropped to levels seen earlier in the year.
• Industrials was primarily de-grossed by Multis/Quants with every industry being de-grossed. Yesterday was
the third largest de-grossing day of the year.
 

 
 
 
 
Global AI (GSXGGLAI) names saw the largest net selling since end of July. Net/Gross allocations have fallen
back to the lows seen after July moves.
