1) Heavy Shorting + Record Tech Selling = Sharp Net Leverage Reduction
October has tested the AI trade. A hotter-than-expected US CPI print and renewed doubts over the payoff
from heavy AI capex spending sparked a sharp unwind in semis and AI infrastructure names that began in
Asian memory stocks. HFs have heavily net sold global equities MTD, driven by short sales far outpacing
long buys (~5 to 1). Overall Long leverage now sits in the 73rd percentile vs. the past year (94th percentile
5-year), while Short leverage has risen sharply and is approaching 5-year highs in the 99th percentile
(short squeeze risk alert?).
Nearly all major regions (sans Europe) are net sold so far in October, led by North America and EM Asia.
Within Asia, Taiwan, Japan, and Korea are the most notionally net sold markets MTD, while Australia and
Hong Kong are the most net bought. Net allocation to Korea now stands at 3.3% (vs. peak level of ~8% in
June), the lowest level since early February.

Global Info Tech stocks saw the largest 2-week % net selling (Oct 5th – Oct 16th) on our record (10+
years, -3.3 SDs), driven by long-and-short sales. Mag 7 net exposure has risen to a new record high, while
the difference between US Semis and Software net exposures has continued to narrow.

2) SpaceX Blowout Earnings
SPCX +8% following strong operating results driven by revenue across all three operating segments with
outsized dollar momentum in the Super Intelligence segment (driven by increased scale in their Cloud
Services business). Starship remains the core economic enabler - management expects a steady
acceleration in Starship launches, ultimately targeting weekly launches in 1H27 and roughly twice-weekly
by the end of 2027 as reusability improves. In addition to the fundamental takeaways above, SPCX
management disclosed that both SPCX and TSLA have formed special committees consisting of
independent directors to discuss the idea of a potential business combination.

Elon on the earnings call: “We are also, frankly, hounded relentlessly by every hyperscaler and by dozens
of smaller companies to rent more of our compute.” “I don’t think we are going to have a demand issue
here.”
Lateral moves from SPCX print: Hyperscalers lower on increased competition (GOOGL -1%, MSFT
-50bps, AMZN flat) + Semis/Chips higher (SMH +2%). SMCI is up +12% on its positive pre, guiding Net
Sales well above consensus. DELL +5%, HPE +4% in sympathy.
Source: Goldman Sachs FICC & Equities, Marquee, as of 21 Oct 2026.
