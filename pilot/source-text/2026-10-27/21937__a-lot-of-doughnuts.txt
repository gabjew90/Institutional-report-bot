Wall Street has a zero problem
Anyone who has ever worked on Wall Street knows there is one thing you don't want for your Christmas
bonus: a doughnut. Zero. Nothing.
When it comes to hedge fund positioning right now, Wall Street is suddenly handing out doughnuts left,
right and center. From net leverage to long/short ratios, zeros are popping up everywhere.
Zero conviction. Zero hedging. Zero AI outperformance.
Disclaimer: This article also comes with zero conclusions. Just doughnut observations. The mandatory
contrarian take is, of course, that positioning this low should be bullish. But somehow, this time it just
doesn't feel right.
0th percentile
Let's start with a proper zero. US long/short net leverage has collapsed to 43.4%, putting positioning at
the 0th percentile over the past five years.
