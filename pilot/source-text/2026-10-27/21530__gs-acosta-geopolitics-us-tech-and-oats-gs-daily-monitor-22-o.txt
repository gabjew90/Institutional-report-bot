Geopolitics, US Tech, and OATs - GS Daily Monitor
Good morning,
 
Bottom-line: Geopolitical concerns and mixed reactions to US tech earnings weighing on global equities. The
Houthis claimed responsibility for attacks in Yanbu and Dammam airport. French 10-year OATs continue to
widen over Bunds, reaching levels not seen since 2011. Bank of Korea held rates steady with a balanced
presser. The US State Department ordered non-emergency personnel and their families to evacuate from
Saudi Arabia.

• In Equities, global markets in the red given rising geopolitical concerns and mixed reactions to US tech
earnings. 
• Tesla reported a cut to FY capex guidance by $5bn ($25bn —> $20bn). 
• In Commodities, the Houthis claimed the attacks on the Aramco site in Yanbu and Dammam's airport. 
• Brent neared $112 a barrel as the US ordered some diplomats to leave Saudi Arabia given Houthi
threats. 
• In Europe, French 10-year OATs spread over Bunds increased to 175 points, the highest level since 2011. 
• In Turkiye, the central bank cut rates for the first time since the outbreak of the Iran war. 
• In Asia, Bank of Korea held rates steady with the presser balanced between hawkish and dovish signals. 
• Governor Shin made it clear that BOK would not stand in way of recent KRW appreciation.
• In Emerging Markets, the US State Department ordered non-emergency personnel and their families to
evacuate Saudi Arabia. 
• In Russia, President Putin held more than three hours of calls with US envoy Witkoff. 
• Chart of the Day: Brent futures have climbed to a 5-month high as it nears $112 a barrel. A report that the
US State Department ordered non-emergency personnel to evacuate Saudi Arabia has been in focus this
