The small-cap squeeze?
Russell is showing signs of life after an aggressive sell-off. The index is testing a major long-term
trendline, weekly momentum is turning higher from deeply oversold levels, and easing yields are
providing some relief. Meanwhile, small-cap volatility remains relatively contained compared with
tech, and aggressive CTA shorts could quickly turn into buying fuel. Is the stage being set for a sharp
squeeze higher?
Big in small caps
Russell has shown some signs of life lately. The index is trading around the major trendline that has
held since May 2025. Yes, we are below the 200-day moving average, but recall that small caps saw a
similar dip below this level in March before bouncing aggressively. Could history repeat itself?

The rates connection
Russell is very sensitive to rising yields. The inverse is also valid. Russell has shown some life lately as
the 10-year has taken a pause. As rates have come down, the RUT/NDX ratio has caught some bids.
Note the spread just reversed on the lower part of the trend line.
