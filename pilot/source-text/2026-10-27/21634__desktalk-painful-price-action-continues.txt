DeskTalk - PAInful price action continues
By Varshi Karamsetty
AI jitters have featured heavily this week, with US stocks retreating once again. Broader risk sentiment remains dampened by
escalation risk in the Middle East, with gold down -2%, oil prices nearing +3%. Risk appetite should remain cautious into
major catalysts next week (FOMC, BoJ) with no clear anchor for intraday markets. 
Sentiment is worsening after mixed tech reports overnight. NDX is now -2.5% and the D1 baskets that were outperforming
during Wednesday's session are returning weaker (Hyperscalers basket CGUSHYPR down -3.2% at print). Clear flight to
safety at a sector level, with consumer staples outperforming amid renewed strikes on Saudi Arabia and US and Israel
planning for a joint military campaign against Iran after midterms. 7658 in SPX marked the downside level - our futures trader
warned that if a break sustains into the close, further downside is warranted

Persistent French bond weakness completes this week's trifecta of risk off catalysts. OAT-Bund 10y spread crossed 2011
highs, with front-end OATs plunging as much as -11bp before recovering towards -4bp into the close. With no near-term
catalyst in sight, the path of least resistance for OATs remains wider spreads. Bunds surprisingly outperformed, up 7bp across
the curve. Eurozone consumer confidence dropped further to -17.9 but the larger focus is on PMIs Friday. Should we see a
turn in growth, this could clearly weigh on broader EUR FI and renew contagion risk. Our economists warned that while there
were several differences between France and the periphery from the 2010-12 debt crisis, there are also similarities. Dig
deeper.
Treasuries have outperformed on safe haven demand with ultra bonds (WN) now above the level to cover shorts (today's
close is the official trigger) with momentum stalling as we break 108-16 resistance. Our desks have seen constant call buying
in TY and FV, targeting yield levels as low as 4.41% on 5s (current 4.82%) and 4.60% on 10s (current 5.01%). Gilts are
quietly rallying into the close. Fiscal risk premia looks too high in the UK, but our macro strategist prefers to assess post
budget.  
USD rally is picking up in the NY morning. USDJPY volumes remain +26% higher than 30d averages but until major catalysts
clear (FOMC, BoJ next week), JPY gains may be capped. We are seeing a return of interest in USDJPY and cross-JPY put
spreads after a period of unwinds. EURUSD breaks 1.12 at print. FX Strategy exit their short EURCHF trade at 0.903 having
reached their target. We remain short EURNOK, which should continue to benefit from geopolitical and EU risks.
