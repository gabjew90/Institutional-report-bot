[GSX] The Momentum Reckoning Continues - Back to Sept Lows
Guillaume Soria
22 Oct 2026

The GS High Beta Momentum Pair (GSPRHIMO) has erased all YTD gains (recall the
pair was up ~70% at its peak in June), following a 43% drawdown as AI has materially
re-rated lower. Today we are seeing another leg lower in the past winners
(GSXUHMOM) are underperforming with broad AI/Semis selloff as the debate between
costs and returns was reinforced by META's earnings. This is the worst performance of
AI Beneficiaries (GSTMTAIP) vs Top 500 ex AI (SPXXAI Index) since Deepseek
Monday.
We continue to like the broadening trade and SPXXAI Index has outperformed the SPX
Index by 6pp in October. SPXXAI Index is quickly closing the gap vs SPX and the
correlation of AI (GSTMTAIP) and SPXXAI is reverting lower (recall AI is ~50% of SPX
and 45% of the SPX names have negative beta to the index).
Factor positioning has rotated with momentum exposure at the lowest level since late
June 2025 (still elevated vs history, at 73rd percentile 5-year) and at the same time our
Prime team noted the exposure to profitability is now at 5 year high, which we are
seeing in basket flows: our quality index GSP1QUAL is sector/industry/factor neutral
and is up ~20% in 3m (~13v) driven by strong underperformance of the short leg (lower
quality).
It has historically worked to buy the dip in the momentum factor and, with cleaner
positioning and extreme drawdown vs history, we favor limited-loss (reach out for
pricing) for clients looking to buy the factor.
