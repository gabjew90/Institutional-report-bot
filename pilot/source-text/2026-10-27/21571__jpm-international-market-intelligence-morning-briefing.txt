Report: Weekly Wrap | Echoes of 2023 (and 2018)...but Aggregate YTD Returns 
Still Quite Positive Despite MTD Drawdown 
  
Summary: There’s been a lot to digest this week with Tech selling off, yields lower 
despite the hot inflation prints, and overall positioning still around neutral in the US.  
Overall the moves we’ve seen MTD are very much in line with the rotation 
potential that has lingered since the large build up of momentum / AI longs vs. rate 
sensitive / consumer shorts.  In many ways the recent moves look quite similar 
to this same time in 2023 (which saw a dip, followed by a rally into YE).  We still 
think an eventual stabilization and 4Q rally are more likely than not, due to a much 
stronger performance cushion than another similar year (2018), though there are 
certainly multiple risks that could change the outcome. 
Key Points: 
• What happened this week? Poor performance, de-grossing, and 
rotations: HF performance drawdown accelerated to -2.1% MTD globally 

(from -0.8% last week).  Eq L/S -2.3% MTD, Quants -1% to -2%, Multi-Strats -
2%. APAC lagged the most (down to -3.8% MTD) with alpha deteriorating 
(APAC L/S spread +0.2% → -1.3% MTD), and both EMEA and US L/S 
spreads also worsened.  Multi-Strat de-grossing was close to the worst 
we’ve seen in the past 2 years, though net flows weren’t that negative and 
US positioning was still around 40th %-tile (though 11th and 8th for Europe and 
China/HK). 
  
• Why did this happen even though positioning was low/neutral in 
aggregate? Crowded skews + high gross: Even with neutral aggregate net 
positioning (US TPM ~40th %-tile, 4wk +0.5z), the backdrop was fragile: 
gross still high LT, 3m combined flows into Tech had been near multi-year 
highs, and NDX long vs RTY short futures skews were extreme again. Even 
with the recent selling of Semis/AI, some extremes are still present: AI Core 
Revenue positioning remains 94th %-tile (12m) / 99th (LT), Semis vs 
Software skew persists (Semis L/S still ~3.0x vs Software L/S ~1x), and 
Utilities remains heavily under-owned (HF positioning near 10yr lows). 
  
• Echoes of 2023 (more than 2018) — rate/oil/quality setup, but YTD 
cushion argues for resilience: Macro and factor returns are similar to 3Q23 
(US 10yr +82bps in 3Q26 vs +73bps in 3Q23, Oil >+40% vs ~+30%, Quality 
outperforming Beta, Tech > Small Caps), while positioning is still light/neutral 
in the US but more bearish in Europe and China/HK. A key similarity to 2023 
(and different from 2018) is that there’s a stronger YTD performance cushion: 
HFs are still about +6% YTD (through Oct 15 est.), and equities are up ~+13% 
MXWD / +14% SPX YTD—historically a setup that more often supports 
positive 4Q outcomes unless fundamentals (earnings/data) deteriorate.
