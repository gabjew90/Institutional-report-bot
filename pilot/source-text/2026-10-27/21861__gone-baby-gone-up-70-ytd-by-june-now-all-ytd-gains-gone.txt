Round trip
Up 70% by June. Back to zero by October. The AI momentum trade has gone from Wall Street's biggest
winner to one of its biggest headaches. The positioning charts are confusing. Some suggest the pain
may not be over, and some are screaming "we are done." Let's have a look at the charts that matter.
Gone baby gone
The GS High Beta Momentum Pair has erased all YTD gains. The pair was up ~70% at its peak in June.
GS trading desk: "Thursday we saw another leg lower in the past winners with broad AI/Semis selloff as
the debate between costs and returns was reinforced by META's earnings. This is the worst
performance of AI Beneficiaries vs Top 500 ex AI since Deepseek Monday."
