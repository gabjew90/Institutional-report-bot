Russell 2k Futures: Mixed Flows Surrounding Recent Credit Concerns (2)
Commitment of Traders covering the onset of High Yield credit concerns revealed an about-face from Asset
Manager in Russell 2k futures. Given demand elsewhere, Non-Dealer net positioning actually rose. Subsequently,
High Yield credit continued to deteriorate. However sentiment remained stable. The retracement in US 10 year real
yields likely provided some solace. Will this persist with other equity growth barometers worsening?
 
• Commitment of Traders covering the onset of High Yield credit concerns revealed an about-face from
Asset Manager in Russell 2k futures. Asset Manager sold $2.2bn Russell 2k futures from October 6th - 13th as
CDX High Yield rose 9bps and Russell 2k Index lost 1.7%. Notably, the Asset Manager selling ended a 6 week
stretch of purchases which totaled $4.8bn. Liquidation ($1.6bn) was the main driver.
• Given demand elsewhere, Non-Dealer net positioning actually rose. Hedge Fund ($1.9bn), Other ($160mm),
and Non-Reportable ($700mm) all bought. 
• Subsequently, High Yield credit continued to deteriorate. During October 13th - 21st, CDX High Yield widened
by an additional 10bps.
• However sentiment remained stable. Over the 6 sessions, Russell 2k price outperformed its peers; funding
traded flat in outright and relative terms, exhibiting the same perseverance previously highlighted; and normalized
25 delta put-call skew cheapened.
• The retracement in US 10 year real yields likely provided some solace. USGGT10Y Index slipped 11bps to an
approximate 1 month low, alleviating the overall interest rate burden on small cap companies.
• Will this persist with other equity growth barometers worsening? GS's Cyclicals vs Defensives basket
dropped ~5% throughout the past 2 weeks.
