Tech fatigue
Goldman's Privorotsky reports growing fatigue and caution among institutional investors following
meetings in New York. Prime brokerage flows reveal some of the most aggressive tech selling in recent
memory, with managers cutting net exposure while largely maintaining gross. AI allocations have
fallen significantly, although none of the investors he met were outright short. Heading into year-end,
capital preservation dominates. AI demand remains robust, but funding, power constraints and the
sustainability of capex are increasingly being questioned.
Erratic skew
Downside protection exploded last Friday, with the SDEX index surging as panic took hold. A few
calmer sessions saw the entire spike reverse, but with markets selling off sharply again today, skew is
once again moving aggressively higher. The violent repricing of downside protection in both directions
tells us plenty about the current state of this market: erratic.
