KOSPI hasn't forgotten oil
While SPX and NDX appear to have moved beyond the oil narrative, KOSPI continues to track inverted
crude remarkably closely.
There is a structural reason. Korea imports virtually all its crude, with oil accounting for more than a
third of primary energy consumption.
This is a semiconductor powerhouse sitting on top of an energy-dependent industrial economy.
The Nasdaq may have moved on. Korea hasn't.
