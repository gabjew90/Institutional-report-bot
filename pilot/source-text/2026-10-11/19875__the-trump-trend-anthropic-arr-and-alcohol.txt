Slowed to a crawl
According to data from TickerTrends, Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate exploded from roughly
$10 billion in January to $74 billion by July 24. Two months later, on September 24, the figure stood at
just $76.8 billion. That's barely 4% growth over two months, following a more than sevenfold increase
during the first seven months of the year. Revenue hasn't peaked, but the growth trajectory has
changed dramatically.
