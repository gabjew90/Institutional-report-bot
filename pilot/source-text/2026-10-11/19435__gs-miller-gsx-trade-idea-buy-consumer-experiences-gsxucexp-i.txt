[GSX] Trade Idea: Not All Gloomy in Discretionary - Buy Consumer Experiences (GSXUCEXP Index)
The GS Consumer Experiences Basket (GSXUCEXP) has underperformed SPXXAI Index despite the fact
that spending growth on experiences accelerated from 1% in Q1 2025 to 6% in Q1 2026, versus 2% for
broader services. The basket trades at attractive valuations, revisions appear close to inflecting, and
positioning is not overly crowded.
While higher fuel costs have weighed on Consumer (worst performing sector on our monitor YTD), not all
is gloomy in the space. Demand for Consumer Experiences has remained high and the basket's exposure to
higher income cohorts has helped insulate it relative to the broader complex, leaving it well positioned if
oil prices stay elevated or in a potential de-escalation scenario.
The Consumer Experiences Basket recently exhibited negative correlation with AI, but we expect that
relationship to become more positive as the value of experiences that cannot be replicated digitally may
continue to rise, similar to the period following peak Metaverse enthusiasm in 2021 when consumers
gravitated towards physical activities and social experiences following a tech heavy period.

This view is supported by the company commentary we are hearing across the categories that comprise the
basket, reinforcing our bullish outlook ahead of earnings season:

• 40% Live Entertainment, Sports & Media Experiences (FWONK, LYV, TKO, MSGS, DIS, SPHR,
MSGE, LLYVK, BATRK): The strongest evidence for the experience economy continues to come from
live events. DIS delivered 4% domestic per-capita spending growth alongside higher attendance, LYV
reported more than 10% fan growth and no signs of consumer weakness, TKO stated physical experiences
command the largest share of the consumer wallet, MSGS delivered record playoff gate revenues and
accelerating in-arena spend, FWONK/LLYVK continue to report sold-out Formula 1 hospitality and
record MotoGP attendance, while SPHR sold 3.6 million tickets generating $450mm from The Wizard of
Oz. Latest on Live Entertainment from our Research team here.
• 20% Leisure & Theme Parks (MTN, CHDN, RRR, FUN, PRKS, OSW, PLAY): Consumer demand
remains resilient across destination leisure, local entertainment, and experiential spending categories.
CHDN continues to benefit from a "flight to quality" around the Kentucky Derby franchise, and OSW
reported 14% growth in pre-booked wellness services. Meanwhile MTN, FUN, and PLAY provide
exposure to destination leisure, local entertainment, and out-of-home experiential spending that should
benefit disproportionately if consumer sentiment improves and fuel costs moderate.
• 20% Hotels & Travel (MAR, HLT, H, WH): Travel demand continues to hold up better than consumer
sentiment. MAR reported luxury RevPAR growth of more than 9% in the U.S. and Canada, H saw leisure
transient RevPAR rise 7% with luxury and lifestyle leading performance, HLT delivered 3.9% RevPAR
growth, and WH reported record satisfaction scores alongside lengthening family vacations and average
drive distances increasing to 360 miles despite higher gas prices.
• 20% Cruises (RCL, CCL, VIK, NCLH): CCL reported strengthening booking trends throughout the
quarter, with June representing an inflection point as bookings turned positive year-over-year and
accelerated further through July and August. RCL noted travel remains the #1 leisure category where
consumers intend to spend more, VIK is 96% booked for 2026 with 2027 already 53% sold, and NCLH
continues to see consumers prioritize travel and experiences despite airfare headwinds.
