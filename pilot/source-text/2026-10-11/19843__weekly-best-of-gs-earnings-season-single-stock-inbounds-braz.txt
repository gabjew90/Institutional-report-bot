Midterms… Alec Phillips’ election monitor provides a good rundown of where things stand. On the
Senate, Prediction markets imply a 65% chance that Democrats win a Senate majority, which requires a
net gain of 4 seats. Polling averages show Democrats ahead in 7 Republican-held seats as well as all of
their own close races (Exhibit 3), but most sit within the margin of error. On the House, Prediction markets

imply a more than 90% chance that Democrats will net at least the three seats they need to win the House
majority. Democrats lead the generic ballot by an average of 8.9pp, implying a roughly 12pp swing from
the 2.6pp popular vote margin that House Republicans won with in 2024. Our US Midterm Elections
webinar series has been launched and you can register HERE.
Capital Markets… 3Q recap from our IBD team. Investors are looking ahead to the upcoming Q3 earnings
season and point to the mid-term elections as a potential clearing event – historically, markets have
underperformed heading into mid-terms (median of down (3.5)% in the month prior to mid-terms since
1974) but outperform post (median of up 6.5% in the 4 months following the elections) which sets up well
for ECM heading into year-end. Full deck available if interested.
