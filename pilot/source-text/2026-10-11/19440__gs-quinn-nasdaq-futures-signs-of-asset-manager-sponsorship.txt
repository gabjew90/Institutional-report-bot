GS Quinn - Nasdaq Futures: Signs of Asset Manager Sponsorship?
7 Oct 2026
Robert Quinn  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  FICC & Equities
7 Oct 2026
Throughout the past week, AI optimism powered Nasdaq index to new all-time highs. Futures aggregate
open interest also gained considerably, signaling a potential dominance of long initiations. That said,
funding market changes reflected simultaneous short selling. And sponsorship from the largest Non-Dealer
cohort remained questionable.
• Throughout the past week, AI optimism powered Nasdaq index to new all-time highs. During September
29th - October 6th, Nasdaq rose 2.9%, topping S&P (+1.9%) and Russell 2k (+0.8%). Over the same time
frame, GS's AI Leaders and Broad AI baskets jumped 6.4% and 5.6%, receiving a boost from significant
infrastructure agreements.
• Futures aggregate open interest also gained considerably, signaling a potential dominance of long
initiations. OI increased in 3 of the 5 sessions for a net $8.9bn. The largest upticks occurred alongside
price strength on October 2nd and 5th.
• That said, funding market changes reflected simultaneous short selling. Nasdaq 3 month funding
cheapened in outright terms and moved sideways relative to S&P. Thus outright shorts and/or hedging
conceivably fueled some of the OI builds.
• Sponsorship from the largest Non-Dealer cohort remained questionable. Asset Manager gross longs have
generally declined since reaching a nominal max on June 2nd. Moreover, the historically positive
correlation between Asset Manager gross long changes, in contract terms, and price was nil as of the last
Commitment of Traders update on September 29th.
