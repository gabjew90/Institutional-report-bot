Another muted session with lower with defensive posturing under the hood (NDX > SPX > RTY) as
global yields continued to back up. Our US Cyclicals vs. Defensives pair (GSPUCYDE) traded -159bps.
Russell underperformed NDX for the 4th straight day (and 17th time in the last 20 sessions) as small caps
continue to bear the brunt of the rates pain though some relief this afternoon with a decent 39bn 10YR
auction printing 1.6bps thru at 5.3% (vs 5.316% going in). Our floor was a 4 on a 1-10 scale in terms of
overall activity levels finishing -630bps for sale, largely driven by macro products and to a smaller extent
tech.
Shawn Tuteja had a good flag on Tech positioning last night: While Overall Market Net Positioning is Still
Low, net exposure on our prime book to Mag7 as a % of Total US exposure is near 22%, which is the
highest on record since the start of 2022. Semis and Semi Equipment as a % of Total US Exposure now sit
at ~12% (vs a peak in June of 14% but also vs starting the year ~6%). Our prime data shows that US Tech
stocks in September had their largest % buying in a month since Feb '25 (and were the only sector net
bought on our prime book in the month of September).
POST BELL: COST August total comps were +11.4%, with core comps +7.6%. The US comp (ex gas and
F/X) was +8%. Feedback into this sales update was data was strong. The +8% is solid, but does feel like
it's lower-end of some of the strong +8-9% expectations we had been hearing.
DERIVS: Little panic in equities today despite the pullback across indices -- we saw vols offered across
the curve (most notably in NDX) and skew was bid. Flows were on the quieter side today outside of a
couple big hedging flows, and net exposure generally remained light, although we saw some of the action
in HYG/IYR as well. On a sector level, we saw buyers of 1-3m upside in XLF as the group is -6% over
the past month. The desk thinks gamma is cheap at these levels, especially since we realized today's
implied move intraday to the downside. Tomorrow's straddle goes out at 41bps. (TY Shayna Peart)
Financials EPS kicks off next Tuesday with JPM, C + WFC reporting in the morning. It was a ROUGH
September for the sector, closing in the red 16 of 21 trading sessions in the month, tied for the worst
month since November 1999 which also saw 16 negative performing days in 21 sessions. The 4th quarter
has been quite FAVORABLE to the sector over the years.
