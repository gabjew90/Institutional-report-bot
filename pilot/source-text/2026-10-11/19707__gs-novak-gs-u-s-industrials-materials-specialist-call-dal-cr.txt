In GIR, Lizzie out with cruise preview: Heading into earnings, we remain constructive on the cruise
sector, given encouraging signs of resilient and accelerating consumer demand, with recent datapoints
from both CCL's 3Q results / bookings commentary alongside NCLH's positive pre-announcement
pointing to improving demand across all geographies. Looking ahead, we believe the competitive
environment is approaching an inflection point as supply growth moderates, comparisons ease and

underlying demand trends improve, making for a compelling setup into year end and further into 2027.
With NCLH's positive update already in the rearview, we turn our focus to RCL where the bottom line is
that we expect RCL to beat 3Q net yield guidance by ~80bps and flow through the beat to nudge up its FY
net yield guidance (i.e. maintain the implied 4Q net yield outlook), and for improvements in core
fundamentals to more than offset fuel headwinds. For RCL, we raise our 3Q/2026 net yield estimates to
0.8%/2.2% FXN and raise our 2026 EPS to $17.90. For NCLH, we raise our 3Q/2026 net yield estimates
to -7.6%/-4.6% FXN, and maintain our 2026 EPS at $1.51. Against this backdrop, we would continue to
highlight Buy-rated RCL given 1) we see a clean setup for a beat and raise into 2H and 2027, with no
concerns on 1Q headwinds or yield/cost spread; 2) we expect an acceleration in net yield growth in 2027
vs. 2026, and 3) there's the potential for a positive catalyst for Perfect Day Mexico, against what is still an
