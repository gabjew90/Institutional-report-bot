Alberto Gandolfi on Datacenter Economics: We believe Utilities are increasingly moving to a new
integrated business model ("DC Infrastructure Services"), which would provide land, grid access and
energy to datacenters. We estimate that the Utilities under our coverage currently own c.25 GW
connection points (and land) – the vast majority in Europe – which could be worth c.€20 bn based on
recent transaction values. We simulate a range of conversion rates for these CPs and conclude that for
Solaria monetizing their grid access could ultimately add c.45% to its share price. PPC, Drax, Fortum and
RWE also offer meaningful exposure to this theme.
