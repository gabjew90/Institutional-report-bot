EXECUTIVE SUMMARY – Resilient growth, strong earnings, 
favorable post-midterm seasonality, and light retail participation 
support further upside. Leadership remains concentrated in AI 
and mega-cap technology, but election clarity, lower yields, or 
earnings beats could broaden the rally into small caps and 
beaten-down sectors. The principal risks are renewed food and 
energy inflation, another Fed hike, and any pullback in AI capital 
spending. 
  
MACRO | TOPICS COVERED 
  

• Oil’s shift from panic to fatigue — and why diesel, not 
crude, may now be the more important inflation signal. 
• The next commodity trade — where conviction is 
building across agriculture, food inflation, and Q1 risk. 
• Rates, energy, and the consumer — how a dual 
squeeze is reshaping sector leadership beneath 
resilient indices. 
• AI or nothing? — what extreme Nasdaq-versus-Russell 
positioning says about market concentration. 
• The underappreciated retail setup — why sidelined 
equity buyers and heavy bond ETF demand could matter 
next. 
  
MIDTERMS | TOPICS COVERED 
  
  
• The most likely election outcome — and why a change 
in congressional control may produce less policy 
change than headlines imply. 
• Why gridlock could be bullish — the historical market 
pattern following midterms and the case for buying 
election weakness. 
• A potential small-cap squeeze — how bearish Russell 
positioning could react to lower yields or greater policy 
clarity. 

• The commodity consequences — what diesel policy 
could mean for copper supply, soybean oil, and 
renewable fuels. 
• When election trading gets real — why the final two 
weeks may matter far more than today’s positioning. 
  
EARNINGS | TOPICS COVERED 
  
• A strong quarter with a high bar — what exceptional 
growth expectations mean for the market’s reaction 
function. 
• The AI capex question — why spending guidance may 
matter more than headline technology beats. 
• Where surprises could emerge — the setup across 
financials, industrials, healthcare, and consumer 
stocks. 
• Inflation’s next earnings test — how rising input-price 
signals could pressure margins into 2027. 
• Broadening without a tech selloff — the case for 
laggards and small caps to rally alongside resilient 
mega-cap technology. 
  
POTENTIAL INVESTMENT IMPLICATIONS 
  

•         UPSIDE – Small caps and lagging sectors could catch up if 
yields ease or retail returns.  Crude could surprise to the upside 
into Q1 
•         CORE – Potential upside for US stocks due to neutral 
positioning, positive seasonality, light retail participation, and 
strong earnings.  Technology remains favored, but strong 
earnings expectations leave a high bar.   
•         RISKS – Watch diesel, food inflation, Fed tightening, and 
AI capex guidance. 
  
MONETIZATION MENU – Below came from JPM US Market 
Intelligence October 5th Morning Briefing 
•         We still like Tech as a core long but would no longer pair 
with an RTY short given squeeze risk from a move lower in oil / 
yields, which points to using a derivative expression to play the 
squeeze. Within Tech, there is likely to be a broadening, with 
Semis and Mag 7 potentially outperforming the Tech tape. 
Software seems to be slowly gaining traction but remains a 
non-consensus bullish play. The AI theme is likely to persist 
and we like owning the theme.  
•         Overall, we like Tech in the near-term with earnings likely to 
provide additional support. Ex-Tech, we think you need to see 
the yield curve bull steepening to see a persistent rally in 
Cyclicals; excluding AI-related plays, we favor Banks given the 
growth reboot, potentially steeper yield curve, and favorable 
capital markets outlook. Rate-sensitive sectors like Real Estate, 
Telecom, and Utilities may participate in a squeeze but we find 
those sectors most likely to lag the SPX, ex-AI plays. The 

biggest catalyst would be a US / Iran deal, which would 
squeeze EU and RTY higher; plus, the COVID Reopening 
Theme (Airlines, Autos, Cruise Lines, Discretionary, Hotels, etc) 
is another area of interest with a preference to play via options.
