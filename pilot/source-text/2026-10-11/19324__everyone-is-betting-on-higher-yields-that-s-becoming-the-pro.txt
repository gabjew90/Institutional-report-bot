The crowded rates trade
JPM notes that CTA momentum signals in US Treasuries and Bunds reached extreme bearish territory
at the end of September.
Trend followers have been riding the bond selloff, but the positioning is now increasingly vulnerable to
mean reversion.
GS CFTC data shows managed money deeply net short US Treasuries (a crowded position), while news
flow confirms trend followers were the standout strategy of 2026 (+15.7% 9M via SG CTA Index) largely
on bond shorts and oil longs.
A pause in yields could force some of these shorts to take profits, potentially adding fuel to a bond
bounce.

JPM
Not everyone is short
The bond positioning picture is more complicated than it looks.
JPM highlights a sharp divergence: CTAs are heavily short duration, while discretionary managers are
still carrying an overhang of long duration exposure.
That creates two-way risk.
CTA profit-taking could trigger a tactical rally in bonds, while further discretionary liquidation could
keep yields moving higher.
The next move may come down to who blinks first.
