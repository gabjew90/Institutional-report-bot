Spin Cycle
AI Seeing Dispersion as Index Chop Continues
WHAT YOU SHOULD KNOW: 
Market continues to chop bulls and bears alike. Today is the 13th time QQQ has crossed
above or below its 50 DMA since August 4th. It's the 31st trading day since 8/4, and
QQQ is still within that day's range. The chop continues. Interestingly, when QQQ starts
below its 20 and 50 DMA (but above 200 DMA), then opens the next day above its 20
and 50 DMA like it did today, it performs well over the next 5 days (median +1.13%
positive 73% of the time), but turns negative looking out 30-40 days (median -1.95% and
-1.5%) and negative 70% of the time. This chop is happening because we are starting to
see dispersion within AI. The NDX is ~flat since mid-August, but the percent of names
above their 200 DMA has gone from 77% to 56%. We are seeing some semis breakout
and get back near 52wk highs, while others continue to struggle at or near their 200
DMAs. The question for the overall SOXX and ultimately the SPX is which names are
telling the true story?
■
Spin Cycle. Market continues to chop bulls and bears alike. Today is the 13th time
QQQ has crossed above or below its 50 DMA since August 4th. Today is the 31st
trading day since 8/4, and QQQ is still within that day's range. The chop continues.
■
Short-Term Bullish, Medium-Term Bearish. Interestingly, when QQQ starts below
its 20 and 50 DMA (but above 200 DMA), then opens the next day above its 20
and 50 DMA like it did today, it performs well over the next 5 days (median +1.13%
positive 73% of the time), but turns negative looking out 30-40 days (median -1.95%
and -1.5%) and negative 70% of the time.
■
Breadth Divergence. The NDX is ~flat since mid-August, but the percent of names
above their 200 DMA has gone from 77% to 56%.
■
AI Dispersion. Given that NDX divergence, it's not surprising that we are starting
to see dispersion within the AI trade. Names like Advanced Micro Devices (AMD,
not rated), Qorvo (QRVO, not rated), and Skyworks Solutions (SWKS, not rated) are
showing strength and back near 52wk highs, while others like Lam Research (LRCX,
not rated), Applied Materials (AMAT, not rated), and Western Digital (WDC, not
rated) are still off 30-40% from June highs and at or near their 200 DMAs.
■
What Does It Mean? The good news is this means stock selection has a higher
importance. Unlike in Q2 when you could throw a dart at any semi and make money,
that's not the case right now so there is more alpha to be had. The bad news is this
means the overall AI trade is not as robust, and we are seeing that with the fact that
SOXX still remains below its declining 50 DMA. For those bullishly inclined, we would
focus on the names near new highs rather than those that are lagging.
■
The Deciding Factor. The question for the overall SOXX and ultimately the SPX
is which names are telling the true story? In 2000, after the March/April initial
correction, there were many tech names that went on to make new highs in the
summer and fall, but many of the leaders into the peak did not, and ultimately those
names proved to be the true 'north star'. So as we move into the fourth quarter, we
should start to get a better sense of who is telling the truth.
