 
Asian Carnage: A Recap Of The Shocking Moves 
Across The Continent 
 
BY TYLER DURDEN 
THURSDAY, OCT 15, 2026 - 05:45 PM 
Asian markets came under extreme pressure overnight and volume soared across the Goldman 
trading desk, which notes that overall notional traded came in up +58% vs. Goldman's previous 
20 day average. Despite the large regional price action, the sell skew was moderate as the desk 
was 1.2 X better to sell: that asid, it was net to sell in every single market with the sole exception 
of Australia (1.4 X Better to Buy).  
As expected, the desk was most active in Information Technology (1.4x better to sell), 
Industrials (1.1x better to sell) and Materials (1.1x better to sell) vs buys in Consumer 
Discretionary (flat), Consumer Staples (flat).  
The good news: there was no panic in the flow today (maybe except Korea) although the selling 
was robust and consistent across the trading session. Typically, the majority of 
order/flow/activity come in the early morning session. Today, the selling and activity continued 
throughout the entirety of trading. Traders, that were expecting a bounce or some reprieve, 
found themselves coming back to the market time and time again. In addition, it is worth noting 
that the selling was broad based with defensive sectors also coming under pressure albeit to a 
lesser extent. 
The below is an effort to summarize some key stats from the markets today, courtesy of 
Goldman's Asia trading desk
