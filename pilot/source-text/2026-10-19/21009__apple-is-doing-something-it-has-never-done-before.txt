The new safe haven
AAPL is trading near all-time highs, closing in on NVDA as the world's most valuable company. The
stock has emerged as the ultimate defensive play within tech, with a forward FCF yield of around
3.15%, above that of the NDX.
The most striking development? Apple's rolling two-month correlation with the NDX has turned
negative for the first time in history. A remarkable divergence that highlights the intensity of the flight
to safety within tech.
