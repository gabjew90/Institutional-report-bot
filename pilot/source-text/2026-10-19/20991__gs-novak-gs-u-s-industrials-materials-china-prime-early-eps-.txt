Couple of data points to highlight from China – full note here: Stronger-than-expected trade data: Chinese
exports and imports climbed 36.3% yoy and 31.0% yoy, respectively, in nominal USD terms in September,
both notably above consensus expectations. Semiconductor exports and imports surged 136% yoy and
75% yoy, respectively, but the increases were driven almost entirely by higher prices. September’s trade
surplus reached a record high of $133.5bn.

Divergent inflation: China’s PPI inflation surprised to the upside in September, rising 5.0% yoy (vs. the
Bloomberg consensus of 4.4%). However, CPI inflation was only 0.9% yoy, slightly below the market
expectation of 1.0%. This divergence highlights the difficulty of passing higher commodity and upstream
prices on to consumers, providing further evidence of weak domestic demand. We have revised up our
full-year 2026/27 PPI inflation forecasts to 2.8%/0.3% (from 2.0%/0.0% previously) but revised down our
CPI inflation forecasts to 0.8%/0.6% (from 1.0%/1.0% previously).
