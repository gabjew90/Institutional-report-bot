BofA October Global Fund Manager Survey
Bottom Line: heading into midterms, FOMC & Q3 earnings, investors remain bullish on macro & markets, running low 3.9% cash levels, waiting for ~6%
yields before flipping from stocks to bonds; relative to FMS positioning, big consumer +ve drop in oil to <$80/bbl = the Q4 bull surprise, AI capex cut = the
bear surprise.
On Macro: "boom" optimism highest since Feb'22, inflation expectations lowest since Jan'25, bond pessimism lowest since May'25; 6 in 10 investors expect
double-digit EPS growth next year; 76% say no Fed hike at Oct 28 FOMC.
 
On Crowds, Risks & AI: most crowded trades = long global semis (45%) & short USTs (27%); biggest tail risks = disorderly bond yields (31%) & AI bubble
(23%); most likely source of credit event = AI hyperscaler capex (39%) & govt debt (28%); AI capex cuts coming…no say 57%, yes say 30%; AI bubble in
'27...no say 32%, yes say 45%.
