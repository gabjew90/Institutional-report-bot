DB CoTD: From Silicon to Cyclicals 
 
 
US Q3 earnings season starts in earnest in the next hour, with several of the large financials reporting. 
Financials themselves are expected to be among the weaker sectors for YoY earnings growth, but the overall 
S&P 500 picture is rather different. Our equity strategists Binky Chadha and Parag Thatte expect earnings 
growth of +34% YoY, matching Q2 and well ahead of the +27% consensus. As today’s CoTD from the team 
shows, these are record growth rates outside recessionary bounce-backs. 
Binky and Parag see three powerful tailwinds behind the earnings boom: accelerating AI-demand growth 
(+54% YoY, contributing 20pp to the headline); a rebound in cyclical growth (+14%, contributing 8pp 
excluding Tech, Energy & Materials); and higher oil and commodity prices (+108%, contributing 7pp from 
Energy & Materials). See their Q3 preview piece here for more. 

Europe joins the party in earnest next week, where our equity strategists expect Q3 earnings growth of +25%, 
some 5pp above consensus (full preview here). So, barring surprises, this should be a very strong earnings 
season on both sides of the Atlantic — and indeed globally. 
There is one interesting wrinkle. Despite what is likely to be stellar earnings growth for Tech, Binky and Parag 
downgraded the sector to neutral in their weekly piece on Friday. Given Tech’s weight, that is clearly a 
potential headwind for the headline indices. However, they argue that rotation into other sectors on the back 
of a strong earnings season should offset this. If so, the broadening of earnings growth could perhaps favour 
the more cyclical European market at the margin.
