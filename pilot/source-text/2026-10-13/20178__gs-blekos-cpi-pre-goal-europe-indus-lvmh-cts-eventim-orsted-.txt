GOAL KICKSTART - Better asymmetry into Q3 earnings after valuation reset
| • | In our latest GOAL asset allocation update we shift to OW Equities over 3m and UW Credit over 3m,
while moving to OW Bonds over 12m and OW Cash tactically. Earnings growth remains supportive for
equities and the recent valuation de-rating has improved asymmetry into the Q3 earnings season. | | • | We
increasingly see long-duration bonds offering better medium-term value as inflation normalises and
central bank pricing becomes less hawkish; tactically we still prefer shorter-duration fixed income (OW
cash). |
EUROPE MULTI-INDUSTRY - Cross-checking views on inventory levels across end markets
| • | China’s manufacturing inventories appear to be above normal levels in September but have fallen
recently, while European inventories remain steady but elevated, and US inventories remain, on average,
lean, with only Machinery and Automotive continuing to be significantly above long-run averages. | | • | A
point worth noting for large parts of the sector is that electrical equipment inventories continue to look the
most depleted in the US, standing at all-time low levels, with inventory depletion seen across end markets
including Metals, Machinery, Hardware, Manufacturing, Computer Equipment and Chemicals. |
LVMH Moet-Hennessy Louis Vuitton (LVMH.PA) - 3Q fashion miss highlights challenged Chinese
demand; Buy
| • | LVMH delivered a 1% cFX sales growth in 3Q26, broadly in line with estimates (+1% GSe/Visible
Alpha Cons). | | • | However, in its key profit-driving division, Fashion and Leather, sales fell -5% cFX
(VA Consensus -2% / GSe -2.5%) which compares to +1% in the prior period (2Q26) but importantly,

CTS Eventim (EVDG.DE) - Europe's market leader meets rising competition; initiate at Neutral
| • | We initiate on CTS Eventim with a Neutral rating and €68 price target. CTS Eventim is Europe's
leading ticketing and live entertainment platform and the global number two. | | • | Historically viewed as a

high-quality compounder in Europe, shares have de-rated ~50% over the last 18 months as investors have
reassessed the sustainability of its historical growth profile amid increasing competitive intensity and
concerns around capital allocation. |
Orsted A/S (ORSTED.CO) - Downgrading to Neutral
| • | Our rating change reflects three key factors: (1) sovereign rates across Europe and the US have risen
by +c.50-65 bp since late August (+c.100 bp YTD); we note that Orsted features among the most capital-
intensive companies in the industry; (2) when compared to the rest of the sector, we expect Q3 results to
be somewhat underwhelming, which could lead to negative consensus earnings revisions; and (3) although
our new DKK 155 PT still implies +c.15% upside, this is now below the sector average and no longer
sufficient to support a Buy rating. |
Zurich Insurance Group (ZURN.S) - Reinstate at Neutral: Balancing attractive profit growth, valuation
and execution risk
| • | Zurich offers an attractive growth profile with a targeted >9% EPS CAGR (2025-2027) ex. Beazley
and guided proforma mid-single digit EPS uplift from the Beazley transaction. | | • | Valuation has come
off peak levels, with the 1-year forward consensus P/E compressing by c.2.0x since April 2025 and our
2027e PE of 12.8x only at 0.5x to 0.7x premiums vs. Allianz and Generali, respectively. | | • | Additionally,
while the total capital return yield is on the lower end of the peer range, the spread vs. local risk free yield
screens as attractive, in our view. |
