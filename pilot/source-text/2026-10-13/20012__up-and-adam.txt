Monday, October 12, 2026
Median household net worth ticked up 2% (on an inflation-adjusted basis) between 2022 and 2025, reaching $216k, according to the 
Fed’s Survey of Consumer Finances. However, wealth gains were uneven: net worth for households in the top 10% of incomes rose 31%, 
whereas the bottom 40% saw declines in their net worth. A key factor behind the divergence is the fact that affluent households have 
benefited from rising asset prices—equities and homes—while persistent inflation is pressuring lower-income consumers.
