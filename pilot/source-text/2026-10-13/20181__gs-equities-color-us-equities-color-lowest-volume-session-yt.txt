Today was the lowest volume trading session of the year. As expected, activity muted and sparse news to
trade on for the holiday today (exchange volumes -20.7% vs 20d avg). Standouts included semis weakness
with Memory leading the group lower, driven by: combination of 1) China memory progress, 2) follow-
through from Korea (KOSPI -365bps), 3) ongoing turn in flows (desk much better for sale across
memory/HDDs into the end of last week). In addition, Mag7 continues to trade firm, today META +4% on
the introduction of "Muse Wonder" + ongoing "flight to quality" rotation. Elsewhere, crude continued to
move higher on weekend escalations in the Middle East. Our floor was a 2 on a 1-10 scale in terms of
overall activity levels with overall client skews benign.
Week ahead: CPI Wednesday the main event, consensus 0.6% headline, 0.2% core. A 10bp downside
surprise on core would be meaningful for rates. Banks start Tuesday, ASML Wednesday, TSMC Thursday,
plus PPI, retail sales and then Warsh Friday.
DERIVS: The vol desk was quiet as fixed income was closed for the holiday. Despite various catalysts this
week such as EPS kicking off, CPI on Wednesday, and several Fed speakers, the S&P implied move
through Friday's close went out at 1% last week (~96bps as of this morning). That registers as one of the
lowest 1w implied moves in the last ~6y. Flow wise, we saw clients turn to HYG downside given the bond
market closure … 50k Jan 77 73 put spreads were bought in the market (nearly 300k total options trading
today). As we continue to witness a lack of conviction, low sentiment, and narrow breadth, the desk thinks
S&P upside is cheap into month-end. We prefer owning 30Oct SPY/QQQ gamma for exposure to earnings
without midterm premium + still capturing the Fed and 65% of S&P market cap reports. The S&P implied
move for tomorrow went out at 34bps. (TY Shayna Peart)
