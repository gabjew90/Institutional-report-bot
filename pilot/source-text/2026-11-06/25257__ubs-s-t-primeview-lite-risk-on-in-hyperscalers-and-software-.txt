PrimeView (lite): Risk On in Hyperscalers
and Software Post Midterm Results
Amandine-Parisa Kara
05-Nov-2026, 03:59 ET (EST)
Hedge funds focused on long buying in Hyperscalers and Software post midterm results, while flows in Power
and Neoclouds were notably muted despite yesterday’s sharp sell-off.
Hedge fund flows in the UBS PB Book supportive of yesterday’s risk-on price action:
• Hyperscalers were the most bought segment of US equities yesterday (+1.1std), driven by 100% long
buying! This comes after hedge funds had de-risked the names into earnings/midterms, albeit with high
volatility in flows.
• Software & SaaS was also strongly bought (+1.6std), with 93% of flows driven by long buying,
concentrated in AI Software names {UBXXAISP} / {UBXXSORT}. This is a continuation of September's net
buying (covering + long buying). 
• Hardware (-0.4std) and Memory (-0.3std) remained net sold. Both segments have been de-risked since
May, which is when HF de-grossing began ahead of the summer unwind. Charts below.
• Rate Cut Winners {UBXXCUT} were net bought +2.5std yesterday, driven by 73% long buying.
• Hedge funds had strongly covered shorts and long bought into US Staples through October ahead of
midterms with a Dem win priced. HFs were therefore well positioned for yesterday's bounce. Chartsbelow.
Contra market flows:
• US Power: despite the names falling -6.3% yesterday {UBXXVOLT}, hedge fund flows were muted.
• Neoclouds: flows were similarly muted despite the names falling -8.4% yesterday. Taken together, hedge
funds do not appear to have driven the sharp moves in either Power or Neoclouds, suggesting the selling
came from elsewhere, potentially macro funds.
• Semis: flows were also muted despite the space falling -3.2% yesterday. However, hedge funds had already
significantly de-risked Semis in October, taking positioning back to July lows, leaving less exposure to
unwind yesterday.
• Materials was the clearest profit-taking pocket (-3.2std), as hedge funds used the bounce to reduce
exposure. Long selling was concentrated in Specialty Chemicals {UBXXSCHM} (-1.5std).
Europe: Broadening Into Laggards
In Europe, the Ex-AI market was the most bought basket, in-line with the broadening price action into
laggards {UBXEX600}.
• From a factor perspective, High Momentum (+1.7std) and Low Quality (+2.2std) saw the strongest net
buying. Importantly, >70% of flow was driven by long buying, with only ~30% from covering, as HFs added
risk rather than simply closed shorts.

• Luxury saw +2.2std of covering, its strongest day of net buying YTD. Consumer Discretionary saw +1.5std
of covering.
• High Quality and Energy exposure continued to unravel as the market priced US/Iran closer to a
resolution, extending a trend in place since mid-October. Hedge funds have also been adding shorts in EU

UBS Strategy: Buy Semis and Hyperscalers
UBS Equity Strategy recommends buying Semis at these levels, supported by a smoother pace of
data-centre buildout given the Democratic win, expectations for buybacks, intact underlying demand and
valuations at multi-year lows. This comes against light positioning in the UBS PB Book, with US Semis
exposure back at July lows (chart below). Equity Strategy also recommends buying Hyperscalers (cloud
revenue growth ~tripled QoQ).
