A Closer Look
Sector Roundup
Demand for AI Continues to Exceed Supply of Compute
Microsoft: “In Azure, we expect revenue growth of 49% to 50% in constant currency with continued efficiency 
gains across our CPU and GPU fleet, helping to offset some 3P GPU-as-a-Service contracts that have been delayed 
to H2. Customer demand continues to exceed available supply. And looking beyond Q2, year-over-year growth will 
continue to reflect the timing and scale of engineering efficiencies delivered in the quarter and new capacity adds, 
as well as a strong prior year comparable that included accelerating growth in the second half.”
Alphabet: “Our AI investments are driving increased usage and overall query growth, including commercial 
queries. In fact, our year-over-year query growth rate is the highest it's been in the last five years. Google Cloud 
revenues grew 103%. Our AI infrastructure and solutions are the major drivers for Cloud growth, and our 
customers see us as the leading provider here. So there's huge demand. We are working hard to bring more 
capacity online to meet this long-term demand.”
Amazon: “Q3 was another strong quarter for Amazon. In AWS, our plan is working. Despite lapping a comparable 
year-over-year period where we added a lot of capacity, AWS grew 39.3% year-over-year, accelerating for the sixth 
straight quarter, our fastest growth in nearly five years. Backlog is $578 billion, growing triple digits year-over-year 
and well diversified. We're installing an AI accelerator about every 14 seconds and almost as fast as we install 
capacity customers are gobbling it up. Our core compute and storage revenue grew at their fastest rates in several 
years.”
