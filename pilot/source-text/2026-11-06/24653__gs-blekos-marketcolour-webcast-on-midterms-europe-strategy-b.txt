## Marketcolour + Webcast on Midterms / Europe Strategy / Bayer
/ Zalando / Gecina (dg) / Heidelberg ##
Ioannis Blekos
4 Nov 2026
Good Morning,
US equities finished Tuesday's trading session mostly higher: SPX +36 bps to 7,568 (SPX vs SPX >70bps, Tech +131
bps, Materials -193 bps, Staples -176 bps), NDX +68 bps to 28,180 (Semis up ~2.5%), and RTY +23 bps to 2,812.
The Data Centers basket (GSTMTDAT +3.70%), and the AI Basket (GSTMTAIP +2.78%) outperformed. In Europe,
EU Miners (GSXEMINE -2.8%) underperformed with copper at an 11-week low and EU Big Oils (GSXEBOIE -1.2%)
sold off alongside Brent.
 
GS Webcasts on Midterms: 1/ US Midterm Elections: First Take – 13.00 UKT. Link 2/ Views from D.C. and the
Trading Floor – 15.15 UKT. Link
 
Baskets: In a Democratic sweep scenario, our favorite long is Affordable Care Act Beneficiaries (GSHLCACA).
 
Tech / Flows: Much more balanced 2-way picture on our desk today, with some of the ongoing supply across
Semis/AI now met with select buy tickets... (vs. last several sessions where supply was met with almost zero defense).
Encouraging to see Meta and Orcle both trade well today following recent capital raises as we remain in a "paper-rich"
environment (concentrated in AI), important to see the mkt absorb large issuance.
 
Europe Strategy: We raise our STOXX 600 EPS growth forecasts to 18% for 2026, 7% for 2027 and introduce our
forecast for 2028 of 5%. The upgrade reflects the results of our updated earnings framework, with strong contributions
from Financials and Commodities. Link
 
AI Financing: Hyperscalers have issued $271 billion of debt so far this year—above our full-year 2026 forecast
for $250 billion. The true scale and scope of AI-related debt issuance is also much larger than ‘just’ the five
hyperscalers. Through November 2nd, we estimate $657 billion of year-to-date, global, AI-related debt issuance
across syndicated credit markets—underscoring the importance of managing overall exposure. Link
 
Bayer (Buy / PT EUR66 from EUR63.5 / 30% Upside): We remain Buy rated driven by the potential imminent court
ruling on the glyphosate settlement, which could potentially enable Bayer to move past the litigation overhang, coupled
with strong performance in Pharma, as the division enters a new phase of growth, which we believe remains under-
appreciated by the market. Link
 
Gecina (DG to N / PT to EUR72.6 from EUR80.4): Following our recent asset tour with the company, we revise our
forecasts lower, with 2026-30E EPS changing by -8% to 0%, factoring-in longer lead-time to let pipeline project
lettings, slightly lower rental reversion, and we also remove our share buyback assumptions Link
 
Heidelberg: Decent Q3 vs cautious expectations, should be well received. Q3 sales and recurring ebit c.3% beats vs
VA, with a return to +ve price cost alongside +ve volumes for 2nd consec qr. Europe the +ve highlight with strong EU
cement – calling out Eastern & Northern Europe inc Germany, NAM in line which comes as a relief after more cautious
statements / downgraded guidance from CRH and Amrize. Reiterating FY guidance also comes as a relief, some
feedback in recent days expecting it to be downgraded.

 
Zalando (BUY / PT EUR44 / 60% Upside / 10x FY 27 Nopat / 12.6% FCF Yield): 3Q26 results demonstrated ongoing
robust growth in the higher margin 3P and Retail Media businesses, both of which grew at +17.8% yoy. From a B2B
perspective, the company continues to leverage its differentiated suite of offerings across logistics and software to
drive strong, margin accretive growth and notably signed a number of new customers for its ZEOS fulfillment and
Scayle businesses. Link
 
US Earnings Snippets: ANET +6% .. wow, big acceleration to +59% y/y growth in 3Q (highest growth since IPO) //
AMD +7% .. (much?) better than feared on Revs and GMs (esp after Intel last week) … // PCOR +13% . .nice beat /
acceleration .. 3Q Revs $402mn or ACCEL to +19% y/y vs guide $382-384mn or +13.3% y/y (vs +16% y/y last qtr) –
inclusive of 2pts of growth from DroneDeolpoy acq… cRPO +29% y/y vs +22% y/y last qtr // BKNG -7% AHs after
guiding to sharp y/y decel in 4Q RNs ..
