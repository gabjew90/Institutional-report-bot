Polls close in parts of Kentucky and Indiana. Early results could provide an initial indication of

7pm
Georgia and South Carolina
Polls close at 7pm. Georgia historically reports results quickly. In 2024, the first results were
reported shortly after polls closed, more than 50% of the vote was reported by 9pm and 90% by
10:30pm. South Carolina reports at a more moderate pace. In 2024, the first results came 20
minutes after polls closed, with 50% reported by 9pm and 90% shortly after 11pm ET.
Results in both states should be relatively unsurprising . Sen. Jon Ossoff (D-GA) is polling 8pp
ahead of his Republican challenger. Sen. Darline Graham, the sister of the late Sen. Lindsey
Graham, is running in a state President Trump won by 18pp in 2024. If Graham underperforms, it
could signal a broad national Democratic wave extending into deeply Republican (R+15) states
and districts. If Ossoff underperforms, it could signal a less favorable Democratic environment
than current polling suggests. Georgia also has a competitive gubernatorial race worth
watching for potential implications for the state's data center policy.
Florida
Results in three districts redrawn to favor Republicans will be an early test of Republicans'
redistricting strategy. Florida's 14th and 22nd congressional districts are R+4. Florida's 25th
congressional district is R+3. Democratic incumbents are defending FL-14 in the Tampa Bay area
and FL-25 in Broward County. FL-22 is an open seat. If the Democrats can hold FL-14 and FL-25
and win FL-22 despite the Republican lean of these districts, it would suggest that redistricting
may deliver fewer Republican gains than anticipated.
7:30pm
Ohio
The Ohio Senate race is one of four competitive Senate races in states President Trump won by
double digits in 2024 (Trump won Ohio by 11.2pp). Democrats' path to retaking the Senate
majority likely runs through Ohio. Polls close at 7:30pm ET. Ohio historically reports results
quickly. In 2024, 50% of votes were reported by 9pm and 90% by 11pm ET.
North Carolina
North Carolina is a must-win for Democrats if they are to retake the Senate majority. With
Democrat Roy Cooper leading by nearly 11pp in the Real Clear Politics polling average, the race
will be an early benchmark of polling accuracy. Results broadly in line with the polling average
should increase confidence in Democratic leads in states with later poll-closing times. Polls
close at 7:30pm ET and historically reports results quickly. In 2024, 50% of votes were reported
by shortly after 9:00pm and 90% by 11:30pm ET.

Texas

should be interpreted cautiously.
New Hampshire
Democrats are favored to win the Senate race, but Republicans have identified New Hampshire
as a potential pickup opportunity on a Senate map where they are otherwise largely playing
defense. Polls close by 8pm, and the state reports reports at a moderate pace. In 2024, 50% of
votes were reported by shortly after 10pm and 90% by 2am.
Kansas
Kansas last elected a Democratic senator in 1932, FDR's landslide presidential victory, but
recent polling suggest the race could be competitive. Polls close at 8pm. Kansas historically has
reported results quickly. In 2024, the first results were reported shortly after polls closed, 50%
by 10pm ET, and 90% by midnight. Kansas also has a history of defying polling. Most notably in
2014, incumbent Republican Sen. Pat Roberts won by 11pp after trailing in the polling average
by a point. However, 2014 was a strong Republican midterm nationally, while this year’s
political environment favors Democrats.
Mississippi
President Trump won Mississippi by 23 points in 2024, making the Senate race a test of the
upper bound of Democratic gains. Polls close at 8pm ET. In 2024, over 80% of votes were
reported by midnight. If the Senate race is competitive, it would suggest a "blue tsunami."

consideration or what precise attributes are required to be eligible to be categorised by such terms. This means there are different ways to evaluate a
company or an investment and so different values may be placed on certain sustainability credentials as well as adverse ESG-related impacts of
companies and ESG controversies. The evolving nature of sustainable investing considerations, models and methodologies means it can be
challenging to definitively and universally classify a company or investment under a sustainable investing label and there may be areas where such
companies and investments could improve or where adverse ESG-related impacts or ESG controversies exist. The evolving nature of sustainable
finance related regulations and the development of jurisdiction-specific regulatory criteria also means that there is likely to be a degree of divergence
as to the interpretation of such terms in the market. We expect industry guidance, market practice, and regulations in this field to continue to evolve.
