Blue Sweep, Red Dollar, Green Equities
Dimitrios Laloudakis
04-Nov-2026, 07:19 ET (EST)
United States: Democrats take Congress with wins across key races in South Carolina, Texas, Ohio, Kansas,
Michigan, and Iowa. As of now, Democrats have 228 seats in the House (218 seats for majority) and 54 seats
in the Senate (51 seats for majority). The asset class reaction so far has been clearly risk-on with S&P 500
+1.1% and trades back near 7,700 index level. US rates bid a few basis points across the curve with 10s
rallying 3bps and back down near 4.90%. Within G10 FX, notable bearish USD price action with selling across
the board. Higher beta currencies e.g. NZD (+70bps), AUD (+63bps), and EUR (+50bps) leading G10 today.
FX Divergence has been max short USDs in G10 in the systematic model + quantamental. Figure #1 plots the
evolution of systematic positioning over time.
• Sweden: Riksbank delivered a 25bps hike increasing policy rates to 2.0%. Broad SEK strength is somewhat
muted except for USDSEK (-35bps). A core view that has been reinforced in FX Divergence publications has
been for USDSEK and EURSEK lower, both of which were originally flagged at above 10.0 the figure and
11.30 level, respectively, Figure #2 plots USDSEK tactical macro valuation charts.
• Antipodeans: New Zealand’s labor data has deteriorated sharply (-0.1% vs. +0.4% estimate) originally
causing NZD weakness. However, the pro-risk stance exhibited post US-midterm elections has superseded.
Expectation here would be to fade the rally given the surprise RBNZ decision should still loom on investor
positioning. With that said, 0.56 the figure or lower may offer asymmetric opportunities to engage in longs as
UBSe still views the December meeting as a hike with ~16bps priced in.
• Eurozone: October’s inflation reading that has broadly come in-line with market estimates with headline
accelerating to 4.0% vs. 3.8% prior vs. core CPI reading remaining unchanged m/m. A core view entered in
both the systematic model (10/27 longs) as well as confirmed quantamentally in the latest FX Divergence.
