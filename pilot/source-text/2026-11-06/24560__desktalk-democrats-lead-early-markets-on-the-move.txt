DeskTalk - Democrats lead early, markets on the move
By Preston Pagli
Markets are on the move as polls close across the East Coast, with early results favoring Democrats. So far, the AP has
called 21 House seats for Democrats vs. 10 for Republicans, while Virginia, Rhode Island, New Jersey, Delaware, Georgia,
Illinois, and Massachusetts Senate seats have been called for the Dems. 
As expected, the initial market reaction has been risk-off. Equity futures, US yields, and the dollar are all lower, while tech
stocks and Asian equities are under clear pressure, with Nasdaq futures down 0.5% and Nikkei -2%. If current results
continue to hold, watch our 'Blue Sweep' basket (CGUSBLUW)
The 10y UST yield has fallen as much as 5bps to 4.91%. In FX, activity is subdued to start on our Asia eTrading desk,
though JPY and CHF are outperforming early on, with some of those gains now being pared back.
In rates, attention will turn to Wednesday's Quarterly Refunding Announcement at 08:30 EST / 13:30 GMT. Our strategists
expect the Treasury to reduce issuance sizes in the 20-year and 30-year sectors.
Prediction markets are increasingly pricing in a strong night for the Democrats with the House at 98% and Senate
control at 92%. For US users, track the key races and up to date odds on Citi Velocity's new Prediction Market dashboard.
Elsewhere, oil prices are trading lower after President Trump told the Financial Times that the US has "no immediate plan" to
strike Iran.
