DeskTalk - Markets absorb blue wave, green light for risk
By Varshi Karamsetty
Decent pickup in risk sentiment in the London morning after a blue wave was confirmed by the Democrats overnight. In the
near-term, markets could shift back into fundamentals which should continue to support risk assets, with fiscal/AI policy more
of a 2027 risk. 
Quite a reversal with SPX e-minis now trading +0.7% at 7652. Initial concerns following a Dem win in the Texan
Senate/Governor centered on risk of data center moratorium, but timelines of when and if these materialize matter.
Roadblocks may eventually appear from Jan 2027 when we see a change of power, until then, earnings/AI funding concerns
will remain in the forefront. Last night's positive AMD/ANET earnings should help tech sentiment intraday, with nothing yet to
invalidate the "Santa rally" we typically see into year-end. 
Next SPX e-minis resistance levels stand at 7724 (50dMA) while we fast approach CTA levels to flip long at 7670 (max long
levels at 7765). Levels are further away for Nasdaq are further away at 29674 (100dMA) with levels to flip long not until
29908. European equities continue to hold outperform and our D1 desk's preferred play is CGDORBND Index, a combination
of EU banks, semis and electrification names. 
UST rally has also stalled and mostly reversed in the front-end. Our macro strategist still thinks the path forward is a likely
steeper curve, but in the very near-term, QRA announcement is in focus (13:30 GMT). Citi is out of consensus looking for
$3bn cuts in 20y and 30y issuance, which could continue the rally. 
Risk-correlated currencies bounce higher with antipodeans best performers and ZAR rallying in line with gold. Volumes are
only marginally higher vs 30d averages with leveraged selling of AUD and CNH against JPY and GBP. Real money names
are less active, but JPY buying against EUR stands out. The idea that a local USD top being put in place last week may start
to play out, our trader writes. US data remains the key source of support, but if growth indicators begin surprising to the
downside, that support could quickly erode. EURUSD remains a buy on dips, but the 1.1260 has proved difficult to breach. 
Ultimately, all our desks agree that midterms has been viewed as a clearing event, with no changes to near-term narratives.
For now, Trump's willingness to work with the Democrats and confirming no near-term strikes on Iran (though oil prices are
only -1% weaker) are noteworthy, but headlines are worth watching ahead. As we go to print, AP has called 54 seats to the
Democrats, with 2 (Alaska and Mississippi yet to be called). Results were much quicker to be called overnight with the first
coming through at 00:55 GMT (19:55 ET) for Georgia.
