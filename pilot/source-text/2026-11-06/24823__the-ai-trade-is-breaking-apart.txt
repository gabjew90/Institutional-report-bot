The great tech rotation
Goldman's trading desk highlights a clear rotation within tech, with hyperscalers outperforming by
350bps while semiconductors lag by 250bps as investors use them as a source of funds. The logic is
interesting: restrictions on data center expansion could make existing compute capacity more
valuable, supporting pricing power while reducing capex requirements and potentially freeing up cash
for buybacks. Meanwhile, the broader AI complex remains under pressure, with AI Leaders down
500bps, power-related stocks down 600bps and data centers down 540bps.
The next phase of the AI trade may favour those monetizing compute rather than those building it.

Has the damage been done?
Goldman's Callahan highlights the extraordinary valuation reset in semiconductors, with forward P/E
multiples collapsing more than 40% over the past two months, from 28x to just 16x. The SOX now
trades at roughly a 2-turn forward P/E discount to the S&P 500, near the widest valuation gap seen in
15 years. With so much multiple compression already behind us, the key question is how much of the
policy uncertainty and AI infrastructure risk has already been priced in.

Relative semis stress
While the VIX is collapsing, semiconductor volatility (VXSMH) has barely budged, leaving a massive
gap between the two. The broader market may be stabilizing, but semiconductor volatility suggests the
stress is far from over.
