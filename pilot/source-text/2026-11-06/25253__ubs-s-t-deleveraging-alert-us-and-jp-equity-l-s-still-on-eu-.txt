Deleveraging Alert: US and JP Equity L/S
Still “On”; EU Equity L/S Pauses; US Quant
“On”
Amandine-Parisa Kara
05-Nov-2026, 04:34 ET (EST)
Gross outflows in Equity L/S have moderated across both the US and Europe, but our Deleveraging
Indicator remains “on”. October was the worst performance month YTD for Equity L/S in both US and Europe.
While gross reductions have now stabilised, performance remains tough and as such the indicator remains
“on” (MTD numbers shown below).
In Japan, Equity L/S reduced gross exposure by -1std, following Tuesday’s market close. As a result, the
indicator remains active for a second consecutive session.
Quant strategies have also had a difficult start to the month performance-wise. US Quant gross outflows were
muted yesterday, while EU Quant strategies reduced exposure by -0.7std.
