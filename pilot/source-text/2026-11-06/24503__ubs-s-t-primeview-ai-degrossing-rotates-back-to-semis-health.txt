PrimeView: AI Degrossing Rotates Back to
Semis; Healthcare & Longevity Profit
Taking
Josh Guilliard
03-Nov-2026, 03:00 ET (EST)
Last Friday’s degrossing shifted away from Hyperscalers and back towards Semis, with selling also
extending into AI Capex {UBEQUSAC} and Optical names {UBXXOPTC}. Last week was the third-largest
week of degrossing YTD, with gross outflows running at 25x the 1Y average. On Monday (yesterday), Semis
degrossing continued but at a much more muted pace (<0.5std), while we saw small dip buying in Memory
(+0.5std) and Hardware (+0.7std). Flows are settling ahead of the midterms.
Following two consecutive days of heavy systematic selling, UBS Equity Derivs strategy now estimates Risk
Control strategies turning buyers today, and scope for recovery thereafter. Combined with the pullback in
both price and positioning, this creates an attractive re-entry setup in Hyperscalers post-midterms;
particularly if the event proves to be a clearing catalyst, given Hyperscalers remain the higher-quality
expression of the AI theme (chart below). Importantly, US Semis net exposure is now back at July lows,
while Hardware and Memory exposure has continued to decline since the summer unwind as hedge funds
focused attention and capital on Hyperscalers.
Hedge funds took profits in our Longevity & Wellness basket {UBEQLGLWL} last week following a +6.5%
move since 8 October. This basket is a UBS Equity Strategy top-pick. Healthcare was the third most net sold
sector last week on profit taking (after Semis & Media Ent). Healthcare has provided a defensive
counterweight to the latest AI unwind. Against that backdrop, we have seen profit-taking in both Healthcare
and Longevity & Wellness since July NB: positioning remains heavily net long in both baskets, with L/S
ratios above 4x (chart below).
