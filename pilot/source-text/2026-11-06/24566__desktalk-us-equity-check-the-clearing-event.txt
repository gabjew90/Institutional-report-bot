DeskTalk - US equity check: The clearing event
By Preston Pagli
After a sharp reversal overnight as the US midterm election results became more clear, equities began trending upwards, with
SPX now on track for its best single day in months, with the election being treated as a clearing event.
The SPX is up 1.8%, led by a sharp rally in the Mag 7 after Texas Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa pledged to crack down on the
AI buildout and "stop the data center takeover," easing concerns around aggressive AI-related CapEx spending. Our US
Electric Utilities team discussed the potential implications here. By contrast, hardware names are under pressure, with the
SOX down nearly 3%. Sector performance is broadly positive, with Industrials and Energy the only laggards as oil tumbled
10%. My colleague Varshi Karamsetty took a closer look into the drivers. Elsewhere, Healthcare is among the top-performing
sectors amid expectations of expanded Medicaid subsidies under a Democratic Congress.
On the cash equity desk, flows are skewed toward Consumer Staples, while Info Technology, Financials, Consumer Disc.,
and Energy are seeing net outflows. Beneath the surface, vol has collapsed as election-related event risk fades. Spot VIX is
down roughly 20% to 17, while put skew remains contained, suggesting investors have yet to aggressively rebuild hedges.
Looking ahead, earnings remain in focus, with Arm Holdings, Qualcomm, and AppLovin reporting after today's close. Before
then, a key CTA level to watch in the SPX (ESZ6) is 7,670 (ref $7,740). A close above that level would flip the index back
long, pointing to systematic buying flows on Thursday.
