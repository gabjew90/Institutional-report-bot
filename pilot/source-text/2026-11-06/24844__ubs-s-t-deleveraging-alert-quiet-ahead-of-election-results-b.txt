Deleveraging Alert: Quiet Ahead of Election
Results, but US & EU Indicators Remain
“On”
Amandine-Parisa Kara
04-Nov-2026, 03:14 ET (EST)
The US Equity L/S and Quant Indicators remain switched “on”, marking the fourth consecutive session for
Equity L/S and the second for Quants. US Quants cut >3std of gross exposure yesterday, while Equity L/S
reduced gross by a more moderate -0.5std, given substantial de-grossing already. The Fundamental L/S
Hyperscaler and Semis deleveraging appears to have bled into Quant strategies and spread to EU/Japan.
• The Europe Equity L/S Indicator also remains “on” for a second consecutive session, with Fundamental
Equity L/S cutting a moderate -0.5std of European gross yesterday.
• Japan was closed for Culture Day (02/11).
• US Equity L/S flows were muted yesterday ahead of the election results. The most notable move was
+1.2std of net buying in Ex-AI names, split 50/50 long buying/short covering.
• European flows were similarly subdued. The most meaningful activity was -1.2std of net selling in EU
Banks, driven 75/25 by shorting/long selling. Meanwhile, defensive Healthcare/Pharma continued to see
buying (>1std).
• Hedge funds continue to liquidate European Energy longs while adding shorts, extending a theme that
has been in place since mid-October (chart below).
