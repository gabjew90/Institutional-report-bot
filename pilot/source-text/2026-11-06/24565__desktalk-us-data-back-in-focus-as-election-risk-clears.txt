DeskTalk - US data back in focus as election risk clears
By Preston Pagli
US data returned Wednesday morning as markets continued to digest the outcome of the 2026 US midterm elections, where
Democrats completed a blue sweep. See our experts cross-asset first take here.
ADP private payrolls increased by 86k in October, comfortably above expectations, and a positive sign ahead of Friday's
nonfarm payrolls report, where our economists forecast a 65k gain, in line with consensus.
September's Trade Balance surprisingly narrowed to -$105.0bn, little changed from the prior month, while exports rose 1.9%,
marking their strongest monthly increase in four months.
The ISM Services index fell to 54.5 from 54.9 in September, below expectations though still firmly in expansionary territory.
Prices paid fell to 73.0 from 74.0 in September, the second-highest reading since 2022. 
The most anticipated macro reading for US rates aside from the election was the Treasury's Quarterly Refunding
Announcement. The Treasury set next week's 3y, 10y, and 30y auctions at a combined $125bn, in line with expectations.
The most notable development came in the guidance. The Treasury revised its language on coupon issuance from
maintaining auction sizes for "at least the next several quarters" to simply "the next several quarters," opening the door to
auction size adjustments in 2027.
USTs are little changed following the data, after a sharp reversal in risk following last night's election results. The benchmark
10y yield had fallen as much as ~7bps to below 4.90% before quickly retracing higher as the event risk cleared, sending US
equities soaring with SPX ~1.8% higher. The dollar is weaker, but off the morning's lows with our G10 FX Strategists finding
dips attractive here. 
Looking ahead, Friday's nonfarm payrolls report will be a key input into December's FOMC decision. Our economists will be
closely watching average hourly earnings. A softer reading would reinforce Chair Warsh's view that the economy is not
overheating and support the case for a hold in December.
