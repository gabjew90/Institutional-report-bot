DeskTalk - Democrats secure the Senate, risk assets retreat
By Preston Pagli
The 2026 US midterm election results are clear, with Democrats outperforming expectations and completing a "blue sweep."
Just before midnight ET, the AP projected Democrats would regain control of the Senate, successfully flipping Iowa,
Kansas, North Carolina, Ohio, South Carolina, and Texas, giving the party 51 Senate seats according to current AP
projections.
One of the most shocking results of the night was incumbent Greg Abbott's loss to Gina Hinojosa in the Texas Gubernatorial
race, which our strategists had been flagging as a key race due to the risks to the AI trade. This marks the first Democratic
Texas Governor since 1990. 
Markets have reacted in a clear risk-off fashion. SPX futures trade 1.3% weaker while the pain is more clear in tech, with
Nasdaq-100 futures lower by 2.3% lower. In Asia, the Kospi and Nikkei trade -3% and -4.5% respectively. Watch our 'Blue
Sweep' basket (CGUSBLUW) as US equity markets open Wednesday. 
The Citi eFX trading desk has been busy, reporting broad based inflows into JPY while segment by segment flows were
more mixed across USD, EUR, and CNH. Leveraged accounts were net buyers of JPY, GBP, CAD, HKD and  sellers of USD,
EUR, AUD, CHF, CNH. Investor segment flows skewed into USD, JPY, and EUR. 
In response to tonight's election, President Trump told the NYT he is "absolutely willing to accept those results”.
