DeskTalk - Election night repricing underway
By Preston Pagli
It is shaping up to be an early election night, with Democrats now on track for a a larger than expected Senate majority,
while DDHQ has projected that the Democrats will regain control of the House.
Markets are reacting sharply to the prospect of a larger-than-expected Democratic victory. Nasdaq-100 futures (NQA) are
down nearly 2%, with weakness spilling into Asia with the Kospi -3% and the Nikkei -4%. The move is being driven largely
by concerns around the future of the AI data center build out, particularly as the Texas gubernatorial race appears increasingly
likely to flip blue. Prediction markets are currently giving Gina Hinojosa (D) a 94% chance to defeat incumbent Greg Abbott
(R). Democrats have pressed on the data center issue this election cycle, with roughly 125 Democratic campaigns across
House, Senate and gubernatorial races running on data center energy usage. Looking further out and based on tonight's
results our strategist Scott Chronert sees the possibility of markets pricing in the risk of of higher corporate taxes after '28. 
USTs are benefiting from the risk-off backdrop as investors seek safety. Yields are lower across the curve, with the
benchmark 10y falling more than 7bps to break below 4.90%.
The dollar is extending its decline alongside lower US yields, with the DXY (-0.4%) slipping to its weakest level in a month.
Our G10 FX Strategy team continues to favor fading USD weakness ahead of upcoming US data and the potential for
further escalation in the Middle East, while near-term focus remains on election results and Wednesday's potentially dovish
Quarterly Refunding Announcement. Asia currencies are making notable moves with USDJPY -0.70% with our eFX desk
seeing JPY inflows from leveraged accounts thus far. 
For now, we will await the rest of the results to roll in through the night. Citi Velocity live coverage and insights will continue
through the US Midterms Content Feed.
