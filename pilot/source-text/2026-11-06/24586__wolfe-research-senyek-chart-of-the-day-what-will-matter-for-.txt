Wolfe Research - Senyek - Chart of the Day - What Will Matter for
the AI Trade - 4 Nov 2026
Chris Senyek (with Adam Calingasan, Drew Barrett, Peter Englert, Samuel Kinney)  ·  Wolfe Research  ·  Portfolio Strategy
4 Nov 2026
• Despite moratoriums, bottlenecks, and general negative consumer sentiment around the AI Buildout,
construction put in place for data centers has hit new highs and year-to-date surpasses 2025’s total dollar
amount. Over the course of 3Q 2026 reporting season, fears over peak capex growth have had a
stranglehold on markets, while worries over incremental profitability have weighed on the AI complex.
Though the AI trade has wavered over the past week, we continue to believe that demand for compute
remains robust. More specifically, we viewed earnings results last night from AMD and SMCI as very
solid.

• Our sense is that the surprise victory by Hinojosa in Texas and subsequent “Blue Wave” across Congress
spells trouble for the AI trade in the very short-term. With solid 3Q 2026 earnings reports out last night, it
will be telling, in our view, whether stocks trade with fundamentals today or revert to choppy macro price
action. Along this view, we’ll be closely watching how the new governor of Texas, Gina Hinojosa, handles
the ongoing pause in data center permitting as the state boasts one of the largest backlogs in data centers
from a power capacity perspective and could likely serve as a model for opposition response to the
buildout.
