Report: Prime Time / Monthly Wrap | Many Signs of De-Risking as HF 
Performance is Hit Hard 
  
Brief Summary: October is a month that most HFs will probably want to forget.  
There’s been seemingly nowhere to hide from a strategy perspective as there have 
been challenges across the board.  In many ways, most of this can be traced back to 
AI / Tech as credit concerns and lackluster earnings have not been able to stem the 
tide thus far.  What’s perhaps surprising is that the broader indices in the US and 
Europe were basically flat to up this past month, which makes the HF pain stand out 
even more.  In our view, there have been a lot of different signs of de-risking over the 
past month, which—taken together—could suggest an improvement is due soon.  
However, the lack of capitulation (at least prior to today), concerns about escalation 

in the Middle East post US midterm elections, and lingering AI concerns do make it 
hard to have strong conviction on the path forward. 
  
Key Points: 
  
-       Performance deteriorates sharply in October: Global HF net returns 
were down ~4% on a rolling 20d basis as of last Friday, with the MTD 
estimate at -2.6% through Thurs, Oct 29 (likely close to -3.5% after Friday). 
The global long-short spread was -2.2% MTD, with declines across all 
regions: -1.5% Asia, -1.9% N. Am., -2.4% EMEA. 
-       Alpha rebound potential, but risks remain: Prior -3z events (in 20d L-S 
performance spread) saw positive forward 20d spreads in 6 of 7 cases (avg 
+1%). De-grossing has been prevalent (Multi-Strats especially), and 
Momentum exposure is at >12m lows, though risks include potential Quant 
de-grossing and still-elevated US Tech flows/futures positioning. 
-       US positioning drifts lower but not sharply: The US Tactical Positioning 
Monitor fell to the 33rd %-tile as of Oct 29, though 4wk change was neutral. 
HFs cut net leverage earlier in Oct, before adding slightly this week.  Retail 
stayed sidelined (sentiment at 6th %-tile), ETF flows stayed positive (+1z), 
and CTA positioning fell MoM but is only 44th %-tile LT in US equities. 
-       US Midterms and seasonal Bank trades in focus: The D1 team’s Split 
Congress Pair (JPMSC26P) has rallied to 2yr highs, with HFs more bullish on 
Split Congress longs via short covering (mainly HC), though shorts (Cap 
Markets stocks) also saw buying/covering. Separately, US Banks have a 
strong Nov seasonal track record, Financials ETFs saw the strongest inflows 
last week, and HFs remain net long Banks. 

-       TMT vs. Defensives sentiment gap echoes prior lows: Social Media 
sentiment dropped to the 6th %-tile, coinciding with a wide TMT-Defensives 
spread also at the 6th %-tile — similar to several prior episodes where Tech-
led weakness was followed by Tech-led rebounds (prior episodes: late 
Mar ’26, Mar/Apr ’25, early Aug/Sep ’24, late Apr ’24, and end of Dec ’22). 
-       US Health Care and Utilities diverge: HFs flipped to buying Biotech and 
Med Tech after months of selling, with HC flows the strongest of any sector 
last month. Conversely, Utilities rallied but HFs kept selling, leaving the group 
with the lowest net positioning of any major industry. 
-       Europe positioning rebounds off lows: EU Positioning hit a low of the 
5th %-tile before rebounding to the 14th %-tile.  The recent dip and rebound 
suggests potential for further gains. EU Momentum positioning has declined 
with very negative flows over the past 3m, but HFs are still long Banks and 
bearish on Autos, Lux Goods, and Software. 
-       China/HK positioning remains depressed: China/HK aggregate 
positioning is the most bearish of any major region at just the 5th %-tile.
