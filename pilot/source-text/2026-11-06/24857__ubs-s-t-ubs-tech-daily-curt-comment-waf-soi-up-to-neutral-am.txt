WAF
Q3 revenue was 7% above cons and EBITDA was 13% above. Based on updated FY'26 guidance, we would
expect LSD% increases to FY26 and FY27 consensus.
1) Q3 revenues were €355m, 7% above consensus and up 18% y-o-y and c11% q-o-q, driven by strong wafer
demand across 300mm and 200mm;
2) EBITDA was €81m vs cons €72m, 13% above, mainly driven by higher wafer shipments, with limited impact
from product mix and price;
3) Non-LTA prices edged higher for 300mm and started to stabilise for 200mm, with first positive pricing points
in Q3. We see this as positive and continue to expect pricing to accelerate into next year;
Siltronic updated their full year guide and now expect flat sales y-o-y vs. low to mid-single-digit % y-o-y
decline in revenue (cons. -2%), and narrowed their EBITDA margin range to 22-24% vs. 20-24% previously
(cons. 22.7%).
Soitec upgrade to Neutral and raise our PT to €130 as Photonics growth accelerates, while smartphone and
AI spending risks limit upside.
We upgrade to Neutral, reflecting faster Photonics growth, resilient near-term pricing and greater scope to
reallocate smartphone RF SOI capacity to datacenter applications. We now expect Photonics to reach 50%
of revenues by C2028E, versus 28% previously. However, we see the shares as fairly valued, given
uncertainty over AI infrastructure spending and smartphone headwinds from macro uncertainty and
higher memory prices
Tim on AMD:  AMD's superior server roadmap and flexible supply situation drove guidance that was better
than feared post the INTC guide-down.
AMD reported solid numbers and guided about in-line w/ our model - and better than feared post INTC's guide
down - as server CPU upside is more than offsetting client downside. On top of this, AMD walked up C2027

2
guidance to a range that is roughly consistent with our model but well north of Street numbers as it is (we
think) in a better place supply-wise than INTC, both in terms of wafers and multi-sourced substrate supply. On
top of a superior roadmap, this supply advantage is proving out with this guidance and should not be
under-estimated going forward as both client and server are on N2 (as opposed to INTC's different nodes) and
recent substrate investments are finally starting to pay off. Gross margin commentary for 2027 - while
consistent with prior 55-56% messaging - is nonetheless encouraging since commentary suggests ~60% of
incremental data center dollars are coming from the lower-margin GPU business. Net, we walk numbers up
once again to ~$19 in C2027E and ~$30 for C2028E - a range that we long suggested was possible. C2029 is
still a long way out, but once the calendar turns to C2027, this should become a bigger part of the debate and
even assuming a significant deceleration in growth, we think ~$40 EPS is on the table. While we are raising
numbers, in recognition of multiples having compressed across the semis landscape, we are bringing our PT
down from $730 to $700, but we maintain Buy.
Arista
Q: How did the results compare vs expectations?
• Rev of $3.66B, up ~59% YoY beat our $3.31B est by ~10% (Guide $3.3B / Visible Alpha cons $3.3B).
• 'Product' rev of ~$3.2B was up ~59% YoY beating our $2.87B estimate and the Visible Alpha cons of $2.86B.
• GM of 64.0% beat the guide of ~63% and our 63.0% estimate and the Visible Alpha cons of 63.1% despite
stronger hyperscaler revenue in the mix.
• OpM of 49.1% was at the high-end of the 48% to 49% guide beating our 48.5% estimate (Visible Alpha cons
48.9%) as operating expenses only increased x% YoY.
• Diluted EPS of $1.20 beat our $1.07 estimate by ~12% (Guide $1.06 to $1.08 / Visible Alpha cons $1.08)
Q: What were the most noteworthy areas in the results?
A: Product revenue growth of ~67%% accelerated from 39% in the June qtr. Although Product revenue topped
our estimate by ~$323 million, gross margin was strong at 64.0%, 98 bps above our forecast. Finally,
operating expense came in at 14.9% of revenue, 40 bps above our forecast capping operating margin at
49.1%.
