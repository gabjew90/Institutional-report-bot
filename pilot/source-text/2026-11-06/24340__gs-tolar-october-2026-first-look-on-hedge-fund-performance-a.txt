L/S Performance Estimates: Fundamental L/S -3.5% and Systematic L/S -3.6% in October,
the first time on our record (since 2016) that returns from both cohorts fell by -3% or more.
 
 
 
 
Trading Flows: Global equities saw the largest $ net selling since March, driven by an equal
amount of short and long sales.

Exposures: Overall book net leverage fell at the fastest pace since March while gross
leverage increased (driven by mark-to-market). Fundamental L/S net leverage declined led
by reduction from Asia L/S funds.

Regional Positioning: Every region (sans DM Asia) was net sold, led by EM Asia and North
America.
 
 
 
US Sector Positioning: HFs rotated out of TMT and Cyclicals and into Defensives in
October.
