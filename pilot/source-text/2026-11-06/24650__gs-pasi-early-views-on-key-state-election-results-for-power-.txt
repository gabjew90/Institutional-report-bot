Elections have been a key focus area for investors across the power and utilities space due to the focus on
affordability and data centers across the US. As we wrote in our 3Q preview, we have been most focused
on Texas, Georgia, Ohio, and California, given the potential implications for data center development and
thus pace of power demand, pricing, and utility investment, as well as wildfire liability reform. Consistent
with our Macro team's post-election views, we believe Democratic wins could increase near-term
uncertainty around data center development, particularly in Texas and Georgia, though we continue to see
these markets as important longer-term areas for load growth. We believe the Texas gubernatorial outcome
could drive negative reactions for exposed stocks including Buy rated VST, NRG, SRE, and XEL, as well
as Neutral rated CEG and AEP. In Georgia, we believe the PSC election outcome could be negative for
Neutral rated SO. In Ohio, we believe the gubernatorial outcome could be less constructive for Buy rated
FE and Neutral rated AEP, with more limited impact on Buy rated DUK. Finally, in California, we now
await clarity on a potential special legislative session before November 15th. If called and depending on
the outcome, it might be a positive for Buy rated PCG and SRE as well as Neutral rated EIX. We focus on
state election results in this note, though recognize potential implications from federal results exist as well.
