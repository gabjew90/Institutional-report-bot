The AI capex dilemma
According to Privorotsky, anxiety around AI capex is reaching extreme levels as fresh bond issuance
raises questions about the cost and availability of capital. The valuation reset is brutal. SOX trades at
just 12.16x blended 24-month forward earnings, below the Liberation Day and Iran escalation lows and
approaching 2022 levels. Nvidia trades at just 10x, its lowest multiple since 2013.
The market is questioning whether 2027 marks peak earnings as financing constraints threaten the AI
buildout. The bull case is that AI adoption remains in its infancy. The bear case? Capacity is expanding
faster than demand can absorb it. Is this a generational buying opportunity or the beginning of a much
bigger earnings reset?
Time for tech bonds?

According to Hartnett at BofA, US tech bonds are down 9% YTD, with relative performance versus IG
bonds at 30-year lows. Hyperscaler debt issuance has exploded to $182bn in 2026, up from just $17bn
in 2024. But Hartnett sees opportunity, with Mag7 bond yields of 6–8% looking attractive. Meanwhile,
$89bn in hyperscaler equity issuance suggests the era of massive tech buybacks may be ending. The
AI buildout still needs financing, but perhaps the better risk/reward is moving from the equity to the
credit side of the capital structure.
