Market Internal Insight 
Systematic Selling Exhausted; Real Money Support Points to Recovery 
Today is supposed to be my day off, but I guess market doesn’t want me to rest.  ☹  After a second consecutive day of heavy systematic selling, our model estimates that 
Risk Control should finally turn to buyers tomorrow, while CTA selling is largely complete with limited incremental impact from here.  This sets up for a potentially 
quieter Tuesday ahead of the midterm election, with scope for a recovery beginning Wednesday post-election, particularly given the persistent buy-the-dip 
support from retail, foreign investors and multi-asset managers over the past three days. 
 
• 
Friday: I estimate ~$130-150 bn of systematic selling across CTA ($70-80 bn), Risk Control ($30-40 bn) and Leveraged ETFs ($30 bn).  Excess Flow was more resilient 
in Nasdaq, with only -$8 bn to sell in Nasdaq, versus -$24 bn to sell in S&P 500 given most CTA sell triggers were in S&P 500 
• 
Today: I estimate ~$85-115 bn of systematic selling across Risk Control ($65-85 bn), CTA ($10-15 bn) and Leveraged ETFs ($10-15 bn).  Excess Flow was better in 
S&P 500, with only -$16 bn to sell in S&P 500 (Chart 1), versus-$53 bn to sell in Nasdaq given most CTA and Leveraged ETF selling, together with some Risk 
Control deleveraging were concentrated in Nasdaq (Chart 2) 
• 
Tomorrow: Risk Control is estimated to buy +$10-15 bn.  CTA & Leveraged ETFs rebalance will depend on market moves 
• 
Over the last 3 down days, Real Money Support was Strong: UBS RMM clients bought-the-dip in all 3 days (Chart 3), foreign listed US ETFs bought all 3 
days (Chart 9) and Multi-Asset Managers / Risk Parity bought today with Eq/Bond Correlation back to Negative (Charts 6 & 7) 
• 
Corporate buyback is in low seasonality this and next week (Chart 10).  Will be in high seasonality of +$35-55 bn per week starting week of Nov 16 (Chart 11)
