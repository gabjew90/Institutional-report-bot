We're seeing MASSIVE retail selling in US single stocks in the first 30 minutes of today's cash session.
This marks the largest 30-minute selling episode since October, by a significant margin
Jai Malhi, Senior Equity Strategist comments; "today, retail are heavy sellers of single names - particularly
in tech. This is on track to be the largest amount of retail selling since the late July capitulation (chart below).
Interestingly, back then we saw retail selling continue for a number of days and continue when the market
began to recover. Clue: look for insti buying to help any recovery."
Source: Vanda
