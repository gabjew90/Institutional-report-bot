Derivatives trades / themes discussed this week:
#1. Rates has driven a significant flip in cross-asset correlations in October with average correlation levels now
at multi-year lows.
#2. Cross-asset implied correlation hasn’t yet responded to flip in realized creating attractive opportunity for
risk-off hybrid hedges.
#3. Mid-term volatility premiums rebounding into the event, but optionality through the rest of this week still
looks good value.
#4. Leveraged-fund investors have been buying-the-dip, including on Friday, although rebalance flows
involved much larger selling.
#5. Rebecca Cheong also noting ‘buy-the-dip’ in RMM and Excess Flow on Friday, and options investors
continue to fade moves.
#6. Offshore China Equities rebounded supported by rotation to Defensives; low FXI implied vols offer tactical
upside.
