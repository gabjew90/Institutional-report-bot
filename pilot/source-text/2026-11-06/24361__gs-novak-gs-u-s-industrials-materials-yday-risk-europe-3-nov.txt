Privo on risk: The hard part is disentangling how much of this is fundamental versus technical. The market
is nervous about the midterms, energy and the rates backdrop. People have had a very difficult time
hanging on to positions and, having made money in the first half, have been playing defense to protect
those gains. The clearing of the political event should be important. The S&P has been higher in the 12
months following all 19 midterms since 1950 … no guarantees but gridlock has generally been seen as
supportive for equities, although AI and data center restrictions remain an important risk to watch. We
remain on the back foot in a tricky environment. Everyone seems to be in some variation of risk reduction,
trying to maintain stability across scenarios without getting too far out the risk curve. We saw a lot of
index related hedging Friday. The upside is that sentiment and positioning have come down meaningfully,
and my gut is that the clearing of events will be positive through time. But we also need to find stability in
the AI trade as the market gets comfortable with corporate capital structures and the durability of revenue
supporting the spend. The first hurdle is avoiding an extreme midterm outcome … Texas will be important
to watch. After that, the questions are whether the Middle East moves towards progress or re-escalation,
and whether lower oil can deliver sustained relief in rates.
