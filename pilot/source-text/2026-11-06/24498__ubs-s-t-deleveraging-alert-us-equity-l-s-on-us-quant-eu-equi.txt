Deleveraging Alert: US Equity L/S “On”; US
Quant, EU Equity L/S & JP Equity L/S
Activate
Amandine-Parisa Kara
03-Nov-2026, 07:40 ET (EST)
Equity L/S hedge funds in the UBS PB Book de-grossed -0.8std of US equities and -2.6std of EU equities
yesterday. Combined with continued pain in consensus trades (Hyperscalers, Semis etc), this led the US
and Europe Equity L/S indicators to trigger. Our prediction framework also foresees further
deleveraging today (performance estimates below).
In the US, de-grossing was concentrated in Semis (PrimeView: AI Degrossing Rotates Back to Semis;
Healthcare & Longevity Profit Taking). In Europe, de-grossing was broad-based across sectors, driven by
both short covering and long selling.
US Quants cut -1.0std of gross exposure, while EU Quants added +1.1std yesterday. The US Quant
indicator has therefore also triggered, while the EU Quant indicator remains “Off”.
Japan Equity L/S has also triggered on performance pain, while Japan Quant is close to the threshold but not
active.
