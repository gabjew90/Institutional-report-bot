provides an interesting counterpoint. Mid-terms have typically been followed by a risk-on move

roughly 10% vs historical mid-term-year performance that was around flat.

EU Earnings Screen: asymmetric earnings reactions call for selectivity
As the European earnings season enters its final stretch, a meaningful amount of event risk
remains: around 190 STOXX 600 companies, representing approximately 25% of index market
capitalisation, are scheduled to report over the next two weeks. Earnings delivery has been
strong so far, but median price action has remained negatively skewed, with misses punished
more severely than beats have been rewarded (see Q3 Earnings – Hurdle cleared, Europe stands
out). With the market’s reaction function remaining asymmetric, earnings risk remains firmly in
focus, making it particularly important to assess where options markets may be under- or
overpricing the upcoming events.
Against this backdrop, we screen for relatively cheap/expensive earnings vols across liquid
option names within the Stoxx 600 (SXXP) universe scheduled to report in next two weeks.
