Equities finished on the lows after a ~400bp intra-day fade (NDX now up 5% YTD). No one headline drove the roll
though some pointed to an AMD short report which tapered overall sentiment. Overall, it just felt like more questions
than answers out there today with most wondering what gets this tape to feel better? INTC was +2%, PANW +6% on
earnings, a successful debut for the largest IPO in history today which saw robust demand from a variety of client
types. Outside of capital markets activity, our floor was a 7 on a 1-10 scale in terms of overall activity levels. Asset
Mgr and HFs net sold macro products vs net bought pockets of consumer and financials. No sector screened
materially for sale. Liquidity remains poor which is exacerbating price action (S&P top of book avg just $4mm on the
touch). POST BELL EPS:  ESTC -8% // INTU -13% AHs   
 
DERIVS: We saw extremely wide ranges in spot and vol with vol rallying hard on the sell-off post IPO open. Flows
were quiet even as we realized the straddle high to low multiple times on the day. SPX and NDX fixed strike vols
were bid - with gamma tenors closing just under +1v. Short dated options are pricing a great deal of fear with spot
VIX above 28 (sharply inverted) and the tomorrow straddle pricing in a 1.43% move. To put into context, spot VIX
traded more than 4v over UX1 - the most inverted term structure since April 2025. We haven't seen any appetite to
fade the move, and flows were categorized by put and put spread buying. We did also see vol lower offense being
played with a VIX Dec put tree bought 20k times. Going into regular expiry tomorrow, we estimate we are right
around max dealer short vega. From here, it seems like the market needs some of those options to be unwound to
settle into a more stable equilibrium. – TY Gail Hafif
