Thursday, November 19, 2026
For the first time since April 7, all four major US equity benchmarks (S&P 500, Dow Industrials, Nasdaq Composite, and Russell 2000) are 
trading below their 200-day moving averages. Underscoring this weakness, 49% of S&P 500 stocks are currently below their own 200-
DMA, versus a five-year average of 43%. Yet history suggests this technical weakness is not necessarily bearish. Over the past 20 years, 
when the S&P 500 traded below its 200-DMA, the median 12-month return was 10.9%, with gains 78% of the time.
