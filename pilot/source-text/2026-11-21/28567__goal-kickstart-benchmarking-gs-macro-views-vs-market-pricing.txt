Benchmarking GS macro views vs. market pricing for 2027
In 2027, our economists expect global real GDP growth of 
2.8% (slightly above 2.7% in 2026, but with risks tilted to the 
downside), as lower energy prices and still-strong AI capex 
outweigh tighter financial conditions and more neutral fiscal 
policy. They expect inflation to slow in 2027, as the impulse 
from US tariffs, energy and AI-related pressures fade. As a 
result, they expect only incremental policy tightening 
through early 2027 in most major economies before gradual 
normalization cuts later in 2027 as inflation comes down. 
Markets have already repriced growth lower across regions 
over the past few months: our RAI PC1 “Global growth 
factor” dropped as cyclicals sold-off relative to defensives, 
equities underperformed bonds, and credit spreads 
widened (Exhibit 1). While growth pricing showed some 
strength last week, the valuation re-set in risky assets and 
our Risk Appetite Indicator still close to 0 suggests a lower 
bar for risky assets to deliver positive returns if growth 
remains at healthy levels next year, even though both upside 
or downside tails remain possible.
