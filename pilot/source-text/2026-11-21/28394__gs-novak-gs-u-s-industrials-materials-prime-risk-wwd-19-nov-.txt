Prime Services: Industrials is the second most net sold US sector MTD, driven by short-
and-long sales (~2 to 1). Nearly all subsectors are net sold, led by Electrical Equip, Ground
Transportation, and Construction & Engineering. US Industrials net exposure has fallen to the
lowest level in more than 2 years. Factor exposure to Medium-term momentum is on track to
decline for a 6th straight month to the lowest level since Dec ’22 (20th percentile 5-year). At
the same time, factor exposure to value has risen sharply to 5-year highs amid increased
stagflation concerns.

Two notes from GIR to flag:
 
Sue Maklari out with reads from home center earnings to her coverage: Small
Ticket, Replacement Categories Lead Demand: This week, Home Depot and Lowe's
(covered by Kate McShane) reported F3Q results with both companies highlighting
demand led by maintenance and small ticket repair. More specifically, HD's US comps
grew 2.2% YOY vs +0.1% in F3Q25 with gains in 13 of 16 departments while LOW's
comps rose +1.8% YOY, vs +0.4% in F3Q25 with 7 of 13 categories expanding. Areas of
strength included plumbing (MAS, FBIN), appliances (WHR), paint (MAS), water heaters
(AOS) and lumber (WY). In our view, the tone of both calls and outlook was consistent
with our building products coverage, as the spike in mortgage rates above 7% and
underlying macro uncertainty are resulting in greater-than-expected weakness into year-
end. That said, price and mix are improving as manufacturers look to offset rising
inflation while new products help stimulate demand. As we expect these pressures to
persist, we are focused on company-specific initiatives to drive industry relative
outperformance and highlight Buy-rated FBIN, MAS, MHK and TREX as being among
those best positioned. Full note:
