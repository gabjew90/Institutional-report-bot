Apple
Apple has been the ultimate anti-AI trade, outperforming the rest of Mag7 as investors sought shelter
from the tech carnage.
AAPL is up 30% YTD, more than twice the return of the next-best Mag7 stock. The stock has started
coming off its highs and is approaching the trendline in place since April. The 50-day moving average
comes in another 1.5% lower.
Could Apple become the funding stock for investors looking to pick up beaten-down AI names?

Extremely rare divergence
BTIG highlights just how extreme Apple's outperformance has become. On November 17, AAPL closed
just 1.2% below its 52-week high while SPX was trading below its 200-day moving average. Over the
past 20 years, this combination has occurred on only 14 trading days, clustered in three periods: 2007,
2010 and 2011.
BTIG/Bloomberg
The Apple warning
According to BTIG, Apple was lower 40 trading days later in all 14 previous observations, with an
average decline of 15.6%. Average returns were already -7.6% after 10 trading days and -12% after 20
days. The observations were clustered in just three historical episodes, so the sample is small.
Apple: Expensive or not?
According to GS's early-November snapshot, Apple traded at around 35x forward earnings versus 19x
for NDX, a historically extreme premium. The FCF comparison tells a different story. Apple's FCF yield
was slightly above NDX's, with the relative spread broadly in line with historical trends.
GS
Peak Apple margins?
Apple's margin story is getting more complicated. GS highlights the possibility that FY26 marked peak
gross margins, with the figure reaching 48.8%. Consensus estimates point to 47.1% in FY27 and 47.4%
in FY28. Services growth is slowing, while higher memory costs and the eventual rollout of AI-related
inference could put additional pressure on margins.
