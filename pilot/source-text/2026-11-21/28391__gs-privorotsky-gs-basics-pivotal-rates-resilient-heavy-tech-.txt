GS Privorotsky - GS Basics Pivotal. Rates Resilient. Heavy Tech
Selling. 19 Nov 2026
Rich Privorotsky  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  Managing Director, Global Banking & Markets, FICC & Equities
19 Nov 2026
Markets: Futures sharply higher driven by NDX. US absorbed plenty of bad news yesterday, but Asia
struggling. TSMC exposed basket down 8%, Asian data center names under pressure. New BIS rules
tighten advanced AI chip licensing across parts of Asia and Brazil, while restricting offshore compute
access for Chinese customers. … delayed projects, displaced demand and excess infrastructure capacity.
Hard to quantify, but clearly was not fully priced in Asia. The offset is positioning… two months of
aggressive selling (see below) have left valuations depressed across global semis. Doesn’t take much good
news to squeeze, and although the BIS implications may not be fully digested in parts of Asia supply
chain, if they can be compartmentalized that's encouraging.
Rates: Hawkish Waller, Fed minutes and ECB regulatory noise, yet bonds rallied. Back end well bid, curve
bull flattening, front end broadly unchanged. Australia particularly interesting… big jobs miss, 2yr
rallying hard and cuts starting to price...now potentially signaling the end of hiking cycle. Market is
increasingly pricing weaker growth as capex expectations come down, commodities roll over and
inflationary pressures appear to be fading. Feels like a lot of parallels to end of 2018. Same late cycle
slowdown, very different policy implications. Rate price action was encouraging and suggests a lot of
policy is already in the price. Internals of equity market very defensive and now commds pointing same
direction.
Commodities: Strong dollar, elevated real rates and attractive all in credit yields are providing competition
for gold/precious. Not a savings glut...it's a spending glut, a less supportive backdrop for precious metals
and multiples with the Fed tightening. China adds another headwind… weak growth, Shanghai equities
near their lows and little sign of recovery. Potential restrictions on AI/data center investment compound
the problem. Copper has become a proxy for that theme, making its resilience increasingly difficult to
reconcile with collapsing AI infrastructure equities. There are nuances around physical inventories,
regional premiums and Chinese stockpiling, but the broader commodity weakness is striking. Precious and
industrial metals, grains, gasoline and diesel all under pressure. A broad disinflationary impulse that could
materially change the rates narrative.
Risk: Today feels important. SPX precariously holding its 200d, street gamma positioning is meant to be
very short but vol was soggy yesterday for the first time in a while (particularly post VIX expiry). With
VIX in the mid 20s and Thanksgiving next week, there is a meaningful potential tailwind if the market can
simply stabilize. Stability can beget stability. I get the issues in AI… there are a myriad of them. But an
awful lot is now reflected in price. If rates can play ball, there is a very real and legitimate bull case for
equities. In hindsight the whole market changed when the Fed changed its posture… and it may well end

the same way. Today: Walmart earnings, claims and Philly Fed, 10yr TIPS auction at 18:00. Watch JPY
approaching 160 and obviously broader tech today.
