Good Morning,
US equities finished in the red marking the 6th consecutive session lower for the  (6% during that stretch): SPX down
-202bps to 7175 (11/11 sectors -ve, VIX +16% to 28.47, Consumer Disc. -3.03%, Communication Svcs. -2.92%),
NDX down -221bps to 26517 (NDX now up 5% YTD), and RTY -218bps to 2655. The AI trade finished lower, reversing
early morning strength, as the GS AI Semis basket finished (GSBSMHX -2.91%), along with the GS Data Center
basket (GSTMTDAT -2.29%).
 
Interesting: Industrials is the second most net sold US sector MTD (behind Info Tech) where net exposure has fallen
to the lowest level in more than two years.  Consumer Staples is among the most net sold US sectors MTD,
suggesting managers have been fading the sector’s price rally.
 
Flow: Asset Mgr and HFs net sold macro products vs net bought pockets of consumer and financials. No sector
screened materially for sale. Liquidity remains poor which is exacerbating price action (S&P top of book avg just $4mm
on the touch).
 
Mutual Funds: 42% of large-cap mutual funds are outperforming their benchmarks YTD vs. the historical average of
37%. Mutual funds were underweight AI-exposed equities ahead of recent poor performance across the AI complex.
Large-cap mutual funds were 722 bp underweight the Mag 7 at the end of Q3 2026, effectively unchanged during the
quarter. High return dispersion and an improvement in market breadth have supported mutual fund returns. Large-cap
mutual funds are most overweight Industrials (+200 bp), followed closely by Health Care (+199 bp, a 10-year high) and
Financials (+196 bp). Basket: largest mutual fund overweights (GSTHMFOW). Link
 
Prime: Global Fundamental L/S managers are down slightly by -0.2% so far in November (+8.9% YTD as of Nov
16th), driven by beta losses partially offset by positive alpha on the back of short side gains. Hedge funds have heavily
net sold global equities so far in November, driven by long-and-short sales (~3 to 1). Single Stocks collectively were
net sold in 7 of the last 8 weeks. Fundamental L/S net leverage has declined by another -7.6 pts MTD to the lowest
level in more than five years (now below Liberation Day lows
 
CTA: Some weakness however in the broader market driven potentially by systematic flow while liquidity is low,
weakness exacerbated at the US open and causing many names to erase earlier gains. Chart below. 
 
Europe HC: We reiterate our Buy ratings on AstraZeneca given its best-in-class mix of growth and pipeline
optionality for which valuation looks increasingly attractive; Bayer on account of an underappreciated Pharma
business and asymmetric risk reward; and Argenx (on CL), Galderma and UCB on account of outsized revenue
growth which we believe will continue to support premium valuations. We remain cautious on Novartis (Sell) as we
believe patent expirations will likely continue to weigh on earnings momentum and the downside risks are not
adequately reflected, with the stock trading at a premium to the sector. Link
 

Var Energy down to N: Our new gas assumptions lead to a cut in our 12m price target to NOK51 from NOK70,
leaving c.7% upside. We continue to like Vår's industry-leading exploration success and short-cycle production
delivery, but we move to Neutral given the longer-term downside risk to TTF prices, while noting upside risk to TTF for
this winter. Link
 
Schindler (Sell / PT CHF250 / 15% Downside): Overall, we think the lack of more precise quantitative margin
guidance (and consensus already close to best-in-class margins), and the lack of an increase in cash to be distributed
to shareholders, has been taken cautiously by the market, especially in the context of Schindler's strong share price
performance relative to the sector in recent months (up 20% in L3M vs sector down 2%). Link
 
FLSmidth:Christian reiterating buy.. now a contrarian stance (all feedback post CMD targets and CFO change has
been resoundingly cautious…but arguably this is reflected in the -20% plunge in the stock).
 
Rational (BUY / PT EUR833 / 35% Upside – CMD takes): Whilst direct monetization mid-term is limited in our view,
and there were no new changes to the financial outlook, we see it as continually strengthening Rational's moat vs
peers and improving customer benefits. Link
