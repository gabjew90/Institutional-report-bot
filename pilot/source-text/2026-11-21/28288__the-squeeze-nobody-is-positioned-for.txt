The puke
Fundamental long/short funds continue cutting exposure. Gross leverage has fallen 4.4 points so far in
November to 206.3%, putting it at the 23rd percentile over the past year. Net leverage has dropped an
even sharper 7.6 points to 41.6%, the lowest level in five years.
GS
Tech liquidation at extremes
Hedge funds continue to aggressively dump global tech. On a trailing six-week basis, selling has been
by far the most aggressive in a decade, with a staggering -4.4 z-score. The global tech long/short ratio
has collapsed from a YTD peak of 2.9 to just 1.79, approaching 10-year lows.

The gamma trap
Nomura estimates aggregate SPX dealer gamma at approximately negative $7.3bn, near the bottom
of its historical range.
Negative gamma can amplify moves in either direction. We have seen the downside impact, but don't
forget that dealer hedging can also accelerate a squeeze higher.
Nomura
Poor liquidity
Don't forget that poor liquidity works both ways. With dealers already sitting on deeply negative
gamma, any squeeze higher could be amplified by thin liquidity.
