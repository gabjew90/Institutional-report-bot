DeskTalk - US equity check: Technically speaking
By Preston Pagli
US equities are under heavy pressure with the SPX down 1.5% and on track for a sixth consecutive losing session, its longest
streak since April 2024. Nvidia's blowout earnings failed to become the clearing event markets had hoped for while sentiment
deteriorated further after another potential "clearing event" in the Anthropic IPO, debuting 34% above issue price before
reversing sharply. 
Beneath the surface the selloff is notably broad with all 11 sectors down and >80% of constituents weaker. Vol is also flashing
red with spot VIX to 28 while the spending the majority of this month above 20. Flows on the cash desk are skewed toward
buying of Healthcare, Industrials and Energy and selling of Info Tech, Financials, Consumer Discretionary, Comm Services,
and Consumer Staples
Technically, SPX has broken below a key support trend line from the Liberation day low (below) at $7,245 (ref $7,218), while
systematic flows have remained a key driver, with all indices "max short" and sellers into strength. SPX (ESZ6) trades roughly
3% away from the trigger to "reduce shorts" at $7,474, while NDX trades 6.5% away. See (CITI DERIVS: CTA Update)
Our equity trading strategy desk outlined in their most recent note, as earnings season comes to a close, the next leg of
idiosyncratic catalysts will feature a number of year-end investor days, with Q4 typically a big quarter for these events.
