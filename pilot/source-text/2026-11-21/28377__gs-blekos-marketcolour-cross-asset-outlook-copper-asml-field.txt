Airtel (I) / GTT & Technip ##

Good Morning,
US equities finished mixed in a choppy trading session: SPX -23bps to 7323 (7/11 sectors +ve, Energy -325bps,
Consumer Discretionary +184bps), NDX+42bps to 27116, and RTY +46bps to 27236. Elsewhere some rotational
dynamics were at play (+ possible "proxy" trade) with Hyperscalers firm (AMZN +3% and GOOG +1%). In Europe, AI
capex names actually hold up pretty well (ASML +2%, STM +2%, IFX +1%), while basically everything else traded
lower. Materials and energy led the way lower, following the global compression of commodity prices.
 
Interesting: GTA 6 – launches today after 13yrs & has already generated more than $1.65 billion in preorder sales.
Some 75% of those sales have been on Sony's PlayStation 5, while the remainder were on Microsoft's Xbox Series S
and X consoles. GTA 5 has remained among the top 20 bestselling titles every single month. Network Traffic Records.
Preloads triggered massive bandwidth spikes, with Comcast reporting it as the largest single network event in its
history and UK provider Virgin Media O2 recording its highest-ever traffic peak.
 
Cross-Asset Outlook (OW SPX, TOPIX, 10Y UST & GILTS & UW USDHY): We remain modestly pro-risk but
favour more balance across and within assets as nominal and earnings growth approach a peak following the AI
capex boom and energy-driven inflation boost in 2026. Near-term risks remain elevated, notably AI concerns, renewed
energy shocks, more aggressive Fed tightening and persistent fiscal concerns. The balance between slowing growth
and rates relief should drive risk appetite in 2027. A peak in nominal growth need not end the bull market, especially if
inflation falls and real growth remains resilient. Easing energy prices and normalising inflation should limit further policy
tightening and allow bond yields to decline gradually. Link
 
Copper: -5% yesterday as a combination of a) global growth concerns (BCOM -2%), b) bearish Chinese demand
feedback from Shanghai CESCO conference, and c) expectations of US exports triggered significant short-selling on
SHFE. Bull counter is: direct AI copper demand won’t drop as capex committed, scrap stays tight for at least two
quarters, mine supply doesn’t rebound in 2027, State Grid tenders are increasing into year end, and the US
administration will be pro-active around trade policy.
 
Central Banks: We expect the Fed to deliver two more 25bp hikes in December 2026 and March 2027 to a peak
policy rate range of 4.25-4.5%, followed by gradual cuts beginning in November 2027 to a terminal rate range of 3.25-
3.5%. We expect the ECB to deliver one more 25bp hike in December to a peak policy rate of 2.75%, before beginning
to normalize back toward a 2.25% terminal rate in 4Q27.
 
ASML Fieldtrip - takes: 1) Strong momentum on High NA and the associated ecosystem, 2) Deep competitive moat
gives sustainable long-term advantage, 3) We see multiple avenues for output optimisation over time, 4) Installed
based management and upgrades business to see strong momentum in our view, and 5) Match Act concerns
overdone, in our view. Link
 

Airtel (Initiation / PT 260p / 15% Upside / 15x Ebitda): We expect Airtel Money to deliver a solid revenue ex-FX
CAGR of c.26% over FY26-31E, driven by rising penetration of Airtel Africa's subscriber base and growing ARPU as
customers move beyond wallet services into higher-value payments and financial services. Link
 
Oil Services / GTT our preferred exposure, Technip down to Neutral: We now identify c.280 mtpa of capacity
reaching FID across 2025-30, a c.52 mtpa or 23% increase from c.228 mtpa in TP2026 earlier this year. GTT is our
preferred exposure in the LNG space: we reiterate Buy with a €210 12m PT, implying 12.4% upside. Technip Energies
also remains well positioned, but following strong share-price performance we remove the shares from the Buy list
primarily on valuation. Link
 
UNICREDIT: Germany reach broad agreement on CBK. 'We have reached common ground and UniCredit has made
clear commitments to Germany as a business location, to the rights of Commerzbank employees and to a continued
constructive dialogue with the works council in the further process,” Klingbeil said in a statement to Reuters.
 
NKT Q3 miss: Op ebitda -4% below consensus, with weaker than expected top-line in Transmission and Distribution.
The weakness is attributed to tough comps (lapping of big projects in both Transmission & Distribution) and various
timing effects, with the guidance maintained but NKT saying they expect to end the year at high end of the range –
implies €~430m vs consensus €424m or a c.1% upgrade.
 
HLMA 1H 5% beat, but photonics light and guidance unchanged... organic growth +12.3% vs consensus +11.5%,
driven by outperformance in Safety whilst E&A (which contains photonics) missed expectations. Photonics grew +30%
in 1H and is still guided to grow “approximately 30%” for the fy – expectations have been coming down but this is still
light.
