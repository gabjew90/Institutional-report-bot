Greetings from California. Rebecca’s travel = volatility again? Hopefully not for long this time...  Overall,
today’s flow Highlights below suggested that we are close to the end of the anticipated weakness ahead of the
Thursday IPO, unless more cyber attacks and outages keep happening that trigger further Retail Anxiety and
Unwind.
One data point captures the current investor mood particularly well: 120+ client DL requests in just the past
two weeks, compared with a normal run rate of 50–80 clients per month. The surge speaks to the unusually
high level of investor anxiety and uncertainty in this market.  This came through clearly in my first day of client
meetings. There were a number of good, recurring questions, so I wanted to share my answers below as a
way to put the recent market moves into perspective.
For additional context, I would also point you to two of my recent nots: “11/13 Market Internal Weekly:
Special Q&A Edition; Amid News-Triggered Weakness, Real-Money Support Strengthened Further”

Rebalancing”
• IPO Historical Analog: In the 11/13 Weekly, I suggested that market could be weighed by IPO this week as
SPX fell -5.8% on average during the three weeks ahead of Major IPO, partially due to cash raised for
purchase. Post-IPO, SPX rallied +9.5% on average the next 3 months, likely because excess cash that wasn’t
allocated was deployed back to the market subsequently. So far this time, SPX -5.2% over the last three
weeks, in-line with history
• “Trend vs Volatility” Reminder as explained in the 10/13 Weekly: Major Trends have been set by the two
largest holders of US Equities – Retail Investors (42%) via both direct trading and self-directed retirement
funds and Foreign Investors (18%) as they own 60% of US Equities togetherVolatility (around the Trendline)
has been dictated by downside feedback loop following headline shocks. This feedback loop was mainly
driven by fast money accounts including Hedge Funds, Systematic Funds (Risk Control, CTA, Risk Parity),
Leveraged ETPs, Short Covering and to some extent, 0dte options
• “Volatility” - Washed-out Positioning suggested Bottoming of Current Local Mini-Trend: In yesterday’s
Market Internal Insight published by Megan, we highlighted the even more washed-out fast money positionings
especially the close to 5-year low UBS PB L/S Net Exposure.  Risk Control, CTA and Leveraged ETP
exposures are also very low @ 2%, 5% and 11%-tile vs 1Y, respectively. This implies even if there is another
external shock, an intraday -2% drawdown doesn’t have the same quant feedback loop to turn into a -4%
drawdown on the same day anymore

Releases create short-term noises most of the time unless it is a meaningful shiftMAYBE – FOMCYES –

Beginning of Covid when people started working from home without knowing when they could returnYES –
When Bitcoin dropped massively that triggered Margin Call and they need to Sell Equities to fund margin
