GS Prime
Nets
Current positioning is typical of slower growth: US L/S Hedge Fund net exposures per MS PB Content
came into today at 42% – that net is at one-year lows and just the 8th %ile since 2010. Typically, Hedge
Fund exposures will track fundamentals: stronger earnings growth = more length, slower earnings
growth = less length. At these levels of net exposures (which will almost certainly be lower after today),
the median historical forecast next twelve-month earnings growth for the S&P 500 is just 5% (data
since 2016). Consensus EPS growth for next year is currently at 16%. In fact, when HF nets have been
near current levels, expected NTM earnings growth was at most 10% historically.
