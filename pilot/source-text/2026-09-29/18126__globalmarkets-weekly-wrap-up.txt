services, supported by strong uptake of Meta’s consumer AI agent

When do equities start to care about rising bond yields???
The US 10-year yield has breached 5.1%, with a 16bp daily rise, 
the second-largest one-day move in two years. For equities, GS 
argues the rate of change matters more than the level. The 
moves that tend to get stocks’ attention are roughly 50bp over a 
month or 30bp over two weeks. We are close, but not there yet: 
the 10-year is up about 35bp over the past month and 25bp over 
two weeks. Still, as GS’s Ben Snider has highlighted, with the S&P 
trading around 19x forward earnings, higher yields are already 
working against valuations in the background.
