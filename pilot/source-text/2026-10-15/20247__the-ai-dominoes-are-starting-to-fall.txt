The tech selloff is spreading
The cracks in the tech trade are getting harder to ignore.
Nasdaq is puking, semis are down 12% from their highs, and small-cap tech is getting hammered.
Meanwhile, South Korea's memory meltdown is spreading, with one of the most important pillars of the
global AI trade now testing critical technical levels.
The big question: is this just another oversold shakeout, or the beginning of a much broader tech
unwind?
NDX
NDX continues to trade inside the "eternal" range. Today's puke has taken the index back to the middle
of the range, where the trend line in place since April and the 100-day moving average also converge.
There is little edge in trading the middle of a range, but today's aggressive price action shouldn't be
dismissed.

Soggy SOX
SOX is down 12% from its latest high on October 6. The index is now well below the 100-day moving
average, but still comfortably above the 200-day.
RSI has reached its most oversold levels since late July. The big line in the sand to watch is 11,200.

Smoked
Small-cap tech is getting properly hammered today, down 5% as of writing. The 200-day moving
average is approaching fast, and the sector has suddenly reached deeply oversold levels.

Boom in Korea
The latest leg lower in South Korea's memory meltdown is starting to spread to the rest of tech.
EWY is testing its 200-day moving average for the first time since the KOSPI bull market began.
Momentum is awful.

The general is falling
KOSPI remains one of the most important pillars of the global AI trade. We have been highlighting the
weakness in this market for the past week.
The worrying part is that Korea's memory meltdown is no longer looking like an isolated problem.
Weakness is now spreading across the broader tech complex. When one of the generals of the AI trade
starts falling, the rest of the army should pay attention.
