October 14th 2026; All references to "we/us/our" refer to the views and observations of the desk.
Day 2 of earnings and the tape continues to be choppy & heavily influenced by shifting concerns relating
to macro & datacenters .. One of those markets where the bulls just can’t catch a break – when AI goes
higher, Fins has often traded lower at a inverse correlation (posted on this yesterday), and when the market
gets worried on the AI capex buildout (and what the funding picture looks like from here), the
fundamental connection between Financials & capital markets & AI gets reestablished..
Rates have round tripped this mornings CPI print and while financials are off the lows – it still doesn’t feel
great and the tape is really moving against those levered to economic growth .. There’s a clear tilt towards
defensive exposure in the market with GS’ Long cyclicals vs defensives basket -6% off early October
levels … Is the rest of the markets’ cyclical exposure catching up to what Financials (& capital markets)
have been saying for a month now?

The sentiment on these earnings reactions has been sharply negative, and people are latching on to
negative datapoints far more intently than positive datapoints (and honestly, this even seems to be
‘narrative piling on to price action’ with us struggling to even find reasons behind the underperformance in
Citigroup yesterday) – All of that said, at this point it's important to emphasize price action is questioning

the level of EARNINGS GROWTH we’re going to see, not credit issues (see Bank P/TBV multiples) .. in
fact, credit quality is close to the bottom of the list on what people are debating about now (pipelines &
cap market backlogs, capital, operating leverage, margin levers)
Feedback on todays’ earnings reports BAC PGR STT MS BLK in order of feedback volume ...
BAC .. -5% (as of send) LINK to Ramsden/GIR first take
• Initial feedback tilted negative, with feedback on the quarter summed up as ‘it’s what they guided to mid
quarter in weaker trading and lack of expense offset, but even worse than what was indicated’ … Stock
was indicated -8% in the pre and we got a better 2027 NII guide (NII story is key to the bulls here), with
the stock failing to rally for a bit which prompted plenty of feedback (with a decent cohort of longer
duration investors) wondering what gives? How can the stock continue to trade -8% lower when NII
numbers need to go higher?
• At the current point, the bulls are emphasizing that the NII-upside story is still in tact and that’s their
primary focus – on the other side, bears are pointing to capital, they are at 10.9% and we have GSIB
pressure coming at start of new year so there’s not much excess capital at the moment … they kept a
strong pace with the buyback in 3Q but going forward may have to slow or pull back on balance sheet
growth (which could be a problem in a world client demand remains strong) – add on to this piece on
‘capital inflexibility’ and bears also argue that recent regulatory focus may limit levers on the expense side
if we move more into a slower revenue environment
PGR … -6% .. LINK to Cox/GIR first take
• Slower growth and weaker margin in Personal Auto in focus .. Plenty of negative feedback coming in
from the insurance crowd on 1) slowdown in Direct (questions around whether agents already playing a
part .. also some pushback to those views arguing any potential agents impact is far too soon or agents
should help PGR on growth given it’s a lower cost option with any potential implications instead likely on
margin) & 2) worsening trends in pricing (agency/direct annualized premium per policy -5.0%/-3.6%) …
Feedback quite loud from the insurance specialist crowd – with pushback calling the move an overaction
to what was simply a worse than expected monthly.
STT .. -6% .. LINK to Blostein
• Top line miss coming from Asset Servicing & other fees while NII & NIM was slightly better … initial
negative feedback on the implied 4Q guide (5% downside to GS est vs buyside expecting upside) –
Ultimately feel bears were emboldened at a squishy top line (even if modest) in the context with the
stock’s outperformance (+35% YTD & premium multiple vs past 5 yrs heading into earnings) – Flows in
trust bank space have really picked up suggestive of real debate in the stocks - stands out as one of the few
pockets of financials that we are seeing defense amidst the current tape.
MS … -3% .. LINK to Ramsden/GIR first take
• PT margin in wealth (30bps miss vs cons) got picked at, but the broader overhang here is mostly macro /
rates / cycle related .. noise in datacenters not helping here, and while the 3Q ‘normalization’ in capital

markets items (IB, Trading) at MS was better than feared – this market is forward looking and discounting
the pace of earnings growth moving forward
BLK .. flat .. LINK to Blostein/GIR first take
• Really good quarter – have gotten next to zero pushback here (desk had crowding at a 8/10) – Bulls
believe 1) the stock’s really underappreciated here relative to the success they’ve been seeing on organic
fee growth (11% this qtr vs bogey in 8-9% range) and margin (adj margin up to 47.8% vs 45.9% last qtr
and 44.5% the qtr before … Blostein est margin ~49% adj for performance fees) and 2) it’s got a deep
moat & it’s business model is immune to disruption ..
