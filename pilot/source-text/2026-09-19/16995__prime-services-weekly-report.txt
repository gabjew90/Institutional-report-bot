Key Takeaways 
◼ 
The GS Equity Fundamental L/S Performance Estimate2 fell -1.02% between 9/11 and 9/17 (vs MSCI World TR +0.35%), driven by alpha of  
-0.95% on the back of long side losses and beta of -0.07%. The GS Equity Systematic L/S Performance Estimate2 rose +1.28% during the 
same period, driven by alpha of +1.21% on the back of short side gains and beta of +0.07%. 
◼ 
Overall book Gross leverage +4.6 pts to 309.2% (65th percentile 1-year) and Net leverage +0.7 pts to 77.5% (34th percentile 1-year).  
Overall book L/S ratio -0.3% to 1.670 (25th percentile 1-year).  Fundamental L/S Gross leverage +2.7 pts to 207.7% (30th percentile 1-year) 
and Net leverage +0.1 pts to 56.4% (65th percentile 1-year).  
◼ 
Global equities were net bought (+0.5 SDs 1-year), as gross trading activity continued to increase driven by long buys outpacing short sales 
(3.9 to 1).  
o 
Regional flows were split, as buying in North America and Europe outpaced selling in Asia (EM + DM).  
o 
Macro Products were net bought, driven by long buys and short covers, while Single Stocks were modestly net sold, as short sales 
slightly outpaced long buys. 
o 
Comm Svcs, Cons Disc, and Industrials were the most $ net bought sectors this week, while Health Care, Energy, and Info Tech were 
the most net sold. 
◼ 
European equities were net bought at the fastest pace in 5 months, driven by short covers and to a lesser extent long buys. 
◼ 
HFs net sold global Energy at the fastest pace in 4 months, driven by a roughly equal number of short and long sales.

European equities were net bought at the fastest pace in 5 months, driven by both short covers and long buys 
◼ 
European equities were net bought at the 
fastest pace in 5 months, driven by short 
covers and to a lesser extent long buys (1.9 to 1).  
◼ 
Germany, France, and Ireland were the most $ 
net bought markets on the week, all driven by 
short covers and long buys, outpacing net selling 
in the UK (long + short sales), the Netherlands 
(long sales), and Italy (short + long sales).  
◼ 
Macro Products (long buys + short covers) and 
Single Stocks (short covers) were both net 
bought and made up 63% and 37% of the total $ 
net buying, respectively. 
◼ 
7 of 11 sectors were net bought, led in $ terms by 
Industrials (short covers), Info Tech (long buys + 
short covers), and Cons Disc (long buys), while 
Energy (short + long sales), Financials (long 
sales), and Staples (long sales) were the most 
net sold. 
◼ 
Gross/Net allocations to Europe (as a % of the 
global Prime Book) now stand at 17.4%/15.8%, 
which rank in the 63rd/14th percentiles vs. the past 
year and in the 58th/3rd percentiles vs. the past 
five years.

◼ 
HFs net sold global Energy at the fastest pace 
in 4 months, driven by a roughly equal number 
of short and long sales. 
◼ 
The sector was net sold in both North America 
and Europe, driven by short and long sales, while 
Asia (EM + DM) saw little net activity.  
◼ 
On a subsector level globally, both Oil, Gas & 
Consumable Fuels and Energy Equip & Svcs 
were net sold on the week. 
◼ 
The Prime book is now over-weight Energy vs. 
the MSCI World Index by +0.4%, which ranks in 
the 91st percentile vs. the past year and in the 
66th percentile vs. the past five years. 
◼ 
Gross/Net allocations to Energy (as a % of the 
global Prime Book) now stand at 4.7%/4.5%, 
which rank in the 77th/92nd percentiles vs. the
