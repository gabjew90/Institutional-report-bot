Good Morning,
US equities ended Thursday higher, as the Nasdaq and S&P 500 both posted their best days since 4-Aug:
SPX +114bps to 7637 (9/11 sectors +ve, Tech +2.20%, Consumer Disc. +1.43%), NDX +173bps to
29,446, RTY +55bps to 2874. Big tech saw strong gains with the GS Quantum Computing index
(GSX1QNT1) +6.69%, and the GS AI Inference Chips (GSXYINFR) +4.46%. In singles, GNRC +18.3%
after announcing a $2.4 billion deal to sell data center generators to Amazon, and FLNC -15.4% due to
downward revision to FY26 revenue. In Europe, Momentum (GSPEMOMO +80ps) continuing to rebound
from earlier this week as we saw a turn back towards Cyclicals (GSPECYDE +50bps).
Interesting: Our PB team puts US fundamental L/S Gross Leverage at 208.8% (31st percentile 1-year, 79th
percentile 5-year), and Net Leverage at 48.8% (5th percentile 1-year, 9th percentile 5-year).
US Equity Strategy – No earnings bubble / 12m PT 8700: While there are indeed factors contributing to
"over-earning" today, our base case is for S&P 500 earnings growth to decelerate, not collapse, in coming
years. We forecast EPS growth of 11% in both 2027 (to $415) and 2028 ($460), driven by solid GDP
growth and a fading tailwind from AI investment that gradually transitions into a growing boost from AI
productivity. Link
Flow: Asset managers finished $396mm better to buy with demand in macro products, tech, and
industrials vs. small supply in consumer discretionary and energy. Hedge Funds finished $658mm better to
buy with demand in tech, consumer discretionary, industrials, and financials vs. supply in macro products
and communication services.
