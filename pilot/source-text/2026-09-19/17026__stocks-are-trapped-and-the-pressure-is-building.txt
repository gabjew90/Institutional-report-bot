Waiting for the break
US equities are sitting at a technical inflection point. NDX is coiling inside a huge triangle, SPX remains
range-bound and Russell is testing major long-term support. With oil driving yields and yields driving
equities, the next move in rates could decide which index breaks first.
The indices are stuck, but the pressure is building.
Trapped
NDX remains trapped inside the huge triangle formation. The major rising trend line has held, and we
are once again above the 100-day MA. Watch closely for a break above the shorter-term falling trend
line. Pressure has continued to build inside this dynamic formation, and any breakout risks becoming
aggressive.
