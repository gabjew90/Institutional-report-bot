Peaks and shovels 
     
   
 
 
Scores on the Doors: oil 76.1%, global stocks 13.1%, US stocks 11.6%, cash 2.6%, US$ 
2.0%, HY bonds 1.6%, gold 0.2%, IG bonds -1.4%, govt bonds -2.4%, bitcoin -12.8% YTD. 
The Price is Right: since June 1st Nasdaq top, Fed hike expectations deflated the AI 
enablers e.g. blue-collar semis (-31% - Chart 3) and independent power producers 
(UTES); bulls rotated to financials (Chart 4). Bad news: “GDP boom” hopes are over, good 
news: Fed clarity, peak in 2yr yields = MAGS flirting with record highs. 
The Biggest Picture: no Fed’s been this terse since 2007 (Chart 2); return of credibility 
and end of “oversharing” are long-term bullish for market resilience, but require higher 
bond risk premiums; hawkish Fed/BoJ/ECB/BoE say the CPI decline “too slow”, FMS 
investors say AI buildout “too fast” (record net 33% say companies overinvesting); 
solution: barring trade peace (Trump-Xi Sept 24th) or oil peace (refiners maxed out), best 
hope for big upside is a peak in hyperscaler capex. 
Tale of the Tape: 3Ps are all peaking; positioning still too bullish per FMS, flows, Bull & 
Bear @ 9.5; especially given profits moderating in 2027… even the 10-15% EPS 
consensus implies ISM sustained somehow >53 (Chart 5); clearly the “run it hot” policy 
posture is done; not yet time for defensives but quality, value, and yield look prudent… 
key Q4 risks are also what’s working now: commodities, credit, China bonds (see p. 2).

energy ETFs. 
Commodities: big risks are in the 3Cs … commodities +45% YTD and outperforming US 
equities past five years, confirming the secular shift to real assets; note dynamic & 
diversified indices outperforming traditional ones… QCITR +296%, SDCITR +205% vs 
BCOMTR +119% past decade (Chart 6… for more details see commodity ETF initiation); 
the broad market risk today is that record $6.40 diesel rhymes with >5% CPI (Chart 7) 
and refineries are already running at max capacity (Chart 8). 
Credit: leveraged loans and HY bonds rallying since the Fed, HY spreads just 30bps from 
record lows… sudden repricing of credit risk would be the most likely signal that Fed 
overestimating GDP; nimble private clients note the last five years AAA CLOs had the 
best returns and smallest drawdown of any fixed income sector, and the best risk-
adjusted returns across major assets save US equities (Chart 9); zero-duration, minimal 
credit-risk, floating-rate assets yielding >4% are rare. 
China: few investors ask about falling GDP, strengthening CNY, or deflationary exports; 
“China Shock 2.0” in Europe visible in record German trade deficit, falling German 
industrial production on pace to reach 2010 levels soon; note China the only major 
economy with falling bond yields in 2026, best-performing bonds since Mar’20.
