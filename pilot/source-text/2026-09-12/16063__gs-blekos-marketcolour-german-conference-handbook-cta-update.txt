Europe Food: Previously, we expected a cumulative -22% decline in raw material costs for manufacturers
over 2026-27; now we forecast -9%. This moderation could limit industry price reductions, in our view,
despite sustained muted consumer demand in key regions, which could delay a volume recovery. Sell
Lindt - wider price premium to the market vs history exposes it to greater volume pressure than peers, in
our view, and we expect price reductions to weigh on pricing in 2027, leading to +2.2% organic growth.
