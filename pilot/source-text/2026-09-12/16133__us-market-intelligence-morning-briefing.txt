pricing a ~63% probability of a 25bp hike at the Sep 16 FOMC meeting. This print should

hotter print that spikes Oct or Dec rate hike expectations, which are currently ~27% and

2.5%. All those numbers are under the new methodology that the BEA will begin using with 
the August PCE. We will refine that forecast following tomorrow’s CPI report.

US MKT INTEL VIEW 
We maintain our Tactically Cautious / Neutral view ahead of Friday’s CPI report. The 
near-term setup remains binary: a dovish print could ease the pressure from yields and 
rates volatility, allowing investors to refocus on resilient consumer and corporate earnings 
growth, while a hawkish print shall keep the September Fed meeting live with rising bond 
vol pressuring stocks. Beyond these immediate catalysts, our base case remains 
constructive in absence of renewed inflation/bond yield spikes. We think it is prudent to 
remain neutral/cautious ahead of the CPI and Fed hurdles. 
  
•         MONETIZATION MENU (Unchanged WoW; AUG 31) – We start with a +NDX vs. -
RTY position. Within Tech, we like Mag7 as the favored near-term play given the inability for 
AI / Memory / Semis to sustain a bid. Software remains attractive especially if Semis 
continue to fall. Generally, we think that SMID-caps are likely to underperform as part of this 
tactical call. Parts of Cyclicals can perform especially if we see stronger than expected ISM 
prints this week. Here, we like Large-Cap Banks given the economic growth story 
accompanied by loose financial conditions and Consumer Discretionary if energy prices 
move lower. Within Defensives, Healthcare tends to be the best performing sector into 
midterms. We do not have a strong view on Cyclical vs. Defensive outperformance over the 
next two weeks. As hedges we like shorting Credit ETFs and buying VIX products. Until we 
get through the Fed Meeting on Sep 16, the Dollar Debasement trade may struggle, so 
makes sense to switch cash positions into derivative positions. 
•         OLD MONETIZATION MENU (AUG 24) – We like the barbell approach but with eye 
towards Momentum having some more downside risk we think it prudent to play the 
broadening theme. Within our barbell approach, that would spreading risk from 
AI/Memory/Semis to include Mag7/Software. Within Cyclicals, spreading risk from AI-related 
Cyclicals to include Financials, Energy, and Metals/Miners. These moves will benefit from 
further broadening and take advantage of the ‘Dollar Debasement’ trade which may extend 
in the near-term. Regardless, Precious Metals remain attractive. For those concerned on

ROADMAP TO 26Q4 
As we wind down the quarter, we wanted to share some of the catalytic events as we 
approach Q4. So far, midterm election seasonality has failed to materialize; and, at this 
point, it seems unlikely to do so with the dominance of A, an unresolved conflict in the 
Middle East, and the potential for 26Q3 earnings season (which spans the election) to 
deliver another quarter of more than 20% EPS growth with widening margins. 
If we are wrong on midterm seasonality, buy Healthcare, which is typically the best 
performing sector into midterms. Then, size up on Election Day as the post-election setup is 
positive.  
  
Since 1990, the SPX rallied +5.4%, +11.4%, +12.5%, and +14.7% at 3 month (78% hit 
rate), 6 month (100% hit rate), 9 month (100% hit rate), and 12 month (100% hit rate) tenors 
after midterm election days. If you go back 1950,  then the returns at the 6-, 9-, and 12-
month marks maintain their 100% hit rate for positive returns and the 3-month hit rate 
increases to 89%.
