Peak Rates, Trough Semis: The Pain 
Needed to Reset the AI Cycle 
Industry Overview 
   
 
 
$361bn cash burn puts rates at the center of the AI debate 
We increasingly see peak rates as a necessary condition for a durable SOX trough, as 
rates now affect AI demand, not just semi multiples. CSP cash burn is deteriorating from 
~$140bn in CY26 to $361bn in CY27 per cons. With the 10-yr. near 5% and additional 
Fed tightening possible, we think the SOX is increasingly discounting a question cons. 
ests. are yet to answer: who funds an incremental dollar of AI capex, and at what return? 
Higher rates could break capex, but may be necessary 
The same rate pressure hurting semis today could ultimately help create the bottom. US 
cloud capex is running ~$1.2Tn annualized vs. ~$1.5tn+ expected in CY27. Holding spend 
near today's run-rate implies ~20% CY27 capex haircut (~30GW vs. ~40GW expected) 
and up to 35-40% downside to AI semi EPS. Higher rates raise WACC, weaken marginal 
project IRRs + make external financing more expensive, potentially forcing lower-return 
projects out of buildout. N-t capex reset would hurt, but could make the cycle healthier: 
less speculative capacity, more disciplined spending, an under writable earnings base. 
Don't wait for semis to ring the alarm 
We don't expect chip companies to call the turn first. Semi suppliers are downstream of 
their customers' capital allocation: customers reset budgets → orders/backlogs adjust → 
semi ests. get cut. Waiting for NVDA/AVGO or broader semis to guide cautiously before 
customers change spending is likely waiting too long. Our historical work shows the SOX 
typically bottoms 2-6 mos. before fundas and subsequently rallies ~42% on avg. as est. 
cuts become understood. The signal we want is therefore not higher earnings ests. It is 
lower capex/EPS ests. that stop producing lower stock prices. Peak rates can establish 
the multiple floor; quantified capex and EPS cuts can establish the earnings floor. 
Watch customers first, then TSMC for the CY28 signal 
1) Watch early-CY27 CSP capex/FCF guidance to see whether financing pressure is 
changing spending behavior, 2) watch TSMC's CY27 capex: as one of the industry's more 
disciplined capacity planners, CY27 investment made increasingly serves CY28 demand. 
Lower CY27 CSP capex expectations + resilient TSMC capacity investment could equal 
n-t excess expectations are being cleared while supply chain still sees durable CY28 
demand. History rhymes: in both 2018 and 2022 SOX downturns, equities anticipated 
the eventual fundamental improvement vs. waiting for reported earnings to trough. 
Defend n-t; add AI beta when bad news stops working 
Until we see (1) peak-rate visibility, (2) hyperscaler capex resetting, (3) semi EPS cuts 
becoming quantifiable and (4) stocks holding despite those cuts, we favor lower direct 
AI-capex beta, incl. TXN/ADI and selectively CDNS/SNPS, recognizing EDA's premium 
multiples retain duration/rate sensitivity. Once those signals converge, we would rotate 
first toward quality leaders NVDA/AVGO, then higher-β connectivity/optics and semicap 
as capex visibility improves. We are not waiting for the news to get better. We are 
waiting for peak rates and bad news to stop making semi stocks worse. That, more than 
the eventual Fed cut or earnings trough, could mark the start of the next SOX upcycle.
