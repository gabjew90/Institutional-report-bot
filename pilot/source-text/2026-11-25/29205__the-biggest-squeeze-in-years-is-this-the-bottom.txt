From panic to euphoria
What a market. NDX is staging one of its most violent upside reversals in years, SOX is surging 14%,
and the AI complex is squeezing hard.
Moves of this magnitude have historically occurred near major market lows. But despite the monster
rally, important technical resistance remains overhead, while positioning suggests there could be
more room to run.
From downside to upside panic
NDX printed its biggest down candle in a long time on Friday. Today, we are seeing the biggest up
candle in a long time. Despite futures surging 7% as of writing, the descending trendline and the 21-
day moving average remain overhead. As we have been saying for some weeks now, welcome to
extreme markets.

Pivotal moves
Callahan highlights that NDX single-day rallies of more than 5% have been extremely rare over the
past 15+ years. Previous episodes include April 2025, November 2022, March 2020 and December
2018. Interestingly, all occurred at or near major market lows.

Beta explosion
What a reversal. Following one of the most painful weeks for hedge funds this year, GS's sector,
industry and factor-neutral Beta Index is having its second-best day in more than a decade, surpassed
only by Liberation Day. The AI complex, retail favorites and former momentum winners are all surging.
GS
The momentum disconnect
NVDA is lifting the AI trade after a brutal week, but something unusual is happening beneath the
surface. According to GS, 12-month momentum is outperforming both 3-month and 6-month
momentum by the largest spread on record.
Momentum correlations are also breaking down, with the correlation between 12-month and 3-month
momentum turning negative, while the 12-month versus 6-month correlation has fallen to a two-year
low.

GS
Room to rebuild
Momentum and AI have traded almost in lockstep this year, but hedge fund exposure has been cut
aggressively. According to GS Prime Brokerage, medium-term momentum exposure now sits at just the
20th percentile over five years, while market sensitivity is at the 22nd percentile over the past year.
GS sees room for the AI complex to recover further if the rebound holds and concerns over AI capex
and returns on investment ease.
