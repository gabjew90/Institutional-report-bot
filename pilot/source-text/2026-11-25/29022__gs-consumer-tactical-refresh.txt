GS Consumer: Tactical Refresh
Scott Feiler  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  Managing Director, Global Banking & Markets, FICC & Equities
Mon 23 Nov 2026, 6:17am ET
Consumer Health This Week. It won’t be a one-size fits all approach to understanding consumer health,
but here are some very quick thoughts. If you want to listen to our views on the tactical set-up in
consumer,
• We think you will get generally positive reads this week, including likely upside out of ANF, BBY,
DLTR, DKS & SJM.
• The hill to climb: We think BURL is the stock with the biggest nerves around it (vs Consensus), but
worth noting TJX acted well on EPS day last week (+8%) on a somewhat mixed print and ROST was only
-2.9% on comp 200 bps below more elevated investor expectations.
• Glass half full? Last week, beats were rewarded and misses acted pretty decent. PB data showed very
heavy shorting in Discretionary names last week, but performance from reporters on T+0 was pretty
impressive, even if the rest of Retail lagged (-5% on the week).
• The misses (acting better) really stood out. ARMK was +3% on a miss, VIK +10% over a 2-day period
on a miss, TGT +4% on a miss vs the bogey, TJX +8% on a gross margin miss, GAP +11% on a cut &
ROST just -2.9% on a miss vs some of the most elevated whispers out there.
• Tidbit that stood out last week: GAP noted the early-November slowdown was materially weather-driven
and that business returned to trend once temperatures cooled. That stock was +11% in response.
While Staples are near all-time highs (+3% the last 1 month and +19% YTD), investors are not embracing
the defensive nature of the sector. Instead, we continue to see selling and would expect underperformance
today. The price action in Staples remains among the largest topics/inbounds in consumer. Feedback from
consumer dedicated investors has been that the move higher in Staples stocks has been more challenging
for investor performance than the move lower in discretionary longs. It is obviously mostly driven by
factor rotations/positioning dynamics in the market, along with some small help from a better HPC EPS
season. What is interesting is our GS PB data shows investors continue to try and press the group, with no
major interest in hiding out here on the long side. The selling in other sectors (Fins, Real Estate &
Discretionary) was more than Staples on the week, but the 20D Staples selling still leads all sectors.
Top-Down View on GS PB Flows. Heavy Shorting. Extreme Levels.

GS PB shows US-listed ETF short flows increased +11.1% last week and the largest % shorting since
March and 3rd largest in the past five years – led by shorting in both Large Cap & Small Cap Equity,
Financials, and Credit ETFs. US Single Stocks collectively were shorted every day on the week and saw
the largest weekly shorting activity since Jun ’22 (third largest in the past decade).

Source: Goldman Sachs FICC & Equities, Marquee, as of 23 Nov 2026.
