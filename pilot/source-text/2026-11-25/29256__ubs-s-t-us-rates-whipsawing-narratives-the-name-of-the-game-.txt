US Rates: Whipsawing Narratives The
Name Of The Game
Swati Raghupathy
22-Nov-2026, 20:25 ET (EST)
Just 1.5 weeks ago, Governor Waller’s comment on a 50bp hike, alongside the hot CPI and payroll prints, led
to a 25-30bp sell-off in rates, but the market has retraced most of that move last week as the equity/AI story
gets rattled. Has much fundamentally changed, though?
Speaking to equity investors, the move appears more technical, and plenty of interest to buy the dip exists, but
it feels like catching a falling knife at the moment. There is also the risk of headlines around President Trump
considering new Iran strikes as the US military warns of strain on forces.
Flows in rates: January vs. March FOMC and December vs. January steepeners were popular after Governor
Waller’s comments, and these are seeing exits in this move. The STRIP is down 15-20bp Friday alone, with
Z6Z7 sharply flatter by ~20bp last week. Questions are being asked about the threshold levels of an equity
move that could put the Fed on hold in December. The desk thinks the move is a bit overdone, with core PCE
and GDP pointing to strength, so it feels like the market could see a retracement.
USD spreads positioning is long on the Street and has been called a trade that keeps on giving. The desk has
finally seen trimming of long spreads this week from hedge funds, while real money has been more on the
sidelines in the belly. Additionally, receiving in swaps from foreign accounts is adding to the pressure. CTAs
had added short duration above 5% in 10s, and with 10s back to the 4.85-4.95% area, they are probably
covering too.
Vol: The Street has been trading with a long gamma bias, which has served well. The top left is up 13-15nv,
while deep vega is down, with 10y10y down 0.8nv and 10y20y down 1nv. Note that spot-vol correlations in the
top left and right are the opposite. This makes sense, as deep vega is driven mainly by exotic desks getting
short into the rates sell-off and needing to buy, while vega is already quite rich and gamma skew is now
inverted. During a rally in the underlying, the price action therefore makes sense. Customer flows have been
two-way in gamma and calendars, including 3m5y and 10y tails, with buying also seen in 1y30y vs. selling the
3m line.
