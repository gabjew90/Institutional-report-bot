Jensen is Back as CEO
  
Bottom Line:
Jensen Huang returns as Nvidia CEO and President. The board voted to reinstate Mr. 
Huang, who resigned two weeks ago. His return will be effective immediately.
 
Key Points
 
Monday morning, NVDA announced the reinstatement of Jensen Huang as President 
and CEO effective immediately. The board voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang, 
reaffirming confidence in his ability to lead NVDA as the company navigates the 
ongoing AI revolution and potentially faces complex government probes. In our view, 
his return represents the best outcome for the company/stock, as we expect investors 
will welcome the return of NVDA’s long-tenured founder. Mr. Huang is an AI visionary 
with deep political connections and has long-standing relationships across the semi 
supply chain. NVDA offers the industry’s comprehensive full stack AI platform, now with 
Vera Rubin ramping. We reiterate our Outperform rating and $240 price target.

Company Description
NVIDIA is a technology leader in accelerated computing and 
AI. NVDA started as a supplier in GPUs for graphics/gaming. 
NVDA is now a leading full-stack AI technology supplier. 
Data center technology includes GPU, CPU, DPU, switching, 
and CUDA software. Industry-leading hardware, software, 
and networking solutions enable AI training and inference.
