In an unexpected turn of events, NVDA announced Jensen has been reinstated as CEO of NVDA. It was noted that
the board received input from senior levels of the U.S. govt. that Jensen's reinstatement poses no national security
risk. While, we view this announcement as a significantly positive development for NVDA, we remain SW rated, as
we believe there's still uncertainty regarding the potential for add'l developments that may be uncovered as part
of the board's investigation, as well as the SEC investigation into accounting issues. We await further clarification
before revisiting our rating.
NVDA reinstates Jensen Huang as President and CEO following unanimous board vote. NVDA this morning announced that
Jensen Huang has been reinstated as President and CEO and as a member of the board, effective immediately, following a unanimous
board vote after discussions with Huang and input from senior levels of the U.S. government. The board said it was satisfied with
Huang's candor and ability to lead NVDA and is confident his reinstatement poses no national security risk. Colette Kress, who had
served as interim CEO, will return to her continuing role as CFO, while Harvey Jones will remain the board's chair. The independent
board committee investigating reports that Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort will continue its investigation, with Huang
committed to cooperating. Separately, NVDA recently formed an independent National Security and Export Control Committee to
oversee trade compliance and national security risks and is commencing a search for a new Chief Compliance Officer, while continuing
to cooperate with ongoing government investigations and inquiries.

Valuation

We remain Sector Weight and see a fair value of $160, based on 9x our FY28 EPS estimate of $16.99.

uncertainty remains regarding the potential for additional developments that may be uncovered as part of the
board's investigation, as well as the SEC investigation into accounting issues. We await further clarification
before revisiting our rating.
