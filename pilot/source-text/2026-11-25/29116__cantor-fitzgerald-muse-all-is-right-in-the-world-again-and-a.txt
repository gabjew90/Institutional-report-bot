Company Update
All is Right in the World Again (and a Very Happy Day)
This morning, pre-market open, NVDA's BOD unanimously agreed to
reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO, President, and Board Director. If you were
on a desert island for the last 2 weeks, this would come as no surprise. But
for those who were not, what a wild and crazy couple of weeks.
First and foremost, we continue to believe this is what is best for NVDA and
the United States. As highlighted by the press release, NVDA's BOD received
input from senior levels of the US Gov't that Jensen's reinstatement poses
no national security risk. And for NVDA, we think worries about talent
retention, future product roadmap, market share and margins all go out the
window. We continue to expect NVDA to lead the United States in AI with
again, the most optimistic and inspirational stance on the technology. As for
potential changes at NVDA - we highlight the hiring of a Chief Compliance
Officer. We also wouldn't be surprised if the company were to install a more
established Chairman (and perhaps review entire BOD) as well as have the
new National Security and Export Control Committee report directly to the
Chairman.
For the broader AI market, we believe this is a win for innovation,
confidence in the necessary spending (and support for spending) on
Compute for Super Intelligence, and the United States continuing to lead
globally. We cannot overstate how critical we feel this reinstatement is for
the U.S. standing globally, as well as near-term, incremental confidence in
the durability of today's AI infrastructure spending outlook.
Specific to AI, we view this as an incremental win for open-source models
vs. closed-sourced. We continue to expect NVDA to close on the Hugging
Face acquisition, and believe that a strong NVDA reduces the risk of
regulatory capture at the frontier-model level and is therefore a win for the
democratization of AI in the United States and for the entire world.
Based on the above, we reiterate our Overweight rating and $250 price
target, and adding NVDA back to our TOP PICKS.
