*Sales desk commentary. Not a product of UBS Research. For institutional investors only.*
• United States: The US equity rout continues with indices lower by 200bps with the NDX trading below its
200d moving average now for six consecutive days. Some descriptive statistics / charts highlight the current
status of the drawdown. Figure #1 highlights that if the NDX trades below 200d, it is typically short-lived
outside of recessionary periods. Figure #2 highlights that current drawdown from 200d is ~6%, which ranks
slightly more than -1 standard deviation relative to the past 20yrs. Figure #3 highlights that the current
drawdown from 52-week high is ~15%. With that said, the US 1y1y receiver outlined in FX Divergence is
performing today as US rates are well-bid across the curve (figure #4). 
• Eurozone: French data registered a relatively low reading across most confidence metrics e.g. business
confidence coming in at 95 (vs. 96 estimates and 97 prior). Figure #1 aggregates composite business
confidence, manufacturing industry sentiment, and consumer confidence overall, which is mostly dragged
lower by the third variable. Despite underwhelming data and news of a deteriorating budget, EUR has
maintained its new range of approximately 1.10 with limited downside pressure.
