EU Energy - Freight Costs - we are now starting to see a moderation, led by the Atlantic basin, where
freight on the main routes has fallen by c.60-70% from that peak while the Hormuz-China leg remains
more elevated at c.$30/bbl (-24%). As Atlantic freight normalises and the tightness in the North Sea
physical market unwinds, the freight advantage of North Sea barrels into Europe has narrowed to c.$3-
5/bbl from c.$13-17/bbl at the peak, easing the headwind for the Atlantic-exposed producers like Buy-
rated Galp, and narrowing the tailwind for the Norwegian producers.
