"AI Banker" is Back, Jensen Huang Reinstated as CEO and the AI
Cycle is BACK!
NVDA this morning announced that former CEO Jensen Huang would return to helm the
company and resume his position on the board. Key points: 1) NVDA board has reinstated
Jensen Huang as CEO of NVDA, while Colette Kress will resume her role as NVDA CFO,
2) we also note headlines (LINK) stating USG does not possess evidence that Jensen
engaged in unlawful conduct, 3) NVDA to continue with internal and govt investigations
with Harvey Jones set to remain as board chair, and 4) the BoD noting input from senior
USG officials and confident Jensen Huang does not pose any national security risks.
We believe reinstatement of CEO Jensen Huang RETURNS Investor CONFIDENCE in
the AI trade, driving upside for memory, optical and analog suppliers that have seen
SIGNIFICANT multiple compression over the last few weeks. NVDA is trading ~10x NTM
P/E vs AMD at 28x and SOX at ~12x.
Key Points
Jensen Huang reinstated as CEO of NVDA, with Colette Kress as CFO. NVDA board
announced that it will unanimously bring back Jensen Huang as its CEO (Link) after
receiving input from "senior levels of the United States government" and a reinstatement
petition receiving 200K+ signatures (Link). Interim CEO Colette Kress will resume her
role as CFO. Additionally, we note NVDA plans to hire a Chief Compliance Officer and
establish a National Security and Export Control Committee. The BoD, which will continue
to be chaired by Harvey Jones, also noted that reinstatement is in the best interests of
NVDA stockholders, employees, partners and customers, with input from US govt officials
confident that Jensen not a national security risk. This follows growing reports of US
intelligence not possessing evidence that Alice Huang is a Chinese spy.
Renewed confidence in the AI cycle - news positive for the supply chain, with
memory, optical, and analog and neoclouds. We believe Jensen Huang is considered
an aggressive AI dealmaker, advancing the technology frontier and intercompany
cooperation incl. cloud financing/backstopping. We see his reinstatement as a major
positive sign for the computing market, potentially driving upside for NVDA's supply chain
across the memory, optical, and analog markets. We also note potentially stronger
tailwinds for the neoclouds with more generous financing/backstopping back on the
table.
Continue to see NVDA spearheading the AI roadmap, while peers could see some
competitive challenges. With Jensen back as CEO, we believe lower chance of the
negative impacts outlined in our scenario analysis (share loss, ASP/GM headwinds,
China revs), see UPSIDE - NVDA is trading ~10x NTM P/E vs AMD at 28x and SOX at
~12x. - and we believe could see NVDA continue to spearhead the AI cycle and technology
roadmap while lowering competitive risk from others like AMD (Outperform).

Price Target Calculation and Key Risks
Investment Thesis
We see NVDA remaining the leader in the AI training and inference chips for Data Center applications, but see NT risks from the US govt
investigations, leadership challenges, and roadmap execution. We continue to see NVDA executing its roadmap towards >$1T Blackwell
+Rubin revs C25-27E and into a >$3-4T TAM by 2030E as $25-45B/GW top-line opportunity, with strong Blackwell ramps in 2026E, Vera
Rubin and its Groq LPU now in production, with >30% ASP tailwinds on VR200 NVL72 racks. Additionally, we note upside from CPU
ramps with Agentic AI as NVDA sees F27E CPU revs at ~$20B with F28E potentially up >100% y/y. We see Gaming (we estimate >75%
PC share) are expected to remain healthy and stable, while NVDA’s automotive ADAS pipeline remains strong.
Base Case/PT
NVDA remains well-positioned in the machine learning, deep learning, and AI markets. Our $220 PT reflects ~13x our F28E EPS, below
its historical range ~15-30x while remaining the unquestioned market share leader in merchant AI GPU shipments.
Bull Case/PT
NVDA has significant market-leading opportunities in the AI/DC, Gaming, and Automotive markets; we see a bull case PT of $325, ~16x
our upside F28E EPS of $20.43.
Bear Case/PT
If NVDA is unable to maintain strong growth in key markets as well as fails to continue to capitalize on AI opportunities, we see a bear
case PT of $120, at ~8x our downside F28E EPS of $14.80.
Risks
Escalations of export restrictions to China and other geopolitical tensions (Taiwan) or a significant pullback in AI Server spending could
be major headwinds. We would also note limited CoWoS capacity at TSMC (2330 TT, covered by Kevin Wang Mizuho HK, Buy), and other
suppliers, potential for FTC lawsuits, and growing competition both domestically and in China, where shipments have yet to resume
following re-approval of shipments from the US govt.
