◆ Jensen Huang reinstated as President, CEO, and Board 
Director; US government input in the decision 
NVIDIA Corp (NVDA US, USD125.53, Hold, TP USD170.00) 
(Priced as of 20 Nov 2026) 
Jensen Huang reinstated effective immediately: After announcing the CEO 
transition on 12 November (see our note, CEO transition announced as Jensen 
Huang resigns, 12 Nov), NVIDIA today announced that effective immediately, Jensen 
Huang has been reinstated as President and Chief Executive Officer and as a 
member of the Board.  
A sharp U-turn from the Board: According to their statement, the board of directors 
voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang following a series of discussions and 
meetings with him, after which they were satisfied in his ability to lead NVIDIA. 
According to the Chairman of the Board, Jensen Huang’s reinstatement is in the best 
interest of the stockholders, employees, partners, and customers. Interestingly, at 
their 3QFY27 earnings conference call on 17 Nov, the Chair of the Board had 
categorically stated that the board did not intend to reinstate Jensen Huang as the 
CEO (see our note, NVIDIA: As good as it gets given CEO uncertainty, 18 Nov). 
US government’s input: NVIDIA’s Board received input from senior government 
officials, which gave it confidence that his return to the position did not present a 
national security risk. Recently, US President Donald Trump had also publicly 
supported Jensen Huang’s reinstatement as CEO (Bloomberg, 16 November), saying 
that he could return as a big group of people would like him to return. 
Market reaction: The stock is trading +c28.7% (vs NASDAQ +6.0%) at 9:44 AM ET
