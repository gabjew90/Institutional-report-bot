Don't Call It a Comeback; NVIDIA Reinstates Jensen
Overweight

Our Call
The NVIDIA BoD voted to unanimously reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO. We see this as 
a major, unexpected clearing event, though expect some level of overhang to persist as 
national security & export controls investigations unfold.
 
Initial Thoughts
We see Mr. Huang's reinstatement largely answering multiple investor questions / 
concerns (neocloud / broader AI ecosystem financing support, supply chain 'handshake'
agreements, long-term company vision, etc.). We also note the Board received input 
on the decision from senior levels of the U.S. Government, and based on that input, is 
confident that Mr. Huang's reinstatement poses no national security risk—increasing our 
confidence in a positive outcome of the Board's investigation.
 
President Trump stated that "The Attorney General of the United States has confirmed 
that the U.S. Government does not possess evidence that [Jensen Huang] has engaged in 
any unlawful conduct."
 
NVIDIA also announced that it is commencing a search to fill a new Chief Compliance 
Officer position, to oversee compliance across NVIDIA and report to the Nominating and 
Corporate Governance Committee.

Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks
Nvidia Corporation (NVDA)
Investment Thesis
Our Overweight rating is based on our positive stance on NVIDIA’s competitive positioning in gaming GPUs and expanding growth 
opportunities in data center, HPC, and emerging / expanding AI opportunities (autonomous vehicles, healthcare, robotics, etc.). We see 
sustainability in NVIDIA's CUDA software platform differentiation, as well as the company's continually deepening system software 
capabilities. We see NVIDIA as one of the most attractive secular growth stories in large-cap semis.
Target Price Valuation for NVDA
Our $250 price target is ~11x P/E and 9x EV/EBITDA on our C2028E. While we think NVIDIA should trade at least inline w/ a SOX P/E, our 
valuation reflects increased risk related to CEO transition & China export restrictions / investigations.
Risks to Our Price Target and Rating for NVDA
Risks include: (1) increased competition in the cloud data center, high-performance computing, and PC gaming markets (2) the continued 
development of new markets, including artificial intelligence/machine learning and autonomous driving, (3) delays in product introduction 
due to use of third-party process technology, component availability, etc., (4) search for a permanent CEO, and (5) export restrictions.
