NVIDIA Corporation
BoD Reinstates Jensen
NASDAQ: NVDA | USD 165.95 | Outperform | Price Target USD 230.00
Sentiment: Positive
Before the open on Monday (11/23) NVDA announced that Jensen Huang has been reinstated as President, CEO, and Board
member, effective immediately, while Colette Kress will continue serving as CFO (after serving as interim CEO). An independent
board committee investigation around foreign intelligence efforts targeting Mr. Huang is ongoing and will proceed to conclusion,
with Mr. Huang's full support. NVIDIA is also searching for a new Chief Compliance Officer to oversee compliance across the
company and report to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee.
We maintained our positive stance on the stock despite Mr. Huang's departure due to our view that: 1) demand is significantly
outpacing supply; 2) product roadmap is set for next 3 years; and 3) industry supply tightness should limit NT competitive risk.
We believe Mr. Huang's reinstatement makes the bull case stronger by alleviating near-term concerns around talent retention,
customer relations, and NVDA's support for customer financing and neocloud guarantees. While we expect external and internal
investigations into export restrictions and other issues to continue, the administration's public support for Mr. Huang (through a
Truth Social posting this morning) should significantly reduce the keyman risk if not completely eliminate it, in our view.
NVDA's business fundamentals remain strong. Vera Rubin ramps are off to a strong start and management recently raised the
FY28 revenue outlook to ~75% growth vs. ~70% previously (on a lower base), despite some impact from new export restrictions.
Supply purchase commitments increased significantly, highlighting strong visibility and demand conviction. NVDA has about $98B
of cash, and we are modeling ~$400B of FCF over the next 5 quarters, which should be enough to support supply commitments,
aggressive cash returns ($227B remaining on buyback authorization), and selective customer financing and neocloud guarantees.
At 10x CY27E EPS, we believe valuation is compelling (~60% discount to Mag7). Reiterate Outperform and $230 PT.
