Major U.S. stock indexes finished the week sharply lower, with the 
S&P 500 Index posting its worst week since April 2025, as 
concerns surrounding AI-related investments and expectations for 
tighter Federal Reserve monetary policy weighed on investor 
sentiment. The Nasdaq Composite fared worst, falling 10.02%, 
while the S&P MidCap 400 Index held up best but still dropped 
6.99%. Within the S&P 500, all 11 sectors declined, with consumer 
staples and utilities holding up best. Tech and AI-related market 
segments faced sustained selling pressure throughout the week 
amid concerns about 
elevated valuations, financing for 
infrastructure investments, and tighter restrictions on advanced 
semiconductor exports. Shares of NVIDIA finished the week 
sharply lower as positive earnings results failed to alleviate 
broader concerns surrounding AI-related investments and 
uncertainty over the company's leadership following the 
departure of former CEO Jensen Huang. The highly anticipated 
IPO of AI lab Anthropic, which raised a record USD 81 billion, did 
little to mitigate fears around the AI trade. Meanwhile, Fed 
minutes reinforced expectations for further tightening. US 
Treasuries yields declined across the curve amid equity market 
weakness. Pan-European STOXX Europe 600 Index fell 5.80% in 
local currency terms, its steepest weekly decline since March. The 
the Nikkei 225 index dropped -7.7%. Gold, silver and cryptos 
tumbled while the dollar was bid. 
AI & monetary policy worries weigh on market sentiment

the US 3-2-1 crack spread was $65.56 a barrel. Today it’s $41.90,

roughly 9 basis points off headline CPI, all else equal.
