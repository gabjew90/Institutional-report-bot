Sell low, buy high
What a market. NVDA exploded 30%, SOX surged 14%, and the AI complex staged one of the most
violent reversals in years.
The extraordinary moves come after hedge funds slashed exposure, semiconductor valuations
collapsed, and positioning reached historic extremes.
Here are the charts that matter after today's massive squeeze.
Pivotal moves
Callahan highlights that NDX single-day rallies of more than 5% have been extremely rare over the
past 15+ years. Previous episodes include April 2025, November 2022, March 2020 and December
2018. Interestingly, all occurred at or near major market lows. More here.

Prices vs profits
Also before the great squeeze kicked in:
The semiconductor crash has been driven primarily by multiple compression rather than collapsing

Prices have collapsed. Earnings haven't.
Bloomberg/Authers
The great hedge fund unwind
According to GS PB, hedge funds have been aggressively cutting risk, with US single-stock shorting
reaching its highest weekly level since June 2022 and the third highest in a decade. ETF shorts surged
11% in a single week, one of the largest increases in five years. Importantly, the data only runs through
Thursday and does not capture Friday's selling.

1st percentile
Fundamental L/S net leverage has dropped to 42.3%, the 1st percentile over the past year. Beta-

five-year low. More here and here.
