US WEEKLY KICKSTART - Three conditions to support an S&P 500 correction bottom
We are watching for one of three conditions that would support an equity market bottom and recovery.
One catalyst would be news that increases investor confidence in the trajectory of future earnings
associated with AI investment and monetization.
Another potential catalyst would be an improvement in the macro backdrop, such as a dovish signal for
Fed policy.
Third would be a major decline in investor positioning, such as has often marked the bottoms of past
drawdowns.
HEDGE FUND TREND MONITOR - Bruised and broadening
Hedge funds have struggled amid the recent surge in equity market volatility.
As the AI trade has unwound, hedge funds have broadened their equity exposures.
GOOGL and QCOM ranked as "Falling stars." But a majority of this quarter's "Rising stars" were non-AI
stocks, including ALL, HD, IQV, and MRK.

Interparfums SA (IPAR.PA) - Growth inflection priced in; down to Neutral
We believe the upside is now largely priced in. Since our initiation at Buy on 24 November 2025, the
shares have risen by 65% (stock-split-adjusted), driven predominantly by a valuation re-rating: the stock
now trades at 24x 12-month forward P/E, compared with 16x at our initiation.
Given the company’s double-digit organic sales growth, supported by new product launches, and its
comparatively strong growth outlook versus our beauty coverage, we view the current valuation as broadly
appropriate.
Puig Brands S.A. (PUIGb.MC) - Rabanne comeback drives FY27 acceleration; Buy (on CL)

We expect organic sales growth to accelerate to around 5%, from roughly 4% in FY26, as key initiatives
scale: Rabanne’s first masculine franchise launch since 2021, La Favorite’s global rollout including the
US, and Charlotte Tilbury’s expansion into Brazil and Chile.
Skincare should provide additional support as premium segment laps easier comps, while Uriage remains
positioned to still deliver double-digit growth from a small base.
