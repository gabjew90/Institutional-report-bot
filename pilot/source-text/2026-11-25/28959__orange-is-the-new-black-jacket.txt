Orange is the New Black (Jacket)
Tactically Cautious Again as SPX Back into Resistance
WHAT YOU SHOULD KNOW: 
Risk happens fast. We were looking for a rebound back to the 7250-7350 range, and
while we didn't anticipate it to happen in one day, with SPX hitting a high of 7295, the
risk/reward has once again shifted to the downside. There are no shortage of stats out
there, but what many of them will show is that extreme one-day gains like today are
more often associated with markets that are trending down, not up. Further, as we
discussed on Sunday, last week's low was likely temporary, not a final bottom. As such,
we would begin to again take a more cautious/defensive posture and use rallies north
of 7300 to reduce exposure anticipating another leg lower.
■
Orange is the New Black (Jacket). In the big picture, the semi/AI trade peaked in
June, some five months before the issues began with Jensen/Nvidia (NVDA, Not
Rated). Thus, while the recovery today is a welcome development and certainly
helping SPX to get back above 7200, it's unlikely to change the primary trajectory of
the semi/AI trade which has been under pressure for much of the last six months.
■
SPX 4% Upside Day. Since 1990 there have been 40 prior days when SPX gained 4%+
while closing below its 200 DMA. Some of the dates did come at the end of declines
(March '09, Dec. '18, March '20), but many of them occurred in the middle of deep
bear markets. The avg. five-day return was -2.09% and negative 57% of the time.
30-day returns are essentially a coin-flip with avg. gains +0.75% and positive 56%
of the time.
■
NDX 6% Upside Day. Since 1985 there have been 42 prior days when NDX gained
6%+ while closing below its 200 DMA. Average and median five-day returns are
-2.64% and -3.10%, respectively, and only positive 39% of the time. Median 30-day
returns are -5.35% and also positive just 39% of the time.
■
SPX -8% Weekly Candle. Last week SPX closed more than 8% below its open. As
we discussed in our weekend note, the median return for the following week was
+5.75%, but EVERY time the low of that -8% week was broken. We nearly got the
median move today, which suggests risk/reward now skewing negatively again.
■
FX Not Buying the Equity Move. One of the issues for markets the last few weeks
was the dollar breakout. Jensen returning to Nvidia (NVDA, Not Rated) is not doing
much for that as the Bloomberg Dollar Index (BBDXY) is up strongly today to a fresh
cycle high.
■
Bitcoin Giving It Back. XBT rallied 16.7% from the weekend lows to this morning's
high. It has since faded -4%, and is often a good tell on the liquidity trade.
