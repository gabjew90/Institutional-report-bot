The internet made information cheaper. AI agents may make switching cheaper. A surprising number of
consumer businesses benefit from the fact that customers rarely revisit existing decisions. Today, the time
and effort required to compare options, renegotiate contracts, switch providers or cancel services acts as a
meaningful barrier to competition. Agentic AI introduces the possibility that these activities become
automated which makes it easier for consumers to act on better alternatives rather than sticking with
existing providers. As AI agents increasingly perform these tasks on behalf of users, profit pools that have
historically benefited from customer inaction could come under pressure.
GSXUSWCH focuses on industries where customer stickiness is driven less by product differentiation and
more by the effort required to take action. Insurance, telecom, travel, subscriptions, marketplaces, and
certain fintech businesses all benefit when customers do not continuously optimize their choices. If AI
agents reduce the cost of comparison and execution, competitive dynamics could shift meaningfully,
putting pressure on pricing power, retention rates and customer lifetime value.

Industries in Focus:
• Traditional Retail Deposits: account holders often leave cash sitting in the same accounts instead of
regularly moving money to higher-yield alternatives.
• Investment & Asset Deposits: account holders infrequently reassess where their assets are held or
whether better options exist.
• Discovery & Search Friction: consumers often settle for familiar brands and research tools rather than
spending time searching for the best alternative.
• Subscription & Contract Friction: effort is required to revisit, renegotiate, cancel, or switch recurring
services and long-term contracts.
• Lead-Gen / Interface Friction: consumers repeatedly use the same websites, apps, and interfaces to
discover and purchase products.
