US MKT INTEL VIEW – We published below in the Aug 24, 2026 Morning Briefing 
We are moving to a Tactically Cautious / Neutral view, which is to say that Equity 
market fundamentals remain strong but that near-term variables are likely to cause 
markets to chop sideways. Here is what is driving the change in our tactical view: (i) The 
Fed – Equity bull markets tends to end with either a hiking cycle or a recession. Currently, 
a recession is highly unlikely to manifest over the next few quarters, but Warsh’s 
comments now make Sep a live meeting. What is unclear is if the Fed hikes, what is the 
scale / length of the hiking cycle, e.g., would he go in Sep / Dec and conclude the hikes or 
potentially go 50bp in Sep? NFP and CPI are the critical inputs with CPI on Sep 11 the 
more important of the two prints given Warsh’s view that the US is at full employment ad 
Friday’s print is unlikely to drive a material change in the U.3 unemployment rate. (ii) 
Positioning – make sure to read the weekly update from Positioning Intel but the TL; 
DR is that positioning is not giving a clean read on market direction and history suggests 
a choppy next 3-4 weeks. The team also have flagged that a sharp rebound in 
Momentum factor is unlikely given recent activity. (iii) Credit Spreads – Historically, there 
is a spike in issuance in the two weeks post-Labor Day driving spreads wider which can 
pressure Equities. Given the recent headwinds to the AI trade stemming from CDS 
spread widening, this is another variable to monitor. (iv) Seasonality – September 
screens as the worst performing month for the SPX followed by Q4 being the strongest 
quarter of the year. Seasonal moves may be exacerbated by midterm election 
seasonality, which tends to be negative into the election and then strongly positive post-
election. Since 1990, the SPX rallied +5.4%, +11.4%, +12.5%, and +14.7% at 3 month 
(78% hit rate), 6 month (100% hit rate), 9 month (100% hit rate), and 12 month (100% hit 
rate) tenors after midterm election days. (v) Equity Rotation / Momentum Unwind – 
Momentum (JPMPURE Index) is down more than 21% from its June ATH and ‘Classic’ 
Momentum (JPPQMO Index) is down nearly 34% from its June ATH. These moves fall 
outside the typical drawdown ranges of 10-15%. (vi) AVGO Earnings – may prove 
spectacular and fail to drive meaningful stock appreciation in AI / Memory / Semis.

In summary, we think market fundamentals (econ & earnings) remain intact but see the 
next 2-3 weeks being choppy. The index may not see a dramatic pullback, but it would 
not surprise to see additional turmoil at the sub-index and single stock level. This argues 
for reducing net-longs and potentially adopting a $-neutral or market-neutral approach. 
Keep an eye on (i) US / Iran to see if a deal is possible or if we see actions that drive oil 
prices higher and (ii) does the US / Canada trade debate expand into something more 
serious.  
  
•         MONETIZATION MENU (AUG 31) – We start with a +NDX vs. -RTY position. Within 
Tech, we like Mag7 as the favored near-term play given the inability for AI / Memory / 
Semis to sustain a bid. Software remains attractive especially if Semis continue to fall. 
Generally, we think that SMID-caps are likely to underperform as part of this tactical call. 
Parts of Cyclicals can perform especially if we see stronger than expected ISM prints this 
week. Here, we like Large-Cap Banks given the economic growth story accompanied by 
loose financial conditions and Consumer Discretionary if energy prices move lower. 
Within Defensives, Healthcare tends to be the best performing sector into midterms. We 
do not have a strong view on Cyclical vs. Defensive outperformance over the next two 
weeks. As hedges we like shorting Credit ETFs and buying VIX products. Until we get 
through the Fed Meeting on Sep 16, the Dollar Debasement trade may struggle, so 
makes sense to switch cash positions into derivative positions. 
•         OLD MONETIZATION MENU (AUG 24) – We like the barbell approach but with eye 
towards Momentum having some more downside risk we think it prudent to play the 
broadening theme. Within our barbell approach, that would spreading risk from 
AI/Memory/Semis to include Mag7/Software. Within Cyclicals, spreading risk from AI-
related Cyclicals to include Financials, Energy, and Metals/Miners. These moves will 
benefit from further broadening and take advantage of the ‘Dollar Debasement’ trade 
which may extend in the near-term. Regardless, Precious Metals remain attractive. For 
those concerned on Midterm Election seasonality, Healthcare tends to be the best sector 
also acting as an anti-Momentum trade.

ROADMAP TO 26Q4 
As we wind down the quarter, we wanted to share some of the catalytic events as we 
approach Q4. So far, midterm election seasonality has failed to materialize; and, at this 
point, it seems unlikely to do so with the dominance of A, an unresolved conflict in the 
Middle East, and the potential for 26Q3 earnings season (which spans the election) to 
deliver another quarter of more than 20% EPS growth with widening margins. 
If we are wrong on midterm seasonality, buy Healthcare, which is typically the best 
performing sector into midterms. Then, size up on Election Day as the post-election setup 
is positive.
