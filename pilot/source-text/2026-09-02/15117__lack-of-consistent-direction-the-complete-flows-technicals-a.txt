BY TYLER DURDEN 
TUESDAY, SEP 01, 2026 - 12:00 AM 
Exiting Nvidia/Jackson Hole week and days to go until another closely watched jobs report 
(according to some this could be the second consecutive negative print in a row), there is some 
confusion how hedge funds should be positioning as we close out the confusing month of 
August, and transition out of summer and into fall. Here is what stands out according to 
Goldman Sachs' trading desks: 
1.​ Hedge Funds modestly re-grossed but their Nets stayed virtually flat on the week, i.e. 
low from historical standards (3rd percentile over 1 year for Fundamental L/S…). 
2.​ No sign of significant re-risking from Long Onlys. 
3.​ While arguably running at relatively low speed, the ‘non-economic’ bid of 
CTAs/Corporates/Retail is still alive. And relevant in the context of summer (il)liquidity. 
4.​ Sentiment is decaying to neutral-to-bearish levels while fundamental investors seem 
hesitant to chase markets higher… when the SPX is one solid day away from new highs. 
Or, as Goldman's Pierre Sabourealt writes, add 3) + 4) and you get, all else equal, a fairly 
resilient tape.  
Let's now take a closer look at all the various technicals that matter to the Goldman traders:

●​ Buybacks: From Goldman's US buyback desk, for the week ending 28-Aug-2026 : 
“Activity across our desk slightly moderated last week, reflecting a natural deceleration 
as we move toward the Labor Day holiday in the US. Our desk volumes finished the 
week at 1.2x vs 2025 YTD ADTV and 1.1x vs 2024 YTD ADTV, with flow heaviest in 
Tech, Financials, and Health Care sectors. We anticipate volumes will remain at this

The Sentiment Indicator (SI) is a measure of aggregate positioning and risk sentiment in the US 
equity market. The Sentiment Indicator tracks investor positioning across the more than 80% of 
the US equity market that is owned by institutional, retail and foreign investors. To calculate the 
Sentiment Indicator we run a Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on six weekly and three 
monthly indicators that span these three investor types. 
CNN FEAR & GREED INDEX: Neutral 
Latest reading at 50/100, down from last week’s 56/100, now down to ‘Neutral’ territory. As of 
Aug 31.  
The Fear & Greed Index is a compilation of seven different indicators that measure some aspect 
of stock market behavior. They are market momentum, stock price strength, stock price breadth, 
put and call options, junk bond demand, market volatility, and safe haven demand.

JPM POSITIONING INTELLIGENCE (link):  
1.​ The market took the key catalysts for this week in stride as US Tech did relatively well, 
though the gains were still led by Software.  HF performance did fine, but continues 
chopping around.  As we look at what could transpire post Jackson Hole, the view is 
mixed as history suggests a lack of consistent direction and net positioning remains 
largely neutral. 
2.​ HF performance improving, but crowding still a drag: Global HF performance ticked up 
WoW (+0.3%) to +1.1% MTD for All Strategies, with Equity L/S +1.5%. Quant/Multi-Strat 
remains mixed, and US crowding continues to be challenged, with Tech and Health Care 
shorts up ~3% over the past 5d. 
3.​ HF Flows show gross stabilization and US > Int’l this week: US gross flows were flat this 
week: longs added while shorts were also covered. Net flows skewed positive in North 
America (+2z) versus slightly negative in EMEA/APAC (< -1z). 
4.​ Positioning largely neutral: US TPM 1wk/4wk changes turned slightly positive (first time 
positive for 4wk since late May), but the level is essentially unchanged at -0.2z / 37th %- 
tile since 2015. EU, Japan, and China/HK positioning measures also read as relatively 
neutral. 
5.​ 1-month equity backdrop looks choppy / mixed (with a near-term bounce bias): Historical 
patterns suggest SPX tends to be up 1wk post-Jackson Hole, but 3–4wks are typically 
neutral/mean-reverting into Sep FOMC. Midterm-Sep seasonality is mixed/flat when SPX 
up YTD thru Aug. US TPM increase in past 4wks suggests very near-term bounce 
followed by more mixed-to-negative outcomes over the next few weeks/month.
