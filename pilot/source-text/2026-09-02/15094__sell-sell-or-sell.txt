Those machines
Our favorite friends the systematics are back, sending a rather asymmetric message. Buybacks are
fading too. And elsewhere in market plumbing, McElligott describes the collapse in vol as “epic” and
warns that major trading losses may soon surface. September suddenly looks a little more interesting.
Sell sell or sell
The problem isn't simply that systematic positioning is high. It's that there isn't much incremental
buying left.

$9bn in an up market."

Goldman agrees
Goldman agrees with the message that CTAs have considerably more room to sell. Goldman's one-
month conditional projections show relatively modest additional buying in an up market but a violent
unwind under the “Down Big” scenario, both globally and in the S&P.
