DeskTalk - US retail sales add to hawkish case
By Preston Pagli
US retail sales for October topped expectations, with the control group rising 0.6% MoM vs. 0.3% expected, pushing front-
end yields higher and extending the run of stronger-than-expected US economic data seen this month.
September retail sales were also revised higher across nearly every category, most notably the control group, which was
revised up to 0.5% from 0.1%.
Beneath the surface, a decline in higher ticket items such as cars and furniture masked a broad increase across several sub-
components including health and personal care, gas stations, and building materials.
The market took the report as further validation of the increasingly hawkish message coming from Fed officials that began
ahead of CPI, when Governor Waller argued in favor of an additional 50bps of rate hikes. The choir got louder today with
comments from non-voter Musalem, who said the Fed may need "several" more hikes before spring and suggested the
real policy rate should be roughly 1 percent higher. 
Front-end UST yields jumped ~7bps to 4.851% while the dollar is extending higher with DXY +0.16% to 103.72. US equities
are paring pre-market gains with ESA -0.25% as all eyes turn to Nvidia earnings after the bell today. 
Looking ahead, Fedspeak continues with Governor Waller at 09:10 EST, followed up by US Industrial Production MoM at
09:15 EST, Cleveland Fed President Hammack on the UST market at 09:30 EST, and NY Fed's Williams at 14:45 EST.
