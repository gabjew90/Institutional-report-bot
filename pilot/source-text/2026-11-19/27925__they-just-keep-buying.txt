They just keep buying 
Why is the dollar rallying even as US tech stocks have sold off so much? It is a question we have been 
pondering given our argument that the opposite would happen. First, to state the obvious, the market has 
also repriced a more hawkish Fed given recent commentary pushing the interest rate differential wider, but 
the dollar seems to be overshooting this move (figure 1). Second, and more intriguingly, foreigners have been 
very aggressive buyers of US equities even as the stock market has tumbled. Our tracker following more than 
100 foreign-domiciled ETFs shows extremely large dip-buying of US stocks from foreigners over the last two 
weeks, the largest buying spree so far this year. Suffice to say we are doubtful both the widening in interest 
rate differentials and aggressive buying of equities can continue if market turmoil persists. We will be 
monitoring foreign investor behaviour closely in coming weeks.
