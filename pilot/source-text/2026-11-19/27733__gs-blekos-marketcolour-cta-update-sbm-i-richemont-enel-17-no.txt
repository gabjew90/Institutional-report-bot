SBM Offshore (Initiation / PT EUR43 / 25% Upside): SBM is the global leader in FPSOs, with c.40%
market share. A deepwater FID wave is seemingly building across Guyana, Brazil and West Africa, and
only a handful of contractors are able to deliver large, gas-heavy units. We see SBM as the best-positioned
company to capture this cycle and do not believe current consensus reflects the pace of FPSO (floating
production storage and offloading) awards we expect.
Richemont (Buy / PT CHF230 from CHF225 / 30% Upside): We increase our underlying profitability
assumptions following the strong margin delivery excluding one-offs, although the more pronounced
improvement in 2027E primarily reflects a one-off tariff refund. Our 2027E EBIT margin forecast rises to
23.1% (or 22.2% excluding one-offs) from 21.9%, driving a 6.1% increase in adjusted EPS, with smaller
upgrades of 1.7–2.7% across 2028–30E.

Enel (BUY / PT EUR12 / 20% Upside): Enel is due to present a Strategic Update in Feb-2027, potentially
at a CMD, in our view. Despite regulatory intervention, we see upside risk to the current targets. For 2028,
we estimate clean EPS at €0.83 (c.11.5x P/E), slightly ahead of guidance and Visible Alpha Consensus.
For 2030, we project €0.92 (10.5x P/E), a touch ahead of guidance and +9% ahead of the street.
