## Marketcolour + CTA Update / SBM (I) / Richemont / Enel ##
Ioannis Blekos
17 Nov 2026

Good Morning,
Weak start to a heavy catalyst week, as US equities finished broadly lower: SPX -192bps to 7416 (8/11 sectors -ve,
VIX +18.55% to 23, Tech -2.98%, Communication Svcs. -2.82%, Energy -2.09%, Staples +0.34%), NDX down
-267bps to 27197 (NVDA -4.5%, DRAM -6%, SMH -5%, Neoclouds -7%), and RTY -170bps to 2742. Thematically,
GS AI Semis basket (GSCHSMHX -5.48%) and GS Power Up America basket (GSENEPOW -3.09%). In Europe, EU
Semis (GSSBSEMI -2.3%), Miners (GSXEMINE -2.7% w/ AAL LN -4.3%, BHP LN -3.5%, ANTO LN -3.1%, RIO LN
-2.1%) while EU Defense (GSSBDEFE +70bps) caught a bid. . SX5E has outperformed SPX by ~8% since early Oct...
 
Interesting: Since 2008, the total market average trade size has declined by (47%) to 136 shares per trade. Excluding
the opening and closing auctions, on exchange average trade size is even smaller, at 108 shares year-to-date
 
Basket Idea: EU Staples (GSXESTAP Index) look particularly vulnerable. They just registered their best monthly
performance on record amidst recent market rotation, diverging from more lacklustre fundamentals (1y fwd EPS
revisions lagged market by >10pp YTD), pushing the sector to trade at a 16% premium to SXXP
 
CTAs are long Europe and short US. Short term thresholds in Europe are ~2% lower. According to our data CTAs are
as short NDX as they have been over the last decade. Chart below.
 
Flow: US-listed ETF shorts decreased -8.6% – the largest % covering in 7 months – led by covering in Large Cap
Equity, Credit, and Tech ETF. Global equity funds saw net inflows in the week ending November 11 (+$98bn vs
+$89bn in the previous week). DM saw continued strength in net inflows driven primarily by US funds.
 
SBM Offshore (Initiation / PT EUR43 / 25% Upside): SBM is the global leader in FPSOs, with c.40% market share. A
deepwater FID wave is seemingly building across Guyana, Brazil and West Africa, and only a handful of contractors
are able to deliver large, gas-heavy units. We see SBM as the best-positioned company to capture this cycle and do
not believe current consensus reflects the pace of FPSO (floating production storage and offloading) awards we
expect. Link
 
Richemont (Buy / PT CHF230 from CHF225 / 30% Upside): We increase our underlying profitability assumptions
following the strong margin delivery excluding one-offs, although the more pronounced improvement in 2027E
primarily reflects a one-off tariff refund. Our 2027E EBIT margin forecast rises to 23.1% (or 22.2% excluding one-offs)
from 21.9%, driving a 6.1% increase in adjusted EPS, with smaller upgrades of 1.7–2.7% across 2028–30E. Link
 
Enel (BUY / PT EUR12 / 20% Upside): Enel is due to present a Strategic Update in Feb-2027, potentially at a CMD,
in our view. Despite regulatory intervention, we see upside risk to the current targets. For 2028, we estimate clean
EPS at €0.83 (c.11.5x P/E), slightly ahead of guidance and Visible Alpha Consensus. For 2030, we project €0.92
(10.5x P/E), a touch ahead of guidance and +9% ahead of the street. Link
