Nvidia Corp. (NVDA): Strong outlook despite uncertainty, with steady commitment to balancing
shareholder returns and ecosystem support
• We expect the stock to hold its gains following a solid quarter with an uptick to CY27 guidance ahead of
Street expectations, set against neutral-to-negative investor positioning (per our conversations).
• Although significant uncertainty remains on several fronts, we believe management's commentary should
bolster investor confidence in the fundamental outlook for both Nvidia's business and the broader AI
ecosystem across a number of dimensions.
Whitbread (WTB.L) - Earnings and ROCE set to inflect sooner and more steeply, unlocking material
shareholder returns; upgrade to Buy
• We upgrade Whitbread to Buy with a 12-month price target of 3,700p, implying c.32% upside. We
believe Whitbread is approaching an inflection point.
• Earnings, returns on capital, and shareholder returns are now set to improve sooner and more steeply
than the market expects, yet the shares trade close to their trough multiple of the last 10+ years.
