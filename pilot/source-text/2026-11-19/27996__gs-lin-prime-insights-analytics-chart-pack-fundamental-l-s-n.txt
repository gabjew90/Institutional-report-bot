October’s performance drawdown has given way to a cautious November. Global
Fundamental L/S managers are down slightly by -0.2% so far in November (+8.9% YTD
as of Nov 16th), driven by beta losses partially offset by positive alpha on the back of
short side gains, while Systematic L/S returns are up +1.1% MTD on the back of a sharp
recovery last week. TMT and Asia L/S funds continue to experience more performance
pressure and are down -4.5% and -1.4% MTD, respectively.
 
Hedge funds have heavily net sold global equities so far in November, driven by
long-and-short sales (~3 to 1). Single Stocks collectively were net sold in 7 of the last
8 weeks, and the pace of selling has picked up notably MTD. ETF short flows have
become more volatile, suggesting investors are actively using Macro Products to hedge
and manage exposures. In turn, Fundamental L/S net leverage has declined by
another -7.6 pts MTD to the lowest level in more than five years (now below
Liberation Day lows). Following persistent de-grossing and heavy net selling by
hedge funds in the past month, equity market could be setting up for a tactical
rally in the near term, as seasonality and technicals are set to become friendlier in
the coming weeks (link). That said, it’s worth noting that other sentiment measures

including GIR’s Sentiment Indicator (link) and Risk Appetite Indicator (link) do not point
to depressed positioning from a broader set of market participants. 
 
From a regional perspective, EM Asia and North America are both heavily net sold
so far in November, outpacing modest net buying in Europe and DM Asia. Within
Asia, Taiwan, Korea, and China are the most net sold markets MTD. Hedge funds are
now O/W Asia (DM + EM) by +8.4% relative to MSCI World ACWI (vs. record high
of +20% in June and +6.5% at the start of 2026).
 
Thematic and factor highlights: 
 
1) On a trailing 6-week basis, the pace of selling in global Info Tech since early
October is by far the fastest in the past decade (-4.4 z score), led by selling in
Semis & Semi Equip stocks.
2) Industrials is the second most net sold US sector MTD (behind Info Tech) where
net exposure has fallen to the lowest level in more than two years.  
 
3) Consumer Staples is among the most net sold US sectors MTD, suggesting
managers have been fading the sector’s price rally.
 
4) Hedge funds have unwound risk in US Health Care for 9 consecutive
weeks, and de-grossing in Biotech has accelerated again in November, driven by long
sales.  
 
5) Factor exposure to Medium-Term Momentum is on track to decline for a 6th
straight month to the lowest level since Dec ’22 (20th percentile 5-year), while
factor exposure to Value has risen sharply to 5-year highs amid increased
stagflation concerns.
