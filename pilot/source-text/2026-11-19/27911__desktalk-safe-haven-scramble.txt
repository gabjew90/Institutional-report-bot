DeskTalk - Safe-haven scramble
By Preston Pagli
A sharp rotation out of US equities and into Treasuries following a flurry of headlines has investors on edge ahead of several
key catalysts, including Nvidia earnings after the close, a major US equity issuance on Thursday, and any geopolitical
developments stemming from President Trump's Asia trip, with a trilateral meeting between Trump, Xi, and Putin slated for
01:30 EST on Thursday (full schedule here). 
A CNN report detailing the NSA's internal communications network is down is sowing additional uncertainty into the market
following a series of outages this week, though there is no evidence of a cyberattack, the report says. 
US equities sold off aggressively, with the SPX (-1%) falling more than 1.5% at its lows as software and tech shares led the
decline. The NDX (-1.4%), which dropped more than 2%, has since pared losses after former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang
released his first public statement since stepping down, saying he would be "honored to answer the calls" for his
reinstatement.
Investors have shifted toward traditional safe havens, with gold, Treasuries, and Citi's US Long/Short Risk Index
(CISURISK) all moving higher. The benchmark US 10y yield (5.10%) dropped more than 7bps peak to trough, while gains
are now more modest. 
In FX, the dollar reversed lower, with DXY falling to 103.42 after initially rallying on a firmer-than-expected US retail sales
print. The eFX desk reports volumes running 81% above average between 07:30 EST and 10:30 EST, with net buying in
USD and JPY, while EUR selling was primarily driven by corps and investors. Activity in EURCHF also picked up materially
amid the flight-to-quality backdrop.
Looking ahead, Nvidia's earnings after the close remain the key catalyst, with investors focused on whether management
addresses the mounting calls for Jensen Huang's return. This morning, Citi's equity experts from across the firm held a
discussion to discuss fundamentals, positioning, risk/reward and flows ahead of NVDA earnings. See the replay here.
