DeskTalk - Nvidia Earnings: Final views across Citi
By Preston Pagli
Nvidia is set to report earnings at 16:20 EST / 21:20 GMT today, with the call set to begin at 17:00 EST / 22:00 GMT. Nvidia
has shed nearly 30% since its last earnings report following a broad repricing in semis and the resignation of CEO Jensen
Huang. Investors focus will remain on the numbers, but any guidance as it relates to the succession plan and future
leadership are likely to be just as important. 
Just today, Huang issued his first statement to say "If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of
our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as
CEO". 
Options markets are pricing in an implied move of roughly +/-8% for Nvidia tonight, while our equity trading strategy desk is
tracking Wednesday as the largest SPX event for the next month at +/- 101bps. We bring together the latest Citi analysis
and previews ahead of earnings. 
NVIDIA Corp (NVDA.O) - Preview - Investor Focus on 2028 Visibility & Backlog Movement
Our Research Analyst Atif Malik is expecting a modest EPS beat on results/guide with higher sales offset by lower gross
margins due to HBM price increase. Malik expects Nvidia to reiterate its prior FY28E (CY27E) revenue outlook of 70% YoY
growth. 
Key investor topics include:
2028 order visibility and backlog conversion, especially on Neocloud customers
Supply constraints
Rubin ramp
CEO investigation. 
 
  
NVIDIA Corp (NVDA.O) - Nvidia Without Jensen? Lowering Target Price Again
On November 12 ahead of Jensen Huang's resignation as CEO, our Research Analyst Atif Malik wrote, "a leadership
transition creates an overhang on the stock given his central role in strategy, product vision, customer relationships, and
investor confidence", and lowered his PT to $225, or 20% discount to prior 19x P/E on increased uncertainty but keep Buy as
it is trading ~11x P/E, in line with prior trough.

NVDA Into Results: Beyond Picks and Shovels
AI picks and shovels led investor positioning for more than three years, yet money is now leaving memory, semi cap, analog
and even compute hardware. Worries over data center spending, the midterms and Nvidia itself are driving the exits. The
open question is the margin outlook and strong cash return as support.
