Tech at a breaking point
NDX is approaching major make-or-break levels after another brutal round of selling. Goldman's latest
positioning data show just how aggressive the liquidation has become.
Hedge fund leverage has collapsed, tech selling has reached decade-long extremes, and the rotation
out of momentum into value continues.
A lot of risk has already been taken out of the market, but the similarities to the nasty 2018 tech sell-off
are difficult to ignore. Back then, the market kept falling much further than most expected.
Make or break
NDX is trading very close to major make-or-break levels. The long-term trendline from the Liberation
Day lows comes in here, alongside the key levels from last year.
We are well below the 200-day moving average, and as we pointed out yesterday, the 21-day has just
crossed below the 200-day (a light death cross).
A similar cross earlier this year coincided with the local lows, but the same negative cross in March last
year was followed by the Liberation Day puke.

The puke
Fundamental long/short funds continue cutting exposure. Gross leverage has fallen 4.4 points so far in
November to 206.3%, putting it at the 23rd percentile over the past year. Net leverage has dropped an
even sharper 7.6 points to 41.6%, the lowest level in five years.

The great de-grossing
Hedge funds have been aggressive net sellers of global equities so far in November, with long sales
outpacing short sales by roughly 3 to 1. On a trailing four-week basis, the de-grossing has been the
largest since February 2023 and one of the biggest episodes of the past decade.

GS
Tech liquidation at extremes
Hedge funds continue to aggressively dump global tech. On a trailing six-week basis, selling has been
by far the most aggressive in a decade, with a staggering -4.4 z-score. The global tech long/short ratio
has collapsed from a YTD peak of 2.9 to just 1.79, approaching 10-year lows.
GS
"Inverted"
Net exposure in US Semis & Semi Equip has now fallen below net exposure in US Software for the first
time since the start of 2026. A remarkable reversal in relative positioning between two of the biggest
tech subsectors.
GS
Momentum out, value in
Hedge fund exposure to medium-term momentum is on track to decline for a sixth consecutive month,
reaching the lowest level since December 2022 (20th percentile over five years). Meanwhile, value
exposure has surged to five-year highs amid growing stagflation concerns.

GS
2018 déjà vu?
The tech sell-off has been brutal, but we have been getting a strange sense of déjà vu. It took us a
while to figure out why, until we went back and looked at the nasty autumn 2018 sell-off. So far, tech is
following that pattern remarkably closely.
There were plenty of opportunities to catch the falling knife back then, but the market kept falling
much further than most thought possible. NDX eventually lost around 25% from its peak to the late-
2018 lows.
The current sell-off feels nasty, but the similarities to the 2018 pattern are striking.
