BofA November Global Fund Manager Survey 
Bottom line: investors bullish on macro (just 3% predict recession) and markets (cash at 
uber-low 3.6%, say global stocks to be best performer in ’27, need 6% yields to go OW 
bonds); but euphoria on boom & AI over…investors slash tech OW by most since ’06 
(Chart 1); consensus bear risk…global hikes = recession, bull risk = tech credit event 
concerns fade; we stay tactically risk-off…Nov FMS does not show enough investor 
“fear” to buy.  
On Macro & Micro: 1st time since June investors predict “soft landing” (50%) not “no 
landing” boom (42%); inflation expectations lowest since Oct'24 as GDP/EPS optimism 
peaks; big “pause AI capex” FMS message…just 16% want corps to boost capex (lowest 
since May'20) and 52% investors expect an AI hyperscaler capex cut in the next 6 months.  
On Risks: most crowded trades…long global semis (39%), short China (14%), long Mag7 
(12%); most likely source of credit event: AI hyperscaler bonds (38%), private credit (30%).  
On AA: allocators remain long stocks & short cash but pare bond UW to smallest since 
Nov’25 (5.8% magic GT10 yield needed to go OW), say gold at record “undervaluation”; 
on sectors…big healthcare OW, big energy UW, mass short-cover discretionary/staples, 
big cut to tech OW (FMS history suggests best entry points when tech cut to UW).  
On 2027: investors say best-performing asset classes will be international stocks (36%), 
US stocks (24%) & gold (17%), best performing stock markets to be Nasdaq & MSCI EM 
(21%); best performing currency = Japanese yen (42%); FMS investor targets for end-
2027…UST 10-year yield 4.9%, Euro-dollar 1.12, S&P 500 7,926, gold $4,341/oz.  
Contrarian trades: based on Nov FMS extreme positions…long cash/gold-short stocks, 
long consumer-short health care, long REITs-short banks, long energy-short industrials.
