“We’ve got this”: Confident, reassuring call; 
compelling valuation 
Reiterate Rating: BUY | PO: 280.00 USD | Price: 155.46 USD   
   
 
 
Solid, confident growth outlook, raising estimates 
While it may be premature to sound the all-clear, NVDA’s earnings call was very reassuring 
as the company, under Interim CEO Kress, appears to be pulling together strongly in 
defiance of the skeptics. Notable positives: 1) FY28/CY27 YoY Sales growth raised to 
75% from 70% off a higher 2HFY27/CY26 base; 2) Over $230bn in buybacks now to be 
executed by CY27-end; 3) Demand still exceeds supply despite increased China scrutiny; 
4) Supply chain alignment; 5) Higher scrutiny of riskier neocloud shipments. Meanwhile, 
valuation at <10x CY27E PE (vs. 30x median) indicates perhaps the most compelling risk-
reward across our coverage. Outstanding risks: 1) 10Q filing delayed; 2) CEO transition 
could take time; 3) Macro risks re neocloud funding, China scrutiny; and 4) Headline, 
financial risks from ex-CEO’s departure and increased government scrutiny. Overall we 
tweak FY27/28/29E EPS by +6%/+9%/unch to $9.62/17.17/23.17. Reit Buy, $280 PO and 
flag management’s confidence (Interim CEO Kress stating “we’ve got this”). 
Compelling stock on 10x CY28E PE even in bear case 
Our base case is for FY29/CY28E ~30% sales and 35% YoY pf-EPS growth towards 
$23.17, or implied 6.6x PE. In a bear-case scenario of zero sales growth YoY, NVDA could 
still generate ~$16 in pf-EPS or 9.7x implied PE. Note our base case is also conservative, 
assuming modest sequential growth off exit CY27 quarterly levels.  If we assume more 
robust 40%+ YoY in a bull-case scenario, NVDA could generate close to $26.59.  Longer-
term (CY30E) we forecast EPS power of ~$30, intact with some share loss assumptions 
(CY30 AI data center share 50% vs. 65% currently) offset by cost controls and buybacks. 
Risks: Ex-CEO government scrutiny, AI capex 
We believe NVDA’s generational low <10x forward PE reflects an unwarranted level of 
risk. In a normal environment the stock would be trading close to its 30-35% EPS growth 
CAGR for CY26-30E.  However, AI capex and DC buildouts remain in flux, and there are 
still headline or financial scrutiny/risks with NVDA’s ex-CEO, and incremental export 
restrictions on shipment of high-end AI products overseas. Rising cost of memory and 
high competition from custom chips could also pressure NVDA’s industry-leading GM, 
while supplier commitments could become a burden should growth fail to materialize.

Price objective basis & risk 
NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) 
Our $280 PO is based on 16x CY27E PE ex cash, within NVDA's historical 15x-56x 
forward year PE range, which we believe is justified by NVDA's leading share in fast-
growing AI compute/networking markets, offset by lumpiness in global AI projects, 
cyclical gaming market, and concerns around access to power. 
 
Downside risks are: 1) Potential for enhanced reputational and financial risk and 
leadership transition due to ongoing US government scrutiny around Nvidia CEO, 2) 
Competition with major public firms, internal cloud projects and other private companies 
in AI and accelerated computing markets, 3) Larger than expected impact from 
restrictions on compute shipments to China, or additional restrictions placed on activity 
in the region, 4) Lumpy and unpredictable sales in new enterprise, data center, and autos 
markets, 5) Potential for decelerating capital returns, and 6) Enhanced government 
scrutiny of NVDA's dominant market position in AI chips.
