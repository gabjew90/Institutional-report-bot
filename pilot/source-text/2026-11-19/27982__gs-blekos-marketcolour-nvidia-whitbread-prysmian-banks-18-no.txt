18 Nov 2026

Good Morning,
US equities continued to trend lower today: SPX down -103bps to 7340 (all 11 sectors -ve, Vix >25, S&P500 below
its 200-dma for the 1x since April, Financials -1.87%, Utilities -1.60%), NDX down -72bps to 27001, and RTY
-150bps to 2701. Thematically, GS High Beta Momo basket (GSPRHIMO +1.92%) and GS Momentum Hi/Lo basket
(GSP1MOMO +1.67%) outperformed. In Europe, SXXP –1.8% (worst day since March) as 92% of stocks closed red -
EU Semis (GSSBSEMI -2.9%) and Software names (GSSBSFTW -4.4%) got hit.
 
Nvidia on the Call: “Token prices have fallen nearly 60% since May, accelerating token usage and demand for
compute. As a result, GPU rental prices are rising across generations. Hopper GPUs have re-contracted at up to twice
their original price under the multi-year agreements, while the B300 rates have risen more than 60% since their
introduction.”
 
Nvidia (BUY / PT UDS220 / 40% Upside / 8.6x PE ‘28): Against neutral-to-negative investor positioning -> Revs
$115.8 bn vs Cons $109.5 bn; gross margin 74.9% vs Cons 73.5%; data Center revs $107.6 bn vs Cons $102.0 bn;
mgmt increased its revenue forecast for CY27 to +75% growth (up from +70% prior) given strong demand; Rubin is
“the fastest product ramp in history” with $20bn of revenue in its first quarter; financial commitments in support of
customers and supply chain partners are at $518bn. Link
 
Nike: investor day was not enough; mgmt sees FY29 as turning point; still expects sales to fall by high single digits in
FY2027 as it cuts Sportswear and Jordan supply and resets its China business; transcript here: Link
 
Whitbread (Up to B / PT GBP37 / 32% Upside): Earnings (PBT is set to grow at a 14% CAGR over FY27-31E),
returns on capital (ROCE to expand by c.680bps by FY31E (vs. FY27E)), and shareholder returns are now set to
improve sooner and more steeply than the market expects (scope to begin buybacks next year), yet the shares trade
close to their trough multiple of the last 10+ years. Link
 
Prysmian (BUY / PT EUR174 / 60% Upside): We raise our FY26-28E adj EBITDA by c.3%-2% to reflect the 3Q26
beat (here) and our increasing barometer for 4Q26 (Exhibit 8). We see Prysmian above the top end of 2026 EBITDA
guidance and expect the company to introduce 2027 adj. EBITDA guidance at their full year results >€4bn Link
 
EUROBANK: announced acquisition of 65% of Bulgarian bank Fibank, worth E1bn for 100% financed by excess
capital + E750m ABB (4% of market cap). In terms of maths, see EPS accretion at 7% and 12% PAT accretion pro
forma for the share capital increase and return on consumed capital of 21% proforma for for FL costs synergies.
 
BNP: BNL deep dive. New 2030 targets with Revenue growth >3% vs consensus at 2.4%. NII growth of 2.5% and
fees of 4%. C/I at 56% vs consensus 57% . Cost discipline with savings offsetting inflation. RwA down 0.5% CAGR vs
consensus up. They see Loan and deposits stable and modest growth in CA modest ie slight mix improvement.
 
SMITHS GROUP 1Q ORGANIC REV. 0%. First take this is light vs whispers for ~3%. A slow start had been
expected, however, and the company maintain fy guide for c.4% with view for it to be “accelerating through the year,

with John Crane weighted to H2 and Flex-Tek to H1” after ME disruptions have impacted JC through this first part of
the year.
