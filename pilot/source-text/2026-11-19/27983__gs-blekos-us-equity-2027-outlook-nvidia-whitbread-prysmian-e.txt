2027 US Equity Outlook: The beginning of the middle
 
• We forecast the S&P 500 will rise by 17% to 8,700 by year-end 2027...
• ...We expect EPS growth will slow from roughly 30% in 2026 to 18% in 2027.
• ...Key driver: Earnings, a solid economy and continued AI investments.
• ...Valuation: The S&P 500 forward P/E has declined by 19% in 2026 and now sits near its 10-year average -> rates
and AI uncertainty will keep the P/E near its 18x.
• ...Themes: AI Productivity Candidates (Ex.50), Buyback Aristocrats (EX.51), Energy Infra, Transport, HC
(biopharma, managed care), Selected Staples (beauty, energy drinks, nicotine).
 
Nvidia Corp. (NVDA): Strong outlook despite uncertainty, with steady commitment to balancing shareholder returns
and ecosystem support
 
• We expect the stock to hold its gains following a solid quarter with an uptick to CY27 guidance ahead of Street
expectations, set against neutral-to-negative investor positioning (per our conversations). 
• Although significant uncertainty remains on several fronts, we believe management's commentary should bolster
investor confidence in the fundamental outlook for both Nvidia's business and the broader AI ecosystem across a
number of dimensions.
 
Whitbread (WTB.L) - Earnings and ROCE set to inflect sooner and more steeply, unlocking material shareholder
returns; upgrade to Buy
 
• We upgrade Whitbread to Buy with a 12-month price target of 3,700p, implying c.32% upside. We believe
Whitbread is approaching an inflection point. 
• Earnings, returns on capital, and shareholder returns are now set to improve sooner and more steeply than the
market expects, yet the shares trade close to their trough multiple of the last 10+ years.
 
Prysmian (PRY.MI) - Reiterating Buy post 3Q26; deep dive on telecom submarine
 
• We raise our FY26-28E adj EBITDA by c.3%-2% to reflect the 3Q26 beat (here) and our increasing barometer for
4Q26 (Exhibit 8). 
• We see Prysmian above the top end of 2026 EBITDA guidance and expect the company to introduce 2027 adj.
EBITDA guidance at their full year results >€4bn, as it continues to see mix improvements from its existing backlog
in Transmission, and progressive improvements in Digital Solutions margins on fiber price tailwinds and higher
volumes from Channell (40% EBITDA margins).
 

EUROPE MULTI-INDUSTRY - What PPI tells us about Multi-Industry
 
• In line with the latest developments in the Iran conflict and improved tanker traffic, Capital Goods PPI in the US
decelerated sequentially in October but remains well above the 20-year median level (+2.7 pp). However, China
PPI for October indicated a substantial sequential rise (+0.7 pp vs September 2026). 
• In Europe, September PPI data showed a sequential acceleration and remained above the historical median (+2.5
pp). We map raw material moves that are most relevant to each product category on the respective PPI charts and
also present company-level correlations alongside these datapoints in the Appendix.

Neste OYJ (NESTE.HE) - Volumes growth, strong FCF generation and undemanding valuation; reiterate Buy
 
Dassault Systemes (DAST.PA) - Key takeaways from 2026 Capital Markets Day
 
• In our view, any re-rating will likely be contingent on solid sales execution and on delivery against the pathway to
revenue acceleration towards double-digit growth. 
• We expect investor focus to remain on sales execution, the AI product rollout, and any proof points of AI
monetization.
