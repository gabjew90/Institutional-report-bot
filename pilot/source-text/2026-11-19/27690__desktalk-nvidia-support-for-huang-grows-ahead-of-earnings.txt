DeskTalk - Nvidia: Support for Huang grows ahead of earnings
By Preston Pagli
Nvidia (-2%) is paring early losses after falling as much as 4% earlier in the session. The stock rebounded after President
Trump reiterated his support for former CEO Jensen Huang, telling reporters outside the White House before departing for
Asia, where he is scheduled to meet Chinese President Xi Jinping, that he "wouldn't mind" seeing Huang return as CEO.
The comments come amid a growing number of executives calling for Huang's reinstatement. A petition advocating his return
has garnered nearly 180,000 signatures, including 8,900 current Nvidia employees. Just this morning, the US Commerce
Department issued new rules requiring licenses to purchase advanced chips in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, and
Brazil, aimed at limiting Chinese access.
Following Huang's resignation, our research analyst Atif Malik lowered his Nvidia price target to $225, a 20% reduction
from the prior 19x P/E, maintaining a Buy rating on the stock. With Nvidia set to report on Tuesday, Nov. 17, Malik expects a
modest EPS beat, driven by stronger revenue partially offset by lower gross margins. He also forecasts FY28 (CY27E)
revenue growth of approximately 70% YoY. Key investor focus areas include: visibility into 2028, particularly among neocloud
customers, supply constraints, the rubin product ramp, and finally whether interim CFO Colette Kress can provide any new
details into the CEO investigation, with our TMT specialist also notes GM may also be in her control. Our equity trading
strategy desk is currently tracking Wednesday, the trading day following earnings, as the largest SPX event priced by options
for the next month at +/- 101bps.
