The $625 billion question
Ahead of Nvidia's earnings, Deutsche Bank's Jim Reid highlights some extraordinary numbers. NVDA
was earning just $5bn annually when ChatGPT launched in 2022. Today, that figure is approaching
$240bn, while consensus sees an astonishing $625bn by 2031. That 2031 estimate has more than
doubled in just one year.
The earnings trajectory is extraordinary. The market's willingness to pay for it is another story.

The great Nvidia disconnect
Nvidia has lost 35% from its October peak despite an extraordinary upward revision in long-term
earnings expectations. As DB's Jim Reid points out, NVDA now trades at roughly 6x projected 2031
earnings.
Either the market is dramatically underestimating Nvidia's future earnings power, or those forecasts
are far too optimistic. Something has to give.

The $10 trillion question
DB's Jim Reid highlights some extraordinary Nvidia math. If the company delivers the $625bn in
earnings currently projected for 2031, even a 16x multiple would put its market cap at a staggering
$10 trillion.
The long-term upside math is spectacular, but the immediate technical picture is considerably less
impressive. NVDA is testing its massive trendline dating back to 2023. The stock remains below its 100-
week moving average, although the 200-week is still comfortably lower.

From perfection to skepticism
Nvidia earnings are approaching, and the setup is fascinating. Investors are increasingly questioning
hyperscaler spending, competition from custom chips and the sustainability of Nvidia's extraordinary
margins.
Meanwhile, retail investors have been loading up on NVDA puts.
The fundamental debate is intensifying, the technical picture is fragile, and bearish positioning is
building. That is a potentially explosive combination heading into earnings.
