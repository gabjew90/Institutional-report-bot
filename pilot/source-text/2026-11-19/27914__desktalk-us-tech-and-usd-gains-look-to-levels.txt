DeskTalk - US tech and USD gains, look to levels
By Rui Ding
Solid NVDA earnings gives relief to the AI trade; USD also enjoys a renewed bid as the European session gets underway.
Gold is the notable underperformer (spot -2.4%). We flag levels to watch for ES1, EUR, GBP, ZAR with choppy price action
still to come (for today, mind Trump in China and FOMC minutes). 
NVDA raised its outlook, forecasting strong sales and revenue for FY27 - Citi analysts review here. We highlight for macro
observers - Nvidia's more selective approach to financing AI infrastructure projects could moderate growth for some neocloud
operators if funding becomes constrained. 
NQA contracts +1%, more modest gains for ES1 but this comes after risk-off price action on Tuesday. ES1 200d MA at 7,374.
CTAs could be signaled to halt their selling in SPX and move back to moderately short if we rally back above 7,474 (+0.5% vs
7439 ESZ6), according to our model. 
USD getting another leg higher against European currency complex and also EM FX. EURUSD trading at 1.0960 right in the
middle of support. GBPUSD getting closer to the year's triple bottom at 1.3140/60 that our trader is watching as support after
UK CPI came in line (but services tad softer). 
ZAR drop this morning has raised questions - our trader says the slide in gold prices and a stronger DXY are bringing us
through 17.00 right now and we are seeing some stoppish price action. 17.20-25 next level to watch.
