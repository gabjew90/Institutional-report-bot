Key takeaways
• Not enough FMS "fear" to buy risk...just 3% predict recession, cash low 3.6%, all global stock bulls

• Consensus bear risk...global hikes & recession, bull risk...concerns over tech credit event reverse

BofA November Global Fund Manager Survey
Bottom line: investors bullish on macro (just 3% predict recession) and markets (cash at uber-low 3.6%, say global stocks to be best performer in '27, need
6% yields to go OW bonds); but euphoria on boom & AI over…investors slash tech OW by most since '06 (Chart 1); consensus bear risk…global hikes =
recession, bull risk = tech credit event concerns fade; we stay tactically risk-oﬀ…Nov FMS does not show enough investor "fear" to buy.
On Macro & Micro: 1 time since June investors predict "soﬅ landing" (50%) not "no landing" boom (42%); inﬂation expectations lowest since Oct'24 as
GDP/EPS optimism peaks; big "pause AI capex" FMS message…just 16% want corps to boost capex (lowest since May'20) and 52% investors expect an AI
hyperscaler capex cut in the next 6 months.
On Risks: most crowded trades…long global semis (39%), short China (14%), long Mag7 (12%); most likely source of credit event: AI hyperscaler bonds
(38%), private credit (30%).
On AA: allocators remain long stocks & short cash but pare bond UW to smallest since Nov'25 (5.8% magic GT10 yield needed to go OW), say gold at
record "undervaluation"; on sectors…big healthcare OW, big energy UW, mass short-cover discretionary/staples, big cut to tech OW (FMS history suggests
best entry points when tech cut to UW).
On 2027: investors say best-performing asset classes will be international stocks (36%), US stocks (24%) & gold (17%), best performing stock markets to
be Nasdaq & MSCI EM (21%); best performing currency = Japanese yen (42%); FMS investor targets for end-2027…UST 10-year yield 4.9%, Euro-dollar
