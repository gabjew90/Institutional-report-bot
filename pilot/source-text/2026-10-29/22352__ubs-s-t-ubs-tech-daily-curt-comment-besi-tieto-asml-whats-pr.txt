What Is REALLY Priced In for ASML?
Our framework points to significant dispersion: ASML, STM and NXP screen as the most de-risked, with
current share prices implying >50% cuts to our 2028E AI EPS estimates. By contrast, Nokia and Soitec
screen as the most demanding, with valuations still implying upgrades or only modest downgrades
ASML
1) The market is already discounting a material AI reset
Extrapolating ASML’s 2011–21 EPS CAGR to 2028E suggests non-AI EPS of €40–45, versus c.€85 in
buyside expectations, implying roughly half is AI-related. Applying historical valuation (26x) to the non-AI
base leaves only 11x for AI earnings, consistent with a c.50% haircut. On €40–45 non-AI EPS, ASML would
trade at c.30-35x, versus historical 1Y/2Y averages of c.31x/27x. We see an asymmetry: valuation reflects a
meaningful AI correction that our compute work does not yet support.
2) Translating the valuation discount into GW suggests 25GW downside
We estimate every 1GW of AI compute represents €500-900m of lithography spend, or roughly 0.6-1% of
ASML’s 2028E revenues and 2% EPS. On our framework, the ~50% haircut to AI-related EPS currently
implied by the valuation equates to ~25GW coming out of 2027/28E capacity expectations. Against our
forecast of ~50GW of industry additions in 2027E and 60-70GW in 2028E, this suggests the market is already
pricing in c.20% downside to AI compute demand across 2027/28E.
3) €85 of 2028E EPS does NOT require $300bn+ WFE
Our investor conversations suggest the buyside bogey for FY28E sits at around €85/share of EPS (Sell side
€78/share). We reverse-engineer that number through ASML’s P&L, units and ASPs to derive the underlying
WFE assumption.We estimate €85/share can be supported by roughly 115-120 EUV shipments and
$250-275bn of WFE in 2028E. That is meaningfully below the often-discussed >$300bn WFE scenario.
