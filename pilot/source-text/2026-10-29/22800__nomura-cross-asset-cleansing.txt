• Look...it just comes down the brutal $$$ reality of the past decade's-worth of Asset-Allocator money
flows into the Hedge Fund -space...where THE unequivocal winner has been Multi-Strats, on account of
the remarkably consistency in doing their job and generating (generally) uncorrelated returns under the
challenge of such remarkable $scaling, in large-part being a function of tremendous risk-management
procedure & discipline
• But no doubt, sometimes you can be a victim of your own success, "Too much of a good thing"....and
with the Multi-Strat HF universe being THE $$$flow winner of the Asset-Allocator money flows vs
Directional / Single-Strategy HFs, the "Leverage" component required across the RV / Market-Neutral -
framework simply creates "Perpetual Crowding" -risk (while also acknowledging too that the proliferation
of Street QIS businesses with "bolt-on" risk-premia can also of course then contribute to "Crowding"
dynamics, but with risk of "liquidity" to contribute to unwind in stress / drawdown periods)
• As such, there's been a lot of discussion about strategy and Pod stop-outs this year, a few different waves
from initial Iran War period and the Rate Vol / Energy shock, to the July Momentum unwind, to the

September Hawkish CB tremors and most recently, the October OATs knock-on bleed, with the folks at

– The heavy hurt in European high yield: "Young people have never traded a real bear market"
– "Hedge funds hired too many morons" and other unpopular opinions as the industry pukes PMs
• Sure, Multi-Strats get a lot of the press / association, and generally have comp structures which can
incentivize PMs into Long-Conxexity / Defined-Risk "Right-Tail" profiled -trades, where the cost of Carry
/ Theta bill can bleed you, but with the occasional massive payout....so you need to have some "funding"
trades "on" to cheapen out your Premium spend...
• But this is of course in-addition to the core Fixed-Income RV sleeve of this universe, which will tend to
have an opposite profile, "Left-Tailed" and essentially short Vol / short Gamma, with high hits on
accumulating small & steady wins but then subject to the occasional "terminal" drawdown
• So we get these periods of accumulated +++ performance over a low-Vol background through a lot of
PMs who are doing a lot of the same "rational" trades, but the almost Asset growth in this part of the HF
universe, the near pervasive demand for bodies to fill seats to manage that money (potentally "talent
dilutive"), and inherent high leverage either required (RV) or deployed (Options Convexity) into both
"Asymmetric Payout" =trades plus core "Carry"--creates these periods of rolling stop-outs...it's just the
reality, an offshoot of the strategy's own success
• But the absolutely remarkable nature and aforementioned "consistency" of Multi-Mgr returns-despite
these underlying components seeing these natural periods of occasional high "stop-out" turnover-is the
very "concept validation" as to why they work and are so favored by Allocators!!!...bc by the nature of the
model, a Pod will go through a drawdown limit, and their trades come-off as they lose their capital in
mechanical, unemotional fashion...Then it's on to the next, with diversifying teams and return-streams
powering on the next day
• So yes, we've seen Multi-Strat (and Macro) Pod liquidations and stop-outs in fits-and-starts over the
course of the year, and again in conjunction with this latest round of "Momentum Unwind"....But this too
shall pass, as it's just the nature of the business, with performance dynamics then playing their part and
cleansing the excess Positioning and Leverage out of the system, and likely, causing some much-needed
hiring "rebalancing" as well
