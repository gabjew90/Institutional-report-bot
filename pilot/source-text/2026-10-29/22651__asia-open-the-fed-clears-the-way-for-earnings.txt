Asia Open - The Fed clears the way for earnings
By Preston Pagli
Fed Chair Warsh skewed slightly dovish, helping drive a rally across rates despite the Fed refraining from signaling a
clear direction for December. Markets focused on Warsh's comments that most policymakers do not view the economy
as "running hot," and that the accumulation of incoming data will guide the next policy decision. UST yields fell ~10bps
peak to trough, the dollar weakened, and equities gained ground up until the close. CAD strengthened ~0.4% following a
25bp rate hike in the name of "risk management". Equities are navigating a volatile after-mkt session as blockbuster
earnings from Microsoft and Alphabet pulled markets in opposite directions, with Microsoft +3% on strong results while
Alphabet slumped 11% after disappointing search and YT growth. Looking ahead, a relatively light data calendar ahead
of the ECB and US Core PCE Thursday will include Japan foreign security purchases and Sweden GDP at 07:00 GMT.

In Focus‌
FOMC: The FOMC left rates unchanged as expected in a 10-2 vote, with Fed governor Lisa Cook and the Cleveland Fed's
Hammack dissenting in favor of a 25bp rate hike. The policy statement was largely unchanged, with the primary adjustment
being an upgrade to the characterization of economic activity, which was described as "strong" rather than "solid." The
committee also said the accumulation of more information will help the committee decide the policy stance. The most dovish
highlight of the event was Warsh saying that most at the Fed do not think the economy is running hot.
Rates: USTs rallied after Fed Chair Warsh delivered a dovish tilt in his press conference. Front-end 2y yields trade as much
as ~10bps lower peak to trough, in a sharp steepening of the curve. 2s, 5s, and 10s remain 'max short' within the CTA
systematic world while 30's trade in 'short territory' with the trigger to flip long at 107-02 (ref 109-09)
Equities received a boost post-Fed after trading mostly flat on the day up until blockbuster earnings from Alphabet and
Microsoft sent headline indices lower after Alphabet fell 11% after-mkt as Q3 revenue ex-TAC of $111.2B missed consensus,
with declines in search and YT. Intra-day vol on the session softened with spot VIX trading below 17 and VVIX lower to 96,
from 110 last week. Flows on the desk were better to buy with inflows skewed toward Info Tech, Healthcare, Consumer
Discretionary, Industrials, Energy, while outflows led in Financials and Comm Services. Oracle surged 11% after its investor
day, where management laid out a long-term free cash flow outlook. 

FX: The dollar weakened following dual catalysts including a more dovish than expected Warsh press conference and reports
Vice President JD Vance reached out to Iranian leadership. DXY trades -0.20% to 102.13. EURUSD trades 1.116 ahead of
the ECB decision on Thursday where a hold is expected. Our G10 FX Strategy desk has been EUR and remains short
EURNOK as their preferred expression. On the eFX trading desk volumes led in EURNOK, USDCAD, and EURHUF while net
flows were skewed toward CAD after the BoC hiked rates 25bps, in line with market expectations but to the surprise of many
economists. Outflows led in HUF and AUD.
Commodities: WTI settled at $101, while Brent was little changed at $114, with the spread between the two widening on Gulf
Coast pipeline pressures. European TTF gas surged more than 5% through €100 for the first time since December 2022,
driven by rising heating demand and low European stockpiles. Gold traded higher to $4,066 (+0.2%), recovering from earlier
losses after the Fed held rates and offered no clear signal on December.
