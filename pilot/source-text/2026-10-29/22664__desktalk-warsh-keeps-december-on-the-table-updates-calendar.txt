DeskTalk - Warsh keeps December on the table, updates
calendar
By Preston Pagli
The FOMC left rates unchanged as expected in a 10-2 vote, with Fed governor Lisa Cook and the Cleveland Fed's
Hammack dissenting in favor of a 25bp rate hike.
The policy statement was largely unchanged, with the primary adjustment being an upgrade to the characterization of
economic activity, which was described as "strong" rather than "solid." The committee also said the accumulation of more
information will help the committee decide the policy stance.
In the press conference, Fed chair Warsh re-iterated productivity is strong and capital investment remains robust, while
inflation remains elevated. Warsh characterized growth as accelerating and broadening. On financial conditions, Warsh
alluded to the fact they have tightened, but was unwilling to call them "tight". The most dovish highlight of the event was
Warsh saying that most at the Fed do not think the economy is running hot. 
Warsh also unveiled a revised FOMC meeting structure for 2027 and 2028. While the Committee will continue to meet eight
times per year, one meeting in 2027 and two meetings in 2028 will be designated as strategy meetings and will not include a
policy rate decision.
US rates rallied during the press conference with front-end yields as much as ~10bps before retracing modestly while the
broad dollar index softened (DXY -0.25%). Headline equity indices remained mostly unchanged.
