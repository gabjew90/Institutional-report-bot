Cross-Asset: We remain modestly pro-risk in our asset allocation: OW Equities for 3m/12m, balanced by
UW Credit (for 3m/12m), OW Cash for 3m and OW Bonds for 12m. AI concerns, Middle East tensions

Global Macro: Global growth has been remarkably unremarkable in 2026, with fiscal easing and the AI
boom offsetting drags from energy and tariffs. Our baseline forecast is a similar pace in 2027, but
downside risks are building because of higher energy prices, higher bond yields, and the prospect of
slower AI investment growth next year. With AI labor market effects becoming more visible and inflation
likely to decline, we expect fewer rate hikes than priced for most major DM central banks.
Gjensidige (DG to N / PT NKK 273 / 5% Upside): We expect the pricing environment to normalise as
claims inflation moderates. While the earn-through of prior pricing actions should continue to support
profitability, we expect the pace of further margin expansion to become more measured from here.
Reflecting lower growth and margin expansion, we reduce our FY27e-30e EPS by c.2.4% on average.
