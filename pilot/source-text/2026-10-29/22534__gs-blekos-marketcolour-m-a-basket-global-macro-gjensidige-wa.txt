Good Morning,
US equities finished Monday higher: SPX +70bps to 7676 (7/11 sectors +ve, Consumer Disc. +1.11%, Industrials
+1.40%, Communication Svcs. -1.42%), NDX +109bps to 29273, RTY +38bps to 2786. Brasilian EWZ -20% having
its worst session outside of covid after President Lula pulled off a comeback win yesterday in the Brazil election
(BRLUSD down another 9%). Breadth recovered showing positive signs of stabilization in the AI trade with the GS
Data Center Basket (GSTMTDAT +2.60%), and The AI Semis basket (GSCBSMHX +2.52%). In Europe, EU Defense
(GSSBDEFE +2.4%), EU Semis (GSSBSEMI +2.3%) caught a bid. French Banks (GSXEFRBK +2%) stabilised on the
Moody's relief.
 
Interesting: Since 2024, ATSs (alternative trading systems = dark pools) have captured an incremental +2.3% of U.S.
equities market share on a per share basis, and +3.5% in additional market share on a notional basis. This contrasts
with OTC activity; since 2024, OTC market share gains have been negligible at +0.1% on a per share basis, and the
segment has lost (2.8%) notional market share over that same period
 
Flow: Still little signs of offense being played across AI / Tech. Bounces we've seen over the last 2 weeks are mostly
driven by cessation of high velocity supply.
 
M&A Basket / GSTRACQN: Additions including Abivax, Haleon, Moncler, Burberry, Prada, Swatch Group, Rightmove
Plc, Scout24 and Zegona. c.65% of the basket comprises Industrials, Consumer Discretionary and Healthcare stocks,
with c.35% UK-domiciled. Link
 
Cross-Asset: We remain modestly pro-risk in our asset allocation: OW Equities for 3m/12m, balanced by UW
Credit (for 3m/12m), OW Cash for 3m and OW Bonds for 12m. AI concerns, Middle East tensions and mid-term
elections could sustain near-term volatility. That said, we think earnings support, lighter positioning and lower
valuations should help equities into year-end. Link
 
Global Macro: Global growth has been remarkably unremarkable in 2026, with fiscal easing and the AI boom
offsetting drags from energy and tariffs. Our baseline forecast is a similar pace in 2027, but downside risks are
building because of higher energy prices, higher bond yields, and the prospect of slower AI investment growth next
year. With AI labor market effects becoming more visible and inflation likely to decline, we expect fewer rate hikes than
priced for most major DM central banks. Link
 
Gjensidige (DG to N / PT NKK 273 / 5% Upside): We expect the pricing environment to normalise as claims inflation
moderates. While the earn-through of prior pricing actions should continue to support profitability, we expect the pace
of further margin expansion to become more measured from here. Reflecting lower growth and margin expansion, we
reduce our FY27e-30e EPS by c.2.4% on average. Link
 
Wartsila: orders beat, p&l in-line - Order growth +45% vs consensus +33%, organic growth +16% vs consensus
+13%, but reported sales only in-line and margins at 14.2% are a 10bps above consensus 14.1%, driving a 1% beat.
The orders beat is entirely driven by a massive result in Energy, whilst Marine was weaker than expected due to

Equipment orders. Guidance is unchanged from last quarter, with demand on next 12 months expected to be stable at
these high levels.
 
Allianz pre (BUY / PT EUR465 / 10% Upside): Allianz reports its 3Q'26 results on November 12th. Overall, our 3Q'26
operating profit estimate is c.1% below company-collected consensus (published 26/10/26) primarily driven by P&C
(-2%) and Asset Management (-2%). Our lower P&C operating profit estimate is underpinned by a c.15bps less
favourable headline COR and c.2% lower Insurance Revenue, where we expect the focus to be on the rate / volume
trends in both Commercial and Retail lines. Link
 
ORANGE: beat and raises EBITDA guide keeps the OCF beat for Q4 to potentially have four quarters of beat & raise it
seems. Topline beats at €12.19B driven by both Spain 1.9% ahead and France 1.1% ahead). EBITDAaL 0.9% beat at
€4.14B (+4.6%) and slightly higher capex means OFCF is 1% ahead.
 
Apple pre (Buy / PT USD390 / 15% Upside): We expect AAPL to deliver in-line F4Q26 results, which including (1)
iPhone revenue growth of +15% yoy (2) Services revenue growth of +10% yoy (3) gross margins of 47.8%. Link
