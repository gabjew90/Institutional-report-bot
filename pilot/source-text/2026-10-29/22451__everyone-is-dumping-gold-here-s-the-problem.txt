The squeeze setup
Gold is testing critical levels after a brutal washout. ETF outflows are accelerating, CTAs are running
sizable shorts, and the potential for a sharp positioning squeeze is building. The last time CTAs were
this bearish, gold surged. The setup is getting interesting, but the dollar remains the key obstacle to a
sustained rebound.
The big levels
The $4,000 level is absolutely critical for gold, having held as support on several occasions this
summer. Gold is now trading well below its 200-day moving average, with initial resistance around
$4,125, right where the 21-day moving average comes in. A big technical test for the bulls.

Selling the gold lows?
GLD just recorded its biggest weekly outflow since March. Investors are heading for the exits after the

The CTA short
CTAs are running sizable gold shorts. The last time positioning was this bearish was just ahead of the
brutal short squeeze in August. History doesn't have to repeat, but the setup is getting interesting
again.

Gold convexity
Goldman's CTA models suggest the potential for significant momentum-driven buying if gold starts
recovering. With systematic positioning already heavily short, a sustained reversal could force CTAs to
cover shorts and eventually flip into buyers. The positioning that amplified the selloff could become
fuel for the next squeeze.
GS
Don't forget the dollar
Before getting too excited about a gold bounce, keep a close eye on the dollar. The two have moved in
an almost perfect inverse relationship throughout the year. A sustained gold rebound may need the
dollar to roll over first.
