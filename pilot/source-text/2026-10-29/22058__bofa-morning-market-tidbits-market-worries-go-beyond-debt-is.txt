issuance—the bigger question is whether tech firms will be able to monetize their investments

Will firms be able to monetize their investments? 
The equity market is selling off in tandem with rising tensions in the Middle East and 
concerns around the AI trade, reportedly related to the need for further debt issuance to 
finance the investment boom. But the common narrative doesn’t add up. If concerns 
were primarily around debt issuance, long-end US rates would be selling off amid fears 
of increased supply. Instead, long-end rates are rallying, which is more indicative of a 
market that is also questioning the ability of tech firms to monetize their investments. 
The case for hikes will remain unless something breaks 
To make matters worse, French yields rose to their highest level since 2002 and are also 
contributing to the risk-off mood. Unfortunately, social unrest does not bode well for 
fiscal consolidation ahead of the 2027 presidential election. In the near term, recent 
market developments give some additional cover for the Fed not to surprise markets 
this week, but the case for further hikes will remain intact unless something breaks.
