The volatility explosion
Same Nasdaq levels, a very different volatility regime. Tech implied volatility has surged 40% in little
over a month, weekly NDX implied moves have doubled, and relative tech fear is approaching
historical extremes. Meanwhile, credit stress is feeding into volatility, downside skew is repricing, and
momentum traders are enduring a record year of violent swings. The indices may look relatively calm,
but beneath the surface, volatility is anything but.
Tech fear repriced
What a difference a month makes. Back in mid-September, tech volatility was depressed, with VXN
hovering around 20 despite NDX trading at roughly the same levels as today. Fast forward to now, and
VXN is near 28, with NDX essentially unchanged. Same index level, 40% higher implied volatility.

No more Teflon tech
Tech volatility has surged alongside widening hyperscaler CDS spreads, with VXN sharply
outperforming VIX. The repricing of AI credit risk is increasingly showing up in tech options, even as
headline equity indices remain relatively resilient.

Relative stress explodes
According to GS, last week's pain was concentrated in tech, triggering a sharp repricing of NDX
volatility relative to SPX. The NDX/SPX volatility spread surged to the 98th percentile of its five-year
range, with Thursday's widening the second largest in five years.

The revenge of the straddle
Equity volatility has picked up, but the repricing in NDX is particularly striking. In just a few weeks, the
weekly NDX straddle has gone from pricing roughly 1.5% moves to around 3%. That's a doubling of
implied weekly moves.
GS
Downside in fashion
SPX downside skew has surged, although client flows show less demand for downside protection than
expected. GS attributes this divergence to historically low net exposure, leaving investors with less
need to hedge. The repricing of downside risk is running ahead of actual hedging demand.

Exhausting
As GS's Garrett points out, few charts capture trader exhaustion better than the extreme moves in the
GS Momentum basket. The basket has recorded 30 daily moves of more than 5% in either direction in
2026, exceeding every other year in the dataset, including 2000, 2009 and 2020. And there are still
two months left in the year.
