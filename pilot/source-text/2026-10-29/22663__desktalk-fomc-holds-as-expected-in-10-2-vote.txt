DeskTalk - FOMC holds as expected in 10-2 vote
By Preston Pagli
The FOMC left rates unchanged as expected in a 10-2 vote, with Fed governor Lisa Cook and the Cleveland Fed's
Hammack dissenting in favor of a 25bp rate hike. 
The biggest surprise was the dissent from Cook. In contrast, many had expected Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan to favor
a hike. Our economists believe Logan's decision to support holding rates steady likely reflects a willingness to wait until
December before delivering further tightening, a modestly dovish signal relative to expectations.
The policy statement was largely unchanged, with the primary adjustment being an upgrade to the characterization of
economic activity, which was described as "strong" rather than "solid." The committee also said the accumulation of more
information will help the committee decide the policy stance.
The initial market reaction is mostly subdued with front-end yields unchanged while the dollar has firmed to start. Equity
markets remain positive with SPX +20bps and NDX +5bps, with the SPX seeking to snap a losing streak of the past 6
meetings. OIS odds have now put December at 87% from 83%.
We will wait for Chair Warsh's press conference at 2:30 for further commentary.
