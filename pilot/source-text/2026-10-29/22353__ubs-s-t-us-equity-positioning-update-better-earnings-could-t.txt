US Equity Positioning Update: Better
Earnings Could Trigger Short Squeeze In AI
Names
Conor Lyons
26-Oct-2026, 20:17 ET (EDT)
US hedge funds continued to de-risk last week, with the pressure concentrated in AI. Selling shifted from
Semis toward Hyperscalers, culminating in the strongest two-day reduction in Hyperscaler longs since covid,
before funds abruptly reversed course Friday. The broader positioning trend remains toward lower AI beta with
long exposure being cut alongside increased AI shorts. The reduction in long positioning and the surge in the
short side of exposure pressed Nets to the 17%tile on 1y lookback, exposing the potential for a short squeeze
on better-than-expected earnings in the coming days.
Composite manager positioning saw similar risk reduction, flipping to 0.5std underweight from last week’s
overweight. The systematic investor landscape continues to be split between CTA’s and Risk Control. CTAs
have been persistent sellers of equity exposure for weeks, and last week was no exception, reducing equity
exposure to the 11%tile. Risk Control, on the other hand, continues to hold exposures near the highs; despite
some vol flare up induced selling. Risk Control exposure is in the 93%tile; with a 2% decline in SPX estimated
to create $155bn of selling. Into month-end; Pension and Target Date funds are estimated to buy ~$30 bn of
equities, but only $3.7 bn of demand will be in US equities.
This week represents the busiest earnings week of the quarter and will likely determine the direction of travel
for equities in the short-term. The average option implied earnings move this season is 5.4%, which is below
last quarter, but in line with longer-term averages.  However, early reporters have been underperforming their
implied moves by the widest margin since covid. Tech names are implying an average earnings move of 7.3%,
the smallest earnings move in the past four quarters.
