PrimeView: Hedge Funds Reversed Course
And Rebuilt Hyperscaler Exposure on
Friday
Josh Guilliard
26-Oct-2026, 06:25 ET (EDT)
Hedge funds in the UBS PB book continued to aggressively de-gross AI exposure from Monday through
Thursday last week, although the composition of that de-risking shifted. While MTD activity had been
dominated by Semis de-risking, last week’s selling moved increasingly towards Hyperscalers. Hardware and
Memory, by contrast, saw modest long buying and short covering.
That completely reversed on Friday. Hedge funds fully re-grossed the Hyperscaler exposure they had
de-risked earlier in the week, funded by renewed de-grossing in Hardware and Memory (charts below).
These aggressive one- or two-day de-grossing episodes, immediately followed by an equally
aggressive buy-the-dip, are becoming increasingly common in our flow picture. Rebecca Cheong, UBS
Equity Derivatives Strategy, makes an excellent point on the structural forces behind this dynamic. There has
been a defining shift in the composition of market capital. Long-duration capital, including retail, retirement
and foreign investors, has grown alongside the proliferation of shorter-duration capital, notably hedge funds
and quants. The former increasingly anchors longer-term market trends, while the latter’s more opportunistic
trading style amplifies short-term volatility. The result: corrections are becoming more frequent and
sharper, but recoveries are also becoming faster and more persistent. Rebecca is thus bullish in the
medium term.
Performance took a hit in Europe, US and Japan last week with Equity L/S and Quant down in all three
regions. 
• L/S in Europe down 33bps last week, in US down 1.94% and in Japan down 2.3%.
• Friday was positive in US, +60bps for Eq LS.
• Quants down 71bps in Europe, 1% in US and flat in Japan last week.
China was a bright spot for Equity L/S managers who, after peak-to-trough losses of c.3.5% in Oct, are nearly
back to flat.
