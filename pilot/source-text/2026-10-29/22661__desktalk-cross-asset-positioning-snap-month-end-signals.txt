DeskTalk - Cross asset positioning snap, month-end signals
By Varshi Karamsetty
The blitz of catalysts has begun overnight with RBNZ and AU CPI. Most interesting positioning changes in FX where GBP
max shorts could be covered on a benign budget outcome. UST and EGB tactical positions have flipped long but structurally
short into FOMC, ECB. Nasdaq positioning has deteriorated but remains net long into tech megacap earnings tonight.
Equities: Nasdaq positioning has deteriorated after last week's selloff, but overall positioning remains net long. Averaged
unrealized losses are at -4.3%, -1.8% for S&P. The most significant positioning risk remains the highly profitable Russell 2000
short base, where profitability levels are near historical extremes. European banks increased net bearishness again with
$1.3bn shorts initiated amid ongoing French fiscal fears. In Asia, bearish activity continues with Hang Seng seeing the largest
outflows. Kospi flows were more balanced but positioning remains -2 on a -5/5 scale. 
CTA signals for S&P to flip short or move back max long remain close to current levels (1%) at 7656 (-1%) and 7761 (7735
ESZ6), respectively. While CTAs are moderately short Nasdaq, signal to add shorts are far away at 28533. CTAs are ‘max
short’ Russell for the third week and the level for the funds to cover their shorts is at 2849. 
Rates: David Bieber highlights the biggest change has been markets flipping into longs tactically in both USTs and EGBs but
remain structurally short. Critical level is at TY 105-24 where tactical longs under-pressure vs resistance at 106-10 where
systematics are active. EGB core positioning is long outright but short spreads, with long side in focus.
FX: Latest positioning model shows that while USD saw net leveraged selling, this was mostly offset by RMs. CFPI remains
+7.7 out of max 10. Historical patterns suggest recent leveraged selling could drive further outflows from other client
segments, regardless of upcoming events. GBP max short positioning has eased amid HF buying, moving their net position
neutral. An in-line budget could drive further inflows especially into an expected Nov MPC hike as well. EUR leveraged short
positioning has extended, with RMs potentially following post ECB. CAD shorts have reduced as well into an uncertain BoC
decision. 
Commodities: CTA desk model shows the majority of energy complex carrying long signals (nat gas is max short). Precious
metals remain in max short territory. For Oil, the CTA sell level to trim their longs is over 16% lower than current levels at
84.49 (CLZ6 ref 100.85). Meanwhile CTA signals to cover shorts in gold and silver are 7.4% and 18% higher than current
levels, respectively.
N.B. Month-end takes place this Friday. Preliminary models point to modest USD selling from FX hedging flows. Across asset
classes, October's gains in FI and underperformance in equities generate offsetting rebalancing signals, with the strongest
flow pointing to buying back into underperforming Asian equities (+1.9).
