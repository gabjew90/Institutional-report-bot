Privo on risk: We’ve stabilized in front end rates, objectively good for risk. Energy remains too high for
comfort, but that’s more likely to produce chop than a sustained selloff absent another escalation. Credit is
the one to worry about. Still flashing yellow to red relative to equities. We need credit to calm down, and
if it does, I think that could be a meaningful catalyst for a broader rally. Hyperscalers + Fed are the main
event this week. As outlined yesterday, focus on four things. Capex: flat/up is fine, down would be
disastrous for picks and shovels. Backlog: how quickly is it converting into revenue? FCF: how much of
that revenue is actually translating into cash generation in the out quarters? Financing: how do they fund

the next leg, and do they increasingly choose equity over debt given the strength of their underlying
businesses? These are the questions that matter far more than another round of headline beats. Flows were
a little more supportive yesterday. Having been net sellers of tech for much of the past few weeks, we
finally saw some client demand engaging on the other side (at least in Europe). Feels like the modal view
remains that we chop around until we clear next week’s political events. Optimism in pockets, but
vigilance still warranted.
3 points from earnings so far:
1. Defense is catching a bid – not just as defensives in our view and not positioning related. LMT –
consensus long up mid single digits on a clear report. NOC – consensus short – up similar. We believe the
underperformance since the excitement earlier in the year, combined with a clean set of results (so far) and
progress on agreements that could lead to uptick in volumes going forward, as well as the midterms that
are right in front create a favorable backdrop into year end. Watching GD and LHX this week – the latter
of which has particularly low expectations.
2. Non-residential construction sticks out favorably. Data center helping no doubt but some of the troubled
areas of the last few years whether it’s warehouse, retail, commercial office (amongst others) appear to be
getting better or less worse. URI commentary, ALLE organic growth and FAST all supportive. We think
this could set up CAT well this week tactically after a large sell off. As an aside, heading into November
with seasonality on the side of resi names – ALLE is now the top member in the homebuilding index. Yes,
you read that correctly.
3. Europe reads have been obvious – we generally think the moves have happened – and would suggest
the other side could make more sense for prints. ATCOA – IR, ELUXB SS – WHR, SCHN SW – OTIS to
name a few.
