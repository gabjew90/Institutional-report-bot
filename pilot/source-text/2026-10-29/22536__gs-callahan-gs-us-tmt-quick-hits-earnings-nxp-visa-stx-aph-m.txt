GS US TMT .. quick hits ..
While market breadth remains narrow (~60% of S&P names lower on the day), as we settle
back into the familiar “AI vs the rest” trade today, the NDX is up +135 bps on the day and
trying to turn in “back-to-back” >1% daily gains for the 1x in nearly ~3-months  …
… in terms of how it feels on there -- on the flow side, our TMT trader (P. Bartlett) notes
that it still feels like supply-abatement is driving most of this bounce as we've started to
put some of the high-velocity supply / length reduction in the review mirror, it does feel
like this tape is forcing the issue re: the potential for the ‘bounce’ to turn into a ‘rally’ as a
few headwinds ease and/or turn into tailwinds (positioning / valuations, earnings t+1
reactions, financing, Rates angst, etc) ahead of FOMC tmrw, Mag 7 earnings THE next few
days and the US Mid-term Election next week ..
… just looking at the board today into year-end, it really stands out that a number of
Software / Services names are (quietly) back near ~52wk highs (Payroll names, Fin / Biz
Services, pockets of SaaS) vs. a number of AI / Supply Chain names down ~30-50% from
YTD highs and sporting challenging looking charts (below trend lines / 200-dma’s) --  is this
a ‘tell’ or an ‘opportunity’ into 2027? ..
Illustrative of this, the chart below = YTD normalized performance of the SPX ex-AI index
(white) vs the NDX (blue) – the former (SPXXAI) reeling following a ‘test’ of its all-time-
highs, while, the latter (NDX), is bouncing off the lows of its Summer trading range.
