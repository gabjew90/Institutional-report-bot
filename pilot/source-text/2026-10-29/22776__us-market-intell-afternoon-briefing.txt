FED DAY VIEWS FROM MKT INTEL – [We share the comments below immediately after the 
press conference.] The question remains as to why the Fed held when growth has accelerated to 
likely an above-trend pace, aligning with the last 2 Flash PMIs reports indicating ~5% real GDP 
growth pace within increasing inflationary inputs. Small business surveys point to a desire to hire 
workers despite headwinds from tariffs, energy, and AI uncertainty (time it takes to replace 
workers).  One explanation is the focus on financial conditions tightening, incrementally, with an 
expectations of further tightening primarily via credit markets as a reason for fewer hikes. 
Regardless, this is a dovish outcome. The next rungs on the wall of worry are GOOG/MSFT 
tonight and AMZN tmrw and then we move to next week’s midterm elections where some point 

 
 
 
 
 
 
to an AI sell-off in the event of a Blue Wave … we would be buyers of any dips as we see the 
primary risk stemming from a spike in kinetic conflict in the Middle East. There has been chatter 
of a 3-day war, but Iran has indicated that it would likely target Saudi / UAE energy infra at a time 
when the SPR is at multi-decade lows. The worst case scenario would be that conflict escalating 
and Russian refining capacity getting hit, again. So, we buy Tech / AI, plan for some broadening 
but hedge war risk via longs in crude and equity vol.

US MKT INTEL VIEW – We published below in the October 26th Morning Briefing 
We remain Tactically Bullish. The SPX has lost 2.4% over the last 2 weeks, including a shift toward 
defensive sectors. MTD, SPX is -36bp with 8 / 11 sectors positive and Energy, Staples, Utilities, HC, 
Financials, Materials, RE, and Discretionary all beating the index. What makes us think that stocks will 
move higher? Generally speaking, an economy growing at/above trend GDP and positive earnings 
growth produces a bull market. Flash PMIs, for the second consecutive month, point to an economy 
growing ~5% annualized, in real terms. So far, about a quarter of the SPX has reported earnings with no 
dips to the pre-earnings season forecast (~13% revenue growth, ~30% EPS growth, and ~15% margins 
… full details in the Additional Thoughts section). So that is the basis for the view. A refocus on macro 
and micro fundamentals, rather than AI fears, should be supportive of stocks. That said, the signal is 
less clear as we have yet to have a complete positioning washout despite sentiment being firmly in the 
Fear region.
