Sales & Trading Commentary For Institutional Use Only Not a Product of Research
Macro and derivs trades over the last week:
• Week's flows dominated by positioning-driven unwinds in Equities with UBS PB deleveraging indicators
flashing red across regions.
• Selling very focused in Tech but is now being led by Hyperscalers as opposed to Semis in previous
weeks, actually saw small net buying in Memory and Tech Hardware where positioning was already low.
• Interestingly, a lot of the incremental shorts being added on PB desk are on AI Tech names which is new.
• AI Industrials also under selling pressure while more defensive sectors saw buying (some of which
covering), such as Healthcare, Defense, Packaging, Staples.
• On the back of the squeeze higher in staples, saw multiple XLP downside trades looking to fade the move.
• Also saw AI unwinds across Japan and Taiwan. Japan in particular continues to see large persistent HF
degrossing, led by Tech.
• Korea Tech also sold but magnitude of selling was much smaller than previous weeks and Korea Memory
flows were actually mixed (buying SEC / selling Hynix), highlighting the extent to which Korea positioning
had already been reduced.
• By contrast, China Internet names continued to see selling on cash pad despite already very low
positioning, led by names with elevated AI capex spend.
• China onshore also under pressure prompting more buying by National Team, end-of-week saw a pick-up in
CSI call option trades, mainly on CSI300 as index is more defensive, outperf levels are elevated and vols are
low.
• FX saw a bit of spill-over from risk-off moves with USD-upside demand, EUR selling and CHF buying but
also saw buying of GBP and AUD over the week.
• One persistent trade was USDCNH downside, saw demand throughout the week for downside digits and put
spreads to play a continued appreciation.
• Rates reaction much more acute than in FX, the move on Thursday drove a surge in outright Treasury
calls with skew seeing a significant shift towards calls.
• There was also a lot of demand for USD front-end upside but preference remains for call spreads i.e.
looking for a more moderate unwind of Fed hikes being priced in as opposed to back-end where investors
more willing to position for a potentially large rates rally.

• Europe rates flows more mixed though with both downside and upside EURIBOR trades going through,
highlighting the continued fiscal concerns.
• Finally, Brazil equity flows turned slightly more cautious ahead of 2nd round on Sunday with some
trimming on long equity side unwinds of EWZ upside but in general, felt like clients were willing to keep upside
positioning despite the already strong rally and close polling.
