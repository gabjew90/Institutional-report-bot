The GS Equity Fundamental L/S Performance Estimate fell -1.48% between 10/30 and 11/5 (vs MSCI
World TR -3.55%), driven by beta of -2.07%, partially offset by alpha of +0.60% on the back of long side
gains.
 
The GS Equity Systematic L/S Performance Estimate fell -0.61% during the same period, driven by
alpha of -1% on the back of short side losses, partially offset by beta of +0.39%.
Overall book Gross leverage +0.1 pts to 314.2% (85th percentile 1-year) and Net leverage -1.8 pts to
71.8% (4th percentile 1-year). Overall book L/S ratio -1.2% to 1.592 (3rd percentile 1-year).
•  Fundamental L/S Gross leverage -6.2 pts to 205.8% (19th percentile 1-year) and Net leverage -4.1 pts to
45.3% (2nd percentile 5-year). 
• Beta Adjusted Fundamental L/S Gross leverage -13.1 pts to 216.5% (22nd percentile 3-year) and Beta
Adjusted Net leverage -7.6 pts to 57.2% (4th percentile 3-year).
Global equities were net sold (-1.0 SDs 1-year) as the pace of de-grossing increased (-0.8 SDs 1-year,
largest $ de-grossing since July), driven by long sales outpacing short covers (5 to 1). De-grossing
activity was led in $ terms by Europe and DM Asia.
• All regions (sans DM Asia) were net sold, led by EM Asia and North America.
• Single Stocks were net sold for an 8th straight week, driven by long sales, while Macro Products were net
bought for a 3rd straight week, driven by long buys and to a lesser extent short covers.
• 8 of 11 global sectors were net sold, led by Info Tech, Industrials, and Energy, while Real Estate, Utilities, and
Materials were the only net bought.
 

EM Asia was net sold for an 8th straight week, while DM Asia saw the largest net buying in 6 months.
 
 
 
 
Global Industrials saw the largest net selling in over 5 years.
