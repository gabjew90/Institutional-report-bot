US EQUITIES COLOR: TAPE WATCHING
U.S. Equities Color
6 Nov 2026
Mkt bounced today with Semis up ~3.5% which felt ‘healthy’ and not just a beta squeeze. Activity levels were
extremely quiet in HT with inbounds very focused on feeling out sentiment and not much follow through
beyond that into the weekend. The flow we did see appeared more constructive with L/O tickets balanced
within TMT, with tails of Semis supply paired with a few green shoots of demand vs a much clearer sell skew
of “All Things AI” yesterday. HFs finished net buyers today, driven by a broad cover bid across sectors /
macro expressions as it was another large top-down session with ETF % eclipsing 37% of tape today (vs
28% ytd avg). 
 
Next Week: we’ll get a mix of macro (CPI Tuesday & PPI Friday) + micro (Monday: CEG, CNH, FERG, KKR,
AAON, HRB, OXY, SANM, SKYD, TTD, and TTWO; Tuesday: CRWV; Thurs: AMAT, CBRS, and CSCO). The
straddle to next Friday's close went out at 2.48%.  
   
Prime: US equities were modestly net sold this week, driven by the largest single stock net selling in 4
months partially offset by buying in Macro Products. Info Tech was by far the most $ net sold global (and US)
sector - this week’s % selling in global Tech stocks was ~on par with the largest net selling weeks on our
record (since 2016). Also worth noting that 4 the top 5 net selling weeks in global Info Tech on our record
have occurred within the past five months.
 
Risk Exposures (as of COB Nov 5th): US Fundamental L/S Gross leverage fell -8.7 pts – the largest
weekly decline since Covid (March ’20) – to 207.5% (26th percentile one-year), while US Fundamental L/S
Net leverage fell -3.7pts to 43.3% (0th percentile five-year).
