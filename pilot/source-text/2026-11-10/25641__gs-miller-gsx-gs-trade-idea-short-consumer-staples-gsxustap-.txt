[GSX] GS Trade Idea - Short Consumer Staples (GSXUSTAP)
Louis Miller
6 Nov 2026

We like shorting our Diversified Staples Basket (GSXUSTAP). Earnings yield and
dividend yield of the basket is unattractive relative to fixed income (treasuries,
credit, etc.) and there is not enough growth in the complex to justify lofty
valuations. Earnings revisions have been rolling over and valuations are now in
the 90th percentile relative to the last year. 
The valuation premium of S&P Staples to SPX is at the highest level in 10+ year while
the Staples Basket (GSXUSTAP) Div Yield of 2.84% is 2.05pp below the 10y, the 1st
percentile of the last 10 years. GSXUSTAP has outperformed SPX by nearly 20% in the
last month and is now up 1% more than SPX over the last year. Historically, Staples
have lagged in the 3m following similar events with the exception of late 2021/early
2022.
We like this trade delta one because, with rates where they are, you get paid to be short
on swap while puts look rich and put skew is near the lows.
Staples have significantly outperformed SPX – historically, the next 3 months
have had a negative relative return
