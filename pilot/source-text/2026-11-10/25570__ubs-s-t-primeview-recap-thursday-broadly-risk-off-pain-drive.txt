PrimeView (Recap): Thursday Broadly Risk
Off; Pain Driven by AI Beta, Only 7th Lgst
Degross
Josh Guilliard
06-Nov-2026, 05:15 ET (EST)
Yesterday was the largest tech sell-off since Covid, but not in terms of hedge fund flows. Hedge fund
sells outweighed buys almost 6x and HF cut 1.1std of US gross – but were unlikely the driver of Thursday’s
move and the degrossing was only the 7th largest degross day since 1 Oct. For comparison, Oct 28th was
a -2.3std degross day, Oct 29th was -2.1 and Oct 21st was -2.2.
The US selling yesterday was driven more by selling of broader AI-related beta (by macro, trend following
systematics, retail) rather than cutting of favoured single stock trades (by equity LS). Put differently –
attribution analysis shows hedge funds were making money on relative trades within pockets (Neoclouds,
Optical & Networking) despite the overall baskets being down significantly. This lines up with the selling we
observed in October shifting from broad long selling to healthy RV stock picking within the baskets: aggregate
performance estimates for the US show +27bps for Equity L/S managers yesterday and quants down
49bps. Intraday in the US the crowded L-S trade was up 2.5% at peak and closed up 1.2%.
US selling yesterday was concentrated to the crowded AI Winners (-1.1std in AI Winenrs and -0.9std in
Crowded Longs) and Hyperscalers (-1.2std). Semis selling was moderate (-0.4std long selling yesterday).
Flows were muted in Memory names (despite the -16.6% move) and in Hardware names. To reiterate, the
muted flows are on the back of hedge funds having de-risked the AI value chain since the summer and
focusing on single stock relative value within AI names, which is evident in how the most liquid names were
cut in the de-risking rather than the most crowded. The outsized cutting of liquid names is consistent with
what we have previously published in our deleveraging analysis. Buy flow was weak, with US Pharma the
most bought in the US at +2std, but only +13bps on an absolute basis.
In Europe, hedge fund flows skewed to buy with +0.9std long buying concentrated to dip-buying in Banks
(+2.6std) and Autos (+3.0std). Intraday today in Europe, Reinsurance is down significantly but crowded
longs are outperforming shorts in Reinsurance by 2.5% (crowding t-stat of 12) whereas there is modest pain
for those L-S in Airlines (-76bps, T-stat 3.7).
Quant strategies suffered yesterday, posting losses globally (table below).
