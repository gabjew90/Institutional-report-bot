The great positioning flush
The week's market turmoil triggered one of the sharpest positioning resets in years, with hedge funds
aggressively cutting exposure and broader equity positioning turning underweight. Here are the charts
showing the scale of the unwind — and how far positioning has diverged from the earnings backdrop.
Forced selling hits a record
Barclays estimates that the latest selloff triggered the largest single-day selling requirement from
leveraged ETF rebalancing on record. Another reminder of how quickly mechanical flows can amplify
market moves.
Barclays
AI gets dumped
Goldman reports that hedge funds sold AI stocks at their fastest pace in more than five years. On a
rolling 20-day basis, selling reached a five-year extreme of three standard deviations below normal.

From crowded to deserted
Deutsche Bank's measure of megacap growth and technology positioning has collapsed to the 7th
percentile of its historical range. One of the market's favorite trades has become decidedly unloved.

Outright negative growth
At current levels, tech positioning is in line with not only a sharp deceleration but also outright
negative growth and large estimate cuts.

Leverage keeps falling
JPM reports that hedge-fund net leverage dropped another 4% last week, approaching 12-month lows,
while equity long/short net exposure has fallen to three-year lows.
JPM PI
The biggest flush since Wuhan
Goldman reports that US fundamental long/short gross leverage fell 8.7 points in a single week, the
largest weekly reduction since the March 2020 Covid crash. The scale of the deleveraging is becoming
historic.

GS Prime
Back to zero
Goldman reports that US fundamental long/short net leverage fell 3.7 points last week, the largest
decline since Liberation Day, leaving positioning at the 0th percentile of its five-year range.
GS Prime
Momentum gets washed out
Global hedge-fund exposure to momentum has fallen toward three-year lows, according to JPM. With
positioning now close to neutral and the six-month positioning/price indicator back near its summer
lows, the unwind may be approaching exhaustion.
