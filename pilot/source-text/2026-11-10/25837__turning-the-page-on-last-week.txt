S&P 500’s implied move through this Friday is 2.29%. Macro focus for the week will be CPI tomorrow
(GIR at +16bps for Core MoM). Last Friday’s strong jobs print (+165k vs ~65k expected) calmed the
growth scare narrative but has now pushed the December hike odds to >80%.
Since the start of October, GS clients have now net sold -$30b of tech stocks (TMT + Comm Services) on
our U.S. trading desk (-$6b of this was last week). For context, during July’s momentum drawdown our
clients net sold -$23b of tech names. On the green shoots front, Friday (11/6) was the first day in 7 trading
sessions our desk ended with a buyskew in tech (+$1b)...
Investors are in the process of re-calibrating datacenter build expectations on the back of last week’s mid-
term Election (especially in TX and OH). Investor feedback suggests current market prices are implying
limited y/y capex (or GW) growth in 2028 -- well below prior market expectations for 30-40%+ y/y
growth in 2028 … a level in markets that a lot of our clients believe is an attractive entry point (yes, I am
also in this camp).
