the GS Weekend Macro Call (listen here Alec Phillips (Chief US Political Economist) on the implications
for the AI Build-Out: The big surprise out of election night for the data centre and AI issue was not really

moratoria on permitting… with gubernatorial wins in Texas, Georgia and Ohio, in theory up to around
40% of planned capacity is impacted… I think we could end up with a temporary moratorium that lasts
several months until new rules can be put in place.
