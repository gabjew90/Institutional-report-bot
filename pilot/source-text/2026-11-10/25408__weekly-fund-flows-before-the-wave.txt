Global fund flows, week ending November 4 
Flows into mutual funds and related investment products were positive across 
n
both equities and fixed income. 
Global equity funds saw net inflows in the week ending November 4 (+$89bn vs 
n
+$56bn in the previous week). DM saw continued strength in net inflows driven 
by US and Japan funds. EM also saw meaningful net inflows driven by continued 
domestic inflows to Mainland China funds. At the sector level, technology funds 
once again saw the largest net inflows despite recent AI-driven equity volatility, 
though these data do not capture the extent of the moves in the latter half of the 
week following the US midterm elections. Telecom funds saw the largest net 
outflows.  
Flows into global fixed income funds remained well-supported across fund type.  
n
Long-duration, short-duration, and inflation-protected bond funds saw inflows 
in the week ending November 4. In EM, hard-currency bond funds saw continued 
net outflows while local-currency bond funds saw net inflows. Money market 
fund assets increased by $140bn. 
Cross-border FX flows were positive and remained well-supported in G10 and 
n
Asia driven by flows into USD and CNY. The pace of flows into USD has seen a 
re-acceleration this year after a more subdued pace in 2025 (see Chart of the 
Week).
