Tactical Takes: More De-grossing and HF Pain
John Schlegel  ·  J.P. Morgan  ·  Positioning Intelligence
Fri 6 Nov 2026, 10:09am ET
Summary: As US stocks reversed lower on Thurs, the declines were sharpest in Tech, but broad enough to
drive negative returns across HF strategies. The global HF return was greater than a 3z decline yesterday
and marked a continuation of the challenges that HFs have seen in the past month. The strong de-grossing
continued for a 4th day and the acceleration this week comes after persistent de-grossing over the past few
weeks. Below are a few quick takes on recent changes...we'll have more in our Weekly Wrap later today.
1. HF performance hit hard on Thurs, but most of it beta-related (at least in US): Across HF strategies, we
see them down -1.6% globally on Thurs, putting them down about -1.8% MTD. The 1d return is the worst
since Apr 4, 2025 and the 3m net return is now at a -3z level, in line with early Apr 2025 as well.
Performance is varied across regions and strategies, but most figures are around -1 to -2% MTD. It seems
like alpha is most negative for Quants in N. Am., Multi-Strats in EMEA, and Eq L/S in APAC.
2. HF Flows: De-grossing, but Net Flows Flat: we saw a 4th day in a row of ~1.5z de-grossing on
Thursday, which makes this look quite similar to late July/early Aug. However, net flows have been
relatively paired off as both longs and shorts are reduced. In terms of rolling gross flows, Mult-Strat de-
grossing continues to persist and the 20d de-gross is now larger than any other period outside of Covid.
3. US TPM falls, but not yet at a clear "attractive" set-up for US stocks: Given the retail selling and
general de-risking, our US Tactical Positioning Monitor (TPM) saw a -1.7z 1wk change, which put the
level of positioning back to the 20th %-tile since 2015-similar to lows in March and July of this year.
However, the speed of the recent decline has been somewhat limited (as much of the recent pain has been
more rotational than broad index until yest). Hence the 4wk change is only -0.5z (on 3d avg), whereas the
trigger when this is attractive is -1.5z.
4. Semis the clear group sold: no surprise here, but Semis has seen very strong net selling lately with
Retail selling the group and other AI-related industries yest, while HFs have net sold Semis at a nearly 4z
level over the past month (though we saw HFs' Semis flows more net neutral yest, post large selling on
Wed). Furthermore, the selling of Semis by HFs on a rolling 1m basis is now back at July '26 level, while
on a 2m and 3m basis, the selling is around a 4z level (which is something we haven't seen in past data).
