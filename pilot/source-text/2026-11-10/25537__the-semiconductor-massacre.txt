Know your semis vol
Semiconductor volatility is exploding, with VXSMH approaching its yearly highs, currently around 64. At
current levels, VXSMH implies daily moves of roughly 4% going forward. Extreme. Back in October
(here), we highlighted how the divergence between KOSPI VIX and VXSMH had become unsustainable.
Korea was pricing in panic, while US semiconductors remained remarkably calm. That disconnect has
now closed. For those who played our long semis vol logic, it is time to say thank you and move on.

SOXLed
SOXL is back to April levels, leaving many scratching their heads, especially as SOX remains well above
those levels. Leveraged ETFs are still widely misunderstood by market punters. Leverage works both
ways, and daily resets and volatility drag can be brutal. These products need to be properly
understood before being traded.
