Research in focus this morning
 
China Industrial Tech.  2027 Outlook: Valuation Reset and Sector Preferences. We refresh our 2027 outlook for the
sector, favoring the AI robotics supply chain driven by frontier-model commercialization and resilient automation
demand, alongside Data Center Electricals where overseas progress remains solid despite a delayed 800VDC mass
adoption to 2028. We forecast China grid investment to grow 6% year-over-year in 2027 with increasing emphasis on
UHV, while cutting 2026-2030 sector EPS estimates by 5-10% on average, primarily across battery and energy
storage names due to raw-material inflation and margin pressures. We highlight preferred names including Inovance,
Shuanghuan, Sanhua, Kstar, Envicool, Hongfa, Sieyuan, Huaming, and Nari Tech, and we upgrade Jianghai
(002484.SZ) to Buy with a 12m TP of Rmb43.9 driven by an increasing AI product mix and solid earnings visibility. 
 
Nintendo. 2Q profit miss on softer hardware sell-in; lowering OP estimates – Buy. We maintain our ¥9,800 12m TP
following an underwhelming 2Q operating profit of ¥73.9 bn, which missed expectations as Nintendo Switch 2
hardware sell-in lacked momentum despite a robust software beat. Consequently, we lower our FY27E operating
profit estimate by 6% to ¥401.8 bn and cut our FY28E–FY31E estimates by 2-7%, reflecting reduced hardware
shipment assumptions partially offset by higher software tie ratios. While hardware penetration has been more

gradual than anticipated following recent price hikes, we expect active consoles and earnings to enter a renewed
growth phase as the dedicated software lineup expands. 
 
Sony.  2Q beat and raised guidance on entertainment strength – Buy. Sony reported a strong 2Q operating profit
beat at ¥622.3 billion, driven by record box office for Spider-Man: Brand New Day and robust PlayStation
engagement. Management raised FY3/27 operating profit guidance to ¥1,870 billion, prompting us to increase our
FY3/27–FY3/31 operating profit estimates by 6% to 9% to reflect a revised ¥160/USD forex assumption and the
stronger entertainment and I&SS outlook. We anticipate continued earnings expansion fueled by deepening
PlayStation monetization, Crunchyroll growth, and cross-segment synergies, and accordingly raise our 12m TP to
¥4,800.
