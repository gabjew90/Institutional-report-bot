No-Micro, All-Macro Setup Has Investors Looking 
Underhedged 
 
BY TYLER DURDEN 
WEDNESDAY, SEP 02, 2026 - 08:25 PM 
Authored by Frank Monkam via Bloomberg, 
Cross-asset correlations are strengthening as macro catalysts - including this Friday’s nonfarm 
payrolls, the flare-ups in the Middle East and the global rates tantrum - pile up, and investors 
are looking underhedged heading into the fray. 
That’s especially true as we start the month with a thin dose of micro catalysts, having cleared 
the 2Q earnings season. 
The weeks ahead augur choppiness for markets, yet investors remain remarkably light on 
macro hedges across most major markets.  
In the equities space, the VIX is underperforming the VIXEQ index of volatility in individual 
names, albeit only at a -0.5 z-score. 
 


[Page 2]
 
Similarly, the MOVE index - a measure of bond-market volatility - sits at a 0.2 z score and a 
seasonal low. Both measures are on a one-year look-back. 
 
As oil and rates take the lead in driving macro price action, and their reaction functions 
propagate throughout markets, the cleanest hedge is the simplest hedge.  
Picture an insurance market that’s effectively still willing to write investors a 
property-and-casualty policy with storm clouds already overhead. 
At these prices, no need to figure out which window breaks first. You cut the check for the 
premium.
