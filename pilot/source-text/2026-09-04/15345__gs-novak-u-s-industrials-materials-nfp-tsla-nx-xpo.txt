All eyes on August payrolls – GS preview here: We estimate nonfarm payrolls rose by 40k in August,
somewhat below consensus of +55k. On the negative side, big data indicators of job growth slowed
sequentially. Additionally, August payrolls have exhibited a consistent negative bias in initial prints over
the last decade. On the positive side, layoffs remain low and there is potential scope for rebounds in
employment in leisure and hospitality and local educational payrolls after large declines over the past
couple of months. We estimate that the unemployment rate was unchanged on a rounded basis at 4.1% (in
line with consensus), reflecting the stabilization in continuing claims. We forecast a 0.4% month-over-
month increase in average hourly earnings, above consensus, reflecting positive calendar effects.

$497mm. EBITDA beat $73mm vs. $69mm. Volumes continue to track normal seasonality pattens and

TSLA – Mark Delaney out after Cybercab event – full note here: Tesla hosted a Cybercab launch event for
the media on September 3rd in Austin, and is now offering robotaxi rides in the Cybercab per posts on X.
Tesla commented that is has now completed 1 million miles of unsupervised robotaxi operation. The
company emphasized the low cost of Cybercab, aided in part by using its unboxed manufacturing
approach and camera-only sensor stack. In addition, Cybercab offers usability features, including media
and movie integration, and disability focused features such as braille.
Separately, Tesla also launched a form for operators to indicate interest in owning a fleet of Cybercabs,
operating a hub and infrastructure, or hosting a robotaxi event. Recall Tesla had previously noted that,
over time, it would expect consumers to be able to put their vehicles into the robotaxi fleet with Tesla
serving as the demand aggregation platform & fleet orchestrator (similar to a ridehail app). We also note
that the Tesla Robotaxi website now lists the Robovan as "arriving soon", though recall the company has
faced delays in their product timelines in the past.
Finally, per recent government filings, there are now 45 Cybercabs registered in Texas, out of 420 total
registered Tesla vehicles (which we note may not all be operational, could include test vehicles, and some
could be operated with a safety monitor).
