EMERGING MARKETS MONETIZATION – Ellen Wang / Victoria Campos 


[Page 14]
The global momentum/tech rebound drove EM 7% higher since the July-end low, 
despite choppy price action. Broadening and AI continue to dominate the narrative in 
EM, with the recent global macro backdrop net supportive. We are bullish on EM 
given (i) improving macro; (ii) weaker USD; (iii) AI CapEx tailwinds, but continue to 
expect divergence in EM performance at the country/sector level and view country-
relative trades as more relevant for the monetization strategy in this environment.  
•         EM MONETIZATION MENU – Key themes and trades include: (i) TACTICALLY 
LONG China H-shares over A-shares: the pair has been trading well in July given 
the momentum rotation. Despite this outperformance, H-shares still lag A-shares YTD 
and we see further room into China Tech earnings next week. (ii) We also express 
this view via a TACTICALLY LONG in China Internet (KWEB). (iii) TACTICALLY 
LONG Taiwan following the Global Tech selloff in July. Taiwan offers a less volatile 
exposure to AI and Asia Tech relative to Korea. 
• HEDGES – (i) We like long LatAm (ILF) as a diversifying play 
against weakness in the EM AI theme and for relative resilience 
against geopolitical risks. (ii) India (INDA) equity strength has been 
driven in part by a rotation during the AI de-risking phase, but foreign 
investor flows have also turned more supportive again, supported by 
improving macro. You may consider a L/S pair with LONG India and 
SHORT Southeast Asia (ASEA).

Report: Prime Time / Monthly Wrap | A Further Reset on Many Fronts 


[Page 15]
  
Brief Summary:  In many ways, August appears to have been a further reset 
following the July volatility.  HF performance and market returns rebounded, but in 
a modest fashion with continued chop underneath.  The choppy HF performance has 
likely been tied to the lingering de-grossing that we saw throughout much of Aug, but 
with seasonal trends suggesting re-grossing (even if only modest), it could support a 
better alpha environment going forward.  Relatedly, we continued to see Momentum 
reduced and a rotation out of Semis / AI winners into Software / AI laggards.  
Aggregate positioning moved back to neutral in most regions (e.g. US rebounded, 
while Japan fell a little).  Taken together, these suggest a lot more neutrality on 
many fronts, even if some of the prior skews still persist when looked at on a longer-
term basis. 
  
Key Points: 
  
• HF performance rebounded a little in Aug (+0.6%).  Asia was a small 
positive though alpha was mixed and crowded shorts were a drag in the US, 
particularly in HC and Tech.  
• Gross flows bottomed in the US: The de-grossing persisted through the first 
few weeks of Aug, before flipping to re-grossing over the last week.  Global 
gross flows were only -0.2z last month.  Seasonal trends suggest a further re-
gross. 
• US Positioning back to neutral (similar in other regions): Our US TPM’s 
4wk change rebounded back to +0.5z from a low of nearly -2z in late July.  In 
line with this, the level of US positioning is back to ~40th %-tile since 2015. It’s 
similar in other regions, while HF net leverage is also around the 50th %-tile on 
a 5yr basis.  While net positioning seems neutral, the near-term directional set-
up for stocks appears very mixed (Weekly Wrap on Aug 28) 


[Page 16]
• Momentum continued to be sold, though 1H build-up has now been 
reversed: Global net exposure to momentum is back to YTD lows and the 
buying we saw in the factor in US, EU, and Japan have been almost fully 
reversed.  However, performance going forward is still unclear (Weekly Wrap on 
Aug 21). 
• US Software still covered, while Cyclicals mostly sold: Combined (HF + 
Retail + ETF) flows showed strongest selling in Financials (a reversal MoM), 
while Industrials and Materials led the HF selling and Energy was still sold into 
strength.  AI Vulnerable / Software was the most bought theme last month and 
mostly short covering, though we did see some longs added at the end of the 
month.   
• Europe anti-AI rotation continues; Cyclicals de-risked ex-Energy: EU AI 
Disruption (JPEUAIL) was bought mostly via short covering, but EU AI 
Facilitators (JPEUAIID) was net sold on short adds and Semis was most sold 
industry. Cyclicals (JPDEECYC) kept seeing long reductions + short adds, 
while Europe Pure Oil (JPEUOIL1) saw renewed net buying; Banks were net 
neutral (after July selling) and Health Care (Pharma) was bought. 
• Asia: Japan bought broadly, while Korea/Taiwan still de-risked; China/HK 
mixed: Japan saw broad-based buying in Aug led by Financials and Cyclicals, 
China local was sold while HK was bought, and thematically there was buying 
in China Defensives/Health Care. Korea and Taiwan continued to be sold (as 
in July); Korea selling slowed MoM but there’s still no clear shift back to buying 
even as performance stabilized.
