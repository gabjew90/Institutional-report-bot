Global earnings remain strong, with more than half 
n
of companies through Q3 reporting. With 60% of the 
S&P 500 reported, EPS growth is tracking at 33% yoy, 
putting Q3 on track to match Q2 as one of the strongest 
quarters in recent decades outside recoveries. Europe is 
also ahead of expectations, with EPS growth expected 
at 23%. 
Beneath the headline numbers, the picture is more 
n
balanced. Energy and AI account for much of the 
growth, while median-company EPS growth is similar at 
12% in the US and 11% in Europe, suggesting healthy 
underlying breadth. 
The key question is whether this momentum can 
n
persist. Growth is no longer accelerating and upgrades 
versus downgrades have moderated, pointing to softer 
momentum into 2027. Market reactions also remain 
asymmetric, with misses underperforming by roughly 
twice as much as beats outperform.
