Deleveraging Alert: Indicator Triggered for
US Equity L/S; Quants Close to Trigger
Amandine-Parisa Kara
02-Nov-2026, 03:48 ET (EST)
Hedge funds reduced gross exposure by -2.3 std on Wednesday, -2.1 std on Thursday and -0.4 std on
Friday last week, with the de-grossing concentrated in Hyperscalers. Notably, Friday’s de-grossing was
relatively modest (-0.4 std) compared with what the price action would have suggested. Combined with
continued pressure in these names, and the resulting hit to performance (see Friday performance estimates
below), this has triggered the UBS Systematic Advisory Deleveraging Indicator for US Equity L/S
strategies. 
Despite similar performance pressure in Europe, de-grossing was more moderate last week, at <1 std in each
session. As a result, the Europe indicator has not triggered. Japan also remains “off”.
