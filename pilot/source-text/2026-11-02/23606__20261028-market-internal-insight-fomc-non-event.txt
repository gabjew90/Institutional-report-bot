Market Internal Insight 
FOMC Non-Event; RM Support Intact; Excess Buy Flow & Barbell Outperformance in AI/QQQ and 
High-Short-Interest/IWM 
FOMC was a non-event with excess flow small at below $20 bn, which means it didn’t present a challenge to my bullish view nor trigger volatility.   Broad-based 
market moves were benign as well with SPX +0.3% and QQQ +0.6%.  However, factor moves were quite big with AI Semis +2.5%, UBXXSHRT (Short Interest 
Basket) +1.6% and AI Power +1.3%.  QQQ should benefit from Tech rally while IWM should benefit from Short Squeeze.  Today’s Tech-led rally is consistent with the 
relatively stronger (than SPX) excess buy flow in Nasdaq (+$10 bn) especially after 2pm post-FOMC (+$8 bn). 
 
In terms of real money support, it remains intact with UBS RMM clients buy flow @ 78%-tile vs 3Y and Foreign Listed ETF inflow in 12 of 12 days (including today) as 
well as 25 of last 26 days since Sep 23.  From a systematic/quant perspective, two key cohorts to watch are 1) CTA which is expected to favor Russell 2000 with RTY1 
exposure low @ 10%-tile vs 3Y but has 6 close buy triggers within +3.5%; 2) SPX Dealer Gamma has had big swing the last 2 days between -$25 bn and +$20 bn 
intraday. While long gamma over +$15 bn constrained SPX moves intraday, the collapse to below -$15 bn (typically after 3:30pm) the last 2 days empowered end-
of-day market volatility. 
 
Excess Flow (Charts 1 & 2): +$13 bn of excess buy flow in S&P 500 (SPX +0.3%) and +$10 bn of excess buy flow in Nasdaq (+0.6%) 
• 
Pre-FOMC @ 2pm, volume was super light this morning with cash, futures and ETFs volume running @ 78%, 69% and 31% of average 
• 
Before 2pm, excess flow was +$11 bn and +$2 bn of buying in S&P 500 and Nasdaq, respectively

After 2pm, there was an obvious buy preference in Tech with SPX excess flow @ +$2 bn, while Nasdaq excess flow @ +$8 bn 
 
UBS RMM (Chart 3): RMM Clients bought today at 78%-tile vs 3Y.  Buy Tilt in Telecom, Semis and ETFs.  Sell Tilt in Energy, Tech and Financials 
 
Foreign Listed US ETFs (Chart 4):  Inflow has improved from Sep quarter-end low (seasonality) and have been buying at +1x to +1.7x std dev the last few weeks

They bought in 12 of 12 days (including today) as well as in 25 of last 26 days since Sep 23 
• 
Today (FOMC Day) was another buy day – at a pace slightly higher than the last 2 weeks’ average daily flow

SPX Dealer Gamma (Charts 5 & 6): Dealer Gamma has had big swing the last 2 days between -$25 bn and +$20 bn intraday 
• 
On Oct 27 (Yesterday), dealers went long gamma above +$12 bn between 1:40pm and 3:10pm, when SPX move was almost flat.  From 3:10pm 
onwards, gamma fell sharply below -$15 bn @ 3:40pm and collapsed to a low of -$25 bn @ 3:50pm.  During this short gamma period, SPX volatility 
resumed on its down move 
• 
On Oct 28 (Today), dealer gamma was non-material most of the day below $5 bn but then collapsed to -$20 bn @ 3:50pm 
• 
While long gamma over +$15 bn constrained SPX moves intraday, the collapse to below -$15 bn (typically after 3:30pm) the last 2 days empowered end-
of-day market volatility
