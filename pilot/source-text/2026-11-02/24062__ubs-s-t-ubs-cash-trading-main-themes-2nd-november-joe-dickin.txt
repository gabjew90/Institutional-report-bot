#Markets : EStoxx +20bps to start and +90bps off Fridays lows now, US futures seeing some early relief (ESA
+40bps, NQA +80bps) after a heavy session Friday that finished off the lows but still below the levels seen at
the European cash close (S&P 500 -1.8%, Nasdaq -2.9%). The AI trade remained the main drag.
Semis/Memory/Neoclouds all sharply lower, and big tech was broadly weaker with NVDA (-4.2%) and SPCX
(-6.4%). Treasuries offered some offset though feedback indicated more of a concern that a bid toTreasuries is
starting to imply a growth scare, yields 2-8bps lower and the curve steepening further. Asia bouncing off early
morning lows; Tokyo Electron (+6.5%) is giving the semi-cap complex some relief after earnings, though
Kioxia (-16%) on a softer outlook shows how high the guidance bar remains - roughly inline with ADR move.
KOSPI +2% trades near the day's highs after reversing a -2% intraday low, helped by continued National
Pension cash buying (net +$190mn today) following Friday's +$640mn, its largest single day of buying in
nearly five years. China +1.1% looks through a soft PMI print on reports that financial regulators will meet
banks and securities firms today to discuss measures to support the capital market. Crude is optically lower
(-3-3.5%) on reports that President Trump wants to give diplomacy with Iran more time before potentially
resuming strikes after Tuesday's midterms, Reuters reports that Qatari mediators are holding a third straight
day of talks with Iranian representatives. Part of the move reflects the front month rolling from Dec26 to Jan27
(Brent $109.5, WTI $100.5). That makes tomorrow's midterms the near-term pivot for both the Iran timeline
and broader risk appetite. The AI complex likely needs more than one positive print out of Tokyo to signal the
derisking has run its course

and Michigan consumer sentiment, alongside German industrial production, Eurozone retail sales, Canadian
