Sales & Trading Commentary For Institutional Use Only Not a Product of Research
Macro and derivs trades over the last week:
• HF flows more constructive than headline index moves would suggest.
• Early week HF flows more bearish with Hyperscalers leading the selling, but Tech flows improved
throughout the week led by Semis, Friday sell-off in particular saw decent buy skew from HFs (including in
Hyperscalers).
• Also saw buying of Software (mainly Quality software) and AI Industrials/Power (albeit more short
covering than outright).
• Derivs desk also saw decent Tech upside demand though unlike in cash was led by Memory, mainly
DRAM (e.g. Nov and Dec calls spreads) and EWY ($8m prem. spent on Nov calls), however note that most of
the memory upside came on Wed after Hynix strong results but before Samsung weak results.
• Also saw broader index flows flip from hedging at start of week (e.g. $35m prem. spent on Nov downside)
to upside later in the week (e.g. SPX and QQQ call ratio structures).
• LO flows more defensive, selling out of crowded names and rotating into defensives.
• Asia flows also a bit mixed, saw Japan Tech buying (after many weeks of HF degrossing) while Korea
flows were mixed (upside buying in Derivs including KOSPI2 KO calls, selling in futures, flat in cash) and
Taiwan flows were skewed to sell.
• China flows particularly weak with continued HSTECH selling (ongoing for week) while on CSI flows, not
seeing much buying come through on this sell-off (clients want to see some signs of stabilisation first).
• Brazil sell-off saw divergent reactions across Eq and FX, Equities initially saw dip-buying (both cash and
derivs) followed by two-way activity thereafter while FX initially saw BRL unwinds followed by fresh USDBRL
downside later in the week.
• DOminant FX theme continues to be EURUSD downside demand (including via EURBRL downside).
• USDCNH downside buying also persists (including via CNH correl structures vs EUR, SGD and AUD).
• Starting to see some profit taking on USDKRW downside given how strong the KRW rally has been.
• On Gold, been seeing intermediaries (which includes Sovereigns) buying at 4k level but broader active
money Gold flows have been quite muted and our desk feels like Gold is being used as a source of funds
amid the AI sell-off. However, on equity side, ETF desk saw strong buying in Metals ETFs, particularly
GDXJ.

seeing a lot of demand to fade the move.
