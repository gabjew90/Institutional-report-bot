#Markets : EStoxx +10bps to start, the US session closing marginally higher (S&P +0.28%, Nasdaq +0.27%)
post the FOMC, rates held unchanged in a 10-2 vote, very little given to shift the market narrative on the Dec
meeting really - pricing drifting to ~83% for a 25bps hike (~>90% prior). Warsh did push back on the idea that
Tech is immune to rates and the structure of meetings will change into next year (one meeting will not hold a
vote). After the close, Nasdaq futures fell as much as 1% as Alphabet (-10% aft-mkt) missed in Search, putting
AI cannibalisation concerns back at the centre of the debate. MSFT (+2.9% aft-mkt) went the other way, with
Azure growth printing 48% (+3ppt beat). ORCL (+10% aft-hrs) rallied after CFO Maxson guided FCF to turn
positive in 2H28 and top $30bn in fiscal 2029 as capex declines. Asia trades mixed off that split read-through.
The KOSPI is lower as Samsung (-5%) reversed an initially positive reaction to good results — perhaps some
evidence of travel and arrive post SK Hynix. TAIEX (-1.5%) is weighed down by disappointing capex guidance
from hyperscalers (no increase in FY26/27 framework), Japan is flat into the Topix rebalance, and China is
softer on mixed earnings and read-across from Tech earnings, China Semis -3%, Hong Kong Tech lower
across the board.
#FOMC : The FOMC held rates at 3.75–4.00% in a 10-2 vote, with Cook and Hammack preferring a 25bp hike,
while stressing that incoming data will determine the appropriate policy stance and upgrading its assessment
of activity from “solid” to “strong”. Warsh struck a hawkish tone, highlighting stronger growth, elevated inflation,
robust productivity and double-digit investment growth, while reiterating the Fed’s commitment to the 2%
inflation target. He also pushed back on the idea that tech is insulated from monetary policy, pointing to greater
discrimination between investments and widening credit spreads. Despite the rhetoric, markets are pricing
around 21bp of hikes by December (~83% probability of one hike) and 70bp cumulatively by October 2027,
with the latter notably lower than Tuesday’s pricing. The FOMC held rates at 3.75–4.00% in a 10-2 vote, with
