Risk Control Selling Likely Early This Week
Before A Subsequent US Equity Recovery
Rebecca Cheong
01-Nov-2026, 18:24 ET (EST)
Near-term systematic downside pressure, but a more constructive setup once that flow is absorbed.
Risk Control is estimated to sell $75-100 bn on Monday, which could add further downside pressure to
Equities and potentially trigger the remaining NQ1 CTA close sell triggers.  Depending on Monday’s SPX
return, Risk Control may have more meaningful selling on Tuesday only if SPX drops more than -2% (See
Below for Full Scenario Analysis).  Otherwise, the systematic selling impact from both Risk Control and CTAs
should begin to fade, setting the stage for another sharp recovery.
Friday’s market internals provide three supports for the recovery view:
• Minimal Excess Sell Flow (Charts 1 & 2): Despite QQQ -3.5%, Nasdaq Excess Sell Flow was only -$8 bn,
even smaller than the already modest -$24 bn S&P 500 Excess Sell Flow with SPY -1.8%.  This suggests that
while the sharp index declines triggered significant systematic selling, it was largely offset by
bargain-hunting demand
• Strong Retail Support: Late-day Excess Flow reversed sharply to +$72bn of buying, consistent with
aggressive UBS RMM buying from 2:45pm onward. Retail was once again buying the dip, with the strongest
activity concentrated in Tech ETFs and Semis/Memory stocks, among Friday’s worst-performing segments
• CTA Selling Close to Exhaustion: CTA selling was an important driver Friday, with 10 sell triggers across
ES1, NQ1 and RTY1. However, exposures have already fallen to 19%, 13% and 15%-tile vs 3Y, respectively.
Only NQ1 retains 3 close sell triggers; ES1 and RTY1 triggers are farther away and less clustered,
suggesting limited additional CTA impact from here
