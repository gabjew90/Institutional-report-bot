• UBS Quant Research: Alpha from Hedge Fund Crowding & Crowding Momentum
The crowding signal measures the level of hedge fund positioning in individual stocks, identifying names with
the strongest institutional conviction. The signal has generated consistent alpha since 2017 with a 5.3%
annualized return and a Sharpe ratio of 0.83 globally.
The crowding momentum signal measures changes in hedge fund positioning over the prior 12 months,
capturing stocks attracting increasing institutional conviction. The signal has delivered a 6.9% annualised
return with a Sharpe ratio of 0.60.
Crowding Monograph "A Comprehensive Crowding Factor" Belcaid
• UBS Systematic Advisory: Enhancing Hedge Fund Positioning With Short Interest Data (HF X SI)
Double-sort portfolios that combine low Days-to-Cover (lightly shorted stocks) with high hedge fund crowding
on the long side, and high Days-to-Cover with short crowding on the short side, generate substantially stronger
risk-adjusted returns than either signal alone.
• UBS Quant Research: Alpha from Hedge Fund vs. Retail Positioning (HF X Retail)
This signal combines hedge fund crowding momentum with retail flow, going long stocks where institutional
conviction is increasing but retail investors are net sellers (and vice versa on the short side). The signal
delivers strong alpha with a 12.2% annualised return and a Sharpe ratio of 1.07, nearly doubling the Sharpe
ratio of the standalone crowding momentum strategy (0.60).
