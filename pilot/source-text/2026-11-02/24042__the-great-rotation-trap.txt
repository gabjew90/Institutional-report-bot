The great rotation
The great rotation is accelerating. Small caps are crushing tech as falling yields fuel the Russell revival,
while the Nasdaq has suffered one of its most brutal relative selloffs in decades. But the moves are
becoming extreme. Tech is testing massive support, valuations have reset and relative fear is surging.
Has the rotation gone too far, setting up another violent reversal?
The Russell revival
The Russell has crushed the Nasdaq over the past month. One of the biggest relative rotations in years,
with small caps suddenly becoming the place to hide from the tech carnage.

Tech P/E

The bottom line
The Russell revival is real, but the rotation is looking increasingly stretched. With tech testing major
support, valuations resetting and relative fear approaching extremes, the risk of a violent reversal is
growing. Chasing small-cap outperformance here may be just as dangerous as chasing tech was at the
highs.
