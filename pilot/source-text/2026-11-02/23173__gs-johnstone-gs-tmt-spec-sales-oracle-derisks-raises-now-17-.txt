Optical M&A: Bloomberg and Reuters report that Marvell is in advanced talks to acquire Lumentum,
which has a market capitalisation of roughly $83bn, with a deal potentially coming as soon as next week.
The combination would pair Marvell’s DSPs, custom silicon and Celestial AI optical fabric with
Lumentum’s high-power external lasers (UHP/CW sources), used in Nvidia’s co-packaged optics (CPO)
designs. Nvidia has already invested roughly $2bn in each company, highlighting the scarcity of reliable
light sources as AI clusters begin adopting co-packaged and near-packaged optics. Lumentum rose 9–11%
after hours, while Marvell fell more than 8% on financing and integration concerns around a stock-heavy
deal. The read-across is positive for the broader optics/CPO supply chain, particularly Coherent, a direct
peer with its own $2bn Nvidia deal. In Europe, Nokia looks the clearest beneficiary, with positive
implications for Aixtron too.

TECH
• Adyen: more spending, stronger growth? The debate is whether investment lifts growth above roughly
20% or merely sustains it. Management expects operating leverage over time; bears question whether
growth now costs more. The previous hiring push hit margins while growth held rather than accelerated.
The positive: A record 2026 customer cohort, specialised US hiring, equity-based pay and acquisitions
could extend growth. Management still backs roughly 20% as a guardrail—not guidance—and expects
acquisitions to become significant but has not quantified the revenue upside. The concern: Dropping the
FY28 margin target of at least 55% within a year, without a new medium-term revenue framework, adds
uncertainty after previous resets. Management says 55% is achievable but would sacrifice longer-term
growth, given the opportunities identified through acquisitions and customer feedback.
What matters next: Do analysts raise revenue forecasts or cut earnings and wait? With acquisition upside
and margin recovery unquantified, I fear the latter; US feedback is a useful check. Capital priorities
remain organic growth, selective M&A consistent with the single platform, then surplus cash returns—
with no timing, amount or trigger specified. The next catalyst the February’s 2027 outlook for revenue
guidance, EBITDA and CapEx commentary, and acquisition updates—not separate Orb or Talon.One
forecasts—to test whether spending delivers growth, leverage and cash returns. Still think stock down
12% is overdone but as one PM said “You cannot sleep well at night owning adyen and its going to remain
in the penalty box until the communication becomes more consistent and more reliable”

• Amazon / Globalstar: The FCC has approved Amazon Leo’s $11.5bn acquisition of Globalstar, giving
Amazon 25 MHz of internationally harmonised mobile-satellite spectrum, around two dozen active
satellites, approval for the 48-satellite C-3 constellation and ground stations in Texas, Florida and Alaska,
