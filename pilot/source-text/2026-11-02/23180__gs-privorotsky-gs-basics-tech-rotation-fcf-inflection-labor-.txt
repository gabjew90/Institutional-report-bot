GS Privorotsky - GS Basics Tech Rotation. FCF Inflection.
Labor/AI 29 Oct 2026
Rich Privorotsky  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  FICC & Equities
29 Oct 2026
Markets: Samsung disappointing, particularly the lack of shareholder returns against a higher bar post
Hynix. Advantest ripping on a significant guidance upgrade… another reminder of robust AI demand.
Hyperscalers: The hyperscaler tape was messy on headline execution, but the underlying compute
trajectory remains robust: one mega cap platform shed ~10% after hours following consumer ad misses
even as Cloud growth remained robust. Second was cleaner...core cloud beat whispers, positive quarterly
FCF with total contracted obligations expanding. While an optically "light" capex outlay reflected third
party facility delivery sequencing rather than reduced appetite, with next quarter guided back up.
Aggregate spending intentions across both platforms remain flat to higher with power and cluster delivery
remaining the primary bottlenecks;. The market has moved past questioning capex durability. The new
question is value capture: as running models gets cheaper, does that benefit hyperscaler returns on capital,
or expand gross margins for enterprise applications?
Oracle: Arguably the most important development for credit. Stock rallied double digits as management
outlined a path to positive FCF in H2 FY28 and $30bn+ in FY29, with capex stepping down in FY29.
Incremental capital raises capped at $20bn through FY30, excluding refinancing. Oracle officially guided
to non-GAAP operating margins of 40%+ by FY30 on $225 billion+ in revenue (with high-20s project
returns on fully ramped gigawatt, with ~$16bn annualized revenue per fully ramped gigawatt. Impressive
economics on paper, although dependent on execution, utilization and financing assumptions. Not a capex
cut… an attempt to demonstrate that the economics work and the funding cycle has an end. Potentially
very significant for levered AI credit, although still need to see spreads respond.
Fed: Thought Warsh was balanced. Direct references to tightening FCI early in the press conference,
suggesting awareness of how much work markets are already doing. Two hawkish dissents, Cook and
Hammack, but Logan staying put was a positive surprise. Growth assessment upgraded, inflation still
elevated, so hardly dovish. December still looks like a hike, but little reason for material repricing. Fed
conscious of both sides of the mandate, with tighter FCI potentially allowing patience. A fairly volatility-
suppressing event.
ECB today: Two hikes since June. Question is whether December brings another. French sovereign stress
increasingly doing some tightening for them. Does Lagarde acknowledge FCI and sovereign pressure, or
focus on underlying inflation and energy driven second round effects? With European gas touching
€100/MWh, wouldn’t hold my breath for a dovish pivot, although some room for repricing if financial
conditions get more airtime.

Energy: Vance’s letter to Iran feels like a last minute attempt to secure a diplomatic win. US pressure
intense, but Iranian position resolute with no obvious capitulation. Never say never, but feels like we’re
racing toward post midterm escalation. Remarkable that European equities are looking through €100 gas
and elevated oil… feels like market is pricing this wont last much longer.
Risk: Pain trade is hyperscalers u/p while de-risked picks and shovels start working. Hyperscalers might
weigh on sentiment, but the broader AI demand and spending message remains supportive. Advantest’s
upgrade reinforces the micro, while supportive overnight developments n the optical space helps the
broader group. Credit remains the most important thing to watch. Stability improving, with Oracle
potentially more consequential for the levered infrastructure complex than either hyperscaler print. But
we’re transitioning from a market rewarding capex growth to one demanding evidence of returns. For
now, micro remains supportive and I’d expect dips to get bought. Geopolitical lottery ticket remains a pre
midterm US/Iran meeting… an enormous positive surprise if it happens.
side-note:
G10 central banks, some election surprises, AI jitters ebb and flow
