The SOX setup
Semiconductors have been taken to the woodshed. SOX is testing major technical support, volatility
has exploded and valuations are back at AI-cycle lows. Yet the underlying semiconductor economy
remains remarkably resilient. Is this a brutal shakeout or the beginning of something much nastier?
Huge in SOX
SOX tested the longer-term trend line and range lows at Friday's lows, before closing right on the 200-
day moving average. These are major support levels, but the moves have been violent lately. Even if
support holds, this space needs more time to heal.
LSEG Workspace
SOX vol
SOX volatility has gone from dormant to aggressively bid in just a few weeks. Friday's fear was extreme
across equity indices, but note that SOX volatility remains well below the panic levels seen during
previous semiconductor crashes.
LSEG Workspace
Standout SOX
On the way up, SOX volatility was aggressively bid as investors chased upside exposure. Now, SOX vol
is trading at elevated levels relative to VIX, but for the opposite reason: investors are scrambling for
downside protection. From upside panic to downside fear.

LSEG Workspace
Low in SOX
The SOX forward P/E has collapsed to just 16x, back at AI-cycle lows. The valuation reset has been
brutal, but the question is whether the market has already priced in a much worse earnings outlook
than the fundamentals currently suggest.
GS/Bloomberg
The real "SOX" economy isn't cracking
Goldman highlights that Korea's electronics sector accounted for roughly 60% of the country's annual
GDP growth in Q3. Tech exports remain strong, machinery investment accelerated, and semiconductor
equipment imports continued to rise. While semiconductor equities have suffered a sharp valuation
reset, the underlying activity data continue to tell a very different story. Another reason to question
whether the recent SOX washout has gone too far.

Love that pattern
SOX is showing an uncomfortable resemblance to the dot-com bubble. If the historical roadmap holds,
we could get one more powerful bounce before the real damage begins. Not exactly a comforting
comparison.

The bottom line
SOX is testing major support, valuations are back at cycle lows and fundamentals remain resilient. The
ingredients for a powerful bounce are there. But volatility suggests the healing process isn't over.
Cheap doesn't automatically mean safe.
