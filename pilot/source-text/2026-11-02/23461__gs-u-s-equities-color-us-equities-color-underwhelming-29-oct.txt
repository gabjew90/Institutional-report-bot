magnitude of the moves. Felt very top-down with SPX volumes explosive at +35% vs 20d avg.
 
DERIVS: Another test for the AI trade today as disappointing Google earnings stifled budding optimism in the
market. The main theme of the day was the vol crush for most of the session as QQQ front month fixed strike vols
were offered down a vol at the lows, with SPY down more than 80 bps of vol. The magnitude of the crush was
observable in VIX futures moving nominally lower. This is reflective of vol premium being taken out of the market
post-several prominent tech prints. Unsurprisingly, skew was also bid on the move lower - particularly in SPX
gamma tenors. Earlier in the session, we saw a cust away buy 15k SPX 31Mar 5500 puts as a crash hedge. In the
singles/thematics space, we saw clients come in to hedge single-name tech exposure and more sparsely add
convexity in higher conviction positions. This market continued to trade thematically with moderate SPX moves
failing to fully capture the AI complex today (GSTMTAIP -1.5%) being used as a source of funds for everything else
(SPXXAI +1.5%). The S&P implied move through tomorrow is 0.70%. (TY Gail Hafif)
