The tech snapback
Nasdaq positioning has snapped back hard. After briefly dipping negative, Non-Dealer net exposure
saw buying every week from August 4 through September 1, totaling a record $51.3bn in notional
terms, according to Quinn.
A big part of the move came from option-market and broader hedge unwinds, although genuine
buying also showed up across several cohorts. Bottom line: Nasdaq futures positioning is now relatively
elevated heading into a packed micro and macro calendar.
GS
Cover
Hedge funds led the move with $37.5bn of net buying, almost entirely driven by short covering
(+$38.6bn). The near‑term strength in Nasdaq futures is being generated by short covering, not by
fresh aggressive longs.

Puts - suckers only?
These are longer-term charts, but worth flagging: put exposure has collapsed. Shorts have been
covered and downside protection has been stripped out at the same time.
