US Strategy: The largest US AI hyperscalers are on track to spend $800 billion in capex in 2026 and
consensus expects $1.1 trillion in spending in 2027. Hyperscalers need to generate annual AI revenues of
roughly $300 billion in the next few years to break even on their investments. We estimate that AI users
would need to spend roughly $1 trillion annually on AI applications in order for the hyperscalers to
generate solid returns on investment vs USD1.5trn in global software spending.
Cross-Asset: More Bonds? Inflation volatility, fiscal risks and less negative equity/bond correlations
suggest that duration may provide less reliable protection than before the COVID-19 crisis. Finally, bond
term premia remain relatively low in a long-run context. Investors should favour the parts of the curve
with the best carry vs. convexity – with relatively low rates volatility, selective option overlays can help.

Europe Insurance – Pension Reform / limited impact: We estimate that the new AVD market opportunity
(prior to market appreciation) could reach €139bn to €184bn over a five-year period. After overlaying the
individual insurers' new AVD product strategies, we find that the year 5 EPS impact in either scenario is
limited (+/-0.5%), driven by the CSM mechanics (which only release into earnings slowly over time) and
diversified business models (where German Life represents only c.6% of group earnings on average, and
Riester is only a portion of this).
Essilor (N / PT EUR165 / 14% Upside / 19x PE): We have lowered our estimates for revenue growth by
40-170bps between FY26/27/28e reflecting a more conservative AI glasses ramp following Meta's own-
branded launch. Our EBIT and EPS are lowered by 6-7% over the same period.
Meta: Increasingly appears to be moving from building AI infrastructure toward productizing that compute
across a scaled consumer ecosystem. In our view, the key differentiation is more about Meta’s ability to
combine AI capabilities with global distribution, first-party data/personalization, compute, consumer
interfaces, and an expanding ecosystem of third-party connectors.
