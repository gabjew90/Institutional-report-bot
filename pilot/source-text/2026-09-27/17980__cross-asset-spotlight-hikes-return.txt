 Cross-Asset Brief: Are Higher Bond Yields a 
Headwind for Equities? Key Debates in Under 5 
Minutes – August 2026 (28 Aug 2026)  
Signals, Flows, & Key Data: A weekly  summary of key cross-asset 
monitors, data, moves, and models tracking sentiment, fund 
flows, and positioning. Last week, UST 2Y yields rose 12bp, 
USDJPY rose by 2.1% and S&P's forward P/E compressed to 19x.
 Key highlights from last week:  
• UST 2Y yields rose 12bp after the Fed hike. Our US rates strategists think 
market-implied Fed path placed too much weight on restrictive Fed policy 
persisting into late 2027 and our rates strategists recommend staying in 
SFRZ6Z7 flatteners.
• USD/JPY rose  2.1% after the BoJ hike. Our FX strategists stay bearish the yen 
and see upside in the pair, which they believe is mispricing relative policy 
stances.
• The S&P 500 fell 0.1% on the week, despite being up post-Fed hike. Our US 
equity strategists  expect quality leadership to continue, but highlight tactical 
downside risks from higher energy prices and the midterms.
• Brent fell 0.7% but touched $110/bbl intraday. Our commodity strategists 
observe that Brent calendar spreads have rallied as multiple supply 
disruptions, declining inventory buffers and stronger physical demand 
tighten the oil market.

COVA: Cross-Asset Correlation-Valuation Framework
Our correlation-valuation (COVA) scorecard aims to identify good portfolio diversifiers at 
reasonable prices, rewarding assets that have: 1) a more negative correlation to equities, 2) 
a more stable correlation, 3) a more negative downside beta, 4) more attractive 
valuations, and 5) more attractive carry. The higher the score, the better the portfolio 
diversifier is. For more details on the framework, see here.

 Cross-Asset Monitor: Levels, Changes, and Valuations
Key highlights from last week
• Topix (+1.6%) outperformed MSCI ACWI (-0.5%) while the FTSEMIB 
underperformed (-1.8%). Healthcare (+1.6%) led gains in global equity sectors while 
materials (-2.3%) led losses.
• US IG tightened 3  bp while EUR IG widened 2 bp. France 10Y saw a +2.5 SD move.
• The US Treasury curve twist-flattened.
• The KRW  saw a -2.9SD move against the USD. DXY gained 1.1%.
• Silver (+3.0%) outperformed the BCOMSP (-0.2%) while iron ore (-1.1%) 
underperformed.
