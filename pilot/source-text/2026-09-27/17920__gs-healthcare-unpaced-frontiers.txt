Jonathan Chan  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  Healthcare Equity Research

Unpaced Frontiers
Saturday, September 26, 2026
A complicated macro week (10Y piercing 5%, Geopolitical Calming/Oil -8%, Trump-Xi meetings, etc.)
exacerbating a highly incongruent micro week in healthcare – where specialist focus centered around two
key subsector level themes (1) continued Tools momentum and (2) Persisting positioning pain in SMID
biotech that have both been gaining momentum (albeit in diverging directions), while (3) on-going
consternation around upcoming STARs results in Managed Care saw some late in the week alleviation.
(1) Tools legging higher again this week (GSHLCTOO now +82% since May), with the most tangible
fundamental focus remaining on (a) favorable commentary on near and intermediate term growth visibility
from companies on the conference circuit (GIR out on 9/20 with an intraquarter Pulse Check on recent
commentary), while (b) AI Drug Discovery themes have fully breached escape velocity in
generalist/thematic-centric investor interest (in the face of more measured/skepticism from specialists)
more recently – with recent frontier lab headlines adding kindling to this theme (see Wet Lab headlines
last week, CRISPR-like headlines this week) that has drawn in a wider-than-usual aperture of investor
inbounds, and where initial expressions have skewed towards a subsector/basket approach – though there
has been some stock-level targeted expressions emerging among specialists (i.e. Agilent Discussing AI
Lab Buildouts during an NDR with our team, MTD +17% in 2W with focus on lab automation/liquid
handling, beyond the names that had been tagged on this theme much earlier i.e. TWST +151% YTD).
(2) Persisting positioning pain in SMID biotech has remained front-and-center (see our recent note
Marking-To-Market On This Tough Stretch In Biotech) – where the macro set up worsened this week
(rates, AI mindshare monopoly with Agentic focus this week, technical/calendar impacts, etc), while
negative bias in newsflow persisting this week (ACAD -13% Thursday on Ph2 ADP miss, CLDX -11.5%
Tuesday with Safety Focus Despite positive Ph3 CSU results, IMVT -7% Wednesday on miss in CLE
though balanced by VKTX +36% Tuesday on positive SC Maintenance study. Marking-to-market on
positioning/crowding/performance since August – (i) our Crowded Biotech (GSHCBT50) / Low Crowded
Biotech (GSHCBTXC) pair has drawn down -17.6% since 8/1– now back to 2023 levels. (ii) Price action
in our VIP HC vs. Most Short baskets (all HC, not biotech specific) also suggests un-windy behavior
during this time period – the pair -11% since early August, interestingly a bleed lower in VIP longs since
mid-August, while Most Short has been more volatile – this corroborates the acute positioning pain we’ve
observed in biotech, Payors vs. Providers and Medtech during this period. (iii) Biotech Grosses and Nets
(as % of total US Exposure within our PB book) have both come in from 5Y highs – Gross Exposures on
8/1 were at 91st percentile on 1Y basis and are now at 63rd percentile. Meanwhile, Net Exposures on 8/1
were at 43rd percentile on a 1Y basis and are now at the 9th percentile. The majority of degrossing activity

our PB has observed more recently has been long selling, with HC by far the most net sold US sector in
the GS PB book September MTD (net sold for six consecutive weeks in the GS PB book) -- (while
incremental shorting activity, has also tightened up nets). (iv) The Biotech Long/Short Ratio (by MV) in
our PB has also retraced – now at its lowest level since December 2024 at around 3.0x (vs. a peak level of
more than 5x in late 2025). (v) And while the tactical set up on both macro and micro front remains
clouded, the significant derisking observed in our data + near-term catalyst path with earnings and clinical
calendar should refocus on fundamentals – where recent conversations suggest a quality bias in what could
get re-grossed first and where we have sensed elevated focus in-and-around several of the commercial
launch stories within our team’s recent resumption of Biotech coverage.
(3) Managed Care also saw a late-in-the-week bounce, HUM, CVS +5% Friday, catalyzed by an upgrade
of the former away from us. Upcoming STARs results have remained the near-term event – which has
been among recent overhangs (i.e. STARs, rising Dem sweep odds, Hospitals v. MCO positioning
unwinds, etc.) that initially accelerated earlier in the month around the release of cut points, despite most
companies (ex ALHC) mostly sounding constructively around 3Q utilization/MLR trends. Past precedent
would suggest STARs outcome would be released the back-half of the second week of October – with the
vol surface in HUM showing an ~8 vol kink that week for context – though some focus on the potential
for an earlier release (i.e. via Plan Finder) could be a possibility.
