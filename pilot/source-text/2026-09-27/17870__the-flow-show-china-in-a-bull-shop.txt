Scores on the Doors: oil 62.5%, global stocks 12.7%, US stocks 12.5%, US dollar 3.0%, 
cash 2.7%, HY bonds 0.6%, gold -1.1%, IG -2.8%, bitcoin -3.7%, govt bonds -4.0% YTD. 
Zeitgeist: “Policymakers never run out of ammunition. But they can run out of 
credibility, and when they do, more ammo needed to restore financial order.” 
Zeitgeist: “Trump would love Xi’s bond yields, but Xi don’t want Trump’s stock market.”  
The Biggest Picture: 2020s era of fiscal excess, political populism, de-globalization, 
resource colonization, trade/AI wars, inflationary boom (Chart 3); US nominal GDP up 
63% in past 6 years, price of 30-year US Treasury bond down 60% (Chart 2); until yields 
impact growth, investors will likely stay long stocks (waiting for blow-off top in US tech), 
short bonds (waiting for blow-off top in yields).  
Tale of the Tape: since Warsh flipped hawkish at Jackson Hole, GT10 yield +60bps, US$ 
+2%, stocks -1%; bad news...new global monetary tightening cycle (60 hikes this year vs. 
20 in '25) as central banks scramble to restore credibility; good news...stock price action 
shows confidence they succeed (Magnificent 7 stocks peaked when Fed cut 
rates/credibility Oct’25, broke out to new highs when Fed hiked last week – Chart 4).  
The Price is Right: VAR shock risk always high when volatility of collateral of financial 
system (aka MOVE Index – Chart 5) jumps 35% in 2 days; if global financials (IXG) 
<$125, MOVE >125 risk-off deleveraging event coming; and if US-Iran deal drops oil 
another $10 but yields keep rising then big risk-off; policymaker panic to cap yields has 
begun, time to nibble bonds…returns = 10% in 5-year, 14% in 10-year, 22% in 30-year 
UST should yields drop 100bps next 12 months.
