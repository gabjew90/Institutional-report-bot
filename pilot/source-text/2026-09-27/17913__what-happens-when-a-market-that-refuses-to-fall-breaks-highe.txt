What's the pain trade?
The warning signs are piling up: tech CDS spreads are widening and bond vol has exploded. Equities
simply refuse to care.
That disconnect makes it easy to get bearish, but price remains the final arbiter. With NDX still holding
its range, the more interesting question may be where the real pain trade sits from here.
Remember CDS tech spreads?
Several of the "hot" tech CDS spreads are back to recent highs. NDX cared about this back in July. This
time around, the broader NDX simply does not care.

Vol does not care
There is more to equity vol than how CDS spreads are trading, but it is still worth pointing out the
massive gap between selected big tech CDS spreads and equity vol.

Bond vol connection gone
SPX has also held up against the MOVE surge. That divergence can last if stocks see higher yields as a
sign of stronger growth. It gets harder to sustain if bond-market stress starts forcing broader risk
reduction.

That feeling
Equities continue to shrug off most negatives. It is easy to get bearish looking at some of the
divergences above. There are fundamental bullets to worry about, but the tape simply refuses to trade
that way.
To us, the cork-in-water analogy continues to hold. The question we keep asking ourselves: what
happens if NDX takes out the range highs?
There is only vacuum above.
