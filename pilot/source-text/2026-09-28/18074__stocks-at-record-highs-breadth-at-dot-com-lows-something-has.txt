Light positioning, heavy divergences
Positioning has been cleaned up, but the market underneath remains anything but calm. Institutional
and systematic longs have been cut sharply, hedge funds are building Nasdaq shorts, while options
traders continue to chase upside.
At the same time, breadth has deteriorated to extremes even as index volatility stays remarkably
subdued. Light positioning, narrow leadership and cheap upside optionality make for an unusual setup
into Q4.
Longs trimmed
GS notes that institutional equity length has been trimmed meaningfully. US index positioning has
fallen from a record 48% of open interest in May to 36%, while CTA/global systematic equity exposure
has dropped from $145bn in August to ~$70bn.
After selling nearly $75bn over the past month, GS now estimates CTAs could buy ~$25bn over the
next month in its baseline scenario.
The positioning overhang has become considerably cleaner.

Hedge funds just put on a monster Nasdaq
short
Nasdaq was making fresh all-time highs, but hedge funds were doing something very different
underneath the surface.
GS/CFTC data show hedge funds sold $14.9bn of Nasdaq futures in the five sessions through
September 22. The increase in shorts was $15.8bn, the largest weekly short build in more than a
decade.
Some of this may hedge the aggressive call buying we've seen, but falling Nasdaq funding suggests
this wasn't all delta hedging.
New highs above. One of the biggest HF shorting waves in 10+ years below.

Under the hood
The index looks calm, but underneath it the market is moving a lot. AI/tech is ripping while Energy,
Financials and Utilities have been hit. The gap between individual stock moves and the S&P 500 move
is at the 95th percentile over 30 years. Low correlation between stocks is keeping index vol artificially
low.

The index is hiding a bear market underneath
More than 40% of Russell 3000 stocks have fallen 20%+ since June, even as the S&P 500 remains near
its highs. Meanwhile, the S&P forward P/E has already compressed to around 19x.
MS's Mike Wilson sees this as a mid-cycle valuation correction rather than a fundamental break, with
earnings revisions breadth approaching cycle highs.
MS
Bad breadth
70% of SPX stocks are trading below their 50-day moving averages. Historically, reversals from
similarly poor breadth have coincided with SPX moving higher.
GS
Profit warning mode
Market breadth is in full “profit warning” mode. The median stock trades 16% below its 52-week high,
pushing breadth to its weakest level since the Dot-Com Bubble.
