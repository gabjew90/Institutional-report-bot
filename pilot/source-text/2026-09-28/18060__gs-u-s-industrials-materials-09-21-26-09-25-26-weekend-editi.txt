CCL reports Tuesday pre-market with the call at 10am. 5% implied move with the stock 50/50 in recent
earnings days. Fiscal Q3 here. Lizzie’s preview here: After CCL's pullback of ~20% over the past month,
we see considerable valuation support at this level, with much of the bad news (higher oil prices and some
demand weakness) likely now priced in. What we are less certain about is calling an inflection point into
earnings, as our analysis points to pockets of heightened close-in discounting and Street estimates that are
still too high for 2027, in a year where net yield growth is likely back-half weighted, and not enough
bookings visibility by earnings to apply a high degree of certainty on how demand is shaping up until
Wave Season. On paper, our analysis points to a better year for cruise in 2027: industry Caribbean capacity
growth is lower than 2026, we lap NCLH's promotional pressure, and CCL is pulling ~5% of its capacity
out of the Caribbean and into (usually higher yielding) Europe. However, the impact of NCLH's base-
loading strategy on the industry is a key unknown, and with ~20% of CCL's customers usually brand
switchers, and many of the brands more value-oriented/price sensitive, it leaves CCL more levered to both
macro and promotional pressures than peers. Said differently, we're still missing a positive catalyst in the
