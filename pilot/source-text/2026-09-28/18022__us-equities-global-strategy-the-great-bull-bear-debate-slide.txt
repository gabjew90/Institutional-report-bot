THE BULL MARKET WILL END – BUT WHY AND WHEN?
Source: SG Cross Asset Research / Equity Strategy
1) Equity valuations still stretched
•
Stocks still look expensive relative to bonds despite recent PE de-rating
•
Valuations sustained by blockbuster trailing eps pushed way into the future
2) What could pop the ‘bubble’= The Fed
•
A tightening cycle has started and 11/14 times ends in a recession
•
Biggest risk is that Fed discovers (or thinks) it is behind the curve
3) Is Greedflation (aka profits-led inflation) a problem (again)?
•
Despite soaring energy costs, total unit costs look moderate
•
But like in 2022, companies are ‘taking advantage’ to boost profits via prices
4)Watch the technicals for a sign of breakdown
•
Breadth is very poor – “Never have stocks owed so much to so few”
•
Technical measures of breadth are deteriorating rapidly
