The Asian tech meltdown is getting uglier
KOSPI is crashing, volatility is exploding, and oversold conditions have reached levels not seen since
2022. Yet the selling shows little sign of stopping.
Meanwhile, the divergence between Korea and the rest of the semiconductor complex is becoming
increasingly extreme. Even Taiwan, seemingly immune to the carnage, is starting to roll over.
With positioning still crowded and doubts around AI capex growing, the risk of a broader unwind is
rising.
The big question: does Korea finally catch a bid, or is the rest of the AI trade next?
Crashing
KOSPI continues to crash, closing down 7.6% in a brutal overnight session. The longer-term trend line
has been broken, and the index is now trading well below its 200-day moving average.
The late-July selloff found support slightly below current levels. Is that KOSPI's last line of defense?

The vol explosion
KOSPI volatility has been rising lately, exploding higher overnight. During the melt-up, the unusual
spot-up, vol-up dynamic highlighted just how distorted this market had become. Now we are seeing the
opposite extreme: spot crashing, volatility exploding higher. Welcome to pure panic.

Time to hedge Taiwan?
With positioning still at record levels, Taiwan's AI trade looks increasingly vulnerable to near-term
downside. Inflation concerns, questions around AI capex and growing Chinese competition are adding
to the risks. The longer-term story may remain intact, but crowded positioning and elevated
expectations make tactical downside hedges increasingly attractive.
