GS TMT SPEC SALES – Thursday 15th October
Markets overnight: Global equities sold off sharply after hotter-than-expected US core inflation revived
rate-hike concerns and intensified the unwind in AI trades. The Nasdaq - 2%, SOX - 4.5% and software
lost 3%, with neoclouds, servers and optical names hit hardest; the weakness spread to Asia this morning,
where Korea’s Kospi fell 8%, Samsung and SK Hynix dropped more than 9%. %. High-beta, growth,
momentum and liquidity factors all underperformed, hurting TMT-focused funds, while defensive areas
such as telcos and selected application software held up better.
The main concern was credit & AI financing - CoreWeave unsecured bonds traded into distressed territory
(<80 cents, yields >15%), drawing ‘Yikes’ from Musk and highlighting leverage risks across AI neoclouds
and credit markets. Spillover hit Nebius and Oracle equity and credit. Gartner’s report did not help
sentiment as it expresses the view that much of the sector’s planned investment may struggle to earn an
adequate return. Plus, some pointing to the latest Ramp AI Index on employee spend slowing. A lot of pain
out there as the GS Prime data showing (after the NY close) that Global Fundamental LS managers down
0.8%(-1.7 StdDev) mainly beta. Now down 2% MTD but still up 10.6% YTD. While TMT focused funds
hurt the most: down 2.7% today (-2.6 StdDev), down 4.5% MTD but still up 13% YTD.
