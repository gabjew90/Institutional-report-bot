What’s Moving Since the Open?:
Bloomberg as of 10/14/26. Past performance is not indicative of future results.
CPI: Core CPI increased by 0.33% in September, above expectations. The upside surprise was partly
driven by a firmer-than-expected reading in the shelter category, with OER increasing by 0.35% month-
over-month and contributing to the core by about 5bp more than we expected. The strength in OER was

concentrated in the Midwest (+0.68%, SA by GS), well above our estimate of the underlying trend in the
OER category. The travel services, autos, and recreation goods categories were also firm. Based on details
in the CPI report, we estimate that the core PCE price index rose 0.32% in September (vs. our expectation
of 0.20% before the CPI release), corresponding to a year-over-year rate of +3.09%. The upward revision
to our September core PCE forecast largely reflects the upside surprise to our OER, hotels, and recreation
forecasts. Full note here.
Quality factor – full note here: The index is near all-time highs, but under the surface the market is
rewarding quality. This doesn’t only reflect higher rates, but a shift in focus from growth to the
composition of growth. The rate of change of growth should decelerate in the next 12 months in AI and
non-AI pockets of the economy, which could be a backdrop that is net positive for quality given the factor
tends to outperform as earnings growth rolls over. Our sector, industry, and factor neutral Quality Pair
Index (GSP1QUAL) is up 20% over the last 3 months to a 12m high on just ~13% realized vol. This has
been driven by a sharp selloff in the short leg (GSX1BFQS). Quality can also be used as a hedge to AI,
momentum or beta exposure given the strong negative correlation to these crowded themes (Momentum
positioning is 78th percentile on 5y and Beta is 81st of 5y). However, technicals are now stretched in the
Quality Factor (GSP1QUAL) with an RSI of 83 after being up in 16 of the last 18 trading sessions. A
softer CPI print or evidence that rates are peaking could trigger a reversal and a relief rally in the longer
duration lower-quality names: GSX1BFQS 1m 105% call indicatively costs 1.38%, 25d.

Early going but tough start for cyclical earnings though reactions have broadly been muted (RPM +3%,
FAST up 1% as of now, DAL flat) but underlying data points have been soft. RPM miss, fiscal Q2/full
year lower. DAL miss and cut. FAST one time benefits driving headline beat with September topline
slowdown. We like the set up for RPM going forward, more cautious on DAL and FAST. What sticks out
from each?
RPM: broad consumer weakness and price/cost pressures. Highlighting a slowdown in education and
healthcare markets as well as product shortages due to supplier raw material challenges. CPG is clearly
very volume sensitive. Energy and infrastructure strength in PCG. Leaning into share repurchase. Whether
it is realistic to assume inflation expectations step down in the coming quarters.
DAL: Acceleration in RASM in Q4 yet TSA data has turned negative. Are we seeing elasticity in demand
at this point. Can CASMx trend to the low single digit range for 2027 or is this highly dependent on
growth. What will happen to RASM CASM spread in 2027 with the lack of industry bankruptcy.
FAST: New slide deck – nice! Data center driving ~1/3 of growth. Company views September as normal
course vs. economic slowdown. Pricing 2.4% slowed sequentially and will be a smaller driver in 2027 vs.
2026. Gross margins 40bps benefit in Q3 from several factors – sourcing savings, supplier rebates and
tariff reflects. 20bps timing related.
