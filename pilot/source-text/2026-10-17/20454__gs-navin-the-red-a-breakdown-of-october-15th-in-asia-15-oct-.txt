Markets came under pressure today and volume rallied significantly across the franchise. Overall notional
traded came in up +58% vs. our previous 20 day average. Despite the large regional price action, the sell
skew was moderate as the desk was 1.2 X better to sell. We were net to sell in every single market with the
sole exception of Australia (1.4 X Better to Buy). As expected, the desk was most active in Information
Technology (1.4x better to sell), Industrials (1.1x better to sell) and Materials (1.1x better to sell) vs buys
in Consumer Discretionary (flat), Consumer Staples (flat).
There was no panic in the flow today, but the selling was robust and consistent across the trading session.
Typically, the majority of order/flow/activity come in the early morning session. Today, the selling and
activity continued throughout the entirety of trading. Traders, that were expecting a bounce or some
reprieve, found themselves coming back to the market time and time again. In addition, it is worth noting
that the selling was broad based with defensive sectors also coming under pressure albeit to a lesser extent.
The below is an effort to summarize some key stats from the markets today.
We focus mostly on North Asia given the AI exposure of those markets.
