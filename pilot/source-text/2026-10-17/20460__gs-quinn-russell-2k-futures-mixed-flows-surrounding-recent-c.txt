Russell 2k Futures: Mixed Flows Surrounding Recent Credit Concerns
Deterioration in High Yield credit pressured Russell 2k on October 14th. Futures aggregate open interest
jumped significantly. While the combination of lower price and higher open interest signaled short selling,
funding spread richening indicated that long buying also transpired. Hedge Fund likely initiated downside.
The identity of the contrarians - and whether they provide ongoing support - remains a mystery. Though
note Russell 2k futures survived a widespread equity sell-off in early October 15th trading.
• Deterioration in High Yield credit pressured Russell 2k on October 14th. CDX 5 Year High Yield spreads
widened to a 6 month high with CoreWeave contagion serving as a major catalyst. GS's High Yield Debt
Sensitive basket plunged 4.9%. And Russell 2k, which contains many sub-investment-grade companies,
lost 1.9%.
• Futures aggregate open interest jumped significantly. Russell 2k OI gained $1.8bn, marking the largest 1
day move in 2026 excluding roll periods.
• While the combination of lower price and higher open interest signaled short selling, funding spread
richening indicated that long buying also transpired. Russell 2k 3 month funding rose in outright terms and
relative to S&P.
• Hedge Fund likely initiated downside. Since July, the short-term correlation between weekly changes in
Russell 2k Hedge Fund Shorts and CDX High Yield was positive according to Commitment of Traders.
Said differently, when credit worsened, Russell 2k Hedge Fund shorts increased.
