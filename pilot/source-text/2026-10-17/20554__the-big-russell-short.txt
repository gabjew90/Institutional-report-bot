The next big pain trade?
Russell is getting crushed, with technical levels breaking, credit stress mounting, and systematic sellers
adding to the pressure.
But beneath the ugly price action, an increasingly interesting setup is emerging. Yields are reversing,
oversold conditions are extreme, and speculative shorts have become massive.
With hedge funds and CTAs heavily positioned on the short side, could Russell become the next big
pain trade, this time to the upside?
The big small cap puke
Russell broke below its longer-term trend line yesterday, although drawing trend lines is hardly an
exact science.
The index is now trading well below its 200-day moving average, but recall that the last time Russell
undershot this key level, buyers stepped in aggressively and triggered a sharp reversal.

Oversold
Russell is trading at deeply oversold levels. Buying something simply because it is oversold is hardly a
strategy, but several factors could set the stage for a reversal, at least in the short term.

The CDX connection
Deteriorating high-yield credit added further pressure to Russell 2000 yesterday. CDX 5-year high-yield
spreads widened to six-month highs, with CoreWeave-related contagion a major catalyst.
Goldman's High Yield Debt Sensitive basket plunged 4.9%, while Russell, with its significant exposure to
sub-investment-grade companies, fell 1.9%.

The big short
Since July, CFTC data has shown a positive short-term correlation between changes in Russell 2000
hedge fund short positioning and CDX high-yield spreads.
In other words, as credit conditions deteriorated, hedge funds increased their Russell shorts. With short
positioning now very large, the potential for a sharp short-covering rally is becoming increasingly
interesting.
GS

CTAs could buy a lot
CTAs have aggressively sold Russell 2000 exposure and are now their shortest since 2023.
This leaves room for systematic buying to provide additional support to equities, particularly if the
Russell starts moving higher.
We have seen two major crowded trades blow up recently (AI and bonds). Could a squeeze in Russell
be the next pain trade to unfold?
