Nvidia Corp. (NVDA): Announces reinstatement of Jensen Huang as President and CEO
• Strategic and ecosystem: As we had written previously, we believe Mr. Huang's vision, reach, and
external convening power has been important to both Nvidia and the broader AI ecosystem. However, it
remains to be seen whether the company will continue to negotiate new multi-party investments among
ecosystem partners at the same scale as it had previously done.
• Leadership and culture: As we had written previously, we believe Mr. Huang has been integral to
Nvidia's corporate culture and ethos - and we believe his return will largely reinforce the company's
existing culture. We continue to expect the company to execute on its previously announced product
roadmap.
• Financial and regulatory: As previously reported in the press, the US Department of Justice has been
conducting an investigation into whether some of Nvidia's customers did engage in export control
violations following shipments of products to jurisdictions including Singapore.

Vallourec (VLLP.PA) - 15% sell-off looks overdone as earnings and FCF inflect sharply in 2027; Buy
• Vallourec shares fell c.15% following 3Q26 results as investors focused on materially weaker-than-
expected 4Q guidance, continued Middle East shipment disruption and a temporary US operational issue.
We think the reaction is overdone.
• 3Q EBITDA of $181mn was broadly in line with Visible Alpha Consensus, while 4Q EBITDA guidance
of $170-210mn/mid-point of $190mn was materially below expectations, reflecting continued Middle East
logistics costs and shipment delays as well as the temporary US operational issue in September-October
2026.
ITM Power (ITM.L) - Upgrade to Neutral following share price underperformance
• We upgrade ITM Power from Sell to Neutral, primarily on valuation following significant share-price
underperformance. The shares are down 61% over the last six months, materially underperforming the
broader European hydrogen space, and we believe the risk/reward is now more balanced at current levels.
• Our upgrade is therefore primarily driven by share-price performance rather than a material change in
our fundamental assumptions.

RWE (RWEG.DE) - Strategic update preview: multiple avenues to drive mid-teens EPS growth to 2032
• We preview the March 2027 strategic update, at which we expect: (1) RWE to target a near doubling of
earnings over 2027-32 (+13% EPS CAGR), thanks to multiple growth avenues and stronger investments;
(2) a material portfolio shift, away from that of a pure generator, becoming a vertically integrated business
with a large exposure to power grids (c.35% of profits); and (3) target leverage to increase to c.3-3.5x,
given a larger share of regulated profits. By 2032, we forecast EPS at €6.28, implying a P/E of 9x. Our PT
of €74 implies c.35% upside.
