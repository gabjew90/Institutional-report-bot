DeskTalk - SPX: Three more charts
By Varshi Karamsetty
SPX has posted its best day since April 2025’s Liberation Day as Jensen Huang’s return revived the AI trade. But the easy
gains may be behind us. Three more charts on the technical climb ahead, sector breakdown and the frontier-model race. Our
last update.
It's only the beginning. SPX is still below all three major moving averages, so the technical climb remains steep. However
the bounce in equal weight index (SPW) is a healthier sign, pointing to broader participation beneath the headline index. CTA
levels are still far to reduce max short levels, at 7474 for SPX and 28670 for Nasdaq. Stay tuned for a fresh CTA update later
today.

Breaking it down further. While SPX remains ~6% below late-Oct levels, consumer staples and discretionary are still up
3%, with retail and homebuilders also outperforming. Tech and energy remain the clearest weak spots. Consumer resilience
is timely as we head into Thanksgiving and holiday season. Earnings have been average, but management have been
optimistic and US credit card data did improve last week. We highlighted few consumer nuances here.

What's next. We were due for an oversold bounce, but from here, our TMT spec salesperson sees investors likely taking
profit rather than chase moves like Nvidia. Attention remains on the AI trajectory, especially frontier lab model updates.
Gemini 4.1, Mistral Small 4.1 and Isaac GR00T among key releases this month. AI data center slowdown emerged as a risk
post US midterms, so we are monitoring commentary from governors and governors-elect for signs of tighter restrictions. For
now, we think that key states may slowdown and raise the cost of projects than halt the buildout completely. Dig deeper in
DeskTalk - Mapping the US data center buildout.
