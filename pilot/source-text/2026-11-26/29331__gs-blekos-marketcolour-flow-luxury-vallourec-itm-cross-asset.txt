Cross-Asset: We are modestly pro-risk for 2027 but favour greater balance across and within assets. After
the 'risk off' of last week, 12m return potential for equities looks more attractive. Using our current 12m
forward return forecasts, the efficient frontier should be steeper in 2027, relative to this year, with higher
equity returns, especially after the sell-off. And with more negative equity-bond correlations we would
expect more curvature with increasing cross-asset diversification benefits.
Luxury Tracker / Buy LVMH, Prada, Moncler, Richemont vs Hermes, Swatch: Overall, early Q4
datapoints show that global trends appear to be broadly in line with 3Q26 with the US continuing to show
strength even if the basis of comparison and possibly a "wait and see" attitude ahead of the midterm
elections may have weighed on growth, while China has continued to show weakness post a steep
sequential deceleration in Q3.
Vallourec (Buy / PT EUR26.90 / 80% Upside): We revise 2026/27/28 EBITDA by -7%/+1%/+1% and our
PT to €26.9 from €26.6 before, implying c.80% upside. We see the post-results sell-off as a particularly
attractive opportunity and expect the valuation gap versus Tenaris to narrow as EBITDA and FCF recover
through 2027.
ITM Power (Up to N): The shares are down 61% over the last six months, materially underperforming the
