Risk appetite: Gone missing
Where are all the risk-loving hot heads? Sentiment measures are near the bottom of their historical
ranges, hedge fund net exposure remains low and credit funds are seeing outflows. A quick look at
where positioning and risk appetite stand.
Sore sentiment
Investor sentiment has fallen sharply, with the AAII bull-minus-bear spread near the bottom of its long-
run range.
Deutsche Bank
Risk demand hits fear
Morgan Stanley's Global Risk Demand Index has moved into its "fear" zone, another sign of subdued
risk appetite.
Morgan Stanley
Low nets
Hedge funds continue to run low net exposure. JPMorgan data put equity L/S net leverage around the
19th percentile on a 12-month basis.

Risk exposures remain light
Goldman Prime tells a similar story: US fundamental long/short gross leverage is at the 33rd percentile
and net leverage at just the 7th percentile, both measured over the past year.
GS Prime
Further de-risking
JPMorgan's Tactical Positioning Monitor points to further de-risking over the past week, with the overall
level approaching the July lows.

Credit fund outflows
