Market breadth worth highlighting:
A&D set up / sentiment into Q3: Over the last several years aerospace has been a strong candidate to
generally buy dips with the growth viewed as largely macro agnostic. Believe the view on aerospace has
evolved – not true just for this subsector – but generally market has gravitated to becoming much more
stock specific vs. thematic. Think the most important point for aerospace heading into Q3 is that while the
stocks are behaving similar to March / April (higher jet fuel, fears of global travel slowdown) – what has
changed is that we are 7-8 months further into the conflict in the Middle East and jet fuel staying elevated.
Making maintenance decisions had no time to change / evolve / adjust in March but now time is working
against the outlook for growth. Think investors had wanted to gravitate length in aerospace to the original
equipment side including BA and the supply chain but has proven frustrating and difficult with the myriad
issues tempering the enthusiasm. Think names that are more owned here include BA RBC CRS ATI DPC
with folks warming to HONA given the valuation. More debated are WWD (need to guide) HWM (CPP
deal debate) and underowned would include TDG HEI TXT. In defense there has been widespread
confusion as to why the defense primes have generally acted so poorly even during risk off and a period of
geopolitical escalation. We hosted a dinner with our global head of aerospace and defense investment

banking which yielded some interesting insights into potential sources of weakness across the primes –
reach out to discuss. Midterm potential blue wave is a clear overhang for now – uncertainty exists whether
this will / could serve as a clearing event and desk believes SPCX lock ups serve as added pressure as
well. Think generally investors are more positive on LMT GD, mixed on RTX and more negative NOC
LHX. Fed IT remains a source of funds with some debate whether the debate around DOGE could
reaccelerate – article suggesting as much last week – let us know if you missed. Where do you differ on
the set up for Q3 and positioning heading into earnings?

GS Eco on Q3 – full note here: The Q3 2026 earnings season will kick off during the week of October 12.
By the end of October, roughly 70% of S&P 500 market cap will have reported. Most of the hyperscalers
are scheduled to report during the last week of October.
Consensus expects S&P 500 year/year EPS growth of 27% in Q3. This compares with realized growth of
33% in Q2, excluding some accounting distortions. Analyst estimates show the beneficiaries of AI
infrastructure spending accounting for over 50% of S&P 500 EPS growth this quarter alongside
hyperscaler capex growth of 116%.
We expect most companies will once again surpass consensus earnings estimates this quarter. For the
median S&P 500 stock, consensus estimates show year/year EPS growth slowing from 14% in Q2 to 9%
in Q3. Input cost pressures will likely limit the magnitude of sequential margin expansion in Q3, and the
dollar should provide a modestly smaller tailwind to S&P 500 revenue growth this quarter than last
quarter. However, recent economic growth data have been strong and S&P 500 earnings revision breadth
has remained positive.
