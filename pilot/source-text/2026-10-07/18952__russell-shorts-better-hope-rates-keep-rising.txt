The squeeze setup is getting interesting
Rates remain painfully high, but something underneath the surface has started to change.
The 10-year has stopped making progress, bond vol may be getting ahead of the underlying move,
and Russell has quietly started outperforming again.
That matters because small-cap positioning remains washed out and shorts are still crowded.
Rates don't even need to collapse. If they simply start moving lower, the squeeze fuel is already there.
Slowing
The US 10-year remains at very elevated levels, but despite the feeling that rates have simply
continued higher, we're actually trading around the same levels as five sessions ago.
From a technical perspective, the 10-year could easily trade back down toward the 21-day MA at
5.08%. That alone would provide some relief.
A test of the 50-day MA, currently much lower around 4.87%, would be an entirely different story.

Too much, too fast
Despite the 10-year making little progress over the past few sessions, bond vol has continued to push
higher.
Is the vol stress starting to get ahead of the underlying move?
Any reversal lower in rates could potentially be amplified if elevated bond vol starts resetting quickly.
The same vol dynamic that helped accelerate the stress could start working in reverse.

Green shoots
We outlined our contrarian Russell logic a few sessions ago, and small caps have started to move
higher.
So far, the long-term trend line is holding. Russell is back above the 200-day MA and is now testing a
break above the negative trend channel that has been in place since mid-August.
Still early, but the technical picture is starting to improve.
