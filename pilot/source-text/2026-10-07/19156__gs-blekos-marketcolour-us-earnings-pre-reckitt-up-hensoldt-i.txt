Eurofins (Buy / PT EUR89 from EUR85 / 20% Upside): While top line remains under pressure with

strategic IT investments, its optimized hub-and-spoke laboratory network, and ongoing portfolio review
(e.g., contract exits, disposals with lower profitability).
