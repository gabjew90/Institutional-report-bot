Overview
Dispersion trading has become of increasing focus over the past years, as a way for clients to take
exposure to correlation within the equity market. The classic long dispersion trade involves buying single
stock vol and selling index vol. More recently, we have seen more two way flows with clients also trading
reverse dispersion (i.e. long correlation) as dispersion entry points reached all-time highs earlier this year.
The dispersion trade can be implemented in a variety of formats, the most popular being delta hedged
vanilla options, volatility swaps or corridor variance swaps.
We are now also able to offer clients a way to trade an option on the implied dispersion level, without the
realised component coming into play.

The Structure
TENOR: the trade has an option tenor (e.g. 3m) and a dispersion package tenor (e.g. 1y).
UNDERLYING: the underlying of the dispersion option is the package of forward-starting straddles on
a pre-defined basket of names. The straddles each have a tenor of [1y] from strike date and they strike
on the maturity date of the implied dispersion option.
AT MATURITY of the dispersion option, the option physically settles into the full package of [1Y]
straddles at the strike price. Each straddle is struck at the then prevailing at-the-money spot levels.

PHYSICAL SETTLEMENT of the straddle portfolio ensures that there is a clear & transparent
mechanism for settlement of the dispersion option. The parties may alternatively agree to terminate the
transaction at an agreed price, mimicking a cash settlement at or prior to maturity.
MAX LOSS for buying options is equal to the premium spent. Max loss for selling options is
potentially unlimited.
As the premiums of ATMF option are approximately linear vs volatility in normal volatility ranges,
these straddles provides efficient exposure to the implied dispersion spread (see Figure 2 below).
We can offer 2-way markets on both calls and puts options.
Worked Pricing Example: 3M Put on Implied Dispersion
We show an example of the bespoke basket shown below comprising some US and some EMEA based
stocks.
We target a $1m vega on each leg and see that the resulting current premium for a 1Y ATMS Straddle
package would be $24,875,845 (corresponding to an ATMS Implied vol spread of 24.4v).
We set the 3m dispersion put strike at $21,875,845 (3v lower).
In 3m time, if the price of a 1Y ATMS straddle package is lower than the strike, the investor is in the
money. They can then sell straddle vanilla option package at the strike.
The investor pays $800,000 upfront to buy this this option (equivalent of 0.8v).
