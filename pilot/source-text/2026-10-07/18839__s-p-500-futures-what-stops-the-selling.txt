S&P 500 Futures: What Stops the Selling?
Commitment of Traders exhibited a continuation in the general trend of Non-Dealer S&P 500 futures
selling through September 29th. Categorically, Asset Manager led. Thus higher real rates and/or quarterly
rebalancing provided overhangs. Subsequently, lower probabilities for imminent Fed hikes boosted
equities. But with deferred real rates increasing marginally, S&P futures flows arguably remained bearish.
Given the still elevated net Asset Manager position, expect ongoing challenges until yields convincingly
reverse.
| • | Commitment of Traders exhibited a continuation in the general trend of Non-Dealer S&P 500 futures
selling through September 29th. Non-Dealers sold $10.5bn from September 22nd - 29th, marking their 5th
week of selling in the past 6 since August 18th for a net -$63.7bn. Shorts dominated in the latest release
though long unwinds accounted for >70% throughout the 1.5 month window. | | • | Categorically, Asset
Manager led. In the recent report, Asset Manager sold $11.7bn with Other also selling $1.1bn. Hedge Fund
and Non-Reportable bought $620mm and $1.6bn respectively. | | • | Thus higher real rates and/or quarterly
rebalancing provided overhangs. Asset Manager liquidation equaled $5.5bn. As this coincided with 25bp
jump in US 10 year real yields, the rolling 3 month correlation between Asset Manager gross longs and
USGGT10Y Index moved well into negative territory. Furthermore, the proximity to quarter-end
suggested that general asset reallocation potentially commenced early. | | • | Subsequently, lower
probabilities for imminent Fed hikes boosted equities. Below consensus PCE, dovish Fed speakers, and a
miss on Nonfarm payrolls all reduced hike pricing for October, enabling a 0.70% recovery in S&P index. |
| • | But with deferred real rates increasing marginally, S&P futures flows arguably remained bearish. The
December Adjusted Interest Rate future, a funding proxy, cheapened by ~5bps. | | • | Given the still
elevated net Asset Manager position, expect ongoing challenges until yields convincingly reverse. On
September 29th, before incorporating additional probable sales, Asset Manager net length resided at a
lofty 77% 2 year rank. That said, GS rates research believe recent developments and valuation present a
clearer skew towards lower yields, notwithstanding upward pressure from oil and the resilience of AI
investment plans. |
