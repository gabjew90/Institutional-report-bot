Happy National 401(k) Day!—A Reminder Of How Time Drives Long-Term Wealth Creation
Source: FactSet
Chart of the Day: Valuation Throwback
Data from Fidelity indicates that the average 401(k) balance rose 10.5% in 2Q26 to $156k, marking the fastest quarterly increase since 
2020. Strong market gains also fueled a 17.6% increase in the number of 401(k) millionaires, bringing the total to 769k. However, 19.5% 
of savers carry an outstanding 401(k) loan, up from 19.2% one quarter ago, suggesting some cost-of-living pressures. The key takeaway: 
the average 401(k) millionaire is ~58 years old and has been saving for ~25 years, highlighting the value of time in the market.

Today's  Events
Amid the latest Persian Gulf flareup, Brent closed above $100/barrel for the first time since July 23, and WTI is not far from triple-digits. 
While the US cannot avoid higher fuel prices, the situation is even tougher overseas, where natural gas prices have spiked more than 
oil. Asia’s benchmark (JKM) and its European counterpart (TTF) are up 157% and 182% YTD, respectively. Until LNG shipping recovers, 
Asian and European consumers will be paying more for heat and power. By contrast, US natural gas (Henry Hub) is down 24% YTD.
