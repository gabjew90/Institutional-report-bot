1) Communacopia Day 2 Takeaways and Day 3 Ahead
The overall message from Day 2 was decidedly bullish.
• Resilient Consumer Spending: Multiple companies (BKNG, TOST, Block, ETSY) confirmed that
consumer demand remains steady and highly resilient, with no signs of macro-driven deterioration.
• Strong AI Monetization & Demand: AI is actively driving business acceleration. CDNS reported robust
chip design activity, DT highlighted strong consumption growth (>20%), and VZ is capitalizing on AI data
center connections.
• Operational Efficiency: Companies are successfully leveraging AI and flatter organizational structures to
maintain output while reducing fixed costs, leading to improved operating leverage.
Main highlights:
• Commerce & Travel: Etsy (ETSY) and Booking Holdings (BKNG) reported steady consumer demand.
Etsy is leveraging AI to improve marketplace matching and is partnering with third-party LLMs for
agentic commerce. Booking Holdings is testing GenAI tools (e.g., Penny) and sees LLMs as evolving paid
performance marketing channels. Toast (TOST) and Block (SQ) also noted highly resilient consumer
spending.
• AI & Tech Infrastructure: Cadence Design Systems (CDNS) highlighted robust chip design activity from
traditional semiconductor firms and hyperscalers. SanDisk (SNDK) expects muted NAND supply
alongside rising AI inference demand. Dynatrace (DT) pointed to accelerating core business momentum,
with DPS consumption growing over 20%. MiniMax reported strong ARR ramp-up from its M3 and H3
models despite compute supply bottlenecks.
• Telecom & Hardware: Verizon (VZ) expects 2026 postpaid phone net additions at the top end of its
750k–1 million guidance and sees significant opportunities in AI data center connections. Comcast
(CMCSA) expects modest improvements in broadband ARPU and EBITDA starting in 3Q26, despite
near-term competitive pressures. Crown Castle (CCI) reiterated its leasing guidance of $60-70 million and
is trialing edge data centers. NXP Semiconductors (NXPI) is gradually raising prices to offset input costs.
• Efficiency Gains: Companies like Block and Chime are realizing significant fixed-cost leverage and
product velocity through organizational flattening and unified AI infrastructure.

3) Short Consumer Inertia (GSXUSWCH Index)
The internet made information cheaper. AI agents may make switching cheaper… 3 key points:
• As AI agents increasingly perform these tasks on behalf of users, profit pools that have historically
benefited from customer inaction could come under pressure.
• GSXUSWCH focuses on industries where customer stickiness is driven less by product differentiation
and more by the effort required to take action. Insurance, telecom, travel, subscriptions, marketplaces, and
certain fintech businesses all benefit when customers do not continuously optimize their choices. If AI
agents reduce the cost of comparison and execution, competitive dynamics could shift meaningfully,
putting pressure on pricing power, retention rates and customer lifetime value.
• We like pairing with sector neutral long hedge: {GSCBHSCW Index} or buying medium term put
spreads such as 15Jan27 90% 75% Put Spread indicatively costs 1.81% ref 96.68.
Short GSXUSWCH Index and go long a sector neutral hedge that controls for beta and momentum
(GSCBHSCW Index). This is a 10v pair trade, 57% specific risk and 29% industry bets.
4) AI Companies are Using More Convertibles as Funding
Year-to-date USD convertible bond volume has reached $135 billion, with AI-related borrowers driving
44% of total issuance, on our estimates. Hyperscaler issuers, including Alphabet ($19 billion across two
tranches) and Oracle ($5 billion), have tapped the convertible market this year to optimize debt capacity
and rating agency credit metrics alongside large-scale capex programs.
From an issuer’s perspective, the convertible market remains attractive to reduce borrowing costs and
optimize leverage profiles. Conversion premiums have been widening for newly issued convertible bonds


[Page 6]
and the share of new supply issued with a zero coupon is elevated.
The convertible bond market has established itself as an important funding source for AI infrastructure. In
an environment characterized by tight credit spreads, ongoing dispersion across technology equities, and
substantial capex funding needs, we think convertible bonds remain uniquely positioned to deliver
asymmetric risk/reward profiles and attractive upside convexity for total return credit portfolios.
