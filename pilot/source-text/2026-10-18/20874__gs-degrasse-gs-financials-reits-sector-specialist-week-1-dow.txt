down .. on to week 2 17 Oct 2026
Christian DeGrasse  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  Financials & REITs Sector Specialist, FICC & Equities
17 Oct 2026
Financials and REITs both performed well (+1-2%) and outperformed as the market broadened out from
the tech trade, with beneficiaries across Fins & Reits tending to be a combination of 1) defensive (P&C
Insurance, Exchanges, Healthcare REITs), 2) exposed to the consumer (Payments w/ capital light >
balance sheet fintechs .. Lodging & Retail REITs,) and 3) Information services (where performance
appeared to diverge from some of the more ‘consensus longs’ in Software..) Laggards on the week had a
clear overlap of cyclical exposure (Industrial REITs, Banks) or Capital Markets (Alts, Investment Banks &
moneycenters). Despite the outperformance of both sectors, fundamental investors had a degree of
frustration that price action was driven by the macro – however, there were some ‘preferred longs’ where
price action was indicative of solid follow through across choppy tapes, such as BLK & to lesser extent
MRSH, BNY.

Prints we’re watching next week:
• BX .. Get the sense HFs do want to be long the alts and similar to last quarter, market will likely look for
greenshoots to latch on to for the whole group – that said, incremental concern has crept into the market
on datacenters and investors are starting to ask on alt exposure around some more speculative
assets/credits .. Expect more discussion here on datacenters (bulls have in the past talked about leverage
BX’s funds & platform have to this theme) ..while bears focused on pace of management fee growth &
update on BREIT recovery / BREP fundraising given move higher in rates, and concerns about AI
slowdown (given recent news in credit)

• AXP .. Conversations around agentic commerce have featured AXP’s cardmember services (ie. The rezy
& lululemon credit) – don’t expect this debate to resolve at earnings (too early?) & thus bulls mostly
asking us what bears are playing for here .. Bulls point to improving top line environment to end the year ..
mixed views out there on VCE .. Bears see less flexibility on opex given recent headlines while bulls
believe investment in risk/controls in runrate ..
• NTRS .. Flows in trust banks have been particularly active since STT reported .. Our convos have crowd
more cautious here vs peers into earnings – focused on commentary around expenses & Investments +
potential for diverging rev trends among peers ..
• CBRE .. Focus is on the guide and to what degree higher rates has either ‘paused’ or ‘broken’ pipelines ..
Most feedback the past month has been bearish the brokers (specifically around greater likelihood of a
slower 4Q guide which can leak into 2027 numbers); however CBRE has stood out as the name HFs were
feeling less inclined to remain short into earnings (Julien/GIR calls CBRE ‘approaching trough on trough’
in his preview .. LINK), and this past week we’d point to CBRE flows on desk picking up & inflecting
more positive ..
• WTFC EWBC BPOP .. where’s the bar? These 3 been getting more eyes amidst the higher rate
environment as potential beneficiaries
• Conversely .. Banks w/ CRE & Multifamily exposure getting viewed with extra caution (think FLG,
OZK reporting next week) … Both credit and NIM ..
• FCNCA .. Will FCNCA realize its asset sensitivity (focus on funding costs amidst wind down of the
FDIC note) .. Can expense estimates improve next year? .. Updated thoughts on M&A?
• FITB .. bullish views on efficiency & NIM over near/medium term .. Caution around capital given rate
move
• LAZ .. positioning of 1/10 – most in convos do not expect any resolution/clarity around margin yet
• MSCI .. Expectations on new subs feels low relative to the bullish tone struck at last quarter’s earnings –
but at the same time, more often than not people I speak with like being long into this setup (positioning
score = 6/10)

GS Prime Flow

long buys (4.4 to 1). Capital Markets (especially Asset Managers & Data Services), Insurance, and

Transaction & Payment Processing were the most net sold subsectors, while Consumer Finance and Banks
