Unstoppable to uncomfortable
The AI trade has gone from unstoppable to increasingly uncomfortable in a matter of days.
Goldman's broad AI basket has lost 20% in five sessions, semiconductors have suffered their longest
losing streak in more than a decade, and investors are suddenly asking whether the AI trade has gone
too far.
But here is the confusing part: valuations are back near cycle lows, data-center spending estimates
keep rising, and AI financing costs are hardly screaming crisis.
Is this another violent positioning unwind, or is something more fundamental starting to crack? A few
charts to help make sense of the increasingly confusing AI trade.

Two bogeymen
But is this just another positioning unwind, or are investors finally starting to question the
fundamentals? Here are two top-of-mind fundamental concerns.
1. Credit market stress at the infrastructure layer: AI data centers’ high-yield debt has drawn increased
scrutiny from credit investors, raising questions about the financing model underpinning aggressive
GPU/data-center buildouts.
2. China hardware export data: Recent export figures out of China point to accelerating hardware
exports from China into the rest of the world, threatening the bottleneck thesis in US hardware. China
is exporting ~36% more hardware than a year ago.
