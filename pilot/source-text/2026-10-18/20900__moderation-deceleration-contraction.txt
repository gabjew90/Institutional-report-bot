As good as it gets
The best of the 2026 macro backdrop may already be behind us. Growth expectations are being
marked down, financial conditions are no longer providing the same tailwind, and earnings
momentum is losing breadth. Things aren't necessarily getting bad, but they are incrementally getting
less good. And markets have rarely been particularly fond of the period just after "as good as it gets."

Moderation
And the fading macro tailwind is starting to show up in corporate results, even if the earnings picture
remains far from disastrous.
The very early read from Q3 earnings shows a moderation in EPS beats. With 10% of S&P 500
companies having reported results, 53% of firms have beaten consensus EPS by more than a standard
deviation of analyst estimates. A substantial step down from the average rate of 64% during the past
four quarters.
