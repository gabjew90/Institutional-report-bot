From GIR, Catie raises estimates on AER: We are raising our 3Q 2026 EPS ex-purchase accounting
estimate for AerCap to $5.00 (vs. FactSet consensus of $4.44) from $4.30 prior as we significantly
increase our gain on sales forecast. Higher gain on sales revenue is partially offset by lower rental revenue
due to the higher-than-expected level of asset sales in 3Q and as we update our interest expense outlook.
The company's transaction report noted 85 asset sales (70 from AerCap's owned portfolio), primarily
driven by aircraft sales which came in higher than our forecast (52 owned aircraft sold vs. GSe 29). While
we are raising our FY 2026 asset sales forecast given robust 3Q sales volumes, we now assume the
majority of 2H 2026 sales occur in 3Q and are lowering our 4Q 2026 gain on sales. We are raising our FY
2026 EPS ex-purchase accounting estimate to $19.65 (vs. consensus of $18.99) from $19.50 prior.
Our revised outlook drives our expectation that AerCap will beat 3Q consensus of $4.44 and raise its FY
2026 outlook for the third consecutive quarter as we are forecasting EPS ex-gains of $14.41 (underlying
our $19.50 EPS forecast inclusive of gains), higher than the company's latest EPS ex-gains guidance of

$14.00. In addition to the beat and raise, we expect investors will be focused on gain on sales margin and
net spread. On the former, we expect AerCap to produce a similar gain on sale margin to recent quarters,
bolstered by an elevated proportion of engine, widebody, and current tech asset sales. On net spread, we
are forecasting a sequential decline driven by a combination of a high level of sales (assets part way
through a lease have higher lease yields) and the timing of the roll off of some of the company's lowest
priced debt. Looking to 4Q 2026 and beyond, we expect a material step up in net spread as new aircraft
with higher lease rates deliver, COVID era leases are marked-to-market, grounded aircraft return to
service, and the headwind from marking-to-market the balance sheet subsides. Full note:
