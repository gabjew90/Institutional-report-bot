Perfect storm
Bitcoin has lost roughly a quarter of its value since October 21, with the latest weekend flush triggering
nearly $2 billion in crypto liquidations.
ETF outflows continue, leverage is being flushed out, and buyers have seemingly disappeared. But
there's another twist: with Wall Street scrambling for Anthropic IPO allocations, has Bitcoin become the
market's favorite ATM?

Farside
MARA: Selling Bitcoin to fund the AI
revolution
Here's an irony worth paying attention to.
MARA, one of the world's largest Bitcoin miners, has sold 13,597 BTC for approximately $1.1 billion in
just six weeks, equivalent to 40% of its September-end holdings.
The company used $750 million to repay Bitcoin-backed loans, while future sales could help finance its
AI data-center ambitions.
Including earlier disposals, MARA has now sold more than 40,000 BTC in 2026.
The company that once promised to HODL everything is now selling Bitcoin to help fund the AI
revolution. Even Bitcoin miners are finding more attractive uses for their capital.

Back under water
Bitcoin is once again trading below Strategy's average purchase price. The real question isn't forced

bid when everyone else is selling.

Is Anthropic sucking the oxygen out of
Bitcoin?
And now for perhaps the most intriguing theory of them all.
Anthropic's anticipated IPO has become Wall Street's latest obsession, with reports of enormous
demand for allocations. Some market participants suspect investors are selling liquid assets, including
Bitcoin, to raise cash for the deal.
The comparison with SpaceX is particularly interesting. According to figures attributed to Strive's Matt
Cole, Bitcoin fell 19% in the 50 days preceding the SpaceX IPO and has declined a similar 18% over the
comparable period ahead of Anthropic.
Coincidence? Possibly. But the theory is gaining attention.
The irony would be extraordinary: Bitcoin miners are selling coins to finance AI infrastructure, while
investors may be selling Bitcoin to buy into the hottest AI IPO.
Maybe this isn't just a crypto liquidation.
Maybe it's a giant capital rotation into AI.
Funding
