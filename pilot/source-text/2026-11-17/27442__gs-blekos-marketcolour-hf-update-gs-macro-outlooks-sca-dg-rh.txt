RHM Cut / B&M ##

Good Morning,
US Equities finished Friday the week lower amid a tougher risk background (VIX at 20, 10 Yr at 5.13%, USD breaking
out, Oil > USD90) – SPX -40bps to 7561 (8/11 sectors +ve, Staples +122bps, Energy + 116bps, Tech -172 bps),
NDX -91bps to 25136 (broke 4-wk losing streak, Semis +8% on the week, still below 200 dma, NVDA shed
$500bn of mrkt cap, as AMD + AVGO added ~$500bn+ of market cap, combined), RTY -31bps to 2790. In Europe,
EU Big Oils (GSXEBOIE +1.9%) outperformed while EU Luxury (GSXELUXG -60bps) got hit on Richemont numbers
(CFR SE -3.8%).
 
Stat: Semis have underperformed the S&P by >30% since June (sharpest relative drawdown since 2002). Our GS
AI Semis basket (GSCBSMHX) trades at just 11x 2028 earnings versus nearly 16x for the S&P
 
Interesting: Last week's % net selling in US Financials was the largest in 8 months (99th percentile 5-year).
 
This week: Several key 'AI' catalysts including NVDA (Tues — first report under interim CEO Colette Kress) and BABA
(Thurs), as well as a handful of consumer prints (TSN, ARMK Mon // HD Tues // TJX, TGT, LOW Wed // WMT, ROST,
GAP Thurs // BJ Fri). Slower macro week with focus on US Retail Sales (Tues) and FOMC meeting minutes (Wed).
 
HF Perfomance: Fundamental L/S managers up 1.1% (alpha: -0.3%) last week. Alpha losses were driven by China
equities and Health Care, partially offset by asset selection, crowded longs and short concentration. YTD:
Fundamental LS managers up 9.7% (alpha: +7.1%).
 
HF Positioning: HFs net sold US equities for a second straight week (5 of last 6), driven by short sales in Single
Stocks partially offset by short covers + long buys in Macro Products. US Fundamental L/S Gross leverage rose
+1.6 pts to 209.1% (34th percentile one-year), while US Fundamental L/S Net leverage rose +2.1pts to 45.4% (4th
percentile one-year). 
 
NVDA Flow: Our cash desk also saw persistent NVDA supply from the LO community, with money rotating into
hyperscalers and other pockets of large cap tech. So despite NDX snapping a four-week losing streak (helped by
semis), the appetite to re-engage still feels pretty limited.
 
Dom Wilson: Outlines 10 market themes for 2027: maturing cycle, AI impulse peaking, bonds reclaim macro and
hedging value. (link)
 
SCA downgrade to SELL – Gabriel downgrading SCA to sell. This is a high conviction call (numbers ~10% below
street, -16% downside to pt) that follows on from a lot of work from Gabriel and team on global pulp supply/demand,
further informed by their insights from recent industry events (e.g. London Pulp Week last week). Link
 

RHEINMETALL… -30% eps cuts ... Following Q3 results and the fireside chat with CEO Armin Papperger, Sam on
Friday afternoon took a scythe to his forecasts in an attempt to get ahead of the cuts likely to be presented at the
upcoming CMD. In percentage terms, the largest cuts are in Weapons & Ammo, where commentary in growth
momentum has clearly deteriorated of late. The end result is 2030 numbers come down by -25% at ebit and by -30%
at EPS, landing GSe at ~104 for 2030, meaning the shares still trade on sub 8x 2030e p/e. Link
 
B&M (N / PT to 345p from 220p): We have raised our UK B&M FY27E/FY28E LFL and GM% assumptions to
+2.3%/+3% (from +0.7%/+1%) and +100bp/+30bp (from flat/flat) respectively, driving FY27E/28E PBT +6%/+15% to
£310mn/£344mn. In combination with updated DCF assumptions, this drives a 345p price target (from 220p prior).
Link
 

 
CTA positioning in NDX is back near max short levels.
