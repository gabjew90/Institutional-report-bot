Monday, November 16, 2026
As states and cities consider tighter regulations for new data centers, one potential requirement is behind-the-meter generation. These 
small-scale, on-site power systems reduce strain on the utility grid, helping limit the impact on consumer electricity prices. Market 
research firm Aterio forecasts behind-the-meter capacity at data centers to surge from 4 GW in 2026 to 188 GW by 2031. This should 
boost demand for a broad range of electrical equipment, reinforcing our constructive outlook for the Industrials sector.

Ahead of tomorrow’s retail sales report, consensus expects a 0.2% MoM increase. Earnings from Home Depot (tomorrow), Target and
