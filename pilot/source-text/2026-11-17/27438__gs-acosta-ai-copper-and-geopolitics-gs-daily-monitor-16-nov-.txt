AI, Copper, and Geopolitics - GS Daily Monitor
Good morning,
 
Bottom-line: US equities lower to start the week ahead of AI-related names' earnings this week. Copper sank
to a 4-month low amid tech shares sell-off and weaker Chinese economic data. Ukraine hit the Crimean Bridge
in a record drone strike on Russia. Thailand GDP missed expectations, lifted primarily by AI exports amid
weaker consumption & tourism. Bahrain said it intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks on Sunday. 
 
If you were forwarded this email, reach out to subscribe. 
 
• In Equities, US equities are lower to start the week as AI-related names lead weakness & Asian markets
closing down. 
• Busy week with several AI catalysts in focus as NVDA (Tues) & BABA (Thurs) report earnings this week. 
• In Commodities, copper sank to a 4-month low as tech shares sell-off and Chinese economic data came in
weaker. 
• Crude oil declined amid signs that the physical market is well supplied amidst tensions in the Middle
East. 
• In Europe, Ukraine hit the Crimean Bridge in a record drone strike on Russia overnight. 
• EU Gas dropped as much as 6.2% today as concerns around thin winter inventories balance with record
US exports. 
• In Asia, Thailand GDP missed at +2.9% vs expectations of +3.1% as AI exports lifted while
consumption/tourism remained soft. 
• In India, CPI came in hot as RBI tightening risk renews. 
• In Emerging Markets, Bahrain said it intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks on Sunday with IRGC
targeting a US radar site. 
• In Ukraine, Zelensky said that US envoys asked whether Ukraine would accept ceding Donbas to Russia
in exchange for NATO membership. 
• Chart of the Day: Low levels of EU gas storage have been a concern for the market this year amid the Iran
conflict, but TTF fell in early sessions today.
