 
NeoCloud Bounce Reverses As Operational Risks 
Rise 
 
BY TYLER DURDEN 
FRIDAY, NOV 13, 2026 - 07:10 PM 
Authored by Michael Ball, Bloomberg macro strategist, 
The recent neocloud relief bounce is reversing Friday as execution and funding concerns 
resurface (with doubts about the future capabilities of the 'central bank of AI'), shifting the 
earnings debate beyond a largely set 2027 toward 2028. 
[ZH: Why the Neoclouds are getting smoked: "a big chunk of Nvidia's $50-60BN in quarterly free 
cash flow gets recycled straight back into customers, suppliers and neoclouds, and even a 
temporary pause could pull $50BN or more out of the AI ecosystem. Think of it as QT for the AI 
trade, announced by press release at 8:48am."] 
 
Results from IREN, CoreWeave and Nebius show increasing compute demand is being 
accompanied by greater customer prepayments. However, investors are focusing more on 
funding risks stemming from Nvidia’s leadership change, given its outsized role in financing the 
neocloud ecosystem. That, alongside the execution wobbles highlighted by Nebius, has sapped 
 

 
momentum from the rebound sparked earlier this week by CoreWeave’s earnings and an 
improvement in risk appetite toward the AI trade. 
 
IREN reported a $20.3 billion backlog, CoreWeave ended 3Q with $132.5 billion, while Nebius 
says customers are signing larger, longer contracts at higher prices and already asking about 
2028 capacity. Nebius’ latest deals cleared above $25 million per megawatt, versus 20𝑚𝑖𝑙𝑙𝑖𝑜𝑛−
25 million previously. 
More importantly, customer prepayments are rising. CoreWeave expects about $9 billion of 
customer prepayments and deposits over the next three months. Nebius says prepayments on 
new contracts now cover more than 100% of the associated capex. 
That is sturdier project financing backed by contracted cash flows rather than increasingly 
expensive borrowing.While it doesn’t remove credit risk, it makes any potential slowdown in 
Nvidia’s support less of a threat than Thursday’s price action implied. 
The more visible constraint is execution. Nebius share prices fell after results Thursday as it 
pushed some capacity into 2027 because of power and construction delays rather than 
cancellations.  
The next phase of the AI capex story is increasingly about operational leverage and cash-flow 
improvements, as companies need to protect margins while growing into future earnings 
expectations.  
Neocloud shares were feeling pain Friday morning, with CoreWeave, Nebius and IREN all down 
more than 9%.
