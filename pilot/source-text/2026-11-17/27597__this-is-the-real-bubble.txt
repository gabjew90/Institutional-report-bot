Staple bubble
While everyone is busy debating the AI bubble, perhaps they are looking in the wrong place. The
supposedly boring, defensive corner of the market is now flashing some of the most extreme signals
on the board. The valuation premium of S&P staples to SPX is at the highest level in 10+ years.
Puro Saxena: "The bubble is in consumer staples, NOT in technology where the Forward P/E is near
COVID crash low."

This normally happens next
History isn't particularly kind to this kind of defensive outperformance. Following similar staples rallies,
relative returns over the subsequent three months have typically been negative.

Hedge funds are in
And the hedge funds have finally joined the party. Short covering has driven a sharp unwind in gross
exposure, leaving HFs net long staples for the first time since October 2025.

Vs Tech
S&P 500 Staples just beat S&P Tech by 33% over the last 30 days. That’s the largest such move in ~25
