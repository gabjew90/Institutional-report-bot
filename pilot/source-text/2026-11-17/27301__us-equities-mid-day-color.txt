US Equities Mid-Day Color
Ryan Sharkey  | Jason Klein  | Jack McKeon  |  Ryan Bailey  |  Katherine Reynolds
 
Key Focus:
• Indices in the red today as the AI / Semis trade remains under pressure today. Under the hood, 60% of the SPX
is in the green today and SPX ex-AI (SPXXAI Index) is +40bps. Adding to the angst, rates are higher today
following PPI this morning triggering GS econ to reduce our core PCE estimate and oil is elevated with crude
ticking above $95.
• Semis and AI continue to be under pressure today with NVDA -3.3% (WTD has erased ~$500bn of market cap)
/ AVGO -5.2% / AMD -10.6% providing major pressure on indices today. Unlike yesterday, where there were
intra-Semis rotations and we saw beneficiaries outperform, the whole space is under pressure today
(GSTMTSEM Index -3.8%). Hyperscalers have been the beneficiary of this rotation today. ­Ty Callahan

• News flow has been light with the focus on MSFT’s outage yesterday, the stock is now trading in the green. Our
TMT Cyber Security basket (GSTMTCYB Index) is weak on the day, -1.5%. Focus next week lies on Warsh
speaking on Monday and NVDA earnings Tuesday. 
• Technicals check in: exchange volumes are muted tracking -2% vs 5dma. ETF volumes continue the trend of being
elevated at 32% of the tape while liquidity continues to be challenged with SPX Top of Book just at $4.8mm.
 
Our Flows:
• We are currently a 5 out of 10 in terms of overall activity levels and overall franchise flows are skewed 14.5%
better for sale. 
• Both HFs and LOs are significantly skewed better for sale with supply in information tech, macro products, and
consumer services.  
 
Chart of the Day: 
Within the Technology space, Hyperscalers look particularly attractive. Not only has the P/E fallen sharply, but it has
now virtually closed the gap with the other 495 companies in the US.
