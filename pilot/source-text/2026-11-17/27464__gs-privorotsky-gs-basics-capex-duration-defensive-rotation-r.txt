Rich Privorotsky  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  Managing Director, Global Banking & Markets, FICC & Equities
16 Nov 2026
Asia: Pressure across the AI complex. Taiwan -6%, Korea -3.6%, Nikkei -2.4%, with Advantest -11%,
Lasertec -9%, Tokyo Electron -7%, Hynix -7.81% and TSMC down -5.7%. Massive rotational move with
Japan domestic consumer names +2.75%, healthcare +1.75%. Japan beta -3.97% and high beta momo
-2.81%....AI = beta + momo.
Nvidia: The market treated last week as a tactical redistribution of hardware market share. I worry the real
debate is a much bigger 2nd order question around the duration of the entire capex cycle. Jensen’s role in
accelerating spend and anchoring industry wide infrastructure deployment can't be overstated. The central
question for the tape is whether this remains a rotation or the start of a broader digestion phase in
aggregate AI enterprise capex.
Rates: Multiple sigma moves higher in nominal yields last week, with all three Treasury auctions
reportedly tailing, reinforcing weak demand for duration. PPI was a little tamer, but seemingly made no
difference to the selloff. Historically, moves this violent are difficult for equities, although credit has held
in remarkably well. Perhaps there’s a message here… reduced AI commitments could be negative for
equity growth expectations but positive for credit as balance sheet risk diminishes. Less spending, better
credit, weaker equity multiples.
Flows: Good week for fundamental and quant strategies in our prime book, up just over 1% and ~1.5%
respectively. Fundamental performance largely beta driven, quant driven by alpha. Significant
fundamental re-grossing...Fundamental L/S Gross leverage +2.6 pts (34th percentile 1yr) but Nets barely
budged.. Overall PB booked showed gross getting cut by 2 pts (particularly visible de-gross in
Asia/Europe). Interesting in the US flows remained dominated by single stock shorting, partly offset by
ETF covering. HFs net sold US equities for a 2nd straight week (5 of last 6).... ETF shorts fell 8.6%, the
largest covering in seven months.
UK/France: Burnham speaks ahead of the UK/EU summit, with the reset focused on trade, carbon markets
and youth mobility. The Milburn review puts youth inactivity and welfare reform back in focus, although
any supply side or fiscal benefits will take time. Employment and CPI are the immediate catalysts. Weak
growth and employment against stubborn inflation. France: The social security financing bill faces a
Senate vote Wednesday after its rejection in the National Assembly. Another test of whether the
government can assemble support for fiscal consolidation ahead of the broader budget debate. The issue
for OATs is less the vote itself than whether any credible adjustment survives the parliamentary process.
Week Ahead: Nvidia earnings Tuesday...unlikely to resolve the debate around future AI commitments.
Probably reinforces that the stock is heavily discounted on next years numbers but impossible to know

what is correct multiple for 28+. US retail sales Tuesday will test consumer resilience, followed by a big
week of retail earnings… HD Tuesday, TGT/LOW/TJX Wednesday and WMT Thursday. Watch trade
down, margins and whether the defensive premium is justified. FOMC minutes Wednesday and Fed
speakers, including Williams/Warsh today, matter given the violent rates repricing. Geopolitically, watch
potential Trump/Xi/Putin engagement at the APEC summit later in the week.... Iran on the docket but
seems Ukraine is not. Low expectations for meaningful progress.
Risk: Can the broader market work without AI? Tape is heavily rotational beta/momo come down and low
vol/quality goes bid. with parts of the semiconductor complex approaching historical valuation lows while
consumer staples command enormous premiums (on forward earnings) I’m struggling with the
consistency. Defensives make sense if nominal and real yields are falling alongside growth, but they’re
not. Equities are pricing a slowdown, while rates are pricing persistent inflation and restrictive policy. Is
the better expression to receive rates? Sentiment is objectively depressed… AAII bull/bear indicator near
low end of its range, CNN fear/greed similar ...only beginning to recover. Positioning is the saving grace,
and think that helped drive last weeks squeeze. But with AI leadership under pressure, expensive
defensives and little relief from rates, it’s difficult to identify what drives a sustained recovery. Think its a
push between challenging macro and positioning. Watching Fed Speak today + energy.
