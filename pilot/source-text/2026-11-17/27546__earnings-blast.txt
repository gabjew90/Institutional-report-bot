A Closer Look
Sector Roundup
Dry Powder in Private Markets
KKR & Co: “On the investing side, we deployed $20 billion of capital in Q3 with activity really broad-based across 
geographies and asset classes. This brings us to $98 billion of capital invested over the trailing 12 months, which is 
up 15% compared to the prior period. And with a record $146 billion of dry powder available, we remain incredibly 
well-positioned to build our portfolio for the future.”
Markets May Have Overreacted to Midterm Election Results
Constellation Energy: “I do think we have seen a bit of an overreaction in the market to the election results based 
upon fear that Democrats won't let data centers get built. Obviously, there already has been a great deal of data 
center development in jurisdictions like Illinois and Virginia, where Democrats had control before this election. The 
bottom line is that Democrats care deeply about economic growth, and they care very deeply about the many 
labor jobs that go with data center construction. Indeed, with labor, such a key constituent for Democrats, it's hard 
to imagine that we are going to halt work.”
AI Momentum Expected to Continue in 2027
Applied Materials: “We see 2027 as another record year for the industry and another year of outperformance for 
Applied, in which we expect to make significant progress towards our long-term targets. In short, AI is driving a 
multi-year expansion in semiconductor demand and Applied has never been better positioned to lead and outgrow 
the market.”
