PrimeView: EU Performance Recovering;
HFs Add to Neocloud Shorts & Hyperscaler
Longs
Josh Guilliard
16-Nov-2026, 06:16 ET (EST)
Hedge funds favoured quality compute over funding-sensitive Neoclouds. Following CRWV’s results,
hedge funds used the Neocloud rebound to add >1std of shorts, reflecting funding concerns and Democrats'
scrutiny of data centres. At the same time, hedge funds added +1.7std to Hyperscaler longs, favouring
higher-quality companies with established compute capacity and potentially lower policy exposure. Chart
below.
• US Profit Taking: US Cap Goods was the most net-sold sector. US Software was second most net sold
(-0.8std for the week; -2.5std on Friday), while Biotech also saw significant de-risking (-2.2std over the week).
• In Europe, Healthcare was the most bought sector, extending the defensive rotation underway since
late October. Conversely, hedge funds continued to sell the ex-AI economy, with Low Quality and Cyclicals
the most net sold factors (selling was concentrated in Tech and Software).
• Europe on the road to recovery: Since 5 November, European L/S and Quant strategies have rebounded
+2.1% and +1.4%, respectively, supported by strength in Semis, Capital Goods and Banks, the three largest
hedge fund net exposures in the UBS PB Book. European strategies have now recovered 75% of the
October’s drawdown.
• US and APAC remain under pressure. Both regions continue to struggle following an already difficult
October, although Quant performance has started to improve since 11 November.
