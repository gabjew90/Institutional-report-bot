What is Momentum Made of?
Guillaume Soria  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  Vice President, FICC & Equities
Thu 12 Nov 2026, 12:41pm ET
The momentum regime shift that started this summer is re-emerging as 12m Momentum (GSPRHIMO)
had its best outperformance day in our records vs 3m Mo (GSPRHMO3) and 6m Mo (GSPRHMO6)
yesterday. In fact, correlation between 12m momentum and 3m momentum is now negative as the AI trade
has unwound. The number of AI names in the short leg of 3m and 6m momo has sky rocketed as the
number of AI names in the long leg has moved lower. This is also consistent with the net sector flows
based on momentum tenor, where info tech was net sold in both 3m and 6m momentum, but slightly
bought in 12m mo. There is also industry level dynamics within each sector like Semis vs Software (where
12m Mo will be a net seller of semis and buyer of software), but still tells the same story: the make up of
momentum is changing.
Positioning in momentum has collapsed with exposure on our PB Book falling sharply from its 2026 peak
to the 23rd percentile of its 5-year range, as the consensus winners are no longer crowded (Data Center
basket crowding at the 1st percentile over 6 months). We have flagged that any resumptions in the rotation
could provide opportunities to tactically short former 12m momentum short names or broadening (lower
quality software, staples, etc.) where the fundamental story has not supported the move higher.
If the rotation resumes and 3m and 6m momentum go back to outperforming, 12m Momentum and Beta
could continue lagging while Quality can extend its outperformance. Realistically, momentum volatility
remains elevated (60-day, about 3.5x the S&P 500), so rotations and reversals are likely to stay a feature of
the factor until the market settles on new long-term leadership.
