Systematic sellers
Elevated realized volatility is forcing systematic funds to cut risk. Vol control and risk parity strategies
are estimated to have sold more than $30bn combined over the past week.
The more interesting setup is in vol control. As Goldman points out, these funds could start re-risking in
December/January if volatility settles down, potentially creating a meaningful tailwind for equities.
