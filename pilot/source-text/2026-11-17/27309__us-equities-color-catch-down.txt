US EQUITIES COLOR: CATCH DOWN
U.S. Equities Color
13 Nov 2026
Equities lower to close out the week with broader semis / AI thematics “catching down” to NVDA. Callahan
said well: With the benefit of hindsight, the ‘rubber band’ in Semis (seemingly) got stretched too far. The
macro has also quietly gotten tougher again with VIX at 20 / 10yr back at 5.13% (following hot CPI) / USD
breaking out / Oil back in high-$90s, etc. WTD looked more rotational (NVDA shed $500bn of mrkt cap, as
AMD + AVGO added ~$500bn+ of market cap, combined). This thematic was also present in our flows with a
glaring drumbeat of supply in NVDA from LO community with $s rotating into Hyperscalers and other pockets
of large cap tech. Still ongoing debate on the broader Semis landscape as investor continue to digest recent
newsflow (Nvidia + Election). Overall, both LO and HF cohorts finished moderate net sellers on the week.   
   
Week Ahead: S&P implied move through next Friday (11/20) is 1.93%. Busy week ahead with several key
'AI' catalysts including NVDA (Tues — first report under interim CEO Colette Kress) and BABA (Thurs), as
well as a handful of consumer prints (TSN, ARMK Mon // HD Tues // TJX, TGT, LOW Wed // WMT, ROST,
GAP Thurs // BJ Fri). Slower macro week with focus on US Retail Sales (Tues) and FOMC meeting minutes
(Wed).
 
Prime: HFs net sold US equities for a second straight week (5 of last 6), driven by short sales in Single
Stocks partially offset by short covers + long buys in Macro Products. US ETF shorts decreased -8.6% – the
largest % covering in 7 month, while 8 of 11 sectors were net sold, led by Info
Tech/Financials/Healthcare/Staples. US Fundamental L/S Gross leverage rose +1.6 pts to 209.1% (34th
percentile one-year), while US Fundamental L/S Net leverage rose +2.1pts to 45.4% (4th percentile
one-year).  
   
 This week's % net selling in US Financials was the largest in 8 months (99th percentile 5-year).
