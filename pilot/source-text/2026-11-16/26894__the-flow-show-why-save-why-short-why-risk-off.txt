Why save, why short, why risk-off 
     
   
 
 
Scores on the Doors: oil 54.0%, US stocks 10.9%, global stocks 8.8%, US$ 4.9%, cash 
3.2%, HY bonds -0.5%, IG -2.8%, govt bonds -3.6%, gold -10.4%, bitcoin -18.9% YTD. 
Zeitgeist: “If dollar keeps surging like this it’s going to turn into a wrecking ball.” 
The Biggest Picture: “why save?”…US household equity wealth down ~$3tn past 
month, but still up ~$9tn in '26, up $19tn since Jan'25 Trump inauguration (Charts 2 & 3 
– BofA GWIM equity holdings map perfectly into $74tn US household equity wealth); 
hard to get a recession when consumption 70% of GDP & K-shape rich feeling so flush.  
Tale of the Tape: “why short?”...past week Taiwan announces $15bn fund to support 
stocks (up 50% YTD albeit off 13% from highs), more Price Keeping Operation moves by 
Korea & China authorities...keeps Asia retail bid for tech (and well-timed given NVDA); 
global policymakers see stock markets as “too big to fail,” why no one short stocks.  
The Price is Right: “why risk-off?”...global tightening cycle (>70 rate hikes Jun'26-
May’27) ends Apr FOMC at earliest; meantime US$ surge/gold <$4k = global liquidity in 
retreat, CPI means no release valve for risk assets from lower UST yields despite oil -
15%, cyclicals (lumber -21%, metals -11%, blue-collar semis -26%) say next revision to 
“boom” forecasts (5-6% nominal GDP, >15% EPS in ’27 – Charts 4 & 5) down not up.

On Portfolios: asset allocation diversification winning in '26 despite equity breadth 
losing...25/25/25/25 portfolio of US stocks/bonds/commodities/cash annualizing 12% 
YTD, best return since '21 (Chart 7); worst years for “permanent portfolio” scarred by 
war/macro shocks & policy mistakes (1931, 1937, 2001, 2008), while best years follow 
war/macro shocks (1933, 1950, 2021) or signal mid-cycle reacceleration (1985 & 1995).  
On Price: S&P 500 on track (13% annualized) for 4th consecutive year of double-digit 
gains; since 1871 only happened WWII (1942-1945), post-WWII (1949-1952), and 
internet (1995-1999...when S&P 500 logged 5 straight years of >10% gains).  
On Policy: Oct NFIB survey saw number of small businesses planning to raise prices 
jump to highest level since Jul’22; strong correlation with US CPI and historically 
consistent with at least 3-4% inflation (Chart 8); note Feb'21 was last month that Fed’s 
2% CPI target was met (67 months ago); inflation & lack of credibility why markets now 
pricing in rate hikes FOMC Dec 9th, Jan 27th, Mar 17th. 
On Politics: 8 Senate seats flipped from GOP to DEM (thus far) in midterms; such a 
dramatic electoral swing has only occurred 4 times in 39 US elections since 1950 and 
unemployment rate was on average 6.5% at the time of vote; in '26 U-rate just 4.1% this 
time and massive DEM tsunami; shows how febrile electorates are in populist 2020s, 
reinforces Anything But Bonds secular asset allocation...bar for fiscal stimulus and 
government intervention much lower...why investors don’t want to touch US Treasuries 
until they yield 5½-6½%; note despite DEM tsunami no jump in CDS of states that 
flipped to populist socialism (e.g. Michigan – Chart 9)...good, but Oct NFIB survey 
showed highest number small businesses saying taxes #1 problem since May'21 (Chart 
10)...less good.  
Trades of the Year: note to contrarians...best trades of 2026 were long oil-short 
crypto, long energy-short discretionary, long semiconductors-short telecom, long small 
cap value-short mega cap growth (Chart 11), long Taiwan-short China, long cyber-short 
defense; winning themes of 2026 were war>peace, socialism>capitalism, boom>bust, K-
shape>C-shape, hikes>cuts. greed>fear, AI issuance>AI job losses, IPOs>buybacks. 
concentration>breadth.
