GS Malmstrom - GS Global 2027 Outlook 13 Nov 2026
Jacob Malmstrom  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  Vice President, Global Banking & Markets, FICC & Equities
Fri 13 Nov 2026, 8:51am ET
We are out with GS Macro forecasts for 2027 where Peter Oppenheimer sees a positive backdrop and an
expectation of equities moving higher but with high volatility. His theme for 2026 was a broadening equity
bull market, with greater participation across regions, sectors and themes. This has been correct despite
rising oil prices and bond yields. Main driver has been strong corporate earnings growth across all regions.
Turning to 2027 Peter sees profit and AI capex growth looking poised to slow in most regions. However,
some of this has already been reflected with equity valuations coming down substantially, particularly in
the Tech sector. Growth is expected to slow down in 2027 along with earnings but moderating inflation,
bond yields coming down along with oil prices, Peter sees a path for the equity bull market to continue.
The range of potential outcomes has widened and, given stock concentration in the US and Asia, is
particularly dependent on AI capex progress. Peter's recommended weightings reflect his assessment of
risk-adjusted returns. He focus on:
Growth – As earnings are set to slow and margins moderate, investors are likely to reward these
companies more highly, particularly as valuations become more attractive. We've already seen some of the
AI companies de-rating sharply, reflecting anxiety about peak AI capex speed and the possibility of slower
returns. This is leaving attractive entry points in large capex Hyperscalers, which have strong balance
sheets and diversified income streams. There is also upside in more cyclical growth areas such as memory
and chips. Peter remains OW US and Japan, neutral Asia and EM and UW Europe.
