GS Consumer | Morning Trading Update (US)
US equity futures point to a modestly firmer open (SPX +25bps, NDX +15bps, RTY +25bps), with
the UST 10-year yield down 2.5bps to 4.85% and WTI off 10bps at $88.65, amid light newsflow beyond
the global Microsoft 365 outage (MSFT -1.5% pre-market). NVDA is rebounding (+2%), while post-
earnings, CBRS leads (+17%), AMAT is firmer (+60bps) and CSCO lags (-2.75%). Today's docket
features US PPI, Eurozone GDP, Fed speakers Collins, Bowman, Daly and Jefferson, and the RVTY
investor day. Looking ahead, focus shifts to Warsh on 11/16, NVDA earnings 11/17, retail prints (HD,
LOW, TGT, WMT), Tencent and BABA results, and the Trump-Xi meeting 11/18-11/19. Macro note:
Link.   
 
Consumer focus is on staples/beverages to finish week but also have earnings from DDS, FLO (-9%),
OFRM (-8%) and ratings changes away including a DPZ upgrade and LOW, YETI, FND, TSCO
downgrades. Discretionary lagged the market for the third straight session yday and has now given up
most of the post-election outperformance. Price action feels back to the old 1H26 regime of late and
seems at least partly driven by momentum/factor shifts where 12 month winners have started to
outperform (AI positioning much cleaner) and consumer has less/wrong way exposure there; More on
the momentum dynamics here (link) but found it interesting that future rebalances of momentum should
be supportive of consumer. In addition to the earnings pickup next week, we also have NKE investor
day on Monday-Tuesday.   
 

 
 
Retail  
DDS +0.5%... modest EPS and comp beat. EPS was $8.38 (vs $8.08 cons). Revenue was $1.48B
(vs $1.48B cons). Comps were 1.0% (vs 0% cons), led by home/furniture, men's and juniors'/children's.
Retail gross margin was flat at 45.3%, while SG&A was 30.3% of sales (vs 30.0% last year) on higher
payroll. Ending inventory increased 4%, slightly ahead of sales growth. There was no formal EPS or
sales guidance.
 
Staples   
FLO -9%... miss on EPS and EBITDA with a deep FY guide cut. EPS was $0.10 (vs $0.15 cons).   
Revenue was $1.17B (vs $1.18B cons) on volume of -7.0%. Adjusted EBITDA was $78.9M (vs $97.2M
cons), with margin of 6.8% (vs 8.2% cons). FY EPS guide was cut to $0.63-0.68 (vs $0.78 cons and
$0.75-0.85 prior guidance), revenue to $5.016-5.063B (vs $5.09B cons and $5.070-5.142B prior
guidance), and adjusted EBITDA to $415-430M (vs $460.6M cons and $453-481M prior guidance). The
Nature's Own relaunch is improving more slowly than anticipated, mainly in loaf. CEO: "We expect the
environment to remain challenging in the near term, but we remain confident in the long-term potential
of the business." Conf Call at 8:30am.  
 
OFRM -8%...modest 3Q revenue and EBITDA beats are overshadowed by a steep FY guide cut
tied to the Tractor Wheels recall. EPS was -$0.09 (vs -$0.08 cons). Revenue was $89.1M (vs
$88.4M cons) on volume growth of 32%. Adjusted EBITDA was -$1.0M (vs -$2.6M cons), but gross
margin fell 600 bps to 36.1% on club trade investment, mix and cooler placement costs. FY revenue
guide was cut to $290-300M (vs $320.3M cons and $327-335M prior guidance), and adjusted EBITDA
to -$4.5M to -$7M (vs $2.9M cons and $3-4.5M prior guidance), implying a sharp 4Q hit. CEO: "We are
committed to resolving the Tractor Wheels situation correctly, continuing to earn the trust of families

and retailers, and executing against the significant growth opportunity ahead of us."
