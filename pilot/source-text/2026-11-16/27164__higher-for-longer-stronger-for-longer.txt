*Sales desk commentary. Not a product of UBS Research. For institutional investors only.*
• United States: Microsoft outlook has returned right on time for the morning note. The USD bid continues for
the 4th consecutive day with market cumulative market implied pricing of ~48bps for the December + January
meeting, which has converted to UBS Economics’ view for two consecutive hikes. Over the last two weeks.
The repricing in SOFR whites has been sharp with the reds / greens parallel shifting upwards to where
markets were trading approximately a month ago (figure #1). The evolution of SOFR pricing was temporarily
decoupled from the US growth impulse and rather traded in tandem with oil (figure #2). Later today, the
October PPI is expected to result in 12m changes for final demand and ex food / energy of +6.6% and +5.0%,
respectively, with both making new post-2022 highs (figure #3). The composition driving higher final demand is
concentrated within goods, predominantly in final demand for energy. The recent rates rout has likely reached
a pause given the repricing in SOFR rates vol uncertainty has reached comparable levels during prior bouts of
uncertainty (figure #4 plots forward vol)
• Canada: FX seems to care very little about macro developments given the broad BBDXY bid. For most of the
year, USDCAD has traded within a range of 1.39 to 1.43 as economic data, interest rate differentials, and
evolving commodity prices have weighed onto the currency to breakout into a new range. If the CAD inflation
print undershoots market estimates, expectations to grind past 1.43 level as the current gap in rate differentials
is at the widest all year and likely widens further (figure #5).
• Eurozone: The 3Q26 GDP confirmed the initial reading for +0.4% with y/y results of +1.3%. EURUSD has
not broken 1.10 the figure, but decisively moved through the 1.11 level. Figure #6 plots the medium-term trend
strength and trend quality highlighting that EURUSD is by in large the sharpest laggard
