Company Commentary
Increasing Challenges with Trouble at the Top, a Significant
Founder Premium
After market-close NY Times reports CEO Jensen Huang potentially with another affair
(LINK) with a NVDA direct-report employee. There are also WSJ reports of NVDA board
looking to oust him as CEO and searching for a replacement CEO (LINK). NVDA is down
~9% AH on the news. We believe NVDA's supply-chain moat remains solid but potential
future investigations and distractions could be challenging into 2027E. We believe Jensen
as a founder of NVDA and one of the most successful CEOs in the tech industry could
potentially step down as CEO but remain involved in a non-executive chairman capacity.
Maintain Outperform, as NVDA is a key AI enabler, but there are increasing challenges
with troubles at the top.
Key Points
Key person risk - CEO Jensen potentially steps down with multiple allegations, but
remains non-executive chairman? AMC NYT reported another affair with a NVDA
direct-report employee (LINK). WSJ also noted NVDA board is looking to oust CEO
(LINK). Jensen founded NVDA in 1993; at 33 years he's one of the longest-serving
and most successful CEOs, having built NVDA to ~$4.5Tr market cap. We believe an
unplanned departure for Jensen could be catastrophic as the visionary driving an industry
transformation from CPU compute to GPU accelerated compute, building an ecosystem,
in many ways a personal relationship anchor to CSPs/Neoclouds/supply chain while also
emulated by CSPs/competitors. We believe Jensen could potentially step down as CEO,
but remain as a non-executive chairman not involved in day-to-day operations.
What to do with the stock here? NVDA is down 11% week-to-date. We believe this will
be a significant distraction for NVDA for the next 6-7 months and so the stock pullback
does imply some key person risk discount applied. We believe it could impact some of
the deal execution, but we see any share shifts not that material. NVDA is down ~11%
since Monday vs. SPX up 1%, down 31% m/m vs the SOX down 2%, and now trading at
~11x F28E P/E vs the Rev/EPS growing ~71%/73% y/y. NVDA is a major funding source
for neoclouds, frontier clouds and hyperscalers with NVDA FCF F28E(C2027) ~$340B,
F29E(C2028) ~$500B+, and key to keeping the AI cycle turning.
Earnings on Nov 17 - We see upside into OctQ/JanQ earnings with GB300 and VR200
momentum. Heading into OctQ (Nov 17) earnings we expect strong deliveries (noted
here) with GB300 momentum OctQ/JanQ driving better GMs, combined with VR200
deliveries to OAI, MSFT (covered by Gregg Moskowitz), OAI, and SPCX (covered by Brett
Linzey). We also see NVDA Vera CPU-only racks shipping to AWS, OCI and leading AI labs
as NVDA sees F28E revs up 100% y/y.
Valuation
Maintain NVDA at Outperform with $250 PT, ~16x F28E P/E, as we see NVDA attractive
with F28E Rev/EPS up ~71%/~73% y/y, and leading its peer group with ~72-75% GMs. We
see NVDA leading the AI market with a >$3-4T AI Infrastructure market by 2030E, but there
are challenges with CEO Jensen potentially having to step down creating uncertainties in
roadmap and deal execution.

The Mizuho View
Investment Thesis
We see NVDA remaining the leader in
the AI training and inference chips for
Data Center applications, with the leading
market share. We continue to see NVDA
executing its roadmap towards >$1T
Blackwell+Rubin revs C25-27E and into
a >$3-4T TAM by 2030E as $25-45B/
GW topline opportunity, with strong
Blackwell ramps in 2026E, Vera Rubin
and its Groq LPU now in production, with
>30% ASP tailwinds on VR200 NVL72
racks. Additionally, we note upside from
CPU ramps with Agentic AI as NVDA
sees F27E CPU revs at ~$20B with
F28E potentially up >100% y/y. We see
Gaming (we estimate >75% PC share) are
expected to remain healthy and stable,
while NVDA’s automotive ADAS pipeline
remains strong.
Base Case/PT
NVDA remains well-positioned in the machine learning, deep learning, and AI markets. Our
$250 PT reflects ~16x our F28E EPS, within its historical range ~15-30x while remaining
the unquestioned market share leader in merchant AI GPU shipments.
Bull Case/PT
NVDA has significant market-leading opportunities in the AI/DC, Gaming, and Automotive
markets; we see a bull case PT of $370, ~19x our upside F28E EPS of $19.28.
Bear Case/PT
If NVDA is unable to maintain strong growth in key markets as well as fails to continue
to capitalize on AI opportunities, we see a bear case PT of $150, at ~11x our downside
F28E EPS of $13.97.
Risks
Escalations of export restrictions to China and other geopolitical tensions (Taiwan) or
a significant pullback in AI Server spending could be major headwinds. We would also
note limited CoWoS capacity at TSMC, and other suppliers, potential for FTC lawsuits,
and growing competition both domestically and in China, where shipments have yet to
resume following re-approval of shipments from the US govt.
