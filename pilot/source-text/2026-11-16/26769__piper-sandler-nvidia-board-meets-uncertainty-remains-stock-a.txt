CONCLUSION
Multiple news reports of NVDA board meeting late yesterday to decide the future of NVDA
CEO Jensen Huang. With no definitive succession plan in place we think it's likely the board
could appoint NVDA CFO as interim CEO, while it conducts a search for a new CEO. In this
scenario, Ian Buck the VP of Nvidia's Hyperscaler and HPC business and 22-year veteran
of NVDA is a strong internal candidate to step-up, in our view. He is well liked and respected
by investors. Fundamentally, we don't see a change of CEO as changing the investment
case. Granted there is higher risk. However, this does not change the direction of travel. In
CY27 co should grow revenues +70% and drive $15 EPS. At current 10x trough multiple
we see $150 as floor for the stock. Short term stock to remain under pressure until we get
better visibility but presenting a LT buying opportunity.
•
Board meeting late yesterday: No official company announcement yet following the
board meeting called for late yesterday, as reported in multiple news outlets, to decide
the future of NVDA CEO and Founder. With no apparent succession plan in place it's not
obvious who could potentially fill the NVDA CEO role. In this scenario, we think it likely
that an internal candidate would be preferred, who knows the company, customers and
Datacenter products. Ian Buck as VP of the Hyperscaler and HPC business stands out
to us, a 22-year NVDA veteran who is well known and respected by investors.
•
Fundamentals unchanged: The velocity and proliferation of AI continues with the
demand for compute remaining significantly ahead of supply this year and next.
Importantly, from a product standpoint, the die is already cast on products and roadmaps
for the next 2 generations of NVDA products. While engineering issues are never ending,
the big platform and architecture decisions are already made for the next ~3 years and
the company is soldout for CY27 and largely CY28. Discussions with customers center
around 2029 and beyond. Thus, a change in CEO is not going to alter the direction of
travel from a product or customer perspective. What a new CEO could change would be
around the extent of supply chain and ecosystem support/financing. That said, in FY28
we expect the company to grow revenues +70% y/y and drive earnings of ~$15. Nvidia is
currently trading at a 20-year trough multiple of 10x. We see $150 as a floor for the stock.
While the stock is likely to remain under pressure short term, until we get a resolution
and better visibility, we think current levels present a great LT buying opportunity.
