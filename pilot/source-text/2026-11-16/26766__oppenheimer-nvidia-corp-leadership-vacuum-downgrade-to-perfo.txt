NVIDIA Corp.
Leadership Vacuum? Downgrade to Perform
SUMMARY
We are downgrading shares of NVDA and removing our price target following reports
(WSJ, NYT) of founding CEO Jensen Huang’s imminent departure amidst allegations
of a 2nd extramarital affair. Credible media reports circulated Wednesday evening
that NVDA’s board is preparing to part ways with Huang following a widely reported
relationship with an alleged Chinese spy compounded by new allegations of a
relationship with an NVDA executive. This “one-two” punch appears to have forced
the board’s hand. Huang’s role as the “face of AI” cannot be overstated. His uniquely
skillful, forward-thinking, steady and tireless leadership has been critical to NVDA’s
success in becoming the undisputed AI leader and world’s largest company. Huang is
a hands-on leader with dozens of direct reports. We believe he is deeply involved in
all strategic decision making. We see massive shoes to fill and no obvious successor.
His departure presents a material, potentially thesis-changing event. NVDA boasts
an incredibly deep bench of talent. We see risk that a Huang departure creates
a “brain drain” situation at the AI giant, threatening NVDA’s pace of innovation—a
critical competitive advantage. NVDA reports earnings Tuesday (11/17). While we
see upside to the F3Q (Oct.) print and F4Q (Jan.) outlook, we expect fundamentals
to take a backseat near term. The departure of Mr. Huang could, in our view,
translate to a lingering share overhang. We are maintaining our CY26/27/28E EPS
as we contemplate any future impact on the outlook. Within our coverage perceived
beneficiaries of any NVDA dislocation include AMD, MRVL and AVGO.
KEY POINTS
■DC (93% of sales) expected up 14% Q/Q (+99% Y/Y) to $101.8B. DC splits 81/19
Compute/Networking. DC customer base splits 55/45 Hyperscale/ACIE (AI Cloud,
Industrial & Enterprise). Hyperscale concentrated, with four top customers making
up nearly half of total DC sales. ACIE is more diverse with >250K customers. ACIE
has the potential to grow faster/larger than hyperscale over time, in our view. Initial
VR200 shipments began F3Q, ramping F4Q. VR200 delivers 30x higher throughput
per MW, 35x lower token cost (vs. GB300). VR200 has POs from every major CSP,
AI cloud, and system OEM. Vera Rubin Ultra (VR300) follows 2H27. Vera CPU
expected $20B in CY26, on par with x86 rivals (INTC, AMD).
■Networking (19% of DC) expected up 8% Q/Q (+138% Y/Y). Networking driven by
NVLink (scale-up) and Spectrum-X/Infiniband (scale-out). Spectrum-XGS (scale-
across) expands SAM. We expect mix to increasingly favor ethernet driven by CSP
demand. Edge Computing (7% of topline) expected up 8% Q/Q (+34% Y/Y).
■F2Q FCF $21.4B (22%). We model $185B FCF this year, $347B next year. GM
75% in F2Q. We model GM down 100bps to 74% in F3Q on rising component
costs. We expect GM to bottom at 71.7% in 4Q before expanding into CY28. $26B
returned to shareholders in F2Q, primarily buybacks ($20B). Mgmt has returned
~60% of FCF to shareholders YTD (vs. target 50%). We maintain our CY26/27/28E
EPS estimates of $9.40/$16.84/$23.27.
■Down 7% YTD (SOX +49%). NVDA trades at 7x our CY28E EPS vs. its three/
five-year average of 21x/25x. Shares traded down 7% following credible reports
of Huang’s looming departure. We see the current cloud of uncertainty pressuring
shares near term. Leadership risk forces us to the sidelines. Downgrade to Perform
and remove our $315 price target.

INVESTMENT THESIS
Nvidia has transformed from a graphics company to a premier
leading full stack AI solutions platform company. GPUs were
initially used for graphics in video games. Given its parallel
processing capabilities and thousands of computing cores,
GPUs were ideal for training AI models used in datacenters.
Through the Mellanox acquisition, NVDA expanded its footprint
to high-speed networking with DPUs and scale-up and scale-
out interconnects. NVDA further broadened its DC offerings
with Arm-based CPU. NVDA now provides full rack AI systems.
We see several structural tailwinds driving sustained out-sized
top-line growth in high performance gaming, datacenter/AI and
autonomous driving vehicles.

COMPANY DESCRIPTION
Nvidia is a leading semiconductor company specializing in GPUs, AI accelerators, and data center AI systems. Its chips are optimized for
artificial intelligence, high performance gaming, and autonomous systems. Silicon portfolio includes GPUs, CPUs, DPUs, NVLink, and CPO
switches supporting full rack AI systems. CUDA software ecosystem further accelerates computing workloads.
