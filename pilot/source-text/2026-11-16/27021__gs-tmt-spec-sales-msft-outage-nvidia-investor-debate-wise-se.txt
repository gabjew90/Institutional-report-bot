GS TMT SPEC SALES – Friday 13th November 
Paraskevidekatriaphobia meets its modern upgrade - This morning the same energy hit Microsoft 365
outage - Outlook, Teams and Copilot are still reeling from an overwhelmed core process and a mysterious
traffic surge (no cyberattack, just pure Friday the 13th vibes). Ok I could not resist and an irrational fear of
Friday the 13th is called paraskevidekatriaphobia. The superstition draws on the number 13’s unlucky
reputation — Loki crashing a 13-guest banquet, Judas as the 13th at the Last Supper. MSFT here is still
down and most operating off backup email so reading email has never felt more mythological… or more
dependent on tech that clearly didn’t get the memo about not being the unlucky guest.
 
 Nvidia and Jensen Huang’s resignation dominated trading and investor incoming , with Colette Kress
named interim CEO. NVDA opened sharply lower and traded as much as –14.3% before recovering to close
–4.3%. Broader markets held up better: QQQ finished modestly green after a –1.5% open, while the semis
complex climbed out of an early –3.5% hole. Relative winners included AMD (+6%), AVGO (+5.5%), ANET
(+5%), CLS (+5% on TPU exposure), and GOOGL (+1.8%) as the main non-semi beneficiary on custom-
chip share. On the other side, neoclouds reversed the prior session’s strength (NBIS –17%, CRWV –7%)
on concerns over the loss of Nvidia as the ecosystem’s financing backstop, while China-exposed names sold
off on remote-control rules and potential Megaspeed/Singapore scrutiny (BABA –7%, VNET and GDS both –
11%).
The core investor debate has shifted to structural questions. Nvidia has long acted as lender of last resort
to parts of the AI ecosystem, particularly neoclouds, and much of its strategic edge has rested on Jensen’s
personal relationships—aggressive multi-year capacity pre-purchases, deep ties with Asian suppliers, and
the credibility to underwrite long-duration demand. The key uncertainty is whether a new CEO will have
the same willingness or authority to deploy the balance sheet aggressively. Physical supply is unlikely to
be disrupted near term, but the durability of that pre-commitment model is less clear. A secondary risk is that
if the spying allegations gain further traction, it could revive broader US/China regulatory measures
including a return of the MATCH Act, which would be negative for ASML (and to a lesser extent ASM). For
more in Jim’s take
 
RESULTS
• Wise’s results were broadly in line street BUT more importantly its removed the Belgium overhang with no
admission of wrongdoing. Settlement number not disclosed = it’s immaterial. On US licencing all remediation
completed and expect an update in 2027. On Platform they talk to launching with two large banks in the US
‘silently’ = i.e. they just haven’t done PR around it, but saying momentum is good. Taking this plus inline
numbers should see stock up  
Looking at the results: 1) net revenue growth implies a deceleration in H2 to c.18% yoy ex-fx even(H1 was 22%)
at the upper end of the FY27 guidance (15-20%), which GIR view as conservative given both card and transaction
revenue strength alongside additional optionality from interest income; 2) gross margins were strong in the

quarter(84%) with further upside potential ahead, while the pace of OPEX, though elevated in H1, has reached a
peak and is expected to decelerate going forward, potentially paving the way for operating leverage; 3) Platform
momentum remains strong, with c.6% of cross-border volumes now from Platform (vs. c.5% at FY26 results),
alongside the announcement of two new top-20 US bank wins signed and live in H1FY27, underlining both the
strength of the Platform opportunity and the fast ramp-up of new wins. GIR estimates go up – revenue unchanged;
high GM and slightly opex sees IBT estimates change by -1% to +3% across FY27-31E. The results also point to
some operating leverage to come. 
 
• AMAT: Solid quarter with guide above on revenue and margins good enough given positioning in. Neutral
read to Semicap space as most have already reported. Per GIR “despite the recent sector-wide selloff, we
believe investor positioning was very constructive heading into the print given incrementally more positive WFE
commentary from peers. We believe the company’s results set the company up well to deliver market share gains
against the backdrop of accelerating WFE growth in 2027. Although management set an expectation for industry
WFE growth of ~30% for 2027 that was in line with peers and constrained by cleanroom additions, GIR forecasts
contemplate acceleration to ~45% - which suggests upside to Street expectations.   
   
 
• Cisco Beat and raise on networking and opex; while orders miss. The margin outlook and upgrade was
better-than-expected with CSCO demonstrating strong operating leverage, though growing hyperscale revenue
mix may be headwinds to gross margin in F2H27. F1Q27 product orders growth of 21% yoy (+14% excluding
webscale AI) missed and decelerated qoq from F4Q26 when orders increased +35% yoy (+25% yoy excluding
webscale) with campus orders increasing ~15% yoy (with tough comps driving a deceleration for the 3rd
consecutive quarter) and hyperscale + SP orders also decelerating.
 
• CTS Eventim 3Q26: Mixed its missed on Q3 revenues (driven by Live Entertainment) with group EBITDA in-line
(driven by better Ticketing margins) and the company reiterating its FY26 guidance. 3Q26 group revenues of
€809mn, which was -2.5% below Vara Consensus, driven by weaker Live Entertainment revenues, while Ticketing
was in-line. Group adj. EBITDA of €134mn was in-line with consensus, implying margins of 16.5% (consensus:
16.1%), reflecting slightly stronger than expected margins in Ticketing. The company reiterated its 2026 guidance.
At the group level and for the Ticketing segment, management expects all KPIs to be in-line with/slightly higher
than the previous year. For the Live Entertainment segment, management expects revenues to be in-line with the
previous year and moderate growth in adj. EBITDA and EBIT. GIR making small changes to estimates - 2026-31E
group revenue/ adj. EBITDA change by -1%/0%, on average
 
• NEBIUS - Q3 beat but FY26 guidance lowered on delays that now come through in 2027. Note that its now
guided FY27 revenue to be between $12-13bn (vs cons of $11.7bn). Strong Q3 beat — revenue of $981M
(+7% vs cons), adj. EBITDA of $466M (+30%)— but the stock reversed from +5% to -9% pre market on guidance
(the other debate its exposure to Nvidia). FY2026 revenue guidance seen at the lower end of $3.0–3.4B (vs cons
$3.4B), due purely to third-party GPU capacity delays pushing expected 2026 supply into H1 2027; underlying
contracts remain intact and demand is unimpaired. Note Prepayments in the quarter covered more than 100% of
the capex (vs 50-60% in 2Q) and payback period is now 1 year and 7 months (vs 1 year and 10 months last qtr).
For 2027, revenue guidance of $12.0–13.0B is roughly in-line with consensus of $12.0B, but capex guidance of
$40–45B is a significant step-up versus consensus of $28.8B (~+50% at the midpoint), and expect to cover 75-
85% of this capex through advanced payments, and asset backed financing.

TELCOS 
• TEF –On the call analysts are again asking more questions on the balance sheet, FCF and leverage. Brazil
remains a focus: the BRL/real has fallen approximately 8.6% against the euro since the election result, although
TEF says 80% of its Brazilian FCF is hedged in 2027. With a Spanish election approaching, investors are also
questioning whether a sale of the government’s TEF stake could create a share overhang and whether
government support for an equity raise can still be relied on—or whether that window has closed. In the UK, VMO2
remains highly leveraged (5.6x in 2026 per GIR estimates) despite being ring-fenced and off TEF’s balance sheet,
getting approval of the Netomnia deal particularly important and that rests with the CMA changing tack.
Management’s statement that there would be “no equity raise for VMO2” offered little reassurance. The positive is
that Spanish trading trends and market conditions remain healthy and the underlying Brazilian trends were decent;
my preferred exposure in Spain would be Zegona.
