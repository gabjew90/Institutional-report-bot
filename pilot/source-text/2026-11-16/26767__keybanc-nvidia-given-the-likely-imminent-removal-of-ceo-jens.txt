NVIDIA Corporation
NVDA: Given the Likely Imminent Removal of CEO Jensen
Huang, Downgrading to Sector Weight Given Heightened
Long-Term Execution Risk
We are downgrading NVDA to Sector Weight from Overweight given reports
of the increasing likelihood of the removal of founder and CEO Jensen Huang
by the NVDA board. We believe Huang's departure represents a material
increase in strategic, organizational, and execution risk that warrants a higher
risk premium and lower valuation multiple for the stock. While we expect core
fundamentals to remain strong for the foreseeable future, we believe it will be
challenging for NVDA to find a successor with the same leadership and strategic
vision as Huang.
Huang's imminent departure creates long-term execution and risk to the strategic
roadmap. Our concern centers on the important role Huang has played in building
and operating the company. Huang has operated with broad responsibility spanning
technology, product strategy, customers, ecosystem development, and company culture.
In our view, Huang is not a conventional CEO and has served simultaneously as NVDA's
chief technology strategist, organizational architect, primary customer interface, and
public face. Perhaps more importantly, he has been instrumental in establishing the
Company's long-term strategic roadmap for AI and accelerated compute.
Finding a credible successor will be difficult. We continue to view NVDA as
possessing one of the strongest competitive positions in technology. However, the
removal of Jensen Huang introduces a level of strategic and execution uncertainty
moving forward. Until a credible successor emerges and demonstrates an ability to
sustain NVIDIA's technology leadership, organizational velocity, and ability to make long-
duration strategic bets, we believe a lower valuation multiple is warranted.

NVIDIA - (NVDA)
Company Update
 
Our Case
NVIDIA Corporation is the global leader in accelerated computing and AI infrastructure, powering the
transformation of traditional data centers into “AI factories.” With its cutting-edge GPU architectures like
Blackwell and full-stack platforms spanning silicon, networking, and software, NVIDIA enables large-scale
AI training and inference across hyperscale cloud providers, enterprises, and sovereign supercomputing
initiatives. The Company’s data center segment now accounts for nearly 90% of revenue, driven by surging
demand for AI workloads. Strategic innovations such as silicon photonics networking and partnerships with
TSMC and global governments reinforce NVIDIA’s central role in the AI revolution, positioning it as the
foundational supplier for next-gen digital infrastructure. Given reports of the increasing likelihood of the
removal of founder and CEO Jensen Huang by the NVDA board, we downgrade NVDA to Sector Weight from
Overweight. We maintain our estimates and see a fair value of $170, based on 11x our FY28 EPS estimate
of $15.54.
 
Bull Case
Upside to $300, or 18x our bull FY28 EPS estimate of $17.00.
●New Blackwell architecture drives higher growth and share
gains.
●Blackwell data center GPU adoption continues to ramp quickly,
and demand remains high.
●NVDA continues to outstrip competition for GPUs in AI and
ML, dominating the market for training applications and driving
sustainable growth.
●NVDA gains significant traction in inference and edge
applications as new products targeting these markets roll out.
●NVDA gains traction in China as geopolitical tensions ease.
Bear Case
Downside to $150, or 12.5x our bear FY28 EPS estimate of $12.00.
●Data center growth remains soft or lumpy for an extended period
after aggressive expansion.
●Next-gen Vera Rubin sees push-out due to performance
challenges or supply constraints.
●AMD is increasingly competitive with NVDA’s GPUs, especially
in inference, putting pressure on NVDA’s growth and market
share.
●Hyperscalers increasingly opt for AI ASIC solutions over
merchant GPUs.
●Geopolitical tensions in China do not ease, and NVDA continues
to be restricted from selling into the market.
 
Valuation
NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA)
Given reports of the increasing likelihood of the removal of founder and CEO Jensen Huang by the NVDA
board, we downgrade NVDA to Sector Weight from Overweight. We maintain our estimates and see a fair
value of $170, based on 11x our FY28 EPS estimate of $15.54. By comparison, peers are trading at an
average consensus forward P/E multiple of 33x. We see NVDA as remaining uniquely positioned to benefit
from AI/ML secular data center growth within the industry. With significant barriers to entry created by its
CUDA software stack, we see limited competitive risks and expect NVDA to continue to dominate one of
the fastest-growing workloads in cloud and enterprise. While early days, Omniverse represents an emerging
software subscription revenue stream for metaverse applications that is likely to support further re-rating of
the multiple as it grows and scales.
