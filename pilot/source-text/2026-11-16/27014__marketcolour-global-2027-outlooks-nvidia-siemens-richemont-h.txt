Global Macro 2027: The global economy grew 2.7% in 2026, in line with potential. We expect 2.8% in
2027, as lower energy prices and still-strong AI capex outweigh tighter financial conditions and more
neutral fiscal policy. Table below.
Global Equity Outlook 2027 (OW US & Japan, N Asia & EM, UW Europe): With more attractive
valuations, positive but slower nominal economic and earnings growth, and lower bond yields, inflation
and oil prices, we expect equities to continue to rise, but with high volatility. We focus on Growth
balanced with selective Defensives and beneficiaries of lower bond yields. Attractive opportunities are

emerging in long-duration Growth companies, which should become more valuable as growth rates slow,
particularly US Hyperscalers, which continue to enjoy strong (although slower) earnings growth and
balance sheets.
Nvidia (BUY / PT USD220 / 25% Upside): Based on the available information thus far, we believe these
developments are likely to have a measured direct impact on Nvidia's financials and earnings power over
the next 12-18 months - given the company's long-dated backlog and order book (12-24 months) and
clearly-defined product roadmap (for the next 2-3 years) which is well into the execution phase. We expect
the stock's multiple to experience a greater impact given the level of uncertainty.
Siemens (Buy / PT EUR308 / 10% Upside - Takeaways from management meeting): 1) Siemens has had a
strong start to fiscal FY27 in automation, with double-digit software growth expected for the year; (2) data
center growth visibility remains strong despite potential near-term order volatility and supply-chain

dynamics; (3) the company intends to maintain M&A discipline and (4) Mobility is core to Siemens’ AI
strategy.

remain Buy rated. We need great clarity and specificity on what the reimbursement challenges actually
are, and confirm whether these are transitory vs structural shift
Global Transport: Freight bull market continues in Q4 - Volume growth across global air and ocean freight
remains strong around HSD% / The current US import surge looks to start tapering off during Nov/
Container freight rates have been broadly stable close to historical highs in recent months / European
airlines have taken clearer steps to reduce capacity across their networks, in particular in short-haul
markets.
Hermes (Sell / PT EUR1325 / 5% Downside): We slightly trim our estimates for Hermès for Q4 2026 and
2027. e project 6.1% organic sales growth for 2027 (versus 7% previously) and operating margins up
slightly (+30bps at 40.3% vs 40.7% previously). These are by no means disappointing numbers, in our
view, but with sales growth close to the sector average and the shares trading at a 2027 PE of 27x, on our
numbers, we do not see upside. For the short term, we believe Q4 2026 will see sales grow broadly in line
with Q3 growth at c4.4% year-on-year. Maintain Sell.
