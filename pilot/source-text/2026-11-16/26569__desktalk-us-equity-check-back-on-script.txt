DeskTalk - US equity check: Back on script
By Preston Pagli
Following a modest selloff on Tuesday after a hotter-than-expected CPI print, and Fed Vice Chair Bowman warning of the risk
of "a large correction in equity valuations" tied to AI-related returns, equities are rebounding sharply today driven by renewed
optimism in the AI space. 
Investor focus has shifted back toward the demand story, with strong results from CoreWeave (+26%), delivering a clean
beat-and-raise, reporting 168% YoY revenue growth, margin expansion, and higher guidance.
Our AI Research Head Heath Terry believes demand is not the question, and the debate is increasingly centered on whether
sufficient capacity can be built, and in the case it does, the primary beneficiaries remain the Hyperscalers alongside
infrastructure providers such as CoreWeave and the major NeoClouds.

The NDX is up 7% from Thursday's low and 2.7% today, reclaiming two key levels, the 200dma at 28,017, and last Thursday's
high of 28,437 (ref $28,480). In SPX (+1.23%), a key level to watch into today's close is $7,676 (ref $7,639). A close above
that threshold would trigger systematics to flip long, setting up buying flows on Thursday. 
The SOX is on track for its best day since July, rising more than 7% though last week's pull back once again increased focus
on the Hyperscalers vs Semis trade. As Citi's TMT specialist Jon Zauderer noted on 11/08, conviction is low, but in the near
term investors have favored Hyperscalers over Semis. Options positioning across the average stock in Citi's Semis basket
(CGUSSEMI), shows 1M normalized put skew has steepened less than call skew. 
Looking ahead, Cisco and Applied Materials will report Thursday. Their commentary on AI-related demand and enterprise
spending will be closely watched for confirmation that the AI investment cycle remains intact.
