Good Morning,
US equities finished broadly lower today with SPX down -35bps to 7592 (10/11 sectors -ve, VIX +6.9% to 15.86,
Consumer Disc -177bps, Energy +188bps), NDX down -30bps to 28289, and RTY -166bps to 2799. Commodity
equities saw demand, with the GS Oil basket (GSNOILB +1.30%) and the Natural Gas basket (GSNNATG +0.36%). In
Europe, EU Defense (GSSBDEFE -2.4%) underperformed as well as EU Miners (GSXEMINE -2%) with Gold below
$4,000/oz for the first time in months (XAU -2.7% @ $3880).
 
Global Macro 2027: The global economy grew 2.7% in 2026, in line with potential. We expect 2.8% in 2027, as lower
energy prices and still-strong AI capex outweigh tighter financial conditions and more neutral fiscal policy. Table below.
Link
 
Global Equity Outlook 2027 (OW US & Japan, N Asia & EM, UW Europe): With more attractive valuations, positive
but slower nominal economic and earnings growth, and lower bond yields, inflation and oil prices, we expect equities
to continue to rise, but with high volatility. We focus on Growth balanced with selective Defensives and beneficiaries of
lower bond yields. Attractive opportunities are emerging in long-duration Growth companies, which should become
more valuable as growth rates slow, particularly US Hyperscalers, which continue to enjoy strong (although slower)
earnings growth and balance sheets. Link
 
Nvidia (BUY / PT USD220 / 25% Upside): Based on the available information thus far, we believe these
developments are likely to have a measured direct impact on Nvidia's financials and earnings power over the next 12-
18 months - given the company's long-dated backlog and order book (12-24 months) and clearly-defined product
roadmap (for the next 2-3 years) which is well into the execution phase. We expect the stock's multiple to experience a
greater impact given the level of uncertainty. Link
 
Siemens (Buy / PT EUR308 / 10% Upside - Takeaways from management meeting): 1) Siemens has had a strong
start to fiscal FY27 in automation, with double-digit software growth expected for the year; (2) data center growth
visibility remains strong despite potential near-term order volatility and supply-chain dynamics; (3) the company
intends to maintain M&A discipline and (4) Mobility is core to Siemens’ AI strategy. Link
 
Ottobock (Buy / PT EUR65 / 60% Upside): Overall we are cutting our core revenue/EBITDA/EPS estimates by 0-6%
in FY26/27/28e. We lower our 12-month target price to €65 (from €75 previously). We remain Buy rated. We need
great clarity and specificity on what the reimbursement challenges actually are, and confirm whether these are
transitory vs structural shift Link
 
Global Transport: Freight bull market continues in Q4 - Volume growth across global air and ocean freight remains
strong around HSD% / The current US import surge looks to start tapering off during Nov/ Container freight rates have
been broadly stable close to historical highs in recent months / European airlines have taken clearer steps to reduce
capacity across their networks, in particular in short-haul markets. Link

 
Hermes (Sell / PT EUR1325 / 5% Downside): We slightly trim our estimates for Hermès for Q4 2026 and 2027. e
project 6.1% organic sales growth for 2027 (versus 7% previously) and operating margins up slightly (+30bps at 40.3%
vs 40.7% previously). These are by no means disappointing numbers, in our view, but with sales growth close to the
sector average and the shares trading at a 2027 PE of 27x, on our numbers, we do not see upside. For the short term,
we believe Q4 2026 will see sales grow broadly in line with Q3 growth at c4.4% year-on-year. Maintain Sell.  Link
 
Richemont: Jewellery Maisons came in at +14% cFX in Q2 light of consensus (15.3%, think buyside bar was +16%)
even though momentum remains quite strong. Key positive is operating leverage in the category, 33.4% JM ebit
margin ex one off which is already well ahead of S/S consensus, implying more than 100bps ahead of consensus
expectations. I think the margin story could offer the next leg higher eventually in CFR but not sure of it will offset the
slower topline growth today, especially given positioning.
 
Wise: Results were broadly in line street expectations BUT more importantly its removed the Belgium
overhang with no admission of wrongdoing. Settlement number not disclosed = it’s immaterial. On US licencing all
remediation completed and expect an update in 2027. On Platform they talk to launching with two large banks in the
US ‘silently’ = i.e. they just haven’t done PR around it, but saying momentum is good. Taking this plus inline numbers
should see stock up
