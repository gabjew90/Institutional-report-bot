BTIG Warns "A Correlation-One Downside-Event Is 
Brewing" 
 
BY TYLER DURDEN 
WEDNESDAY, SEP 09, 2026 - 08:20 PM 
Gasoline futures (RBOB) are up ~30% since early August, national retail diesel prices are 
nearing $6/gallon, and one-year inflation swaps are back to their highest level since mid-June. 


[Page 2]
 
 
Regardless of your views on the inflation rate, BTIG's chief technical market strategist, Jonathan 
Krinsky, says that we can probably agree that consumer prices in absolute terms remain broadly 
elevated relative to the last few years, and perhaps this is finally catching up to the discretionary 
sector. 
The sector is flashing an ominous signal with a negative y/y return despite SPX up ~18% y/y. 
Whether it's apparel, leisure and travel, or homebuilders, the sector continues to show more 
vulnerabilities.  


[Page 3]
 
 
Private Equity/Credit Stalling Out... 
After a nice rally from early July, the private equity and credit managers stalled out, and are now 
rolling over again... 
 
Equal-Weight Cracking... 
Further, the equal-weight SPX ex-AI suffered its worst day in two months, and is in its worst 
drawdown since May... 


[Page 4]
 
 
Don't Call It A Comeback... 
SOX getting the AI bulls excited again, yet it still hasn't reclaimed its 50 DMA despite a ~9% 
rally over the last three days... 


[Page 5]
 
​
Bottoms are Events, Tops are Processes...  
In 2000 after initially breaking below the 50 DMA in early April, the SOX crossed back and forth 
above its 50 DMA ten more times before ultimately breaking down in September '00. 
So it essentially had a six-month topping process, getting back within 6% of an all-time high 
twice in June and July '00... 
 
Krinsky ends with an ominous line... "We continue to think a correlation one downside event is 
brewing."
