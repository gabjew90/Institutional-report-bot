Where is the FOMO?
For markets sitting not far from the highs, the flow picture is surprisingly cautious. Risk demand is back
to neutral, equity sentiment has turned negative, US fund inflows have collapsed, ETF flows are weak
and hedge funds are running unusually low exposure. In other words, there isn't much FOMO hiding
under the hood. Except, naturally, in two places: tech and Bitcoin. Here are the 10 flow charts that
caught our attention this week — plus one slightly uncomfortable bonus chart from the very bottom of
the credit barrel.
Back to zero
Despite markets sitting close to the highs, Morgan Stanley's Global Risk Demand Index has round-
tripped back to neutral. Hardly the stuff of euphoric positioning.
MS
Back in black
And it's not just Morgan Stanley. Goldman's US Equity Sentiment Indicator has quickly fallen back into
negative territory. For all the talk about animal spirits, actual positioning looks remarkably restrained.
GIR
Collapsing inflows
This one surprised us. US-domiciled equity fund inflows have effectively collapsed in recent weeks.
Another rather strange look for a market sitting close to the highs.

So far, so cautious. But dig beneath the aggregate numbers and two very familiar pockets of animal

Back buying tech
HFs have net bought US TMT stocks in 10 of the last 11 trading sessions, and on a trailing 2-week basis
in % terms, the recent pace of long buying in US TMT is the largest since Jun ’25 and ranks in the 97th
percentile on a 5-year lookback.
