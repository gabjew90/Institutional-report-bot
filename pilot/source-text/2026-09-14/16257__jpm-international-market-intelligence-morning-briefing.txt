SEPTEMBER 14, 2026 
  
20 SECONDS:  Cautious stance, Global Narrowing & Tech Broadening. USDJPY 
positioning reset. We must pace the frontier. Taylor rule guides policy rates roughly 
100bp higher. Positioning’s Poem. 
  
•         Asia is mixed, with US/EU futures in the red. Calls for tighter AI oversight and 
slower frontier development are weighing on the broader Tech sector, while 
Saudi Arabia’s suspension of operations on the East-West pipeline following 
drone attacks is boosting oil prices (potentially by up to $20/bbl if operations do 
not restart within a few days). USDJPY has stabilised after a material positioning reset. 
•         Ahead of Fed, BoE and BoJ meetings, JPM’s Kasman discusses the 
broadening DM hiking cycle and notes that the Taylor rule points to DM policy rates 
roughly 100bp higher. 
•         We retain our views on Global Narrowing, Tech Broadening & Gold.

•         The key incremental news came from AI. Amodei’s essay called on the 
industry to slow the pace of frontier development. 
•         Recursive self-improvement, “left unchecked, could outrun our ability to

incident was a warning shot: “In 6–12 months, a swarm of more capable but

earnings. This is not a halt: the benefits for growth, healthcare and democracy still 
outweigh the tail risks; AI adoption and token trends remain intact; excessive slowing 
risks ceding leadership in AI; and the administration remains pro-AI.

EMERGING MARKETS MONETIZATION – Ellen Wang / Victoria Campos 
The global momentum/tech rebound drove EM 7% higher since the July-end low, 
despite choppy price action. Broadening and AI continue to dominate the narrative in 
EM, with the recent global macro backdrop net supportive. We are bullish on EM 
given (i) improving macro; (ii) weaker USD; (iii) AI CapEx tailwinds, but continue to 
expect divergence in EM performance at the country/sector level and view country-
relative trades as more relevant for the monetization strategy in this environment.  
•         EM MONETIZATION MENU – Key themes and trades include: (i) 
TACTICALLY LONG China H-shares over A-shares: the pair has been trading well 
in July given the momentum rotation. Despite this outperformance, H-shares still lag 
A-shares YTD and we see further room into China Tech earnings next week. (ii) We 
also express this view via a TACTICALLY LONG in China Internet (KWEB). (iii) 
TACTICALLY LONG Taiwan following the Global Tech selloff in July. Taiwan offers 
a less volatile exposure to AI and Asia Tech relative to Korea. 
• HEDGES – (i) We like long LatAm (ILF) as a diversifying play 
against weakness in the EM AI theme and for relative resilience 
against geopolitical risks. (ii) India (INDA) equity strength has been 
driven in part by a rotation during the AI de-risking phase, but

Report: Weekly Wrap | Macro Risks Reflected among ETFs, CTAs, and Equity 
Themes + YTD Wrap Up 
  
Summary: This week the market was faced with higher oil and higher yields, which 
put some pressure on stocks.  This coincided with small selling among HFs, but 
overall a fairly neutral response.  At a thematic level, there are signs of macro 
extremes among systematic/CTAs with some of this also reflected by HFs at an 
industry level. 
  
Key Points: 
  
1. HF flows/positioning — quiet tape, mild re-grossing, neutral risk:  
Aggregate flows were boring: global re-grossing +0.5z WoW (smaller than last 
week), while net flows were -0.3z. US TPM remains neutral (1wk -0.5z, 4wk -


[Page 16]
0.3z; level -0.2z / 40th %-tile), and HF net leverage basically unchanged 
around 50th %-tile. 
2. ETF flow bearishness — equities & credit flashing risk-off:  US equity 
ETF flows -2z on both 1wk and 4wk (most negative since late 1Q26 / post 
“liberation day” 2025). Credit vs. short-term Treasury ETF flows have also 
reached close to -2z bearish level, which have often lined up with tactical 
lows. 
3. CTA positioning — macro extremes building, equities still middling:  
CTAs are stretched in key macro trades: crude up to ~85th %-tile LT (~70th 
12m), and US bond positioning down near the 5th %-tile (back at prior lows 
seen with similarly high yields). Equity CTA positioning is off MoM but still 
roughly neutral (US/EU/Japan ~56–65th %-tiles; HK lower at ~34th). 
4. Macro-risk themes — defensively positioned beneath the surface:  
Despite neutral top-down equity positioning, several themes have very bearish 
positioning (due to macro risks), e.g.: Homebuilders (rates), Airlines (oil), 
Footwear/Sportswear (consumer + costs), Restaurants (gas + inflation) — 
have all been net sold with L/S ratios in the low single-digit %-tiles. 
5. International highlights — EMEA leadership, EU rotations, AxJ net 
selling:  EU Energy flipped back to buying (+1z) with positioning already rich 
(SXEP L/S ~90th %-tile LT); EU Semis sold into strength; EU HC saw flows 
flip to ~1.5z selling on the leg lower. AxJ had -1.3z net flows (Semis/Energy 
bought, most other sectors sold), while Japan saw small net buying (<1z) led 
by Telecom/Industrials/Energy and selling in Consumer.

2026 YTD Wrap: Risk, Rally, Reset 
Headlines kept shifting, conviction stayed light,  
 Markets searched for footing through a foggy first flight.  
 March brought de-risking, exposure cut back,  
 Hedges went higher as conviction lost track.  
  
Then Q2 found rhythm—the rally gained speed,  
 Momentum and Tech gave the risk trade a lead.  
 Re-grossing gathered as positioning grew strong,  
 But July broke the cadence when the crowding went wrong.  
  
Momentum unwound and the sell-off moved fast,  
 Gross came down sharply as crowded trades cracked.  
 August brought the squeeze, then a rebound took flight,  
 Shorts felt the pressure while the tape found some light.  
  
Now re-gross is building, though the road remains choppy,  
 Flows turn day to day, so the signal stays spotty.  
 From caution to crowding, unwind to reset—  
 A year full of lessons, and we’re not finished yet.
