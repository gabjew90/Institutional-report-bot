3. Agentic AI could harm certain business models levered to inert 
customers. 
Many consumer businesses benefit from the fact that customers rarely revisit existing decisions. 
The time and effort to compare options, renegotiate contracts, switch providers, or cancel 
services is declining. Agentic AI can automate these activities and make consumer switching 
easier. 
As AI agents increasingly perform these tasks on behalf of users, profit pools benefitting from 
customer inaction could come under pressure (GSXUSWCH).

CONCLUSION 
So here we are: Goldman wants you long the thing that has been monkeyhammered 40%-plus 
off the highs, supported by the hope that a 25bp hike is the entire cycle, Iran doesn’t blow a hole 
in the oil complex, and Midterms produce "clarity" instead of the usual circus. 
Maybe they’re right... Positioning is cleaner, and the AI pair has bounced. Power is the one 
segment of the AI group where the prime-brokerage book actually looks washed out. If the left 
tail on rates gets taken off the table and the Communacopia earnings tape holds, you can draw 
a straight line to higher index levels and a 20x multiple fairy tale. That’s the bull case, and it’s 
internally consistent. 
The bear case is more calendar-centric - hiking cycles do not usually arrive as a single 25bp 
courtesy tap, and September into midterms is where liquidity goes to die. Additionally, $105 oil 
and a 5% 10-year are not a good consumer or housing backdrop; they’re a margin-compression 
story (with a geopolitical fuse that's already been lit). 
Agentic AI eating switching-cost monopolies is not a 2027 story if the agents are already in beta.
