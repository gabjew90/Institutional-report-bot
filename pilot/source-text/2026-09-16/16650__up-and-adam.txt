Tuesday, September 15, 2026
As we noted yesterday, calls by leading firms to slow the pace of AI advancement weighed on stocks in the Tech sector. But it wasn’t 
across-the-board selling: the S&P 500’s Software Index rallied 3.2%, whereas the Semiconductor Index fell 4.4%. This divergence shows 
dispersion within the AI ecosystem, as investors differentiate between infrastructure providers and software developers based on 
perceived growth and risk profiles. While near-term leadership may rotate within Tech, we remain constructive on the sector overall.

Saudi Arabia’s East-West oil pipeline remained shut yesterday for the 4th straight day, and there is no stated timetable for resuming 
operations. Since the early days of the US-Iran conflict, this pipeline has been used to bypass the Strait of Hormuz, supplying ~4 million 
barrels/day (4% of global output). Recently, Yemen’s Iran-backed Houthis have intensified attacks on Saudi targets, including energy 
sites. With WTI at ~$102/barrel—nearly the highest since May—relief is unlikely until at least this new aspect of the conflict is resolved.

Pushed higher by another rise in oil prices, the 10-year Treasury yield briefly touched 5.02%, matching its highest intraday level since 
2007, before finishing the day at 4.99%. The move reinforces 5% as an important resistance level. While near-term momentum 
indicators suggest yields are becoming stretched, the advance has been far more orderly than the sharp spikes seen in 2022, 2023, and 
2025. Although our longer-term outlook remains for lower yields, a more sustained decline will likely require some easing in oil prices.

On this day in 2008, Lehman Brothers filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy, the largest in US history and a defining moment of the financial 
crisis. The S&P 500 fell 4.7% that day as fear swept through global markets, and the selloff continued in the months that followed. Yet 
investors who remained disciplined and focused on the long term were ultimately rewarded. Since Lehman's collapse, the S&P 500 has 
risen 537%, or 11% annualized, serving as a powerful reminder that maintaining a long-term perspective is key during uncertain times.
