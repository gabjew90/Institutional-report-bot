Major support sits around $4,200, with the big trend line coming in slightly lower.

Gold’s cheap Fed hedge
GS’s Tony Kim expects a choppy near-term path for gold. A Fed hike, higher energy prices and rising
yields remain headwinds, although much has already been priced and gold continues to hold its recent
range. Institutional and sovereign demand emerged around $4,000, while the longer-term central-
bank and fiscal-support story remains intact.
Into the Fed, GS favors cheap upside convexity. Call skew and risk reversals are not pricing much
upside tail risk, while GLD gamma could amplify the move. Wingy calls carry reasonably well and
would rally sharply in the unlikely event that the Fed does not hike.
