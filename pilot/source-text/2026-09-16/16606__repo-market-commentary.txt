FOMC Preview
Last time around, there was only a 30% chance of a tightening priced-in, not guidance from Fed governors, weak employment
data, and above normal inflation. The Fed did not tighten. In the past 6 weeks, Chair Warsh said he was open to higher rates,
employment is stronger, inflation is higher, a sell-off in the long-end of the market, and the market is now pricing a 92% 
chance of a tightening. Expect a tightening.
I see the chance of two surprises tomorrow: First, an announcement to permanently end Reserve Management Purchases
(RMPs) and a plan to decrease the size of the balance sheet. Second, the market should price the chance, though very
small, of a 50 basis point move tomorrow.
Scott E.D. Skyrm
