BofA September Global Fund Manager Survey
Bottom Line: Sept BofA Global FMS shows investor conviction on macro boom & fast-paced AI capex still strong; but risks of disorderly bonds and DEM
midterm sweep rising; FMS cash jumps from 3.5% to 3.9% as excess bullishness, which stymied summer risk upside, now fading…when cash back in 4-5%
neutral zone, safe to increase risk exposure.
On Macro & Proﬁts: net 8% predict acceleration of already-strong global growth; 55% predict "no landing" for economy, 38% "soﬅ," 2% "hard"; most
investors since Aug'21 predicting double-digit EPS growth next 12 months; investors macro/EPS bulls, sole worry is companies over-investing (record net
33% say so).
On Policy & Politics: Fed seen as behind-curve…ﬁrst time since Sep'22 investors expect ﬂatter yield curve; just 16% of investors say Bessent bond
buybacks will reduce Treasury yields; most likely midterm result seen as DEM House/GOP Senate (44%) but DEM sweep odds rising (31%); most likely
reaction to DEM sweep = "yields up/stocks down" (45%).
On Crowds, Risks & Events: most crowded trade = "long global semiconductors" (53%), biggest tail risk = "disorderly rise in bond yields" (33% vs. Aug
27%), most likely source of credit event = "AI hyperscaler capex" (42% vs. Aug 38%).
