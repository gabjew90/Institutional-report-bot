Good Morning,
US Equities finished the week with strong gains on Friday – SPX (+86bps to 7656), DJIA (+98bps to
52573), NDX (+96bps to 26333) after the market viewed the firmer core CPI reading as a ‘rip the band
aid’ moment into next week’s FOMC decision (September hike odds now sitting near ~90%). Anxiety
remains high (HF net exposure in the 6th percentile, macro shorting). In Europe, (SXXP +50bps, SXXE
+90bps) Cyclicals (GSPECYDE +1.1%), Oils (GSPEOILX +80bps), and Power (GSPEPOWR +70bps)
outperformed. EU Beta (GSP3BETA +1%), High Momentum (GSPEMOMO +1%) and Size (GSP3SIZE
+50bps) outperformed from a factor lens.
Interesting: Over $1.4 trillion has piled into US-listed ETFs this year, which is less than $100 billion away
from surpassing last year’s record haul.
US Equity Strategy: Equities have often performed well during tightening cycles. The S&P 500 has
generated an average return of +9% during the 12 months following the first hike. Today, the market is
already pricing more than three hikes within the next year, and corporate earnings and balance sheets are
both robust. Link
FED: We added a 25bp rate hike at this week’s September FOMC meeting to our forecast last Friday
following the August CPI report. We continue to expect two cuts in 2027 but now expect them in
September and December (vs. June and December previously). We have also raised our forecast for the
terminal rate to 3.25-3.5% (vs. 3-3.25% previously). Link
Flow: Asset managers were better for sale this week with supply in macro products, communication
services, healthcare, industrials, and tech. Hedge funds were better for sale shorting macro products and
supply in the technology space. We saw small hedge fund demand in financials
HF Colour: Fundamental LS managers down 0.4% (alpha: +0.6%), but still up 13.0% YTD. HFs have
bought US TMT in 10 of the last 11 sessions (led by Semis & Semi Equip), with the 2-week pace of long
buying in the 97th percentile of the last five years. Financials and Health Care were the most net sold
sectors.
