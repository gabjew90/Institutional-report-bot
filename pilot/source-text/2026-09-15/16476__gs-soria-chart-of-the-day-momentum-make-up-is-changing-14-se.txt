GS Soria - Chart of the Day Momentum Make Up Is Changing 14
Sep 2026
Guillaume Soria  ·  Goldman Sachs  ·  Vice President, Global Banking & Markets, FICC & Equities
Mon 14 Sep 2026, 12:56pm ET
The momentum regime shift we flagged in August is accelerating as performance of short-term winners
has diverged from longer term winners at unprecedented levels. Today, 3m momentum (GSPRHMO3) is
up 5% today as 12m momentum (GSPRHIMO) is down 6.7%, the largest underperformance in 5 years.
Positioning in the momentum factor has reset materially (41st percentile 1-year), though it remains
elevated on a longer lookback (88th percentile 5-year). Longer term winners have struggled amidst the
worst AI drawdown (GSPUARTI) since ChatGPT’s launch (down nearly 45% from the highs), while
software – the max weight in the short leg of 12m Momentum – has rallied back. Together, these dynamics
are reshaping the makeup of momentum.
Short-Term momentum is rotating into software and away from semis, a shift that started with 3m
momentum and is now beginning to reach 6-month momentum as the selloff persists. Software vs Semis
(GSPUSOSE) is having its second best day on record, so if the trend holds, we would expect to see this
dynamic continue into 12m momentum as well. The correlation between AI & Momentum remains
extremely elevated (90-96% for 1m to 1yr period), but we expect this prolonged selloff in the AI complex
to have lasting impact in the makeup of momentum and as this trend continues, momentum will start
decoupling from AI.
In the short term, we continue to think hedging the momentum factor continues to make sense, especially
given the volatility of the factor compared to more muted index volatility (e.g. SPX down <30 bps today).
If your portfolio has AI exposure, we like hedging with puts on the medium term winners (GSXUHMOM)
or AI Beneficiaries (GSTMTAIP), GSTMTAIP 1m 95% put indicatively costs 2.02%, 27d. For other
hedging implementations such as Momo ex AI (GSPUMOXX), TMT Momentum (GSTMTMOM), and
custom solutions, reach out.
