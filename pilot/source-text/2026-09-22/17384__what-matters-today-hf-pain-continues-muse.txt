1) HF Pain Continues
This year we have seen a significant divergence of performance from L/S Fundamental HFs versus
Systematic HFs… which is getting more pronounced and last week was no different.
Last week: The GS Equity Fundamental L/S Performance Estimate fell -1.02% last week (vs MSCI World
TR +0.35%). Meanwhile, the GS Equity Systematic L/S Performance Estimate rose +1.28% during the
same period.
On the year: L/S Fundamental +12% YTD, but mostly driven by Asian investors… US funds are +8%.
Meanwhile, Systematics are +20% YTD!
As mentioned by my colleague Lee Coppersmith, breadth has become another pretty clear indication of
how much harder the tape has been than the headline index suggests. Less than 30% of S&P stocks are
now above the 50-day moving average.

2) Muse Deserves more of your Attention
I was in NY last week and our TMT Spec Sales (Peter Callahan and Alexander Joseph) were pounding the
table on how good their experience was in using Muse on their day to day and how this is the first real
consumer agent to be released to the broad public.
AI complex is bid up early on continued buzz round Agent momentum (specifically focus on Muse
download rates), which is driving outperformance around plays levered to the trade (CPU’s: INTC, ARM
+5%)
Muse has the #1 spot overall among free apps on the Apple US in just over a week after its September 8
release. Apptopia estimating 1.6mm US downloads in its first eight days. For context, ChatGPT saw
~822k US downloads in its first eight days – not exactly apples-to-apples, but still a pretty remarkable
pace. Meta has basically built a direct to consumer agent factory… shopping, calls, subscriptions, admin,
increasingly acting on your behalf. For anyone already running agents on a server, the idea isn’t new. What
is new is packaging it for everyone with safety rails and a credit card attached. The end state is pretty
obvious… everyone has dozens of agents buying groceries, paying bills, booking holidays, sorting
schools. Your productive capacity becomes you plus your team of agents. Massive efficiency unlock,
massive compute multiplier. Every major lab will build one. Watch the CPU/cloud compute trade, not just
GPUs.
