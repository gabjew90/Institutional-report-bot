The copper puke
Managed money just delivered its biggest copper sale since April 2023, pushing selling to a 3.5-year
extreme just as the metal hit its long-term trend line and 100-day MA.
With copper still tightly linked to the data-center trade, this looks more like liquidation than a broken
structural trend. As long as AI remains alive, don’t get too bearish on copper.
Copper liquidation
As Quinn notes, managed money selling in copper reached a roughly 3.5-year extreme in mid-
September. Between September 8 and 15, funds sold $2.6bn of high-grade copper futures, the largest
weekly sale since April 2023, with liquidation accounting for $2.2bn.
The December contract fell 5.6% from record highs as uncertainty around tariffs, the pace of the AI
buildout and broader financial conditions triggered profit-taking. The White House has yet to decide
whether to impose tariffs on refined copper, while calls for a slower pace of AI development have
raised questions around data-center construction, a key structural source of copper demand.
