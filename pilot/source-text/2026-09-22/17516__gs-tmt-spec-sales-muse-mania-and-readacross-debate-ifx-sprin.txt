GS TMT SPEC SALES – Tuesday 22nd September Overnight markets moved firmly back into risk-on
mode, led by AI and semis. META (best day in 17 months) most talked about name yesterday (now +17%

with investors regrossing parts of the AI trade after a choppy period, although the speed of the move also
triggered plenty of “too far, too fast” debate. AMD crossing the $1tn market-cap mark and joining
Dell/HPE among large-cap AI winners at all-time highs reinforced the message that select pockets of the

incoming calls were all about Muse & Instinct (Muse not available outside the US yet but Instinct is – both
getting rave reviews from those I talk to using them. - just 3 weeks after its launch, Instinct reported it
already had >100k users, w/ its private valuation jumping from $2.25B to a targeted $10B in a month .)
Muse has fundamentally shifted the market narrative from passive, token-generating chatbots to active,
"agentic" systems. Unlike traditional large language models that merely generate text, Muse executes
complex, multi-step actions—such as booking travel, negotiating bills, and managing schedules—by
running inside an isolated cloud virtual machine equipped with its own sandboxed browser. Because these
agentic workflows require continuous web browsing, API calls, and multi-step execution, they are
significantly more CPU- and inference-heavy than basic text queries. Investors quickly connected this
trend to an impending wave of demand for advanced processors, networking equipment, cloud hosting,
and security APIs, revitalizing the broader tech hardware supply chain. Early download performance
highlights why investors are treating Muse as a massive strategic inflection point.
Third-party estimates indicate that Muse is substantially outpacing the historical early mobile launch of
OpenAI’s ChatGPT in the US and Canada. Over its first 12 days on iOS, Muse garnered 1.8 million
regional downloads and achieved 642,000 daily active users in the US, outperforming ChatGPT's early
metrics of 1.3 million downloads and 231,000 daily active users. Meta’s massive distribution advantage
across WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook provides a ready-made channel to scale Muse to billions of
users, giving it an unprecedented competitive edge in user acquisition over traditional startups. The rise of
agentic commerce will potentially restructuring web traffic and is creating a sharp divergence in industry
read-throughs. Near term the clear winners are infrastructure providers, security platforms, and agent
enablers . In contrast, transaction-heavy platforms and online marketplaces face the immediate threat of
disintermediation. However that said last week Shopify (+7%) was seen as at risk and yesterday it rallied
after expanding its agentic-checkout partnership with Muse ..... countering previous concerns that AI

agents would bypass established payment gateways. Meanwhile, Amazon’s decision to block Muse from
browsing and shopping on its storefront underscores an intensifying battle over who owns the customer

type product since OpenClaw but the wrapper on Muse and Instinct have made them available to the
masses rather than just geeks.... Beyond retail and search, the emergence of frictionless AI agents poses a
unique risk to high-inertia industries. See our "Muse/Instinct At-Risk Basket" GSXUSWCH focuses
heavily on telecom, insurance, utilities, and auto-renewing subscription businesses, where corporate free
cash flow historically relies on consumer friction and inertia. If AI agents can automatically compare
pricing, optimize subscriptions, and execute carrier switches at zero transactional cost to the consumer, the
competitive dynamics of these industries will shift structurally. This shift is highly likely to compress
pricing power, lower customer retention rates, and degrade long-term customer lifetime value. In parallel,
China’s tech ecosystems are aggressively escalating their AI models, localized silicon, and cloud
infrastructure . Tencent jumped over 6% after unveiling its Hy Image 3.5 Preview model, a high-
performance image-generator integrated directly into its Yuanbao AI assistant and professional design
tools. This launch showcases Tencent’s strategy of leveraging WeChat’s massive billion-user ecosystem to
drive practical , product-native AI tools. Meanwhile, Alibaba Group used its Apsara Conference to unveil
a highly ambitious vertically integrated roadmap, debuting its proprietary Zhenwu V900 AI accelerator.
Boasting three times the performance of its predecessor, the chip supports clusters of up to 500,000 cards
to train next-generation Qwen models containing 5 to 10 trillion parameters. Backed by an aggressive
target of expanding its global cloud data-center capacity to 20 gigawatts by 2032, Alibaba is positioning
itself as a vertically integrated hyperscale giant, contrasting with Tencent’s distribution-first approach.

TELCOS Sunrise is reviewing a multi-year transformation programme to simplify its operating model,
improve execution and better align the business with customer experience, network quality and changing
technology needs. (This is a new program onto of the existing program and looks like the details were
leaked). The plan is also expected to reduce the structural cost base, with management reviewing supplier
contracts and a potential reduction of up to 450 full-time roles (15% of FTE so pretty big). A formal
consultation with employee representatives and the union is planned for 4Q26 once the required
information is available, while shop, sales and customer-service employees are expected to be largely
excluded and apprentices excluded entirely. Further details will be announced once the review is complete.
