## Marketcolour + Coreweave / Infineon / Siemens Energy /
Zalando / FLS / ABN ##
Ioannis Blekos
11 Nov 2026
Good Morning,
US equities finished in the red amid a hot CPI print (+30bps vs cons 20bps, 3rd consecutive month with a 0.1%
beat): SPX -38bps to 7520 (2Y UST worst day since Oct ’24, 5 of 11 sectors +ve, Financials +0.49%, Tech
-1.40%), NDX -60bps to 27740 (weakness in Semis, Memory, Neoclouds), and RTY -68bps to 2821. 
 
Flow: Our floor was a 5 on a 1-10 scale in terms of overall activity levels with Asset Mgrs finishing moderate net sellers
driven by supply in large cap tech. HFs finished net sellers with supply (short > long) concentrated in tech and macro
products
 
Fed / additional hike in March: We continue to expect a second 25bp rate hike in December and are adding a
third 25bp hike to our forecast in March 2027. A third hike could come earlier in January, but we think March is a bit
more likely because we expect the November and December inflation reports to be softer but then expect larger start-
of-the-year price increases amidst ongoing cost pressures to boost inflation in January.Link
 
CoreWeave (+8% post market / We would add to positions): 4Q implied revenue guidance is 3% above the Street,
margins 100bps above, and capex 22% below; increased clarity around CoreWeave’s financing plan (no additional
parent-level unsecured debt through 2027); additional levers include customer prepayments, OEM financing and
growing operating cash flow; 4-5yr contract are now pricing at $20-$25mn/MW (vs. WholeCo revenue/MW at ~$10mn)
vs. capex at ~$40mn/MW so we now estimate uIRR in the range of 20-30% before accounting for residual value vs.
15-21% prior. Link
 
Infineon (BUY / PT EUR91 / 85% Upside): We raise our FY27-30 revenue estimates by c.2-3% to reflect the
continued strong demand for AI power semis benefitting the PS segment as well as improving demand trends in its
ATV segment. Further, we increase our GM estimates by 0-1pp across FY27-30 to reflect better topline. As such, our
FY27-30 adj. EBIT estimates are higher by 3-4%, reflecting changes to revenue, GMs and operating expenses. Finally,
our EPS estimates for FY27-30 increase 3-4%, in line with our EBIT estimates and factoring in the latest share count
assumptions. Link
 
Zalando (BUY / PT EUR44 / 60% Upside): We hosted CFO yday - 1) a good start to 4Q with autumn/winter trading on
track; 2) the B2C strategy across its three distinct apps has limited overlap (10%), and remain confident in reaching 3-
4% retail media target by 2028; 3) AI and Agentic Commerce are growth catalysts and an efficiency driver; 4)
continued momentum in B2B, with Scayle pipeline full; 5) management reiterated the 6-8% 2028 adj. EBIT margin
target. Link
 
Siemens Energy (CL BUY / PT EUR212 / 70% Upside): If we include dividends, we see today's announced buyback
implying €27bn of returns of value (buyback and dividends) out to FY 2030. This compares to Visible Alpha at €24bn
and GSe at €30bn. The question we see naturally following is how conservative is the capital allocation. We see
tomorrow's Capital Markets Days painting a stronger profit outlook and a material acceleration in cash flow. At the
moment capital allocation out to FY 2028 is for 1/3 of cash flow for capital expenditure, 1/3 for returns of value and 1/3
to be retained. We see this allocation changing as the company adds guidance to FY 2030. Link

 
FLS 3Q beat – Organic orders +32% vs consensus +26%. Org revenues +6% vs consensus +4.6%. Ebita 8% beat
with margins 100bps ahead of consensus. Guidance is narrowed to growth +1-3% (prev 0 to 4%) and margins ~16.5%
(prev 16-16.5%, cons 16.6%). Orders were boosted by large wins, but also saw continued good momentum in PC&V.
 
ABN: 21pxt net profit beat driven higher NII, other income (One off on NIBC gains) and lower LLPs. Revenues are
4pct above consensus with NII 2pct better, fees 2pct lower and other income 82pxt higher. Stripping the one off in
other income, revenues are 1pct better and pre-prov 2% beat with Costs bang in line with consensus. Provisions are
much lower and showing a release due to model updates. Looking at the slides, there is some mix shift from CA to
savings/ time but half of the increase in time is NIBC consolidation. Deposits were down QoQ exc NIBC due to
seasonal spending and migration into securities.
 
ITV: Missed and guided below as digital ad revenues slowed. 3Q26 revenue missed consensus by 1%, driven by a
2% shortfall was in Media & Entertainment (M&E – this is the business that is in the process of getting sold), while
Studios matched consensus but missed GS estimates by 3%. TV advertising revenue fell 8% versus guidance of
around 5%, and digital advertising growth slowed to 6% over nine months from 13% in 1H (like ad supported tiers of
competitors); non-advertising revenue was broadly flat.
 
INWIT 3Q26: Results were in line with consensus and 2026 guidance was reiterated, but the more important takeaway
was a modest positive on the ongoing contract disputes with Swisscom and Telecom Italia. Several Italian authorities
are looking to accelerate the resolution process, which could shorten the period of uncertainty.
