Market Internals Update:
• Three capitulation signals have now been triggered: Excess Flow of -$365bn, the largest on record; UBS
RMM Flow bought the dip at the 68%-tile vs. 3Y, following 1.25 days of selling that represented the largest
selling episode since Covid; and the VIX Cross-Asset Rank at 96%, suggesting equity volatility is exceptionally
elevated relative to virtually every other asset class. Taken together, these signals point to significant
forced-selling and oversold conditions. My expectation is that SPX could establish a low on Monday, as
systematic selling and hedge-fund unwind pressure subsequently begin to fade. 
• Three systematic strategies have sold an estimated $170-210bn in aggregate today: Risk Control
$95-125bn, Leveraged ETFs $50-55bn, and CTA $25-30bn.  I estimate Risk Control could sell another
$200-270bn over the next two days, or approximately $100-135bn per day. After Monday, however, I expect
both Risk Control and CTA sell-rebalancing flows to become very small, substantially reducing their market
impact. Meanwhile, UBS PB L/S Net Leverage @ 18%-tile vs 1Y and L/S Ratio @ 8%-tile vs 1Y, suggesting
net long exposure is already very low and short-squeeze risk is elevated.  
• Investor conversations today have not felt particularly panicked. Many of the questions I received
suggest investors recognize that additional systematic selling is still ahead and are waiting for attractive entry
points to buy the dip. In other words, there appears to be substantial interest in deploying capital into
weakness rather than broad-based capitulation.
