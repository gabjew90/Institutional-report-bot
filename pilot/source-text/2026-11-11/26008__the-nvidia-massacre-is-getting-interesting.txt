The valuation disconnect
NVDA is down around 26% from its October highs, with multiple compression doing most of the
damage. At ~12–13x FY28E earnings, the stock is starting to look increasingly interesting, particularly
given its extraordinary growth profile. The de-rating reflects concerns over AI-capex cyclicality,
customer concentration and infrastructure financing rather than any confirmed collapse in
fundamentals. Weekend headlines surrounding Huang have added another layer of uncertainty. The
next earnings report is a critical test. If demand holds up and financing fears ease, the potential for a
violent mean-reversion bounce is significant.
