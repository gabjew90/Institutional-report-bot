US Equity Strategy "Outlook 2027: After the
derating, how to invest" Parker
Keith Parker
09-Nov-2026, 23:11 ET (EST)
• Rising risks drove a big derating, which has historically set up strong returns
2026 was a year of regime shifts. The S&P 500 fwd P/E fell ~20%, even as 2026 EPS is on track to grow
~33%. AI ROI and regulatory concerns, a spike in yields and credit spreads, higher oil and a drop in net
buybacks all raised risk premia. The midterms added a new risk, as state-level pauses put ~30% of US data
center capacity additions in 2028 at risk. History is supportive though. In years after a >10% fall in the SPX
P/E, SPX has returned a median 20%, with few negatives, as the P/E recouped ~30% of the decline.
• Earnings support 2027, but data center risks to 2028 are hard to size
We forecast SPX EPS growth of 18% to $430 in 2027 and 14% to $490 in '28, with NVDA, MU and AVGO
alone ~45% of next year's growth. Above trend EPS growth tends to weigh on the P/E, but big deratings tend
to partly reverse. Our S&P 500 target for end '27 stays at 8900, or ~18x our 2028 EPS of $490, and our end
'28 target is 9600 on 17.5x EPS of $550 in '29. Data center pauses skew 2028 risks lower, and with policy
details unlikely before next year, we will revisit forecasts as they emerge.
• Drivers of the derating look closer to bottoming/turning
AI capex concerns hit the AI trade even as demand accelerated, with annualized q/q cloud growth nearly
tripling to ~$86bn in Q3 from ~$31bn in Q1. Rates vol should fall if the Fed hikes twice as UBS expects, and
eventual Iran de-escalation is more likely than escalation. Growth worries seem priced as indicators point to
expansion. Net buybacks fell ~$120bn from Q1 to Q3, but surging cash flow points to a $200-400bn rebound
next year, with the big Semis adding $170bn+. AI capex slowing and a further yield spike are the main risks,
but after this much derating, more downside looks priced.
• Strategy: lean into derated growth, AI demand and the expansion
• 1) AI demand is accelerating, so buy the Semis derating and application layer.
We estimate AI revenue potential at ~$2.5tr in 5yrs at the midpoint, above the $2tr low hurdle for $6-8tr of
capex. Yet Semis' P/E has collapsed, pricing a fall in profits, so we are buyers of Semis over Tech Hardware.
We are also o/w Software, now mostly the AI application layer, with P/E to growth near 15yr lows. We like
Hyperscalers, which own scarce compute amid rising capacity headwinds. NDX over RTY is a higher
conviction view, as NDX EPS has risen ~2x more than RTY's, yet its P/E is back near 2022 lows. AI Exposed
Semis and AI Software Pioneers baskets can implement the theme.
• 2) Growth+quality at a discount should lead, with Healthcare most attractive.
Growth and CFROI spreads have widened while valuation spreads are avg. Quality, momentum and growth
have also tended to lead as expansions mature, so value and high beta are funding sources. We are o/w
Healthcare, which tops our REVS rankings as sales growth rises for BioPharma, and fund it with Staples, as
Healthcare trades at about half Staples' P/E to sales growth. We reduce Energy after a ~40% rally, as it
screens rich vs fundamentals. Our Growth+CFROI at a Discount basket over Expensive Fundamentals
captures the theme, with Longevity & Wellness our preferred Healthcare basket.
