TELCOS
• BT is delaying the launch of its Starlink satellite broadband partnership following service disruptions in
Ukraine, despite the service being ready, according to the Financial Times CEO Allison Kirkby said BT
would wait for the controversy to subside, but subsequently confirmed a launch “in the coming months”,
having previously targeted the second half of 2026. The service will connect customers beyond
Openreach’s fibre network, which covers roughly 25 million homes. Ukraine’s Starlink access has since
been fully restored, while the US government denied ordering the restrictions. Kirkby said Starlink
remained well ahead of competitors and intended to partner with UK telecoms operators rather than launch
a rival UK mobile service, despite SpaceX’s plans to compete directly in the US.

• The UK has backed a proposed subsea data cable between Shetland and Norway to strengthen
connectivity and resilience against threats to critical infrastructure, according to the Financial Times
VodafoneThree is leading a feasibility study, starting next summer, to assess routes and landing points. The
cable could support businesses, public services, defence and data centres while providing an alternative if
existing routes are disrupted. It would be the first new direct Norway–Scotland subsea data link in roughly
25 years and follows a separate study into a Shetland–mainland Scotland cable after significant internet
outages. The proposal comes amid growing concerns about interference with undersea infrastructure and
efforts to strengthen monitoring and protection.
