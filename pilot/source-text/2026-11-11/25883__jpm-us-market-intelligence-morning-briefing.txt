implies 8.4% upside from Friday’s close. 
•         TRADE IDEAS – (i) Long 12M Momentum vs. Short 3M Momentum – which is 
essentially Tech vs. Energy / Defensives; (ii) Low Vol is less compelling – after moving 
form 0%ile to 25%ile, the trade is less attractive as the factor will need to see an increase in

prices, borrowing costs, and housing shortages. That said, he sees longer-term value in 

Apparel, Restaurants, and Retail vs. Energy. Regarding Housing, mortgage rates need to 
approach 6.0% to see inflows into the group. 
•         WHAT’S THE MACRO VIEW?  Looking ahead to 2027, JPM Economics expects US 
GDP growth to slow but remain trend-like with modest disinflation keeping the broader 
business cycle dynamics intact, while we [Dubravko & team] estimate EPS growth of 
~20%.  
•         WHY THE UNWIND? This pullback is a de-leveraging event. Investor were chasing AI 
and AI-adjacent plays with record high levels of leverage. Leverage is at-risk when liquidity 
dissipate, which began occurring in late-June. Liquidity dries, equity de-leveraging follows 
triggering a rapid reversal of the Momentum trade. The unwind was magnified by systematic 
& vol strategies plus a liquidation of buyside funds. October was the largest single month 
unwind for Long Momentum, falling ~11% or ~6 standard deviations contributing to 
Momentum L/S declining ~40% from its peak. 
•         WHAT ABOUT MULTIPLES? Equity multiple have compressed ~4x since January, 
one of the most extreme periods of de-rating when excluding recessions. This de-rating is 
magnified by the fact that earnings have been growing double-digits this year. AI companies 
have seen multiples compress ~35% since May, including: Semis compressing 52%, 
hyperscalers 14%, Memory 60%, and Processing Units 50%. 
•         HOW MUCH SELLING IS LEFT? He sees the process as being far along pointing to (i) 
a reset in Momentum crowding from 100%ile to 76%ile; (ii) Levered ETF sales of ~$60bn 
on Thursday and $35bn the previous Friday which have dropped exposure below Jan 2026 
levels; (iii) CTA have flipped to net Short as vol targeters have seen leverage decrease 
from 65%ile to 30%ile with maybe $50bn left to sell; (iv) dealer net gamma imbalance is 
12%ile; (v) corporate buyback, with $1.2T in announced buyback, are likely to provide 
support; (vi) QQQ have seen their RSI dip below 30 which has, on average seen ~4% rally 
over the next five trading days. 
•         WHAT ARE THE CATALYSTS? (i) developments in the Middle East; (ii) MegaCap 
IPOs coming to market; (iii) the ERCOT meeting next month, which has AI Capex 
implications; and (iv) December Fed meeting. More generally, he sees US Midterm 

Elections as a clearing event and expected data center headwinds to be less onerous than 
the market is pricing. 
•         WHAT ARE THE SPX EPS RISKS FROM AI CAPEX? Dubravko and team estimate 
that  every $100B increase in capex translates to ~$9 for S&P 500 EPS (~2.5% earnings 
growth) in 2027. Thus, even if next year’s capex spend comes in at the middle of the range 
at ~$1.5T, that would roughly translate to an EPS impact of ~$18 vs. consensus, adding 4-
5% to 2027 EPS growth. While there are concerns around future (2028) data center 
projects after the recent midterm election results, J.P. Morgan credit analysts expect 
hyperscalers to be able to fund their AI capex needs next year, absent any exogenous 
shocks in credit markets. However, the funding mix will need to see more participation from 
other sources including equity.

POSITIONING INTELLIGENCE –  Weekly Wrap | Multi-Strat Alpha & HF Returns Likely 
to Recover, but Uncertainty Persists 
SUMMARY: Looking back at what we’ve seen over the past 2 weeks, it seems like the 
risks outweighed the tailwinds that we highlighted from our prior note “Weekly Wrap 
| Painful Under the Surface, But Not a Liquidation” (Oct 23, 2026).  Looking back, the 
risks were: “Eq L/S alpha is still deteriorating and YTD returns are declining late in the year, 
CTAs are neutral so could still sell if markets don’t rebound…and Retail demand is soft.”   
  
Fast forward to this week and the question is naturally…have we seen capitulation?  Will 
the market or HF alpha rebound?  The market does not equal HF alpha, and even among 
HFs there could be a varied story going forward.  There’s potential for both to rebound in 
the near-term (and history would suggest a rebound is more likely than not), but the path 
forward is still murky.  We covered some of the key points in today’s earlier note, while the 
below is meant to give broader context for what might happen going forward…though 
upcoming market catalysts are still likely to have a big impact. 
  
 
 

 
KEY POINTS: 
1. Multi-Strat pain may be easing, though de-grossing has continued. After 
several weeks of negative long-short spreads and the largest 20-day Multi-Strat de-
grossing event since Covid, L-S spreads improved over the past week: Tuesday and 
Thursday were positive, leaving 5-day alpha close to flat despite a weak 
Wednesday. Historically, after Multi-Strat 20-day gross flows first fell below the 
5th percentile—as they did on Oct. 27—alpha was higher over the following 20 
days in 9 of 10 cases. Gross flows typically began to recover after about 10 days, 
while alpha stabilized and then improved. The main exception was the post-Covid 
episode. 
2. Broader HF de-grossing may be slowing, but the path is uncertain. From a 
seasonal perspective, US de-grossing is now below its average, and gross flows 
have tended to move sideways from here. The pattern in past midterm election 
years most closely resembles 2022, when de-grossing continued into mid-
month. Gross flows eventually recovered in December in both years, although that 
recovery coincided with a market sell-off and included substantial short additions. 
3. HF returns have often bounced after similar short-term drawdowns—but 
market conditions matter. In the past five years, HFs rebounded over the 
following 5 and 10 days in all eight comparable events in which 5- and 20-day 
net returns fell below -2z. Earlier examples, including late February 2020 and late 
October 2018, were followed by further losses. A rebound in absolute HF returns 
may depend partly on the market and there’s precedence for a rebound post similar 
extreme 1d events near ATHs. 
4. Selling has not yet signaled broad capitulation; regional flows diverge as 
EMEA shorts covered. The broad net selling needed to drive US TPM to a very 
negative four-week change has not yet appeared. HF flows have instead differed by 
region: EMEA and China/HK saw net buying, while North America and Japan 
saw net selling, with North America sharply de-grossed. In Europe, short 
covering reached an extreme 3z level, last seen in late 2022. 

5. Exposure has reset in momentum and AI-related themes. HF net leverage fell 
about 4% over the past week, bringing it near 12-month lows; Eq L/S net leverage 
is also at a three-year low, while All Strategy net leverage remains higher, around 
the 40th percentile on a three-/five-year basis. Global momentum exposure is 
near three-year lows and close to neutral; the 6m z-score of positioning and 
price is also back near its summer lows, suggesting much of the move lower may 
have occurred for now. APAC momentum exposure has also fallen quickly from 
more extreme levels. In US themes, Semis remained the most net-sold and 
positioning is back near early-2026 levels. Software flows have been more 
neutral recently while positioning continues to recover. Broader AI-cyclical exposure 
has fallen quickly, while Defensives ex-Healthcare have begun to rebound. 
 
 
 POSITIONING INTELLIGENCE – Tactical Takes | More De-grossing and HF pain on 
Thurs—worst day since Apr 4, 2025 
SUMMARY: As US stocks reversed lower on Thurs, the declines were sharpest in Tech, 
but broad enough to drive negative returns across HF strategies.  The global HF return 
was greater than a 3z decline yesterday and marked a continuation of the challenges that 
HFs have seen in the past month.  The strong de-grossing continued for a 4th day and 
the acceleration this week comes after persistent de-grossing over the past few 

weeks.  Below are a few quick takes on recent changes…we’ll have more in our Weekly 
Wrap later today. 
  
KEY POINTS:  
1. HF performance hit hard on Thurs, but most of it beta-related (at least in US): 
Across HF strategies, we see them down -1.6% globally on Thurs, putting them 
down about -1.8% MTD.  The 1d return is the worst since Apr 4, 2025 and the 3m 
net return is now at a -3z level, in line with early Apr 2025 as well.  Performance is 
varied across regions and strategies, but most figures are around -1 to -2% MTD.  It 
seems like alpha is most negative for Quants in N. Am., Multi-Strats in EMEA, and 
Eq L/S in APAC. 
2. HF Flows: De-grossing, but Net Flows Flat: we saw a 4th day in a row of ~1.5z de-
grossing on Thursday, which makes this look quite similar to late July/early Aug.  
However, net flows have been relatively paired off as both longs and shorts are 
reduced.  In terms of rolling gross flows, Mult-Strat de-grossing continues to persist 
and the 20d de-gross is now larger than any other period outside of Covid. 
3. US TPM falls, but not yet at a clear “attractive” set-up for US stocks: Given the 
retail selling and general de-risking, our US Tactical Positioning Monitor (TPM) saw 
a -1.7z 1wk change, which put the level of positioning back to the 20th %-tile since 
2015—similar to lows in March and July of this year.  However, the speed of the 
recent decline has been somewhat limited (as much of the recent pain has been 
more rotational than broad index until yest).  Hence the 4wk change is only -0.5z (on 
3d avg), whereas the trigger when this is attractive is -1.5z.   
4. Semis the clear group sold:  no surprise here, but Semis has seen very strong net 
selling lately with Retail selling the group and other AI-related industries yest, while 
HFs have net sold Semis at a nearly 4z level over the past month (though we saw 
HFs’ Semis flows more net neutral yest, post large selling on Wed).  Furthermore, 
the selling of Semis by HFs on a rolling 1m basis is now back at July ’26 level, while 
on a 2m and 3m basis, the selling is around a 4z level (which is something we 
haven’t seen in past data).
