1) Largest Drop in Gross leverage since Covid and biggest in net since Liberation day
Flows: US equities were modestly net sold last week, driven by the largest single stock net selling in 4
months partially offset by buying in Macro Products. Info Tech was by far the most $ net sold global (and
US) sector – this week’s % selling in global Info Tech stocks was ~on par with the largest net selling
weeks on our record (since 2016). Also worth noting that 4 of the top 5 net selling weeks in global Info
Tech on our record have occurred within the past five months.
Performance: Fundamental L/S -1.48% last week, with US Fundamental L/S down almost 2% on the
week, while Systematic L/S -0.6%
Deleveraging: US Fundamental L/S Gross leverage fell -8.7 pts – the largest weekly decline since Covid
(March ’20) – to 207.5% (26th percentile one-year), while US Fundamental L/S Net leverage fell -3.7pts –
the largest weekly decline since the week of Liberation Day (Apr ’25) – to 43.3% (0th percentile five-
year). We finally saw gross leverage being reduced drastically. Personally, I think this is a strong signal
that positioning is cleaning up.

Risk Appetite is low but not at clear buying levels yet. Our proprietary index of risk appetite continues to
move lower sharply. If it hits -1 its a strong buy signal, if it hits -1.5 you sell your house and buy equities.
2) CTAs are Large Sellers of US Equities

Systematic/CTA signals are now negative in around 60% of global equity markets at the short and
medium-term trend horizons, including S&P and most of the biggest ones.
We expect the Systematic/CTA community to sell $28bn of SPX futures in the upcoming 1 week, and
$45bn in the upcoming 1 month in the baseline scenario coming into Friday, which would take the
position to overall net short.
3) Watch US Panic Index and Multiple Surprise Ratio
US Panic Index: readings north of 9 have historically been a good buying opportunity (we hit 9.57 thurs,
and closed the week at 9.2 … reminder this is an equal weighted basket of pctl rank across each of four
indicators (vix vol, spx skew, spx term structure, spx 1m atm implied).
source :: gs ficc and equities / as of 8nov26 / past performance not indicative future returns
Multiple Surprise Ratio: another metric my colleague Brian Garrett created is the “multiple surprise ratio”
– effectively the magnitude of an asset’s 1d % change relative to the 1m implied volatility (t-1). On this
metric, spx experienced the largest MSR in 19yrs this week … ndx top 2 … RTY top 5 … to say the vol
market was underpricing the change is the understatement of two+ decades

4) GIR Says Semis are Oversold

GS Research believes stocks are now clearly in oversold territory. Although we recognize there may be
few catalysts for stocks to move higher before year end, we think risk/reward is compelling for select
names at current levels.
• AI Compute: We favor NVDA and AVGO, which imply no growth from 2027
• Semi Cap Equipment: We see 2027 WFE as a baseline and prefer AMAT & LRCX
• Memory: Approaching oversold levels as we begin to move toward tangible book
