Market Internal Insight 
Strongest Bullish Setup YTD: Expect SPX 10%+ Higher in 3 Months 
Market Thoughts 
Over the weekend, I wrote “One more pocket of forced selling into Monday, but increasingly few sellers left afterward.  I expect a Monday low followed by a sharp 
recovery.”  I was right about few sellers left, but wrong about Monday low since buying forces have simply overwhelmed the lone remaining systematic seller, 
Risk Control.  After today, there should be really very little systematic selling left, if any. 
 
Most important Point – Systematic Positioning is Washed out!  Risk Control Exposure @ 55% - The lowest level since Tariff!!!  The last two comparable 
washouts were 55% on Aug 6, 2024 and 30% on Apr 10, 2025, after which SPX grinded +17% and +24% higher in 4M, respectively.  CTA Equity Exposure @ -5% - 
Negative!!! Historically, negative CTA exposure tends to be a bottoming signal, particularly when real-money investors are not selling, as is currently the case.  The key 
implication is that the downside feedback loop has largely been removed.  Negative headlines can still trigger knee-jerk selling from discretionary investors, but 
systematic funds are unlikely to become the second-derivative sellers that amplify the initial move lower. With real-money support from retail, foreign investors and 
corporate buybacks (returning next week), news-driven selloffs should be shallow.   
 
That leaves a compelling asymmetric setup: washed-out systematic positioning and limited incremental systematic selling + resilient real-money demand + 
short squeeze risk + potential CTA and Risk Control re-risking on the upside. I expect SPX to grind higher in a lower-volatility rally, with at least +10% 
upside over the next three months.
