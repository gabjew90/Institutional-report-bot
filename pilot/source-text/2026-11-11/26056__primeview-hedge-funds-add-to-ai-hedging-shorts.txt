PrimeView: Hedge Funds Add to AI
Hedging Shorts
Josh Guilliard
09-Nov-2026, 05:25 ET (EST)
On Friday, hedge funds continued to reduce AI beta, led by Semis (-0.8std), Memory (-1.0std) and
Hardware (-1.2std). However, flows shifted from >90% long selling to roughly 50/50 long selling and
shorting. Hyperscalers were small net bought (+0.3std), as hedge funds focused on higher-quality names
with available compute and stronger build profiles. This extends the trend seen since early October. Power
names were also net bought ( +1.7std), with ~70% of flows driven by long buying. Essentially, hedge funds 
bottom-fished selectively and added relative-value (RV) trades across the AI value chain.
• Overall, hedge funds added +1.9std of gross exposure to US equities, the 6th-largest re-risking day
YTD, driven by +2std of short exposure and +1std of long exposure. This was therefore a largely
market-neutral/RV risk add, rather than a directional long or short bet. Friday also saw the largest increase
in hedge fund short exposure since June 15, before the summer unwind.
• UBS Head of Cash Equity Trading, Zain Nizami, remains cautious as correlations rise, increasing the risk
that crowded sector and RV trades, particularly Semis vs. Software and Semis vs. Hyperscalers, spill over
into broader portfolio de-risking. Zain recommends buying Semis while selling the recent “hedging
beneficiaries”. From a positioning perspective this looks attractive given net exposure in Semis is back
near July and Liberation Day lows, while Hyperscaler and Software exposures remain high.
