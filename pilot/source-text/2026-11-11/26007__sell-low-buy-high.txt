Super surprised
Goldman has introduced a new metric, the Multiple Surprise Ratio (MSR), measuring the size of a daily
market move relative to the previous day's one-month implied volatility. The results are extraordinary.
SPX recorded its largest volatility-adjusted surprise in 19 years this week, NDX ranked among the top
two and Russell 2000 among the top five. In other words, the options market massively underestimated
the magnitude of the moves. One of the biggest volatility pricing failures in more than two decades.

Nvidia is losing China
China's domestic chip substitution is accelerating. Nvidia's share of Chinese data-center AI accelerator
shipments fell to 34% in H1 2026, with Huawei closing the gap at 28%. TS Lombard argues that US
export restrictions are accelerating the development of China's domestic AI ecosystem. The
unintended consequence of keeping China out of Nvidia's technology may be creating Nvidia's next
major competitor. 
TS Lombard
Nvidia's 75% problem
China doesn't necessarily need to build better chips than Nvidia. It needs to deliver sufficient compute
at a lower price. With Nvidia's gross margins around 75%, Chinese competitors have an enormous
margin pool to attack. TS Lombard argues that the combination of cheaper power, domestic scale and
improving hardware could put increasing pressure on Western AI economics. More here.
