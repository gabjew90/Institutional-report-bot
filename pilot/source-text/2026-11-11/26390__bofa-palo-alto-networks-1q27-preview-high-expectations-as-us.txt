Palo Alto Networks 
1Q27 Preview: High expectations as usual, 
but recent pullback should help 
Reiterate Rating: BUY | PO: 420.00 USD | Price: 326.36 USD   
  
 
 
Still need a strong beat & raise at 18x CY27 EV/Sales 
Heading into 4Q26, Palo Alto’s stock had rocketed up over 140% in the 6mo prior, 
raising the bar (stock declined 9% the following trading day). For 1Q27 results, 
scheduled for 11/18 AMC, the stock has pulled back 25% from recent highs in October, 
helping lower expectations a bit. However, the valuation is still elevated at 18x CY27 
EV/S vs the peer group median at 7x, likely requiring a strong ~3% beat and raise for a 
positive stock reaction. In our view, we look through any volatility around the print and 
focus on the bigger picture: Palo Alto is a clear leader to capitalize on security platform 
consolidation in the age of AI – we reiterate our Buy and $420 PO.  
Key metrics: NGS ARR, Product trends, AIRS 
Key metrics to watch for 1Q start with Next-Gen Security (NGS) ARR, which the Street 
models at $9.55B, up 63% y/y and implying $450mn net new NGS ARR, up 68% y/y. 
Consensus for 2Q NGS ARR is at $9.9B, up 57% y/y. Investors likely want a 3%+ beat to 
both. 1Q Revenue is expected at $3.3B, up 34% y/y and Product rev consensus is up 
25% to $542mn. RPO is expected at $20.9B, up 35% vs $21.2B, up 34% y/y last Q. We 
will also watch Security for AI & Prisma AIRS ARR commentary closely, which last hit 
$120mn ARR and 800 customers. Any gross margin pressure also remains a hot topic, 
and we model 1Q27 GM at 74.6%, down 230bps y/y due to higher cloud and component 
costs. 
Mixed sentiment on firewall read-throughs, M&A update 
Palo Alto benefitted in FY26 from accelerating Firewall bookings up 17% y/y. There are 
signs of positivity in the market, such as new cloud builds, firewall refresh cycles, and AI 
traffic/add-ons all driving new appliance purchases. However, Fortinet and Check Point 
results show signs of slowdown after a strong 1H26. Nevertheless, we believe Palo Alto 
is taking share, winning with platform sales, and enjoying a refresh spread over several 
quarters, which should support 1Q27 results. Lastly on M&A, we look for continued 
positive updates on CyberArk/Chronosphere integration and clarity on the widely 
reported Clutch acquisition for $500mn for non-human identity expansion.

Palo Alto Networks (PANW)

high-end range of appliance companies, which trade at roughly 6-8x forward EV/Sales.

successful SaaS vendors trading at 13-19x forward revenues. 
 
Downside risks to our PO are increased competition and execution-related challenges as 
the company transitions to the cloud and SASE models.
