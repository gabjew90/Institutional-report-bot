Super surprised
Goldman has introduced a new metric, the Multiple Surprise Ratio (MSR), measuring the size of a daily
market move relative to the previous day's one-month implied volatility. The results are extraordinary.
Last week, SPX recorded its largest volatility-adjusted surprise in 19 years. The NDX move ranked
among its top two and the Russell 2000 move among its top five. In other words, the options market
massively underestimated the magnitude of the moves.
