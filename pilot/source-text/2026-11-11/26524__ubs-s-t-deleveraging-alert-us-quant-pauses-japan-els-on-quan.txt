Deleveraging Alert: US Quant Pauses;
Japan ELS “On” & Quant Resumes;

Amandine-Parisa Kara
10-Nov-2026, 03:51 ET (EST)
The US Quant indicator has paused after remaining active for all five sessions last week. However, Quants
cut a further -0.7std of gross yesterday, performance remains challenging (-48bps yesterday) and our model
foresees further deleveraging today. The US Equity L/S indicator paused on Friday last week.
In Japan, the Equity L/S indicator remains switched “on” and the Quant indicator has resumed due to
tough performance (table below). 
Equity L/S hedge funds supported yesterday’s rally, net buying +1std of US equities, split ~50/50 long
buying/covering. Flow was driven by long buying in Hyperscalers (+2.3std) and US Software (+2.1std)
and covering/long buying in US Transportation (+2.4std). In terms of factors, flow remained skewed to
High Quality names (+2.3std). Performance estimates show Equity L/S down a further -64bps yesterday in
the US, caught offside the rally, having heavily de-risked prior and added hedging shorts to Industrials and
Tech. 
In Europe, flows were de-grossing in nature with -1.0std of European equities de-risked. Hedge funds sold
down long exposure by -1.1std, concentrated to profit taking in Tech & Hardware, lifting performance by
+80bps on the day. Hedge funds also covered short exposure by +0.5std, concentrated to Food, Bevs &
Tobacco as the sector squeezed higher.
