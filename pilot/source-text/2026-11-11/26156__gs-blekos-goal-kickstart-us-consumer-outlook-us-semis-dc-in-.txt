GOAL KICKSTART - Tracking the rotations across AI and markets post the midterms
 
• We remain OW equities over 3m/12m, as earnings support, lighter positioning and lower valuations should support
equities into year-end. We remain OW cash though as near-term volatility might linger due to uncertainty around AI
and the Middle East tensions. 
• In the near term, we see three paths to a rebound in risk appetite: (1) easing AI concerns and signs of
monetisation, (2) rates relief, potentially helped by falling energy prices and (3) a larger decline of our RAI below
-1, improving asymmetry to add risk into year-end. We remain OW bonds as we see greater portfolio value in
longer-duration bonds: higher yields and peaking nominal growth should allow for a better buffer for equities.
 
US ECONOMICS ANALYST - 2027 Consumer Outlook: Steady Through the Shocks
 
• We expect consumer spending to rise at a near-potential 2.2% pace in 2027 (Q4/Q4). We look for a softer start to
the year as higher rates weigh on durables demand, energy prices stay elevated, and nonprofit spending declines
after the midterm elections, followed by a pickup as inflation pressures abate and tax refunds and the reversal of
benefit cuts turn the fiscal impulse modestly positive. 
• We see some downside risk from a fading wealth effect. We estimate that it added over 0.4pp to spending growth
in 2026 but should add about half as much next year in our baseline, and it could weigh on spending if AI-related
stocks continue to decline.

Powering Up Europe: Datacenters in Europe: Solid growth path unaffected by US mid terms
 
• We believe that power generators that currently enjoy power land, connection points, and which can sign energy
supply contracts, stand to significantly benefit from this trend. Here we highlight: Solaria (Buy, on CL), Drax (Buy),
RWE (Buy, on CL), PPC (Buy), and Fortum (Neutral). 
• We also note that power grids in Europe are likely to undergo an ongoing acceleration in capex (e.g., EON (Buy),
and many more). Equipment manufacturers remain a key way to gain exposure to this theme; here we flag Buy-
rated Siemens Energy (on CL), Vestas and Nordex.

Pluxee (PLX.PA) - Upgrading to Neutral post underperformance
 
• We upgrade Pluxee to Neutral (from Sell) following share price underperformance and another reset of earnings
expectations. Since being added to the Sell List on October 14, 2024, Pluxee's share price is down -31%,
underperforming both STOXX 600 and FTSE World Europe (€) by -55pp. 
• This underperformance is a result, in our view, of factors including significant regulatory headwinds (particularly in
Brazil) and weaker-than-expected underlying operating revenue trends that have contributed to material downward
revisions to consensus earnings expectations.
