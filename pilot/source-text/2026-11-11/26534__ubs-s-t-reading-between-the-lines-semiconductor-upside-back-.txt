Six Technical Signals That Matter
Price action strengthens the SOX recovery case, which in turn benefits the S&P 500 – which is still on track for
my 8,485 target next year – and the Nasdaq, given given the weighting of semiconductor stocks in each.
Meanwhile, WTI rallies should be considered within the broader topping prospect, gold is basing, don’t chase a
US yield rebound and EURUSD is close to basing.
1. Semiconductors (SOX) Set to Extend Recovery
SOX bouncing off 9,047 DeMark support coincident with supportive DeMark Wave Counts on daily and weekly
charts (Wave 2 and 4 respectively), was an encouraging sign. Furthermore, the index should find support from
the fact 70% of SOX constituents are at / near a DeMark Buy signal, while the daily TD Combo indicator is
close to a Buy signal that would be triggered if we were to see renewed weakness (currently an 11 Count).
Fresh gains beyond 10,498should unlock; 10,878, 11,711 and 12,756.
