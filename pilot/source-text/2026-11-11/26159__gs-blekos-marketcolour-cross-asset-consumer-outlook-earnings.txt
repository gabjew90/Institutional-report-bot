Cross-Asset: We remain OW equities over 3m/12m, as earnings support, lighter positioning and lower
valuations should support equities into year-end. We remain OW cash though as near-term volatility might
linger due to uncertainty around AI and the Middle East tensions. In the near term, we see three paths to a
rebound in risk appetite: (1) easing AI concerns and signs of monetisation, (2) rates relief, potentially
helped by falling energy prices and (3) a larger decline of our RAI below -1, improving asymmetry to add
risk into year-end.
US Consumer Outlook: We expect consumer spending to rise at a near-potential 2.2% pace in 2027
(Q4/Q4). We look for a softer start to the year as higher rates weigh on durables demand, energy prices
stay elevated, and nonprofit spending declines after the midterm elections, followed by a pickup as
inflation pressures abate and tax refunds and the reversal of benefit cuts turn the fiscal impulse modestly
positive.
