## Marketcolour + RHM CEO Today / Prime Colour / Earnings
Overview ##
Ioannis Blekos
9 Nov 2026
Good Morning,
US equities finished Friday higher - SPX +31bps to 7376 (7/11 sectors -ve, SPX vs SPW +70bps, Tech +126bps,
Staples -1.21%, Financials -0.68%), NDX +135bps to 26989 (down ~5% last week, 4-week losing streak, Semis
are now down ~28% over the last 1-month), and RTY +89bps to 2754. The NFP print came in hotter than expected
(165k actual vs consensus 75k - Dec hike odds to ~77%.). AI Semis (GSCBSMHX) gained +5.17%, while Data
Centers (GSTMTDAT) rose +4.99%. In Europe, EU Semis (GSSBSEMI +1.6%) bounced, while EU Defense
(GSSBDEFE -2.4%) sold off again.
 
Interesting: 11 of 20 European sectors with RSI >70, widest overbought reading since December 2023
 
GS Invite: Rheinmetall CEO Fireside – Today at 16.00 UKT: Link
 
CTA – Chart below: In the US, big supply for S&P in a flat tape in both scenarios. Positioning: long $23bn S&P
(71%ile) and short -$1bn NDX (35%ile). Key pivot levels for SPX: Med term: 7547
 
Basket Idea: Short our Diversified Staples Basket (GSXUSTAP). Earnings yield and dividend yield of the basket is
unattractive relative to fixed income (treasuries, credit, etc.) and there is not enough growth in the complex to justify
lofty valuations. Earnings revisions have been rolling over and valuations are now in the 90th percentile relative to the
last year. 
 
Prime: US equities were modestly net sold this week, driven by the largest single stock net selling in 4 months partially
offset by buying in Macro Products. Info Tech was by far the most $ net sold global (and US) sector - this week’s %
selling in global Tech stocks was ~on par with the largest net selling weeks on our record (since 2016). Also worth
noting that 4 the top 5 net selling weeks in global Info Tech on our record have occurred within the past five months.
 
HF Performance: With global momentum down 4.6 SD and crowded shorts squeezing at +3.7 SD, another
challenging week for Systematic L/S managers, down 1.5%, now down for the 4th consecutive week, but still up
14.8% YTD. Fundamental L/S managers down 0.4% this week but remain 8.7% up YTD. Fundamentals TMT
managers saw a huge hit this week, down almost 4%, and now up only 1.3% YTD (versus + 25% mid-year).
 
Europe Earnings: Earnings are beating expectations by 3.4% on a market-cap-weighted basis and 2.9% on an equal-
weighted basis, slightly above historical norms. Price reactions remain asymmetric, with misses being penalised more
heavily than beats are rewarded. Energy, Telecoms, Healthcare and Technology have led the beats. Link
 
Industrials / Datacenters: Prysmian has thehighest exposure to Texas, given its Encore Wire and Channell
acquisitions as well as expansion plans for 8 new medium voltage cable manufacturing lines to be built over the
coming years in the state. Legrand has the largest datacenter business in our coverage (32% sales) and is
significantly more weighted towards the US than others, however it has also derated significantly, while also displaying
one of the most depressed non-datacenter businesses in our coverage due to heavy exposure to European resi. Link
 

UNICREDIT: 17% net profit beat driven by better revenues (7% ahead) and lower costs (2% below) and lower LLPs
(10% below). At top line level, NII is 3% above (one of the strongest NII beat of the season), fees 3% above. S
 
HANNOVER RE: net net expect a lot of focus on solvency miss by 14pp (but absolute level fine) and bears would
probably highlight P&C re insurance revenue small miss. But everything else is fine. This is is the default long in the
space. See stock small down -1%.
 
Lancashire (BUY / PT GBP777 / 15% Upside / 12% Div Yield - underappreciated earnings resilience):
Underwriting portfolio is broader while its earnings stream is less volatile. We therefore expect less contraction than in
the previous soft cycle. Link
