GOOGL Releases Two New TPUs (re: NVDA, AVGO, AMD):  
Jensen is having a bad week…  
From Bloomberg: “[Google] is introducing two new variations of its tensor 
processing units, or TPUs, custom-made semiconductors that help develop and 
run AI software. The latest chips will address the need for confidential computing 
and help models access   wider range of information when handling queries. 
More broadly, they’re meant to serve an industry that’s shifting toward the use of 
AI agents and confronting security challenges. The rollout marks an acceleration 
in product development for Google’s chip unit. The business once released a 
new AI processor every two years. This year, it put out two at the same 
time. And the latest variations, due to be announced Monday, will add additional 
flavors to that pair of chips. The four TPU offerings will optimize different needs, 
Google chip chief Amin Vahdat said in an interview. “This really follows the thesis 
that we have here at Google, which is that the future is going to be about 
specialization, and the upsides for specialization continue to be significant.””  
Internet FAQs: GOOGL, META, AMZN, RDDT, etc.:  
From Doug’s Internet FAQs: GOOGL, META, AMZN, RDDT, etc.:  
• 
“How Does the Election Impact the Data Center Buildout and Capex for 
2027? Post the midterms, we are maintaining our elevated infrastructure 
buildout expectations for 2027 as we believe much of the spending for next 
year is already committed in the form of GPUs, signed power deals, & shells 
under construction or permitted. While data center moratorium fears are 
elevated and policies potentially in flux, we believe AI demand remains 
strong, hyperscalers are seeing returns, & the opportunity remains too great 
to underinvest. Just days after the election, we believe much still needs to be 
sorted out between campaign trail narrative & actual policy. We also believe 

hyperscalers are likely to make contingencies across states & geographies, 
as required. We recognize that building data centers could become more 
difficult and expensive, but we continue to expect spending will remain 
elevated to meet demand. We currently model hyperscaler capex growth of 
~87% to $1.7T in 2027 & +19% to $2.0T in 2028.”  
 
 
 
 
 
• 
“Is Google Search Facing a Structural AI Monetization Challenge   What 
Could Move Shares Higher in the Near-Term? We do not believe Google 
Search is structurally broken, but it faces AI monetization headwinds, just 
as it did a few years ago w/the rollout of LLMs. Growth slowed from 19% in 1Q 
& 17% in 2Q to 12% in 3Q despite Google reporting the fastest query growth in 
five years. Importantly, management identified FX as the single largest driver 
of deceleration, alongside Asia retailer impact from the EU de minimis 
exemption & media/entertainment pullback post-World Cup. Paid clicks rose 
8% and CPC rose 4%, but the concern is that paid clicks growth will slow as 
AI Mode becomes a bigger % of Google searches. Like a couple of years ago 
w/AI Overviews, Google will need to show that a better overall search 
experience via AI Mode can drive incremental query volume, potentially 
higher coverage, & perhaps increased CPC for a more qualified lead. We 
believe monetization can improve as international AI Mode ads and US multi-
turn formats expand.”

META Expanding Teen Protections Worldwide:  
Any reason to think there might be a quarter when Family 
DAPs don’t increase? Just keep that possibility in the back of your mind.  
From a META Press Release: “Ensuring all teens receive these protections 
depends on knowing their age. This continues to be an industry-wide challenge 
as some teens may misrepresent their age online to access experiences they’re 
not old enough to use. We use AI technology to find people we suspect to be 
teens, even if they use an adult birthday, and place them in Teen Account 
protections. Since first introducing this technology, we’ve been placing more 

teens in age-appropriate protections and are encouraged by the results. Based 
on that success, today we’re expanding this technology globally. As a result, 
millions more teens across the world who use Instagram will receive these built-
in protections, even if they’ve misrepresented their age. For parents, this means 
greater peace of mind: no matter where you live, we’re making proactive efforts 
to place your teen into age-appropriate protections — even if they use an adult 
birthday to set up an account. We’re continuing to roll this technology out on 
Facebook and, later this year, we’ll begin launching similar technology globally 
on Threads. This expansion builds on our commitments under our recent 
agreement with 52 bipartisan attorneys general to strengthen how we identify 
teens and children under 13 on our apps.”

DDOG CEO/CFO Call Takeaways:  
From Samik’s DDOG CEO/CFO Call Takeaways: “Core business acceleration is 
broad based, supported by multiple growth drivers. Building on the 
acceleration in non-AI customer revenue growth highlighted in the F3Q earnings 

print, management expressed confidence in the demand environment, pointing 
to a combination of successful sales capacity expansion, platform 
consolidation, broader product adoption, and market share gains. Non-AI 
natives are increasingly migrating to the cloud, adopting additional Datadog 
products, and growing their usage. The company provided additional context on 
moderating customer additions, noting that Datadog typically adds 
approximately 2K gross new logos per quarter, with gross new logos influenced 
by fluctuations among small customers and expanded credits. Datadog has 
broadened its startup program and is allocating more engineering resources to 
high-growth startups, and startup credits can temporarily reduce logo 
additions.”  
“Guidance philosophy remains unchanged, with Datadog emphasizing 
conservatism around consumption trends. Datadog reiterated that the 
company’s forecasting approach incorporates recent consumption trends and 
net retention rates, discounted for usage volatility and its historical beat-and-
raise cadence. With respect to F3Q, the ~$47M revenue beat vs. guidance of 
$1.14B at the mid-point was split roughly 60/40 between the core business and 
Datadog’s largest customer. As for the F4Q guidance, management reiterated 
that the guide assumes only the largest customer’s minimum contractual 
commitment which would be a sequential headwind given the large customer 
consumer materially above the minimum commit in F3Q. For 2027, Datadog 
expects to continue modeling that customer at contractual minimums (only until 
current contract expiry, without assuming renewal in guidance until the contract 
is renewed) and other AI-native customers using risk-adjusted consumption 
trends.”  
TSLA Expands Robotaxi Service to San Antonio (re: UBER, LYFT, GOOGL):  
From TechCrunch: “Tesla is expanding its robotaxi service to another Texas city 
as it seeks to compete with Waymo and position itself as more than just an 
automaker. The company said in a post on X that it will make its robotaxis 
available in San Antonio starting at 5 a.m. local time on Monday. Tesla didn’t 
provide any additional details about the service, such as the hours of operation, 
whether the fleet will offer customers its gold, two-seater Cybercab as well as its 
modified Model Y vehicles, or if these vehicles will have human safety monitors 
on board. But if the company follows its playbook of previous launches, it will 
start with a limited number of vehicles and a confined service area, and expand 
slowly from there.”
