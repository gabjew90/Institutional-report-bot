More detail in next week’s weekly update, but since most of the moves were in equities yesterday and as there
has been a lot of incoming…
I didn’t expect yesterday’s equities sell-off, but after a decline like yesterday, one needs to ask; was that the
end of the bull market and is risk / reward skewed higher or lower from here? I don’t expect this is the end of
the bull market (and SPX remains on track for my 8,485 target next year), and as such, I would not
recommend selling into weakness, as risk / reward into year-end and beyond is heavily skewed to the topside
from current levels.
From a cross-asset perspective, it’s encouraging outside of Japan (which I have said to avoid) and South
Korea, markets like SX5E, Hang Seng (which is a developing Wave 3 on daily, weekly and monthly charts)
and Australia have done OK, while Mexico and India (which I’ve been bullish on over the past month), are up.
Elsewhere, there has been comparatively little collateral damage, suggesting the decline was likely a result of
de-risking rather than de-leveraging and therefore consistent with my view the sell-off while dramatic, was
likely corrective within the broader advance and not the end of the bull market.
SOX is clearly at the epicentre of the de-risking, so it’s noteworthy it was a TD Combo 11 Count yesterday,
meaning it’s possible we get a Buy signal (13), as soon as Monday’s close (09-Nov). Furthermore, 63.30% of
SOX constituents are at / near a DeMark Buy and both daily and weekly TD Propulsion downside targets were
hit at the low yesterday. Typically, when a market falls precipitously in one day to either a confluence of daily
and weekly TD Propulsion Exhaustion levels (as was the case yesterday), or a monthly level (as was the case
for SPX at the Liberation Day low last year), it’s common to see a tradable rebound. Meanwhile, from a time
perspective, we would get a TD Combo 13 Buy signal as soon as Monday’s close (09-Nov), if the market was
to remain under pressure until then (and in doing so, it would be the first daily TD Combo Buy since the market
topped over the Summer). The daily upside TD Propulsion Level (i.e. the level that would relieve the
immediate downside threat), is 9,938, with an early sign of a recovery beyond 9,264 and 9,530.
NDX is lagging by one day, it was a daily TD Combo 10 Count yesterday. Note too, SPW vs NDX ratio was a
daily TD Combo 11 Count yesterday, so we could get a Sell (13), as soon as Mon’s close. [16.83% of NDX
names are at / near a Sell, while 29.71% is at / near a Buy].
