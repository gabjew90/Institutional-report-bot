## Marketcolour + Cross-Asset / Consumer Outlook / Earnings ##
Ioannis Blekos
10 Nov 2026
Good Morning,
US Equities finished higher amid solid volumes – SPX +235bps to 7549 (all 11 sectors +ve, 423 stocks +ve, VIX
-22.31% to 19.92, Tech +4.42%, Indus +3.52%, Comm Svs + 2.49%), DJIA +215bps to 52389, NDX +274bps to
25253 (despite NVDA -4.3%, SOX +6.85%). Also helping sentiment was Crude down ~5% to $85 on further Iran de-
escalation. In Europe, AI names (EU Semis GSSBSEMI +4.82%) and Italian banks (GSXEITBK +4.65% following
Credit Agricole/BAMI M&A and strong UCG earnings) outperformed. EU Cyclicals vs Defensives (GSPECYDE +2.2%)
recovering while we saw some LO buying in old favourites such as RR/ LN, AIR FP, ASML NA, SAP GY.
 
Interesting: US Info Tech net allocation (as % of total US net MV) now stands at 14.4%, approaching multi-year lows
in the 1st/1st/3rd percentiles on a 1-year/3-year/5-year lookback
 
Interesting 2: During last week’s selloff, retail investors stepped in to buy the dip. On Thursday, leveraged ETFs,
which tend to be popular with the retail segment, received record inflows of +$3.3bn. SOXL, a Semiconductor-focused
3X leverage ETF, attracted +$5.1bn in 5-day net inflows, capturing most of the positive buying in the leveraged
category; other beneficiaries included TQQQ, SNXX, GDXU and NVDL
 
Flow: It was constructive to see a mix of covering / long buying across Tech yesterday (ex-NVDA on weekend
headlines) though mkt hesitant to give the ALL CLEAR ahead of CPI today (GIR at +16bps for Core MoM) and
CRWV/NBIS earnings later this week. Our floor was a 5 on a 1-10 scale in terms of overall activity levels. Asset Mgr
flows finished ~flat with demand in tech and fins vs supply in Comm services. HFs finished net buyers largely driven by
macro products.
 
Cross-Asset: We remain OW equities over 3m/12m, as earnings support, lighter positioning and lower
valuations should support equities into year-end. We remain OW cash though as near-term volatility might linger
due to uncertainty around AI and the Middle East tensions. In the near term, we see three paths to a rebound in risk
appetite: (1) easing AI concerns and signs of monetisation, (2) rates relief, potentially helped by falling energy prices
and (3) a larger decline of our RAI below -1, improving asymmetry to add risk into year-end.
 
US Consumer Outlook: We expect consumer spending to rise at a near-potential 2.2% pace in 2027 (Q4/Q4). We
look for a softer start to the year as higher rates weigh on durables demand, energy prices stay elevated, and nonprofit
spending declines after the midterm elections, followed by a pickup as inflation pressures abate and tax refunds and
the reversal of benefit cuts turn the fiscal impulse modestly positive. Link
 
US Semis: AI Compute: We favor NVDA and AVGO, which imply no growth from 2027 // Semi Cap Equipment: We
see 2027 WFE as a baseline and prefer AMAT & LRCX // Memory: Approaching oversold levels as we begin to move
toward tangible book Link
 
Rheinmetall / Ceo Fireside (BUY / PT EUR2100 / 7x Ebitda): Rheinmetall remains confident that the Arminius
programme will proceed + The outlook for artillery ammunition remains supportive + Naval: ighlighted a pipeline of
naval opportunities spanning new-build programmes, maintenance and ship repair. Management continues to see a
strong underlying demand environment across vehicles, ammunition, air defence, naval and emerging
technologies. Link

 
IFX – Q4 inline, and guide inline both for Q4 and FY27. Would note that data centers guide at close to €4B s ahead
of the sellside but buyside was looking for >€4bn. That said the FX guide is conservative and mgt have a reputation of
been conservative and setting guidance it can beat, plus it looks like we might go higher this morning market wise as
bounce continues – Anlog bounce held gains overnight and this is a name in the space investors were looking to own
post guide. Q4: Revenue €4.71B inline, small beat at EBIT at €1.12B vs consensus €1.10B with margin 23.8% vs
consensus 23.3% and guidance ~23%.
 
Sunrise inline with street and guidance reiterate. Q3 Revenue CHF732m and adj EBITDA aL 0.3% beat qat
CHF264.8M. OFCF beat on lower capex while FCF missed due to WC. Adj FCF CHF32.5M, (11%) vs year-ago
CHF36.7M. FY26 guidance confirmed
 
Pluxee up to N / PT EUR12.20: We upgrade Pluxee to Neutral (from Sell) following share price underperformance
and another reset of earnings expectations. We believe the risk/reward is now more balanced. Link
