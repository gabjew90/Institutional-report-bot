Circuit Breakers Fall Short. Over the past 2 months, none of the circuit breakers discussed in this note as
catalysts to fresh highs have manifested. Rate pressures spiraled into funding pressures, oil prices soared as
Mideast conflict continued, hyperscaler earnings fell short, and the Democrats dominated the mid-terms.
Despite continued AI demand, the result has been a violent repricing in AI as investors have demanded a large
discount to add AI risk. Instead, investors have shifted incremental dollars away from Tech, and even away
from equities and into a credit, where AI exposure is growing exponentially. The rotation away from Tech
crescendoed last week as systematic selling triggered over 365bn of excess sell flow on Thursday, the largest
on record. While most of the systematic selling is behind us, with a residual amount Monday, the bigger issue
for the market is absence of long-only demand. This is the focal point for flows from here. 
• Expect US equities to remain vulnerable in early trading this week with another test of technical support / the
200-day moving average likely. Squeeze risk is acute; If that clearing event comes without a corresponding
deterioration in credit, rates or FX, look to add risk rather than protection.
Rotation, Not Inflows. Throughout the AI de-risking, the market rotated into quality – both within AI, and
across sectors. For Tech/AI, that’s meant long Hyper-scaler outperformance vs the broader AI complex, and
especially subsectors heavily dependent of funding (neoclouds). Given extreme outperformance in
hyperscalers, alongside semiconductors giving up most of the post-April rally, there are now rotations from
Hyperscalers back into semis; Friday’s 5% outperformance in Semis vs. a flat hyperscaler basket suggests
it's an important rotational turning point. 
• Long Only Bid Needed. For follow through in broad-base risk, Long only demand still needs to emerge; It’s
unclear how much of a rebound is possible via rotations and systematic selling pressure abating alone. 
Extreme Equity Risk Premium. Rebecca Cheong’s VIX cross asset score hit 96% Thursday, a level typically
suggestive of capitulatory price action as 1M forward returns favor lower VIX with a +90% hit rate. In some
sense, SPX was overdue for a correlated selloff given 2026 is tied with 2017 for the lowest number of days
where all sectors were down in one trading session. While the correlated move lower was challenging for short
vol strategies, dispersion strategies were mixed as the single stock long volatility selection drove differentiated
alpha returns.
• XUBSDVS4: UBS’s Bespoke Dispersion Strategy index which isolates the most popular names in structured
products (largely Tech/AI) hit an all-time high on Thursday & Friday as single stock moves far outweighed
SPX’s -4.4% implied move. This index remains a positive expectation hedge to AI exposures.
CPI and NVDA EPS Up Next. CPI and NVDA earnings on the 17th are the next two circuit breakers for risk,
with SPX pricing ~1% 1d moves for both, among the highest through year-end. Alan expects a softer October
CPI at +0.23% core/+0.26% headline, helped by gasoline and used vehicles. Macro focus is back on the long
end after strong NFP, with a 10Y retest of recent highs a clear headwind for risk. US Econ Weekly
