Top of Mind: Is the AI investment cycle durable?
10 November 2026 | 1:00PM EST | Research | Economics
Recent AI-related market stresses and new regulatory uncertainty are raising fresh questions
about the durability of the AI investment cycle. For answers, we turn to Social Capital’s
Chamath Palihapitiya and GS’ Eric Sheridan and Miriam Wheeler as well as other GS credit
and equity research analysts to discuss the source of the current stresses, who holds the risk
in the AI ecosystem, where the outlook for ROI central to the AI theme now stands, and how
investors can best navigate the current moment.
