**Q:** how is the itnerest coverage ratio calculated

**A:**

The interest coverage ratio is calculated by dividing a company's **EBIT** (Earnings Before Interest and Taxes) by its **interest expense** for the same period.

You can find **EBIT** on the income statement by taking total revenue and subtracting COGS, SG&A, and depreciation/amortization expenses.

A ratio **below 1.5x** is generally considered a red flag for financial distress, while a ratio **above 5x** indicates a strong buffer to meet debt obligations.

This metric tells you how many times a company’s operating earnings can cover its interest payments, serving as a primary gauge for solvency risk.
