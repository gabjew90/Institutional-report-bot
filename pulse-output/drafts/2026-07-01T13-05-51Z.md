# The AI trade splits

## 1. RECAP

[LIVE PRICE RECAP]

June closed with the AI infrastructure trade tearing in half. The hyperscaler cohort had its worst month since Meta went public, down roughly 18% since June 1, while the memory names ran the other way, up 16% on the month with Goldman's custom memory basket sitting on a 250% year-to-date gain. Micron printed a blowout quarter and the broad AI-infrastructure complex sold off anyway. Underneath the headline index weakness, breadth actually held up, with 63% of S&P 500 names positive month-to-date and roughly 60% still above their 50- and 200-day averages, so this reads more like a violent rotation inside the AI trade than a market-wide risk-off. The other big move was oil, which gave back the entire war premium as US-Iran de-escalation headlines landed. Positioning is the tell going into the second half: hedge funds are watching whether the memory-vs-hyperscaler split is the start of the AI unwind or just leadership changing hands.
