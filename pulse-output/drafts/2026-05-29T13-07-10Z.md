## 2. INSIGHTS & ALPHA

### AI capex is the earnings engine, and a real minority calls it a bubble

*The AI capex cycle is paying out in earnings growth right now and that is the entire reason the index is bid, but four desks are putting capital-destruction risk on the same page.*

- Goldman, UBS, and Citi attribute roughly half of this year's S&P earnings growth to companies tied to AI infrastructure investment, which is what is actually driving the market higher rather than multiple expansion
- Marvell guided up on data center visibility through 2027, which is the kind of customer-confirmation signal that resets the duration of the bid for the supply chain
- Dell is set up for an earnings beat and an FY27 guide higher on AI server demand, with Lenovo in the same boat (Goldman); AI server monetization is now showing up at the OEM level, not just at the chip layer
- Jefferies' GREED & fear note frames this as a capex cycle where "a lot of capital will be destroyed" and BofA Securities points out that AI-stock concentration mirrors prior bubble peaks
- Zürcher Kantonalbank flags that the direct GDP contribution from AI investment is significantly smaller than the headline capex numbers suggest, because the spend is import-leaky and capex doesn't translate one-for-one to revenue

What is being repriced is the persistence of the bid, not the existence of it. The bulls and bears agree on the spending; they disagree on the payoff. The bull case is that the buildout reaches every adjacent supply chain — custom silicon, packaging, servers, power, even silver and solar — and the duration extends well into 2027, so any single-quarter guide-down is noise inside a multi-year capex window. The bear case lands when one MAG7 hyperscaler trims a capex guide and the entire picks-and-shovels trade gets re-rated on the same multiple compression that broke prior cycles, with Jefferies explicitly framing this as a capital-destruction setup rather than a soft landing. Where the bull case keeps winning the argument is duration: Marvell's 2027 visibility and the Dell / Lenovo OEM monetization both say the bid has at least four more quarters of customer confirmation behind it before any guide-down lands. Long the picks-and-shovels stack, with $SOXX as the cleanest single-instrument expression and $AMAT and Tokyo Electron as the names that get paid through the cycle regardless of which hyperscaler trims first.

### The Fed split is real and BofA is the outlier

*Two desks see the Fed staying tight because inflation hasn't cooled enough, but BofA's argument is that this FOMC is structurally dovish and will cut the second the labor data gives it cover.*

- SEB and ING both argue the Fed is unlikely to cut again any time soon because services inflation hasn't broken and the energy bleed-through is still in the pipeline
- BofA Global Research's counter is that the dot plot and the Powell language history both point to a fundamentally dovish FOMC, with cuts arriving as soon as a single softer NFP print clears the political ramp
- Deutsche Bank, Goldman Sachs, and UBS all have rate-cut timing being repriced in the front end of the curve in their notes this window, with the directional disagreement on which side of the print the move lands
- The PCE pulse that just printed lands in the middle of this argument: a cooler core read gives BofA's view fresh evidence, but the SEB / ING side will point to services PCE staying sticky as the binding constraint
- No desk on the print-watching side has called the actual cut number; the dispersion across the curve has flattened on dispersion in the call rather than directional agreement

The setup is a directional dispersion trade rather than a timing trade. The bull case for BofA's view is that the Fed cuts on the first soft NFP and the front end of the Treasury curve gets re-bid, with 2-year Treasury yields ($SHY as the cleanest retail-listed instrument) falling 25-40 basis points across the move. The bear case is that the SEB / ING side wins, services PCE stays sticky, and the Fed holds through the summer, in which case the long end of the curve gets the entire bear-steepening because supply is the binding constraint. Where this resolves is the next NFP print: a number below the 85K consensus pulls BofA's view forward; a number above 150K validates the hawkish hold. Long $TLT into the next labor print is the cleanest way to express the BofA side, because the cooler PCE has already done some of the work and the dispersion is paying you for taking a side rather than for waiting it out.

### Consumer pressure is real, the read on it is contested

*Six banks have consumer confidence at record lows and the household saving rate contracting fast, but BofA's pushback is that this is wealthy households spending down a portfolio runup, not evidence of real distress.*

- Goldman, ING, JPMorgan, SEB, UBS, and Bank of America all flag US consumer sentiment at or near record lows in their notes this window, which is a broader consensus than any other softness signal
- The household saving rate is contracting and disposable income is deteriorating across the same five desks, which is the cleanest cross-bank agreement on the data itself
- SEB notes 1Q consumer spending was weak, which is the actual hard-data anchor under the softer sentiment readings
- ING's read: "consumer spending power is coming under increasing pressure," which is a direct call that the soft data is converting to real spending
- BofA's pushback is that the saving rate tends to be lower when net worth to disposable income runs above trend, so a contracting saving rate during a stock-market runup is a wealth-effect drawdown rather than a stress signal

The bull case for the BofA framing is that the K-shape economy is doing its usual thing — the top quintile is spending down portfolio gains while the bottom three quintiles are buckling under sticky services inflation, and aggregate spending holds up because the spenders at the top are doing the work. The bear case is that the K-shape eventually meets a labor weakness print, the top quintile stops spending because the wealth signal flips, and the saving-rate compression turns into a hard demand impulse on the way down. Where this resolves in the next month is the consumer-discretionary tape: a Costco-style miss across the wallet-share names would validate ING's read and force a rotation; a Lululemon-style beat would reinforce BofA's wealth-effect framing. Long $XLP over short $XLY captures the directional bias on the ING side without naming a single ticker exposure; for someone with crypto exposure, the read is that a confirmed K-shape break is the catalyst that pulls retail flows back out of risk and into stables, which is the inverse setup to the runups earlier this year.

### The AI infrastructure buildout is broader than the chip layer

*The capex story is showing up at the hyperscaler-adjacent layer — silicon, packaging, power, miners pivoting to AI cloud — and the breadth is the bull case rather than the headline.*

- 2027 hyperscaler capex revisions are the dominant spending theme this window across Goldman, JPMorgan, Zürcher Kantonalbank, UniCredit, Global X, and LSEG, which is the cross-desk floor for the duration call
- Goldman flags IREN's $1.6 billion purchase agreement under an NVIDIA AI cloud contract as a live example of bitcoin miners pivoting compute to AI services — capacity that already exists getting rerated as AI infrastructure
- UniCredit, Zürcher Kantonalbank, The Market Ear, UBS, LSEG, Global X, Goldman, and JPMorgan all flag adjacent supply chains — custom silicon, packaging, servers, solar, silver — getting rerated as AI infrastructure plays
- The Market Ear and Goldman note hyperscaler CFOs starting ROI scrutiny on AI capex, which is the first leading signal a guide-down lands in the next two quarters
- Chinese internet companies are taking a conservative AI capex approach (UBS) which is a sourcing-risk signal more than a demand signal — it tightens the global supply-chain dependency on the US hyperscalers and the picks-and-shovels names that serve them

The bull case is that breadth is durable: when a power utility, a silver miner, a memory name, and a packaging substrate name all get bid on AI capex, the cycle has at least 18 months of duration left in it because every adjacent name is racing to capture share of an already-funded buildout. The bear case is that the breadth is itself the bubble signal — every cycle peaks when the second-order names start trading on the primary catalyst, and Chinese internet conservatism plus hyperscaler ROI scrutiny is the early-warning signal that the primary catalyst is rolling over. Where this resolves is in MAG7 capex guides through the next two quarters: a single guide-down validates the bear setup; another round of upward revisions extends the bid into 2027. Long the picks-and-shovels names is the cleanest expression and Long $URA / $URNM captures the power-buildout side without paying for the consensus chip-equipment names.

### ETF and crypto flows are softening and bond ETFs are getting bid

*Flow tape across US equity ETFs and spot bitcoin ETFs is deteriorating while bond ETFs still attract money, and China / Korea fund outflows are the cross-asset cleanup signal nobody has called direction on yet.*

- Three desks (Bank of America, BlackRock, Bloomberg) flag deteriorating flows into US equity ETFs and spot bitcoin ETFs as a live concern this window
- Bloomberg has inflows to Treasury and corporate bond ETFs ongoing — the rotation is risk-off without being panic-off, which is a different signal from a 2022-style flush
- Bloomberg and The Market Ear have China fund and South Korea equity outflows running, which is the EM-asia leg of the same rotation
- Goldman has Value-factor outflows running, which is a signal that the rotation is also happening inside the US equity tape — out of cyclicals into Quality / Growth, not just out of equities entirely
- The cross-asset read: equity beta is being trimmed, duration is being bought, and crypto is taking the brunt because retail flows are the marginal buyer

The bull case for the rotation reading correctly is that bond ETF inflows are the early-cycle defensive signal — Treasuries get bid before equities flush, and the divergence between rising bond ETF flows and falling equity / crypto ETF flows is exactly what you see ahead of a vol expansion. The bear case is that the divergence is shallow, the rotation reverses in two weeks as the Iran story resolves one way or another, and the bond-ETF bid is just positioning ahead of the Fed split resolving dovish. Where this resolves is the bitcoin spot ETF flow tape: a second week of outflows confirms the risk-off rotation is real and the right trade is long $TLT against short $BTC; a single week of strong inflows reverses the read entirely. Long $TLT, paired against short $XBI as the speculative-flow proxy, captures the bond-bid / risk-off rotation without taking a side on the Iran story.
