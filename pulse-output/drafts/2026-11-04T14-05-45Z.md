# Democrats sweep, Hormuz burns

## 1. RECAP

Heading into Wednesday's open, the midterm result is the first thing stocks have to price. Democrats took control of Congress overnight, and US stock futures rose while the dollar slipped. Tuesday's session ended with $SPY up 0.30% and $QQQ up 0.62%, before the results were known. The 10-year Treasury yield is 4.92% and the 30-year is 5.31%, both a little lower this morning. $BTC is near $78,200, up 1.0% since 8 PM ET Tuesday.

- **Democrats win Congress.** Trump said the Iran war played a part in the result. The sweep lands on top of three worries the market already had, which are Iran, AI spending and the federal debt, and stock futures rose and the dollar slipped on the news.
- **Tankers hit mines near the Strait of Hormuz.** Iran's Revolutionary Guard said four tankers were on fire after hitting mines near the strait, and India said one Indian was killed in an attack on a ship near Oman. Iran's supreme leader said US hostility will persist whichever party is in power. $USO rose 0.83% Tuesday before these reports, so oil's reaction at the open is the open question.
