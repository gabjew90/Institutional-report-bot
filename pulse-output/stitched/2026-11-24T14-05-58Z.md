# Snapback rally gets tested

## 1. RECAP

Heading into Tuesday's open, Monday's session left $SPY up 4.71% at $722.29 and $QQQ up 7.00%, the first big up day after a week in which the S&P 500 fell about 8.6% and closed lower seven sessions in a row (Goldman). Nvidia led the rebound and is giving back part of it before the bell. $VIXY dropped 12.99%, so traders paid far less for protection by the close. The rally came off washed-out positioning: going into it, Goldman estimated trend-following funds were net short $19bn of US stocks after selling $57bn in a month, and hedge fund net exposure adjusted for market sensitivity sat in the 1st percentile of the past three years. $BTC did not join in and sits at $60,408, down 1.41% today. $GLD rose 3.14% Monday.

- **Oil slid to a three-month low, then bounced on Hormuz.** $USO fell 4.49% and $BNO 4.00% on Monday, and crude touched three-month lows overnight before recovering on a threat to shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. The US is also looking at Iran as a possible culprit in dozens of new cyberattacks on ships, and Iraq's prime minister ordered his ministry to take over oilfields in Kurdish-controlled areas, which the Kurds rejected.
- **Nvidia-led rip, with the first doubts already out.** $QQQ's 7.00% gain was the Nasdaq's strongest session in the data here. Before the open Nvidia is giving back some of that, and Anthropic trades below its $83 IPO price.
