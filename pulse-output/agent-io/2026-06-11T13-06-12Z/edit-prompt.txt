### Oil is one headline away from a different quarter

*The market has absorbed the Hormuz shock so far, and absorption is not the same as resolution.*

The reason prices have not spiked is plumbing, not calm. Supply leakage plus strategic releases plus some demand destruction have filled the hole, but every week of draws makes the next headline matter more. The counter is that a reopening in June ends the trade overnight, and the futures curve is leaning that way. The defense is the inventory math: a 450-million-barrel hole does not refill in a week regardless of the diplomacy, so the downside in oil is capped while the upside is a gap. If Hormuz is still blocked at month-end, the thesis is live and the trade is long $USO into the next escalation headline; if the Strait reopens in June, it is over.

The tension is that benign inflation and a hawkish Fed can coexist right now. The bull case for bonds is that the inflation trend is clearly cooling and the cuts come later this year. The harder read is the gap between Goldman's 4.37% and the market's 4.05%: if the FOMC removes the easing bias next week and the data stays firm, the front end reprices toward the bank's call. The lean is front-end Treasury longs, where Goldman argues the room for a selloff is limited at current pricing; if the statement drops the bias and activity data stays solid, that trade is wrong and the pain is in rate-sensitive equities.

### The World Cup is a real, dated, US-spending catalyst

- JPMorgan is tactically long its 2026 World Cup Beneficiaries Basket, with $GOOGL (ad capture) and $DASH (delivery) as named overweights

The mechanism is simple and the timing is hard: a fixed event with a known calendar funnels spending into ticketing, lodging, rideshare, food, and apparel. The honest caveat is scale. A $17.2B bump is rounding error against US GDP, and the academic read is that sporting-event boosts are short-lived, so this is a trade on specific names, not a macro theme. That is exactly why it works as a basket rather than an index call, because the lift concentrates in a handful of consumer beneficiaries instead of washing out across the market. Does the spending show up in the beneficiary tape before the tournament starts, or only once it does? Long the lodging and delivery names ($DASH, with $HLT and $MAR as the hotel expressions) is the cleanest way to be early.
