This is not two independent hallucinations; it's one mechanism (EDIT
converting `_LEANS` lines into body-text commands) firing on two

as the only backstop, and it caught both instances, but a repair-budget
exhaustion is a coin flip on which one ships. Fixing this at the source
(EDIT_SYSTEM/EDIT_USER instructions explicit that `_LEANS` content
must never be rendered as an imperative sentence in the body, only as
the falsifiable-level language DRAFT itself already knows how to write)
would remove the failure mode instead of relying on the adversarial
check to catch every instance under a shrinking repair budget.

**Bucket:** prompt-session (EDIT_USER instruction fix) with a
deterministic-fixable companion (STEP 7's own suggestion — apply a
