## Methodology note (read this before trusting the rest)

`pulse-context/latest.json` — the artifact this workflow names as "the day's research context dump" — is **not scoped to this run**. Its `dumped_at_utc` is `2026-08-31T20:31:21Z` (6+ hours after this run's `14:42Z` commit), `pdf_count: 44`, `window_label: last-24h` — the signature of a later manual/test `/pulse` invocation, not the scheduled run's 64-PDF "since last scheduled pulse" window recorded in the archive frontmatter.
