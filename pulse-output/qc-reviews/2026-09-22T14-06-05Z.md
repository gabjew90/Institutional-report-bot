### The miss-it test

**Verdict: not yet, and closer than yesterday.**

A trader would miss the MAIN EVENT's framing specifically. "Term premium has moved above investment-grade spreads, which reverses the post-2008 pattern" plus the gigawatt valuation gap is a genuinely distinctive read they would not assemble from a wire, and the Fed brief's December-decides-it close is the kind of stake worth tracking.
