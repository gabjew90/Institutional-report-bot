[inferred] Three consequences worth separating, because they suggest different fixes:

1. **The bug was invisible because the run looked clean.** The post-Rule-6 emptiness check (`synthesis-routine.md:1345-1355`) discards a theme only when facts_agreed, facts_contested *and* predictions are all empty.
