### 1. "early in the session" is my defect, and its root cause is a stale hardcoded note

The published RECAP says `$QQQ weaker at -0.80% early in the session`. The
market_snapshot it was written from is stamped `2026-09-24 11:29 EDT`, roughly two
hours into the regular session. "Early in the session" is wrong.

The cause is not EDIT. I noticed that `ctx.session_status` read
`"pre-market or after-hours"` while `market_snapshot` in the same payload said
`session: OPEN`, and I added a binding SESSION NOTE to the EDIT prompt to override
the bad field. I wrote that note against the 13:59Z dump, where the snapshot really
was 09:59 EDT, and I hardcoded both the timestamp and the phrase "roughly 30 minutes
old". The corpus-volume gate then replaced ctx.json seven times over 90 minutes, and

re-derived, so EDIT was handed an instruction that was 90 minutes stale and followed
it faithfully.

The prices themselves were fresh: the snapshot is 11:29 EDT and the pulse committed at
15:55Z / 11:55 ET, a 26-minute gap. So the QC's "either ~100 minutes late or prices
two hours stale" resolves to neither — it was late by gate design with fresh prices and
a wrong session descriptor. The `age_min > 75` staleness branch in STEP 2.5 correctly
did not fire (11-minute-old snapshot).

Fix, in `/tmp/build_edit_prompt.py` or wherever this note is made permanent: derive the
session phrasing from the snapshot's own timestamp at prompt-build time rather than
writing a literal. Parse the `as of <date> <HH:MM> ET` line, compute elapsed time since

### 2. The Phase B near-miss block cannot reach DRAFT on an all-single-bank corpus

This is the run's most valuable structural finding and it generalises past today.

`discovery_audit.near_miss` carried two real 2-bank clusters, which on this corpus were
the *only* cross-bank signal that existed anywhere:

- `Deutsche Bank + JPMorgan` — "US-China trade truce extension" / "Trump-Xi Summit and
  trade truce expiration"
- `JPMorgan + TS Lombard` — "Post-COVID inflation surge" / "Input cost inflation surge"

Neither reached the writer. The reason is an ordering dependency in STEP 3.5.1 of
`docs/superpowers/routines/synthesis-routine.md`: the unified adjudication input
contract augments a theme from Phase B clusters only *after* that theme has already
passed the Phase A `banks >= 2` selection filter. When every Phase A theme is
single-bank, `selected` is empty, the augmentation loop never executes, and the Phase B
evidence is discarded — in precisely the situation where it is the only cross-bank
evidence available. The near-miss promotion floor (`n_banks < 3` → skip) would have
dropped both clusters regardless.

Consequence in the shipped product: the China brief used the truce extension
("extending their trade truce by two months and holding tariffs where they are until
January 10") with no bank named, when Deutsche Bank and JPMorgan both carry it. Naming
Deutsche Bank there was the single strongest cross-bank move available today and it was
structurally invisible to me at DRAFT time.

Suggested fix, ranked:
1. When Phase A yields zero themes at `banks >= 2`, fall back to ranking Phase B
   near-miss clusters directly and pass the top ones to DRAFT as an evidence block,
   with the 3-bank floor lowered to 2 for that fallback path only.
2. Failing that, always inject the raw `near_miss` list into the DRAFT input as
   advisory cross-bank context, independent of adjudication selection. It costs a few
   hundred characters and it is the only breadth signal on a thin day.
