- **scripts/pulse_lint.py (`_has_signal`, lines 484-488)**: replace the "any single word >3 chars matches" check with a stricter rule — require either an exact-phrase substring match of the theme_key, or a majority (e.g. ≥2 of 3, or >50%) of its significant words to appear, and exclude a small stoplist of generic financial vocabulary ("trade," "market," "rate," "growth," "policy") from counting as a significant word on their own.

  - Information or capability lost: none — purely tightens a false-negative; genuinely-covered themes with distinctive single-word proxies (e.g. "tungsten supply structural shift") still pass under the majority-match branch.
